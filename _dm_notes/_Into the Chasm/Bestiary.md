DEX 14 (+2)  
CON 10 (+0)  
INT 3 (–4)

**Bite**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.

# **WIND-GNAWED SHADE**

_Medium undead-elemental, neutral evil_

**Armor Class** 16
**Hit Points** 24
**Speed** fly 40 ft. (hover)

STR 6 (–2)  
DEX 18 (+4)  
CON 8 (–1)  
INT 6 (–2)  
WIS 14 (+2)  
CHA 12 (+1)

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +5  
**Damage Resistances** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** charmed, grappled, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Condition Immunities** exhausted, poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  

---

### **Traits**

**Windform.** The Shade can move through cracks or openings at least 1 inch wide.
**Echo Drift.** While in dim light or darkness, the Shade has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

---

## **Actions**

### **Whirl-Lunge.**

_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 8 (1d6 + 4) necrotic, and the target must succeed on a **DC 13 Str save** or be pushed 10 ft in a direction of the Shade’s choice.

### **Shriek of Memory (Recharge 6).**

The Shade unleashes a wailing gust. 
Creatures in a 15-ft cone must make a **DC 13 Wis save** or take **4 (1d6) psychic damage** and become **frightened** of the Shade until the end of their next turn.  
Success → half damage, no frightened.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Windstep.**  
The Shade teleports up to 10 ft to a space it can see. A creature occupying the space must make a DC 13 Str save or be pushed 10 ft in a direction of the Shade's choice.

# ❄️ **ENVIRONMENTAL FEATURES (STATIFIED)**

## **Cracked Ice Terrain**

Moving across a cracked area: **DC 12 Dex save**

- Fail → fall prone
    
- When prone → the tile under them breaks, dropping them **5 ft** into a shallow fissure
    

Climbing out: **Athletics DC 10**

No instant-death gaps — intended to be tense, not lethal.

---

## **Edge of the Platform**

Falling off the platform:

- Drop **40 ft** into soft wind cushioning: **2d6 bludgeoning**
    
- Then **DC 14 Str save**
    
    - Success → blown back onto any edge of the platform
        
    - Failure → drift 10–20 ft below, must be pulled up or fly up
        

Flyers can still get buffeted by sudden gusts:

- If flying within 10 ft of the vortex → **DC 12 Dex save** or pushed 10 ft sideways.
    

---

## **Wind Shear Burst (Lair Action at initiative 20)**

A sudden surge of wind crosses the arena.

All creatures make **DC 12 Dex save**:

- Fail → pushed 5 ft in random direction
    
- If this causes crossing a cracked tile → make the Dex save for cracked ice  
    Shades: advantage  
    Wisps: immune
    

---

## **Spell Disruption**

When a PC casts a 2nd-level or higher spell:  
**DC 10 Con save**

- Fail → spell fizzles
    
- PC may force the spell through by taking **2 force damage**
    

This is easy for mid-tactics players to understand and work around.
