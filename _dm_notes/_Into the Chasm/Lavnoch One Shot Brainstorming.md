Haunted Yeti
AC 14; HP 102
STR +4 DEX +1 CON +4 INT -1 WIS +1 CHA -2
Immunities: Cold
Darkvision: 60 feet
Speed: 40 feet / climb 40 feet

Perception: +7; Stealth: +5

_**Fear of Fire.**_ If the yeti takes Fire damage, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.

_**Multiattack.**_ The yeti can use its Chilling Gaze or Echoing Howl and makes two attacks, using Claw or Ice Throw in any combination.

_**Claw.** Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 2d6 + 4 Slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) Cold damage.
_**Ice Throw.** Ranged Attack Roll:_ +8, range 30/120 ft. _Hit:_ 7 1d6 + 4 Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Cold damage.

_**Chilling Gaze.** Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one creature the yeti can see within 30 feet. _Failure:_ 5 (2d4) Cold damage, and the target has the [Paralyzed](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ParalyzedCondition) condition until the start of the yeti’s next turn unless the target has Immunity to Cold damage. _Success:_ The target is immune to the Chilling Gaze of all yetis (but not [abominable yetis](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194868-abominable-yeti)) for 1 hour.

_**Echoing Howl**_. *Wisdom Saving Throw*: DC 13. Any one creature within 60 feet perceives a echoing duplicate of the yeti. This yeti attacks on Initiative Count 10. It can only attack the creature that can perceive it. It has AC 19 but only a single HP. It is immune to all damage except Slashing, Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Force. 

It may make one of the following attacks:
* *Psychic Rend*. _Attack Roll_: +8, reach 30 ft. Hit 1d10 psychic damage plus target must make a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion.
* _Echoing Howl_. The echo creates another echo of itself. 

Bonus Action. Any of the echoes or the yeti. _Death Knell_. Any creature that has less than 50% of hit points must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be slowed for one turn (may take only a action or bonus action, speed halved) and take 3d6 psychic damage. On a success, half as much damage. Recharge 5-6.

Reaction. If the yeti is hit with an attack by someone who can see one of its echoes, it can dispel the echo and force the attacker to reroll the attack, possibly turning a hit to a miss.
