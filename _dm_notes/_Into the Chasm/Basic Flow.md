**Mellusa** (mel-LOO-sah)


The village is structured around three main places:

* The cliff face, split by a cascading and branching river, the **Il-Qamarin**
* The floating palace
* The north (icy) and west (warm) ponds

Time passes 1/4 slower, so it has been ~450 years since downfall
Days pass unpredictably. Long day where the sun doesn't move, rapidly sets, short night, long day where the sun doesn't move, etc. Sun sets about once a decade. Usually in the right place for the feywild.

Recent Events

The domain of Malqen has been damaged lately. In the last few months, a crack into the Plane of Magic has appeared. (TBD what caused it, that is episode 4, but it is in the chasm and related to the planar vortex nature of the chasm)

This "crack" is effectively a spillover of ley lines in some fashion. It is currently manifesting as five gashes in the walls of the shadowfold through which, variously, raw magic, the chasm, metal, and lightning, and ash are spilling. 

Metaphysics of Mellusa - the kestavo can reach into the place, but the people here are born severed from the plane of souls and cannot return to the land of the dead, and the ghosts of these people haunt the storms in the air. Births are rare although how they are achieved is left a mystery. The kestavo should seem faint to Justas although not so faint as to impact magic, like he has to push through ice to find them.

The village is about 500 people and there are basically three factions

* Malqen's loyalists, who have come to worship the west wind and believe that his strength is their survival
* Traditionalist elders who are deeply tied to the rebirth cycle and fearful of change, want to manage decline
* The wild and chaotic, who want to use the strange events to escape and reclaim their lost gods
