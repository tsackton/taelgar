Has Disadvantage on all saving throws against spells and vulnerability to Force, Fire, Lightning and Psychic damage.

**Uncanny Defense**. Has a +10 AC against melee attacks, and radiates an aura of cold that does 1d4+2 damage to anyone who ends their turn within 5' of the echo. Has a -2 penalty on AC against ranged attacks, and vulnerability to piercing damage. 

**Uncanny Resistance**. Has +10 AC against spell attacks, evasion, and 3 legendary resistances. Has vulnerability to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage and melee attacks of opportunity against it has Advantage.
