# Cleenseau Arc 2 Backstory

## Sources and anchors

- [[Cleenseau - Session 25]], [[Cleenseau - Session 26]], and [[Cleenseau - Session 28]] establish the recovery and destruction of Malach's divided phylactery and his apparent defeat.
- [[Malach Timeline]] contains provisional and sometimes competing versions of Malach's early life, education in Tollen, activities in the highlands and Sentinels, research into immortality and chalyte, and alliance with mind flayers.
- [[Campaign Arc Overview]] previously proposed an Enford murder investigation, research into Malach's history in Tollen and the highlands, and an underground confrontation with mind flayers.
- [[Cleenseau Brainstorming Plot Notes]] proposed the mind flayers' possession of a separate soul jar and their imprisonment of Malach in a chalyte construct.
- [[Moon]] and [[2025-04-29 - Moon as Prison of Banished Gods]] preserve provisional setting ideas about the moon as a prison and about an aberrant power attempting to break it.
- [[Ursk]] establishes the krev, rodnya bindings, and rasaka as distinctive magical rites involving collective magical power, binding, and severance.

## Current situation

Malach prepared a separate soul jar through an alliance with mind flayers. This contingency survived the destruction of his divided phylactery and his apparent death at the end of Arc 1.

The mind flayers subsequently betrayed him. They trapped Malach in a chalyte golem of their own creation rather than restoring him freely. Malach cannot release himself from this vessel.

A destructive mind flayer cabal now harvests the souls of magic users in Enford and the Western Marches. Feeding these souls to Malach increases the power contained within the golem. Without sufficient power, Malach remains dangerous but cannot be used for the cabal's intended attack on the moon.

## Hidden truth

The moon is a prison containing dangerous beings. Its precise nature, the identities of all its prisoners, and whether the moon is itself jailer, prisoner, or both can remain obscure.

The mind flayers bound Malach into the chalyte golem using some of the same underlying magic by which the moon contains its prisoners. The golem is therefore a sympathetic model of the moon's prison:

- Malach corresponds to a lunar prisoner;
- the chalyte body corresponds to the lunar prison;
- the binding creates the magical resemblance between them; and
- the harvested souls strengthen Malach and the sympathetic connection.

Once sufficiently empowered, the golem becomes a sympathetic key. The cabal can act upon the smaller prison to break the greater one, releasing what is confined within the moon and causing catastrophic chaos.

## Forces and motives

### The mind flayer cabal

The cabal wants to break the moon and release its prisoners. Its wider motive is destructive: it seeks the destruction of the world, or at least catastrophic and mindless chaos. Its exact beliefs, internal factions, and understanding of the moon remain open.

### Malach

Malach is both the cabal's prisoner and its weapon. He does not control the cabal and cannot free himself, but he remains an independent danger. If left beneath the Sentinels after the cabal's defeat, he may attract and influence creatures in the depths until some future agent releases him.

His ability to resist, manipulate, or bargain with the cabal during the arc remains undecided.

### The PCs

The PCs first encounter the threat through the deaths of magic users. Their opening victory can disrupt the soul supply, but permanent resolution requires understanding Malach's past and constructing a ritual capable of unmaking the sympathetic key.

## Setting rules and constraints

- The Arc 1 destruction of Malach's phylactery fragments was real. His survival depends on the separate soul jar prepared with the mind flayers.
- Malach cannot free himself from the chalyte vessel.
- The golem is effectively invulnerable until the special binding is undone.
- The cabal needs to feed Malach enough souls to make the sympathetic key capable of breaking the moon.
- Defeating the cabal or unmaking Malach is individually sufficient to prevent the immediate lunar catastrophe.
- Complete victory requires both threats to be resolved.
- The moon's exact metaphysical nature should remain no more definite than the arc requires.

## The unmaking ritual

The working ritual logic has three parts:

1. **Identify the prisoner.** A truth recovered in the highlands—provisionally Malach's true name or true origin—allows the ritual to distinguish Malach from the chalyte vessel and the wider sympathetic system.
2. **Sever the binding.** Knowledge or a ritual component from Ursk provides the means to reverse the bond between Malach's soul and the chalyte golem.
3. **Break the correspondence safely.** A third body of knowledge allows the PCs to sever the golem's sympathetic relationship with the moon without damaging the moon's prison. Its source remains undecided; current possibilities place it among the [[Stoneborn]] or [[Dwarven Kingdoms]] of the Sentinels, in [[Duchy of Maseau|Maseau]] or the [[Refounded Alliance of Aurbez|Aurbez]] region, in the [[Plaguelands]], or in the devastated lands south of [[Tyrwingha]].

This logic is provisional. It should be refined through the desired adventures and through decisions about Malach's unique path to lichhood.

The ritual's final effect is unresolved. It may remove Malach from the golem while leaving a vulnerable lich that must be killed, or it may unbind and unmake his lichhood completely.

## Historical connections to develop

### Tharwa Fëa

[[Tharwa Fea|Tharwa Fëa]] was an early research laboratory and scholarly community connected to Malach. The party has already recovered a phylactery fragment there, and the surviving trapped souls and corrupted ritual spaces provide an obvious reason to return. Its records or magical remains should help establish the three requirements of the unmaking ritual.

### Tollen

Tollen is the other principal entry point into Malach's history and research. The precise history of his education, patrons, forbidden work, and departure remains to be settled. Research or adventure there should help reveal the same three ritual requirements, with strong success at Tollen and Tharwa Fëa making the full quest legible.

### The highlands

Malach's true identity or origin is hidden in the highlands. The expedition should culminate at the temple of a dead kestavo. The identity of that kestavo, its relationship to Malach, and the meaning of a dead local god remain open.

### Ursk

Urskan binding and severing rites illuminate the magic used to join Malach to the chalyte vessel. It remains undecided whether Malach or the mind flayers directly drew upon Urskan practice or whether Ursk independently developed knowledge that the PCs can adapt.

### Third ritual destination

The third destination should provide the knowledge needed to break the golem's sympathetic connection to the moon without harming the lunar prison. Candidate regions are the Stoneborn or dwarven realms within the Sentinels, Maseau or Aurbez, the Plaguelands, and the broader devastation south of Tyrwingha. A new Green Sea location is outside the intended scope of this arc.

### Chardon and Drankor

Chardon may appear for one or two sessions as an additional research route or optional destination. Drankor is another possible but currently less likely destination. Neither is required by the adopted structure.
