#### Interlude: Elaine II visits town

Level 7. Elaine II visits town on a royal procession towards the western marches. Mind flayers attempt to assassinate her, partly through proxies. Party hopefully stops, if not, someone else does.

This is why the fey have brought Robin and Celyn here. A big deal.
