Black Horns
============
The black horns belonged to a wayfinder who led a small group of refugees away from the fall of the Gossamer Veil, then drew pursuit away and was killed by Ianto. The horns still remember the unfinished route, and if followed carefully they lead the party to one lost survivor who slowly reveals the connection to Aelirios, Greymalkin, and the Lantern.

That gives you exactly the shape you want: **trophy first, mystery second, backstory revelation last**.


Unicorn
==========

The unicorn belongs in Twilight’s Edge because its story is about **living after irreparable harm**. It survived Ianto, lost the horn, and has made some new place for itself in a realm already defined by thresholds, fading identities, and things no longer wholly what they were.

The question is not merely whether the party can restore the horn. It is whether restoration would heal the unicorn, return it to an old role it has rejected, or erase the person it became after surviving.
## Morlaith’s Realm → **The Stag and Wood-Creature**

These two were fleeing Morlaith when Ianto killed and fused them. The familiar formerly bonded to the wood-creature provides the emotional and investigative link, but only fragmented memories remain.

The central questions are:

- What did they discover?
- Why did they flee together?
- Were they escaping Morlaith herself, one of her servants, or something growing within her realm?
- Did they leave something or someone behind?

This is the trophy most naturally suited to an investigation because the party already knows the victims are dead. Returning the remains means completing their unfinished escape or carrying their warning back into the place they fled.

## Militarized Guardian Realm → **The Tail Feather**

The feather belonged to a phoenix-like guardian associated with this threatened border realm. Its loss extinguished some living defense, perhaps a beacon, aerial warden, warning system, or source of courage that held the realm together during attacks.

Returning it should not simply repair a fortress. It might:

- rekindle a guardian spirit
- hatch or awaken a successor
- restore communication between scattered strongholds
- allow the realm to choose whether to resume an old defensive order

This gives the militarized realm a problem about **defense without reducing it to masonry**.
