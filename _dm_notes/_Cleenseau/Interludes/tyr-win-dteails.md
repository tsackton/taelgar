Yes. We discussed **Tafolwern** as the most obvious Tyrwinghan destination for the party if they leave Twilight’s Grace but do not want to go directly home.

## Tafolwern itself

- Capital of Tyrwingha, population roughly **35,000**    
- Culturally dominant within Tyrwingha, roughly “Paris-like” in its influence
- Strong fey aesthetic without literally being a fey city
- Broad parks, river walks, towers, bridges, terraced gardens, and performance-oriented boulevards
- Decorative faux-Sylvan is fashionable, often beautiful but linguistically meaningless
- The city notes were intentionally incomplete so it could be developed when the campaign actually reached it

## Why the party might go there

We identified Tafolwern as the natural choice if they want:

- Tyrwingha without immediately confronting family or home villages
    
- access to the crown, scholars, temples, magical services, and archives
    
- help identifying the recovered trophies
    
- to report what happened with Ethlenn and Umbraeth
    
- to re-enter mortal society somewhere large enough to offer anonymity, though that anonymity would quickly fail
    

The key framing was:

> Tafolwern is homeland without literal home.

It lets Robin and Celyn return to Tyrwingha while postponing Abenfyrd and Clawyn.

## Likely social tone

We talked about the party arriving as **minor celebrities**, but with stories about them already distorted:

- inaccurate songs or puppet plays
    
- exaggerated accounts of their deaths and survival
    
- rumors about Greymalkin’s wings
    
- people treating them as authorities on Ethlenn
    
- officials wanting to know whether their new fey favor has political significance
    

The broader theme was:

> Can they be Tyrwinghan heroes without becoming possessions of Tyrwingha?

## Possible Tafolwern hooks

Three preliminary ideas came up:

### False Sylvan scandal

A monument or fashionable inscription accidentally resembles genuine magical writing, creating a problem nobody intended.

### Political questioning

Electors, royal officials, or court factions want answers about Ethlenn, Umbraeth, and what exactly the party accomplished.

### Trophy exhibition

The trophies are displayed or examined publicly, and one or more are recognized as the remains of specific beings rather than anonymous magical curiosities.

None of these had been fully developed.


## Arrival impression

The party emerging in Tafolwern would experience:

- cool garden air
    
- city bells and distant crowds
    
- a brighter and busier mortal sky
    
- terraces, towers, waterways, bridges, and formal landscaping
    
- a sharp return to schedules, crowds, officials, and ordinary mortal urgency
    

Because the Pool is formal and probably watched, arriving through it would quickly attract official attention.

The strongest unresolved material is therefore not the gate or general atmosphere. It is **what the party encounters during their first day in Tafolwern once everyone realizes who has arrived**.
