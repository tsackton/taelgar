### **Caelvar, Keeper of the Unlit Fires**

Caelvar rules from the westernmost tower, where the sun remains a narrow line of molten gold along the horizon. He believes a beacon lit too soon is an admission of fear, while one lit too late is an epitaph, and so his entire court is trained to recognize the exact moment when vigilance must become war.

### **Vaelora, the Warden of a Hundred Watchfires**

Vaelora is an ancient fey commander who treats the realm less as a kingdom than as an endless defensive campaign. She is disciplined, austere, and genuinely protective of those under her care, but centuries of siege have left her suspicious of beauty, softness, and any peace that has not yet proven itself.

### **Othrys, the Lord of the Last Muster**

Othrys appears as a tall fey knight in lacquered copper armor, followed everywhere by the faint sounds of distant horns and marching feet. He believes every creature in his realm has a place in its defense, and his deepest flaw is that he no longer remembers how to value anyone except by what they can endure.
