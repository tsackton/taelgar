Here are a few that I think fit the tone of Twilight's Grace. They aren't wild revelers or pranksters. They've been shaped by centuries in Ethlenn's realm.

## Satyrs

**Peregrin**  
A silver-haired satyr in a tailored green coat, carrying an elegantly carved walking stick. Knows every pleasant footpath in the kingdom and always recommends the scenic route.

**Mossfoot Renn**  
A broad, curly-horned satyr with rolled-up sleeves and permanently dirt-smudged hands. Tends vineyards, orchards, and gardens, convinced every plant appreciates polite conversation.

**Callis**  
A young satyr with polished amber horns and an exquisitely crafted flute tucked into his belt. He collects dances from every culture that finds refuge in Twilight's Grace.

**Aster**  
An elderly satyr whose horns are worn smooth with age. He spends evenings beneath shade trees telling stories to children, always insisting someone else tell the ending.

---

## Pixies, Sprites & Little Fey

**Lark**  
A tiny blue-winged pixie carrying a bundle of handwritten invitations tied with silk ribbon. She knows every gathering happening that evening.

**Bramblecap**  
A moss-cloaked brownie no taller than a rabbit, constantly repairing loose hinges, cracked stonework, and squeaky gates before anyone notices.

**Nettle**  
A dragonfly-winged sprite dressed in a tiny crimson doublet. An enthusiastic gossip who somehow knows every engagement, reconciliation, and dinner invitation before anyone else.

**Pennyglow**  
A firefly-like faerie with golden hair and luminous wings. She quietly places candles, lanterns, and flowers wherever she thinks an evening gathering might become prettier.

**Tansy**  
A little flower faerie whose gown changes with the blossoms she's tending. She speaks to everyone as though they've been friends for years.

**Cob**  
A round-faced hob with oversized spectacles and an enormous ring of tiny brass keys. Officially he "looks after public benches," though no one knows exactly what that entails.

I also have one broader thought. I think **Ethlenn's little folk should be "household fey" rather than "forest fey."** Instead of playing tricks or guarding mushrooms, they have become:

- Invitation carriers
    
- Garden keepers
    
- Librarians' assistants
    
- Bakers
    
- Lamp lighters
    
- Tailors
    
- Locksmiths
    
- Fountain tenders
    
- Conservatory gardeners
    
- Bridge custodians
    

They still have all the charm of classic pixies and brownies, but they've become part of a refined civilization. That feels very much like the culture you've been building: the wild has been welcomed, not tamed.
