The **Evening Promenade** is an old and discreet crossing between [[Twilight's Grace]] and mortal [[Tyrwingha]]. It is less formal than the [[Twilight's Pool]], and was traditionally used by courtiers, favored guests, private envoys, and others whose arrival was meant to be received gracefully rather than publicly announced. Travelers rarely find it by accident, although many pass close to its mortal entrance without realizing what it is.

## In Twilight's Grace

On the Feywild side, the Promenade is a broad pale avenue winding through low hills beneath the perpetual dusk of [[Twilight's Grace]]. The sun has already set, and the sky is mostly the deep blue of a clear summer night, though the last faint hues of rose, violet, and amber still linger along the western horizon. The first stars shine overhead, bright and newly visible, while others seem to emerge whenever one looks away.

Silver-leaved trees stand at elegant intervals beside the road, interspersed with terraces, flower gardens, stone benches, and overlooks from which distant towers and illuminated pavilions can sometimes be seen. Slender black lamp posts line the Promenade, each holding a tiny captured star within a globe of silvered glass. They cast no steady pools of light, but twinkle gently, sometimes in answer to the stars above or to other lamps farther along the road.

Travelers are seldom entirely alone upon the Promenade. Courtiers, servants, musicians, masked strangers, and other finely dressed figures may be encountered walking beneath the trees, lingering at overlooks, or seated at small tables beside the road. Some offer wine, fruit, conversation, or quiet advice; others merely exchange a greeting and pass on. These encounters are rarely acknowledged as tests, though visitors are observed closely, and the length and ease of the journey often seem to depend upon the grace with which they conduct themselves.

The Promenade does not end at a visible gate. Instead, the silver gradually fades from the leaves, the distant music falls away, and the lingering colors upon the western horizon begin to dim. After one final bend, travelers emerge upon the Old Lethren Road in [[Tyrwingha]].

## In Tyrwingha

A day’s walk east of [[Caerenad]], the [[Tyrwinghan Road]] passes the remains of an old noble wayhouse called **Lethren Court**. Once used by noble households traveling between [[Eskbridge]] and [[Tafolwern]], it is now little more than a weathered gatehouse, a roofless hall, and a walled orchard whose trees still bear small, dark fruit. Beyond the ruins, a broad side-road turns between two worn stone pillars and follows a low wooded ridge away from the main road.

Locals call it the **Old Lethren Road** and say it once led to a hunting lodge or country estate that has long since vanished. Most travelers who follow it find only an unusually graceful but overgrown avenue which, after several miles, bends back toward the Tyrwinghan Road. No surviving map shows that it ever led anywhere else.

At summer dusk, however, those expected in [[Twilight's Grace]] may notice that the road continues beyond its final turning. The leaves ahead gleam faintly silver beneath their darker surfaces, the sounds of the main road fade, and distant points of light twinkle between the trees. At first they resemble newly risen stars, until the traveler realizes that they hang within slender black lamp posts beside the road.

There is no clear moment of crossing. Those who continue simply discover that the last colors of sunset do not fade, more stars are appearing overhead, and the Old Lethren Road has become the Evening Promenade.
