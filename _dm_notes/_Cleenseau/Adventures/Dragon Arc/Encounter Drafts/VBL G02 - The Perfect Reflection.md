# G02 — The Perfect Reflection

%% Optional guest suite. Choose A or B; B's curse is disclosed before attunement. %%

## Read aloud — A: A Flattering Mirror

> The mirror makes the whole room look finer. Its reflection of you is rested, unscarred, perfectly dressed.
>
> A small lens rests in the frame. As you reach for it, your reflection reaches first.

## Read aloud — B: The Portrait That Improves Its Guest

> An unfinished portrait almost has your face. A brush moves by itself, correcting the expression. A silver brooch like an open eye is fastened to the frame.

## Room

- **20 by 30 feet.** Open door at near end; mirror or portrait at far end; low dressing screen.
- Exits remain open.

## A — Turn the mirror

- Frame's back shows a plain face beside a polished one. Rotate the pivot to the plain face: **action**, no roll; lens releases.
- Attend to an accurate, unflattering detail: glamour stills **1 minute**, revealing the pivot.
- Covering, turning, a suitable trick, or **Dispel Magic (3rd-level effect)** also works.

### Taking an active lens

- Reflected hand reaches out first: warn and allow safe withdrawal.
- Continue: action, **DC 14 Wis**. Success releases the lens. Failure: **7 (2d6) psychic**; lens remains, handler **restrained within 5 feet** of intact mirror.
- Ally's action frees them; victim may repeat the save with an action. Covering, turning, or breaking mirror ends restraint. No recurring damage.
- **Frame:** AC 13, HP 15; immune poison/psychic. Breaking it leaves lens intact.
- Smashing while adjacent gives one warned **DC 14 Wis** save: **7 psychic** on failure, none on success; broken mirror cannot restrain. Smash from beyond 5 feet or cover/still it first to avoid this.

### Treasure — Lens of Inconvenient Clarity

- No attunement or curse; **once per dawn**.
- Action to look through it for **1 minute**: see invisible creatures/objects within **30 feet** that would otherwise be in line of sight.
- Does not see through walls, darkness, or all illusions. Always shows the user's ordinary appearance.

## B — Finish the portrait

- Sitting is voluntary; portrait flatters the sitter.
- Attend to the plain study on its back: brush stills. A helper can remove the brooch with an action.
- Turning it to the wall or **Dispel Magic (3rd-level effect)** also permits safe removal.
- Removing or carrying the brooch does not curse anyone.

### Treasure — Brooch of the Admired Guest

- **Attunement; 3 charges, all return at dawn.**
- While wearing it, before a Persuasion check in conversation, spend a charge to add **1d4**. No mind control.
- **Disclose before attunement:** closed-eye motif pricks its painted tongue; wearing it makes the voice ingratiating. Explain the mechanical curse. Identify reveals it; **DC 14 Arcana** can identify it by study, but failure does not hide the warning.
- **Curse:** while attuned, worn or not, voice remains ingratiating and spoken Intimidation checks have disadvantage. Speech and choices remain free. Cannot end attunement normally.
- **Remove Curse:** ends attunement and its restriction; does not permanently cleanse the item.
- **Destroy:** AC 15, HP 10, immune poison/psychic; ends curse.
- **Permanent cure:** 1 uninterrupted hour restoring the plain study with chalk, charcoal, or paint while brooch is present; no proficiency required. Keeps the benefit. Study is on a removable **2-foot-square panel** whose magic works outside the room.
- Optional gentler version: retain the altered voice and omit the curse.
