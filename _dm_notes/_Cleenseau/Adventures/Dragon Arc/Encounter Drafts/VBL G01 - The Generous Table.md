# G01 — The Generous Table

%% Optional guest suite. Choose A or B and its treasure. %%

## Read aloud — A: Excessive Hospitality

> Four chairs surround a table laid with a cloth embroidered with tiny balconies and stairs. A pear squeezes up through a full platter, then another. A little bell rings.

## Read aloud — B: The Feast of Borrowed Senses

> Six little dishes hold stone, coiled copper, and black salt. Their labels read “Summer Rain,” “First Applause,” and “An Enemy's Defeat.” A cup waits beside them. None of it is food.

## Room

- **25-foot square.** Door stays open. Service alcove may connect to another guest suite.
- Optional exploration and treasure; no mandatory combat.

## A — Still the table

- Food is fresh and wholesome. Uneaten food carried more than **20 feet** away becomes ash; eaten food remains nourishing. No portable ration supply.
- Alcove contains a bowl engraved with an empty table. Place it at a setting and attend to the finished meal: **action**, stills that setting for **1 minute**, no check.
- Other clear demonstrations of a completed meal, such as clearing or turning a plate, work. **DC 14 Arcana or Investigation** identifies the method early.
- **Two settings stilled together:** whole table pauses. An action folds and removes the cloth safely.

### Chairs

- Leaving with a full plate or lifting the cloth while active makes chair arms begin closing. Show this warning; withdrawing is safe.
- Continuing: **grappled, escape DC 13**. Ally's action releases a latch. No damage; no renewed grab while moving away.
- **Chair:** AC 13, HP 10; immune poison/psychic.
- Smashing the table stops it but tears the cloth; **1 hour of sewing** repairs it.

### Treasure — Cloth of the Offered Seat

- No attunement or curse; **once per dawn**.
- Action to unfold: create a stationary, horizontal **5-foot-square platform**, visible within **30 feet**, lasting **10 minutes**, supporting **500 pounds**.
- Cannot intersect creatures or objects, move, or crush. Cloth remains in the user's hands; action to fold ends the platform.
- Border fades **one round before expiry**; unsupported creatures fall normally when it ends.

## B — Taste and clear

> “First taste. Then understand what was done to it.”

- Willing sampling conveys sensations; swallowing is unnecessary. No stolen memories or trapped souls.
- First sample is exhilarating. Sampling again before clearing the cup: **DC 13 Con**; failure gives disadvantage on the next taste/smell Perception check within **1 minute**, then ends.
- Washing the cup with water or attending to its possibility as an empty cup clears the effect immediately.
- Cut-and-rejoined copper and cracked stone reveal materials damaged and reshaped into luxuries.
- Destroying the dishes makes their contents ordinary and safe. Sensations fade when carried out. No chair trap in B.

### Treasure — Cup of the Distant Taste

- Works outside after being washed once. No attunement or curse.
- **3 charges; regain 1d3 at dawn.**
- Action and one charge: sample a visible substance within **30 feet**. Learn its actual present taste and whether it is poisonous to the user, without suffering its effects.
- Does not identify spells, curses, safe doses, all ingredients, concealed substances, or future transformations.
