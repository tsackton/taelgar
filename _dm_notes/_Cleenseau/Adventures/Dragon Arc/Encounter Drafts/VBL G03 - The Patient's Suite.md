# G03 — The Patient's Suite

%% Optional guest suite. Choose A or B. The former patient is gone; this room introduces no new captive. %%

## Read aloud — A: The Cure in the Cabinet

> Six silver feet tap inside a glass cabinet. Tiny crutches lean beside it. A painted dragon watches a limping guest kneel.
>
> An empty seventh outline waits in the middle.

## Read aloud — B: Pain Kept in Crystal

> A clear crystal rests in a padded frame. Something dark contracts within it when the light changes. The bed is spotless.
>
> The chair beside it bears the deep impression of a gripping hand.

## Room

- **30-foot square:** bed, cabinet, stool.
- Second open passage leads to the antechamber or another suite.
- No initiative unless needed. Exits stay open.

## A — Complete the recovery

- Mural shows a cruel cure: the leg repeatedly altered for relief.
- Inspect the seventh outline to find a clear bottle; no check.
- Put the stool before the cabinet and attend to a completed recovery: **action**, no roll. Feet still; bottle becomes obvious.
- Covering the feet, using tools, or **Dispel Magic (3rd-level effect)** also works.

### Grasping feet

- Feet tap toward an approaching wrist: warn and allow safe withdrawal.
- Continue: **DC 14 Dex** or grappled. Action, **DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics** to escape; ally's action turning the soles upward ends the grip.
- No attacks or damage.
- **Glass:** AC 12, HP 5. Breaking it grants access but does not stop the feet.
- **Wooden base:** AC 13, HP 12. Breaking it stops them.
- Both immune poison/psychic.

### Treasure — Three Draughts of Sure Footing

- Action to drink one dose; lasts **10 minutes**.
- Advantage on checks/saves against being physically moved against the drinker's will or knocked prone; does not protect against banishment.
- Climb speed equal to walking speed on ordinary stone; no ceiling movement or support on nonsolid matter.
- Three doses total; no stacking, refilling, or curse.

## B — Handle with care

- Crystal contains stored pain, not a person. Three clips hold it in its padded frame.
- Touching it gives a harmless warning sting. Lifting it barehanded deals **7 (2d6) psychic once**; holder can put it down immediately.
- Gloves, cloth, tongs, or Mage Hand handle it safely.
- Close the frame's shutters while attending to the clear outer shell: calms it for **1 minute**. **DC 14 Arcana** identifies this early; optional.

### Treasure — Vessel of the Lesser Hurt

- Padded carrying frame included; requires **one hand**. No attunement.
- **3 charges; never recharge.**
- Reaction **after damage is rolled, before HP loss, concentration, or death checks**: spend one charge to reduce one damage instance by **2d8**, minimum 0, for self or an ally within **30 feet and line of sight**.
- After last charge: inert quartz worth **25 gp**.
- **Crystal:** AC 13, HP 8; immune poison/psychic. Hairline cracks warn of breakage.
- Breaking it while charged destroys the item and releases one burst: creatures within **10 feet**, **DC 14 Wis**, **7 (2d6) psychic**, half on success.
