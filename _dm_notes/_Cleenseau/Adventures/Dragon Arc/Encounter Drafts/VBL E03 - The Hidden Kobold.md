# E03 — The Hidden Kobold

Proposal; two alternate predators for an optional rescue discovered through [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]]. Shared assumptions: [[04 Encounter Packet - Vraedhul's Broken Lair]]. Neither kobold identity nor species names below are adopted setting lore.

## Shared situation and stakes

A lone kobold has left the habitual trail to hide. It knows a route toward Krauzeth's old approach and has recently seen watchers and a signaling arrangement. It does not know the full eruption story. It is not automatically a friend, betrayer, former beast keeper, or existing named NPC.

The kobold is a Small humanoid, Intelligence 10, and has AC 12, HP 9, speed 30 feet, STR −1, DEX +2, CON +0, INT +0, WIS +1, CHA +0, initiative +2, and ordinary mortal vulnerability; use death saves if reduced to 0. The predator searches for it rather than starting with it already in its jaws. Draw a refuge 20 feet across, the party's approach 30 feet away, two rocks providing cover, and an escape gully 30 feet from the refuge. Good scouting puts the predator another 40 feet from the refuge and permits a plan before initiative. Poor scouting puts it at the refuge, taking its first probing action. **Do not apply an unseen extra round of progress.**

Initiative begins only when someone intervenes or when the party watches the probing unfold in real time. Explain what each stage means. The creature needs **two separate actions** to open the refuge, then a later action to seize the kobold, then another later action to begin feeding. None of these actions also makes its normal attacks. From the poor-scouting start, the first probing action is still to come in initiative, so the party has at least three enemy turns before a seizure. An action that braces the shelter with adequate timber, stone, or a DC 14 Athletics check removes one opening step; it cannot reduce progress below zero. While a PC blocks its access, it fights or attempts to go around instead of advancing the search.

The PCs' objective is **extract the kobold and get it to safety**. Its ankle is trapped under a small loose stone inside the refuge; an adjacent character can use an action to shift that stone, coax it out, and guide it through a PC-sized gap. No Persuasion check is required for a clear rescue. It moves with the rescuer on its next turn. Both predator flavors abandon this hunt once the rescued kobold reaches the narrow broken gully and sight is blocked: too many stone crevices offer easier cover than they can search. State that accessible escape route when the party assesses the scene. Remaining PCs can cover the retreat. Picking it up uses ordinary carrying capacity and movement. Distraction, food, control, flight, and moving the refuge are legitimate solutions.

After two probing turns, seizure can occur on the third. An exposed undefended kobold is seized automatically with that dedicated action; a PC blocking access prevents it. If seized, the kobold is grappled, not instantly swallowed. A PC can free it with an action and a DC 14 Athletics check, or by breaking the grapple normally. Feeding on a later turn reduces it to 0 HP and releases it; the creature must spend another action later to harm it again. Healing or extraction can still matter. No attack targets the kobold while the creature is responding to adjacent active attackers.

**Conversation afterward:** "Don't go over the open stones. They watch those. The little path goes under the split ledge. If they send a warning, they'll shut the way behind them." If using the lens watchpost, it saw flashes; if using the runner version, it saw a runner's bell-cord. Do not give it both incompatible observations. A short description or sketch locates the third approach. If it dies or refuses to accompany them, the original tracks still reach that route. It seeks shelter away from the lair rather than automatically becoming a permanent follower.

## A — The mouth beneath the slab

> A small trail disappears under a tilted slab. Something pale has flattened itself against the stone above it. Its belly draws inward; the rock underneath makes a soft, sucking noise. A clawed hand pulls farther back into the dark gap below.

The predator is a broad, low animal with a mouth that slides around its underside. It is using that mouth to peel the slab upward, sniffing through the widening gap. Its form changes, but its location remains clear.

**Slab-mouth:** Large monstrosity (proposed game type), Intelligence 4; AC 15, HP 98; speed 30 feet, climb 20; STR +4, DEX +2, CON +3, INT −3, WIS +2, CHA −2; initiative +2. No resistances. Normal action: two lashes, +7 to hit, reach 10 feet, **11 (2d6+4)** bludgeoning each. On one hit per turn it may grapple the target instead of dealing damage; escape DC 14. It can hold only one creature, and carrying the kobold occupies that capacity. The search/seizure/feeding sequence replaces its normal action.

It attacks a PC that gets between it and the refuge. A thrown ration or carcass gives it something else to sniff: one action by a PC reliably draws it 15 feet toward the food on its next turn, once per encounter. That costs its probing action. It flees at 25 HP if not cornered, leaving anything it holds.

**Balance:** deliberately below a full entrance fight. Four PCs can kill it quickly; a straightforward rescue should be easier still. The difficulty is separating it from the shelter without leaving the kobold exposed. No surprise attack or damage aura is added. Strong fresh level 10 option: HP 118, unchanged attacks and timing.

## B — Hunting ribbons

> The cleft is scarcely wide enough for a hand. Above it, two strips of something translucent flutter without wind. One slides into the crack. Inside, a little voice makes itself stop breathing.

Use **two ribbon hunters**. With poor scouting, one is at the refuge and one 15 feet above it; with good scouting, both are still at least 40 feet away and approaching. Adjust the read-aloud accordingly. They are pieces of separate predators, not an arbitrary splitting swarm. Each is a Small monstrosity, Intelligence 4, and has AC 14, HP 42; speed 20 feet, fly 30 feet (hover); STR +1, DEX +4, CON +2, INT −3, WIS +2, CHA −2; initiative +4; no resistances. Action: one cut, +6 to hit, reach 10 feet, **13 (2d8+4)** slashing. Instead of cutting, one ribbon can grapple a Small or Medium creature at +6 versus AC, escape DC 14; no damage on the grapple. Its flying speed is halved while carrying someone.

Only **one hunter can advance any opening, seizure, or feeding stage per round**, even after the kobold leaves the narrow refuge. They share the two-step opening progress, but a second hunter cannot accelerate any stage. The outside hunter threatens rescuers; the probing hunter chooses between work and defending itself. A seized kobold is pulled toward the gully's opposite end rather than lifted beyond recovery. Use the shared delayed feeding rule. Both flee once one dies and the survivor is below half HP.

This flavor gives two enemies and an airborne interception problem. Keep total HP at 84; do not also add the slab-mouth. A web, forced movement, or similar control that separates hunters may solve the encounter. If the PCs rescue from a distance without exposing themselves, let that be a clean win. Exit to the party's chosen entrance, usually [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]].
