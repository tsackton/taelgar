# E03 — The Hidden Kobold

**Choose A or B. Goal:** extract the kobold and reach cover. **Entry:** [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]].

%% Kobold identity and allegiance remain open; this is not an assigned existing NPC. %%

## A — The mouth beneath the slab

> A small trail disappears beneath a tilted slab. Something pale has flattened itself against the stone above it. Its belly draws inward; the rock makes a sucking noise. A clawed hand retreats into the dark gap below.

### Slab-mouth

**Large monstrosity · AC 15 · HP 98 · Initiative +2**

**Speed** 30 ft; climb 20 ft. **Intelligence 4.** No resistances.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +4 | +2 | +3 | −3 | +2 | −2 |

- **Lashes:** two attacks, **+7**, reach **10 ft**, **11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning** each.
- Once per turn, replace one hit's damage with a grapple, **escape DC 14**. Holds one creature maximum.
- **Food lure:** a PC action throws food; once per encounter, draws it **15 ft** toward the food next turn and costs its probing action.
- **Retreat:** at **25 HP**, flees if not cornered and drops its captive.
- Stronger option: **HP 118**.

## B — Hunting ribbons

> Two translucent strips flutter above a narrow cleft without wind. One slides into the crack. Inside, a little voice makes itself stop breathing.

### Ribbon hunters ×2

**Small monstrosities · AC 14 · HP 42 each · Initiative +4**

**Speed** 20 ft; fly 30 ft (hover). **Intelligence 4.** No resistances.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +1 | +4 | +2 | −3 | +2 | −2 |

- **Cut:** one attack, **+6**, reach **10 ft**, **13 (2d8+4) slashing**.
- **Grapple instead:** **+6 vs AC**, Small/Medium target, no damage, **escape DC 14**. Flying speed halved while carrying.
- **Only one hunter advances any rescue-threat stage per round**, including after extraction.
- Poor scouting: one at refuge, one **15 ft above**. Good scouting: both at least **40 ft away**; adjust description.
- Carry a seized kobold toward the gully's opposite end, keeping interception possible.
- After one dies, the survivor flees below half HP.

## Setup and rescue

- Refuge **20 ft across**; party **30 ft away**; two cover rocks; escape gully **30 ft from refuge**.
- Good scouting allows preparation with predator **40 ft away**. Poor scouting starts adjacent, with **no completed probing actions**.
- Initiative starts on intervention or when watching the probing unfold.
- **Threat sequence:** probe → probe → seize → feed. Each requires a separate action **instead of attacks**.
- Blocking access prevents progress; predator fights or goes around.
- **Brace shelter:** action with suitable timber/stone, or **DC 14 Athletics**, removes one opening step, minimum 0.
- **Extract:** adjacent action shifts the stone trapping its ankle and guides it through the gap; no social check. Kobold follows on its next turn. Carry normally if needed.
- **Safe:** kobold reaches the gully and breaks sight; predators abandon the hunt.
- **Seizure:** dedicated action automatically grapples an exposed, undefended kobold. PC action + **DC 14 Athletics**, or normal grapple-breaking, frees it.
- **Feeding:** later action drops it to **0 HP** and releases it. Further harm requires another action. Use death saves; no attacks on it while responding to adjacent active attackers.
