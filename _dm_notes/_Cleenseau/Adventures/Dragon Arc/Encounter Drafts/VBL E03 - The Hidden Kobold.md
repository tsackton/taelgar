# E03 — The Hidden Kobold

**Goal:** defeat the Cindermaw and its brood, recover the kobold alive if possible, and discover where it came from. **Entry:** [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]].

%% Encounter revised 2026-10-25. The Cindermaw replaces both earlier predator variants. This is homebrew intended as a strong fight for four 10th-level PCs; it has no tested CR assignment. The kobold is a new, unnamed survivor from those sheltering in Krauzeth's old lair, not an assigned existing NPC. Exact scouting bands and victim HP states remain adjustable running mechanics. %%

## Why the kobold is fleeing

- **Why stay until now?** The survivors have shelter, supplies, familiar tunnels, and strength in numbers. Leaving means crossing the dangerous Plaguelands.
- **Why leave today?** It woke with its blanket sinking into the stone of its sleeping chamber. The others moved deeper into the mountain; it decided to go outward.
- **How was it caught?** It slipped out with food and its few possessions. The predator found it crossing exposed ground and drove it beneath a slab or into a cleft.
- **What does it want?** Survive, retrieve its bundle, and get beyond the river. It has no larger plan or ambitious destination.
- **The others:** they think staying together underground is safer; it thinks they are waiting too long. It left voluntarily and still cares about them.
- **After rescue:** it willingly explains how it left and warns about the watchpost. It can guide the party to an observation point, but returning underground frightens it. Directions repay their help; it does not volunteer for the expedition.

> “We kept moving the beds. Every time another room went wrong, we moved the beds. Last night I decided I was finished moving them.”

## Scouting determines the opening

Use the result from E02; keep the same Cindermaw statistics and two starting cinderlings for every result. Good scouting reveals the cinderlings and shapes growing inside the parent, allowing preparation.

| Result | Kobold | Predator and party |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Good** | **9 HP**, trapped in the refuge. | Predator at least **40 ft** from the refuge; party about **30 ft** away, with time to position and prepare. |
| **Middling** | **3 HP**, extracted, grappled, and struggling. | Predator beside the refuge, holding the kobold; party about **30 ft** away. Feeding is next unless interrupted. |
| **Bad** | **0 HP**, unconscious and dying, no failed death saves yet. | Predator has begun feeding beside the refuge; party about **30 ft** away. The kobold needs protection and healing immediately. |

- The bad result starts with a saveable, almost-dead kobold. Give everyone one turn before the predator can feed again: further feeding cannot occur before its **second turn** in initiative. The kobold makes death saves normally.
- The two starting cinderlings pick through rubble near the parent. On a good result they are well clear of the refuge; on middling or bad results they are near the feeding site.
- The parent holds the injured kobold in one forelimb. The cinderlings attack active opponents rather than feeding on the victim.

**Good:**

> A six-limbed creature of cracked black stone advances toward a tilted slab, followed by two smaller shapes picking through the rubble. A clawed hand retreats beneath the slab as something presses outward against the creature's glowing flank.

**Middling:**

> The creature crouches on six limbs of black stone, holding the struggling kobold beneath a furnace-bright mouth. Something moves inside its cracked flank; a glowing lump presses against the shell from within.

**Bad:**

> The creature lifts its head from a small body in the ash. One clawed hand closes weakly around the strap of a battered pack.

The movement in the bad-result description is a fading reflex; the kobold is unconscious when initiative begins.

## Cindermaw

A heavy elemental predator of basalt and molten stone. Smaller creatures grow beneath its shell and tear free as the plates crack apart.

**Huge elemental · AC 18 · HP 240 · Initiative +1**

**Speed** 35 ft; climb 25 ft. **Resistance:** fire. No other damage resistances or condition immunities.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +5 | +1 | +4 | −3 | +2 | −1 |

**Saves:** Strength +9, Constitution +8, Wisdom +6. **Perception:** +6. Unlisted saves use ability modifiers.

- **Shatter a Plate — twice per fight:** when it fails a saving throw, it can succeed instead, losing **20 HP** as part of its shell breaks away. This loss cannot be reduced.
- **Multiattack:** makes two attacks, choosing Crushing Limb or Slag Hurl for each.
- **Crushing Limb:** **+9**, reach **10 ft.**; **21 damage**: **14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning** plus **7 (2d6) fire**. It can replace the fire damage with a grapple, **escape DC 17**; holds one creature.
- **Slag Hurl:** **+9**, range **90/240 ft.**; **21 damage**: **14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning** plus **7 (2d6) fire**. Useful against flying or distant PCs.
- **Furnace Eruption — recharge 5–6:** replaces Multiattack. **30-foot cone**, **DC 17 Dexterity**, **28 (8d6) fire**, half on success. Its throat must visibly brighten at the end of its preceding turn; it cannot erupt without that warning. Starts charged but untelegraphed, so its first possible eruption is on its second turn. After use, roll recharge at the start of each turn; once ready, it can show the warning at turn end.

### Legendary actions

**Two uses**, refreshed at the start of its turn. Spend one immediately after another creature's turn to do either of the following; it cannot use these while incapacitated.

- **Move:** up to half its speed, provoking opportunity attacks normally.
- **Strike:** one Crushing Limb or Slag Hurl attack. **Only once between its own turns.**

### Shed the brood

> The cracks along its back widen, showing small shapes pressing against the stone from within. A glowing lump tears free, unfolds little hooked legs, and begins crawling toward you.

- Start with **two cinderlings**, picking through nearby rubble.
- At the **end of its turn**, the Cindermaw can shed **two more**, each appearing in an empty space within **10 ft**. It can do this **twice per fight**, with **four living cinderlings maximum**. If there is not room for both within that cap or on the battlefield, it waits without spending a use.
- Newly spawned cinderlings take their first turn immediately after the Cindermaw's **next** turn. The two starting cinderlings act after its first turn normally.
- The Cindermaw cannot spawn while incapacitated. Spawning does not use its action or a legendary action.
- When the Cindermaw dies, all remaining cinderlings crumble into inert slag. Maximum **six cinderlings across the fight**, including the starting pair.

### Cinderlings

**Small elementals · AC 14 · HP 18 each**

**Speed** 30 ft; climb 30 ft. **Resistance:** fire. No other special defenses.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +2 | +2 | +1 | −3 | +0 | −2 |

- **Claw:** **+6**, reach **5 ft.**; **9 (2d6+2) fire damage**. One attack per action.
- Run them together immediately after the parent, observing the delay for newborns. Saves use ability modifiers.
- Rush active opponents, climb onto cover, and harass whoever carries the kobold.
- **No damaging aura or death explosion.** Killing a cinderling beside the victim remains a useful rescue action.

### Running the fight

- The parent engages close threats, hurls slag at distant attackers, and uses a warned eruption when PCs cluster. It directs the cone away from the kobold it is trying to eat.
- The cinderlings pressure the rescuers; only the parent feeds on the kobold.
- Clearing cinderlings competes with damaging the parent enough to collapse the whole brood. Healing and extracting the kobold compete with both.
- Track **two plate breaks**, **two spawn uses**, living cinderlings, and whether the eruption is charged and has shown its warning.

## Setup and rescue

- Refuge **20 ft across**; party **30 ft away**; two cover rocks; escape gully **30 ft from refuge**.
- Use the scouting table for the victim's condition and the predator's progress; good scouting allows preparation, middling begins after seizure, and bad begins after feeding.
- Initiative starts on intervention or when watching the probing unfold.
- **Threat sequence:** probe → probe → seize → feed. Each requires a separate action **instead of attacks**.
- Blocking access prevents progress; predator fights or goes around.
- **Brace shelter:** action with suitable timber/stone, or **DC 14 Athletics**, removes one opening step, minimum 0.
- **Extract:** adjacent action shifts the stone trapping its ankle and guides it through the gap; no social check. Kobold follows on its next turn. Carry normally if needed.
- **Safe:** kobold reaches the gully and breaks sight; the parent abandons the meal to fight the PCs. Cinderlings continue to harass active opponents, including anyone still carrying it in the fight.
- **Seizure:** a dedicated action automatically grapples an exposed, undefended kobold. A PC's adjacent action with **DC 17 Athletics**, or normal grapple-breaking, frees it.
- **Feeding:** uses the parent's **entire action**, replacing Multiattack or Furnace Eruption. A first feeding action drops the kobold to **0 HP**; the parent keeps its grip. Further feeding requires a separate action, using its normal attack damage. Use death saves; once attacked at close quarters, it concentrates on the dangerous intruders. The bad-start response window above applies; feeding cannot be taken as a legendary action.
- **Recovery:** freeing the kobold does not itself heal or stabilize it. Healing, stabilization, and carrying it clear remain useful actions while others fight.
- **Death:** the body, its belongings, and the escape trail remain discoverable. The predator has not consumed all evidence; use the clues below.

### Kobold

**Small humanoid · AC 12 · HP 9 · Speed 30 ft · Initiative +2 · Intelligence 10**

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| −1 | +2 | +0 | +0 | +1 | +0 |

> “Don't go over the open stones. They watch those. The little path goes under the split ledge. If they send a warning, they'll shut the way.”

- Knows the **back entrance** and its alarm: **lens flashes OR runner's keyed bell**, matching E05. It can identify the usable route, explain who guards it, and warn that an alarm may prompt sealing the passage.
- Knows little about the eruption. Its certainty is that its home keeps changing; it does not supply physical confirmation of Vraedhul's death.
- Offers directions willingly after rescue. It can point out the route and guide them as far as cover overlooking the watchpost, then wants to continue toward the river.

## If the kobold dies

The loss costs conversation and a knowledgeable guide. Investigating the attack site still reveals a route to the back entrance.

- **Escape trail:** small footprints approach from the mountain; running steps and drag marks show where the attack began. Follow the outbound trail backward.
- **Dropped bundle:** bedding, provisions, and personal belongings lie farther along that trail. Someone was leaving an inhabited home.
- **Worked route:** following the trail reveals cut footholds, cleared rubble, and a narrow passage concealed by overlapping slabs. These lead toward the watchpost.
- Give these clues when the party examines the body and site, then follows the trail. They are the physical payoff of investigating this encounter, not an entrance identified during the initial panorama.
- Living testimony adds guard information, an alarm warning, and confidence about which route remains usable. Without it, the party can inspect the watchpost and learn its defenses there.
