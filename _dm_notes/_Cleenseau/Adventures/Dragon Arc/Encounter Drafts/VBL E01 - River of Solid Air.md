# E01 — River of Solid Air

%% Local dimensions and mechanics remain drafts. Pack statistics, detour distances, and the barriers' regional limits are open. An aerial current remains a proposal; flight has no blanket prohibition. %%

> Upstream, pale seams multiply until the valley seems filled with fractured glass. Downstream, torrents rise into the air, carrying stones before dropping them again. Between them, a narrow shelf crosses the river, interrupted by two clear walls.
>
> A black shape swims beneath a frozen ripple, then rises through empty air. Its plated brow strikes an invisible surface. A square of sky falls apart.

**Goal:** cross, take the upstream route, or withdraw safely. **Next:** [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]].

## Terrain and opening

- River **90 ft wide**; crossing shelf **15 ft wide**.
- Solid-air walls at **30 and 60 ft**: **5 ft thick**, roughly **1,000 ft high**, continuing beyond view.
- Safe pockets at **20 and 45 ft**: each **10 ft long**, widening the shelf to **35 ft**.
- Creature starts at **65 ft**. Demonstration charge moves it to **50 ft**, breaking **only the far wall**.
- Initiative: someone approaches within **60 ft** or deliberately disturbs it. Demonstration costs no PC a turn.
- Walls: each **10-ft-square section AC 15, HP 25**, immune poison/psychic. Destroying a section leaves a permanent gap.
- Inspection reveals local thickness and breakability without a roll. Object-damaging spells work normally; these are solid objects.
- Water beside the shelf flows normally. Flight, teleportation, ropes, and breaking walls remain useful.

Near bank **0** → pocket **20** → wall **30** → pocket **45** → wall **60** → creature **65** → far bank **90**.

## Folded current

**Large elemental · AC 16 · HP 126 · Initiative +1**

**Speed** 35 ft; swim 30 ft; fly 30 ft (hover). **Intelligence 4.** No resistances.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +5 | +1 | +4 | −3 | +1 | −2 |

- **Head strikes:** two attacks, **+7**, reach **5 ft**; **13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning** each.
- **Brace — end of turn:** stone plates lock around its forequarters. Mark a straight lane **10 ft wide, up to 50 ft long**; show endpoint and height. It may rise or descend. Once marked, it cannot change.
- **Charge — start of next turn:** replaces movement and action with up to **50 ft** along that lane. **DC 15 DEX**, **22 (4d10) bludgeoning**, half on success; failure also pushes **10 ft** along the lane, stopping at obstacles. One hit per creature.
- Charge breaks a **10-ft-wide gap** through each wall crossed; stops at a bank or unbreakable rock. Gaps remain.
- Unable to move: charge fails, with no replacement strikes. It may brace again at turn end.
- **Bait:** action making a clear splash within **60 ft**, before bracing, directs the next lane toward it if the route is straight. A thrown stone works. Ambiguous lure: **DC 14 Animal Handling or performance**.
- Every hostile charge gives a full round's warning. Later lures cannot redirect a committed charge.
- **Retreat:** at **30 HP**, withdraws into the river if unpursued. Does not pursue beyond the banks.
- **Optional tuning:** gentler **HP 110**, strikes **11 (2d6+4)**; stronger **HP 150**. Charge unchanged.

## Falling into the river

- **DC 13 DEX** to catch the shelf; failure leaves the PC swimming nearby.
- Climb back: **15 ft movement**. An ally's action and rope retrieve them without a check.
- End a turn in the current: **DC 13 Athletics** or drift **15 ft downstream** toward a gravel bank **40 ft away**.
- Recovery bank lies before the dangerous cataracts. No added cold damage.

## Upstream alternative

- Orenlas: **about 130 miles away**. Party on foot; may obtain horses; **one flying griffon**.
- Immediately upstream: thicker solid-air formations. Farther north, ordinary water offers another crossing.
- Beyond that crossing: **roaming chaos-elemental packs** occupy the northern approach to the lairs.
- Show packs or fresh trails before commitment. Griffon scouting, cover, and timing can reveal a route through.
- Avoidance can succeed; the party may return to the direct crossing. No automatic replacement fight.
