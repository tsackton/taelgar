# E01 — River of Solid Air

Proposal; choose A or B. Assumptions and review status: [[04 Encounter Packet - Vraedhul's Broken Lair]]. Both flavors serve the adopted opening: cross the altered river, potentially using a chaos creature to break a passage.

## Shared setup

**Objective:** get the party across. **End:** everyone reaches either bank safely, or the party chooses a detour. Fighting to the death is unnecessary.

Sketch a river 90 feet wide. A rough, solidified-current shelf provides a walkable 15-foot-wide crossing; two transparent vertical panels cut across it at 30 and 60 feet. Each panel is 40 feet wide, 60 feet high, and 5 feet thick. Water beside the shelf runs normally. At 20 and 45 feet, 10-foot-long rocky pockets widen the shelf to 35 feet, leaving room to step out of a charge's line. Place the creature at 65 feet. Give these dimensions when relevant; they are a playable local sketch, not adopted river canon.

Near bank (0) → safe pocket (20) → panel (30) → safe pocket (45) → panel (60) → creature (65) → far bank (90). All distances are feet along the crossing.

Ash strikes empty air and trickles down a straight edge. A spray of frozen water hangs above the current, with liquid still sliding around its base. The animal drags its forehead along a clear surface, leaving a milky scar. Its first demonstration is against a barrier, before anyone is attacked: **it can break this material**.

The panels are solid objects, not antimagic fields. Each 10-foot-square section has AC 15, HP 25, immunity to poison and psychic damage; reducing it to 0 opens a permanent gap. A suitable thunder effect that damages objects works normally; effects that cannot target objects still follow their actual spell text. A character examining a panel can identify its limits without rolling. Flying above or around the finite panels, teleporting, building a line, or taking a half-hour upstream detour works. The detour costs time, not an invented resource penalty. A bypass earns the next scene without a mandatory replacement fight.

## A — The glassback

> The river has stopped in the middle of a ripple. Beyond it, something broad and low scrapes its glass forehead against the air. The scrape makes a sound like a knife drawn around a wineglass. A sheet you could not see a moment ago is suddenly outlined in white.

The glassback reacts to hard vibration. Before initiative, it makes a short demonstration charge from 65 to 50 feet, breaking only the far panel. The near panel remains for the party to address. Begin initiative if someone approaches within 60 feet of it or deliberately disturbs it; no one loses a turn to the demonstration. It does not hunt fleeing PCs beyond the bank.

**Glassback:** Large elemental (proposed game type), Intelligence 4; AC 16, HP 126; speed 35 feet, swim 30; STR +5, DEX +1, CON +4, INT −3, WIS +1, CHA −2; initiative +1. No damage resistances. On a normal turn it makes two head strikes, +7 to hit, reach 5 feet, **13 (2d8+4)** bludgeoning each. At the end of its turn it may brace: describe its forequarters becoming black glass and mark a straight, 10-foot-wide lane up to 50 feet from it. The lane cannot change afterward. At the start of its next turn, if able to move, it uses its movement and action to charge along that lane instead of making head strikes. Creatures in it make DC 15 Dexterity saves: **22 (4d10)** bludgeoning, half on success; a failed save also pushes them 10 feet along the lane, stopping at solid obstacles. No creature is hit twice by the same charge. The charge opens a 10-foot-wide gap through every panel it crosses; it stops at a bank or unbreakable rock. Restrained, stunned, or otherwise unable to move: the charge fails, with no replacement head strikes that turn. It can brace again only at the end of its turn.

**Charge movement and baiting:** a charge grants up to 50 feet of special movement, replacing its ordinary movement and action. Before it braces, an action making a conspicuous impact within 60 feet draws its next lane toward that impact if there is a straight route. No check for a clear loud impact; DC 14 Animal Handling or suitable performance only if the lure is ambiguous. State the selected lane immediately. A later lure cannot change an already committed lane. A character can lure it while another prepares a safe pocket. Its first hostile brace, like all later ones, gives everyone a full round of warning before that charge resolves.

**Recovery:** a shoved creature entering water can catch the shelf with a DC 13 Dexterity save; failure leaves it swimming alongside, not carried instantly away. Climbing onto the shelf costs 15 feet of movement; an ally's action and rope remove the check. A swimmer who ends a turn in the current makes DC 13 Athletics or drifts 15 feet downstream toward a gravel bank 40 feet away. No extra cold damage is added during this short encounter. At 30 HP the glassback retreats upstream if unpursued. If killed or controlled early, the object-breaking and bypass options remain.

**Balance:** one creature against four PCs is vulnerable to focused damage and control. Its danger comes from a clearly announced line while PCs work on the crossing. Expect roughly 2–4 active rounds if they engage; no legendary resistance is added to preserve the trick. Strong fresh level 10 option: HP 150, unchanged damage and DC.

## B — The folded current

> A black shape swims beneath the frozen ripple. It reaches the edge of the water and keeps swimming, its body rising through empty air. For a moment the river seems to have folded upward with it. Then its plated brow strikes something invisible, and a square of sky falls apart.

Use the same map, HP, AC, attacks, and telegraphed charge. The creature resembles a river predator extruding plates of stone; it has a **30-foot flying speed and can hover**, but cannot pass through the panels. Its charge lane may rise or descend in a straight line; show both endpoint and height. It reacts to splashes rather than impacts. Throwing a stone into a chosen patch of water is a reliable lure, with the same action and timing as A.

For a slightly gentler opening, use **HP 110** and replace each head strike with **11 (2d6+4)** bludgeoning. Keep charge damage unchanged because its entire point is to reward observation and movement. There are no additional water spirits or automatic terrain attacks. The creature's last movement does not undo any gaps.

This flavor emphasizes water and air exchanging roles; A emphasizes a solid animal breaking impossible matter. Neither opens a full portal or grants unrestricted reality shaping. Both lead to [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]].
