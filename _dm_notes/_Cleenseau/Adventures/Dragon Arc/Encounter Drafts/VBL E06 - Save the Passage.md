# E06 — Save the Passage

%% Selected redesign, 2026-10-25: a chain-driven steel barricade replaces the counterweighted jaws and cast-stone seal. The machinery, retreat wicket, and eight-defender roster are adopted for this encounter. Numerical mechanics and statistics remain running drafts for four level 9 PCs, not tested CR assignments. Puller species and NPC identities remain open; no established named kobolds are reassigned. %%

**Goal:** get everyone through and secure access before the defenders seal the tunnel. **Entry:** [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]]. **Next:** underground kobold tunnels to [[VBL E08 - The Death View]]; no surface crossing.

## Read aloud — warned arrival

> A barred steel gate blocks the far doorway. Through it, two broad-backed figures haul on heavy chains while a kobold stands between three long levers. Gears clatter inside the walls.
>
> Two armed kobolds face you from the chamber; a third guards a little hinged door among the bars. Behind them, one kobold raises a copper-wound staff while another lifts a smoking ceramic bomb from a padded rack.
>
> The director points at the guards, then pulls a lever. A solid steel plate begins edging out of the right-hand wall.
>
> “Back through! Before the plate!”

**Unwarned:** all three assemblies are parked, the doorway is open, and the crew scrambles to its stations. Do not award progress before initiative. The warned read-aloud previews movement; resolve it using the round procedure below.

Show that the pullers supply the motion, the director selects the movement, and the plate will cover the guards' retreat door.

## Layout and positions

![[vbl-e06-kobold-barricade.png]]

*Machinery schematic, not a scale battle map. This approved diagram predates the expanded roster: add the third guard at the wicket, the spellcaster behind the grille to the director's left, and the bombardier to the right.*

- **Outer chamber:** 40 feet wide and 40 feet deep. Party enters opposite the barrier. Two guards begin halfway across; the third covers the wicket.
- **Doorway:** 10 feet wide and 12 feet high. Three closely spaced tracks sit within a thick stone surround. The upright grille slides from the left, armored plate from the right, and locking frame from the left. Their final positions form a layered barrier across the same doorway.
- **Rear room:** 20 feet deep. Director's lever platform is 10 feet beyond the grille; pulling stations flank it. The onward tunnel opens behind the director. Both ranged attackers begin on this side.
- **Side bays:** machinery pockets, not walking routes around the barrier. Chains and carriage racks are exposed where tracks enter the doorway. A switch can divert the plate into a maintenance siding.
- **Retreat:** guards use the grille's wicket. There is no separate bypass hatch.
- **Motive:** protect their refuge. A credible truce can stop the work; the defenders need not fight to the death.

## Alarm-dependent start

| Approach | Start |
| --- | --- |
| No warning | All assemblies parked; doorway open. Quiet arrivals may observe, position, or talk before revealing themselves. Otherwise normal initiative; no automatic surprise. |
| Warned; party comes promptly | Grille closed, wicket open, plate and frame parked. Roll initiative before further work. |
| Warned; party deliberately delays | Defenders can finish sealing. Explain this consequence before the party rests or leaves. |

Brief conversation or searching at the watchpost adds no progress beyond the warned start. Alarm or detected intrusion starts closure; the machine has not been running for months.

## Run the machinery

- Each puller spends its **action hauling**, contributing **one haul** to the engaged operation. One remaining puller works at half speed; ratchets preserve completed work.
- The director spends an **action** selecting, stopping, or reversing an assembly, or setting the plate's track switch. The last setting remains engaged until changed. Anyone reaching the controls can operate them; a guard or puller can replace the director.
- Announce intended movement at the start of the round through calls and visible machinery. Changing a setting still requires an action.
- Resolve hauling **at round's end**, after everyone has a response window. Record which operation each haul powered; never transfer work between assemblies. Completed work counts even if its puller subsequently falls. A jam or successful brace can prevent the pending movement.
- Each operation below needs **two hauls**. One haul leaves partial work held by the ratchet; the second completes it. Excess work does not automatically carry into the next operation. The plate's two advances use the same setting; selecting the frame requires another control action.
- While jammed or successfully held, no additional work accumulates against that assembly. Clearing the obstruction does not release deferred movement.

| Operation | Work | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close grille | 2 hauls | Grille spans doorway; wicket still opens. |
| Bring plate halfway across | 2 hauls | Half the doorway becomes solid steel. Wicket on the remaining side stays accessible. |
| Finish closing plate | 2 more hauls | Plate covers wicket, blocking passage and sight. |
| Advance and engage frame | 2 hauls | Frame clamps the assemblies into the doorway. |

With both pullers uninterrupted: **four rounds from open**, or **three from the warned start**. In the warned start, the wicket is available through round one, covered at the end of round two, and the frame locks at the end of round three. Interference, replacement operators, or waiting for guards can lengthen this.

Reversing requires hauling: undo operations in reverse order with the same work. Once locked, first reverse the frame to release its clamps, then reverse the plate. Control access does not instantly reopen the barrier.

## Wicket and moving steel

- **Wicket:** 2½ feet wide, 4 feet high. Small creatures pass normally; Medium creatures squeeze. Opening or closing uses an ordinary object interaction.
- It latches from the crew's side, but guards need it open for retreat. If latched, an adjacent **action, DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools**, forces or releases it.
- Wedging the wicket open prevents latching; it does **not** stop the separate plate from covering it.
- Attacks through the grille give **half cover** where bars interfere. The plate grants **total cover** across the portion closed. Apply actual spell targeting and sight requirements.
- If someone remains in the plate's path, allow withdrawal to either accessible side. Someone insisting on staying without stopping it makes **DC 15 Dexterity**: success reaches an accessible side; failure takes **14 (4d6) bludgeoning** and is pushed into the outer chamber. No instant death or burial.

## Interfering with the machine

These are examples, not an exhaustive solution list. Useful spells and sound physical plans work according to their actual effects.

| Action | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Jam exposed track or gear | Adjacent action, sturdy object, **DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools**. Stops that assembly until someone reaches the obstruction and spends an action clearing it. |
| Break pulling chain | Each **AC 18, HP 25**, immune poison/psychic. Breaking one disables its pulling station. Repair needs spare links and several minutes. |
| Use controls | Adjacent action; no check for an observed control. Select, stop, reverse, or set switch. Reversal needs hauling. |
| Divert plate | Set switch while plate is at its junction. Its next movement sends it into the siding. Reverse that movement to recover it before trying the doorway again. |
| Hold assembly | Action, secure footing or substantial brace, **DC 18 Athletics**. Prevent next movement while remaining there. Suitable magic or a sufficiently strong fixed obstruction can work without a roll. |
| Pry aside unlocked plate | Before frame locks: action with crowbar or equivalent leverage, **DC 18 Athletics**. Opens enough space to use wicket. Crew can haul it closed again. |

### Pullers and director

- **Puller:** two melee attacks, **+7**, reach 5 feet; **12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning** each. May replace either with a normal grapple or shove. Hauling uses its action instead.
- **Director:** one crossbow attack, **+5**, range 80/320; **6 (1d6 + 3) piercing**. Usually operates controls or clears interference.
- Pullers initially haul. A threatened puller can abandon its station to fight, grapple, or shove an intruder toward the doorway, slowing the machine.
- Director normally halts rather than strands a retreating guard. If a PC reaches the platform, it calls a puller away to help. If the director falls, another defender can take over; a puller doing so gives up hauling actions.

### Guards — fighting withdrawal

- **Hooked spear:** two attacks, **+6**, reach 5 feet; **7 (1d8 + 3) piercing** each. Once on its turn, a hit can pull the target 5 feet toward the guard instead of dealing damage.
- Two forward guards delay the approach; the wicket guard protects the opening, helps companions through, and prevents pursuit.
- At half-closed plate, forward guards disengage and retreat through the wicket; the wicket guard follows last. Retreat earlier if overwhelmed.
- PCs can follow, grapple a retreating guard, obstruct the wicket, or exploit the director's reluctance to close on its people. A desperate director may abandon someone outside; make that choice visible.

### Spellcaster — hinder the advance

Short iron staff threaded with copper wire. Watches whoever approaches the wicket or interferes with machinery. **Spell attack +6; save DC 14.**

- **Ray of Frost, at will:** action, range 60 feet, +6 to hit; **9 (2d8) cold**, target's speed decreases by 10 feet until caster's next turn.
- **Web, once:** standard spell, **concentration**. Catch the party's approach, leaving the guards' retreat lane clear.
- **Shield, once:** standard spell, reaction.

Usually opens with Web, then frost rays against characters escaping it or rushing the gate. Breaking concentration immediately helps. Normal targeting and visibility apply; a closed plate prevents targeting the outer chamber through it.

### Bombardier — fire and acid

Leather apron and padded rack of ceramic bombs. Fire bombs have smoking wick caps; acid bombs have green glass ampoules in their necks. Describe the difference before the first throw.

**One bomb per action, thrown up to 60 feet. Two fire bombs and two acid bombs.**

| Bomb | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | **10-foot radius; DC 15 Dexterity; 10 (3d6) fire**, half on success. Flammable objects ignite; Web burns according to its spell rules. |
| Acid | **5-foot radius; DC 15 Dexterity; 10 (3d6) acid**, half on success. Exposed unattended objects in the splash also take the rolled damage, including machine chains. |

- **Sling:** one attack, **+5**, range 30/120; **5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning**.
- Bombs affect allies normally. Avoid acid near machinery and explosions among retreating guards. PCs closing with guards, fighting beside chains, or seizing ammunition can exploit this.
- Fire can destroy Web; acid can damage the barricade. Do not waive these interactions for the defenders.
- Throws need a clear path. The bombardier cannot throw through the closed solid plate.

### Coordinating the defense

Guards buy space, spellcaster slows pursuit, and bombardier punishes characters stopping to fight or work on an obstruction. Director controls closure and retreat. Keep machine speed and statistics unchanged rather than adding more toughness for the expanded roster.

A PC through the wicket forces choices: ranged attackers can turn inward, relieving pressure outside; a puller can defend the platform, slowing closure. Director stays focused on machinery where possible. Once the plate blocks the outer chamber, ranged attackers prepare to retreat or confront PCs already inside.

## End conditions

- **Success:** get everyone through, or stop machinery while preserving a usable opening. Disabling it with the plate closed does not itself reopen the route.
- **Defenders beaten:** when sealing is no longer plausible, survivors withdraw deeper. Stranded guards may surrender. A negotiated truce can also end the encounter.
- **Party split:** keep playing. Characters inside can reverse machinery or stop the frame; those outside can pry aside the plate while unlocked.
- **Locked, everyone outside:** substantial dismantling or suitable magic can reopen it; another entrance remains available. Clearing it over hours exposes the party to the lair's unmaking.
- **Retreat:** defenders finish sealing and do not pursue out of the lair. Once secured, the passage leads onward through the underground kobold network.
