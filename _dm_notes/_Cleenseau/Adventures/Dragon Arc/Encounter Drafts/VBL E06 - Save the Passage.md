# E06 — Save the Passage

%% Choose A or B; either watchpost can lead to either mechanism. %%

## Read aloud — A: The Counterweighted Jaws

> Two enormous stone jaws frame the passage. One has already tipped inward. Kobolds haul at a wheel and chain while their foreman watches you.
>
> “Hold them here. We only need the last teeth.”

**Unwarned:** both jaws remain braced open; workers scramble to their controls.

## Read aloud — B: The Cast-Stone Seal

> Grey material flows from vats flanking the passage into a frame across it. Two kobolds tend the feeds. Their foreman watches the shrinking doorway.

**Unwarned:** the vats are closed; workers scramble to open them.

## Setup

- **Goal:** get everyone through and secure access. **Next:** kobold tunnels to [[VBL E08 - The Death View]]; no surface crossing.
- **Room:** 70 by 40 feet; exit opposite entrance, 10 feet wide, 12 feet high. Apparatus is 5 feet deep.
- Party at entrance; defenders 40–60 feet away. Work platforms 15 feet apart; maintenance ledge along near wall. All controls are reachable from the near side.
- **Defenders:** two workers, two guards, one foreman. Workers operate machinery; guards delay; foreman protects their retreat. A credible truce can stop the work.

### Starting progress

| Approach | Start |
| --- | --- |
| No warning | **0/4**. Each worker's first action readies their station; no first-round progress. Quiet arrivals may position or talk first; otherwise normal initiative, no automatic surprise. |
| Warned; party comes promptly | **2/4**, workers ready. Roll initiative before their next work actions. |
| Warned; party deliberately delays | Defenders may finish. Explain this risk before the party leaves or rests. |

**At each round's end:** advance **at most one step** if a ready worker spent an action working and the mechanism is not jammed, broken, or successfully held. Completed work counts even if that worker subsequently falls. Only the two original workers can operate it. Failed checks and other initiative counts never add progress.

## Defenders

*Small humanoids; speed 30 feet.*

| Creature | AC | HP | Initiative | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Worker ×2 | 13 | 14 each | +2 | +0 | +2 | +1 | +0 | +1 | +0 |
| Guard ×2 | 14 | 18 each | +2 | +0 | +2 | +1 | +0 | +1 | +0 |
| Foreman | 16 | 60 | +3 | +2 | +3 | +2 | +1 | +2 | +1 |

- **Workers/guards:** one attack, +4 to hit, reach 5 feet or range 30/120; **5 (1d6 + 2)** damage.
- **Foreman:** two hook attacks, +6 to hit, reach 5 feet; **10 (2d6 + 3) piercing** each. One hit may replace damage with a 10-foot pull.
- No Pack Tactics, spells, or extra bonus actions. Optional tougher foreman: **HP 80**; sealing never accelerates.

## A — Stone jaws

| Step | Passage |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Open. |
| 1 | Brace released. |
| 2 | Upper jaw halfway down; 8-foot-high gap. |
| 3 | Lower jaw rises; 4-foot-high gap, Medium creatures squeeze. |
| 4 | Locked shut. |

**Adjacent actions:**

- **Jam:** DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools. Stops progress until a worker spends an action clearing it.
- **Reverse brake:** DC 15 Athletics with a lever, winch, or help. Remove one progress, minimum 1.
- **Break ratchets:** each AC 15, HP 20, immune poison/psychic. One broken stops work until two separate repair actions; both broken prevent prompt repair.
- **Hold jaw:** physically brace the 10-foot span; action, DC 16 Athletics. Prevent next step while remaining there. Failure: DC 14 Dex or **10 (3d6) bludgeoning**; then displaced to near side. Suitable holding magic works without this check.

**At step 4:** anyone in the gap makes **DC 15 Dex**. Success: choose which side to reach. Failure: **14 (4d6) bludgeoning**, pushed to near side; nobody is buried.

**Beyond the jaws:** inside handle opens a **4-foot-square maintenance hatch** with an action; Medium creatures squeeze. A physically capable familiar can operate it. Teleportation and other suitable access work normally.

## B — Poured door

| Step | Passage |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Valves closed; open passage. |
| 1 | Flow starts. |
| 2 | Lower 6 feet filled; 6-foot opening above, normal climbing access. |
| 3 | Only a 2-foot side gap; Small creatures squeeze. Medium creatures need expansion or another method. |
| 4 | Material sets into an inert stone plug. |

**Adjacent actions:**

- **Close valve:** DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools. Stops progress until a worker spends an action reopening it.
- **Break feed line:** each AC 14, HP 18, immune poison/psychic. One broken stops work until two worker repair actions. Spillage is harmless.
- **Keep material fluid:** describe concrete attention/manipulation; action, DC 15 Arcana removes one progress, minimum 1. No concentration. A suitable material-changing effect may work without the check.
- **Beyond the frame:** opening the overflow with an action drains unset material, permanently stopping the seal through step 3.

**At step 4:** anyone in the gap makes **DC 15 Dex**. Success: choose a side. Failure: **14 (4d6) bludgeoning**, pushed to near side. Inside, a light lever opens a **4-foot-square hatch**; a capable familiar can use it.

## End conditions

- Passage permanently disabled, or party controls the inside hatch: defenders retreat or surrender.
- A temporary jam creates a crossing window; it does not permanently secure access.
- If sealed with nobody beyond, this route is closed. Options: clear it over hours while exposed to the unmaking, use another entrance, or employ suitable magic.
- The barrier is **5 feet deep**. Stone Shape, Passwall, teleportation, and other effects follow their actual text.
