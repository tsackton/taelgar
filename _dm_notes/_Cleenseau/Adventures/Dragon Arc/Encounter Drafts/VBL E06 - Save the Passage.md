# E06 — Save the Passage

Two sealing mechanisms for Krauzeth's main entrance fight. Use one after [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]]. Proposal assumptions: [[04 Encounter Packet - Vraedhul's Broken Lair]]. These mechanisms are local defenses prepared for emergencies, not creatures pointlessly destroying the entrance for months.

## Shared battlefield and objective

A 70-foot-long, 40-foot-wide receiving chamber narrows to a 10-foot passage at the far wall. The far passage leads into the kobold network toward Vraedhul's mountain. Two waist-high work platforms stand 15 feet apart beside its mouth. A near-side maintenance ledge runs along one wall. The PCs enter at the opposite end; defenders are at 40–60 feet. Everything necessary to interfere with the sealing mechanism is reachable from this side.

The objective is **secure usable access and get the party through**, not kill the rearguard. Workers visibly act on the passage. Tell the party what the remaining steps would do and which parts can be interrupted.

Two workers, two guards, and a foreman defend the chamber. Workers spend their actions on the seal unless directly prevented. Guards delay intruders; the foreman orders retreat, takes cover, and protects workers. They prefer a closed refuge to dying and will negotiate a retreat if offered credible safety.

**Profiles:** workers AC 13, HP 14; guards AC 14, HP 18. Both speed 30, STR +0, DEX +2, CON +1, INT +0, WIS +1, CHA +0, initiative +2. One tool/spear/sling attack +4, **5 (1d6+2)** damage, reach 5 or range 30/120. Foreman AC 16, HP 60; speed 30; STR +2, DEX +3, CON +2, INT +1, WIS +2, CHA +1; initiative +3. Two hook attacks +6, reach 5, **10 (2d6+3)** piercing each. On one hit it can replace damage with a 10-foot pull toward itself. No Pack Tactics, hidden spells, or bonus actions. All have ordinary conditions and vulnerabilities.

### Alarm-dependent start

- **No warning:** progress 0 of 4. The workers are not at their controls. A PC who observed quietly can begin conversation or positioning before hostilities; otherwise roll initiative as everyone notices one another. The workers need their first actions to reach and ready their platforms, contributing no progress that round. No automatic surprise condition is assumed.
- **Warning received, party follows promptly:** progress 2 of 4, both workers ready. The setup and first irreversible parts have already happened during the approach. State the damage immediately. Roll initiative before another work action. This is the intended harder start, not a hidden penalty beyond it.
- **Party leaves for a lengthy unrelated excursion after the warning:** the kobolds finish closing the refuge. Say this risk when they leave the ongoing warning behind. On returning, use the sealed result below; do not pretend the defenders waited indefinitely.

**Work cadence:** at the end of each round, advance **one step maximum** if at least one ready, able worker spent its action working that round. Two workers provide redundancy, not double speed. An action used to fight, flee, recover a dropped control, or ready a station is not a work action. Mark four visible spaces on scrap paper. Do not also add progress on initiative 20 or whenever a PC fails a check.

## A — The counterweighted jaws

> The low passage ends between two immense stone jaws. One is already tipped out of its socket. A kobold at a ratcheted wheel shouts; another answers from a hanging chain. The foreman turns a hooked blade toward you. "Hold them here. We only need the last teeth."

Four steps: **release outer brace → lower top jaw halfway → tip lower jaw upward → lock jaws together**. At progress 0 both are held open. At 2 the gap is 8 feet high; at 3 it is 4 feet high, requiring most Medium creatures to squeeze. At 4 it closes. This is a gate-like collapse into prepared sockets, not falling entire mountains onto PCs.

Counteractions:

- **Jam either ratchet:** adjacent action, DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools; success halts all progress until a worker spends an action clearing it. Failure does not advance progress.
- **Reset the near brake:** adjacent action, DC 15 Athletics with a lever, winch, or assistance; success removes one progress. It cannot undo the first released brace but may keep progress at 1. A fitting control spell or magic that actually holds a jaw can stop movement without a check.
- **Destroy a working control:** each ratchet AC 15, HP 20, poison/psychic immunity. One broken ratchet halts progress until an adjacent worker uses **two separate repair actions**; both broken prevent ordinary sealing entirely. No replacement controls appear.
- **Hold a jaw physically:** a creature able to brace the 10-foot span can use an action and DC 16 Athletics to prevent the next progress step, but must remain at the jaw. Failure costs position: DC 14 Dexterity or **10 (3d6)** bludgeoning, then it is displaced to the near side. The jaw cannot pin and damage it every turn automatically.

At progress 4, anyone in the gap makes DC 15 Dexterity: on success choose either side, on failure take **14 (4d6)** bludgeoning and remain on the near side. No instant burial. PCs already beyond the jaws find an obvious inside release handle; one action drops a narrow maintenance hatch, allowing the rest to crawl through. Therefore **getting even one ally through is a legitimate way to preserve access**. Teleportation or a familiar capable of physically operating the handle can achieve the same thing.

## B — The cast-stone seal

> Two cracked vats flank the passage. A kobold works a long handle, and grey material crawls into the gap like porridge climbing a spoon. Wherever it touches the frame, it becomes beautifully fitted stone. The foreman is watching the doorway rather than your weapons.

An old Vraedhul creation supplies a local possibility of stone flowing and then fitting perfectly. It is a defense the kobolds can operate, not a new portable artifact cache.

Four steps: **open flow → fill lower third → fill upper third → set the stone**. At progress 2 a 6-foot-high opening remains at the top, reached by climbing 5 feet. At 3 only a 2-foot service gap remains at one side: Small creatures can squeeze through, Medium creatures need enlargement of the gap or suitable magic. State these dimensions before movement decisions.

Counteractions:

- **Close a valve:** adjacent action and DC 15 Athletics or thieves' tools; success stops all progress until a worker spends an action reopening it. No loss on a failed check.
- **Rupture a feed line:** AC 14, HP 18, poison/psychic immunity; either broken line stops progress until repaired with two worker actions. Spilled material solidifies in a harmless heap, not a new area-damage trap.
- **Keep existing stone fluid:** an adjacent action, describing a concrete attempt to sustain its already visible flowing state, and DC 15 Arcana; success removes one progress, minimum 1. Another method that demonstrably changes or moves the material can do the same without this specific skill. No ordinary PC concentration slot is occupied by this encounter action.
- **Push through before setting:** flight, climbing, dimension travel, or working with a Small companion is useful. Beyond the seal, an accessible overflow valve can be turned with one action, draining the unset material and permanently stopping the sequence even from progress 3.

At progress 4 the plug becomes inert stone, and a creature in the remaining gap makes the same DC 15 Dexterity save and 14 damage as A, ending on the near side on failure. An ally already beyond can open a separate 2-foot-wide maintenance hatch with one action; Medium PCs can crawl through it slowly, without a squeezing combat after the defenders flee.

## End states and balance

Once the mechanism is permanently stopped or an inside hatch is controlled, defenders retreat or surrender. The objective is achieved even if they escape. A temporarily jammed mechanism gives a crossing window; if the party wants to stay, it must secure it rather than assume a one-round delay is permanent.

If the seal completes with no one beyond and no bypass, this route is lost **for the present attempt**, even if every defender dies. Ordinary rubble-clearing takes hours and risks further unmaking; make another entrance the practical option. Stone-shaping, passwall, teleportation, or other actually suitable abilities can reopen access. Do not declare their normal magic ineffective just to enforce failure. Loss is access and expended resources, not automatic adventure defeat.

The combined watchpost and passage are a linked commitment. This fight alone has 124 enemy HP and modest direct damage; worker actions create the real demand. A competent party may stop it with one area spell, a fast infiltrator, or control. Preserve that win. Strong fresh level 10 option: foreman HP 80; never shorten the two-turn warned window or add a second progress step per round.

Success leads through short narrated kobold tunnels to [[VBL E08 - The Death View]]. The tunnels connect peaks underground; do not add a surface bridge over the workshop chaos.
