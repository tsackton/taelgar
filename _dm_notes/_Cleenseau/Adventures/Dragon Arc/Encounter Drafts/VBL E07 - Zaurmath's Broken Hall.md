# E07 — Zaurmath's Broken Hall

%% Choose A or B. This route follows a coherent lower shelf beside the expulsion. %%

## Read aloud — A: The Floor Remembers Water

> The black floor ripples beneath a carved dragon. Three broad plates lie across it like lily pads. One turns wet and glassy; another darkens and holds still. Beyond them, a tunnel opens in solid rock.

## Read aloud — B: Rooms Trying to Become Wind

> Three stone ribs span the hall. Dust stops dead beneath an iron ring, then streams sideways as the ring bends. A stone leaf tears loose and flutters across the floor.

## Setup and bypasses

- **Goal:** reach the far tunnel. **Next:** short tunnels to [[VBL E08 - The Death View]].
- Hall **80 by 60 feet**, entrance opposite exit. Safe landings extend 15 feet from each end; damaged middle is 50 feet long.
- Watch safely for **1 minute** to learn the pattern without a roll. **DC 14 Arcana or Investigation** learns it sooner or grants advantage on a manipulation check.
- **West ledge:** 5 feet wide, one 15-foot gap and one cracked outcrop. Rope, adequate jumping, or flight can solve them directly; otherwise **DC 14 Athletics** across the gap and **DC 14 Acrobatics or Athletics** around the outcrop. One lead character can rig a route for everyone.
- Continuous catch shelf lies **10 feet below** the west ledge throughout the damaged middle. Failed ledge check: fall for **3 (1d6) bludgeoning**. Ally's rope or **DC 12 Athletics** gets back up.
- **East breach:** 10 feet wide, visibly dangerous: loose outlines, bones, stones being unmade. Entering it: **DC 16 Dex**, **27 (6d8) force**, half on success; expelled onto nearest coherent floor either way. No automatic repeated damage or unannounced shove into it.

## A — Cycling plates

- Three **10-foot-square plates**, at **15–25, 35–45, and 55–65 feet** from entrance; 10-foot gaps between them.
- Liquid-stone basin lies **10 feet below**. Brass rails and their footholds remain solid through every phase; ordinary glass is firm.
- Starting phases: **near = stone; middle = wet; far = water**.
- Every round's end: **stone → wet → water → stone**. Wet stone remains safe and warns of the next change.

### Hold a plate

- After learning the pattern: action adjacent to a plate or touching its rail makes the plate stone for **3 rounds**, no check. Save its current phase.
- Maintain contact; no concentration. Release or incapacitation ends the hold. One plate per character; starting another releases the first.
- Skip its next three end-of-round advances. Immediately after the third, restore the saved phase; resume normal cycling next round. Early release restores that phase immediately, without an extra advance. Announce the return.

### Water and falls

- Enter a water plate, or have one turn beneath you: **DC 14 Dex** to catch a rail.
- **Success:** hanging; spend 10 feet of movement to climb onto a foothold or solid plate.
- **Failure:** fall for **7 (2d6) bludgeoning** into basin.
- Ally's action pulls a fallen character up; otherwise **DC 12 Athletics** plus 15 feet of movement reaches safety.
- Crossing the basin costs triple movement and one **DC 12 Athletics** check. Failure deals **7 bludgeoning** before the far climb. No drowning or petrification.
- Flight over the central route works; no extra check once the plan clears the hazards.

## B — Gust lanes

- Floor remains solid; ceiling **20 feet high**.
- Three 10-foot-wide gust lanes at **20–30, 35–45, and 50–60 feet** from entrance.
- Gusts push **west toward the catch shelf**, away from the eastern breach.
- **Initiative 20:** mark next rib with motionless dust and a straining ring. At the following initiative 20, that lane blows for one round and the next is marked. Cycle **near → middle → far**.
- Enter an active lane or start a turn there: **DC 14 Str**, at most once per round. Failure: **7 (2d6) bludgeoning** and push 10 feet west. Success: half damage, no push. Shelf falls/recovery use the rules above.
- Timing a crossing through inactive lanes works without a check.

### Stop a gust

- After observing: action touching rib or floor socket supports its still-air state for **3 rounds**, no check.
- Alternatively jam the ring with a tool: **DC 14 Athletics**.
- **Dispel Magic:** special 4th-level effect; DC 14 with a 3rd-level slot, automatic with 4th or higher. Lane inactive for **1 minute**.
- Flight meets the same lanes below the ceiling. Timing, teleportation, and suitable wind effects remain valid solutions.
