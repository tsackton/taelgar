# E02 — Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance

Two presentation flavors for the same brief scouting structure. Proposal status and route index: [[04 Encounter Packet - Vraedhul's Broken Lair]]. This is not a compulsory fight or a sequence of three mandatory scouting encounters.

## Shared procedure

After [[VBL E01 - River of Solid Air]], give the party the two obvious sights below. Ask what they do. They can follow either immediately. Only deliberate scouting reveals the third-route signs and can lead to the hidden kobold.

Place the road on the left of the panorama, the expulsion on the right, and the sheltered ridge and gullies between. A safe overlook is a short walk from the river, with all three areas visible. Exact mileage is unnecessary. If they scout, ask **where they get an overview and how they look for inhabitants**. Sensible inspection during travel counts; no special word or declaration is required. Resolve a single reconnaissance pass. No roll is needed for the basic findings. A safe vantage, familiar, flight, careful ground search, or another sensible method works; divide the three findings among the scouts and reconvene without playing out three separate trips.

Give the three basic findings first. **Only if they follow the fresh predator lead**, use one DC 15 check for approaching unseen or detecting movement early: Survival for interpreting fresh tracks, Perception for scanning from cover, Stealth for a close approach, or another genuinely relevant method. Help can give advantage. If the whole party scouts noisily, do not allow four independent rolls until one succeeds. On success, the party is 30 feet from the refuge, the predator is at least 40 feet from it, and they have time to prepare before initiative. On failure, they are 30 feet away with the predator adjacent to the shelter, its first probing action still to come. The kobold is still alive in either case. Flying scouts see the tracks only if they inspect the ground closely or relay another scout's observations; aerial height is not automatic microscopic vision.

Give these findings during that pass:

| Lead | Concrete observation | Useful inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal entrance | The paving can be followed despite its bends. At the end, an enormous metal claw moves above a bare forecourt, and a voice reaches the hills. | They know from Orenlas that tribute is expected. The broad exposed approach offers poor concealment; giving something valuable is likely easier than sneaking. |
| Zaurmath | A rising distortion throws out a recognizably carved arch. The same small shelf beneath its lower edge remains in place through successive eruptions; a dark passage opens behind it. | A surviving entrance exists beside the worst chaos. They can watch before crossing and avoid the unformed center. |
| Krauzeth | Narrow bootprints repeatedly use sheltered gullies and gaps between displaced stone, avoiding road and eruption. On a distant ledge, cut footholds and a low screen of piled rock suggest an inhabited approach. | There is a third way in with living occupants. Following the track is possible even without an NPC guide. |

The fresh deviation from the third trail leads behind a slab or into a bent cleft, where disturbed ash and irregular scraping betray a hunting creature. The kobold itself remains hidden until someone follows and investigates. Continue to [[VBL E03 - The Hidden Kobold]] if they pursue it. If they decline, keep the third-route lead: no detached NPC scream drags them back.

**Timing:** narrate one look around and one decision, roughly 5–10 minutes at the table if there is no rescue. Do not charge checks for every hundred feet or make the eruption progress on each question. They can reconsider their route. No useful tribute cache is found.

## A — The road on its side

> Past the river, the country opens. The paving ahead climbs a wall of broken stone, turns sideways, and resumes its old direction farther up the slope. It is a grand road, even now: every slab was cut to the same enormous scale. Far to the other side, something like a standing whirlpool rises against a mountain. An arch comes out of it whole, turns slowly in the sky, and falls apart before it reaches the ground.

The road and whirlpool lie on different sides of the panorama, not along one convenient line. Between them are ashy hollows, tilted blocks, and a low ridge. A stone slab with its old weathered face now downward makes an easy overlook. A party can climb its ordinary sloping back without a roll. Its former underside holds pale roots suspended like snapped wires; nothing here requires a living forest.

Deliver the formal clue by looking along the road, the Zaurmath clue by watching two eruptions, and the third clue from repeated bootprints sheltering along the ridge. Each is a finding within one pass, not a destination to visit. This flavor makes the land's physical dislocation the central image.

## B — The interrupted procession

> Rows of stone attendants mark the beginning of the grand approach. One stands upside down on its own plinth. Another has been spread into a broad sheet, though its carved hands still hold the same empty bowl. Their road makes a courteous curve toward the mountain. Across the open land, a different peak throws a flight of stairs into the sky. For several seconds the steps continue arranging themselves for feet that are not there.

The attendants are inert sculptures, not a tribute puzzle or an extra combat. The road behind them is broken into shallow zigzags, still navigable on foot. One broad plinth offers a stable viewpoint with shelter below it. The same three observations are available. The bootprints pass behind the statues rather than along their ceremonious frontage; the habit is practical concealment, not a secret password.

This flavor introduces the dragon's grandeur and humiliating hospitality before the formal entrance. Nothing found in the bowls is useful payment. Heading straight up the road leads to [[VBL E04 - The Arborean Gatekeeper]]; the third trail leads toward [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]]; investigating the coherent shelf leads to [[VBL E07 - Zaurmath's Broken Hall]].
