# E02 — Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance

%% Opening revised 2026-10-25. The front entrance is immediately available. Scouting discovers the endangered kobold; the kobold or evidence at the attack site leads to the back entrance. The numerical scouting bands remain adjustable running mechanics. %%

**Goal:** observe the domain and decide whether to approach the front entrance or investigate the outskirts. **Timing:** roughly **20 minutes** across E01 and E02; the predator encounter is additional play if pursued.

## The domain beneath the cloud

> Three mountains rise into a single churning cloud of dust and broken stone. Along its underside, fragments of walls and stairways emerge into daylight, hang for a moment, and are pulled back into the grey.

- Glimpses of mountainsides establish three peaks. The cloud obscures their summits and the interior of the domain, including the workshops and Vraedhul's remains.
- The **grand road** is the obvious destination: a followable approach to the formal entrance beneath the cloud's edge.
- **Zaurmath's lair** is the heart of the visible destruction, feeding the cloud over all three peaks. It is clearly unsafe; an expedition into it will be improvised.
- Sheltered gullies and displaced terraces give the outer slopes texture and places to investigate. An aerial overview does not identify the back entrance.
- Flying higher reveals the cloud's extent. Lower passes and observation of the outer slopes provide practical information.

## The grand road

> Stone attendants border the broken road, each holding an empty offering bowl. At its far end, a claw large enough to crush a wagon slowly opens beside the entrance.

- Distorted paving remains followable. A tilted slab or broad plinth offers an easy overlook without a climbing check.
- The final forecourt is exposed; watching reveals deliberate movement from the constructed guardian.
- Orenlas's information supplies the connection: this is the entrance where visitors brought tribute.
- Observing the defenses and choosing a landing place are useful scouting results in their own right. The front entrance is a viable route.
- Following the road leads directly to [[VBL E04 - The Arborean Gatekeeper]]. The statues and bowls provide no useful tribute.

## Zaurmath's destruction

> Above Zaurmath's ruined lair, enormous slabs rise into the cloud. A staircase briefly emerges beneath them; the rock supporting it stretches into thin strands and tears away.

- Show large pieces of terrain being consumed, including places that momentarily look solid.
- There is no prepared surviving shelf or environmental entrance to offer here. [[VBL E07 - Zaurmath's Broken Hall]] is an unused earlier draft.
- An investigation into the destruction remains a player choice; improvise its consequences from the visible conditions.

## Scouting the outskirts

- Ask what they want to learn and how they scout. Flight, a familiar, a ground search, or patient observation can discover the endangered kobold in [[VBL E03 - The Hidden Kobold]].
- A deliberate search of the outskirts finds the predator scene. Scouting rolls determine how early they notice it and how favorable their starting position is.
- Use **one lead Perception, Survival, or suitable approach check**; Help or an effective method can improve the result. Avoid a sequence of repeated search checks.
- The discovery is the predator and its victim. The connection to a concealed entrance comes from helping the kobold or examining its escape trail and belongings afterward.

| Result | What they find | E03 start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Good: 15+** | Predator working toward the refuge; kobold trapped but largely unhurt. | Time to choose positions and prepare. |
| **Middling: 10–14** | Predator has extracted the kobold; it is injured and struggling. | Intervene promptly to stop feeding. |
| **Bad: 9 or lower** | Predator already eating the kobold; it is barely alive. | Immediate protection and healing needed. |

- Resolve uncertain scouting before giving the close-up description, so it matches the kobold's condition.
- Keeping the kobold alive earns conversation, directions, and warnings. Its death still leaves physical clues to the back way.
- The back-way discovery leads to [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]]; the party can still choose the front entrance after learning about it.

## Color to drop into pauses

- **Impossible weight:** A boulder rests on the tips of a dead thorn bush. Its shadow lies beneath it, perfectly ordinary.
- **Continuing decay:** A distant terrace cracks, its broken edge hanging on thin stone threads. Then the threads become smoke.
- **Former grandeur:** An overturned statue still holds its offering bowl level. Its wrist has twisted twice around to keep it upright.
- **The cloud's edge:** A row of windows turns slowly inside the dust. Grit ticks against your equipment, and a block of dressed stone drops into view before curving upward again.
