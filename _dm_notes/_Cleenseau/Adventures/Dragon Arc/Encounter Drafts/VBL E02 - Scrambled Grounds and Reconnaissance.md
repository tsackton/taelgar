# E02 — Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance

**Choose one description. Goal:** choose an entrance.

## A — The road on its side

> Beyond the river, enormous paving slabs climb a wall of broken stone, turn sideways, and resume their course. Across the hills, a standing whirlpool of architecture rises against another mountain. An arch emerges whole, turns slowly, and falls apart.

- A tilted slab provides an easy overlook: ordinary climb, no roll.

## B — The interrupted procession

> Stone attendants line the grand approach, holding empty offering bowls. One stands upside down; another has been stretched into a sheet. Across the hills, a mountain throws stairs into the sky. For several seconds they keep arranging themselves for feet that are not there.

- A broad plinth provides an overlook. Statues are inert; bowls contain no useful tribute.

## Run the scene

- **Road left; expulsion right; sheltered ridge and gullies between.**
- Both obvious landmarks can be followed immediately.
- Deliberate scouting reveals all three leads below. **One reconnaissance pass; no roll for basic findings.** Flight, a familiar, or ground inspection can help.
- Tracks require close inspection, including by flying scouts.
- Allow roughly **5–10 minutes** at the table; no recurring search checks or countdown.

## Optional rescue lead

- Fresh tracks leave the third trail for a slab or cleft; scraping and disturbed ash reveal a predator.
- Following it leads to [[VBL E03 - The Hidden Kobold]].
- **One DC 15 Survival, Perception, Stealth, or suitable approach check;** Help can grant advantage.
- **Success:** party **30 ft** from refuge; predator at least **40 ft** away. Time to prepare.
- **Failure:** party **30 ft** away; predator adjacent to refuge, **first probing action still pending**.
- Kobold alive either way. Ignoring or losing it does not erase the third-route tracks.
