# Vraedhul's Broken Lair — Short Critique Cards

Start here for the short versions. Each A/B pair is an alternative, not two encounters to run consecutively. The linked notes contain read-aloud text, terrain, numbers, actions, spell interactions, rewards, and failure handling. All are proposals for critique beneath the agreed opening; none chooses a flavor for you. Full index and review record: [[04 Encounter Packet - Vraedhul's Broken Lair]].

## 1. River crossing

### A — The glassback

The river holds the shape of a stopped ripple. Invisible planes of solid air obstruct the crossing. A low, wedge-headed beast scrapes its glass forehead against one, then charges through it. Its attacks commit to a clearly announced straight line. The party can bait it into opening the remaining passage, break the air themselves, or find another way across. The creature is useful while alive; its death alone does not solve the crossing.

### B — The folded current

A plated river predator swims out of the water and continues swimming through the air. It strikes the invisible barriers with its brow. Same crossing objective and telegraphed charge, but splashes attract it and its charge can rise or descend. The image emphasizes substances and directions trading roles. Flying above the finite obstacles, teleportation, and a longer upstream detour remain genuine solutions.

Run either from [[VBL E01 - River of Solid Air]].

## 2. The grounds and reconnaissance

### A — The road on its side

Beyond the river, the old grand road climbs a vertical face, turns sideways, and resumes farther along. In roughly the opposite direction, a standing whirlpool of architecture ejects an arch. A short reconnaissance pass from a tilted slab reveals the exposed tribute gate, a coherent opening beside the upheaval, and bootprints using sheltered gullies toward a third route. No prolonged wilderness tour or three separate search checks.

### B — The interrupted procession

The grand approach begins among stone attendants with offering bowls. One is upside down; another has been stretched flat while its carved hands still hold their bowl. Across the land, stairs eject themselves into the sky and keep arranging themselves. The attendants are inert atmosphere, not another puzzle. The same brief scouting procedure supplies three useful entrance leads; following a fresh deviation can reveal the hidden kobold.

Run either from [[VBL E02 - Scrambled Grounds and Reconnaissance]].

## 3. The hidden kobold

### A — The mouth beneath the slab

Fresh tracks lead to a tilted slab. A pale, flattened animal is peeling it upward with a mouth that slides around its underside. The kobold inside has a trapped ankle. The party must extract it and cover its escape, not merely kill the animal. The predator's work has visible stages and can be interrupted. Rescuing the kobold provides a warning about the third entrance's guards and alarm.

### B — Hunting ribbons

Two translucent strips of predator circle a narrow cleft, one probing inside while the other threatens rescuers. The party can pin them, divert them, intercept a seized kobold, or carry the kobold into a sheltered gully. Only one predator advances the rescue danger per round. Good scouting gives a preparation window; a poor result starts with the hunters at the shelter, with the kobold still alive.

Run either from [[VBL E03 - The Hidden Kobold]].

## 4. The formal entrance

### A — The maker in the bronze dragon

An enormous bronze likeness of Vraedhul examines offerings with eye, mouth, and claw. A bound Arborean maker presses against its gold collars. One self believes it is home, one understands the ruin, and one continues enforcing tribute. Fitting valuable payment opens the door straightforwardly. Alternatively the party can bring forward a cooperative perspective, expose and loosen bindings, sneak through maintenance access, or fight to disable the gate.

### B — The orchard of gold

The same imposing dragon has grown branches through its metal bones. Golden fruit resembles hands and faces; the voice comes from every leaf. The captive maker believes it is cultivating wonders until attention brings a different self forward. Its binding points are cages around fruit, claw-root, and sounding branch. The admission options remain the same; this flavor makes the maker's original creative nature more visible and unsettling.

Run either from [[VBL E04 - The Arborean Gatekeeper]].

## 5. Krauzeth's outer watch

### A — Signal lens and glass hound

A tethered, translucent-skulled hound occupies the approach while kobolds guard a signaling lens. The signaler must visibly align the light and then transmit on a later turn. The party can talk through the watch, sneak past, obscure or break the beam, remove its source, calm the hound, or fight while preventing the warning. A warning starts the sealing response beyond; casualties alone do not decide success.

### B — Runner and kiln beast

A creature exhales warm ash behind its chain. A kobold fits a keyed handle to its wrist and prepares to run toward a muted bell. It must reach the bell and open its sound channel to warn the inner defenders. Intercept the runner, steal the handle, block or silence the bell, or avoid detection. The beast's ash attack is announced before it fires. This variant makes the alarm mobile.

Run either from [[VBL E05 - Krauzeth's Watchpost]].

## 6. Preserve the passage

### A — The counterweighted jaws

In the receiving chamber, two workers lower enormous stone jaws around the onward passage while guards delay intruders. The earlier alarm determines how far the four-step closure has progressed. Jam a ratchet, work the brake, destroy a control, hold the jaw, or send someone through to release the inside maintenance hatch. Killing everyone can still be too late; getting one ally through can be enough.

### B — The cast-stone seal

Grey material crawls from two vats into the passage and becomes perfectly fitted stone. The workers operate the dragon's old flowing-stone creation. Shut a valve, rupture a line, sustain its existing fluid possibility, or move through before it sets. All controls are reachable from the party's side. Once someone reaches the far side, an overflow valve or maintenance hatch can preserve access. No defenders have been sealing it for months: the warning triggered this.

Run either from [[VBL E06 - Save the Passage]].

## 7. Zaurmath's environmental entrance

### A — The floor remembers water

The onward tunnel is visible beyond a hall whose decorated floor plates alternate between stone, wet stone, and water. Solid rails, stable landings, a slower ledge, and a lower basin provide recoveries. The party can read the cycle, hold an existing stone possibility through the old creation, rig ropes, jump, or spend magic. The unforming breach occupies an obviously more dangerous side of the room and never needs to be crossed.

### B — Rooms trying to become wind

Carved ribs frame lanes of air that are turning into gusts. Dust gathers and iron rings strain before each lane blows. The party can time the crossing, still a doorway's existing calm possibility, jam a moving ring, dispel an effect, or use the safer ledge. Gusts push toward a catching shelf, not secretly into the extreme breach. The geometry stays understandable throughout.

Run either from [[VBL E07 - Zaurmath's Broken Hall]].

## 8. Confirm Vraedhul's death

### A — The skull in the wound

From an inward balcony on the mother's mountain, the party sees the workshop chaos turn. An immense jaw appears within it, still joined to a skull whose bone stretches into white lines across the catastrophe. The dragon is unmistakably dead, consumed by an eruption from Limbo. Watching reveals the residue continuing to change things. No extra puzzle or required roll delays the accomplishment.

### B — The spine across the workshops

Through a broad rock window, a vast skeleton spans the workshops: bone enters a roof, emerges as a stair, and becomes bone again. A recognizable skull anchors the image. The body has been transformed through the catastrophe rather than left as an ordinary corpse. All three entrance routes reach this shared place. The view is safe enough for a pause; costly information magic can provide useful answers rather than being arbitrarily blocked.

Run either from [[VBL E08 - The Death View]].

## 9. Guest room — The Generous Table

### A — Excessive hospitality

A luxurious table keeps replacing food faster than anyone can finish. Its chairs try to seat departing diners; its embroidered cloth contains a portable platform enchantment. Explore the service alcove and its image of an empty table, demonstrate a completed meal, or physically stop the mechanism. The prize is a once-daily stationary floating platform. The joke is hospitality that cannot tolerate a satisfied guest.

### B — The feast of borrowed senses

Little dishes hold stone, copper, and salt labeled with experiences such as applause or triumph. A cup lets visitors taste sensations imposed on damaged materials. Clear the cup between samples to prevent minor sensory confusion. Washed and carried away, it can safely taste a visible substance at a distance and detect poison. Strange sensory luxury replaces the more physical trap of A.

Run either from [[VBL G01 - The Generous Table]].

## 10. Guest room — The Perfect Reflection

### A — A flattering mirror

The mirror shows a magnificent guest in an unbroken room. Its silver lens is useful treasure, but the flattering reflection reaches for it first. Attend to an accurate detail, turn the frame toward its plain face, cover the glamour, or take another practical approach. Pulling heedlessly can produce a short psychic grip. The recovered lens grants brief, limited sight of invisible things.

### B — The portrait that improves its guest

An unfinished portrait begins correcting a seated visitor's appearance. A brooch in its frame helps persuasion, but its uncorrected magic makes the wearer's voice ingratiating and spoken intimidation harder. The curse is disclosed before attunement and does not censor words or thoughts. Remove Curse, destruction, or an hour restoring the portrait's portable plain study provides a clear removal path.

Run either from [[VBL G02 - The Perfect Reflection]].

## 11. Guest room — The Patient's Suite

### A — The cure in the cabinet

A perfect bed faces a cabinet of little silver feet and a mural of a grateful limping supplicant. The tapping feet catch an intrusive wrist but can be stilled, covered, turned, or broken. Acting on the creation as a satisfied patient reveals a bottle with three sure-footing doses. These provide situational climbing and resistance to physical displacement without a hidden dependency or relapse.

### B — Pain kept in crystal

A spotless bed, a chair dented by gripping hands, and a dark shape contracting inside a crystal tell the story of a cruel cure. The stored pain is not a captive soul. Gloves, tongs, a padded frame, or stilling the creation allow safe handling. Its three charges can reduce later damage. Breaking it spills a small warned pulse rather than starting a new plot.

Run either from [[VBL G03 - The Patient's Suite]].
