## Route

**Travel → river flyover → observe the cloud-covered domain.**

- **Direct approach:** front entrance → death view.
- **Scout first:** endangered kobold → survivor or physical clues reveal the back way → choose front or back entrance → death view.
- Optional guest suites follow the death view.

Allow roughly **20 minutes** for arrival and observation; the optional predator encounter is additional play. Scouting determines whether the kobold is still sheltered, seized and injured, or already dying. A survivor provides directions and warnings; a dead kobold's trail and belongings still lead to the back way. Zaurmath's lair feeds the cloud and is visibly unsafe; improvise if the party enters the destruction.

## Shared rules

- **Stats:** original fifth-edition shorthand. Unlisted saves use ability modifiers; no hidden resistances, immunities, or extra actions. Use printed averages or roll damage.
- **Warnings first:** show charges, traps, and sealing work before resolving them. Sound plans work; roll for meaningful uncertainty.
- **Magic:** apply actual targeting and spell text, including object restrictions. Flight, control, and teleportation can solve encounters.
- **Limbo:** specific room manipulations stabilize an existing possibility. Follow their listed durations; no general concentration cost.
- **Rest:** stable ground permits rest unless a specific pursuit intervenes. No global decay clock.
- **Difficulty:** E03's Cindermaw and brood are homebrew intended as a strong fight for four 10th-level PCs, without a tested CR assignment. For other encounters, select any optional HP adjustment before play; level 10 alone needs no increase.
- **Rewards:** scouting gives information, not ready-made tribute. Guest treasures are optional proposals; disclose curses and removal methods.

## Guest-suite placement

- Proposed: viewpoint → shared antechamber → ordinary main corridor toward audience area.
- Suites branch off the antechamber. G01's service door may join G02; G03's second door may rejoin the corridor.
- Use any, all, or none. Audience chamber and personal lair remain undeveloped.
