## Shared rules

- **Stats:** original fifth-edition shorthand. Unlisted saves use ability modifiers; no hidden resistances, immunities, or extra actions. Use printed averages or roll damage.
- **Warnings first:** show charges, traps, and sealing work before resolving them. Sound plans work; roll for meaningful uncertainty.
- **Magic:** apply actual targeting and spell text, including object restrictions. Flight, control, and teleportation can solve encounters.
- **Limbo:** specific room manipulations stabilize an existing possibility. Follow their listed durations; no general concentration cost.
- **Rest:** stable ground permits rest unless a specific pursuit intervenes. No global decay clock.
- **Difficulty:** select any optional HP adjustment before play. Level 10 alone needs no increase.
- **Rewards:** scouting gives information, not ready-made tribute. Guest treasures are optional proposals; disclose curses and removal methods.

## Guest-suite placement
