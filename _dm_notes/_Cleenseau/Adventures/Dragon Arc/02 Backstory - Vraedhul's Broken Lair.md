## Current situation

Vraedhul is dead. About three months before the expedition, an eruption of Limbo through the local weak point consumed her and devastated her domain. The recent wider spill of Limbo into Taelgar through weak spots accounts for strange events and disruptions in the Plaguelands.

Residue remains and is progressively unmaking the lair. The central workshops, where the local chaos began, are probably already pure chaos. Other places remain intact, have become mutable, or have passed beyond coherent form.

Zaurmath died quickly while defending his mother. His lair has the worst chaos among the surviving entrance routes and contains little surviving explorable space. Krauzeth fled with kobolds; the PCs later killed him and have relationships with some of his former followers.

Enough physical and magical structure survived for the domain to persist for months. Its deterioration is now accelerating.

## Relevant backstory and hidden truth

### Vraedhul and her craft

Vraedhul was immensely powerful and drawn to this location by its connection to Limbo. She exploited its capacity to bend order, cultivating strange and cruel possibilities in existing things and infusing objects with extraordinary properties.

Precious objects and people could both become raw materials. The cruel joke is the overriding character of her favorite creations. She enjoyed surrounding herself with dangerous and cursed objects; her personal lair housed favorite finished work.

She sent cursed objects into the world and sometimes charged heavily to remove the curses. She was unwelcoming to unworthy visitors and expected an appropriate gift before granting attention. Weirdness, cruelty, value, and potential for transformation could make an offering interesting.

Her promises did not provide reliable protection from her choices. The intended tone is dragon tribute, power, and material transformation; the entrance should not depend on fey bargain wording, contractual loopholes, or reciprocal obligations.

### Why the lair has lasted and is now changing faster

The adopted explanation applies Limbo's self-amplifying transformation to the surviving domain:

1. Substantial physical and magical structure persisted after Vraedhul died.
2. Early changes were isolated pockets.
3. Material already transformed became easier to change again.
4. Pockets spread and joined, accelerating the breakdown.
5. The eventual state is an unformed wasteland or cyclone of chaos.

The spreading-residue application is an adventure-specific extension of the Limbo principles, not an independently established universal rule.

The site remains in Taelgar. Local effects can resemble Limbo, but the PCs have not entered the plane itself. Much more direct or complete reality shaping, as seen in Dunmar's actual Limbo adventure, is not the baseline here.

### Her remains

There is no intact fresh corpse waiting in the personal lair. Recognizable skeletal or skull-like remains have been transformed, stretched through, or intertwined with the central workshop chaos.

A clear view toward the center makes her death recognizable. All three successful entrance routes give access to one shared discovery location on her mountain, before the supplicant-quarter crawl. The exact terrace, aperture, framing, and anatomical image remain to be finished.

The essential revelation is partial: an eruption from Limbo has consumed Vraedhul. The view does not supply the full account of the eruption or all its continuing consequences.

## Geography

### Three peaks and central workshops

The original domain included Vraedhul's lair, one lair for each offspring, supplicant accommodations, workrooms, and kobold quarters.

Working schematic, not a measured map:

            1          A
                        W       2
            B                      C

                        3

- A is Vraedhul's peak.
- B and C are the offspring's peaks; the precise assignment of the old map labels and entrance numbers to each offspring is not fixed here.
- W is the workshop complex around the bases between the peaks.
- Entrance 1 is the grand formal visitor approach into Vraedhul's mountain.
- The other entrances approach Krauzeth's and Zaurmath's lairs.
- Dragons flew between peaks. Underground kobold tunnels connect them; there are no adopted direct surface bridges or corridors between the peaks.
- The offspring routes reach Vraedhul's mountain through this network, without requiring traversal of the unformed workshops.

The shared outer terrain is dangerous and inhabited. No exact approach map or set of outside encounters is selected.

### Vraedhul's main complex

Core surviving areas:

- Supplicant suites, increasingly elaborate for guests she favored or mocked.
- A throne or audience chamber.
- A huge personal lair, with favorite creations and the richest treasure.

Working arrangement of those areas:

                S       S
        S                    AA       LL
                             AA       LL
            S        S

S marks suites, A the audience chamber, and L the personal lair. This records the rough relationship, not exact room counts or shapes. The personal lair may be a vast cavern or a mountain-top pavilion; that choice is unresolved.

Individual suite passages connect to a shared antechamber. Kobold tunnels also connect to it. The adopted crawl direction is a short main route with optional suites and some cross-connections; the actual passages and relation of the shared viewpoint to the antechamber still need mapping.

## Forces and motives

### The bound Arborean maker

The selected guardian concept is a being from Arborea that cultivated magical wonders. Vraedhul bound a creature not naturally her ally, finding the forced service useful and amusing. A captive maker used to collect tribute and enforce her cruel admission standards is the central contrast.

The maker supplies the social element at the formal entrance. It is associated with the grandiose representation of Vraedhul: the working image is a colossal likeness with eyes that examine, a claw that handles offerings, and a mouth that speaks and controls entry. Exact anatomy and the maker's physical integration remain to be constructed.

Limbo has changed the captive so that three incompatible versions coexist:

| Version | What it believes |
| --- | --- |
| In Arborea | It is back in its own realm. |
| Truth-aware | It recognizes its actual predicament and the present ruin. |
| Vraedhul's guardian | It is still serving as Vraedhul's gate guardian. |

These beliefs are adopted. Exact temperament, motives, visible signs, transitions, and what the truth-aware version witnessed remain open. The earlier proposal of an aspiring new self or a version that genuinely values Vraedhul's authority is not a replacement for these three versions.

The party can deliberately influence which version comes forward through actions, what they present, and where they direct the maker's attention. This provides a social challenge and a first example of the recurring attention principle.

An appropriate valuable gift should still make admission fairly straightforward. The NPC is not a requirement to satisfy three independent judges. Loosening its binding is an optional way through, involving cooperation and practical interference with the entrance's defenses. The precise act, difficulty, and extent of release remain undecided.

Its name, gender, exact creature type or stat block, origin, and former creations are not selected. A djinni was considered and then replaced by the Arborean-maker concept; a deva is not an adopted species assignment.

### Loyalist kobolds at Krauzeth's entrance

Loyalist kobolds remain in or occupy the old lair. A partially tamed creature and its kobold watch form the working outer defense. Alarm traps may supplement it, but exact traps are not chosen.

A warning prompts an organized retreat into the kobold network and an attempt to seal the passage behind them. They have not spent three months continuously destroying the entrance. Some delay intruders while others undertake the triggered destruction.

Their exact loyalty, leaders, knowledge of the PCs and Krauzeth's death, and relations to named earlier kobolds remain open. No existing named faction member has been assigned to these defenders.

### Outside inhabitants

The outside should contain chaos, elementals, and other dangers. Some may be remnants of Vraedhul's servants and others may be uncontrolled inhabitants of the changed landscape. No particular creature roster or encounter has been adopted.

## Setting rules and constraints

- Attention can stabilize one existing possibility within a Limbo-altered creation. It can affect form or function, but does not grant unrestricted creation or world reshaping.
- Opportunities to shape events through will are primarily mediated through Vraedhul's creations.
- Transformation should remain learnable. Clear warning signs, recognizable terrain, and useful observation support informed decisions.
- Stable landmarks and coherent passages coexist with mutable areas and unformed zones.
- Larger deterioration matters across delays and absences; the adventure should allow patient exploration during a visit.
- Binding still holds the maker after Vraedhul's death. An anchor in the entrance rather than her living will is a possible explanation, not a finalized mechanism.
- Dangerous and cursed magic is part of Vraedhul's legacy. Lasting effects imposed on PCs must have a clear path to removal.
- No particular outside object or cache is available as useful tribute.
- No continuing scavenger, magical feeding process, or portal-fuel economy is part of the current background.

## Design ingredients

### Formal entrance

An intimidating manifestation of Vraedhul's authority, with a dangerous captive behind or within it. The Wizard of Oz influence is theatrical scale, voice, and representation. The creation has real power.

A mouth, claw, and eye can be coordinated parts of one entrance encounter. Specific effects, body vulnerabilities, binding anchors, and routes behind the spectacle remain construction questions.

### Krauzeth's entrance

A combat-focused route whose outer warning changes the inner defenders' preparedness and progress in sealing the passage. Preserving access, rather than merely killing defenders, matters.

### Zaurmath's entrance

One substantial ruined chamber with a visible onward tunnel and a gradient toward an unforming breach on one side:

- Relatively stable edges permit observation and investigation.
- Changing ground supplies the main traversal hazards, with recognizable precursors and recoverable mistakes.
- The unforming breach is unmistakably more dangerous and can be avoided entirely.

Possible crossings use outer ledges, with climbing and exposed gaps, or a more direct route through changing material. Skills, equipment, and suitable magic should genuinely help. Exact physical transformations and tells are not yet fixed.

### Shared discovery and later knowledge

The clear view confirms death and a Limbo eruption. Consequences and details are reserved for further exploration. Attention and existing possibilities recur through the creations; their specific applications in guest rooms and the later capstone are unresolved.

## Questions, conflicts, and provisional ideas

### Source boundaries

The original [[Dragon Arc - Backstory]] leaves death uncertain and offers several possible causes. This adventure adopts that a Limbo eruption killed Vraedhul and that Zaurmath died defending her. The exact event sequence remains open; the old suggestion of a predator or scavenger causing the destruction is not adopted.

[[Arborea]] establishes wild growth, not a general species of magical craft spirits. This maker, its captivity, and its changed perspectives are adventure-specific worldbuilding. Its relationship to deva metaphysics is optional and unresolved.

Later Dunmari experiences and DR 1727 research provide DM inspiration rather than automatically available DR 1720 testimony or objects.

### Rejected proposal

The earlier elemental proposal is rejected and superseded by the bound Arborean maker as the formal entrance guardian. The stair-maintaining elemental and associated relief encounter are not retained as outside options. The general intention to include elementals and other dangers in the landscape remains; no specific outside encounter is selected.

### Unchosen construction examples

- Specific maker forms such as branches confined in gold, amber eyes, living metal flowers, enchanted resins, bone, or living gemstones are illustrative.
- Specific attention cues such as showing tribute, engaging with its creations, or examining the binding are promising examples; no fixed trigger list or password mechanic is adopted.
- Gold around wrists, a heart or gem anchor, and physical maintenance mechanisms are possible binding details, not fixed facts.
- Specific Zaurmath-route warnings such as a wet sheen before stone liquefies, or dust gathering before air hardens, remain examples to construct and calibrate.
- A viewing crystal or device that must be solved before recognizing the remains is not an adopted extra obstacle.
- A personal-lair cavern versus mountain-top pavilion is unresolved.
- Simultaneously coordinating several creations in the audience chamber was proposed but deferred without adoption.
