# Five Went East

## At a glance

This is a one-session adventure for three level-nine PCs traveling from [[Tyrwingha]] toward [[Orenlas]] through the wild [[Plaguelands]]. There is no road. Soon after leaving the Tyrwinghan foothills, the party encounters the survivors of a five-person [[Rangers|Ranger]] embassy that sought advice and aid from Orenlas after a high tide of [[Limbo]].

Two Rangers apparently escaped an unstable overlap containing fragments of the embassy's journey. Three are believed trapped inside. One of the apparent survivors is actually a creature from the [[Far Realms]], related in some fashion to the [[Ithilids]]. It murdered one Ranger, copied another, and maintains a one-way psychic connection to the living original. That connection gives it access to the original's memories.

The infiltrator wants sincere companions to carry it through the protections around Orenlas. It begins as an eager guide, helps rescue the first two Rangers, and becomes a protective skeptic when the party prepares to seek the third. If the party believes it and turns back, their honest trust gives it a strong chance of entering Orenlas with them.

### Goal

Enter the overlap, recover the three Rangers believed trapped there, and bring the survivors out.

### Best outcome

The PCs rescue Bryn, Eira, and the real Taliesin; expose or defeat the infiltrator; escape the collapsing overlap; and receive both a reliable entrance into Orenlas and the location of [[Cedric Cyfenwid|Cedric Cyfenwid's]] nearby tomb or memorial.

### Adventure flow

1. Defend the two apparent survivors and their anchors.
2. Receive the mission and enter the overlap with false Taliesin.
3. Rescue Bryn from the ravine and its three-moment predator.
4. Rescue Eira from the shelter beneath glass rain.
5. Decide whether to abandon the third Ranger or enter the final fragment.
6. Find Maelona's body and two Taliesins; reveal the infiltrator.
7. Defeat or contain the escaping infiltrator, free Taliesin, and flee the collapsing overlap.

## The five Rangers

| Ranger                                 | Fate                                     | Role                           | Key skills                                             | Contribution                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Arianwen** (ah-ree-AHN-wen), she/her | Genuine escapee, human                   | Expedition captain             | Survival, Insight, Persuasion, leadership              | Holds the outer entrance steady and provides the final beacon                      |
| **Bryn** (brin), he/him                | First trapped Ranger, human              | Plaguelands scout              | Survival, Perception, Stealth, archery, Ranger signs   | Finds the second fragment and guides the party through folding distance            |
| **Eira** (AY-rah), she/her             | Second trapped Ranger, elf               | Emissary and healer            | Medicine, Persuasion, Elvish, history, Orenlas customs | Stabilizes the retreat and explains how sincere welcome affects entry into Orenlas |
| **Maelona** (MY-loh-nah), she/her      | Murdered Ranger, lizardfolk              | Planar researcher and recorder | Arcana, Investigation, cartography, ritual lore        | Her body, notes, and possible testimony provide evidence against the infiltrator   |
| **Taliesin** (tal-YES-in), he/him      | Trapped original; identity copied, human | Feywise pathfinder             | Nature, Survival, fey lore, mental discipline          | Knows the correct Orenlas approach and the location of Cedric's tomb               |

### Arianwen

Arianwen is calm, direct, and accustomed to creating agreement without demanding obedience. She coordinated the shared concentration that allowed anyone to escape the overlap. Doing so left her mentally exhausted; returning would introduce a competing, unstable will and probably kill her.

At the table, use Arianwen to establish the five-person accounting, describe each Ranger's role, and provide a trustworthy baseline. She recognizes that false Taliesin is more eager and demonstrative than usual but initially attributes this to fear and relief.

### Bryn

Bryn is terse, observant, and practical. He trusts physical evidence more readily than intuition or theory. He occasionally worked with Taliesin but did not know him intimately.

After being rescued, Bryn remembers that Taliesin was still behind him with Maelona when the expedition separated. False Taliesin claims that they separated later and that he escaped alone.

### Eira

Eira is patient, diplomatic, and good at keeping frightened people focused on immediate needs. She carries the embassy's formal tokens and understands the customs surrounding entry into Orenlas.

Eira remembers Taliesin assuming responsibility for the expedition's central point of stability. She did not see him escape but accepts that it could have happened after she became trapped. Once suspicious, she supports evidence-based magical testing.

### Maelona

Maelona documented the lingering effects of the Limbo tide so that Orenlas could advise Tyrwingha from useful evidence. She realized that something was wrong during the initial disaster, and the infiltrator killed her before escaping as Taliesin.

Her intact notes record the expedition's movements immediately before the separation. If the PCs can question her corpse, she remembers being attacked by something wearing or forming Taliesin's face, but she cannot explain its nature.

### Taliesin

Taliesin is cautious, restrained, and deeply familiar with the fey-touched borderlands. His strong mental discipline made him the final anchor holding the overlap together. He cannot perceive the infiltrator's one-way psychic connection.

Taliesin had a secondary instruction to check the condition of Cedric Cyfenwid's tomb or memorial. He knows its location and can take the party there after the rescue. The infiltrator can read this knowledge but will not volunteer it.

### False Taliesin

The infiltrator reproduces Taliesin's appearance, voice, memories, and surface habits through its psychic connection. It does not reproduce his temperament perfectly. It performs what it thinks a loyal Ranger should look like, making it braver, warmer, and more eager than the real Taliesin.

Its behavior progresses as follows:

1. **Eager guide:** Takes risks, protects the survivors, and pushes for the first rescues.
2. **Helpful companion:** Uses stolen knowledge to navigate and earn trust.
3. **Protective skeptic:** Emphasizes instability and argues that continuing will kill the Rangers already saved.
4. **Reversed accusation:** Claims the real Taliesin is the duplicate that murdered Maelona.
5. **Escape:** Once decisively exposed, abandons persuasion and tries to flee the overlap.

## Clue ladder

Do not require every clue. The confrontation should confirm or reveal a likely answer rather than function as a difficult locked puzzle.

1. False Taliesin behaves with unusual eagerness compared with Arianwen's description.
2. In the first fragment, he predicts where the predator's next manifestation will appear. Only the trapped Taliesin has observed enough repetitions to know this.
3. Bryn remembers leaving Taliesin behind with Maelona.
4. In the second fragment, false Taliesin anticipates a transformation that occurred after his claimed escape.
5. His attempts to “help” stabilize Eira's shelter introduce contradictory mental images and make it less stable.
6. Eira remembers Taliesin taking responsibility for the central anchor and never saw him relinquish it.
7. After Bryn and Eira are removed, one living will still unmistakably anchors a deeper fragment.
8. Maelona's wounds indicate deliberate psychic violence rather than random transformation.
9. Maelona's notes or corpse place Taliesin at the attack, but also indicate that another presence was involved.
10. Direct truth-telling, mind-reading, divination, or another sensible level-nine magical test can reveal the infiltrator. Do not add a special immunity to defeat a good plan.

Personal-memory questions alone do not work because the infiltrator reads Taliesin's answers as he formulates them. The infiltrator uses that fact to reverse the accusation: “It read my mind, copied my shape, and killed Maelona. Now it wants you to carry it out.”

## Scene 1: The attacked shelter

**Purpose:** Begin with action, establish Arianwen and false Taliesin, and make the infiltrator appear brave and useful.

**Opening:** In broken country beyond the Tyrwinghan foothills, the PCs see flashes of reflected light coming from a shallow hollow. Three small arrangements of Ranger equipment hold the hollow in a consistent shape. Two distorted predators are attacking the position.

**Situation:** Arianwen is incapacitated at the center. False Taliesin is defending her and the anchors. The predators are attracted to the stable matter and attack the anchors when no creature obstructs them.

### Folded scavengers

Use two folded scavengers.

- **AC:** 16
- **HP:** 38 each
- **Speed:** 40 feet
- **Attack:** +6 to hit
- **Multiattack:** Two claws, each dealing 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage
- **Folded Step (Recharge 5–6):** As a bonus action, teleport up to 20 feet between two visible rocks, shadows, or pieces of broken terrain.
- **Cornered Snap:** A scavenger adjacent to an anchor can replace one claw with a bite against it, dealing 10 damage automatically.

Each anchor has AC 13, 15 HP, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. A scavenger attacks a PC who threatens it; otherwise it tries to destroy an anchor.

If one anchor is destroyed, Arianwen can restore it after the fight. If two are destroyed, she must devote all her remaining concentration to repairing the entrance and cannot grant her advantage during the final escape.

False Taliesin attacks for 7 damage or uses the Help action each round. Once during the fight, he intercepts an attack against Arianwen or an anchor. This is sincere self-preservation as well as calculated heroism: he needs Arianwen's belief.

**Ends when:** The scavengers are defeated or driven away and Arianwen is safe.

## Scene 2: The briefing and entrance

**Purpose:** Give the PCs a finishable rescue goal and establish the apparent accounting.

**Situation:** Arianwen explains that five Rangers left Tyrwingha, two escaped, and three remain. The embassy was seeking advice and assistance from powerful Orenlas after the Limbo high tide. False Taliesin eagerly volunteers to guide the PCs back.

Arianwen cannot safely reenter. She remains outside and fixes the entrance in her mind. She gives the PCs a personal description or identifying object for Bryn, Eira, and Maelona.

**Entering:** The entrance appears when the party agrees upon the Rangers' original intended direction and follows the same unremarkable trail sign. If they disagree about where “forward” lies, the landscape returns them to the shelter.

**Ends when:** The PCs and false Taliesin enter the first fragment.

## Scene 3: The ravine of returning footsteps

**Purpose:** Rescue Bryn, establish the overlap's rules, present the first substantial clue, and give the PCs momentum.

**Situation:** A shallow ravine folds back upon itself. Ranger signs appear in the wrong chronological order. Bryn repeatedly completes the same scouting circuit but cannot reach the group. A Plaguelands predator occupies three moments of its attack simultaneously.

### Three-Moment Stalker

Place three identical tokens near a split boulder, a dead tree, and a Ranger cairn.

- **Large monstrosity**
- **AC:** 16
- **HP:** 114
- **Speed:** 50 feet, climb 30 feet
- **STR:** +4; **DEX:** +4; **CON:** +3
- **Saves:** Dex +7, Con +6, Wis +5
- **Skills:** Perception +5, Stealth +7
- **Attack bonus:** +7
- **Save DC:** 15

#### Three Moments

The manifestations share hit points, saves, and limited abilities. They act separately on initiative 18, 12, and 6. Each can move and take one action.

A single area effect damages the stalker only once; use the highest damage suffered by a manifestation. Conditions affect only the targeted manifestation. An incapacitated manifestation disappears until the end of its next turn. The creature has one reaction per round shared between all manifestations.

#### Collapsing Timeline

- At 75 HP, remove one manifestation and its turn.
- At 37 HP, remove a second manifestation and its turn.
- At 0 HP, the moments collapse into one dead or dying animal.

#### Actions

**Raking Claw.** +7 to hit, reach 5 feet; 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

**Crushing Bite.** +7 to hit, reach 5 feet; 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage. Use only against a prone creature.

#### Folded Pounce

Once per round, when a manifestation hits someone already hit by another manifestation that round, add 7 (2d6) force damage. The target must make a DC 15 Strength save or fall prone.

#### Slip Aside

Once per round when a manifestation is hit, the stalker can use its shared reaction to move a different manifestation up to 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The triggering damage still applies.

### Stabilizing the ravine

A PC adjacent to one of the three landmarks can use an action to make a DC 15 Arcana, Investigation, or Survival check. On a success, the associated manifestation cannot use Slip Aside and has half speed until the next round.

Three total landmark successes reveal Bryn's true position. Defeating the stalker also holds the ravine still long enough to reach him.

Before a manifestation appears at the split stone, false Taliesin accurately predicts its arrival. The real Taliesin learned this pattern while experiencing the fragments from the central anchor; an apparent escapee should not know it.

**Success:** Bryn joins the group and can identify the route into the second fragment.

**Cost:** If the PCs fail to stabilize the landmarks, reaching Bryn after the fight costs time and exposes each PC to 7 (2d6) force damage.

**Ends when:** Bryn is removed from his repeating circuit.

## Scene 4: Shelter beneath glass rain

**Purpose:** Rescue Eira, make earlier success useful, allow the infiltrator to sabotage the party, and create the decision to continue or retreat.

**Situation:** Eira maintains a small stable shelter while glass droplets hang above it. Beyond the shelter, earth, vegetation, air, and flame continually exchange forms. Releasing Eira removes another stabilizing will and accelerates the collapse.

### Challenge tracks

- **Progress:** Begins at 0; rescue succeeds at 6.
- **Stability:** Begins at 5; maximum 6.
- **Collapse:** Mark each time Stability reaches 0.

| Progress | Stage                  | Objective                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–1      | Cross the glass rain   | Reach Eira's stable circle                       |
| 2–3      | Release the anchor     | Take over the concentration holding her in place |
| 4–5      | Reopen the return path | Carry Eira out before the shelter closes         |
| 6        | Complete               | Everyone reaches temporary safety                |

Each PC gets one significant challenge action per round. A suitable action against DC 15 adds 1 Progress. Failure removes 1 Stability. Repeating the same approach without changing the situation raises the DC to 17.

A PC can instead make a suitable DC 15 check to restore 1 Stability. Useful skills include Arcana, Athletics, Acrobatics, Insight, Investigation, Performance, Persuasion, Survival, and relevant tools. A well-matched spell can grant automatic Progress, bypass a stage, restore Stability, or prevent that round's hazard. Expensive magic should produce a significant result.

At the end of every round, reduce Stability by 1 and resolve the environmental event.

### Environmental events

**Round 1 — Glass Rain:** Anyone outside firm shelter makes a DC 15 Dexterity save, taking 10 (3d6) slashing damage on a failure or half on a success.

**Round 2 — Solidifying Air:** Each PC makes a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity save. On a failure, the PC is restrained. The PC or an adjacent ally can use an action and make a DC 15 Athletics or Arcana check to free them. A restrained PC can attempt an action that does not require movement with disadvantage.

**Round 3 — Distance Shear:** Each PC makes a DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure, the PC becomes separated. Their next challenge action cannot add Progress unless it addresses navigation, communication, or reunion. One successful group-oriented action can recover everyone separated this way.

**Round 4 and later — The Shelter Forgets:** Choose glass rain, solidifying air, distance shear, or burning ground. Burning ground requires a DC 15 Dexterity save and deals 10 (3d6) fire damage on a failure or half on a success. Environmental damage becomes 14 (4d6) after the first Collapse.

### False Taliesin's sabotage

False Taliesin offers to help stabilize the shelter.

**First sabotage:** At the end of the first round, remove 1 additional Stability. A perceptive PC notices that the shelter lurched immediately after Taliesin's adjustment. He claims his attempt failed because the fragment is unusually unstable.

**Second sabotage:** If nobody watches or stops him, remove 1 additional Stability at the end of the next round. A PC examining him can make a DC 14 Arcana, Insight, or Investigation check. Success reveals that he deliberately introduced a contradictory shape or direction. Failure reveals that his method was clearly wrong but leaves intent uncertain. A PC explicitly watching him makes this check before Stability is lost and prevents the sabotage on a success.

Once challenged, false Taliesin stops overt sabotage and begins emphasizing that the overlap is becoming deadly.

### Collapse

When Stability reaches 0, everyone makes a DC 15 Constitution or Wisdom save, taking 14 (4d6) mixed force and elemental damage on a failure or half on a success. Eira suffers one Collapse Wound, Stability resets to 2, and play continues.

After the first Collapse, Eira is seriously injured. At the second Collapse, she begins dissolving into the fragment. Saving her requires an immediate major intervention: powerful healing or protection, accepting her damage, or sacrificing 2 Progress. Without intervention, she dies or becomes irretrievable.

At 4 Progress, Eira is released from the anchor. At the end of each subsequent round, she either restores 1 Stability or grants advantage to one challenge action.

**Bryn's assistance:** Once per stage, Bryn can grant advantage on a check involving navigation, movement, or Ranger signs.

**Ends when:** The party reaches 6 Progress or abandons Eira.

## Scene 5: The choice after the second fragment

**Purpose:** Give the infiltrator its best chance to win.

Removing Eira does not end the overlap. One living will still unmistakably anchors a deeper fragment. False Taliesin argues that continuing is irresponsible:

> “We nearly lost Eira. The next collapse will kill someone. Maelona would tell us not to trade four lives for hers. We have to turn back.”

He tells the truth about the danger while concealing the real reason for his fear. If the PCs detected sabotage or impossible knowledge, this reversal should look suspicious. If they did not, it can sound like a brave Ranger finally accepting reality.

### If the PCs leave

They return with Bryn and Eira and report Maelona missing. Arianwen and the rescued Rangers sincerely accept false Taliesin. Their collective belief gives him a high chance of passing Orenlas's protections. The party gains some guidance toward the border but does not receive the location of Cedric's tomb. Taliesin remains trapped.

### If the PCs continue

Bryn identifies a path opened by the remaining anchor. False Taliesin accompanies the party because leaving alone would expose him and cost him their belief. His deception is now unlikely to survive close investigation.

## Scene 6: The fifth Ranger

**Purpose:** Reveal the truth and allow the PCs to resolve it without an artificially difficult identity puzzle.

**Situation:** Maelona's body lies near the entrance to a stark island of stable ground. The real Taliesin sits at its center, exhausted but alive. On seeing him, false Taliesin immediately reverses the accusation:

> “That creature killed Maelona, read my mind, and made itself look like me. Do not let it leave.”

Both Taliesins know the same personal memories. Use the accumulated clues, Maelona's evidence, Ranger testimony, and any reasonable magic. Truth magic works normally. The infiltrator may resist according to the spell's ordinary rules, refuse to answer, or use technically true statements, but it has no special immunity.

Once decisively exposed, the infiltrator attacks to escape. If the party remains genuinely uncertain, continue the confrontation until their actions force it to commit or they choose whom to abandon.

### Far Realm infiltrator

- **Medium aberration**
- **AC:** 16
- **HP:** 95
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Saves:** Int +8, Wis +7, Cha +8
- **Skills:** Deception +9, Insight +7, Stealth +6
- **Resistance:** Psychic damage
- **Attack bonus:** +8
- **Save DC:** 16

**Multiattack:** Two Psychic Lash attacks.

**Psychic Lash:** Ranged or melee spell attack, +8 to hit, range 60 feet; 13 (2d8 + 4) psychic damage.

**Mind Shear (Recharge 5–6):** Creatures in a 30-foot cone make a DC 16 Intelligence save. Failure deals 18 (4d8) psychic damage and removes reactions until the end of the creature's next turn. Success halves the damage.

**Folded Step:** As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to a visible space adjacent to an unstable surface or fragment boundary.

**Borrowed Warning:** As a reaction when attacked, add 3 to AC against that attack. Describe the infiltrator using a stolen memory of the attacker's habits or a warning pulled from Taliesin's mind.

The infiltrator has no legendary resistance and cannot automatically teleport away when injured.

### Escape objective

Mark the remembered exit from the fragment. The infiltrator must reach it and use an action to align itself with the stolen route. This requires concentration until the beginning of its next turn. If concentration holds, the exit opens and it can cross using its movement.

The PCs know when it begins opening the exit. Grappling, incapacitating, breaking concentration, blocking the exit, or freeing Taliesin can stop it.

If Taliesin is freed during the fight, the overlap starts collapsing. The infiltrator's exit closes at the end of the following round. This gives the party a dangerous but effective way to trap it.

If reduced to 0 HP, the infiltrator is killed or can be captured with nonlethal damage. Its Ranger shape fails visibly. If it escapes, it is expelled into the western Plaguelands without anyone's trust and has lost its route through Orenlas. Its central plan has still failed.

**Ends when:** The infiltrator is defeated, captured, or escapes; and the PCs decide whether to free Taliesin.

## Scene 7: The unmaking

**Purpose:** Provide the physical climax after the identity reveal and make every rescued Ranger contribute.

Freeing Taliesin collapses the overlap. Run three rapid obstacles. For each obstacle, ask each PC how they contribute. Two successful DC 15 checks complete an obstacle; two failures first impose its listed cost. Appropriate magic can provide an automatic success or cancel a failure.

### 1. The shelter shatters

Glass rain, hardening air, and burning ground occur simultaneously. Eira provides one automatic success if alive and conscious.

**Failure cost:** Each PC takes 10 (3d6) mixed elemental damage, and one injured Ranger needs to be carried.

### 2. The ravine folds closed

The exit appears in several directions at once. Bryn provides one automatic success if alive and conscious.

**Failure cost:** The group becomes separated. Recovering everyone consumes precious time; one carried body, piece of evidence, or expendable item must be abandoned unless a PC spends a significant magical resource.

### 3. Find Arianwen

The party must fix the genuine escapee in their shared thoughts and move toward her. If at least two of the original shelter anchors survived, everyone has advantage on their first check.

**Failure cost:** A final wave deals 14 (4d6) force damage to each PC. A Ranger who already suffered a Collapse Wound is in mortal danger unless protected.

**Ends when:** The party emerges beside Arianwen and the overlap dissolves behind them.

## Resolution

### Complete success

Bryn, Eira, and Taliesin survive; the infiltrator is exposed; and the party returns with Maelona's remains or reliable evidence of her death. Eira and Taliesin can identify the correct approach to Orenlas and secure a legitimate welcome. Taliesin tells the PCs about Cedric Cyfenwid's nearby tomb or memorial.

The tomb is safe. Visiting it is a short narrative and roleplaying scene that can reveal Cedric's history without starting another adventure.

### Costly success

The infiltrator is exposed, but one or more Rangers die or are lost. The party reaches Orenlas with incomplete testimony, fewer guides, or only part of the information about Cedric.

### Visible partial success and hidden failure

The party leaves after rescuing Bryn and Eira. They believe false Taliesin and help carry him through Orenlas's protections. Taliesin remains trapped and Maelona's murder is undiscovered. The infiltrator's presence creates a later Orenlas adventure.

### Severe failure

Multiple Rangers die, the party loses the external beacon, or the infiltrator enters Orenlas after the rescue collapses into confusion. Reaching Orenlas then requires finding another concealed route or earning aid without the embassy.

## Pacing

- Attacked shelter and briefing: 25–35 minutes
- Ravine and stalker: 35–50 minutes
- Glass-rain rescue: 45–60 minutes
- Decision and final fragment: 30–45 minutes
- Infiltrator fight, collapse, and resolution: 45–60 minutes

If time runs short, reduce the stalker's HP to 90, complete each glass-rain stage with one success, or run each escape obstacle as one group check. The Cedric scene can occur at the beginning of the next session without weakening this adventure's resolution.
