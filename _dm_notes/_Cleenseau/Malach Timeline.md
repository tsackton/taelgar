Malach, the lich king. 

Malach was born in 1520, although his given name was not Malach (his true name is lost to history). He was born the son of a struggling leatherworker in the Free City of Tollen. He was quick witted, devious, and convinced above all else of his own greatness. Although much has been lost of his early life, by age 10 his father was deeply in debt, and Malach was working as a household indentured servant to try to pay his father’s debts.  

Little is known of the next decade of his life, until he was admitted to the University in Tollen in the years leading up to the Great War. He was expelled after two years for dabbling in forbidden magic, but several powerful patrons he had impressed helped to prevent harsher consequences. Little is known of his activities during the Great War, except that his patrons mysteriously died, and records of his whereabouts, including magical ones, vanished. 

Malach does not surface again in the annals of Sembara until he was revealed as the lich king who had replaced Robert I.

What was he doing during this lost time? 

During the Great War he learned much, never a reliable ally of Cha’Mutte or other dark forces, but always aiding those who would help him learn more about magic, especially the ancient and powerful magic of the elves. He was especially drawn to secrets of the dead, and dark places beneath the earth.

Malach emerged from the Great War with a belief that immortality was possible, and was his sacred right. He labored in secret, in a abandoned dwarven city under the Sentinel Range, which he fortified. Amongst the chaos of the Blood Years, he lurked brewing potions to prolong his life and occasionally sending spies to Sembara, while he hunted for the secret to eternal youth, which he believed was to be found in chalyte.  

In 1599, during the Great Purge, his fortress was destroyed by an Sembaran army lead by Cece’s daughter, Elleth. Although Malach escaped, and in fact managed to hide his existence, 60 years of research, crafting, and magical dedication was destroyed. In that moment, Malach gave up his quest for eternal youth, and instead vowed to destroy the House of Sewick, slowly, and terribly.

  

But his potions destroyed, and his research lost, Malach was an old wizard, magically much younger than he seemed, but still… to truly punish Cece and the House of Sewick, he felt he needed more. He recalled ancient necromantic rituals he had studied in the secret libraries of Tollen [ not sure this really makes sense, but perhaps there is a secret hidden guild of necromancers or something like that in Tollen, or perhaps it was lost lore, not sure ] and, after he ensured the death of Elleth, he set about preserving his life force forever. No longer interested in an eternal youth, he allowed his body to be corrupted and started the journey to lichhood.

  

Of course it is easier said than done… and he delved deep in the mountains, seeking information, magical artifacts, etc. There he met and formed an alliance with a group of mind flayers (see below). 

  

A dozen years later, he emerged from hiding and slowly and carefully, he ensured the death’s of each of Cece’s children. By 1622, Robert I and Elaine, an infant, were the only ones surviving.**
