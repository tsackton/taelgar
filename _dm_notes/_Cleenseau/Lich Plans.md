Trying to detail this a bit more... curious what you think. I still don't have the specific motivation fully worked out but I don't need it right now.

  

His goal is basically to have Sembara destroy itself - that is, he doesn't want to _invade_, that is too risky, he wants to engineer a civil war and split duchy from duchy. Given the background we discussed, i.e with the Dukes being quite independent it seems plausible that it could work.

  

His ultimate goal is to break Sembara into multiple warring factions, one of which at least in turn goes to war with Tyrwingha. If he can engineer a 10 year bloody civil war, that would be ideal. Obviously this won't happen, given it is set in stone that a group of adventurers will "kill" him in summer 1720 before anything too awful happens. 

  

In particular, if Sembara splits into several warring sets of duchies, and then one of those warring sets of duchies attacks Tyrwingha, he is hoping that Elaine (current queen of Tyrwingha, heir to throne of Sembara) will never be able to take the throne and with the succession in doubt, the warring dukes will get worse and worse.

  

How does he think he can do it?

  

His plan is basically:

  

1. Ensure Robert I has no heirs basically by assassinating them all, and then assuming Robert's position to make sure he can control events. 

2. Destabilization relationships with highland kingdoms and Tollen, to make sure they don't come to Sembara's aid as much as possible

3. Create a massive wave of undead across Sembara, focused on Wisford

4. Mismanage the responsible badly, and try to manipulate at least one or two dukes into defying him during this mismanagement (focused on the Duke of Wisford)

5. Charge them with treason and hope they don't accept it and push back. (early March)

6. With luck, 2 dukes are now defying the crown, potentially in some type of open warfare or semi-open warfare. Crack down on them and their districts in an unusually brutal way, which hopefully creates a backlash, and so on (April - dies towards end)

7. Create some kind of problem with demons or devils or something (not sure what), and semi-publically get help from Umbraeth

8. Engineer a situation where he can try to force Tyrwingha to let Umbraeth "invade" (i.e. chasing some demon or something)

9. When they refuse, invade Tyrwingha 

  

The point of 78/9 is basically to try to create a situation where the populace is willing to invade Tyrwingha because (at least some of them) think Tyrwingha is unreasonably against their protector, Umbraeth.

  

The lich is going to die shortly after step 5, so the latter part of it won't ever actually happen. But I want Umbraeth to have some idea of what was going on and know stuff about the lich to set up going to the Feywild for more info later. 

  

But that part I am not making up right now. My focus is on the undead wave and mismanaging the response. The idea is:

  
1. The lich controls 5 key people: the Baroness of Aveil, the Duke of Arnsbury, and the generals of the Army of the South, West, and North.

2. He has spent the last 6-8 months destabilizing things. Making sure key border lords are distracted, messing with the army, pissing off allies, etc.

3. He had access to some sort of powerful spell or magic item or wish that allowed him to cause some percent of dead bodies across Sembara to turn into undead and rise from the grave. This doesn't work perfectly, i.e. he can't just make every dead person become undead, and it works much much better on the unburied dead, in particular battlefields and even more particularly places where Sembara _lost_ so there are large numbers of dead Sembarans. It is also somewhat random, i.e. in one village it might do nothing, or just have one or two zombies appear, in some other village 60% of the dead will arise (but this is much rarer, the almost nothing is much more common).

4. This is mostly focused on the broad area between the Wistel and Enst, and it completely spares Embry, but some scattered impacts elsewhere

5. This creates two effects: widespread fear and panic, as almost everyone, especially in the south sees _some_ dead rising, and then a couple of clusters of "big groups" of undead

6. Eventually the big groups of undead all get defeated, and the King announces this was because the Night Queen had withdrawn her favor from the realm and he claims that a group of the elders of the Night Queen were working with a cabal of necromancers from Zimka and Skaer barbarians to conquer the realm, and this there first step. He in particular tries to get a lot of people upset at Zimka/highlanders. He also announces that he has foreseen this will keep happening every new moon until he stops the elders and their Zimka allies

8. He announces a big wealth tax but intentionally mismanages collecting it (i.e. stealing from halflings as much as possible, pissing off lizardfolk, etc - this isn't perfect because plenty of people wouldn't follow through, but he tries). 

9. He uses the last bits of the powerful item from step 3 to generate a second, much smaller wave, on the next new moon, and then shortly afterward announces that someone in Telham is working the Tollenders  and has his ally, the Duke of Arnsbury, lead the charge against Telham in court/public opinion. At the same time, he is probably secretly encouraging Tollen and Tollender-aligned factions to spread proof of the Duke's innocence. Potentially he even launches the Army of the North against Tollen, not sure what you think about a Battle of Something between Tollen and Sembara in 1720.

10. In the meantime he has (intentionally) pretty much destroyed the Army of the South, and the Duke of Wisford is struggling to protect his duchy, which the king is ignoring, from the 2nd wave of undead

  

So at some point in the spring, the Duchies of Telham and Wisford are like powderkegs, the Duke of Telham under suspicion, that no one in Telham really believes, of being behind the attacks and the Duke of Wisford furious with the loss of life in his duchy and the lack of attention paid - the lich engineers a crisis where he arrests them both, and hopes that they fight him rather than going meekly to their deaths. Both of them will, and by April or May Telham and Wisford are effectively in open revolt.

  

He starts raising an Army of the Interior to put down this nonsense, and starts threatening the barons within those duchies, and the other dukes, and so on - but before he gets very far some group of adventures kills him.
