Thyron
He lounges in the branches above the trail as though gravity is optional.
A shaggy mountain goat that walks places it absolutely shouldn't.
Telling stories that begin true and become increasingly impossible.
- Wry - Restless - Graceful - Mischievous - Unhurried

Thyron is tall and lean, with wind-tangled white hair falling to his shoulders, pale skin touched by the cold, and bright silver eyes that seem permanently amused by something only he can see.

**Clothing**  
He wears a weathered gray-green cloak fastened with a carved antler brooch over layers of soft wool and supple leather, his boots stained with mud and moss.

A small detail I like for him: despite lounging in trees and scrambling after his mountain goat, his clothes are never torn and never quite dirty, as though the forest itself refuses to inconvenience him.

 
Kyron
He moves with the confidence of someone who has never once been lost.
A broad-antlered elk
Challenging people to increasingly ridiculous dares.
- Bold Self-assured Boisterous Charismatic Reckless

**Appearance**  
Kyron has the same pale complexion, silver eyes, and white hair as his siblings, though his hair is worn loose and swept back from his face, giving him the look of someone perpetually striding into the next adventure before the current one has ended.

**Clothing**  
He wears a long hunter's coat of dark green wool trimmed with pale fur over sturdy leathers,, nothing is arranged carefully, as though every item was acquired on a whim and simply left where it landed.

A small detail I like for Kyron: he always seems to know exactly where he is, even in places that should be impossible to navigate, and speaks directions with such certainty that people often follow him before realizing they never asked where they were going.

Myron
They stare at things long enough to make others uncomfortable. 
A giant moth with silver-patterned wings.
Tossing herbs into the fire to change the color of the smoke.
- Quiet - Observant  Dreamlike Patient  Unsettling

**Appearance**  
Myron shares the white hair, pale skin, and silver eyes of their siblings, but carries them differently; their gaze lingers too long, their expression shifts too little, and they often seem more interested in something just beyond a person's shoulder than in the person themselves.

**Clothing**  
They wear layered robes and wraps of pale gray, moon-white, and faded blue, loose enough to drift with every breeze, embroidered with subtle patterns of leaves, moth wings, and curling mist. Small pouches of herbs, dried flowers, and curious seeds hang from cords at their waist, filling the air around them with faint unfamiliar scents.

A small detail I like for Myron: their giant silver-winged moth is rarely far away, and when they fall silent—which is often—the moth will sometimes settle nearby and stare at the same thing, making others wonder whether Myron noticed something or is simply waiting for something to happen.

Lyron
Her laughter arrives before she does
A peacock with too many tail feathers
Teaching dances nobody has ever seen before.

- Playful
- Charming
- Mercurial
- Expressive
- Irreverent

**Appearance**  
Lyron shares the pale skin, silver eyes, and white hair of her siblings, but wears hers in a loose tumble of braids and curls adorned with flowers, ribbons, and bits of shining glass; her smile seems to appear as naturally as sunlight through leaves, and her eyes rarely rest on one thing for long.

**Clothing**  
She dresses in layers of soft fabrics in pale blues, silvers, and woodland greens, with embroidered hems that catch the firelight when she moves. Bells, beads, feathers, and charms are woven into her clothing in seemingly random patterns, and she somehow manages to look both elegant and entirely unconcerned with practicality.

A small detail I like for Lyron: her peacock is almost as impossible as she is, its extravagant silver-and-blue tail trailing behind it like a royal cloak, and it has an infuriating habit of appearing in the middle of whatever performance, dance, or conversation Lyron is currently enjoying, as though determined to share the spotlight.
