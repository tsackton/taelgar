So what's the adventure leading up to that? I think that Orumei should ask the party to get the other Dukes on their side. 

A few things:
- Why would Orumei as this of the party as opposed to doing it themselves?
- How complicated should I make this?

In general I want this to be more interactive and not a dungeon crawl. I want to give people the opportunity to talk and develop their characters a bit. In Angry GM terms the X factor here is interaction, not combat/dungeon crawl. 

So let's say that the adventure is: 

Orumei is recruiting allies. For reasons, they cannot send djinn or air elementals to a particular Duke. Maybe missions have gone to both living Dukes - one promised to stay neutral but wouldn't risk troops in an attack on Sulmona, the other turned away djinn negotiators with some reason that means the party has a chance.
