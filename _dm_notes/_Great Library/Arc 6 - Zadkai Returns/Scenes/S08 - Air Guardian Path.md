# Air Guardian Path

Zadkai presence: One of Zadkai's patrols has defeated the air guardian and carries its key, the air gemheart, plus a second air gemheart. They are trapped in the air guardian path, however. 

## Entrance

The entrance to the air path simply requires one to step into the tapestry. This has its own set of risks, however! See below. 

## General Conditions

The air guardian's path is a large expansive sky, dotted with clouds. 

Anyone who cannot fly immediately starts falling when they enter this realm. They will not, however, fall forever. After about an hour of falling, will find themselves falling past their original location. But this is essentially impossible to detect, as there are no visible landmarks anywhere in the realm. The clouds seem to move: some flash with lightning, others seem dark and gray like rainclouds, still others float and blow as if caught in a fast wind. But, they don't stay still, and none appear to be close. 

Creatures fall 10 feet per second in this demiplane. 

Every entrant emerges from the same stationary ten-foot aperture, at the same orientation. The aperture remains traversable in both directions but is visually concealed from within by an illusion. A rope or arm can span it, and carefully anchored entrants can return without solving the later search problem. 


### Resting

There is very little solid ground anywhere in the air path, so resting is challenging. See below for details. 

## Layout

There was much more structure, originally. Zadkai's team destroyed the guardian, which make the clouds decohere and closed the portal.  Now, they are stuck and cannot find the exit. 

### Zadkai's Camp

Not actually a camp - they are just floating in space. 

One djinn, two air elementals using the aeromancer stat block, 10 air sparks

[Djinn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194971-djinni) (1) - no plane shift
[Air Elemental](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194864-aarakocra-aeromancer) (2)
[Air Sparks](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485765-air-spark) (10)

As soon as they spot anyone, they will approach. Won't necessarily immediately attack, but will be somewhat hostile. However, desperate for a way out, so will initially be willing to cooperate. 

Have the key, and two air gemhearts. 

#### Negotiation

The djinn will offer a gemheart in exchange for aid, both for a safe way out, but will never offer the key. However, if the party negotiates well, might be able to fight the djinn in the entrance chamber, not the air realm. Djinn will be honest and not attempt a surprise attack, preferring to escape and report to Zadkai. Djinn can use Wind Walk to aid travelers if desired and on good terms. 

### Exit

The exit from the air guardian realm is concealed by a nearly perfect illusion, that makes it essentially impossible to see the exit. The illusion is programmed to look like a door while anything is sticking through it that it can't model, and then slowly close itself to nothing. 

Two ways to discover; others possible. 

(1) Visual mismatch. If watching the right space, can see in the distance a cloud flashing with lightning, which has a brief delay in a narrow patch. 
- Always happens in the same place. 
- Patch shifts - it is close, not distant. Can be detected by two observers pointing at the discrepancy at the same time.
- This can lead to considering illusions, and testing. Physical objects thrown through the door will disappear for instance. 

(2) Auditory mismatch. Can still hear sounds from the other side when close. Potentially can work with echos, too, so can use a crude form of echolocation to pinpoint general location. Then, backt to the test-its-an-illusion framework. 

If they have a reasonable proposal that it is an illusion, allow a Study check against DC 21. 

Dispel Magic can work, but requires defeating an 8th level spell (DC 18)

Truesight reveals completely and automatically. 



## Treasure

**Air gemheart**:
A glowing white diamond, swirling with clouds and sky. As an action, can crack gem and breathe the air inside. Feel buoyant, lighter; effects last for 1 hour, and don't require attunement. Only one elemental gemheart can be active on you at a time; activating another ends the previous gemheart's effects.
- Gain a fly speed equal to twice your Speed, and can hover
- Resistant to lightning damage
- If you cast a spell that does elemental damage, you can change the damage type to Lightning
- Your lungs are always full of air, so you can hold your breath indefinitely. If the air is forced out of your lungs, such as by having the wind knocked out of you, you must take another breath of air to regain this benefit.
- As a Bonus Action, can cast Shocking Grasp using your choice of ability score as your casting stat. Its damage is fixed at 1d8 lightning + your ability modifier.
