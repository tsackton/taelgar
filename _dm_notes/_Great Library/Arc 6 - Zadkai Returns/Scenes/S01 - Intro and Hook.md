# Intro and Hook

**Three parts: basic intro/overview of the situation, do a little roleplaying/character intros, then have the roc show up with the hook.**

## Intro

Narrate the current situation and reintroduce the group to Taelgar. Sweep up the west coast, start with Dunmar, come around to Apporia and Chardon, then past Lake Valandros into the Northern Provinces. 

Situations to note:
Dunmar - highlight the Chardonian control of Darba (free port under Chardonian suzerainty), major chalyte operations, tons of ocean traffic, vibrant
Apporia - vibes, unchanged really but increased traffic through the canal maybe
Chardon - bustling, etc; no major changes. but narrate some of the fallout of the Fausto/Cleansed stuff
Lake Valandros - fortifications on the northern shore, tons of castles and forts, but this is not really a hot war right now
Voltara - oligarchy run by the [[Chalyte Oligarchs of Chardon]], civil war in the [[Auratan Family]] etc, they won't care about the details

The exact extent of Voltara control might not be obvious to the party. Dinia and Brelith and others have been working I think in the Suwi area, on the western slopes of the mountains and in the mountains. They carry out occasional guerilla harrasment to keep the Voltara people south of one of the rivers, but honestly Voltara probably doesn't care about them much - they have a solid port established and why chase down rebels to the north? 

The unclear vibe is how the rest of the world is responding, but the party probably has no way of knowing this so don't need to invent. 

Improvise as needed, sticking with the basic geopolitics of:
- Chardon is pouring all its money into Chataans and doesn't have a clear path to reconquest of Voltara, which was always far from the core anyway. However, it invests a lot of money into Lake Valandros and keeping "counterfeit" chalyte out of the market. 
- Voltara is a oligarchy ruled by the chalyte families (cf [[Chalyte Oligarchs of Chardon]]). This is a mercantile kingdom, not so much a "Cleansed/Cult of Apollyon". they use chalyte for armies but also are deeply invested in selling chalyte products, and probably pushing east to establish routes over the mountains. sell a lot at the port on the slate sea, too, but to where is not always clear because of chardon's control. so this is really more about economic warfare at this point. vibe is all about chalyte marks - voltara might forge chardonian marks, or sell unstamped/unlicensed chalyte, etc. 
- The "rebels" are mostly along the slate sea coast - this was always lightly settled and somewhat outside Chardon's orbit, and has become more so
- The "Cult of Apollyon" still exists and causes trouble for everyone, mostly operating in the eastern shores of Lake Valandros, stretching north a bit. Details are fuzzy but might hear rumors. 

For many in Voltara, it is really not much different - one distant ruler for another. There isn't really popular unrest or anything. 

## Character Intros

Start with Dinia, describe her camp. She has moved lower in the mountains, and now runs a fortified house of hospitality. Takes in refugees, tells the old tales of the northern people before the Great War, preserves history. Brelith has helped her build up fortifications, and runs the kitchens. 

This is a fortified household offering shelter, teaching, and hospitality, supported by gardens, gifts, and the work of its residents. Describe a guesthouse or hall where Dinia keeps the old tales and Brelith keeps everyone fed.

Then make each player describe their character. Physical description, what they are wearing/carrying, etc. 

Let the conversation go on for a bit. Dinia can tell them what's been happening - will be very curious where they were. She is worried about Zadkai - will remind them. The tower reappeared, but then left a few months ago, drifting south. For what purpose she doesn't know, but she doesn't trust it. She can also mention the roc has been looking for them. 

## Silverstorm

At some point, [[Silverstorm]] shows up, aka Silver Feathers of the Storm Cloud, Thunder and Lightning. Massive - 200 foot wingspan, at least. Overshadows the compound completely. 

Greats [[Silver Tempests]] joyfully, though initially confused about Blue and Mabist, addresing Brelith instead and asking after Aelar and Aglath. Happy to see them alive, and thriving. 

Gets serious: as I once helped you, now I must ask for your help. Terrible rumors have reached me. You are familiar with the wizard [[Airion|Airion the Mistspeaker]], now dead? You may recall the warlock Zadkai, who serves [[Azravan]], Prince of the Unconquered Air, Duke of the Scouring Wind. The Scouring Wind believe that humanity must die for the sins of the wizards who enslaved genies, and now are seeking the hidden knowledge of Airion. Airion left something in a secret lair, and Zadkai wants it, for some bad purpose. 

I know that humanity is not perfect, and I have my own quibbles with your kind, but most of us do not believe that humanity is irredeemably evil. We do not know what Zadkai seeks, but fear it cannot be for the benefit of us, or humanity, and many will die if he recovers it and learns to use it, whatever it is. 

Hence, I come to you, my friends. Search Airion's old lair in Tawir Forest, on a bare peak known as Mt. Graybane. I believe that is where Zadkai has gone, so there is no time to waste. I can fly you as far as the forest edge, but Zadkai likely has a large force of elementals with him, and I am not stealthy. You will likely do better if you can approach quietly, without much fanfare. 

Find what Zadkai is seeking and bring it to Orumei. We must learn what Azravan is preparing.

[[Orumei]], Duke of the Returning Wind, based in [[Sulmana]]. 


Unknown to Silverstorm:
He spent many years studying the connection between planes, and was among the foremost wizards of our age in this. I have learned that one of the things he created was a mechanism that could stabilize and even expand portals between planes, creating large passages an army can march through. 
The thing in Airion's lair would allow the Scouring Wind to launch an invasion of terrible devastation.
