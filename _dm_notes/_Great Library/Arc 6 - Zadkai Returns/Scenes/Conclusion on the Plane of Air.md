**this supercedes other Plane of Air ending notes**

There are three fundamental outcomes that determine the next adventure, but all functionally end the session. 

Option 1: the party reaches the Elemental Plane of Air with the contents of the vault. Silverstorm brings them to [[Orumei]], and I narrate the sweeping landscapes of Sulmuna. We pick up with the adventure from there. 

Option 2: the party reaches the ELemental Plane of Air with some, but not all, of the keys. Same as above, but the next adventure is different. 

Option 3: if the party is defeated by Zadkai, they are imprisoned, and reach the Plane of Air as prisoners of the Scouring Wind, with the Scouring Wind in possession of the treasure. This doesn't end the adventure, but presents another challenge and path.
