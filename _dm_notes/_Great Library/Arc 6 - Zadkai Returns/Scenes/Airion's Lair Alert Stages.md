# Airion's Lair Alert Stages

Three levels of Alertness, which determine Elemental attitudes to invaders. 

Lair starts at Alert Stage 1, due to Zadkai's troops influence. 

Level 0: No alert
Guardians are friendly and will chat if able, nothing suggests danger, no concerns, most creatures start Friendly or at least Indifferent. 

Level 1: Mild alert
Triggered by death of one guardian. 
Guardians begin Hostile and will fight if an intruder does not establish their legitimacy (e.g., association with Airion or clear allowance/permission to be in his lair) immediately. Other inhabitants are unaffected. 

Level 2: High Alert
Triggered by the death of two guardians. 
Guardians will attack on sight. Other Elementals begin Indifferent at best, though some will be hostile. A subtle atmosphere of threat and dread is perceptible, light dims and pulses. 

Level 3: Emergency Alert
Triggered by death of three guardians. 
All inhabitants will attack on sight as a aversion field turns Elementals in the lair hostile and grumpy. All elementals begin Hostile. Patrols of living spells will begin - these are trivial to defeat but will make resting nearly impossible as they will begin to collect anywhere they are killed at greater and greater numbers. Lights run along the walls, previously invisible runes on the floor of the vault / lair level 1 flash. Anyone who stays in one place for more than 15 minutes begins to attract runes, which cluster around them. After 20 minutes turn into living spells and attack. For a high level party, treat these as nuisances, but means resting is impossible because every rest is interrupted after 20 minutes by initiative. 

## Resting

At stages 1 and 2, short rest is possible in lair entrance or lair level 1. 

Make it obvious that Zadkai's patrols are here. A long rest has a high chance of leading to the Earth patrol getting the key, and potentially sneaking past the party. This needs to be improvised. 

Two long rests mean the air patrol will figure it out, and attempt to sneak past the party and head for another passage. Will have to improvise. 

If the party rests a third time, a new patrol will arrive and clear out a third passage. 

A fourth rest without getting any keys means Zadkai's team wins and heads for the vault.
