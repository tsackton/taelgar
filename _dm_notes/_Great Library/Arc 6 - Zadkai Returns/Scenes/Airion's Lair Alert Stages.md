# Airion's Lair Alert Stages

Three levels of Alertness, which determine Elemental attitudes to invaders. 

Lair starts at Alert Stage 1, due to Zadkai's troops influence. 

Level 0: No alert
Guardians are friendly and will chat if able, nothing suggests danger, no concerns, most creatures start Friendly or at least Indifferent. 

Level 1: Mild alert
Triggered by death of one guardian. 
Guardians begin Hostile and will fight if an intruder does not establish their legitimacy immediately. Other inhabitants are unaffected. 

Level 2: High Alert
Triggered by the death of two guardians. 
Guardians will attack on sight. Other Elementals begin Indifferent at best, though some will be hostile. 

Level 3: Emergency Alert
Triggered by death of three guardians. 
All inhabitants will attack on sight as a aversion field turns Elementals in the lair hostile and grumpy. All elementals begin Hostile. Patrols of living spells will begin - these are trivial to defeat but will make resting nearly impossible as they will begin to collect anywhere they are killed at greater and greater numbers.
