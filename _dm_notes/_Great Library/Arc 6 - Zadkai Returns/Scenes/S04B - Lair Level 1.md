# Lair Level 1

## Entrance

Reached from [[S04A - Entrance to the Lair|Entrance to the Lair]], via the stair.

Once everyone in the upper room is on the stair (or, when a particular command word is spoken, but Airion was too clever to write it down, so would need to be learned by magic of some kind), there is a grinding, shaking sound for a moment, and the upper passage goes dark while flickering torchlight can be seen below.

## Interior

This is the vault entrance chamber. It is a 30x30x15 bare room, lit by 18 torches enchanted with Continual Flame of various colors. A smashed mirror sits on one wall, behind which is a silver door covered in runes, a moving/drifting image of mists and clouds passing over the door. There are four key holes in the center of the door, all empty. 

A stairwell in the center of the floor leads into a red glow, heat wafting from below.

### Airion's Vault

The basic way this once worked. Airion established an extraplanar vault, which required four elemental keys to open. Rather than simply carry the keys, he gave one to each of four elemental guardians. The vault door was covered by a magical mirror, and by speakign the right command he could summon each elemental guardian to the mirror, and ask for its key. Each would hand over it turn, he'd open the vault, and then when done, carry out the process in reverse. The setup had the mirror covering the vault door; with the four keys in place, the mirror vanished as the door opened. 

Zadkai's team detected the vault door behind the mirror, and smashed the mirror, but could not open the vault. They then proceeded into the fire guardian path, and got stuck somehow (developed in the relevant guardian path). See [[Zadkai's Alert Progression]] and [[S09 - Raid Zadkai's Camp]] for more.
