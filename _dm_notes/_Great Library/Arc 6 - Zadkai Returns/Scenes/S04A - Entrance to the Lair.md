# Entrance to the Lair

The basic entrance is a large round building, about 20 feet tall and maybe 60 feet in diameter, domed. Etched with all kinds of patterns and runes - possibly the locus for the storm and clouds. 

Massive round door has been smashed open, looks like it was battered by a hurricane. Several statues lay broken and cracked outside the doors.
