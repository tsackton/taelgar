# Fire Guardian Path

Zadkai presence: no teams have explored this path yet, at least when the adventure begins. 

## Entrance

The entrance is this: a metal double door with two large handles, looks heavy. glows red-hot; waves of heat radiate from it out into the room. It is not locked, but opening it requires considerable Strength as it weighs something like 500 pounds of solid red-hot metal. 

Need to push it open -- door swing in. 

To move 500 pounds requires a STR of 17 (or combination of other STR; each person can push 30 lb x STR) - no check if you do this, but have to commit to pushing with both hands. Can attempt to avoid touching as much of the hot metal, which means you need an Athletics check (DC 12 + your share of weight / 100). Failure - doesn't budge, but of course can try again, taking more damage. 

Damage: 5d10 fire damage if fully committing; trying to avoid damage potentially reduces to 2d10 or 3d10 fire damage if meaningful, but then requires Athletics check as above. Lights ordinary objects on fire if in contact for more than a minute or so. 

## General Conditions

The fire guardian's chamber also serves as the power plant for the entire lair, more or less. This was not a place Airion went - even with fire immunity it is unpleasant and hot and sweaty. 

### Extreme Heat

The Fire Guardian path is *HOT*. A creature in this area must succeed on a Constitution saving throw at the end of each hour or gain 1 [Exhaustion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ExhaustionCondition) level. The DC is 5 for the first hour and increases by 1 for each additional hour. Creatures wearing Medium or Heavy armor have [Disadvantage](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Disadvantage) on the save. Creatures that have [Resistance](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Resistance) or [Immunity](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Immunity) to Fire damage automatically succeed on the save. Drinking 1 quart of water per hour (1/4 of the daily need in normal conditions) gives advantage on the save; no water gives disadvantage. Can keep water usage regular for a straight roll (1 gallon per day). 

Basic idea - choice between front-loading advantage but risking disadvantage later, or just going for a straight roll. 

Exhaustion gained this way cannot be removed unless plenty of drinking water is available. 

### Resting

Short or long rests are possible in areas noted, though long rests require extreme amounts of water. 

## Rooms

### Entrance (A) and Lava Chamber (B)

The metal doors swing outward into a dark stone passage that continues for 50 feet before opening into a large circular chamber, approximately 80 feet in diameter. The center of the chamber is a large pool of lava, circular as well, about 30 feet in diameter. Fire elemental dolphins jump and play in the pool, and a series of extensive golden runes decorate the floor, seeming to channel and shape the lava through complex circuits in the floor. 

Lava flows from upper right to lower left, emerging into the walls in the lower left. 

Four exits
Lower Left, into the Power Room (C)
Upper Left and Upper Right, both into the Guardian Complex (D)
Lower Right, into the Storage Area (E)

### Power Room (C)

Entrance: unlocked door. 

**C1**
This room is what controls the storm. There is a small portal to the Plane of Fire in area D3 (similar to the Water portal in [[Drowned Tower]]); lava pours out of the portal, flows into the central chamber, is shaped by runes into a series of tubes that then emerge along the walls of C1, where the lava fills a large series of runes carved on the three walls apart from where the door is.  

Entering is hot - oppressively so. A massive runic circle is etched in the floor, inside which is a large fire elemental that seems to be occasionally raking the walls. Flitting around its feet is a fiery lizard; while flitting motes of raw flame seem to dance along the walls. 

[Large fire elemental](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195192-salamander-inferno-master) 13000 XP
[Fiery lizard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485811-crux-of-fire) 1800 XP
[Flame sparks](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485839-fire-spark) 225 XP (use 10, change size to tiny)

The large fire elemental is magically bound to the summoning circle and commanded to maintain the runes that power the storm. If anyone enters unaccompanied by Airion, it is commanded to demand they leave, in five languages (Chardonian, Amani, Common, Dwarven, and Elven). If they don't leave in 1 minute, or if they take any hostile actions towards the creature, the summoning circle, or the walls, it is to attack. **The runes are interpretable to anyone who can cast 6th level spells, and clearly seem to be some kind of weather control system.**

The fire elemental hates this job and would likely prefer to just leave. Cannot, unless the summoning circle is destroyed, which takes at least an action and some kind of check (DC typically around 17; let players be creative). If freed, may lash out in anger before vanishing, especially if someone dealt a solid blow. Pet lizard vanishes with him; flame sparks are mindless and stay and fight. 

The runes are self-healing as long as lava is properly flowing. If the lava is stopped, the runes fade, cool, and eventually can be damaged and destroyed.  Clues this is connected to the storm: possibly from talking to the Fire Elemental; possibly recognizing cloud or something similar in the runs. Fine to improvise here. 

On the far wall there is a secret door. Easiest way to detect is by heat - other walls are hot because lava flows through this, door is not hot - or not as hot. DC 16 to notice heat difference; DC 18 to find door mechanism once deduced; DC 19 Investigation to deduce. 

#### Destroying the Ruins

If the fire elemental is killed, or freed, the lava will begin to pile up. Slow at first, and then very fast after about 10-20 minutes. Eventually, will start melting the floor, and will corrode and destroy the ruins. This will have the effect of eventually shutting off the door (1-3 days later, but will become weaker progressively). 

This is, of course, trigger Zadkai to investigate. 


**C2** 
This is a store room with extra components for the guardian. Specifically, there are 11 fire gemhearts (see below). However most are not usable; Arcana check (DC 13 default; DC 18 good result; DC 23 great result) to sort. 

This is a bare room with three stone shelves. Dusty, obviously long abandoned. Maybe some cracked scrolls, dried out potions, stuff like that. Untouched for ~200 years. Only thing still working is 3-5 of the fire gemhearts. 

### Guardian Chamber (D)

Entrance: neither door is locked and both can be readily opened. A weird red glow comes from beneath door to D1. 

**D1**: Antechamber
This was, originally, a simple antechamber. Recently, however, a pair of magma elementals have found this place, and got trapped, unable to proceed back through the portal, which is warded on this side. They have dug a magma-filled tunnel connecting this room to the portal/entry. 

[Magma Elemental](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6575391-magma-elemental) 2300 XP (2)
[Fire Lizard Pets](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485811-crux-of-fire) 1800 X (3)
[Fire Sparks](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485839-fire-spark) 225 (15)

Will run if reduced <50% hp via the magma tunnel

**D2**: Tunnels and Entry

Increasingly oppressive heat; extensive lava flows and precarious terrain around the door, which is slightly melted and stuck. 

DC 18 Strength check to force open. On failure, DC 16 Dex save to avoid falling in lava; Disadvantage if fail strength check by more than 5. Fall in lava - ask Isaac for damage. 

D3: Guardian.

Iron golem with a glowing red key in its chest stands under a lava waterfall. Giant flaming metal bull beside it. 

[Iron golem](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195090-iron-golem)
[Blaze gorgon](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194932-brazen-gorgon)

Strategy - the iron golem tries to stay in the lava so it constantly heals; failing that, tries to stay in the gorgon's flame aura. Will not necessarily attack immediately, but can't speak. Will hold up hand in a stop motion and wait patiently, but if party doesn't leave will attack. 

If killed, can remove the key and its fire gemheart. 

### Storage Area / Study (E)

Entrance: locked door, DC 18 to open. 

This is a small chamber that would have been used by Airion when he had to come here to do maintenance.  It is a safe place to rest, though remains oppressively so a long rest would be challenging. 

Small shelf with a bunch of books that have melted away over time. Desk and chair; small chest. The chest is weirdly not warm to the touch. Contains a bunch of quills and ink, plus what seems to be a logbook, describing various tweaks to the storm control system and the lava ruins. Describes the guardian as well. ("I have entrusted one of the vault keys to the iron golem, who loves the heat of the lava spring"). 

Can improvise here.

## Treasure

**Fire gemheart**: A glowing red ruby, pulsating with captured flames. Can crack gem and breath the fire inside. Warms you up; effects last for 1 hour, and don't require attunement.
- Immune to extreme cold, and can't feel cold. 
- Resistant to cold damage, and whenever you are hit by a melee attack, the attacker takes an extra 1d8 fire damage
- If you cast a spell that does elemental damage, you can change the damage type to Fire
- As a reaction to taking Cold damage, you can end the shield. Doing so gives you immunity to the triggering damage and resistance to Cold damage until the start of your next turn.
