# Through the treasury portal

There are three possible options here, depending on how the party proceeds and how well they do.

Note that the keys do not have to be inserted in a particular order - this is not a puzzle. 

## Best Outcome

If the party recovers the four keys in good order, without triggering Zadkai's alert, the final outcome becomes dénouement. With all four keys and no sign of Zadkai in pursuit, they can open the vault, recover the mysterious hidden treasure, and quickly escape, though they will need to pass through the storm again unless they find a way to turn it off (or, the roc is plausibly big enough to be undisturbed). Goes to [[S11A - Delivery on the Plane of Air]]

## Intermediate Outcome

If both Zadkai and the party end up with some, but not all, the keys, then aim for a climatic battle against Zadkai and his forces here. Winner gets the keys. Zadkai likely would not choose to fight to the death, here. Give him some way to flee, e.g. via Plane Shift. 

Goes to either [[S11A - Delivery on the Plane of Air]] or [[S11B - Pursuit to the Plane of Air]], depending on outcome. 

## Bad Outcome

If Zadkai gets all the keys - most likely because the party repeatedly retreats from the dungeon to rest - then there is still a chance for a pitched battle against Zadkai here, now with the time pressure of preventing him from opening the vault and taking what's inside and immediately plane-shifting away. 

Likely directly to [[S11B - Pursuit to the Plane of Air]].


## Inside the Vault

The vault is a collection of magical experiments and the instruments used to study them. Worktables hold objects in open support frames, surrounded by diagrams, measuring devices, and loose sheets of calculations. One wall is covered by a sprawling diagram of the elemental planes, studded with gemstones. Small piles of old coins and precious-metal ingots lie among the apparatus.

The recurring theme is Airion's attempt to distill the essence of the elemental planes into physical objects. The Frost Brand and the fire shard are surviving results of that work; their accompanying drawings show successive designs, crossed-out calculations, and studies of how to contain elemental power.

### The Astrolabe and Airion's Notebook

A brass-and-crystal astrolabe, about the size of a human head, stands at the principal worktable. Its nested rings turn around a tiny opening onto blue sky. Fine scales and adjustment screws cover the rings, matching the diagrams in the book beside it.

The astrolabe is Airion's hidden work on controlling stable, large-scale elemental gates. It continually adjusts for changes in the relationship between the planes, keeping the two ends of a gate aligned. Incorporated into a sufficiently powerful gate apparatus, it can sustain the opening and adjust its destination. This is the instrument Zadkai's faction seeks to enable its invasion, and which the party has been asked to bring to [[Orumei]].

The accompanying book is Airion's private working notebook, written in a code he invented for himself. Its pages are crowded with precise drawings of rotating rings and vortices, studies of failed mechanisms, and dense passages of personal symbols and shorthand. Foldout sheets show successive versions of the astrolabe; corrections and marginal calculations overlap earlier work. The feel is a Leonardo da Vinci notebook: meticulous observation mixed with invention, revision, and experiments in progress.

The book records the theory and construction of the astrolabe, along with its operation and adjustments. The astrolabe is the working instrument; the notebook is the means to understand its workings and potentially reproduce them.

### Magical Treasure

- **Frost Brand**, intended for [[Mabist]]. The weapon rests in an open support frame, its blade filmed with frost. Surrounding sheets show the blade in cross-section, with channels and containment patterns drawn through it, alongside sketches of apparatus for distilling elemental cold. Repeated revisions show Airion working out how to hold that essence within the weapon. Use the standard **Very Rare, attunement-required** item: an extra **1d6 cold damage** on a hit and **resistance to fire damage while held**, plus its light and flame-extinguishing properties. [Frost Brand rules](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Templates%3AFrost%20Brand?expansion=33335&iframe=true).
- **Elemental Essence Shard of Fire**, intended for [[Blue]]. A crackling crystal containing a concentrated ember is held in a small brass clamp. Its diagrams show elemental fire being gathered, concentrated, and fixed within the crystal. Other sketches explore how that contained essence responds when a spell is reshaped. Use the standard **Rare** item from *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*, requiring attunement by a sorcerer: when the wearer uses Metamagic on a spell, one target of that spell they can see can be set alight, taking **2d10 fire damage at the start of its next turn**, after which the flames go out. The shard can serve as a spellcasting focus and be attached to a Tiny object, such as a wand or pendant.
- **Immovable Rod.** A brass rod marked with fine measurement lines lies beside the experimental apparatus. It served as a fixed reference point for Airion's work. Use the standard **Uncommon** item.
- **Carpet of Flying.** A rolled carpet depicts swirling clouds and currents, with small corrections embroidered over parts of the original pattern. Use the **Very Rare, 6-by-9-foot** version: **800-pound capacity and 30-foot flying speed**; it can carry up to **1,600 pounds at half speed**. Intended as shared transportation for the party on the Plane of Air. [Carpet of Flying rules](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Items%3ACarpet%20of%20Flying?expansion=33359).

### Coins, Gems, and Precious Metals

- **Old coins worth 400 gp total**, scattered in several small piles among the worktables and apparatus.
- **Two star sapphires worth 500 gp each**, kept with the experimental materials.
- **A mixture of gold and silver ingots worth 700 gp total.**
- **Embedded gemstones worth 2,000 gp total**, which can be pulled out of the elemental diagram covering one wall.

The wall diagram is a map-like model of the elemental planes: overlapping regions, curved connections, and annotated paths show Airion's attempts to represent their relationships. Many small gems are set into the surface at points along the lines and within the regions. Corrections and alternative connections give it the same experimental character as his other drawings.

**Total monetary treasure: 4,100 gp**, including the two star sapphires.
