# Through the treasury portal

There are three possible options here, depending on how the party proceeds and how well they do.

Note that the keys do not have to be inserted in a particular order - this is not a puzzle. 

## Best Outcome

If the party recovers the four keys in good order, without triggering Zadkai's alert, the final outcome becomes dénouement. With all four keys and no sign of Zadkai in pursuit, they can open the vault, recover the mysterious hidden treasure, and quickly escape, though they will need to pass through the storm again unless they find a way to turn it off (or, the roc is plausibly big enough to be undisturbed). Goes to [[S11A - Delivery on the Plane of Air]]

## Intermediate Outcome

If both Zadkai and the party end up with some, but not all, the keys, then aim for a climatic battle against Zadkai and his forces here. Winner gets the keys. Zadkai likely would not choose to fight to the death, here. Give him some way to flee, e.g. via Plane Shift. 

Goes to either [[S11A - Delivery on the Plane of Air]] or [[S11B - Pursuit to the Plane of Air]], depending on outcome. 

## Bad Outcome

If Zadkai gets all the keys - most likely because the party repeatedly retreats from the dungeon to rest - then there is still a chance for a pitched battle against Zadkai here, now with the time pressure of preventing him from opening the vault and taking what's inside and immediately plane-shifting away. 

Likely directly to [[S11B - Pursuit to the Plane of Air]].
