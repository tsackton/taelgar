## Entrance Wall (A)

The entrance to the guardian tunnel is via a stone wall and a packed earth tunnel. Close inspection of the wall reveals cracks running through it, with the oddity that there seems to be loose stone and earth behind the cracks. 

The cracks can be enlarged; the loose earth can be burrowed through. In is 40 feet to A2, where the loose earth ends in an open room. 

You can't excavate - the earth just keeps filling in. Burrow speed or ability to move through small cracks allows passage. Otherwise, can dig, but need to make a Con save every 20 feet (DC 15) or suffer 1 level of Exhaustion. 

DC 18 default for notice or enlarge. DC 22 with a bad approach. As low as DC 15 if clever. 

Can find evidence (DC 25) that most/all of the cracks seem new (caused by Zadkai's patrol). 

From A2, three passages: to the left, passage goes up. To the right, passage goes up steeply. In the center, passage goes down. All three are narrow stone tunnels extending between 50 and 200 feet.

## Treasure

Gemheart allocation: expect the party to find 4 usable earth gemhearts, with a maximum of 5 if they find an optional extra. Exact placements remain to be designed.

**Earth gemheart**:  
A glowing green emerald, swirling with dust and tiny crystals. As an action, can crack gem and breathe the dust inside. Body transforms into living stone; effects last for 1 hour, and don't require attunement. Only one elemental gemheart can be active on you at a time; activating another ends the previous gemheart's effects.

- Do not need to breathe.
- Can control your density, becoming light enough to float or heavy enough to sink rapidly. Can rise, sink, or hover in air or water; horizontal movement requires pushing off something, being towed, or riding winds or currents. You can move up to 30 feet vertically - this takes normal movement but can only be up or down. Pushing off a fixed surface counts as a jump, using your normal standing long jump distance. 
- Resistant to force and bludgeoning damage.
- Immune to forced movement unless you choose to be moved (but not immune to Prone)
