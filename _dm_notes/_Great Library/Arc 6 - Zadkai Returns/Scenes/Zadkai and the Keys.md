Reorganizing thoughts about how the competition between Zadkai and the party for the keys will play out. 

**This note overrides other mechanics that involve Zadkai**

Fundamentally, this is a stalemate, not a race. Once one team has a key, the other team cannot win. But Zadkai is not really interested in a stalemate - he needs what is in the vault. The party can survive a stalemate. 

There are two interrelated dynamics here. One is whether Zadkai knows the party is present. The other is the balance of keys. 

Basic flow chart:

(1) If Zadkai never learns the party is present, then they can simply complete their tasks and win.

(2) If Zadkai learns the party is present and seeking the keys, he will aim to do two things. If he does not posses any keys, he will first try to prevent them from getting the rest of the keys, or take one or more keys from them. Secondarily, he will aim to summon a large/overwhelming force from the Plane of Air to capture them and take them to the [[Scouring Wind]]. How this plays out:

- Zadkai patrols will increase in the lair, and plausibly attempt to beat the party to a guardian. 
- Zadkai patrols will attempt to prevent the party from using the keys, by attacking them at rest and/or guarding the vault door

Eventually, Zadkai will call for backup on the plan of air, and bring overwhelming force. 


This mostly interacts with rests:

- If Zadkai is alerted and the party takes a long rest, assume one of his patrols can sneak past the party (maybe give a DC 25 Perception check). If the party managed to wound a guardian but had to retreat, this is where Zadkai's patrols will get it. If the party did not wound a guardian, assume Zadkai's patrol will attempt to wait and attack when they emerge from a room with the final key. 
- If Zadkai is not alerted, the party gets a free long rest, and it is only if they take a second long rest that more patrols come. 

If Zadkai has a key, will quickly try to get it to safety on the Elemental Plane of Air, but give the party a chance to plausibly attempt to ambush Zadkai's patrol as they leave. If Zadkai's patrol gets away, the key is as good as gone. But, because the party primarily needs to prevent Zadkai from reaching the vault, this is not a true loss. Just makes things harder.
