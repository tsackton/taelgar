# Mt. Graybane Ascent


Wooded lower slopes rise above otherwise fairly flat forest. Unnatural winds intensify toward a bare summit within cloud.

Basic layout - wooded slopes below rising to a bare but always cloud-covered peak. 
Zadkai has parked the tower in a sheltered valley, but it is visible above the trees. Not concerned with crushing/destroying trees. See [[Airion Tower - DM Notes]] if needed. 

Zadkai has an army more or less, though. Would be basically impossible to attack directly. 

Mountain ascent requires passing through the storm. 

## Crossing Airion's Storm

Flight is impossible through the storm - this makes it particularly challenging for Airion's minions to get through and is one reason they have taken so long. Visibility is also essentially zero. But this needs to be a gameplay challenge...

The vibe is basically walking through a hurricane with zero visibility, and then suddenly emerging into the eye of the storm, the clear top of the mountain. Airion's tower stands at the peak, but mostly is carved down, into the mountain itself. 

Game play is passing three hazards on the way up, though might be able to route around. 

### At the Base

If they explore, can find a path/trail that leads into the clouds. Don't have to follow it, but if they don't, see "Getting Lost" below. 

Basic framework is the storm is windy the whole way, but there are three layers:
- Rain
- Lightning
- Wind

### The Trail Becomes a River

This is like one of the stairs in LOTR ascending Mordor. Becomes steep, and then basically just a stair carved into a cliff. Can also pull vibes of White Mountains trails. Visibility is nothing, and basically just following the stair. 

Rain in the storm. Then a trickle of water on the path. Cannot hear anything, because the wind is so intense. Water starts rushing, more and more. Soon becomes difficult to make progress against the flow. See what they do. Can just push on, but fail a DC 20 Strength saving throw and swept off your feet and fall. 

Rushing gully of water, eroded sharply. Stair continues on the other side, across ~15 feet of clouds (cannot see). Once cross the gully, stair levels and moves onto harder rock; rain stops abruptly. 

### Lightning Storm

The rain abruptly stops, but now can hear thunder rolling across the mountain. Cannot see lightning, of course, but air has that electric tinge to it. Pass through a zone of living lightning bolts. There is nothing really to do here, except run. 

[Lightning Bolt](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/486454-living-lightning-bolt)

The lightning bolts do not move: when they get close, there is a crackling sound and a shock, but nothing moving, almost like static lightning hanging in the air. 

The distance is ~200 feet to cross the lightning field. Make sure it is clear that there are hundreds of them - cannot kill all the living lightning bolts. This is really just a damaging trap, though there may be some counterplay (moving fast; lightning resistance; something else?).

### Howling Wind

Come out on a gentler slope of loose rocks, but much more intense wind. No shelter, just horrible howling. Feels like walking throw molasses. Three main dangers:

Bouncing Rocks - dex save, 2d6 damage on fail. If hit, Strength save or knocked off feet and blown random distance away. 
Rockslide: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#Rockslide Use the level 11-16 version, 10d10 damage. 

Then, the general wind. Makes it very difficult to proceed and risks exhaustion if just pushing against the storm. 

Get past the wind, though, and suddenly break out into clear open terrain. 

### Getting Lost

If they are not following a trail, a major challenge is just finding a way forward. Survival checks, and risk exhaustion, though with good / lucky rolls might be able to avoid some obstacles. Still face the rain -> lightning -> wind gaunlet.
