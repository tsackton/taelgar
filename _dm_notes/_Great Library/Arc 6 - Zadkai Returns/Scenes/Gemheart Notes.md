Basic details of gemhearts to keep in one place. 

(1) Do not need to make identification a challenge. Grasping one lets you feel the elemental power drawing you in; 10 minute uninterrupted study tells you what it does. 

(2) All gemhearts take an Action to activate, and a Bonus Action to switch between forms. They require no attunement or Concentration. All last 1 hour.
