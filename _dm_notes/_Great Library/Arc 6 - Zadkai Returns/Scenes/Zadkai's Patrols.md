# Zadkai’s Patrols

Zadkai is not set up to have a set number of forces on his side - the idea is that he has allies patrolling the forest (see, e.g., [[S02 - Journey through Tawir Forest]]), teams searching the lair, possibly some teams stuck in the storm, and teams guarding the camp. 

Zadkai himself will avoid combat and flee if needed, but may engage the party if an opportunity presents. 

As the adventure begins, the following is true:
- Zadkai has sent two teams towards the lair. Note that getting through the storm is likely very difficult for Zadkai's forces, as flying is super hard. The idea is that one patrol is trapped in the air guardian path, perhaps after having destroyed the air guardian, but is unable to return to the vault chamber. So the air guardian path turns into an encounter with Zadkai's patrol. The second patrol is a wild-card that is kind of floating around. 
- Zadkai also has teams patrolling the forest and guarding the camp; these represent backup forces that can be used to shift around defenses and offense. 

## Reactions

Zadkai will react and adapt to what the party does, assuming he learns about it. There are two branches here, basically, plus a bonus "win condition" for the party. 

(1) If Zadkai learns another group is trying to break into Airion's lair, he will increase patrols, pulling back from the forest and sending more teams into the storm. The implication is that if the party encounters a patrol, and someone from the patrol gets away, there will be an escalation trigger, adding another patrol into the mix and potentially making the dungeon crawl harder. If this happens he will also send notice to the [[Scouring Wind]], though not necessarily with clear information about the identities of the party or their goals. 

(2) If Zadkai is attacked and suffers serious losses, especially through any kind of clever plan that results in keys he had possessed getting stolen, or if he is personally in danger during an attack, he will flee to the plane of air, warn his bosses. This shifts the later adventure, but frees the party to recover the keys facing only whomever Zadkai leaves behind. 

The bonus wincon:
If the party manages to get the treasure without Zadkai learning they are present, his teams will likely keep searching for some time, especially if both patrols in the lair are lost. This will make Zadkai nervous and cautious. So this option - perfect stealth - buys some extra lead time for the next adventure and functions as a bonus wincon. 

## Patrol Rosters

Generally speaking, do not need to establish patrol rosters - each encounter should be designed as needed. However, the can use  [[S02 - Journey through Tawir Forest#Zadkai's Patrols|these rosters]] as needed in a pinch. 

At the moment, don't design Zadkai patrols until it is obvious what is needed. In particular, the camp attack should be designed as a specific encounter if it seems like it might be needed: [[S10 - Raid Zadkai's Camp]]. The patrol encounters in the lair should be placed deliberately or designed as a wandering encounter explicitly (for the second patrol). On alert, a third expedition can be designed. 

## Exploration Targets

When the adventure begins, Zadkai has two teams in the lair. One is exploring the Air Guardian passage. One is exploring the Earth Guardian passage. One team (see specific notes) has defeated the guardian and recovered the key, but is now stuck and cannot escape back to the vault chamber (this team must not have any means of extradimensional travel). The other team was exploring but has not yet encountered the guardian; they may fight the guardian and fail/run, continue exploring, or move to a new path as the adventure continues. They will not get a key unless the party weakens a guardian and does not defeat it, and then retreats to rest/recover.
