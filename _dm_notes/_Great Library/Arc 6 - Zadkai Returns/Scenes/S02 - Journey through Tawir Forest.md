# Journey through Tawir Forest

Mostly linear; atmospheric and introductory. Follow directions, handle forest strangeness, and try to avoid Zadkai’s notice.

[[Tawir Forest]]

Tawir Forest is like the ancient oak forests of England or something. Even though it is summer, there is always fog, trails are hard to follow, and surprising things pop up in the forest. Consider running some number of these encounters.

The journey should be about 80 miles, unless they get lost (possible). No map, just run as a series of encounters. 

This is probably enough to run from, just need to expand the lair a bit, though mostly improvise because they might skip it!

## Zadkai's Patrols

Zadkai has patrols of Scouring Wind enemies scouting the forest for anyone approaching. Pick one of these patrol compositions. 

Exact situation will depend a bit how the party approaches the journey through the forest. Can always drop one as they approach Mt. Graybane.

### Storm Patrol

Four Essence of Storms plus a Slow Storm. Statblocks:
[Slow Storm](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4340237-slow-storm)
[Essence of Storms](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485833-essence-of-storms)

These probably fly low over the trees, and if anything moves they dip down to investigate. 

### Djinn Patrol

One djinn, two air elementals using the aeromancer stat block, 10 air sparks

[Djinn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194971-djinni)

## Forest Denizens

Pick some encounters to give a vibe of the place, and also potentially information about Zadkai. 

### The Presence 

A voice speaks from beneath the roots of an ancient oak. It offers to help the party if they remove several iron spikes driven into the surrounding stones. It knows the forest, and can give them guidance on hidden paths. It admits that the spikes imprison it. It refuses to explain why, calling that an old quarrel. It will not reveal its name. 

If the party removes one spike, a massive, earth-covered hand shits beneath the roots. If they remove all, a creature that looks like a top-heavy gorilla, with earthen hands that constantly shift, oversized arms, and a hard body of some kind of dark wood or maybe compressed dirt or wet stone, emerges. 

Will give real advice, and leave, if freed. 

### The Fallen Oak

This is best fairly close to Bald Mountain. A shallow ravine, with a fallen oak forming a bridge of sorts, but many nearby trees stripped of their leaves entirely, and some fallen as well. Clearly windswept. 

The oak is alive, intelligent, and angry. It is wounded and cannot stand. Something (Zadkai) recently tore through the forest, and it believes the party belongs to the same intrusion.

It is not particularly hard to kill: use a Treant stat block with some minions. But if they help, can give them information about Zadkai. 

### The Lair

A medusa has made a lair in the very-long-abandoned caves of a Green Dragon.

[Medusa](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485921-lady-emer) (1)
[Basilisk](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485784-basilisk-mcdm) (4)
[Dragonleaf Tree](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4340023-dragonleaf-tree-poison) (3)

This is basically a three room dungeon. It was once a great cavern (maybe similar to the Fitzban's Green Dragon lair) but now it has collapsed, so kind of a pit. There is a nice stone path down, and the trees have been cleared out (except for the three dragonleaf trees). There is one huge old dead tree partially extending across the top. Several paths and tunnels from the lair have been filled in, and a new chamber for the medusa has been dug. 

The former cave floor is now a statue garden, with the trees. The far side has an overhang, and a low wall, where the basilisks live. A staircase carved in the side of the cavern wall leads up to the medusa's two-room living chambers. 

Use same vibes as Lady Emir from Cloud Fang Keep: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wel/cloud-fang-keep. But she is a loner. 

Signs of the former dragon:
- **Two scales of claw marks.** Deep gouges high on the walls from the dragon; countless smaller scratches around corners and thresholds from the basilisks.
- **The remains of a hoard.** Empty recesses, broken display stands, a few coins wedged irretrievably into cracks. The valuable things are mostly gone, and Lady Emir has her own treasure, but the impression of accumulated wealth remains.
- **A single enormous green scale.** The medusa has kept it, polished it, and displayed it. Whether as a trophy, an ornament, or a reminder of the former owner is unclear. This is with her treasure. 
- **Old grandeur used casually.** A basilisk sleeps in a carved stone basin. A once-impressive pedestal now holds a water bowl. Fine fabric has been dragged into a nest.
- **Petrified wildlife before petrified people.** A stone squirrel beneath an oak, a hare tucked against a root, fragments of a bird below a branch. Enough to suggest that the danger extends beyond the cave.
- **An unsettlingly lifelike deer.** Moss grows along its back, but its eyes and nostrils have been carefully cleaned. Someone maintains it.
- **Statues arranged for company.** Two figures face one another across a table. Another stands beside a doorway as though welcoming visitors. Their original poses don’t quite suit their assigned roles.
- **Basilisk paths.** Broad trails through the undergrowth, polished rubbing stones, muddy belly marks, and sheds of leathery skin caught on roots.
- **Recognizably spoiled animals.** One insists on sleeping somewhere inconvenient. Another follows the medusa everywhere. A third sulks when displaced from its favorite warm patch.
- **A carefully kept living corner.** Ferns, flowers, fresh water, perhaps a comfortable chair. The medusa plainly values living things as well as stone ones.

Encountering the medusa:

Three major options:
- Pass through an area of the forest where things are quiet. Choose to investigate, notice stone animals, basilisk trail.
- Encounter a hunting basilisk, which flees. Choose to chase, or ignore. 
- Camp nearby, medusa sends invitation to tea? Not sure about this.
