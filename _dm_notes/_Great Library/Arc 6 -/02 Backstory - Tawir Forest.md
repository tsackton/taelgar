## Current situation

What is true when the adventure begins? Describe the active problem, relevant recent events, and immediate circumstances without yet outlining scenes.

### Adopted background

Two factions are engaged in a power struggle on the Plane of Air. One views humans as pests to be wiped out and seeks control of gates to enable an invasion. The other distinguishes between good and bad humans and is generally disinclined to meddle in human affairs.

Zadkai has a base camp in Tawir Forest and sends expeditions to discover how to reach Airion's hidden treasury and recover the keys needed for access. Airion's traps and guardians remain in the old lair.

## Relevant backstory and hidden truth

What happened before play that explains the current situation? Use a short lore account, timeline, faction history, hidden truth, or another form suited to the adventure.

### Adopted background

Airion was a researcher who also worked on binding spells. His moral ambiguity should remain intact; his politics can arise without becoming the central issue of this adventure.

Airion hid something in an old lair in Tawir Forest that allows control of stable, large-scale elemental gates. It is an artifact or spell; its exact identity is deliberately undecided.

His hidden treasury is reached through a portal secured by three keys, each held by a guardian. Airion could command these guardians to admit him. The keys were part of his security arrangement, not objects he scattered around the lair for visitors to find.

## Forces and motives

Who or what is involved? What does each relevant force want, what are they currently doing, and what might they do without the PCs' intervention?

### Working faction motives

- **Zadkai's faction:** regards humans as pests that should be wiped out. It wants Airion's gate expertise to enable an invasion.
- **Silverstorm:** knows the party and is allied with the faction opposing Zadkai. He brings the request to recover Airion's hidden work and deliver it to a contact on the Plane of Air.
- **Opposing faction:** recognizes that some humans are good and some are bad, and generally favors noninterference. The user's working motive is that it wants to win the Plane of Air power struggle because a needless war would cost many lives, rather than primarily to save humanity. Its precise leadership and relationship to Mehrangeesa remain open. A contact of this faction on the Plane of Air is the intended recipient of Airion's recovered work.

## Setting rules and constraints

### Established for this adventure

Airion's hidden artifact or spell enables control of stable, large-scale elemental gates. Its exact form and operating details are not needed at this stage.

Opening the treasury portal requires all three guardian-held keys. Intruders must defeat or outwit the guardians to gain access.
