## Ra'ghemdros' Domain

This was once a beautiful garden estate, with water features, extensive grounds, and the manor of the **Concordia Pyrae**, who were dedicated to the knowledge of the elemental plane of fire:
- Symbol: A stylized flame rising from an open book, with sparks forming the shape of a sun above.
- Manor: was originally the central keep of the order, laid out like a Roman villa, with a central pool that was a magically enchanted hot spring and spa (think: Roman baths, in some ways). Obviously much changed. 

The area for about 3 miles around [[Ra'ghemdros Lair - DM Notes]] is within the burn, a region of garish terrain. These effects persist in the aboveground parts of lairs but vanish in the actual lairs themselves. 
- The area is difficult terrain, and movement is difficult, as when you get close the entire area is spongy and soft, you feet sinking maybe 6" - 1 foot into gooey mud
- The entire area is covered in strange red / orange / brown flowers. The colors look very much like a bruise, and the whole area feels like a bruise on the land. But distinctly "hot" colors - no blues/greens. This is in part an aftereffect of Cloudspinner's presence and the power of the sunset. 
- Water in the region is supernaturally fouled. Enemies of the dragon that drink such water regurgitate it within minutes. There is also a thick scent of rot that makes breathing hard. 
- The air is oppressive and heavy. Even trying to move is difficult, and it clouds your head. While in the lair region, Dexterity and Intelligence checks are made with disadvantage. Creatures that don't breath don't suffer these effects.
- The closer you get to the center, the more oppressive the odor and the more garish the colors.

In the center of the lair is the remains of a manor, covering about 1/2 mile from end to end. The manor area is dominated by two large structures. See [[Dragon Lair region|map]].
- The larger structure was once the large, open air thermal pool that was the center of the Concordia Pyrae manor. This is now partially in ruins, and the pool is serves as the entrance to [[Ra'ghemdros Lair - DM Notes]]. Numerous outbuildings are now overgrown with the weird spongey flowers and only vague outlines are visible (gray shaded on map), but the actual central baths are still visible (on map). 
- The smaller structure was once the actual villa, with four stories, a tower, and two basement levels. The above-ground structure is nearly leveled, but a new prison complex exists in the basement (once the tomb and sanctum of the Concordia Pyrae), that holds [[Cloudspinner's Prison - DM Notes]].
- The villa is perched on a small hill overlooking the thermal baths. There is a road that runs from the Pilgrim's Path to a open plaza, and then spilts to lead to thermal baths / manor. The manor is about 1/2 mile from the thermal baths. 

### Encounters

- [[Ra'ghemdros]] can sense any movement that touches the ground, so will be aware if anyone approaches by any means other than flying. 
- [[Ra'ghemdros]] keeps some monstrosities around who survive by grazing on the corrupted flowers but will attack any non-dragonet who approaches. 

### Developments

- [[Ra'ghemdros]] is lazy and greedy, usually happy to just look at her hoard. But sometimes a little bored, and feels pretty invincible. Will not immediately fly out to attack intruders. 
  - If she is aware of approaching strangers, either via burnt flower sense or via dragonet information, and she is aware they kill her monstrosities, she will be curious, and communicate with a dragonet at the Lair Entrance to invite the party to join her. 
  - She does not care at all about killing dragonets, and this will not dissuade her one way or the other, although of course if the party surprises lair entrance dragonets and kills them all before any can speak with them, she won't immediately have a way to communicate. But she'll be all the more curious to see how they deal with the thermal pool, and may invite them in by some other means once inside the lair. 
  - Her one true motivation is if Cloudspinner may be freed:
    - If the party invades Cloudspinner's Prison, and [[Ra'ghemdros]] is aware, she will send minions from her lair to stop them, but also prepare an ambush for when they leave the prison. 
    - If the party actually brings the trapped Cloudspinner out from the prison in a detectable way, she will attack (this will trigger her emerging from her lair to attack by aerial bombardment) and will not relent until she is dead or Cloudspinner is back in her prison. 

### Lair Approach: Monstrosities

A pack of four chimeras patrols the approach. Will attack. Use the [MCDM statblock](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485805-chimera-mcdm) (CR 6). These chimeras will attempt to flee if reduced below 25% HP, but they are relatively low intelligence and they can probably be picked off pretty easily once they panic. 

## Manor Grounds

### Dragonets

Usually 10-15 [[Dragonets - Last Jade]] hang around the thermal pools. They will generally not be interested in combat and if presented with danger will alert Ra'ghemdros telepathically and then flee as quickly as possible. 

If approached in conversation, will happily speak about the area. If the party has proved themselves, one might greet them to lead them to Ra'ghemdros. 

These are true believers. Attitude is sneering, and perhaps grudgingly respectful if party is being invited to speak with Ra'ghemdros. 

### Lair Entrance: Thermal Pool

The lair is connected by a portal from the larger of the thermal pools. 

**Mosaic Tile Fragment (Thermal Baths):** Near the entrance to Ra'ghemdros' lair, amidst the corrupted thermal baths, a single, remarkably preserved mosaic tile lies half-buried in the strange, spongy flora. The tile depicts a stylized flame rising from an open book, the symbol of Concordia Pyrae, a society dedicated to the knowledge of the elemental plane of fire. 

### Prison Entrance: Guards

In the courtyard of the manor, guarding the stairs down to [[Cloudspinner Prison]], are two animated acid breath creatures and a massive dragonblood ooze. 

- Animated breath uses the [Animated Breath](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2059704-animated-breath) statblock with the "Acid" features. 
- The dragonblood ooze uses [dragonblood ooze](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2059726-dragonblood-ooze) statblock with the following changes:
  - It is Huge, instead of Large
  - It has 140 HP
  - It consists of two oozes mashed together, and gets two turns each round, one at Initiative, and one at Initiative - 10. 
  - When reduced below half HP, loses one of its two turns and its size is reduced to Large

These creatures attack anyone who approaches the stairs, unless [[Ra'ghemdros]] has communicated with them directly to grant permission. 

There are the bones of some dragonets here who happened to venture into the area carelessly - Ra'ghemdros does not care.
