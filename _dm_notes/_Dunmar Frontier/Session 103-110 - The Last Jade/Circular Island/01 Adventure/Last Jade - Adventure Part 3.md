## Tower Region

- The terrain here is horrific. Just crazy jagged broken rocks, jumbles of what look like craggy lava flows that have been bulldozed into massive piles. Some kind of combination of Craters of the Moon and the approach to Mordor in the LoTR movies. Travel is exceedingly difficult, basically 2x difficult terrain if trying to walk. So it is slow going.
- The atmosphere is also oppressive. Sleep is nearly impossible, Apollyon's presence is too strong. Taking a long rest requires DC 19 Wisdom save, on failure succumb to dreams and fail to gain any benefit of the rest (but can see visions of Apollyon's reign). After 24 hours, DC 10 Con save or suffer 1 level of exhaustion. Disadvantage if failed to sleep.
- Visually, the tower is obvious. It is an 80 foot tall round structure, made of the same black material that is commonly used on the island, standing against the coast.
