# Adventure: Part 1

Assume the first part of the adventure will be going to the ruined town to investigate the dragonets. But of course things might go off the rails immediately. 

## Pre-Session Timeline Reminder

- May 16th: fought thornblood in the evening. rested
- May 17th: spoke to elder tree; spoke with monkeys; headed into the sea of storms traveling the rest of the day; reached the storm just after sunset. Search a little, but finding no lights wait until morning.
- May 18th: spend some time in the morning investigating and flying around. get through the storm, barely, with Milo/Tilly help. end around lunchtime. 

## Intro

- It is around noon, May 18th, the sea is calm, birds are singing, tropicbirds fish in the water. Something of a Bermuda vibe. Remind about struggling through storm, Milo, Tilly, and crew manning the damaged folding boat, currently fishing for food and intending to sail around the island to scout (see if there are trees close to shore to repair oars, mast), but for now are in shouting range. Will stay within sight of shore - if Delwath sends lynx they should be able to find a place to land briefly to get a message. 
- Beach stretches for a mile in either direction, but there are a number of not-quite-paths in the cliffs that allow an easy climb up. Cliffs are some hard black stone, with shades of gray swirling in places. From here, cannot see the whole island, but remember the view from the water: a large, circular island, ringed by cliffs of black stone, with the occasional sandy beach. Large (150+) statues spaced at even points - could see two, one [[The Wanderer]], one [[The Night Queen]] (wanderer north of your current location, night queen south). Beyond the wanderer statue, what look like ruined towers of the same black stone as the cliff, perhaps in Wellby's eyes surrounded by occasional flocks of small flying things. In the interior, can see a central peak rising in the middle of the island, surrounded by forests, with streams that run from the peak but seem not to reach the ocean. Between here and the mountain is a strange place - a discolored area, hard to see what causes it, but it looks like an angry burn, garish reds and purples like a bruise on the land. 
- The wind blows softly and it feels almost like, if you listen carefully, there is a soft melody from the sands, almost as if the wind is humming a wordless tune. (Perception: sounds elvish?)
- (**Kenzo**: Can sense jade again - almost due west (in the direction of the weird burn). 
- (**Kenzo**: can also sense the aura of calm radiating from the statues like spokes on a wheel towards the center)
- (**Seeker**: Looks, amazingly, almost like marble cliffs intentionally sculpted to look like dramatic eroded cliffs).

## Travel

Assuming they climb up, the paths are a bit more obvious:
- The burn is directly inland from here, hard to judge distance exactly but maybe 5-10 miles. The peak of the mountain is maybe 15-40 miles away. (Actual: 6 and 20, can get more precise with rolls).
- The statue of the wanderer, to the north along the coast, is maybe 5 miles, or less. The statue of the night queen, south along the coast, is maybe 10-15 miles. 
- The ruined towers are just beyond the statue of the wanderer, along the coast. 
- Can see, far in the distance, two other statutes (one northwest, one south), but cannot make out any details beyond "vaguely human-shaped"
- On the summit of the central mountain, there seems to be the remains of some kind of complex of buildings
- A road runs perfectly straight inland from the statue of the wanderer towards the central mountain. Can see that it passes through the edge of the burn area.
- [[Viewing Distances]] for more including what Wellby might see with a perception check. 

Terrain is pretty open, barren land here. 
- There aren't really patrols - [[Ra'ghemdros]] is lazy who would ever come from the ocean? So as long as sticking to the coast and trying to be a little quiet can move unnoticed. 
- Check hex map for distances

Encounters:
- [[Table - Remnants of Journey - Last Jade]] would be appropriate for the journey. Remnants of a refugee camp might be a good option, perhaps with some vague sense of it being a preserved place, e.g. ancient remnants that were more recently roped off. 
- If they cross the travel line between the towers and the burn patch, there is a chance of being spotted by a dragonet patrol. See: [[Dragonets - Last Jade]]
- Otherwise, should not be much in the way of encounters, at least until they reach the Pilgrim Path.
