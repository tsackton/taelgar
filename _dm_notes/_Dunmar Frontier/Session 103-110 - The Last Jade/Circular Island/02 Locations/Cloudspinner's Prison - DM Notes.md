# Cloudspinner's Prison

Cha'mutte constructed a prison for the Cloudspinner in the private tombs and inner sanctum of the manor of the Concordia Pyrae, a Drankorian society obsessed with fire and the elemental plane of fire. 

The area is dark unless otherwise noted. Ceilings are 15' high. 

In some cases, Cha'mutte has simply left in place the defenses of the Concordia Pyrae. In other cases, he constructed his own defenses and guards, which [[Ra'ghemdros]] has maintained. 

## P1: Sanctum Entrance

A spiral staircase descends to the crypts from the manor above. With the manor now largely destroyed, this is easy to find. This room was typically used to during the 10 days of prayer and ritual after a death. 

### Journey of the Soul

The room is designed to celebrate the journey of the soul, during the ten days of passage from the Material Plane, through the Land of the Dead, to what lies beyond. 

The walls are decorated with painting (now somewhat faded) depicting this journey, and in the center of the room is stone table lit by iron pedestals with eternal flame, on which a body would be displayed for mourners to visit. 

### Eternal Flames

Magical flames burn perpetually in ornate braziers placed throughout the tomb, representing the ongoing prayers and mourning during the ten days after death.

### Statues

There are 9 statues in the room, depicting a variety of men and women, usually in relatively elegant clothes. The statues are worn, but not damaged. 

On the far (western) wall, the statue is larger and depicts an imposing man, probably a wizard, seated, holding one hand, palm up, with a flame resting in his palm. The other holds a book with the symbol of the Concordia Pyrae (a stylized flame rising from an open book, with sparks forming the shape of a sun above), and written beneath the statue is an inscription in Drankorian, "With flame comes truth."

The statue radiates evocation magic. There is a small, hard to notice (DC 19 Investigation) rune etched into the fire (5th level spell - to dispel). If anyone who is not a member of the Concordia Pyrae attempts to pass, the fire on the statue flashes. The tarrget makes Dex saving throw (DC 19). On fail, 5d6 fire damage and wreathed in green flames, and Burning (Hazard). On success, half damage, not burning. 

### Exits

Tattered remains of tapestries - not much more than threads, now - hang where the entrances to the crypt, P2, are. 

## P2: Crypt

This corridor functions as the crypt, where members of the Concordia Pyrae who were particularly close for whatever reason to the myth and lore of the Circular Island were buried. Typically seen as a great honor as this is the last remaining piece of Hkar - to be buried on Hkar itself was seen in Drankor was special. 

While the party can investigate the crypts, there is nothing to find, really. The Concordia members were traditionally cremated and the crypts are simply a wall lined with small plaques, most of which have faded, and numerous decorated urns with ashes, many of which are broken/destroyed.

If they poke around, can describe various urns, typically decorated with the Concordia Pyrae symbol ("A stylized flame rising from an open book, with sparks forming the shape of a sun above") and sometimes abstract motifs or symbols that might relate to a person's life. 

## P3: Ash Garden

Beyond the crypt, the tomb opens into a contemplative space, with two ash gardens. Wall decorations here as well. 

- **Description:** Open spaces within the tomb where fine, silvery ash covers the ground, representing purification and the transient nature of life.
- **Details:** Visitors might walk through these gardens as a sign of respect, leaving footprints that slowly fade as the ash magically settles back into place.

The wall at the far end (west) has, carved over the paintings and decorations, the eye + shadow of Cha'mutte (a flaming eye surrounded by a ring of shadows). 

- **Cha'mutte's Eye**: Approaching within 5 feet of the eye triggers DC 19 Wisdom saving throw. On failure, frightened will in Dim Light or Darkness, and cannot approach the eye. Can repeat the save if end turn in Daylight, ending effect on success. Can be removed as normal by anything that removes the Frightened condition. 
- **Secret Door**: Opens with a hidden latch cleverly disguised in the wall mural among a textured part of the wall that is painted like flame. (DC 22 to notice)

## P4: Inner Sanctum

This was once the meeting space and private sanctum of Concordia Pyrae. The basic vibe is "secret clubhouse". Features include:
- Scorch marks on the wall suggest fire has destroyed a lot of this room.
- A large round table in the center of the room, open in the middle. The chairs/seating are long since gone but the table is built into the floor
- Eternal flames, that burn on the floor next to the door, and in the center of the table. 
- At the far wall, a pool of shimmering lava gives off waves of heat, contained in a large semicircular stone basin. The stone basin is magical, with transmutation magic; dispelling the magic allows the lava within to cool to stone and solidify. It will takes months to cool, however, unless aided by water, which can cool it rapidly. The guardian enters the portal simply by reaching down - immune to fire and the lava is not deep. A magical barrier keeps the lava from leaving the basin (or rather it instantly turns to stone if it does). 
- On the north wall, stone shelves survive, but little is on them. However, two apparently fireproof books can be found, both containing lore on the elemental plane of fire. Additionally, the Chronicles of the Concordia Pyrae (also fireproof) contains a history of the order which allows some inference of the history of the island as well. 
- The south wall has a large mirror. This is magical, and allows [[Ra'ghemdros]] to watch this room.
- A huge statue of a bipedal humanoid, about 8 feet tall, holding a massive whip. As you enter the room, you can see the stone of the statue heating up as it transforms into a flesh-and-blood creature of flame and shadow. 

### Combat

The guardian uses the [Moloch](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560875-moloch) statblock, with the following changes:
- The whip does Fire and Necrotic damage
- Instead of Major Image, the guardian can cast Scorching Ray at 4th level
- Instead of Stinking Cloud the guardian can cast Shadow of Moil. 

Anyone pushed into either the fire or the lava gets a DC 15 Dexterity check to dodge the push and avoid contact. Otherwise, take 8d6 Fire damage when you enter or end your turn in lava; 2d6 Fire damage when enter or end your turn in flames.  

When destroyed, the guardian crumbles to rubble. An iron key ring with an elaborate black key is obvious. This opens the portal.

### Secret Entrance

There is a portal to Cloudspinner's Prison hidden beneath the lava. Detect magic will reveal its presence, as probably will ghostly gaze, although getting through the lava is left as an exercise to the reader. 

The portal is locked; the guardian carries a key. 

## P5: Cloudspinner's Prison

This was not part of the original tomb. It was constructed by Cha'mutte, and consists of a small, dark chamber, technically on a demi-plane. The walls are made of shadow and constantly feel like they are shifting. Delwath might feel a strange sense of deja vu. This is similar to Cha'mutte's shadow armbands but permanent. 

In the center of the chamber, a pale gray smoky crystal floats. Through it, one can dimly see a naked woman, her long hair a brilliant tangle of reds and orange that contrasts with the almost silvery-gray tint of her skin (although hard to tell if that is just the crystal she is embedded in). She seems still, unmoving, as if in some kind of suspended state, but you can see her chest slowly, slowly rising and falling. She is definitely not dead. 

Ropes of shadow hold the crystal in place. 

Crystal is about 8 feet long, 3 feet deep, and 3 feet wide, so a total volume of about 72 cubic feet. 

Cloud spinner prison: held by 4 shadow tendrils. Invulnerable to damage unless in sunlight. Each have 40 hp, AC 15, need slashing, fire, radiant, lightning. Dark in the room is horribly oppressive. At the end of any minute spent in the room, must make a DC 19 Wisdom save (unless in daylight). Failing a save gives one level of Cha’muttes weariness. All levels can be removed by a short rest while in sunlight. 
  

Level 1: +1 exhaustion 
Level 2: feel oppressive weight; Str and Dex reduced by 1d4
Level 3: +1 exhaustion
Level 4: mind sapped by shadows. Wis and Cha reduced by 1d4
Level 5: disadvantage on all d20 Tests; +1 exhaustion 
Level 6: speed 0, will to live fading, can only barely remember the sun
Level 7: death
