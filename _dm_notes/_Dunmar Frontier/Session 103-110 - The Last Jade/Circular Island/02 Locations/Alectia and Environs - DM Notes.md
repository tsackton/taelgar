# Alectia and Environs

## Overview

The "city area" on the map is 11 hexes. This is like about 85 square miles, but only a small portion is actually the port: the rest is manor grounds of various societies, plus the grand forum complex. 

Major areas:
- Alectia itself (marked 1): one hex; but the town is really only along the coast.
- Outlying manors (marked 2): This region is a series of widely spaced towers, manors, and other "bases", some ruined completely, some repurposed. These include the towers that can be seen from a distance. These were the domains of various Drankorian societies, for the most part: not private wizard towers but things like pilgrim's aid organizations, research/scholarly organizations, etc. Some of these also have bases elsewhere, but all had a home domain near the city.
- Grand forum complex (marked 3): including the road to the forum, marked with dots.

## Port of **Alectia** 

(_ah-LEK-tee-ah_)

The city itself was nearly leveled by Cha'mutte's attack, and is not a safe place at all. Lingering plague curses haunt the place, and various kinds of haunts and dangers roam the lands. Worse, all the treasure that could be saved has already been gathered by [[Ra'ghemdros]] and her minions, so there is little, or nothing, left to find. 

If the party enters and looks around, or more likely scries or uses magic, they might notice the following landmarks that can still be discerned:

- Docks. Although many appear to have collapsed into the ocean, pilings of several docks remain, made of black marble, and one dock is partially intact but looks wobbly to walk on.
- Large building near the docks. Would have once been several stories, but the roof and upper walls are gone.
- Plaza. Shadows seem to creep over it and it looks dim even from outside
- Temple. Reduced to just foundations - looks like a large clearing now. Maybe can learn it was a temple with investigation. 
- The central road that departs into the interior from the main plaza -- passing through some of the tower-things -- is undamaged. 
- Otherwise just improvise. Ruined buildings, this is not in good shape. Nothing has a roof, nothing is more than one story even though much was once taller. 

From exploring, the basic fact that this was once a port should be obvious, as should that it has sustained major damage, not just from the passage of time, but from violent magic. 

### Encounters

**Plague Echoes**: Alectia suffered from major plague impacts, so [[Table - Plague Echoes|plague zones]] are very common. At least once per hour while exploring should trigger one. 

**Monsters**: Additionally, any [[Table - Circular Island Monsters|random monster]] could exist in the city. Consider adding the following lair action:

- **Crumbling Rubble.** Pieces of the structure crumble into three 5-foot cubes within 30 feet of the ruined keep creature. Creatures in those areas must succeed on a DC 12 plus PB Dexterity saving throw or take PBd6 bludgeoning damage. Those areas become [difficult terrain](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/basic-rules/adventuring#DifficultTerrain).

**Lair:** If the party explores the dock area, might encounter the following lair:

- Setup is one dock is sort-of intact; shallow water (rubble filled with some deeper patches) on sides. Rot angels float and try to lure targets into the water, where bog bodies attack. 
- Three [Rot Angels](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wel/coronal-hollow#RotAngel) lurk over the water. 
- Four [empowered bog bodies](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4516788-empowered-bog-body) lurk in the water. Will try to drag targets into the deep pools to drown them. 

**Dragonets:** Occasionally, [[Dragonet Info]] lurk in the town. These are much more likely than other dragonets to be dubious of [[Ra'ghemdros]], as the town is dangerous but untrafficked so it is a reasonable spot to sneak away, if you can avoid the monsters and the plague echoes. 

### Information

No treasure.  In lairs or ruined buildings, could theoretically find inscriptions or other clues to hint at the former purpose of this place. "Welcome pilgrims", even graffiti. No maps, probably. 

Will have to improvise depending on what they seem confused about or are looking for. 

## Environs

Once these were all kinds of towers and manors, usually constructed from the black marble of the island itself, but often with colorful additions. However, again, largely destroyed in the war, although suffered less than the port city. 

Terrain here is more varied. Any particular area is either ruined gardens / grounds / parks, or a tower/manor (only in hexes marked 2, not plusses).

### Terrain

Could be a park, grounds, whatever. These should be varied. Some are like grand Italianate manors, others like the place in England that is more of a parkland, others like Tulemar, draw random inspiration from anywhere. 

Most are either: overgrown and wild; dead, rotting, or petrified; sickly and unhealthy. 

Can grab an appropriate random encounter from random monsters above if desired, but generally fewer people died in the manors and there is less haunting and miscellaneous lingering bad magic. But mournling and earth sparks could be appropriate.

Dragonets only tend to live in manors with (a) surviving tower and (b) overgrown or wild grounds. 

### Manor

Each manor was once the home base of a [[Table - Drankorian Societies|Drankorian Society]]. Basic improv for manors is:

(a) Decide if [[Dragonet Info]] are present. If yes, there are no monsters, and structure should have some integrity. But dragonets might attack. If no, continue.
(b) Decide which society this was for, improvising from: [[Table - Drankorian Societies]]
(c) Pick a danger, a vibe, and a piece of info from below. Although, again, no treasure as this will have all been gathered by [[Ra'ghemdros]] minions. 

#### Manor Vibes

Could be: big square classic English manor; tower; Roman-style villa; classic fancy fairytale castle; something else. But generally would have a tower of some kind - watchtower, clock tower, etc, if not a tower. 

Again, these are mostly ruined. Less obvious signs of huge magical battle, but not generally truly intact (e.g., often roofs gone, or only three walls standing, so there aren't a ton of rooms to explore. sometimes might an intact tower or something).

#### Manor Dangers

Manors were not hit by plague in the same way, so no plague curses here.

One of two possibilities:
- Lair: use something from above, could e.g. reuse Lacuna or Flintmoth Swarm or Haunts or look at more ideas in [[Table - Circular Island Monsters]]
- Traps: manor is unstable or otherwise falling apart. This could either be damage ([Trap Effects](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2014/adventure-environments#TrapEffects)), magical effects e.g. [Eldritch Storm](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe/dungeon-masters-tools#EldritchStorms) / [Spell Hazards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe/dungeon-masters-tools#SpellEquivalentsofNaturalHazards), or [Unraveling Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe/dungeon-masters-tools#UnravelingMagic). Some manors might instead be [Infested](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe/dungeon-masters-tools#Infested). Could also use [Emotional Echoes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe/dungeon-masters-tools#EmotionalEchoes). 

#### Manor Info

Always should be clear that nothing of value is left. 

- Remains of a teleportation circle. Potentially still faintly magical, potentially with some clues as to what it actually is. Would usually point to either grand forum or interior base of the society. 
- Notes or papers (magically preserved?) giving some backstory or clues about the particular society
- Murals, frescoes, other kinds of art that is painted directly or embedded into the walls. Generally no one bothered collecting these.
- Signs of damage from the war. 

## Grand Forum

This is a huge open space - part coliseum, part amphitheater, still with lingering magic that allows voices to carry. Historically, this would be used for discuss, debate, plays, performances, many other things, including shows of magic. 

It is more intact than other places. Basic layout is big forum / circle in the middle, which is still largely intact. Outbuildings surround it, none with roofs, reasonably ruined. 

Not a lot of danger here. Not a lot of people died here, specifically, so there are not the kinds of hauntings in the port town, and don't have the dragonets or the magic of the manors/towers. 

I think probably okay for this to be relatively uneventful. But, some key pieces of information:

- Teleportation Circles here are **obvious**. Every single society has a portal to get to the Grand Forum, and it wouldn't take that much investigation to realize what they are. Sadly, none are working!
- The magic of the forum still works in fits and starts, with strange echoes of the past. can mostly improvise this but might catch flickers of things:
  - a pilgrim morality play
  - an argument about souls
  - some kind of drama / reenactment of the island as a refugee camp
  - various kinds of comedies and tragedies, steal from greek/roman/shakespeare
- not sure what else. maybe there is graffiti in the latrines? a partially-destroyed bath house complex? some kind of lingering echo / flashback of the full forum in the pre-war days?
- the main goal is this should feel worth exploring, even if in practice there is no treasure to find.
