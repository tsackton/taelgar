some of these should radiate magic
there are a variety of common items mixed in, maybe a dozen rings, 5-6 necklaces, a couple amulets, 10 household goods
there are all common magic items that have minor effects: turn water to wine, cast spare the dying, cleansing stones, various kinds of cantrip-level magic (e.g., light) that would have been not terribly uncommon in Drankor.

### Rings

#### (1) First Impressions

In the dragon’s lair, rings are meticulously organized in rows on floating, translucent shelves, suspended in the air for easy viewing. Gold, silver, and bronze bands reflect soft magical light from above, while the occasional gemstone glimmers brightly. The rings are arranged by metal type, with the more valuable rings displayed prominently on raised platforms or small pedestals.

#### (2) Eye-Catching Examples

1. **A Gold Signet Ring**: Engraved with the crest of a long-lost noble family, the crest itself radiates a faint light as though charged with ancient magic. The intricate detailing around the band is almost hypnotic.
2. **A Twisted Silver Band**: With a ruby so perfect it appears to pulse like a heartbeat, this ring's understated elegance and flawless symmetry draws immediate attention.

#### (3) Value Examples

- **Low Value**: A simple bronze band, slightly tarnished but polished to a soft shine. The only decoration is a simple etched design of interlocking triangles.
- **Medium Value**: A silver ring with a small lapis lazuli set into the band. The craftsmanship is solid, with delicate filigree tracing around the stone.
- **High Value**: A heavy gold ring bearing the imperial seal of the Drankorian Empire, with an emerald carved in the shape of a lion's head. The ring's weight and detailing indicate its importance.
