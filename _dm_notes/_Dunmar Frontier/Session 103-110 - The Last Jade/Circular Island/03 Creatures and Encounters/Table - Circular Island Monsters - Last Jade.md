# Random Monster Encounters

The following monsters might be found randomly across the Circular Island.

## Plague Echoes

These are monsters that are connected to the plague in some way. 

1. [Dirt Mournling](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485824-dirt-mournling), possibly with 10 or more [Earth Spark](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485830-earth-spark) minions. 
2. Three [haunts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485876-haunt). One is Large and has only 75 HP. 
3. One or two [Lacunas](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485919-lacuna), in an area (NOT pilgrimage site) with statuary. If running two and don't want a hard encounter, one can be broken already and at 1/2 HP. 
4. A herd of six [rotbeasts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485985-rotbeast) and a [rotting elephant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4717208-chaos-elephant)

## Magical Echoes

These are monsters that are connected to the magic of Drankor gone wrong.

1. Two [flintmoth swarms](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485841-flintmoth-swarm). Run as one gargantuan swarm that gets 2x actions a turn and can split into two pieces. Requires a place with stone they can be eating. When at 1/2 HP reduced to 1 action.
2. Two [arcane almalgams](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485774-arcane-amalgam). Run as one huge swarm that gets 2x actions a turn and can split into two pieces. When at 1/2 HP reduced to 1 action.

## Ra'ghemdros

These are minions of Ra'ghemdros in some fashion.

1. [Chimeras](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4485805-chimera-mcdm) (CR 6). Usually travel in packs of 2-4. These chimeras will attempt to flee if reduced below 25% HP, but they are relatively low intelligence and they can probably be picked off pretty easily once they panic. 
2. [[Dragonets - Last Jade]]. Might encounter any faction, although the true believers are more common close to the lair, and the questioners are more common far from the lair. 
3. Oozes: some combination of Tar Ooze (with tar ghouls) and Corrupting Ooze.
