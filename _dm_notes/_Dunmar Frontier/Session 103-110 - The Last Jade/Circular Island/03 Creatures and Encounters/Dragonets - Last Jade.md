# Dragonets

## Overview

Dragonets are originally inhabitants of Hkar. Whether they were co-created with humans, or came there from elsewhere early, no one knows, but many little flying dragon-like creatures could be seen in the cities of Hkar, particularly urban magical Hkar.

When Hkar fell, the dragonets scattered. Many ended up in the feywild (pseudodragons). Another large group fled to the far east, and eventually (perhaps) settled in the desert? In any case there are some not part of Drankor.

Many did not like Apollyon, perhaps most. Some looked to Cha'mutte as a hero. It is mostly these that ended up on the Circular Island. 

In the current day, there is a stable population of about 400-500 dragonets on the island. Most of them live among [[Alectia and Environs - DM Notes]].

## Factions

The dragonets of the Circular Island are divided, roughly, into three broad factions. Many of them have adopted the attitudes of [[Ra'ghemdros]], and are lazy and somewhat bored, especially with little left to find. Some are true believers and dedicated followers of [[Ra'ghemdros]], calling themselves the Cult of the Shadow. Some are questioners, wondering really if this is all their is to life, disillusioned by the 
Cult of the Shadow and put off by the nilism of the rest of their kin. 

To determine group randomly, roll 1d4. 

### Loafers (1-2)

- Put in the minimal effort; extremely unlikely to attack. 
- Amuse themselves by games and sport, often cruel; or by mindless destruction. Usually too lazy to bother with the forest. 
- Will be quite interested by newcomers. Attitudes may be teasing/taunting, distain, challenging. But generally anything that upends normal life is not so bad. 
- Often make fun of both other factions. 
- Heavily concentrated around the manor ruins outside Alectria. 
- Initial attitude usually Indiffferent.
- Occasionally find themselves on guard duty in Ra'ghemdros' lair environments. 

### Cult of the Shadow (3)

- Worship Ra'ghemdros. Most likely to spend time braving the storm to search for treasure in the jungles.
- Will always send someone from a group to alert Ra'ghemdros. But, if party speaks of coming to pay respects to her, might be convinced to lead the way. 
- Will not be overly hostile but definitely more suspicious. 
- Often pointlessly scouring places that have been searched a hundred times; very susceptible to bribery. 
- Will speak opening of Ra'ghemdros and assume the party knows of her glory. 
- Initial attitude likely Hostile.

### Questioners (4)

- The most curious about history of their people, the island, Ra'ghemdros and Cha'mutte and Apollyon. 
- Some will have even traveled parts of the Pilgrim Path
- Tend to spend their days socially, together; usually encountered in groups, typically far from the lair. Particularly like the Grand Forum. 
- Will be closed, initially, and need to be convinced to talk: equally worried about the party disrupting everything as hurting them.
- Theoretically most likely to tell stories of dragonets, to have heard stories of Dimitaur, to know the history of the island. 
- Initial attitude likely Indiffferent, but maybe Hostile due to closed/suspicious attitude.
