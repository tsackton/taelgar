# Random Clues

Generally, speaking, Ra'ghemdos does not care about cloth or paper, and so tapestries and books are likely the only treasure left to find. 

## Wall Murals 

1. **Faded Mural (Ruined Port):** On a partially intact wall within a ruined building near the docks, adventurers find a faded mural depicting a bustling port city. Ships adorned with banners bearing the symbol of Hkar unload their passengers - humans of all ages, many looking weary but hopeful, some clutching treasured belongings. Above them, mages channel powerful magic, their hands glowing with divine light. 
2. **The Exodus from Hkar:** A large mural on the side of a crumbling building depicts a massive chunk of land, uprooted and floating across the ocean. Tiny figures (humans) are shown in various states of despair and hope, clinging to the moving island.
3. **The Eight Divines Guiding the Way**: Inside a partially collapsed temple, a mural shows eight towering figures (the Eight Divines) standing in a circle around a glowing beacon, their hands extended outward, casting protective light over people below.
4. **The Colossi of Hkar:** Depicts the massive towering mechanical defenders of Hkar, fighting in battle against a fleet of ships. 

## Graffiti 

 **Desperate Plea**
- **Location**: Etched into a wooden beam in a collapsed house.
- **Message**: "The plague spares none. We flee to the Divines for salvation."

**Graffiti Warning**
- **Location**: Scrawled on the wall of a ruined tavern.
- **Message**: "Beware the shadow that consumes all. Trust not the dragon's lies."

**Dragonet Graffiti**
- **Location:** On walls within the ruins occupied by dragonets.
- **Message:** Scratchings and simple drawings showing a dragon overshadowing a cloaked figure.

## Other Clues


1. **Stone Plaque (Pilgrim's Path):** Embedded in the magical stone path leading from the statue of The Wanderer, the party discovers a weathered plaque. The inscription, written in an ancient language, speaks of the Eight Divines guiding and protecting the pilgrims on their journey to the summit. 
2. **Carved Initials and Dates (Grand Forum):** While exploring the relatively intact Grand Forum, the party discovers a section of wall in a crumbling antechamber where numerous initials and dates are carved into the stone. Most are accompanied by symbols associated with various Drankorian societies.
3. **Defaced Portrait (Manor Ruins):** In a ruined manor, amidst remnants of shattered furniture and broken pottery, the party discovers a large portrait, partially obscured by dust and cobwebs. The portrait depicts a stern-looking human figure dressed in ornate robes, their hands resting on a book that bears the symbol of Veritas Arcanum, the Seekers of Truth. Overlain on the portrait is a crudely painted symbol of Cha'mutte: a red eye. 
4. **Altar of Skulls (Temple Ruins):** While exploring the ruined temple at the Ruined Port, adventurers come across a makeshift altar constructed from stones and rubble. Perched atop the altar are several skulls - a mix of human and animal remains. Inscribed with writing in Draconic that hints at worship of [[Ra'ghemdros]].

## Table of One-Sentence Secrets and Clues

- The island was moved across the ocean by powerful magic to save the people of Hkar from destruction.
- Cha'mutte and Apollyon were once allies whose falling out led to catastrophic events on the island.
- The Cloudspinner possesses secrets that both Cha'mutte and Apollyon desperately sought to control.
- Ra'ghemdros, an ancient black dragon, now rules over part of the island from her lair.
- The divine statues and Pilgrim's Path are still protected by ancient, lingering magic.
- Apollyon's hidden tower contains knowledge and artifacts from before the island's downfall.
- The plague that devastated Drankor was unleashed as a result of betrayal between powerful figures.
- Dragonets on the island are divided, with some questioning their allegiance to Ra'ghemdros.
- The Grand Forum was a center of unity and learning that fell silent after the war.
- Hidden teleportation circles connect various ruins but are no longer functional.
- The elven memorials are sacred places untouched by the corruption affecting other areas.
- Cloudspinner is imprisoned beneath the island.
- Cha'mutte sought to destroy the gods themselves, leading to dire consequences for all.
- Apollyon aimed to ascend as a new god, imposing his will over humanity.
- The forest offers respite but hides remnants of the ancient wars and lost magic.
- The Eight Divines once blessed this island, and traces of their power can still be found.
- The Pilgrim's Path was both a physical journey and a spiritual rite of passage.
