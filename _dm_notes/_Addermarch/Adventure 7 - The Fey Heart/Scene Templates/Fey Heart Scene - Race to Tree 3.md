# The Lost Hunters

Optional encounter for **three level-5 PCs**, in the badger's tunnels between [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 1|the sleeping beast]] and [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]].

## Discovery

At a junction, looking reveals bootprints crossing themselves repeatedly. A directional mark cut into a root has been angrily slashed through. Listening down the side passage catches scraping and raised voices:

> "We came down."
>
> "Then why are we underneath our own footprints?"

[[Gwydren]] points toward the other passage: **"No, that's not the way."** The party can continue safely with him. If they investigate, he waits at the junction; **Gwydren is a noncombatant**.

## The hunters

Two of [[Lord Umbraeth|Umbraeth]]'s hunters and their hound are lost and arguing in an abandoned bedding chamber. Being fey gives them no command over Morlaith's tunnels. Both are capable, frustrated, and unwilling to accept responsibility.

- **Veteran:** Hacking at roots; insists the tunnels are deceiving the hound.
- **Handler:** Insists they should trust the hound and stop damaging the tunnels. Has put down their bow to manage the restless beast.
- **Hound:** Lean and black; presses toward passages the hunters have already tried.

They want a way out. Discovering the party has a guide prompts demands for passage. **If they recognize the heart, seizing and destroying it takes priority.**

## Chamber and approach

- Approximately **40 by 30 feet**, with room to stand comfortably.
- A thick central root mass blocks sight across part of the chamber. Substantial exposed roots provide cover.
- Two heaps of old bedding are difficult terrain.
- The entrance bends behind a root buttress, permitting an initial look from concealment.
- A second passage loops around the chamber and rejoins through another opening: a possible flanking approach, discoverable by scouting.

Watching and listening from cover reveals the three enemies, their distraction, root damage, equipment, and belongings. Moving closer or taking positions risks detection; call for Stealth when the approach warrants it. Leaving without revealing themselves leaves the hunters here.

**Treasure: A Deck of Illusion (https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228420-deck-of-illusions). They are puzzling over it; it is some kind of fey thing.**

## Combat

| Enemy | Stat block | CR | AC | HP |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Veteran | [Shadow Fey Forest Hunter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4340226-shadow-fey-forest-hunter) | 5 | 15 | 104 |
| Handler | [Scout Captain](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Monsters%3AScout%20Captain?expansion=34653), portrayed as a fey hunter | 3 | 15 | 66 |
| Hound | [Dire Wolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775812-dire-wolf), portrayed as a hunting hound | 1 | 14 | 22 |

**Difficulty:** 2,700 encounter-budget XP, between the [2024 Moderate and High budgets](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/dms-toolbox) for three level-5 PCs (2,250 / 3,300). The veteran's poison damage is the main threat. See [[Exploration Failure - Umbraeth's Hunters]] for its detailed running notes.

- **Veteran:** Shoots through gaps and changes position around the roots. Uses Shadow Traveler to reposition; it competes with Charging Archer for the bonus action.
- **Handler:** Releases the hound, then supports it with bow or shortsword. Follows it into melee to exploit prone targets; attacks with advantage gain extra damage. Use Aim and Uncanny Dodge.
- **Hound:** Presses the nearest intruder. Its bite knocks a Large or smaller target prone on a hit; Pack Tactics applies when an active ally is beside the target.
- **Morale:** Either hunter may bargain for escape when the fight turns against them. The handler can call off the hound.

## Exit and XP

- **Success:** Deal with the band and recover its treasure, benefiting the forest and earning **150 XP per PC** for the lost-hunters milestone in [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]]. No additional combat XP.
- **Skip or withdraw:** Continue with Gwydren; no reward for merely discovering the hunters.

Return to the route and continue toward [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]].
