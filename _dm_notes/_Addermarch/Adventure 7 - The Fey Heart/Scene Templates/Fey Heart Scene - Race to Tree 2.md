# Tree Attack

Required forest combat, after [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 1|the sleeping beast]] and the opportunity to investigate [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 3|the hunters]]. This is the second required journey obstacle.

## Setup

Gwydren leads the party out of the badger's tunnels into a small clearing at the closest exit to the Singing Tree. About 30 feet to the left, a massive spreading oak stands in a hollow. From it comes occasional knocking sounds; a group of thorn-trees are battering it with clubby/spiky branches. A path leads straight ahead through the dense woods. Gwydren points to it as the way to the Singing Tree. About 80 feet ahead, a massive boar is rubbing its tusks along a trunk, blocking the path. Stirges swarm around the boar.

Give the party a moment to look over the scene before combat begins.

The boar charges when combat begins, whether the party advances along the path or attacks the refuge's attackers. The stirges follow the boar and attack its victims. The trees ignore combatants until and unless they are attacked.

Backstory is basically: the Singing Tree's angry song has driven the nearby forest into irrational violence. An enraged beast blocks the onward path. Nearby, corrupted trees batter an ancient hollow tree; something inside occasionally knocks back. The beast and trees are part of the same encounter, with two objectives:

- **Clear the path:** Defeat the boar and stirge swarm, then continue toward the Singing Tree.
- **Save the refuge:** Also kill the trees attacking the hollow trunk, allowing the party to explore inside.

The trees concentrate on destroying the refuge unless the party intervenes. The refuge holds throughout the encounter. If the party leaves its attackers alive, they destroy it before the party returns.

## Exits and XP

Overcoming the forest obstacle earns the second required journey milestone: **200 XP per PC**, under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]].

- **Boar and stirges defeated; refuge abandoned:** Leave the trees attacking the refuge and proceed to [[Fey Heart Scene - The Singing Tree Encounter|the Singing Tree]].
- **All enemies defeated:** A door opens in the hollow trunk and its inhabitant invites the party into [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 4|the optional refuge and social challenge]], before continuing to the Singing Tree. Access and hospitality earn no additional XP; the optional milestone requires the later social success.
