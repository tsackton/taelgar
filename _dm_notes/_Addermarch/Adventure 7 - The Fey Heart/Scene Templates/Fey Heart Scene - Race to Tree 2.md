# Tree Attack

Required forest combat, after [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 1|the sleeping beast]] and the opportunity to investigate [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 3|the hunters]]. This is the second required journey obstacle.

## Agreed direction

The forest attacks the party, making the anger of [[Morlaith]] and the Singing Tree tangible. The attackers and encounter mechanics remain to be chosen.

A **strong win** opens access to [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 4|the final optional encounter]]. Ordinary success still allows progress toward the Singing Tree. Access to the optional encounter and success in its social challenge are separate accomplishments.

## Working connection to the optional encounter

%% Speculative ideas generated by AI at user request; not canon. %%

The attacking trees surround an ancient hollow tree and tear at it as well as attacking the party. Something inside occasionally knocks back, giving the party a visible opportunity to do more than survive the fight.

Preserving the hollow tree would count as a strong win. Afterward, a door opens in its trunk and its inhabitant invites the party inside. This is a proposed definition of strong success; the exact criterion is still open.

## Still to design

- The attacking trees or other forest creatures, terrain, and combat objective.
- What distinguishes a strong win, and how the party can recognize that opportunity.
- The costs of ordinary success or failure.

## Exits and XP

Overcoming the forest obstacle earns the second required journey milestone: **200 XP per PC**, under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]].

- **Strong win:** Reveal access to [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 4|the optional refuge and social challenge]]. Its XP is earned through the later social success.
- **Continue on the route:** Proceed to [[Fey Heart Scene - The Singing Tree Encounter|the Singing Tree]], including if the party declines the optional refuge.
