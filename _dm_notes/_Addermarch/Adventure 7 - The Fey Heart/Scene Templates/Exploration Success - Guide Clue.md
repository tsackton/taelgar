# Exploration Success: Guide Clue

The basic idea is that a variety of pieces of information will lead to the guide. Some might be scene specific, others generic. 

## Foxlings

Will give name and basic direction ("across the river") no matter what. On success, will tell more about where he likes to hang out. Tends to all the biggest trees - the ones that grow tall and broad and are ancient. He cares for and tends them the most. Cares about the dryads, especially after Sweetwillow died. The trees can lead you to him

On failure, just "go that way, across the river."

## Dryad's Story
Learned from [[Fey Heart Scene - Fork in the Path]]


Sweetwillow was a friend of Gwydren's. Probably a close friend? He was worried, he saw the creeping corruption, Morlaith no longer doing anything about the darkness that crept across the Sunset Gate or that crept north from the Darkwood where Umbraeth ruled. Morlaith was Morlaith, but not the Morlaith of old. 

He tried to warn her - leave, flee. Come across the river with me. Wouldn't come. 

Never knew why she was targeted. Something about the grove, maybe. Too sunny, too bright and cheery. Morlaith is weak where the forest grows thin. No one knows. 

She died. 

Can spin this out. Gwydren warned me, etc. 

## Canopy

Can get directions and bearing - see the larger trees across the river, gauge how far river is. 

Perhaps can also rescue someone trapped by spiders.
