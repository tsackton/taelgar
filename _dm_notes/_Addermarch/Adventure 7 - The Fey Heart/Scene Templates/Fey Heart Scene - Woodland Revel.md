# Fey Heart Scene - Woodland Revel

Don't want this to be the classic satyrs. Should be something a bit weirder/stranger. 

Drumming in the woods. DC 14 Wisdom saving throw, on failure feel compelled to chase it. Repeat save when taking damage, if deafened, and at the end of 1 minute. 

Small fey. Sprites and pixies and brownies, creepy crawlies. 

Drummer is a stony creature, big arms, covered in roots and moss, but not a large creature itself. Use https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195194-satyr-revelmaster stats if needed.

Just improvise as needed. 

### Dance Mechanics

**The compelled dance:** The drumming magically draws affected characters into the revel and compels them to dance.

**Compulsion stages**

1. **Hear the drumming:** Make a DC 14 Wisdom save. On failure, the character is compelled to dance.
2. **After one minute:** Repeat the save. Failure sends the character into a frenzy.
3. **After another minute:** Make one final save. Failure locks the character into the frenzy.

Before the frenzy locks, taking damage also grants a repeat save. Any successful save ends the compulsion. 

Once the frenzy locks, there are no further saves. Otherwise, after an imprecise stretch of dancing, the character collapses and gains one level of exhaustion. Nearby hunter howls sound, and the revelers vanish.

The compulsion can always be ended through deafening, incapacitation, or unconsciousness. 

## Outcomes

**Dance to collapse:** Characters who fail to escape the compulsion eventually dance themselves to exhaustion and collapse, gaining one level of exhaustion. This brings the hunters close: nearby howls sound, and the dancers vanish, leaving the party to face the danger alone. See: [[Exploration Failure - Umbraeth's Hunters|Exploration Failure: Umbraeth's Hunters]]

**Escape and anger the drummer:** If the party escapes the compulsion but angers the drummer, the encounter ends in a fight or their departure without learning anything.

**Escape and keep participating:** The successful approach is to escape the compulsion while continuing to participate in the revel voluntarily. This gives the dance bard an opportunity to shine. The drummer takes an interest and asks what they are looking for, opening a conversation about Gwydren. See: [[Exploration Success - Guide Clue|Exploration Success: Guide Clue]] and [[Finding the Guide]]
