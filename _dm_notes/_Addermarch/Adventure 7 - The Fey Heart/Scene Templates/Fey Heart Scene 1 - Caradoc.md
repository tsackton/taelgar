# Caradoc's House

The adventure begins at Caradoc's house, with Caradoc introducing the problem. Keep this short but there are core materials and clues Caradoc needs to give. 

Left [[Serethwyn Tor]] on May 28th. Two day's walk back to Caradoc's, so will arrive on May 30th in the evening. Start then - dinner with Caradoc. 

Caradoc will remind them of the circumstance, and also that the clock is ticking. They probably only have a few days, no more than a week, before the tree awakens. (See: [[Fey Timeline]]).

Caradoc needs to:
- Point them to the nearest usable Fey portal into [[Morlaith]]'s domain: [[Fey Heart Scene 2 - Into the Feywild]]
- Warn them if they want to find their way in time, they'll need to find a guide. Hard to know who will help but use your judgement. The denizens of Morlaith's domain are not evil, they are just strange and old. 
- Give them a magic item that can be used at last resort to summon a fey lord for help, but be wary, they will demain a high price. See: [[Summoned Fey Lord Details]]
