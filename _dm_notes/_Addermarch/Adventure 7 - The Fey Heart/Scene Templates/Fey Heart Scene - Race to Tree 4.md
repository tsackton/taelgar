# Hidden Refuge and Social Challenge

Rumplewick, an old [leprechaun](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4635152-leprechaun), shelters a household inside an ancient hollow tree. Proud and fussy, with patched clothes and carefully kept crockery, he answers questions with questions, pounces on careless promises, and enjoys being cleverly outargued. His luck has kept the household safe as the forest grows violent.

## Intact refuge — before the Singing Tree

If the party saves the refuge during [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]], a small door opens onto an impossibly spacious, warm kitchen. The noise outside disappears. Food and hospitality are freely given. A dropped cup lands upright without spilling.

Gwydren mentions their destination. Rumplewick makes the opportunity explicit:

> “I could lend you a little luck against that song. Very useful stuff, luck. But if mine goes off adventuring, whose is minding my house?”

Explain the blessing below so the players know what is being offered.

**Social challenge:** Convince Rumplewick that lending his luck will help protect the household.

- **Concern:** The rescuers have earned gratitude, but lending luck leaves the household more vulnerable while the forest is still dangerous.
- **Useful arguments:** Show the heart and explain how returning it will end the violence; describe a credible plan for reaching the tree; offer practical protection for the household until the song ends.
- **His replies:** To an empty promise of safety: “Splendid. Shall I tell the next tree you've forbidden it?” To a clever argument for ending the song: “So lending away my good fortune is how I keep it? That's a nicely crooked bit of sense.”
- **Resolution:** A convincing plan that addresses Rumplewick's fear earns the blessing. If the outcome remains uncertain, use one **DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. On failure, he keeps his luck; hospitality continues.

### Borrowed Astonishing Luck

Custom encounter blessing:

**Each PC can once use their reaction when they fail a saving throw, before its effects resolve, to reroll it. They must use the new result. The gift lasts until the next dawn.**

Rumplewick stays behind. Saving his household during [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]] earns **150 XP per PC** under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]], whether or not he lends his luck. Securing the blessing earns no additional XP; the blessing itself is the reward for the social challenge.

## Ruined refuge — after the Singing Tree

If the attackers were left alive, the party returns to a ruined home. Everyone survived through astonishing luck. The household salvages bedding and broken crockery, grateful for the song's end but grieving its home. They need help recovering belongings and finding shelter.

Rumplewick still insists on wiping feet: the doormat sits before a doorway with no house behind it. “Lost the house. Kept the manners.” Discovering the survivors earns no milestone; any reward for further help remains undecided.
