# Hidden Refuge and Social Challenge

Final optional encounter before [[Fey Heart Scene - The Singing Tree Encounter|the Singing Tree]], unlocked by a strong win in [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]].

## Agreed direction

A hidden reward that provides respite and a genuine social challenge. The forest should continue to grow stranger; the respite can express that through impossible space and strange hospitality.

The strong combat win unlocks the opportunity. Winning the social challenge earns useful aid and the optional XP milestone. Discovering the refuge or receiving hospitality alone does not earn that milestone.

## Working idea

%% Speculative ideas generated by AI at user request; not canon. %%

Preserving the hollow tree earns an invitation from its fey inhabitant. Inside is an impossible refuge: a spacious warm room or a little garden within a trunk that could not contain it. Outside noises disappear, and an odd host is setting supper.

The host offers rest, but has withdrawn from the angry forest and guards the haven's remaining peace. The party's social objective is to persuade the host to provide aid against the Singing Tree's song. Helping requires surrendering something precious, perhaps lending some of the magic that keeps the hollow quiet.

The players need to understand that reluctance and offer a persuasive argument, reassurance, or acceptable exchange. The hospitality gives them space to discover what matters to the host.

## Outcomes and XP

- **Social success:** Secure useful aid and earn the second optional journey milestone: **200 XP per PC**, under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]].
- **Social failure:** Leave without that aid or milestone and continue toward the Singing Tree.

With Sweetwillow's story already earned, this milestone plus another optional minor can allow level 6 before the finale, as set out in the XP plan.

## Still to design

The host, the exact aid and its cost, the social challenge, and the strong-win condition that reveals the refuge. Hospitality and respite should remain distinct from earning the host's consequential support.
