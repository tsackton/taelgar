# Exploration Failure: Umbraeth's Hunters

The basic vibe here is if the party gets off track, Umbraeth's Hunter's find them and attack. If Umbraeth's Hunters get the heart, they will attempt to destroy it.

Consistent progress toward finding the guide keeps the hunters at bay. Investigating optional locations and gathering useful information count as progress.

Failure brings signs of the hunters. This is a fail-harder mechanism. For example:
- repeatedly following the looping path
- the woodland revel compulsive dance
- failing to find a safe way to cross the river

Excessive resting can also bring the hunters, though careful rests should mostly just advance the [[Fey Timeline]].

Signs of the hunters happen first - howls, etc. A hard push can make them retreat again, though here resting definitely leads to an attack. 

Defeating a hunter encounter gives a reprieve for a while.

## Detailed Encounter Notes

### Difficulty reference

For **three level 5 PCs**, using the [2024 encounter rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/dms-toolbox):

| Low budget | Moderate budget | High budget |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,500 XP | 2,250 XP | 3,300 XP |

Add monster XP directly, without a multiplier for enemy count. These values measure encounter difficulty; the hunters grant no combat XP under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]]. Ratings assume a reasonably ready party; prior damage, exhaustion, and depleted resources can make these encounters harder.

### Option 1 — Wounded Duskhound

| Creature                                                          | Count | Listed CR |  AC |              Starting HP |                 Listed XP |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----: | --------: | --: | -----------------------: | ------------------------: |
| [Duskhound](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5341585-duskhound) |     1 |         8 |  16 | **90**, reduced from 135 | 3,900 before modification |

**Difficulty:** The unmodified creature exceeds the High budget by 600 XP. **Treat the wounded version as provisionally High**; this is a DM estimate, not a recalculated CR. Reducing HP alone does not establish a reliable adjusted XP value.

**DM notes**

- **Horns are a shared defensive resource.** Legendary Resistance and Fey Rebirth each consume a horn. With both horns intact, it also has Magic Resistance and an extra reaction usable only for Escape. Track the horns visibly in your notes.
- **90 HP does not describe its full endurance.** Spending both horns on rebirth can add roughly 42 more points of damage needed to finish it: each rebirth returns it to 1 HP with 20 temporary HP. This estimate assumes rebirth requires a remaining horn; the current trait does not explicitly state that limit.
- **Its offense is unchanged.** Three greatsword hits average 33 damage, and misses still deal 4 each. Two hits can trigger a DC 15 save against being stunned; two longbow hits can instead trigger restraint. Losing a turn is especially consequential for a three-PC party.
- **Budget its actions carefully.** Activating an aura and using Lash compete for its bonus action. Retaliate uses its normal reaction; the extra reaction from intact horns is Escape-only. Escape and its movement teleport can make it difficult to pin down.
- **Most dangerous and least predictable option.** Its control effects and damaging reactions can keep it threatening despite the HP reduction. Avoid treating it as interchangeable with the two lower-budget encounters.

### Option 2 — Yeth Hound and Dire Wolves

| Creature | Count | CR each | AC | HP each | XP each | Total XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [Yeth Hound](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560968-yeth-hound) | 1 | 4 | 14 | 51 | 1,100 | 1,100 |
| [Dire Wolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775812-dire-wolf) | 4 | 1 | 14 | 22 | 200 | 800 |
| **Total** | **5** | | | | | **1,900** |

**Difficulty:** Between Low and Moderate: 400 XP above Low and 350 below Moderate.

**DM notes**

- **Fear is the main source of swinginess.** Baleful Baying requires a DC 13 Wisdom save. A frightened character starting within 30 feet must move away before acting, even along a hazardous route. This can separate the party and expose characters to opportunity attacks.
- **Baying takes the hound’s action.** It cannot normally bay and bite on the same turn. Its bite averages 11 damage, or 25 against a frightened target. A successful baying save grants immunity to all yeth hounds’ baying for 24 hours.
- **Check the party’s damage options.** The hound is immune to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that are not silvered. This can make its 51 HP much harder to remove for some parties.
- **Use the linked dire wolves’ current statistics.** They have 22 HP, Pack Tactics, and an 8-damage bite that knocks a Large or smaller target prone on a hit, with no additional save. Concentrating all four on one PC raises the immediate danger considerably; their low HP also makes the encounter vulnerable to area damage.
- **Light matters.** The hound is banished to the Ethereal Plane if it starts its turn in sunlight. Establish whether the encounter location has actual sunlight before running it.

### Option 3 — Shadow Fey Forest Hunter

| Creature | Assumed count | CR | AC | HP | Total XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [Shadow Fey Forest Hunter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4340226-shadow-fey-forest-hunter) | **1** | 5 | 15 | 104 | **1,800** |

**Difficulty:** Between Low and Moderate: 300 XP above Low and 450 below Moderate. The original option does not specify a count; this assessment assumes one hunter. **Two hunters would total 3,600 XP, above High.**

**DM notes**

- **Strong damage against one target.** Two longbow hits deal 36 damage using the printed averages, including poison damage with no separate save. Charging Archer can add a dagger attack after the required movement, for another 16 damage if it hits.
- **Terrain changes the fight.** Stealth +10, a long-range bow, and three uses of Shadow Traveler make concealment and distance valuable. A concealed opening volley can be much harsher than starting within easy reach of all three PCs.
- **Use the linked version.** It has Evasion, poison damage, and Charging Archer; do not add the older version’s Sneak Attack. Charging Archer’s bonus attack competes with Shadow Traveler for its bonus action.
- **A lone enemy is vulnerable to control and concentrated attacks.** Its three saving throw proficiencies and Evasion help, but it has no Legendary Resistance. Its actual difficulty can fall quickly if the party pins it down.
- **Sunlight weakens it.** It has disadvantage on attacks and sight-based Perception checks while in sunlight.
