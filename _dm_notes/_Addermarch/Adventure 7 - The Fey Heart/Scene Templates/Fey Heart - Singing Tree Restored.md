# Singing Tree Fight

## Singing Tree — Fully Regrown

_Huge Plant_

**AC** 16 (natural armor)  
**HP** 184 (16d12 + 80)  
**Speed** 15 ft.  
**Target CR** 8 (3,900 XP; PB +3)

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|20 (+5)|8 (−1)|20 (+5)|12 (+1)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|

**Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +6  
**Vulnerabilities** Fire  
**Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing  
**Immunities** Psychic  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Druidic, Elvish, Sylvan

### Traits

**Singing Heads.** The tree begins combat with **five** intact singing heads. Each can be targeted individually by attacks or spells that target a creature: AC 13, 10 HP, with the tree’s damage resistances, immunity, and vulnerability. Damage to a head does not reduce the tree’s HP. Area effects damage the tree without separately damaging its heads.

When all heads are destroyed, the tree cannot use **Sing**, **Maddening Chorus**, or **Weep**, and its active song effects end.

**Crushing Branches.** The tree has Advantage on Slam attacks against creatures Grappled by it.

**Grasping Limbs.** A creature can be held by only one of the tree’s grapples at a time. A Slam grapple replaces any existing root grapple on that creature. Its constriction damage consequently applies only once at the start of the creature’s turn. Each grapple ends if the creature moves more than 20 feet from the tree or the tree becomes Incapacitated.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The tree makes three attacks, using Slam or Throw Needles in any combination.

**Slam.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +8, reach 20 ft., one target. _Hit:_ **12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage**. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is also Grappled (escape DC 15). While held by this grapple, it is Restrained and takes **5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage** at the start of each of its turns as the tree constricts it.

**Throw Needles.** _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +8, range 60/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage.

**Sing.** One creature within 60 feet that can hear the tree must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or become overwhelmed by violent impulses.

On each of its turns while affected, it must use its action to make one weapon attack or cast one damaging cantrip against a randomly selected creature it can see and legally target, including the tree but excluding itself. Select the attack or cantrip before determining the target; all attacks from that cantrip target the selected creature. If no legal target exists, the creature can act normally. It otherwise controls its movement, Bonus Action, and Reaction.

**At the end of each of its turns, the affected creature repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.**

The effect also ends immediately if the target cannot hear the tree, the tree becomes Incapacitated, or all its singing heads are destroyed. The tree can continue singing while taking other actions. A creature that succeeds on an initial or repeat saving throw is immune to Sing and Maddening Chorus for 24 hours.

### Villain Actions

The tree can take one villain action per round, at the end of another creature’s turn. Use them in the following order, each once per combat. It cannot use them while Incapacitated.

**1. Grasping Roots.** Each creature of the tree’s choice standing on the ground within 40 feet must make a **DC 15 Strength saving throw**. On a failure, roots pull it up to 30 feet toward the tree, stopping within 10 feet if possible, and Grapple it (escape DC 15).

A creature held by this grapple takes **7 (1d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage** at the start of each of its turns. This root grapple does not impose the Restrained condition.

**2. Maddening Chorus.** The tree uses Sing against every creature of its choice within 60 feet that can hear it. Each makes its own saving throw.

**3. Weep.** The tree sheds two needle swarms into unoccupied spaces within 20 feet. They act immediately after the tree’s turn, beginning on its next turn. They collapse when the tree dies. This action requires at least one intact singing head.

### Needle Swarm

_Medium swarm of Tiny Plants_

**AC** 13; **HP** 18; **Speed** 20 ft., climb 20 ft.  
**STR** 6 (−2), **DEX** 16 (+3), **CON** 12 (+1), **INT** 2 (−4), **WIS** 10 (+0), **CHA** 3 (−4)  
**Vulnerable** Fire  
**Resistant** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing  
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained, Stunned  
**Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 10  
**Languages** —

**Swarm.** Can occupy another creature’s space and move through openings large enough for a single needle. Cannot regain HP or gain Temporary HP.

**Needles.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 0 ft., one creature in its space. _Hit:_ 7 (2d6) Piercing damage, or 3 (1d6) while the swarm has 9 HP or fewer.
