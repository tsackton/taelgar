# Sleeping Beast

After [[Gwydren]] agrees to guide the party, he leads them towards a shortcut he knows. The entrance is blocked by Old Brindlethorn.

## Approach

Gwydren leads the party toward a shortcut through an ancient badger's sett (tunnels): "Come, come." As the woods grow darker: "Don't worry, won't be long."

An earthy mound fills the root-framed entrance. Then it breathes, and loose soil trickles from its flank. Gwydren's confidence turns to concern: she normally sleeps deep underground.

"Old Brindlethorn! Why is she sleeping up here?"

Her tunnels connect distant parts of the [[Thornweald]]; this passage emerges near the Singing Tree.

## The badger

- **Name:** Old Brindlethorn
- **Appearance:** Huge, low, broad; silvered fur packed with earth, small shrubs growing along her back. Maybe 20 feet long but only about 8 feet at the shoulder. 
- **Personality:** Ancient, slow to answer, proud of her tunnels. Remembers Gwydren as a youngster. Values comfortable bedding, sound roots, and quiet; permits travelers who leave her bedding and closed chambers alone.
- **Distress:** The Singing Tree's disturbance has driven her from her underground beds. Proximity to the Fey Heart triggers angry, restless nightmares. **She cannot wake while the Singing Tree is active, but the party can influence her dreams.**

## At the entrance

- Her head and forequarters fill most of the entrance, shoulder against one side.
- **She reacts:** When Gwydren calls her name, her ears turn toward him, her snarling briefly softens, and one paw reaches toward his voice. Her eyes stay closed. Make this obvious without a check: sounds and other sensations can soothe her or influence where she moves.
- **Leverage and support:** A stout fallen limb lies beside a solid root that could serve as a fulcrum. Her shoulder lifts slightly with each movement, showing where a lever or support might fit.
- **Risky access:** A low gap opens and disappears beneath an arching root beside her flank, revealing clear tunnel beyond. Someone could slip through to pull, position supports, or secure a rope from the other side, but risks being crushed against the wall.
- She growls, writhes, and claws in her sleep. Each violent movement works her shoulder and foreleg deeper into the entrance.

Other clues - there are footprints into the tunnel - Umbraeth's hunters. Two humanoids, and a large wolf. DC 15 to discover (Survival). Disadvantage while badger is anxious and moving. 

## Running the encounter

**Goal:** Make the crossing safe for everyone, including Gwydren: settle her movements, shift or support her safely out of the way, or use sufficient magic. Gwydren waits outside until they achieve this; he will not risk a momentary gap between her struggles.

"She hears us, even in that dream. Settle her, turn her, get something beneath that shoulder—then we can go. Quickly, before she wedges herself in."

He also makes clear that killing her means losing his guidance.

**Three successful rounds stabilize the crossing; Closure 5 blocks it.** Both start at 0. Start rounds when the party begins work or spends significant time observing; brief questions are free.

Each PC gets one substantial action per round, in their chosen order: aid another PC, contribute to the approach, or fail and waste the action. Use **DC 14** for uncertain attempts; useful assistance grants advantage, and straightforward preparation needs no roll.

- **Judge the round:** If their actions make meaningful progress toward a safe crossing, mark **one successful round**. A coherent assisted success is enough. Successes accumulate across rounds; failures do not erase them.
- **At three successful rounds:** Everyone passes safely, before that round's Closure increase. No further crossing checks.
- **Then add 1 Closure every round**, including successful rounds. Soothing contributes to stabilization; it does not pause Closure.
- **Most failures only waste the action.** Clearly risky mistakes that provoke a violent movement add **1 Closure immediately**; telegraph that risk before the roll. Being crushed deals **2d6 bludgeoning damage**.
- **At Closure 4:** Gwydren warns that another twist will wedge her fast. Anyone beyond her must return before closure if the party retreats.
- **At Closure 5:** She becomes tightly lodged and the attempt ends. Moving her now requires her conscious cooperation, impossible while the Singing Tree remains active.

Keep the numbers hidden; describe her reactions and the changing gap. Improvise around the party's plan:

- **Soothe or redirect her dreams:** Voice, music, touch, or other sensations can gradually quiet her, turn her, or encourage her to roll aside.
- **Brace, expand, or move:** Use the fallen limb, supports, ropes, leverage, or magic to make the passage safe.
- **Squeeze through:** Reach the far side to help with the plan. Failure deals **2d6 bludgeoning damage**, leaves the PC on the starting side, and adds **1 Closure**. Crossing alone does not resolve the encounter.

Magic sufficient to transport everyone safely can bypass the challenge.

## Exits and XP

- **Success:** Take the tunnels. **150 XP per PC** under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]]; helping her is optional. Continue toward [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 2|the tree attack]], with an opportunity to investigate [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 3|the optional hunters]] first.
- **Failure:** The entrance becomes blocked; take the surface detour, most likely with a forced hunter fight. Options: [[Exploration Failure - Umbraeth's Hunters]]. PCs still beyond her when the gap closes are separated from Gwydren and anyone outside.
- **Kill Brindlethorn:** Gwydren abandons them. Entering her tunnels without his guidance leaves them lost.
