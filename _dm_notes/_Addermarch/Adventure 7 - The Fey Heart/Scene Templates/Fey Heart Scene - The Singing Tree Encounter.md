# The Singing Tree

The tree has not fully regrown. It has no combat turn, hit points, or accompanying enemies; its angry song makes the PCs attack each other while they try to return its heart.

## Setup

A small, misshapen sapling grows from the old root bed, about **60 feet from the clearing's entrance**. An obvious hollow among its roots is where the heart belongs. The heart pulls toward it, making the objective clear.

**Objective:** Get the heart to the hollow and spend an action to restore it.

## The song

Use initiative to track turns and end-of-turn saves.

- **Entering the clearing:** Each PC who can hear the song makes a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.
- **Failure:** On each of their turns, the affected PC must spend their action making **one weapon attack or casting one damaging cantrip** against a randomly selected other PC they can legally target. Choose the attack or cantrip before rolling the target. If needed they must move into range, and they must use the most damaging option possible.
- **End of each affected turn:** Repeat the **DC 15 Wisdom save**, ending the effect on a success.
- **Successful initial or repeat save:** The PC is free of the song for the remainder of this encounter.

PCs who secured **Borrowed Astonishing Luck** at [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 4|the refuge]] can use their one-use reaction to reroll a failed initial or end-of-turn save, before its effects resolve. They must use the new result.

Passing the heart to an unaffected companion, restraining an affected PC, and using mobility magic can all help get the heart to the roots.

## Returning the heart

A PC carrying the heart beside the hollow can spend **one action** to restore it. **No additional check is required.**

Returning the heart immediately ends the song and every remaining compulsion. Award **1,000 XP per PC** for completing the adventure, under [[Fey Heart XP Rewards]].

If the party left the refuge's attackers alive, they encounter [[Fey Heart Scene - Race to Tree 4|the ruined refuge and its surviving household]] after this scene.
