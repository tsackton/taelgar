# The Following Foxes

After traveling for a bit, can potentially hear noises off to the side of the path (DC 22 initially, decreases to DC 15 after the first pass of the Fork in the Path). Actively searching for signs of life or "anything in the forest" can give advantage and/or reduce DC to 15 (they are not great at hiding). 

These are two teenage-ish beastfolk foxlings: Kip and Flo, brother and sister. 

Curious of strangers. But shy, and a little scared of "people" by which they mean non-fey. Initially skittish and wary.

Generally speaking two main approaches to get them to talk. 

(1) Assuage their fear. Convince them that the party is safe to talk to, fey-associated, whatever. 
(2) Exploit their curiosity. Be entertaining and exotic enough to overwhelm the foxling's skittishness

This shouldn't be too hard, most DCs around 15. Guide them through using skills to learn more about the foxes. 

## Outcomes

### Win Over the Foxlings

They can tell the party things - pick something from [[Exploration Success - Guide Clue|Exploration Success: Guide Clue]].

**Won over:** “You need [guide]. They know the old paths. They live across the river—we can show you the road down.”

### Fail to Win Over the Foxlings

Distraction, time, etc add up, maybe get off the path, etc. Add one hunter tick to [[Exploration Failure - Umbraeth's Hunters|Exploration Failure: Umbraeth's Hunters]]

**Still suspicious:** “Go ask [guide], across the river! They know what to do with lost people. Follow that road. Leave us alone.”
