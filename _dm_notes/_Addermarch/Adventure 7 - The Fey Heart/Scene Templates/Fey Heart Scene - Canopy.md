# Fey Heart Scene - Canopy

The road passes an enormous fallen trunk leaning into a living tree, providing a natural ramp into the branches. Through a gap above, they glimpse open sky and something that might be the river; there is a tall, 120-foot tree that would provide an obvious view. The opportunity to scout is immediately apparent. There are also webs, and across the canopy can see a creature wrapped in webs, hanging limp but occasionally struggling, still alive. 

Two options from here:
- Climb the tall tree
- Rescue the spider's prey. 

Combat is avoidable here with stealth. 

### Climb the Tree
 A character can try to sneak past the spiders and climb the tall tree. The view reveals the river and the ancient trees beyond it.
 
- **Lower branches:** DC 8 Strength (Athletics) to reach the spiders’ level.
- **Canopy lookout:** DC 15 Strength (Athletics) to climb the long, exposed trunk toward the viewpoint, 120 feet above the forest floor.

A failed check causes a slip, followed by a DC 15 Dexterity save to catch hold. Failing that save means falling; secured ropes can arrest the fall.

### Rescue

The party can fight the spiders in the lower trees and free their captive. Through *Speak with Animals*, the marten supplies a missing detail about finding Gwydren. This information can be obtained without attempting the high climb.

 A pine marten is wrapped in silk and hanging beneath a thick branch. Its head is partly exposed, and it twists and hisses when the party approaches.

Through *Speak with Animals*, it is frightened, indignant, and eager to bargain for release. It knows Gwydren as the antlered creature who tends the oldest trees across the river, so it can supply whichever detail the party still needs.
