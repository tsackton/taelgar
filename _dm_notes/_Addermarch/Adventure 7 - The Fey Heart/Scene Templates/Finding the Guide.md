# Finding the Guide

Guide is **Gwydren** **GWID-ren**

A lean, stag-headed fey. Lichen grows around the antler bases; his dark green cloak is patched with leaves and moss. Has the manner of an exhausted woodland keeper. Knows individual trees by names nobody else remembers, and regards the hunters as trespassers he has tolerated for too long. He has lived for a long time in the forest. Connected to morlaith's vibe. skeptical of humans, a little bit. but, feels responsibility, and unlike morlaith is potentially somewhat anti-gloomshaper. 

## Where

Guide is reachable only after the river. the ideal is that the party needs 2-3 pieces of information from the guide note to find the guide, though more information makes the guide encounter easier.

post-river, there needs to be opportunities to still get more information if needed, but also an opportunity to actually just find the guide.

## Convincing the guide

they need to convince the guide to help them. the pieces of information they can learn from exploration should build to explain the most likely candidate to be a guide, how to find them, and what they want.

there will be a fallback option for a combat encounter in case they don't get enough information to convince the guide any other way, the guide can send them on a mini-quest (but this costs time and resources)

i think the guide is annoyed by the changes in the forest - umbraeth's hunters roaming loose, and the singing tree corruption spreading. wants things back to "normal".

so in principle has the same goals as the party - this is what makes them a good guide.

the gate here is convincing the guide, through words or actions, that they are on the same side.

so, with information, the party can show the guide they are committed, or they can do the mini-quest to show by action instead of words

## Suspicion

so, the guide is just suspicious in general. the fact that they are carrying the heart makes it more suspicious, not less - everyone knows a human took it! maybe they are doing something nefarious with it.

i imagine the guide is a little paranoid
