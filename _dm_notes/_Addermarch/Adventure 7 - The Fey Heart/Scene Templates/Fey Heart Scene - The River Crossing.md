# The River Crossing

Forest road ends abruptly at a dark, overgrown river. Massive trees grow to the edge. Strangely hot, and silent, still and stifling, but the river is swift-moving and deep. Approximately 50–60 feet across.

### Crossing the River

Two obvious options: 

- **Swim:** A DC 15 Athletics check can get a character across. Failure means the character is swept downstream, eventually scrambling out on the original bank. Failure by 5 or more also causes one level of exhaustion.
- **Find a crossing:** Exploring the banks with Survival (DC10) can reveal a safer crossing point, a shallow or narrow point or other kind of crossing. 
- **Other approaches:** Allow practical solutions involving magic, ropes, improvised construction, or other player ideas.

**Hunter pressure:** A failed Athletics check to swim or a failed Survival check to find a crossing wastes time and counts as stalled progress, see: [[Exploration Failure - Umbraeth's Hunters]].

### Displacer Beasts

Two displacer beasts (1,400 XP total), approximately Low difficulty for three level 5 PCs under the 2024 rules. Run this encounter on the riverbank before the party begins crossing, if combat suits the session.
