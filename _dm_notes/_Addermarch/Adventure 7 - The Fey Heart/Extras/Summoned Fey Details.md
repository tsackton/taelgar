## Adventure Review

**Emergency intervention:** Breaking the wand summons Calanthë’s servants, who intervene decisively and win the immediate confrontation. Their arrival functions as a deus ex machina; it does not require a balanced reinforcement encounter.

They then provide substantial aid in returning the heart: they know the route to the Singing Tree and accompany the party as a powerful escort. The PCs still carry out the return.

**Price:** Accepting this intervention commits the party to Calanthë’s service, launching a Feywild campaign arc intended to span the next 2–3 character levels. Caradoc warns them beforehand that invoking the wand entails a substantial obligation.
