# Fey Heart XP Rewards

Milestones for [[01 Overview - The Fey Heart|The Fey Heart]]. Rewards recognize discoveries and progress toward completing the quest. All awards are **per PC**, not divided among the party.

| Milestone level | XP per PC |
| --- | ---: |
| Minor | 200 |
| Major | 500 |
| Adventure completion — returning the heart | 1,000 |

## Minor milestones — 200 XP each

### Required

- [ ] **Learn the guide’s name.** Identify Gwydren as someone who can guide the party through the forest. See [[Fey Heart Scene - The Following Foxes]] and [[Exploration Success - Guide Clue]].
- [ ] **Learn roughly where the guide lives.** Discover that Gwydren lives across the river, around the big trees. See [[Exploration Success - Guide Clue]].
- [ ] **Journey obstacle 1 — to be designed.** Overcome the first required obstacle between Gwydren and the Singing Tree.
- [ ] **Journey obstacle 2 — to be designed.** Overcome the second required obstacle between Gwydren and the Singing Tree.

### Optional

- [ ] **Learn more precise details about where to search for Gwydren.** Narrow the search to the biggest, oldest trees and learn that the trees can lead the party to him. See [[Exploration Success - Guide Clue]].
- [ ] **Optional journey milestone 1 — to be designed.** An additional opportunity for quest progress between Gwydren and the Singing Tree.
- [ ] **Optional journey milestone 2 — to be designed.** A second additional opportunity for quest progress between Gwydren and the Singing Tree.

## Major milestones — 500 XP each

### Required

- [ ] **Get the party across the river.** Complete [[Fey Heart Scene - The River Crossing|the crossing]] by any workable method, reaching the part of the forest where Gwydren can be found.
- [ ] **Convince Gwydren to help.** Secure his cooperation as the party’s guide. Finding him is part of reaching this milestone, not a separate reward. The interaction is still to be developed; see [[Finding the Guide]].

### Optional

- [ ] **Recover Sweetwillow’s story.** Learn about her friendship with Gwydren, his warning about the spreading corruption, and her decision to remain. This also provides insight into what Gwydren cares about and evidence for establishing trust. Obtain the story through the Sylvan writing inside the dead willow or by getting the willow to talk. See [[Fey Heart Scene - Fork in the Path]] and [[Finding the Guide]].

## Adventure completion milestone — 1,000 XP

- [ ] **Return the heart to the Singing Tree.** Complete the adventure’s central objective. This reward equals two major milestones.

## Awarding the milestones

Award each milestone once, whichever scene supplies it. The [[Fey Heart Scene - Woodland Revel|drummer]], rescued pine marten, foxlings, and canopy scouting can provide missing guide information. A flying familiar can obtain a discovery; scouting itself earns no separate reward. Learning the same clue again does not earn another reward.

Sweetwillow’s story and learning what Gwydren cares about form one major milestone. Do not award a separate minor for the same discovery. Opportunities after the river and before meeting Gwydren can supply missed milestones; they do not add new rewards to this list. Those second opportunities remain to be designed.

Combat earns no separate XP. Defeating Umbraeth’s hunters or the optional displacer beasts is not a milestone. Reward the information or quest progress obtained, whether the party uses combat, conversation, stealth, or another approach.

## Level 6 timing

Each PC currently needs **2,700 XP** to reach level 6. The required route supplies four minor and two major milestones before the tree. Optional progress adds Sweetwillow’s story (one major) and up to three minors.

| Progress | XP gained per PC | Level 6? |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Required route before the tree: 4 minor + 2 major | 1,800 | No |
| Required route plus returning the heart | 2,800 | Yes |
| All 7 minors, without Sweetwillow: 7 minor + 2 major | 2,400 | No |
| Required route + Sweetwillow + 2 optional minors: 6 minor + 3 major | 2,700 | Yes |
| All milestones before the tree: 7 minor + 3 major | 2,900 | Yes |

Learning Sweetwillow’s story is necessary to reach level 6 before the final encounter. With that story, the party also needs two of the three optional minors. Otherwise, returning the heart puts even the required-only route over the threshold.
