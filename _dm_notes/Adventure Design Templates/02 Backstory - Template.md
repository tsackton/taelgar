# Adventure Backstory Template

## How to use this note

Use this note for the fictional situation and world context that supply the adventure's design. Keep established vault facts, adventure-specific inventions, and unresolved possibilities distinguishable. Link the sources you rely on and mark uncertain or speculative material clearly.

This note provides inputs to the Overview; it should not duplicate decisions about the goal, X-factor, inertia, momentum, failure handling, or structure. It does not need to follow the same shape every time. Use only the sections that help this adventure.

## Sources and anchors

Existing notes, facts, places, people, groups, events, or mysteries this adventure depends on.

## Current situation

What is true when the adventure begins? Describe the active problem, relevant recent events, and immediate circumstances without yet outlining scenes.

## Relevant backstory and hidden truth

What happened before play that explains the current situation? Use a short lore account, timeline, faction history, hidden truth, or another form suited to the adventure.

## Forces and motives

Who or what is involved? What does each relevant force want, what are they currently doing, and what might they do without the PCs' intervention?

## Setting rules and constraints

What facts about the setting govern the adventure? Record relevant supernatural rules, geography, limitations, timelines, relationships, or other constraints that later design must respect.

## Design ingredients

Extract useful material from the background without deciding the structure yet. Include only categories that help:

- clues and revelations;
- guides, allies, and other useful NPC roles;
- locations and landmarks;
- hazards and opposition;
- stakes and consequences;
- rumors, secrets, or misunderstandings.

## Questions, conflicts, and provisional ideas

Record unresolved lore questions, source conflicts, uncertain interpretations, and adventure-specific possibilities that have not been adopted. Do not silently resolve them or promote them into established setting facts.
