Two hundred years ago, the world ended in fire and darkness. 

The great lord Cha’mutte and his massed armies reached through the Divine Veil, slew the embodied gods that bound and constrained the children of the Firstborn Divinities, and shattered the Land of the Dead itself. Though the great lord Cha’mutte did not return from beyond the veil, he forever changed the world of Taelgar, reshaping it in his image.
