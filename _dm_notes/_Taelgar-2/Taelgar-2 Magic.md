# Magic after the Godfall

Historically, divine magic was the most common and accessible form of magic in Taelgar. With the gods unable to grant miracles, divine magic is no more. So nothing that depended on divine magic works, though there may be rare and narrow exceptions about preserved relics or other "batteries" of divine magic. 

Arcane magic is still common and powerful; primal magic exists but is likely rare. 

Chalyte is rare and powerful, ala Hextech
Chaos glass is common, and functions like gunpowder.

There may be other kinds of "extraplanar glass" that exist - pending development/invention.
