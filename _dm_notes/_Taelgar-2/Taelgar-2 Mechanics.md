## Species

Species now use standard D&D 5.5e rules, not the homebrew Taelgar rules. Species magic, powered by the firstborn, is gone. 

Halflings, Humans, Dwarves, and Orcs are obvious and clearly available. 
It is possible/likely that the Godfall makes plane-touched humans more common, opening up Aasimar and Tieflings as common playable species and giving Taelgar-2 a bit more diversity of playable species. 
Kobolds and Goblins are both likely to be good options to move from rare/fringe to common. 

For some campaigns, Centaur might be an interesting option. 
For some campaigns, a Hobgoblin might be an interesting option.

For mechanics reasons, it might be interesting to come up with a way to make Goliath work, but not obvious what this would be (unless perhaps a few Stoneborn survive, which I don't love, or something something hand-wavy with Giants). 

Others are not available: 
- Elves, Stoneborn, Lizardfolk are extinct. 
- Fey have closed themselves off to the decayed world of Taelgar. 
- Kenku, without Aerin, are likely no longer a true species anyway and better regarded as monstrosities (or perhaps are still enslaved by hobgoblins, and perhaps can no longer speak). 

## Classes 

Divine magic no longer exists, so Cleric and Paladin are not playable classes. However, I think everything else survives, though the flavor might change. 

Guns are common and available, and standard steampunk rules apply.
