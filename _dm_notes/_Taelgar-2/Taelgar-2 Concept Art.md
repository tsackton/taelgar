# Taelgar-2 Concept Art

Concept art and visual brainstorming for the world described in [[Taelgar-2 Current Day Brainstorming]], [[Taelgar-2 Technology]], [[Taelgar-2 Magic]], and [[Taelgar-2 Species After the Godfall]]. These images establish mood, contrasts, and possible design languages. Individual architectural, technological, geographic, and costume details remain provisional.

## Chardon and the West

### Chardon, City of Wonders

![[taelgar-2-chardon-panorama.png|1000]]

*Chardon seen across the Chasa estuary. Pale chalyte light and monumental towers rise above the chaos-glass refineries, crowded housing, docks, and military river traffic of Low Chardon. This is the clearest overall statement of the city's vertical inequality; the precise skyline and placement of structures are illustrative.*

### The Foundry Market

![[taelgar-2-foundry-market.png|1000]]

*A hiring yard in Low Chardon at dawn. Refugee labor, improvised repair culture, industrial injury, and armed oversight exist beside one another beneath an active refinery. This image is primarily a reference for the lived-in social character of the lower city.*

### The Old Chardon Canal

![[taelgar-2-old-chardon-canal.png|1000]]

*A possible view of the [[Old Chardon Canal]] as a defended southern extraction corridor. Barges and ships move bulk cargo, a short railway serves the locks, and a rare chalyte skyship passes overhead. The geography and canal architecture are mood references rather than a literal map.*

### Chardonian Powered Exosuit

![[taelgar-2-powered-exosuit.png|1000]]

*A chaos-driven, chalyte-regulated work suit designed for docks or refineries and adaptable as rare military armor. The patched frame, replaceable components, exposed pressure system, and dangerous power source are more important than the exact design. This does not imply that powered suits are common.*

## Rumors of Eastern Dunmar

### A Sorcerer-King's City

![[taelgar-2-dunmar-sorcerer-city.png|1000]]

*One possible Chardonian imagining of a fortress-city in eastern Dunmar: a settlement gathered around a guarded well and dominated by a hobgoblin citadel that controls water, magic, and survival. This is deliberately not attached to a named city or ruler.*

### The Road to the Last Well

![[taelgar-2-dunmar-refugee-broadside.png|1000]]

*A stylized refugee broadside or Rememberer's illustration of the eastern wastes. The hobgoblin outriders, glass storm, broken aqueduct, and colossal figure in the clouds should be read as a frightened story rather than an objective record. The figure may be a sorcerer-king, a magical manifestation, propaganda, or nothing more than the shape of the storm.*
