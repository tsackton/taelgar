# Gifting of the Soul

The Gifting of the Soul is a concept from [[Mos Numena]] theology that describes the divine miracle by which a living being receives a soul at birth. It is the counterpart to the [[Gathering of the Soul]], the miracle by which the soul of the dead is drawn into the [[Land of the Dead]].

%%^Campaign:none%%

Sources: [[Sembaran Funerary Traditions]], [[Tyrwinghan Funerary Traditions]], [[Land of the Dead]], [[Magic in Taelgar - Open Questions]], [[Divine Spark]], and [[Soulstuff]].

It is unclear the extent to which in-world knowledge links the idea of the Gifting of the Soul to the idea of "gifted soulstuff" in [[Metaphysics of Souls]] / [[Metaphysics of Creatures]]
