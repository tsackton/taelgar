# Obito
*(oh-BEE-toh)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> An [[Embodied Gods|embodied god]] (they/them)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

Obito, the Music Maker/Merchant, reflects joy, community, friendship, creativity, creation, commerce, and trade.

In halfling religious practice, Obito reflects the idea that all interactions involve both giving and receiving, and they are the divinity that sees the joy in both sides of this exchange.
