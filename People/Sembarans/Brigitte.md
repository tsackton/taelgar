# Brigitte
*(bree-ZHEET)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info

Brigitte “Brick” was an enforcer in [[Gareth of Tollen]]’s criminal gang in [[Peydon]], collecting debts and roughing up troublemakers. She was a towering, broad-shouldered woman with a broken nose and a thick forearm scarred in a knife fight.

Brigitte had worked as a carpenter’s apprentice but was dismissed after breaking a man’s arm in a tavern brawl. Gareth recruited her for her strength.

She was killed attempting to escape custody by the [[Heroes of Cleenseau]].
