# Annabelle
*(ah-nah-BELL)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Sembara|Sembaran]] [[Humans|human]] (she/her)

Annabelle is a steely-eyed warrior devoted to [[The Night Queen]] and a companion of [[Raoul Boulain]], [[Lenora Belles]], and [[Lucien of the Night Queen]]. She defended [[Sembara]] during the [[Undead Attacks in Sembara]]. After the attacks, she helped destroy [[Malach's Phylactery|Malach’s phylactery]] and defeat the lich.
