# Ilsa Lemoine

Ilsa Lemoine commanded the [[Army of the West]]. In January 1720, during the undead crisis, she ordered companies and garrisons not already marching to [[Dunmore]] to depart for the city.

By April DR 1720, [[Claude Leclerc]] had been promoted to command the army.
