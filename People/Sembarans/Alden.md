# Alden
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> An alien fungal entity (he/him)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_PageDatedValue")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Alden is an alien fungal child of the [[Prisoner in the 27th Room]], who appeared as a human servant and guide in [[Lord Hulda]]'s employ at [[Dandelion House]]. He is short, round, pale, quiet, and stronger than he looks. Although attentive and usually smiling, he rarely volunteers much conversation. He has a pronounced fear of fire, keeping his distance from campfires and open flame. He has a deep, almost rapturous, affection for Dandelion House and its dandelion imagery.

Alden, along with many other fungal aliens, was killed by [[Lord Hulda]] when the [[Prisoner in the 27th Room]] was set free and [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 07|disappeared to a strange alien realm]].
