# Rufus of Ashcombe
*(ROO-fus)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Sembara|Sembaran]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Rufus is the adopted son and apprentice of [[Alban of Ashcombe]], administrator of the temple to [[The Wildling]] in [[Ashcombe]]. A trans man originally from a small village along the [[Enst]], he is rapidly learning Alban’s knowledge of local traditions. He rarely speaks about his family background, and his opinions are respected by villagers, particularly young families. Some villagers say he occasionally displays a dark anger.
