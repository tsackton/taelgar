# Elise Moreau
*(eh-LEEZ moh-ROH)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info
> A [[Sembara|Sembaran]] [[Humans|human]] (she/her)

![[elise-moreau.jpg|left|400]]

Elise Moreau is the Baroness of [[Fellburn]] and the twin sister of [[Hubert Moreau]]. During the [[Undead Attacks in Sembara|undead attacks of DR 1720]], she and others countermanded Hubert’s order to keep the city gates closed and rode out to rescue survivors.

On January 25, 1720, [[Jacques Bellemont]] awarded Elise the barony and exiled Hubert for abandoning the people outside the gates, including their father, who was killed.

%%^Campaign:none%%

Little has been canonically established about Elise beyond her [[Fellburn Audience with the Duke|actions during the undead attacks]] and her [[Cleenseau - Interlude - After the Siege of Fellburn|appointment as baroness]]. Fellburn is a [[Fellburn Audience with the Duke|vassal barony of the Duke of Wisford]], but the details of that relationship remain undeveloped.

Elise is intended to be young, ambitious, and brave, somewhat frightened by her unexpected responsibility, and upset about her twin's fate while understanding the judgment against him.
