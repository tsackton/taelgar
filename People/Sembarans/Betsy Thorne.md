The daughter of [[Jon Thorne]], cousin of [[Beatrix Thorne]] and a former member of the Cleenseau town watch.

In late April 1720, Betsy left [[Rosalind Essford|Rosalind's]] guard for [[Asineau]], where she joined the guard. She wanted a fresh start away from comparison with her cousin and felt that [[Ames Benthey]], her former captain, was a poor teacher.
