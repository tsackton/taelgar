# Gwydren
*(GWID-ren)*

Gwydren is a beastfolk, alternatively described as a large moose-headed humanoid or a stag-antlered creature, who has lived in the [[Thornweald]] for many years and knows its old paths. He spends most of his time around the large, old trees of the deep woods. He is a friend of many dryads and nymphs of the forest, including [[Sweetwillow]].

Gwydren stands about eight feet tall, with light brown fur and broad antlers hung with moss. He wears robes of plants and lichen beneath a woven fern cloak.
