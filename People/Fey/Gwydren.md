# Gwydren
*(GWID-ren)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Beastfolk|beastfolk]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Gwydren is a beastfolk, alternatively described as a large moose-headed humanoid or a stag-antlered creature, who has lived in the [[Thornweald]] for many years and knows its old paths. He spends most of his time around the large, old trees of the deep woods. He was a friend of many dryads and nymphs of the forest, including  [[Sweetwillow]].

%%^Campaign:none%%

A weary woodland keeper, wary of humans, who wants the forest restored. He can be found among the ancient trees across the river. The party heard of him in [[Addermarch - Session 21]] but has not yet met him.
