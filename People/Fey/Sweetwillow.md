Sweetwillow was a dryad of the [[Thornweald]]. She occupied a large willow incongruously growing in the center of a bright, sunny hilltop meadow. She was killed by a swarm of death butterflies, a corruption from [[Lord Umbraeth]], [[Harrow]], [[Wend]], or something else, unable to be stopped by the [[Morlaith|Briarheart]] at a low ebb of his powers.
