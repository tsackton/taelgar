Old Brindlethorn is an ancient, cottage-sized badger who lives in the [[Thornweald]]. She is low and broad, with silvered fur packed with earth and small shrubs growing along her back.

Slow to speak and proud of her tunnels, she values her quiet and her privacy. Her extensive sett provides routes through the forest for travelers who know its tunnels, though she demands that those who would pass ask politely, and leave her bedding and closed chambers undisturbed.
