# Tilly Brineheart
*(TILL-ee BRYNE-hart)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Halflings|halfling]] (she/her)

Tilly Brineheart is a halfling spellcaster who sailed aboard the [[Stormdancer]] with its captain, [[Milo Thistlefoot]]. She can shape water, transform into sea creatures, and wield healing magic.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
Captured with Milo and members of their crew near [[Omi]], Tilly was imprisoned in the [[Elemental Plane of Water]] until the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] rescued them. She later helped the Fellowship fight [[Ra'ghemdros]] before departing with Milo to seek their former crew and outfit a new ship.
%%^End%%
