# Tilly Brineheart
*(TILL-ee BRYNE-hart)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Halflings|halfling]] (she/her)

> [!image|aside]
> ![[tilly-brineheart.jpg]]
> *Tilly Brineheart, halfling sailor.*

Tilly Brineheart is a halfling sailor, wanderer, adventurer, and spellcaster of considerable talent. She loves exploration and seeing new places above all else, with the possible exception of her husband [[Milo Thistlefoot]], who shares her love of adventurer. 

Tilly is rarely content to remain in one place for long. Aboard the [[Stormdancer]], she has sailed across the [[Sea of Storms]] to the [[Far South]] and traveled the uncharted southwestern coasts of [[Apporia]].  She is protective of her crew and confident in her abilities, trusting her magic and command of water to keep her safe. She is not one to pass up a challenge when she thinks she can help, no matter the danger. 

Her attitude is cheerful even under trying circumstances. She is brisk, resourceful, and capable of humor under pressure, with enough pragmatism to keep resentment from dictating the plan. She is sustained by her close, affectionate relationship with her husband, [[Milo Thistlefoot]], with whom she travels

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
In DR 1749, Tilly, [[Milo Thistlefoot]], and the crew of the [[Stormdancer]] investigated a strange quiet disturbance off the northwestern coast of [[Apporia]], and were captured by allies of [[Buruli]] near the undersea merfolk city of [[Omi]]. Tilly was imprisoned in the [[Elemental Plane of Water]] until the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] rescued her and her companions, including [[Milo Thistlefoot]]. 

She then traveled with the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] for a time, as far as the [[Circular Island]]. Here, she helped defeat [[Ra'ghemdros]] before departing with Milo to seek their former crew and outfit a new ship.

**Roleplaying notes**:

- Adventurous sea-mage (circle of the sea druid), with a love of new places and a strong sense of responsibility for her crew. 
- Restless in one place, eventually seeking something new. In play, eventually got bored even of Vindristjaran, watching the sea from above, and wanted to find her own path. Not just exploration, but in control of her own destiny, is the way she wants to live. 
- Protective of her crew and her husband Milo; her protectiveness comes in part from her confidence in her magic and her ability to take direct action to solve problems. 
- She has a dry humor and is prone to understatement. Captivity is “quite inconvenient,” for example.
- Her marriage is affectionate and companionable. She loves Milo but is not defined by him. 

**Post-Vindrisjarna Journey:**

- After leaving the Fellowship to find their remaining crew and outfit another ship, she and Milo resume their southern adventures. 
- Session 125 shows their vessel moving rapidly through calm tropical waters, a giant whale’s fluke appearing as Milo calls, “It’s working, Tilly”.
- Session 139 shows that they have reached a tropical port with shining golden domes.
