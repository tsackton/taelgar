# Megren
*(MEG-ren)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Dragonets|dragonet]] (she/her)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Megren is a [[Dragonets|dragonet]] who lives in a well-maintained tower on the [[Circular Island]], with extensive grounds and a small village among the surrounding ruins. Megren was gifted the tower by [[Ra'ghemdros]] as a reward for bringing her a [[Jade Piece of Rai's Hand|fragment of Rai’s jade hand]] found on the mainland, a rare new treasure for her collection. 

Despite accepting this reward, she has little loyalty to the black dragon.
