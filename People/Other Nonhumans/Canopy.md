# Canopy
*(KAN-uh-pee)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> An awakened tree (they/them)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Canopy, also known as the Eldest, is an awakened ceiba tree and the informal leader of the talking plants and animals of the [[Awakened Jungle]]. They remember the magical awakening that first granted awareness to the jungle’s inhabitants.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
In the spring of DR 1749, Canopy was afflicted by chaotic corruption that spread through the jungle’s interconnected roots. On (DR:: 1749-05-16), the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] destroyed the corrupted vines surrounding them, allowing Canopy and the jungle to begin recovering.
%%^End%%
