# Canopy
*(KAN-uh-pee)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> An awakened tree (they/them)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Canopy, also known as the Eldest, is an awakened ceiba tree and the informal leader of the talking plants and animals of the [[Awakened Jungle]]. They remember the magical awakening that first granted awareness to the jungle’s inhabitants.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
In the spring of DR 1749, Canopy was afflicted by chaotic corruption that spread through the jungle’s interconnected roots. On (DR:: 1749-05-16), the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] destroyed the corrupted vines surrounding them, allowing Canopy and the jungle to begin recovering.
%%^End%%

%%
- **Vibe and personality:** Friendly after being rescued, with slow, Entish speech. Surprised by bipedal creatures, whom they think of as strange monkeys.
- **The awakened trees:** The roots connect the awakened trees and plants across the jungle. We are different, yes, but all of us are also one, connected by the links of nutrients and energy that run through the soil. I can feel my siblings rumbling in the distance. Some are sleepy, and some never fully awakened.
- **The experience of waking up:** It was electric - it felt like a fire racing through the jungle, spreading through the roots of the forest - but not a bad fire, it was magic, some kind of ripple of the Plane of Magic itself, maybe, altering reality. It was like a river of energy pouring into my roots, Canopy says, a flood of something wonderful as new pathways opened in my mind. At first only the biggest trees, with the deepest and strongest roots, were awake. But the strange awakening magic infused everything. Soon the howler monkeys, and the bats, who fed on fruits and berries began to gather and talk. Later, some tigers came to speak to the trees. Canopy has even heard that some river dolphins speak now. It was not everything in the jungle, for some reason. The tapirs, for example, never seemed to speak or the small birds and insects and frogs. And over time, within a year or two, no new creatures awoke, but the children of those who could speak inherited their parents minds.
- **The peace of the jungle:** The past 30 years have largely been peaceful, we've stayed in the jungle. No one comes from the outside. Except for sometimes from the sea, somethings big flying lizards come. But the monkeys usually drive them off.
- **The experience of corruption:** The chaos felt like a hot, burning seed that pierced my mind and spread, quickly, through the roots of the jungle. It was not of this world, it felt like a fragment, shrapnel of some kind, and explosion. I was lost in a chaotic dreaming, where nothing was fixed. I felt as if I was floating in some empty space but below me the world constantly changed: a giant rock with overgrown forests one minute, a powerful wind blowing spheres of ice and water the next, then suddenly a rolling pasture turned to an erupting volcano. It felt like nothing was fixed and even as I watched and dreamed the world broke apart and reformed again and again.
