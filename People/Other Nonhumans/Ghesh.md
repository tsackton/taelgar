# Ghesh
*(GHESH)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Dragonets|dragonet]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Ghesh is a reddish-brown [[Dragonets|dragonet]] with a greenish tint on his back, who leads a small group living in a partly ruined tower near [[Alectia]] on the [[Circular Island]]. He is a friend of [[Megren]]. Bossy and competitive, he enjoys gambling and flying contests.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%

[[Kenzo]] [[Session 108 (DuFr)|befriended Ghesh over dice]], giving him a golden circlet in exchange for secrecy and an introduction to [[Megren]]. Ghesh used the treasure to secure [[Ra'ghemdros|Ra’ghemdros’s]] recognition of his group’s claim to their tower. He later [[Session 110 (DuFr)|arranged an audience with Ra’ghemdros]] and supported the dragonets fighting against her loyalists. After her defeat, he and Megren were rewarded with their choice of treasures from her hoard.

- Appearance: reddish-brown, with a greenish tint on his back (u1108–u1115, u1340–u1342). He says he was born seventeen years earlier, during the May 1749 encounter; no exact birth date is established (u1350–u1356).

- Gambling: boasts about winning, elbows companions away from his coins, and becomes surly when he loses. Kenzo’s first win earns stone tokens rather than the coins Ghesh has taken. The dragonet caught cheating by Soul Sight is not identified as Ghesh (u0656–u0663, u1144–u1183, u1317–u1327, u1425–u1430).
- Bargaining: invents a story that the golden circlet came from a newly discovered jungle ruin. He calls down his companions and promises better quarters and an end to constantly moving and sharing territory. He also tells them to report Kenzo’s visit if he has not returned by nightfall (u1634–u1706).
- Competition: challenges Kenzo to a flying race, tries to knock away the raven whistle, and calls its use cheating. Describes the arena games as flying tag or wrestling (u1953–u2023).
- Ra’ghemdros and territory: finds her true believers weird and off-putting. His group sees gifts of treasure as a way to be left alone or rewarded. Larger groups are eyeing their tower, and he wants Ra’ghemdros to recognize it as theirs. He says he is not fond of her and would not mourn at her funeral (u2039–u2071, u2194–u2212).
- Local knowledge: warns about haunted ruins and dreams of choking death; avoids the forest’s speaking trees and has not ventured into the forbidden west. His claim that dragonet ancestors made the island is his belief, not established history (u1434–u1455, u2080–u2157).
- Megren and Kenzo: Megren gives him Kenzo’s elvish coin to add to the treasure he will trade for his tower. Their friendship is explicitly confirmed; she is pleased for him and will not spoil his bargain. Kenzo also gives Ghesh a carved likeness of himself (u2523–u2531, u2639–u2687).
