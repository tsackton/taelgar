# Ra'ghemdros
*(rah-GHEM-drohs)*

%%^Campaign:dufr%%

Ra’ghemdros, the Light Stealer and the Final Shadow, the Devourer of Drankor and the Last Empress of Hkar, the Eternal Night and the Enslaver of the Secret Sunset, was an ancient black [[Dragons|dragon]] and a daughter of [[Cha'mutte|Cha’mutte]] who ruled the [[Circular Island]] for nearly seven centuries.

Once a lieutenant of her father, Ra’ghemdros fought in the attack on [[Amberglow]], where [[Cloudspinner]] wounded her. When Cha’mutte withdrew from the Circular Island following the destruction of [[Drankor]], he left Ra’ghemdros in command, charged with guarding the captive archfey. She established her lair beneath the ruined thermal baths of the [[Concordia Pyrae]], close to Cloudspinner’s prison. Long after her father’s death, she remained there, gathering the treasures of the island and keeping watch over the enemy she could neither forgive nor safely destroy.

Ra’ghemdros was immense, at least thirty feet from snout to tail, with a wingspan of roughly fifty feet. Her scales were an intense, unbroken black, her eyes luminous green. Along her left side, almost from shoulder to tail, Cloudspinner’s wound shimmered red and orange, like a sunset burned into her flesh. Her breath mingled acid and darkness, and she could vanish from sight or become an incorporeal shadow. Beneath the baths, her domain opened into caverns of corrosive pools, acid geysers, and grasping tendrils of darkness.

Among the island’s [[Dragonets|dragonets]], Ra’ghemdros claimed every coin, jewel, and precious object as her own. Concealing treasure could mean death; bringing her a sufficiently pleasing gift could win a tower and the security of her protection. Generations of dragonets scoured the island for tribute, eventually venturing to the mainland as new treasures became scarce. Some served her with devotion, while others learned to flatter her, satisfy her demands, and keep out of her way. Though she rarely emerged from her lair, fear of her power sustained her rule.

Her [[Ra'ghemdros' Hoard|hoard]] filled chambers arranged with extraordinary care. More than a million coins and thousands of pieces of jewelry surrounded displays of ancient art and magical treasures. Rings were ordered by color, earrings covered walls, and she spent hours each day tending and rearranging the collection. She coveted trophies beyond her reach, especially [[Apollyon|Apollyon’s]] throne, and readily bargained with mortals willing to take risks on her behalf. Their deaths mattered little to her: those who volunteered to challenge Apollyon were, in her words, “willing sacrifices.”

For all her grand titles and contempt for mortals, Ra’ghemdros feared Apollyon and remained consumed by her hatred of Cloudspinner. She watched the archfey’s prison through a scrying mirror, reluctant to leave it unattended. She wanted Cloudspinner destroyed, yet feared the retaliation that opening the prison might permit. “All prisons fail,” she warned of Apollyon’s captivity, even as she insisted that her own prisoner would remain hers forever.

Her rule ended in May 1749 DR, when the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] carried Cloudspinner’s crystal prison out of the chambers beneath the villa. Ra’ghemdros pursued them to the summit temples with her dragonet followers and chimeras. There, on [[Session 110 (DuFr)|May 21]], [[Kenzo]] struck the killing blow. The long scar along her side cracked open, spilling the colors of sunset around the entire horizon as the sun rose. Her body crashed through the roof of the Temple of [[The Night Queen|the Night Queen]].
