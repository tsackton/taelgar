# Caloria
*(kah-LOH-ree-ah)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A merfolk (she/her)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_PageDatedValue")`

Caloria was a [[Merfolk|merfolk]] woman who fled [[Buruli]] and sought refuge with [[Dorian Almare]] near [[Tamaro Bay]].

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
When the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] met Dorian in DR 1749, he recalled Caloria entrusting him with a vial of strange water, asking him to keep it safe. His fragmented memories included two other merfolk pursuing her. The Fellowship found her bones and a golden bracelet among rocks just offshore; Dorian recognized the bracelet, confirming her death.
%%^End%%
