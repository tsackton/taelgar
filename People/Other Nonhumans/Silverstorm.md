# Silver Feathers of the Storm Cloud, Thunder and Lightning
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> An [[Elementals|elemental]] (roc)

%% consider adding whereabouts %%

 Silver Feathers of the Storm Cloud, Thunder and Lightning, sometimes called Silverstorm for short, is a roc who was summoned by the [[Silver Tempests]] and then aided them in their defense of [[Voltara]]. 

%%^Date:1752%%
By DR 1752, Silverstorm was allied with [[Orumei]], Duke of the Returning Wind, in opposing the [[Scouring Wind]] and its plans for war against humanity.
%%^End%%
