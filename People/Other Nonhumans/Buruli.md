# Buruli
*(boo-ROO-lee)*

Buruli is a [[Merfolk|merfolk]] warlock associated with the mysterious entity [[Gazankoa]].

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
While investigating rumors of a garden of jade associated with merfolk off the coast of [[Apporia]], the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] learned that Buruli had brought ruin to [[Omi]] a decade earlier, in DR 1739. Through [[Visions of the Sentient Ocean|visions shared by the Sentient Ocean]], they saw how his envy of the Garden of Names had led him to bargain with Gazankoa. Empowered by his patron, Buruli seized the [[Jade Piece of Rai's Hand|fragment of Rai’s jade hand]] that had strengthened the garden, and used its power to open and sustain a portal to the [[Elemental Plane of Water]]. The [[Sentient Ocean]] sealed Omi behind a protective barrier to contain the invasion, while Buruli retreated through the portal with his followers and captives.

After [[Session 100 (DuFr)|exploring Omi]], the Dunmar Fellowship [[Session 101 (DuFr)|crossed the portal]] to the [[Elemental Plane of Water]], where they found prisoners forced to sort artifacts excavated from Omi, seeking clues to the Sentient Ocean’s origins. After rescuing [[Milo Thistlefoot]], [[Tilly Brineheart]], and their surviving crew, they confronted Buruli in his temple to Gazankoa, built inside a vast leviathan skeleton. [[Kenzo]] recovered the jade, and the Fellowship escaped over their rainbow bridge to [[Vindristjarna]]. Buruli pursued them onto the ship above [[Xurkhaz]], where he was killed by [[Faldrak Bronzehammer]] and [[Delwath]].
