# Prisoner in the 27th Room
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> An unknown

The Prisoner in the 27th Room is an alien being that was confined within the central cold-iron chamber of the [[27th House]]. Outside the prison, it could perceive the complete shape of its past, present, future, and unrealized possibilities at once. The surrounding structure moved its chamber through space and time in response to unpredictable mortal choices, preventing the prisoner from anticipating where or when it would be and growing through the walls to freedom.

Listeners recorded the Prisoner's voice under various names. As the Child, it described learning words, time, and the world from a teacher. As the Dreamer, it described mortals as dreaming souls and claimed to perceive their desires and possible futures. As the Thirst and the Prisoner, it spoke of its hunger for those souls, the children it grew inside emptied bodies, and its efforts to escape.

## History

The being came from the black nothingness outside the universe and made its home at the bottom of a bottomless well. There, [[Lord Endless Ending|Lord Revel Beginning]] found it as a lost child and patiently taught it language, letters, stories, games, and the shapes of things in the world. Revel also taught it to grow itself into buildings and stretch its roots outward. When Revel left to begin their party, they promised to return afterward. The child understood that they would not, and remembered its teacher with love despite the separation.

When mortals began visiting, the being found their dreaming souls beautiful and kept them from passing onward. Members of [[Arithrimos Lamperum]] arrived bearing cold iron and weapons, praised its ability to hide souls, and promised it more dreams if it lived inside the house they built around it. They then used the pressure of the trapped souls to power the prison and kept its location moving through space and time so that it could not foresee a way out.

The being's fungal children grew inside bodies whose souls it had taken. Some left through a root extending from the prison, emerging at places and times determined by the movements of the 27th House. These children survived their parent's departure; those in the woods of the Duchy of Tellim continue to make new siblings and search for the hidden door that would lead them home.

On DR 1740-10-06, five travelers entered the central prison and told stories that persuaded the being to release the hundred souls it held. With the prison's source of power gone, the [[27th House]] collapsed. The prisoner emerged in a form that mortal minds could only approximate as a seven-story dandelion of darkness, bone, roots, mold, tentacles, and familiar faces. It then appeared as a child once more, descended through the low stone well, and returned home beyond the universe. There, it could again perceive all of its possibilities clearly and thought not of the souls it had kept, but of the teacher who had loved it into speech.
