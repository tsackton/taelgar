# Moondweller
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Stoneborn|stoneborn]] (he/him)

%% could use metadata - based in the area above [[Am'khazar]] which is not really named or developed; current fate is unclear %%

Moondweller is a [[Stoneborn]] guide and mystic, based in the high peaks above [[Am'khazar]]. When [[Morgana Stoneclaw|Morgana Frostclaw]] established herself over these mountains and brought endless ice and cold, he remained behind while his village fled. 

He met the [[Silver Tempests]], and helped orient them to the events in the high peaks before directing them towards [[Vangebekkr]] and [[Morgana Stoneclaw|Morgana Frostclaw]]'s lair.
