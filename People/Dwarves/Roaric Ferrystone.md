# Roaric Ferrystone
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Dwarves|dwarf]] (he/him), of the [[Ferrystones|Ferrystone Clan]]

Roaric Ferrystone is an elderly, semi-retired blacksmith originally from the [[Ferrystones]] of [[Rinburg]] who lived in [[Beury]] for many years. In DR 1720, he helped establish a smithy in [[Asineau]] and remained there to train [[Samuel Cordwaner]].
