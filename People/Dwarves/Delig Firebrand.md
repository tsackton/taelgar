# Delig Firebrand
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Dwarves|dwarf]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The cousin of [[Hagrim]], trapped in [[Morkalan]] for many years. During that time lost much of his memory and mind, and became known to the people of [[Morkalan]] as the Mad Priest.

%%^Date:1748-08-27%%
Delig escaped Morkalan, along with many other dwarves, when [[Riswynn]] and companions brought the [[Chalice of the Runepriest]] into the realm and put [[Hagrim]] to rest, ending his domain. He traveled to [[Tharn Todor]] in the autumn of DR 1748, but his subsequent fate is not recorded.
%%^End%%
