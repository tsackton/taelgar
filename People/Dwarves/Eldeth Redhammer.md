# Eldeth Redhammer
*(EL-deth RED-ham-er)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info

Eldeth is a talented, if eccentric, artificer. She is known locally in [[Voltara]] as a reputable buyer of gemstones, and is part of a loose community of scholars, wizards, and others attempting to better understand the exact nature of [[chalyte]].

%% 
DM Notes

Eldeth constructed an extraplanar vault for the [[Silver Tempests]], installed in [[Tempest Towers]] as a safe place to store treasure. 
She is known for having idiosyncratic ideas, although in general specific idiosyncratic ideas have not been invented for her.
