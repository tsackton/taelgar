![[martin.png|right|400]]Martin is an Isinguer human and a captain of the [[Rangers]]. He is a seasoned tactician and often serves as the coordinator of Ranger operations within the [[Refounded Alliance of Aurbez]].

Martin is a well-built, dashing man in his 30s, with dark hair and a tidy beard. He generally wears chain mail and a river-green cloak. 

## Background

Martin was the eldest son of wildcat miners on the northern edge of the [[Aurbez Plateau]], raised with stories of his [[Isingue|Isinguer]] heritage. He grew up poor, and joined [[Rangers]] young, with a zeal for the cause of [[Isingue]] instilled by his parents. Although sometimes rash, his brilliant tactical acumen and daring style serve him well; he led numerous raids into the ash storms of the [[Plaguelands]] during his 20s, making a name for himself across the [[Refounded Alliance of Aurbez]]. 

More recently, he has honed a reputation for discipline and clear‑headedness in command. Within [[Rangers]] he is very popular with the rank-and-file, and widely deferred to on strategic questions, though at times he is too eager to hear all sides and encourage lengthy discussion.
