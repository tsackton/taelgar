Master Ernault serves as chief clerk in Lady [[Elowen Duval]]’s household. An older man with a study overflowing with papers and ledgers, he maintains records of [[Carlinshire]] dating back to its creation in the [[Serpentine Wars|wars against Avatus]]. He walks with a cane, but has a sharp mind, and is sensitive about being dismissed as a mere backwater scribe.
