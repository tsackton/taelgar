# Govir
*(goh-VEER)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Dunmar|Dunmari]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)

![[govir.png|right|400]]

Govir is a Dunmari scholar and Master of Records at the [[Archives|Tokra Archives]]. Devout and single-minded, he is gathering [[Session 35 (DuFr)|accounts of the victories and miracles]] through which he believes the gods have upheld the [[Nayan Dynasty]]. He holds [[Yarad|Samraat Nayan Yarad]] in particular esteem, seeing his victories as evidence of divine favor.

His planned book centers on five such deliverances: the [[Northern War]], the [[Exile of Fraternity of the Empty Moon|expulsion of the Fraternity of the Empty Moon]] from Tokra, the [[Battle of Shrevandurg]], the [[Defeat of the Plague Trees]], and the [[Summer of Red Storms]]. Alongside old letters and military records, he seeks the testimony of surviving soldiers, asking how they felt the presence of [[Aagir]] in battle or the mercy of [[Jeevali]] when wounded.

%%^Date:1748-07-09%%

In DR 1748, [[Govir's Story|letters and a map]] uncovered by his colleague [[Hada]] led Govir to investigate ruined fortifications north of Tokra, seeking signs of the gods’ protection against the madness of [[Pandemonium]]. Members of the [[Fraternity of the Empty Moon]] followed him back to the Archives, stole the map, and kidnapped him to explain its secrets. During a month of captivity, he was afflicted with lycanthropy and feared he had killed Hada while transformed.

The [[Dunmar Fellowship]] [[Session 37 (DuFr)|rescued Govir]] on (DR:: 1748-07-02), and [[Riswynn]] [[Session 38 (DuFr)|cured his lycanthropy]] that evening. Returning to the Archives, he found neglected and misfiled records that might have warned of the Fraternity’s return. At the Fellowship’s urging, [[Ardan]] [[Session 41 (DuFr)|entrusted him with putting the records in order]] and provided funds for additional scribes on (DR:: 1748-07-09).

%%^End%%

%%^Date:1748-11-23%%

Govir continued to aid the Fellowship through his knowledge of the Archives, tracing the [[Cloak of Rainbows]] [[Letter from Govir|in the records]] of the [[Aagir Mystai]] and [[Session 71 (DuFr)|finding the poem]] needed to free [[Hralgar]].

%%^End%%

%%^Date:1749-03%%

[[Sura]] also [[Scrying Delwath Tollen Downtime#Dream Messages, Feb/March 1749|asked Govir to search the records]] for signs of [[Agata]]’s influence on Dunmari history.

%%^End%%
