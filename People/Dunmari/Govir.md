# Govir
*(goh-VEER)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info

![[govir.png|right|400]]
Govir is a Dunmari scholar and the Master of Records at the [[Archives|Tokra Archives]]. Devout and single-minded, he is gathering [[Session 35 (DuFr)|accounts of the victories and miracles]] which he believes shows that the [[Dunmari Pantheon|gods]] have upheld and supported the [[Nayan Dynasty]]. He holds [[Yarad|Samraat Nayan Yarad]] in particular esteem, seeing in his victories evidence of divine favor.

His planned work centers on five such deliverances: the [[Northern War]], the [[Exile of Fraternity of the Empty Moon|expulsion of the Fraternity of the Empty Moon]] from Tokra, the [[Battle of Shrevandurg]], the [[Defeat of the Plague Trees]], and the [[Summer of Red Storms]]. He is particularly interested in finding accounts from soldiers, including surviving veterans, that report how the presence of [[Aagir]] in battle or the mercy of [[Jeevali]] when wounded felt to them, personally. 

%%^Date:1748-07-09%%
In DR 1748, [[Govir's Story|letters and a map]] concerning the [[Exile of Fraternity of the Empty Moon|expulsion of the Fraternity of the Empty Moon]] from Tokra, uncovered by his colleague [[Hada]], led Govir to investigate ruined fortifications north of Tokra, seeking signs of the gods' influence in the protection against the madness of [[Pandemonium]] reported by eyewitnesses. Members of the [[Fraternity of the Empty Moon]] followed him back to the Archives, stole the map, and kidnapped him to explain its secrets. [[Govir's Story|During a month of captivity]], he was afflicted with lycanthropy and feared he had killed Hada while transformed.

The [[Dunmar Fellowship]] [[Session 37 (DuFr)|rescued Govir]] on (DR:: 1748-07-02), and [[Riswynn]] [[Session 38 (DuFr)|cured his lycanthropy]] that evening. Returning to the Archives, he found neglected and misfiled records that might have warned of the Fraternity’s return. At the Fellowship’s urging, [[Ardan]] [[Session 41 (DuFr)|entrusted him with putting the records in order]] and provided funds for additional scribes on (DR:: 1748-07-09).
%%^End%%

%%^Date:1748-11-23%%

Over the next few months, Govir aided the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] through his knowledge of the Archives, tracing the [[Cloak of Rainbows]] [[Letter from Govir|in the records]] of the [[Aagir Mystai]] and [[Session 71 (DuFr)|finding the poem]] needed to free [[Hralgar]].

%%^End%%

%%^Date:1749-03%%

[[Sura]] also [[Scrying Delwath Tollen Downtime#Dream Messages, Feb/March 1749|asked Govir to search the records]] for signs of [[Agata]]’s influence on Dunmari history.
