> *Cintra, the Supreme Oracle of Shakun*
  
Cintra is the Supreme Oracle of [[Shakun]], based in [[Karawa]]. A prophet with a direct connection to the goddess, she stands outside the hierarchy of the [[Shakun Mystai]] and serves as guardian of the [[Heart of Shakun]].

Raised in Karawa, Cintra is a powerful spellcaster with a deep faith in Shakun and a strong sense of personal responsibility. She is self-assured and sometimes headstrong, quick to assess others and accustomed to taking charge. Under pressure, she gives blunt, practical directions and turns readily to magic and improvisation to protect those around her.

Her daughter, [[Jumi]], is a magical prodigy strongly blessed by Shakun. Cintra is fiercely devoted to her; Jumi’s safety is the one concern she puts before even her responsibility to the people of [[Dunmar]].

%%^Date:1748-06-01%%
In February DR 1748, Cintra left [[Karawa]] with the [[Heart of Shakun]], seeking healing for [[Jumi]]. She had dreamed for many days that Jumi was sick with a silent illness that would kill her in weeks, or months, dreams she believed came from Shakun; they were actually a trick of the hag [[Agata|Agata Dustmother]]. [[Agata]] took Jumi hostage and held Cintra at [[Shakun’s Wellspring]] under [[Samerki]]’s watch, while the Heart’s absence weakened Dunmar’s border protections. When the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] reached her, Cintra helped conceal them and diverted Samerki with illusions and a bluff. After the Fellowship defeated Agata and freed Jumi, Cintra returned to Karawa with her daughter and the Heart on [[Session 29 (DuFr)|June 1, 1748]].

**Roleplaying notes**: 
- Self-confident.
- Much more self-assured than Candrosa, not wracked by doubt. Forward oriented, trying to plan how to fix things. "My responsibility, my problem."
- Fairly clever and also a powerful spellcaster. Trusts herself. Still angry at being scammed by Agata.
- Deeply concerned about her daughter, the one thing she will put before the good of the people of Dunmar. Knows that logically her daughter is not worth the lives of thousands but emotionally cannot avoid it.
- As far as mannerisms, in contrast to uncertain Candrosa, sweary-but-plain Vola, bubbly Alesh, direct-and-brash Havdar, wise Tharandros: I think she would be self-confident. Quick to assess people, quick to trust them, or not, doesn't constantly second-guess herself like Candrosa does. Also used to authority to a certain extent. Also deeply religious and trusts Shakun above all.

**What people of Karawa say about her (April/May 1748)**

- Strong willed, powerful, the light of Shakun shown through her, she could control Shakun's majesty and blessing in ways others could not.
- Her daughter (Jumi), had late in life, was incredibly dear to her. She would never say who the father was, and some suspected the child may be part divine. 
- Child took ill, and some time later Cintra left with her. Never came back.

**Background and history**

- Grew up in Karawa, always knew she would be the Supreme Oracle one day. Very strong in raw power, although not as strong as her daughter Jumi.
- Trained and was a trusted leader of the inner working of the Mystai and especially maintaining the Heart and the Mesa.
- Got pregnant around 40. No one knows who the father is, and this is one thing that she will deflect and dissemble about.
 
**Cintra's Knowlege of the Plot (May/June 1748)**

- She doesn't know Agata's full plan, but can easily surmise that taking Shakun's Heart was in order to break the barrier. Does know something of Agata, a constant thorn basically, like living with pain, you sometimes forget it is there but it always comes back.
- Why now is a big question. For herself, or a deal? She should be able to plant the idea that Agata made a trade with someone. Given the risks she took, would have to have gotten something quite valuable in exchange.
- Wants desperately to kill Agata but does not think she can do it alone, and does not have a plan yet for how to get her daughter back, which she must do before killing Agata.

**Candrosa's Report on Cintra, told to the Dunmar Fellowship (May 1748)**

- Two and half years ago, the current Supreme Oracle of Shakun, Cintra, had a daughter, a child late in life and a blessing to her and all of us. From a very young age she seemed as one blessed by Shakun's favor, and even before she could speak she could shape the earth around her and paint colors in the desert sands. But then she took ill, when she was not yet 2, slowly wasting away, not eating and grower frailer by the day. We all prayed and prayed, and while I had misgivings in my heart, Cintra was convinced that Shakun was speaking to her, that her daughter would be a great Oracle some day, and to heal her she needed to take her and Shakun's heart south, to Shakun's Wellspring, a sacred grove hidden on the southern end of the Great Mesa. Against the warning in my heart I advised her to go. That was in early February (Feb 5th). She took with her two companions for protection, although she is the strongest of us.
- She was expected to be gone for two weeks, three at the most. A month after she left, concerned for her safety and worried, I sent a group of six acolytes, led by one blessed by Shakun and able to work minor miracles at will, to give her aid (March 1st). They never returned.
- Soon after, the first hyena attacked Karawa, and things went downhill from there. After you returned from the east, before the gnoll attack and your mission to Raven's Hold, I asked another party of acolytes, our strongest warriors, to go southwest looking for Cintra.
- No group has returned. I have tried, since Cintra's departure, to keep up appearances. But without Shakun's Heart, Shakun is weak here.

**Cintra's story of the events, told to the Dunmar Fellowship (June 1748):**

 - She was tricked by Agata to come here; don't think Agata knows about Heart specifically, but knew that luring the Supreme Oracle out of Karawa was key to lowering the barrier, since this has happened once before (Summer of Red Storms)
 - Agata and Samerki in human form, used magic to charm me; the protections around the Heart are too strong and I did not speak of the secret, but I did reveal that the power of Shakun dwelt in my daughter, Jumi. This is true -- she is clearly destined to be the next oracle. But she is not the heart. Despite the magic dulling my mind, perhaps some of the protections of this place helped deceive Agata, for it was clear that she took this to mean that the power to protect the Dunmari dwelt in Jumi. The next thing I knew, Samerki put my daughter to sleep with some magic spell, and they took off flying before I could react. As I shook off the enchantment, Samerki turned back, and warned me not to leave this place, for if I did they would kill Jumi. True to his word, he was back in a day, and always seemed to hang around, usually checking in in the late afternoon. He has some kind of stone or way to message Agata.
 - I spent a week raging at my weakness, and then turned to solutions. Knew the temple would send people to look, and if I could smuggle the heart out at least, without Samerki being the wiser, perhaps things would not be so bad. During this week I did what I could to extend Shakun's protection through this canyon at least, but by myself, I am not strong enough to do much more.
 - When Nisa and her companions came to find me, I thought this was it, I was saved. They even managed to hide from Samerki, but he must have noticed something for he came back about 30 minutes later while they were preparing to climb down the cliff, using a trick similar to the one you used, Stoneborn. He blasted them with a massive blast of cold air, put the ones that survived to sleep, and dropped them one by one from five hundred feet above the canyon floor. There was nothing I could do but watch.
 - I could not let that happen again, so when I thought Samerki had found you I acted. Jumi has Shakun's power in her, and while she is protected by Shakun it is my belief, and I guess Agata's too, that she cannot turn her into a hag daughter. There are rumors of a lost artifact, a mantle of power from Drankor that can create a small area where the gods themselves are silent, and I believe Agata is searching for this. But she thinks I also know a secret way to take my daughter's power back into myself, removing Shakun's protection from her. Indeed, there is a way to do this - the Heart controls initiation and binding into our web of protection, but also withdrawal. I cannot let Jumi become a hag - Agata is a problem enough for us as she is, but with a daughter, especially one as gifted as Jumi, I do not want to think of what she might do. She doesn't seek to rule, or to conquer, but rather to spread misery and doubt. She has been a thorn in our side, yes, but the Dunmari are still a proud, strong people, recovering from our great sacrifices. If she were to gain more power, and especially if she were to gain the mantle of Drankor to shield herself from the gods, I hate to think of what may come. Mistrust, doubt, pain, even the fracturing of our people into a hundred warring bands striving for territory in this difficult land. There but the grace of the gods we go.

**Agata and Cintra Notes:**
 
- Agata tricked Cintra with dream magic into believing her daughter was ill, and the only cure was at the Wellspring, with the Heart.
- Cintra has been under the influence of Agata's dream magic, which convinced her to leave. Now, she is trapped at Shakun's Wellspring, the exit of which is guarded by undead gnolls and, crucially, an oni. The oni killed both groups of acolytes who came to rescue her, and now taunts her each night.
- Agata was too scared/careful to take Shakun's Heart, but in order to keep Cintra from returning has taken her daughter.
- When Cintra when to the Wellspring, she encountered Agata and Samerki, and was charmed. But Cintra also tricked Agata. Agata does not know the true nature or power of Shakun's Heart, and doesn't know exactly what the item is, and so could be convinced it is a person, i.e. Cintra's daughter. In typical cruel hag fashion, she does not want Cintra just to die, and especially does not want her to somehow get a message back to Shakun on her dying breath or something. Ultimately Agata wants to find a way to trap Cintra's soul, or even better bring back her mirror and trap Cintra in a pocket dimension, and then turn her daughter into a hag. She is probably preparing to do so soon, or will do so once she has the mantle of protection and can silence the gods.
