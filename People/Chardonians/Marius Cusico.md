# Marius Cusico
*(MAH-ree-us koo-SEE-koh)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Chardonian Empire|Chardonian]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Marius Cusico is a Chardonian general and former member of the mageguard. In DR 1749, he was appointed to command the land forces in the [[Chardon-Dunmar War]] as a compromise acceptable to both [[Mitus Verina Auratan]] and [[Fausto]]. Shortly after returning from the war, he suffered a mysterious collapse that left him unable to speak, and he now lies in the care of the priests of the Hospital of the Mother.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
Marius had been secretly controlled by [[Fausto]] through the [[Crown of Purity]]. On DR 1749-05-24, [[Kenzo]] took control of the crown and compelled him to halt the assault on [[Songara]] and withdraw from [[Dunmar]]. His eventual release from the crown’s influence left him struggling to recover from prolonged magical domination.
%%^End%%
