# Zahir
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Mawar Confederacy|Mawaran]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Zahir is a sailor from [[Sulqat]], who became famous for his story of surviving the destruction of his ship in a gale off the [[Mawakel Peninsula]], during which is says he was transported to the [[Elemental Plane of Water]]. He claimed to have awoke on a strange shore of a shimmering island, floating in a mysterious sea, where he spent seven years in service to a marid before returning.
