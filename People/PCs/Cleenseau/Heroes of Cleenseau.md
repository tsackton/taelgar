# The Heroes of Cleenseau
>[!info]+ Information
> An adventuring party
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_PageDatedValue")`

A group of four fate-blown adventurers who met in [[Taviose]] during the [[Cleenseau Spider Attacks]]. Since then, they have become well-known in the region.

The heroes consist of:

* [[Robin of Abenfyrd]], a paladin of [[The Wanderer]] from the small town of [[Abenfyrd]] in [[Tyrwingha]]
* [[Celyn]], a rogue and devotee of [[The Wyrdling]], from the small vineyard town of [[Clawyn]], also in [[Tyrwingha]]
* [[Izgil Moonseeker]], a dwarf from [[Nidzahar]], a lunar sorcerer
* [[Viepuck]], a teenaged orphan from [[Embry]], a devotee of a mysterious mind from far away
