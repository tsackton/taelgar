# The Chasm Explorers
>[!info]+ Information  
> An adventuring party  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

The party consists of:

[[Eolo]], a brash young kenku from the [[Far North]]. His village was destroyed by a white dragon when he was young, and he spent much of his childhood raised by Stoneborn in the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]]. Recently he has decided to learn (or teach?) the kenku at the [[Kemeko Monastery]] how to fight. 

[[Ekko]], a kenku. He grew up in the [[Kemeko Monastery]], but as a young adult discovered a mystic connection to the Chasm. He saved one of the [[Vejo Vaikai]] from a fall, and came to live with them in their small community around the headwaters of the [[Teft]].

[[Finnan Oakstride]], a halfling lore-gatherer, interested in ancient tales, wintering in the small village of [[Dravnieki]]

[[Justas Rhostrin]], an itinerant priest, tender of the [[Kestavo]], also wintering in the small village of [[Dravnieki]].

[[Jrain Fanlish]], a traveler, messenger, and sometimes member of the [[Mezhai]].

>In DR 1730, the Chasm Explorers were drawn into [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]] while investigating disturbances around the [[Great Chasm]]. Their expedition recovered the keystone needed to repair an ancient chasm mechanism and helped willing villagers escape [[Melusa]].
