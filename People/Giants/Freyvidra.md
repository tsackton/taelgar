Freyvidra was a frost giant who ruled [[Kaldhalla]] from DR 1552 to DR 1673, having seized the kingdom from [[Storvalda]]. Allied with the children of [[Vimfrost]], she raided dwarves, stoneborn, Vos, and Vargald, collecting tribute she claimed was owed as compensation for Vimfrost’s death. As wealth flowed north to the dragons, [[Isenborg]] fell into disrepair and resentment grew among the giants. In DR 1673, [[Brimskarda]] overthrew her in a coup, defeating her in single combat. She was killed during the overthrow.
