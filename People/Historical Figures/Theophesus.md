# Theophesus
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Drankorian Empire|Drankorian]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)

Theophesus was a Drankorian scholar whose work helped define the concept of [[Echo Realms]]. He collected some of the first maps of the [[Feywild]] and proposed that the Feywild has a physical correspondence with the [[Material Plane]]. 

In later writing, Theophesus applied a similar idea to the [[Dreamworld]] and the [[Plane of Souls]], treating the Dreamworld as a metaphysical echo of the [[Plane of Souls]]. 

In the modern age, his writing is chiefly influential via his work's impact on [[Gaius Devarro]] and the development of the [[Standard Multiversal Model]].
