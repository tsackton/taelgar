# Amloch
*(AM-lock)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Hkar|Hkaran]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)

Amloch was a wizard who lived in [[Hkar]] before the [[The Downfall|Downfall]], and is chiefly known for the [[Binding Stones]], a powerful magical artifact attributed to him.

%%^Campaign:dufr%%
According to a [[Philosopher's Information Concerning Binding Stones|letter]] from the [[Ancient and Honorable Guild of Philosophers]], he captured energy released by the creation of [[Sarqon]]’s extraplanar prison in five stones.
