# Julius Ceptuso
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Chardonian Empire|Chardonian]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)

Julius Ceptuso was a scholar and faculty of the [[Faculty of Metaphysics]] in [[Chardon]], who popularized the traditional model of the [[Energy Realms]] based on five fundamental energies: Earth, Air, Fire, Water, and Magic.
