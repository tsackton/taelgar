# Emyr of Tafolwern
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Tyrwingha|Tyrwinghan]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)

Emyr of Tafolwern was a Tyrwinghan planar geographer and cosmological writer, working in the DR 1600s. He is widely recognized as one of the leading post-[[Great War]] critics of the standard scholarly accounts of planar geography.

He is best known for his treatise [[On Hidden Doors]], a polemic about temporary planar connections, in which Emyr argued against standard orthodox accounts of [[Planar Connections]]. Instead, he argued that elemental vortexes, fey crossings, and similar events belong to a wider class of temporary planar connections shaped by planar drift, which he called planar upwellings. 

He also was a prominent critic of the [[Standard Multiversal Model]] conception of [[Echo Realms]], criticizing the idea of a source and reflected hierarchy.
