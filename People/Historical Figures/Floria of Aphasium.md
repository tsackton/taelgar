Floria of Aphasium was a wealthy Drankorian supporter of the [[Radiant Path]]. Her family’s fortune came from her father’s silversmithing and her mother’s agricultural holdings in the western hinterlands. She spent much of her youth at the family’s coastal villa.

In DR 1036, her father died unexpectedly, followed shortly by her mother. At twenty-five, Floria became the sole heir to both fortunes. She used her inheritance to care for the needy and support resistance to [[Apollyon]], organizing secret networks that carried money, supplies, and fugitives through the western provinces. In DR 1052, a trusted confidant betrayed her, exposing her work to the [[Ashen Cloaks]]. She was seized from her villa and subsequently sacrificed by Apollyon in DR 1053, becoming one of seven souls bound into [[Apollyon's Phylactery]]. 

Her story is recorded in the [[Book of Martyrs of the Radiant Path]], a hidden record and memorial kept by the priests of the [[Radiant Path]] of those disappeared and taken during Apollyon's reign, though her fate was unknown for many centuries, until the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] ventured to [[Drankor]] to defeat [[Apollyon]].
