# Ishara Venn
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Tollen|Tollish]] [[Humans|human]] (she/her)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

Ishara Venn was a Tollish planar geographer associated with the [[University of Tollen]], active in the DR 1500s and early DR 1600s, after the [[Great War]]. She is best known for [[The Seams Between Worlds]], a systematic study of [[Elemental Vortex|elemental vortexes]] and other planar connections.

Ishara's work treats elemental vortexes as temporary points of contact created when a nearly pure expression of an elemental energy draws an Energy Realm close to the [[Material Plane]]. While many modern scholars treat her theories as controversial or fringe, [[Emyr of Tafolwern]] used her account of elemental vortexes as the starting point for his broader theory of planar upwellings.
