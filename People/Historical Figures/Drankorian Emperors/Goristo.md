# Goristo
*(goh-RISS-toh)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Hkar|Hkaran]] [[Humans|human]] (he/him)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_PageDatedValue")`  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

Goristo was the emperor of [[Drankorian Empire|Drankor]] from DR 366 until his death in DR 433. A renowned general and mage, he negotiated several agreements with the peoples and kingdoms of the Sembaran river valleys, culminating in the [[Treaty of Marhavn]], signed in DR 402, which established the [[Volta]] as the empire’s northern border. In DR 423, he signed the [[Treaty of Tyrwingha]] with the [[Archfey Ethlenn]], establishing the formal relationship between Drankor and [[Tyrwingha]].
