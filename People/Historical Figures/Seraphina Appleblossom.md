# Seraphina Appleblossom
*(sehr-uh-FEE-nuh AP-uhl-bloss-um)*

Seraphina Appleblossom was a halfling tavern-keeper who owned [[The Laughing Wave]] on the docks of [[Drankor]]. She grew up in the family inn and inherited it in DR 1020, continuing its traditions of hospitality, good food, and rooftop fireworks. She was known as an excellent cook.

During [[Apollyon]]’s reign, Seraphina initially tried to remain neutral, welcoming dockworkers, fleeing families, and off-duty enforcers alike. Although she hated the spreading cruelty, she hoped that keeping her head down would protect her inn, staff, and patrons.

In DR 1049, she hosted a farewell celebration for halfling families preparing to flee the city. Drawn by the noise and rooftop fireworks, the Omnis Pura raided the inn. Seraphina shielded children and urged guests to escape through the back alleys, but was captured alongside several of the families. The Laughing Wave was ransacked and abandoned.

Seraphina was one of seven people sacrificed by Apollyon whose souls were bound into [[Apollyon's Phylactery|his phylactery]]. Her soul remained trapped until DR 1749, when it was freed in the [[Land of the Dead]] and allowed to pass on.
