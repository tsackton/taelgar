# Camilla of Drankor
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Drankorian Empire|Drankorian]] [[Humans|human]] (she/her)

%% timing presumed to be Golden Age era, certainly pre-Civil War, but not firmly established %%

Camilla of Drankor was an ancient scholar of fey cosmology. Her writings preserve fey accounts of the [[Dreamworld]], the [[Plane of Souls]], the [[Shadowfolds]], and the rumored [[Nightmare Realm]].

According to Camilla, fey traditions treat the Plane of Souls as a pale echo of the Dreamworld rather than as the central spiritual plane assumed by many theologians.
