# Saria
*(SAH-ree-ah)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Cymea|Cymean]] [[Humans|human]] (she/her)

Saria was a respected [[Cymea|Cymean]] conjurer, who left a legacy of extensive correspondence which remains an important source for scholars of magic. The most well-known passages record a year of study in the [[Feywild]] that seemed to last only an evening on the [[Material Plane]], for when Saria returned, the sun had not yet risen on the night of her departure.

%%^Campaign:none%%

Saria's intended vibe is loosely modeled on Hildegard of Bingen: a learned woman whose correspondence, rather than a single formal treatise, established her intellectual authority and preserved a wide-ranging scholarly voice. Her letters should suggest a respected conjurer engaged in the "ordinary life" of magical study, to the extent such a thing can ever be ordinary. 

Her biography, dates, correspondents, or institutional role probably require more of Cymea's history to be established.
