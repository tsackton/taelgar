# Aznoké
>[!info]+ Biographical Info
> A [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] (she/her)

%% presumably ancient-ish, e.g. not still alive, but exact time not decided %%

Aznoké was a [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] mystic whose surviving work presents the multiverse as a living pattern of currents, cycles, and woven connections. In [[The Endless Currents]], she described the Eightfold Essences: the physical energies of Fire, Air, Earth, and Water; the fundamental energies of Magic and Spirit; and the cyclical energies of Life and Death.

Her cosmology is rooted in lizardfolk images of flow, balance, ancestry, and return. Life flows from the [[Positive Energy Plane|Plane of Blossoming Currents]], while death gathers in the [[Negative Energy Plane|Plane of Still Waters]], where life slows, is cleansed, and prepares for renewal. The same poem describes the [[Tharzen Anzinakoa]] as weaving strands of energy and spirit into the [[Material Plane]], a vision that treats planar connection as part of the same cycle as birth, death, and rebirth.

Later scholars cite Aznoké because her Plane of Blossoming Currents and Plane of Still Waters resemble later speculation about the Positive and Negative Energy Planes. Her work, however, is not merely a technical classification of planes. It is a mystical account of the multiverse as a natural cycle of energy flows.
