# Llew Crowther

Llew is one of the Crowther twins of [[Clawyn]], a family of vineyard workers and musicians. He and his brother [[Ellis Crowther|Ellis]] are mirror twins, about six months older than [[Celyn]], with autumn birthdays. Llew is right-handed and Ellis is left-handed; Celyn could always tell them apart by such details, though the twins did not realize how he did it. Both are tall, slender, good-looking, with golden-brown skin and black hair. Their family supports the harvest and winepress work with music, and their older sister seemed likely to take on that role.

Llew favors flutes, recorders, and simple horns. He also works wood and dabbles in making instruments. Caring and fiercely loyal to Ellis, he is more practical, gruff, and direct about refusing unwanted work than his brother. He chafes at village expectations and knows his instrument-making skills may give him more options elsewhere, but remains loyal to his twin. He is straight and has tended toward longer relationships than Ellis; whether he settles with a partner remains open. Neither twin is particularly inclined toward monogamy, although either might agree to it; both could imagine a primary partner alongside less lasting romances. Both brothers speak Tyrwinghan and Sembaran. Llew would be interested in learning other languages for their songs, but the player's sketch did not establish that he had learned them.

The twins have talked of someday leaving the village and made their twin act part of their imagined performance career. Llew is annoyed by the village's view that they are pleasant but unlikely to settle into steady work. The brothers regularly earn money in a market town. The sketch describes Llew as knowledgeable, thoughtful, and somewhat snarky.
