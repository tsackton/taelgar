Ellis Crowther is a singer and left-handed crwth player from [[Clawyn]], and the mirror twin of [[Llew Crowther]]. He comes from a vineyard-working family and was [[Celyn|Celyn’s]] childhood sweetheart.

Tall and slender, with golden-brown skin and black hair, Ellis is charismatic and generous with his affections. He is bisexual and enjoys flirting with people who welcome it, without being unkind or pushy.
