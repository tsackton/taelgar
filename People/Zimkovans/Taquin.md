# Taquin
*(tah-KEEN)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Zimka]] [[Humans|human]]

Taquin was a resident of [[Melusa]] and the parent of [[Zahara]].

%%^Date:1730%%
In [[Into the Chasm - Episode 04|January 1730]], a soul identifying itself as Taquin spoke to [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]], through the infant [[Samir]]. Its last memory was falling toward [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth|the labyrinth]], looking up at Zahara, who stood with tears on their face and a look of determination. Taquin remembered nothing after the fall; how the soul came to inhabit Samir is unexplained, but apparently not uncommon in [[Melusa]].
