Nura is a Zimka woman from [[Melusa]], the sister of [[Kalima]], wife of [[Khaled]], and mother of [[Samir]]. 

%%^Date: 1730-02-01%%
In January of DR 1730, she and Khaled sheltered the [[Chasm Explorers]] when [[Kalima]] [[Into the Chasm - Episode 03|returned from the labyrinth]]. She joined those [[Into the Chasm - Episode 06|evacuated from Melusa]] with her family, and later settled with the other rescued Melusans.
