# Zahara
*(zah-HAH-rah)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info  
> A [[Zimka]] [[Humans|human]] (they/them)  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Zahara is a Zimka dissident from [[Melusa]], the child of [[Taquin]], and the leader and strategist of a small circle of villagers hoping to escape from [[Forquarion|Malquin]]'s rule.

%%^Date:1730%%
After [[Kalima]] returned from the labyrinth, Zahara [[Into the Chasm - Episode 04|hid her in the cliffside mines]], uncertain how Malquin would react to a sacrifice returning alive.

When the [[Chasm Explorers]] [[Into the Chasm - Episode 06|returned to Melusa]], they found Zahara sheltering with a few dissidents, who helped the [[Chasm Explorers]] orchestrate an escape from Melusa. Zahara helped gather villagers willing to leave, and joined those [[Into the Chasm - Episode 06|evacuated from Melusa]].
