# Earl of the Highlands Rhiannon de Vaux
*(ree-AN-on de VOH)*
>[!info]+ Biographical Info

Rhiannon de Vaux is the Earl of High Tyrn in eastern [[Addermarch]], based at [[Marwick]]. During [[Krauzeth|Krauzeth's]] attacks in DR 1720, she encouraged non-essential personnel and upland herdsmen to retreat to the lowlands.
