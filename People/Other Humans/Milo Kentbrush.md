Milo Kentbrush was a human adventurer, an overconfident would-be aboleth-slayer, whose sobbing ghost lingered in flooded caverns near [[Suwi]], still tied to the weapon he had brought to aid in his quest. He had sought to kill the aboleth [[Ithu’rax]], but was killed in the attempt. 

The [[Silver Tempests]] recovered his arrow of aberration slaying from the caverns, and Milo followed them, bound to the arrow as they continued toward Ithu'rax's lair.
