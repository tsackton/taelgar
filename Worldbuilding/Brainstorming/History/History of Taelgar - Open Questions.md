# History of Taelgar - Open Questions

> [!warning] Work in progress — noncanonical
> This is a worldbuilding development document. Do not treat any part of it as established Taelgar canon unless it has been separately adopted into a canonical note.

Questions accompanying [[History of Taelgar]], currently covering the Riving through the Downfall. “Open question” marks an unsettled choice. “Implied gap” marks a boundary or omission that needs development, not a proposed historical storyline.

## The Riving

These questions concern the primordial setting and acts of creation associated with the Riving. “Before” and “during” identify unresolved relationships, not positions on an established linear timeline.

### Primordial beings and ancient powers

**Open question — primordial origins.** What forms did the predecessors of dragons, giants, fey, and elementals take, and how do they relate to their later manifestations? The existence of a particular list of titans, great wyrms, archfey, or elder elementals is not settled. Later creature rules distinguish dragons and giants from the less-defined origins of fey and elementals.

**Implied gap — Cha'mutte.** Was [[Cha'mutte]] a "great wyrm" who predated the Riving, or something else.

**Implied gap — giant achievements.** When did the giants' reality-weaving craftsmanship, rune-supported skyships, and great artifacts belong in their history? The Giants page establishes these achievements in ancient days, but does not place them before or during the Riving rather than in later history.

**Open question — the order–chaos war.** Was the campaign-established war before the Riving or somehow during it? The opposing side was assumed by the players to be order/Mechanus, but remained poorly defined. The history must accommodate the conflict without silently resolving those gaps.

### Planar form and creation

**Implied gap — primordial planar form.** Did the Plane of Creation, or any other fundamental planes, exist distinctly before the Riving, or did their recognizable forms arise through it? The primordial cosmos was simpler than the present Multiverse, but neither its number of realms nor their arrangement is established. The Crown of Purity vision places the Plane of Magic intertwined with the primordial world; it does not settle whether this was already a distinct plane.

**Implied gap — planar chronology.** Which recognizable planes arose during the Riving, and which were separated or reshaped from something already present? The possible creation account in the Multiverse page proposes a chronology, but only the Energy Realms' connection to the Riving is explicitly established by the Primordial Cosmos page. The beginning of linear time and fixed Taelgar do not settle the origins of the Plane of Souls, Land of the Dead, or every Echo Realm.

**Implied gap — creation of the first peoples.** Were the elves, dwarves, halflings, lizardfolk, and stoneborn created in the Riving itself or after linear time began? Were their creations simultaneous? Their creators are established, but the main history does not settle the relationship among these acts of creation.

**Open question — Far Realms.** Did the first cracks to the Far Realms appear during the Riving, during humanity's creation, or on both occasions? This affects when the Sentient Ocean could have reached Taelgar.

### The Void Mind and the Three Despairs

**Open question — reconciling the simplified model.** The February 7, 2025 discussion explicitly agrees to drop the Three Despairs as a required metaphysical category, retaining the Void Mind and mysterious associated powers without canonical creation stories. Thark remains an Embodied God; Jinnik and Umidzka are described instead as malevolent forces, with the possibility that Jinnik and the Mad One are the same entity. The reference pages still assert the older hierarchy, stolen divine power, Thark's parentage through Umidzka and G'Zab, and the Mad One as Jinnik's separate embodied avatar. Which surviving descriptions should be retained as theology or legend, rather than literal metaphysics? G'Zab's place in the simplified model is not established by that discussion.

**Implied gap — avatar language in recorded lore.** Campaign records preserve inscriptions and cult testimony describing the Mad One as Jinnik's embodied avatar or proposing to summon Jinnik's avatar. These establish that the descriptions exist in-world, but do not independently establish two distinct beings. How should those descriptions relate to the possible identification of Jinnik with the Mad One?

## Before Humanity

### Dating and the transition to humanity

**Open question — humanity's creation date.** When was humanity created? Circa CY 1550 is an approximate endpoint, not a fixed date. A working range of roughly CY 1400–1900 allows time both for large nonhuman civilizations and the fading memory of creation, and for the subsequent development of human history; it is not a set of firm canonical bounds.

### The Void Mind, Thark, and the orcs

**Open question — the Void Mind's activity.** Was the Void Mind inactive or unable to act between the Riving and humanity's creation, or already introducing limited danger? When did its opposition to the Divine Presence become an active historical conflict?

**Open question — orc creation.** Were Thark and the orcs created during or immediately after the Riving, later but before humans, or around/after humanity's creation? Could the creation of orcs have prompted the creation of humanity?

**Open question — orc demographic constraints.** The proposed creation account assumes a very small initial population, a very high birth rate, and a very high death rate. How much time would orcs need to grow numerous enough to be noticed by other peoples? Their creation cannot be placed too late to accommodate their subsequent historical presence under this model. These are demographic assumptions to test, not a fixed creation date or an independently established account of early orc populations.

**Implied gap — early orc history.** If orcs existed before humanity, what were they doing, and what contact did they have with other developing civilizations? Where did their communities develop?

**Open question / implied gap — peace and danger.** How would early orc activity fit the long period of broadly peaceful nonhuman development? Was danger confined to particular places, and when did other civilizations begin preparing to defend themselves?

**Open question — humanity and the onset of conflict.** Did humanity's creation enable the Void Mind to intervene, as the “opens the door” proposal suggests, or did conflict begin independently of that event?

### Early civilizations and their interactions

**Open question — the early-empire model.** Were the major societies very large, largely single-species empires with limited contact? Was interaction concentrated in the southern Sentinels, around Tyrwingha and Isingue, and along Green Sea coasts, or was it more widespread?

One possible model concentrates substantial mixing chiefly in three areas:

- The southern Sentinels, where dwarven and elven empires would interact.
- Tyrwingha and Isingue, where elven and lizardfolk empires would interact.
- The Green Sea coasts, where halflings and lizardfolk would interact.

This model would leave the central and western lands dominated by dwarves and elves, and the eastern Green Sea region dominated by halflings and lizardfolk. It would also suggest that early human migrants first encountered elves and perhaps dwarves, and raises the concern that halflings might be made too geographically restricted. These are implications to explore, not established limits on settlement or contact: the three known empires were single-species, but their existence does not exclude other civilizations or mixed communities, and halflings were canonically widespread.

A second proposal allows the Green Sea to reach farther inland, with major nonhuman civilizations meeting near Urlich Pass. This would connect their meeting place to a possible site of the Firstborn's creation and, much later, the final battle with Cha'mutte. It could make their interactions less isolated, but neither this geography nor the creation-site connection is settled, and its placement specifically before humanity remains unclear.

**Implied gap — other nonhuman civilizations.** What other substantial civilizations existed alongside the dwarven realms beneath Labkhan, Alcarinquë, and Urtzabala? Were there other great realms of these same peoples, civilizations of other nonhuman peoples, or mixed communities? The established empires are examples, not an exhaustive map of the early world; their existence does not settle the histories of the lands outside them.

**Implied gap — dwarven expansion.** Did dwarven kingdoms already span the Sentinel Range, and when did dwarves begin settling other mountains?

**Open question — stoneborn's missing history.** Were the stoneborn exceptionally isolated during this period? What part did they play in the early world, and when did their practice of training outsiders begin?

**Open question / implied gap — sparse stoneborn populations.** Were stoneborn even less numerous than elves, living in scattered, secluded retreats? How sparse were their early populations, and how did this affect the scale of their communities and their influence on other peoples? Sparse population and isolation are distinct questions; neither by itself explains their limited place in the surviving historical narrative.

**Open question — halfling origins and distribution.** Where did halfling journeys begin, and how did their early routes develop? Was the Green Sea a homeland of sorts? Any account must accommodate their confirmed widespread early presence.

### Other nonhuman peoples

**Implied gap — kenku.** What communities, journeys, or civilizations did the kenku have before humanity? Aerin's Firstborn connection concerns their creation, not the subsequent history of the people. When did kenku spread through the Green Sea, and how old are their island communities?

**Open question / implied gap — centaurs.** How did the centaurs' emergence as a people relate to the Riving, and what part did they play in the early world? Their traditions connect their freedom and souls to Taelgar itself, but their early migrations and relationships with other peoples remain undefined. The older literal archfey-creation mechanism is not settled canon.

**Implied gap — merfolk.** Had the Sentient Ocean reached Taelgar and created the merfolk before humanity? If so, where did their underwater communities develop, and did they have contact with peoples of the coasts? The timing of the Ocean's arrival depends in part on the unresolved opening of routes from the Far Realms.

**Implied gap — giants, dragons, fey, and elementals.** What societies and domains did these beings establish after the Riving, and how did they relate to the growing civilizations of the children of the Embodied Gods? Which giant achievements belong to these years rather than primordial existence or later history?

**Implied gap — kobolds, goblins, and gnomes.** Which of these peoples already existed before humanity, and where do their early histories belong? Kobold and goblin origins remain undefined. Gnome origins are tentative, and the dwarven Sundering must not be assumed as their established creation story. The restriction that hobgoblins were not created before humanity does not by itself date the origins of goblins.

### Ancient landscapes and missing coverage

**Implied gap — Alcarinquë's extent.** How far west did Alcarinquë extend? Did it reach Ainumarya?

**Implied gap — Tinwerquë.** Did Tinwerquë exist as a great southern elven realm, and how far did it extend?

**Implied gap — wetlands and coastlines.** How large were the ancient lizardfolk swamps? Did the proposed inland Green Sea and convergence near Urlich Pass already exist before humanity, or only later in pre-Downfall history?

**Open question — the southern continent.** Was there already a separate southern continent before humanity, or did a single landmass split during the Downfall? A distinct southern continent is not established canon. The geography questions leave its existence, present condition, and the authority of the mapped southern coastline unresolved; the discussions explicitly retain both pre-Downfall configurations as possibilities.

**Implied gap — the wider world.** What peoples and civilizations inhabited the southern and eastern mainland, the northern and eastern Green Sea, and the lands later called Cymea? How did their histories connect to the better-known forests, mountains, and wetlands of the northwest?

## Humanity and the Downfall

### Dates, contact, and religion

**Open question — intermediate chronology.** What broad ranges, if any, are useful for the first encounters, northern departures, and the Mariner migration? One candidate sequence places contact around CY 2200, northern departures around CY 2500–2800, and the Mariner migration around CY 3500–3650. None of those ranges is fixed; contact before all northern departures is only one live model, and the northern migrations need not fit a single bounded wave. Tollen's founding in CY 3902 and the Downfall in CY 4122 are fixed anchors. The Mariner migration must leave room for its descendants to develop distinct histories before Tollen's founding, and Northerner communities reaching Greater Sembara need not remember themselves as Hkaran.

**Open question — early Hkar.** Was Hkar raised specifically as humanity's homeland, or was humanity created on an existing island? Did humans initially develop in isolation? The island's place in humanity's creation is established; its raising and the length of any isolation are not. The northern departures came first, but the timing and destinations of the southern migration relative to later Mariner departures remain incompletely defined.

**Open question — first contact and northern departures.** How did humans meet other peoples, and who made the first contact? Elves and halflings are possibilities, not decided first contacts. Three relationships remain live: nonhumans discover Hkar before the northern migrations and spur both emigration and movement away from personal deities; northern migrations begin and continue for some time before contact; or small departures precede contact, followed by a larger movement after Hkar is discovered. In the first or mixed account, northern migrants could preserve the last traditions of Hkar's personal-deity phase. None of these accounts fixes the duration, size, or cultural uniformity of the northern migrations.

**Open question — religious development.** How much of the personal-deities → cults → small-gods → pantheonic-religions sequence should become canonical? The developed religious history is explicitly brainstorming. Its older claims about gods creating or inhabiting separate divine realms are flagged for revision and must not be used to date the creation of those realms.

**Open question — Sarqon.** Where does the Chainshroud's defeat belong in the religious sequence? It could fall late in the small-gods era or early in the development of pantheonic religions; CY 2600–2800 was suggested but is not a canonical date. The Philosopher's letter also places the wizard Amloch and powerful arcane work at the imprisonment, so any religious sequence must accommodate that magic rather than assume an entirely pre-arcane setting.

### Migration and the incomplete map

**Open question — southern humans.** Where did the southern migrants settle, what contact did they retain with Hkar, and what happened to their societies during the Downfall? Their existence does not establish a separate southern continent or the older colonial model.

**Implied gap — Dunmari origins.** When did the Dunmari ancestors leave Hkar, where did they first settle, and how did that departure relate to the southern and mariner migrations? Their later journey into the ruins of Drankor must not be confused with their earlier departure from Hkar.

**Open question — Green Sea diversity.** Which coastal, island, northern, eastern, and southern Green Sea societies were Mariner-derived, which had other origins, and where did they mix with nonhuman peoples? When did the Mariner schisms occur, and when had a recognizable Skaer identity developed? Descent from Mariners is established; flight from a particular religious schism is only one possible Skaer origin.

**Implied gap — other early histories.** What was happening in Cymea, east and south of the later Drankorian heartland, and among kenku, centaurs, merfolk, stoneborn, halflings, giants, and dragons during these years? The human migration story and three established single-species empires do not exhaust the history of the period.

### Foreboding, war, and changed landscapes

**Open question — the onset of danger.** When did the Void Mind's opposition become an active historical conflict? How do Elmerca's warnings, the creation of hobgoblins and orcs, and incursions from the Far Realms fit together? The proposal that humanity's creation “opens the door” remains noncanonical.

**Open question — dwarven defense.** What parts of the proposed warnings from Azar, growth of the warrior thuhr, and wars against mind flayers belong in the history? These need not entail a dwarven Sundering or a settled gnome origin, neither of which is adopted here.

**Open question — the Downfall's cause and cost.** How did the defense of Hkar, the role of the Divine Presence, and the island's destruction relate? One possible account has the Void Mind's forces threatening the source of humanity's Divine Spark on Hkar, prompting the Divine Presence to withdraw its power and sink the island. This is a proposed explanation, not a settled cause. The war's start and duration, campaigns, and particular alliances remain undefined.

**Implied gap — the nonhuman empires.** What destroyed Alcarinquë's forests, how did Urtzabala's wetlands change, and which dwarven halls were lost? Was change sudden, prolonged, or different in each place? The devastation of Alcarinquë and surviving remnants do not supply those mechanisms or date every retreat and migration. Urtzabala's reference page specifically leaves the date and process of its disappearance undefined.

**Open question — peoples during the Downfall.** Did elven realms lead a struggle against the Void Mind's forces and hobgoblin armies? Were lizardfolk scattered, with some migrating south? Did dwarves withdraw from their deepest halls and fortify the passages against threats from below? Was sustained contact with southern human cultures lost? These are possible parts of the war and its aftermath, but the established cataclysm does not settle them. Elmerca is associated with foresight; whether she gave warnings that were gradually discounted remains open.

**Open question — the south and east.** Did the Downfall split a continent, and did a southern elven realm exist to be destroyed? How far did catastrophe extend around the eastern Green Sea, and what survived beyond the better-developed northwest?

**Open question — refugees.** Were there other significant Hkaran refugee groups besides those who founded Drankor? Was the Circular Island the principal means of escape, and were its refugees exclusively Mos Numena worshippers? Existing discussions argue for a dominant magical, Mos Numena culture but leave minority survivors possible.
