# Eight Memories of Drankor

## Tim Sackton — November 6, 2022 at 5:09 PM

My assumption is that you don't remember much of your life before the
Feywild, although any kind of domestic memories you can add, and obviously
you remember your name, and that you were a commander in the Drankorian
Legion. You might remember a few random details of your company, or other
bits and things like that. You can also of course make up anything you like
to add, some of which might turn out to be true. There are some notes on
Drankorian culture here
<https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pA8tvWW0tHka6mbHa8ALJS6LKrluF3pEKyviUi3heIc/edit#bookmark=id.h6gn1t2t8qxw>.
There are also some notes on the Feywild here
<https://docs.google.com/document/d/1QMwN38-d-VG2OaFWq07A6hh5ui6yX5mcRnxzt7yVa1o/edit?usp=sharing>
.

I think that for plot reasons it would make sense that when you arrived you
did something -- you can't remember what -- to the archfey whose realm is
now in decay and where the adventure is located. You are now doomed to
wander the Feywild, drawn to this ruined domain, until the archfey is
restored to power and can release you from the curse.

The main thing I guess it would make sense to do is think a bit in advance
about your character's exit, since at the end of the adventure you will all
presumably end up at the fey crossing Seeker is looking for, and you'll
need a reason to disappear from the scene so that you aren't around for the
reunion episode.

Your main memories of your life before the Feywild are focused on a civil
war that occured towards the end of the Drankorian Empire (but before
Apollyon or the Plague), triggered by the murder of Emperor Helea, and
fought between her loyalists and the legions of Emperor Vestian, who
replaced her. You vaguely remember the war, but not details and can't
remember what side you fought for or who won. The following eight things,
all of which plausibly could have occured but not all of which actually
happened, are the clearest events in your mind.

1. You are a young man, newly appointed commander of the 3rd Silvus Legion,
summoned to Drankor to personally swear allegiance to the Emperor. You
remember little of the journey from your frontier fort to the capital, now
it feels like it was almost fey-like, it took no time at all. But you
clearly remember the ceremony. The Emperor Helea, a blonde woman of unusual
presence, with a high brow and aquiline nose, wearing robes of gold and a
thin golden circlet with a single diamond set in the center. The audience
hall - a large chamber, big enough for a few hundred people to fit
comfortably, built of fine marble inlaid with gold, arcane symbols woven
into the decorations. As each commander stepped forward, Helea greeted each
one, saying a few words in private, and receiving their oath. It seemed to
take forever, and no time at all, until it was your turn. You remember
clearly what Helea said to you: "While your oath is to me, your obligation
is to the ideal of Drankor, a haven of safety and peace from the darkness
for all humankind. Remember that, and your command will be a great one."

2. A battle - you remember fighting in a forest. Your soldiers standing
fast, ranks of warriors backed by the war mages of Drankor, warned by the
elves of danger coming, an army of giants from the mountains. The battle is
fierce, but you are victorious. You remember most clearly the face of the
frost giant you killed, huge, his head nearly as big as you, his eyes angry
and clouded. As you dodged out of the way of his massive axe of ice and
iron and struck the killing blow, you remember peace coming to his face,
and a murmur, so low only you could hear it, but you can't remember his
words. Something about a curse, and a dark alliance, the Emperor Helea and
some shadowy creature joining forces.

3. A city in the forest - an elven city, seeming to grow from the trees,
all golden light and elegance, spiraling up in the branches of three
massive trees, much larger than anything you had seen before in your life.
Full of song and wine and beauty, it seemed a timeless place. You remember
bringing an urgent message to the elves, an emissary, but not on official
business. Something you had uncovered, some evil that must be confronted,
but must be kept secret from the Imperial channels.

4. A messenger, riding hard, arriving at the gates of your command, her
horse nearly ridden to death. She greets you with the handshake of the
Peregrin Fellowship, a society of travelers and pilgrims that you are a
member of, a group dedicated to finding fellowship and support on the
frontier. She looks exhausted. The message she brings: The Omnis Pura have
struck the first blow. Emperor Helea has been killed, and war is coming.

5. Hosting a great feast - a celebration, you think. The tables are heaped
with delicacies: meat pies of all description, a roast pig, breads and
cheeses from near and far, even a few rare elven delicacies, cakes made
from the fruit of the sisila tree. Wine flows freely, and your soldiers are
happy, their chants still resonating in your memory: "Long live the new
Emperor Vestian, long may he rule! Long live Emperor Vestian, swiftly may
he defeat the traitors!". You remember even the elven ambassador was there,
and some of the locals, the people who worship strange little gods.

6. Marching at the head of your legion, arrayed for war, leaving your field
camp after several victories against local allies of the Omnis Pura. Now,
you are marching towards Erusum, hoping you will not have to fight, for you
are going to destroy the teleportation circle there, and hold it against
the Omnis Pura and their allies. You remember leading the charge into the
great hall where the circle sits, only to discover that several Omnis Pura
mages lie in wait. Suddenly the world turns upside-down as you are flung
upward, most of your legion with you, crushed helplessly against the
ceiling, as more and more soldiers and mages pour through the teleportation
circle. You black out, and remember nothing more.

7. You are leading a small detachment of your legion, trying to hold down a
hasty fortification, holding out for reinforcements to arrive from Drankor
and Emperor Vestian, trying to protect something important, but what it was
you cannot recall. The griffons flying overhead give a call as the full
moon rises - enemies on the move. Worse, the rebels have a dragon on their
side. You reassure your troops, you have drilled for this, you know what to
do. The soldiers scatter, readying their bows. Once the dragon is in sight,
the war mages unleash a torrent of magical chains and vines that reach up
and grasp the dragon, dragging it to the ground, holding it there. Your
warriors set upon it, some roasted in dragonfire but losses are inescapable
against such a creature, as the tide turns in your favor. Until your dead
troops begin to rise as shadows, attacking your mages, and suddenly the
battle is lost. You remember trying to organize a retreat, that turned to a
rout, shouting to regroup, running, then all is black.

8. You are on the run. You think you escaped from a stockade -- you
remember guards and magic keeping you subdued, until for a moment the
enchantment slipped, and you broke free, running into the night. All is
dark -- the new moon casts no light -- and you can't remember where you
were or even who was holding you prisoner. Your destination is one of the
old treaty stones, places protected by ancient elven magic. You remember
reaching one, seeing the weathered script and the carved symbols on the
towering stone glowing in the starlight. A fireball erupts around you,
scorching you, but the stone is untouched, and you reach for it, hoping for
safety and a way back to your command.
