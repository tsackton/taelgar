# Refounded Alliance of Aurbez

## Mike Sackton — September 5, 2022 at 9:10 PM

I've been thinking a bit about why the people of Aurbez ended up creating
something with such a grandiose name as "the Refounded Alliance of
Aurbez"...

Here's my idea, what do you think?

As the Blood Years ended and the immediate desperate fight for survival
amongst the refugees and settlers in the Aurbez region ended, there was a
political controversy/question:

Should the settlers and refugees, many who grew up on stories of the
greatness of Insingue (although by 1650 no one alive would still remember
it personally) create an alliance with the explicit goal of trying to
cleanse the Plaguelands and refound Insingue?

Or should they abandon the dream of the golden past and live quietly on the
frontier?

The idea would be that by 1700 the first group had "won" the political
argument and the Refounded Alliance was basically formed as an explicit nod
back to Insingue and the "golden" past, and was actively interested in
trying to resettle/cleanse the Plaguelands.

By 1750, it is possible that the original goals/desires -- not having been
successful -- are giving way to a more prosaic political alliance of some
kind, but the founding myth would remain something like: We are the
rightful heirs of a lost great city, and it is the duty of us and our
descendents to remember this and reclaim our land some day.

What do you think?

Mike

## Tim Sackton — September 6, 2022 at 9:42 AM

Yes this is basically what I had in mind. What distinguishes Aurbez from Eg
the Duchy of Massaeu (which also has a fair not of Insingue heritage) is
the explicit founding myth as the heirs to Insingue. I think the “Refounded
Alliance” should be a call back to a historical entity around Insingue. I
was thinking that Insingue would have been the center of something like the
Hanseatic Leauge, called the something Alliance or the Alliance of
something, and Aurbez’s original political ambition was the restoration of
this kind of entity.

How that has played out in the 150-200 years since the first refugees
settled here is an open question and happy to have this develop in game.

Tim
