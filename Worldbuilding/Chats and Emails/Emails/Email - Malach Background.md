# Malach Background

## Mike Sackton — May 19, 2024 at 9:16 PM

I spent a while today thinking about the background of Malach, which I want
to start to establish.

I have two ideas, curious which you like better, or if you have any
suggestions.

*Option 1*
In the early 1500s, when Derik III is a young prince saving Vostok from
dragons, he finds somewhere in the north a young boy, probably 8 or 9,
living alone in an abandoned hobgoblin camp. The boy is mute (it appears)
but clearly a powerful sorcerer or wizard - he has been creating food to
stay alive. He is brought to Embry, and after a few months regains his
voice but never admits to any memories from before his time in Embry.

By the 1520s he is a young wizard, studying in Tollen, and in the 1530s, he
heads west, to study with the elves in Ainumarya, he says. Then the Great
War comes, and he vanishes for some time. He is presumed dead by Derik III
and other leaders in Sembara, not having returned from the west, and of
course there is much destruction in the west.

He next appears in 1648, shortly after Cece takes command of the Radiant
Alliance, clearly elderly, but also somehow still alive despite his
theoretical age of at least 140. He has a hatred of the Shattered Ice clan,
for reasons unclear, and a fortress in the foothills of the Sentinels, and
offers his services to assist the Radiant Alliance.

Cece accepts his offer, but in 1650 he is tried and (supposively) executed
for treason and illicit wizardry.

Not all of gaps are clear, but at least the treason would likely because he
was practicing some pretty dark necromantic magic to try (or partially
succeed) to extend his life. His hatred of Cece is basically because she
betrayed him and destroyed his fortress/research/etc and he barely escaped
his execution with his life. Likely this is when he turned to a lich,
perhaps deciding it was the only way he could prolong his life.

*Option 2*
In 1583, during the "cursed cold" when fire wouldn't heat things, he is
found somewhere on the borders of the Plaguelands, apparently using fire
normally, a young boy of 8 or 9. He is brought to Embry, etc (similar story
as above - a mute, clearly powerful, etc) and adopted by a powerful ducal
family, who in the early 1600s sponsor him to Tollen to study magic.

He spends the next 15 years or so in Tollen, growing powerful, and by 1620
he is one of the most powerful wizards in Tollen, known to be fascinating
with healing, life extension, and the truth and origin of souls. In 1628 he
leaves Tollen, seeking he says, immortality. He pops up here and there over
the next 5 years: in the highland north, on the western wall, asking
questions about Ulrich Pass, in Addermarch and Tyrwingha and the Shattered
Ice Clan hobgoblins, asking about the plaguelands.

In late 1643, he appears, back in Embry, for the first time in almost 2
decades, preaching war against the Shattered Ice clan. When war breaks out
the next year, he is a heavy proponent of the war, and fights with many of
the armies, although there are rumors he is holding back his most powerful
magics, and he is selfish and haughty and not all of the generals like
having him around.

When Cece comes to power, he is a major part of her early councils, and she
knows how ot handle him just right, making him a pillar of strength for the
Radiant Alliance.

But in 1650, he returns from the Enford campaign in chains. Cece is
tightlipped, but stories of devilry, necromancy, and betrayal spread. He is
executed in the fall of 1650.

Same basic deal here, with Cece discovering something bad about him out in
the foothills near Enford, and him escaping her execution and deciding to
become a lich.

Any other ideas you have I'd be happy to hear...

## Tim Sackton — May 20, 2024 at 2:03 PM

I like the vibes of version 2 better. A few ideas / tweaks / suggestions.

For practical reasons, I think the backstory clues / vibes should pull him
towards the Sentinel and the wreckage of Urlich Pass, not the Plaguelands /
Insigue. I think this works as a subplot in the hobgoblin wars, basically.
One possibility would be that he has established himself, probably under a
different name, as basically a local lord among the Shattered Ice clans,
ruling over some territory in what is now the Marches of Enford, and doing
some fairly evil research on immortality that he could not do in Tollen.
Perhaps the new hobgoblin leader who starts the Third Hobgoblin War is less
tolerant of him, and basically gives him an ultimatum (help us destroy
Masau or leave). He has no interest in this, but his base is attacked
anyway, and he ends up needing to flee on short notice after killing a
bunch of hobgoblins. Returns to Sembara seeking revenge, basically hoping
he can play both sides to a stalemate, leaving his little personal fiefdom
in Enford untouched. The war goes better for Cece than he expected and she
uncovers all this past research.

One option for how he survived could be that he prepared a second, secret
base somewhere in the Sentinels, with a clone. Cece learns of his (evil)
research and long alliance with the hobgoblins, and executes him, but he
revives back in the mountains in secret and begins plotting his revenge
against Cece.

I think given Tollen vibes, it would be quite reasonable for him to grow
into a powerful wizard there, and start poking around at some
"forbidden magic", but then overstep some bounds of propriety and be gently
forced out.
