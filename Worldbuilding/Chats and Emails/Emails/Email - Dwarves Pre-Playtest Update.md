# Dwarves — pre-playtest update

Source subject: (No Subject)

## Tim Sackton — November 21, 2019 at 2:29 PM

I updated dwarves again last night, probably final update before playtesting (probably playing with Isaac's friends on Sunday): https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LtFg6GJLYThfAVQlu80/-LuEM82ayWOIlEtGbAgd

Any glaring imbalances on runes? Simplified the charge mechanic so each rune gets a passive effect (no charges), a weak effect (1 charge), and a strong effect (2-3 charges). Generally weak effects can be "upcast" by expending additional charges to create something on par with a 1st level spell, and strong effects are either level 1 spells (2 charges) or L2 spells (3 charges). Items have 1/2 your level rounded up charges, with a max of 4, so generally by 7th you could cast two 1st level spells or one 2nd level spell and one weak effect halfway between a cantrip and a 1st level spell, which doesn't seem too unbalanced.

The minor runes are all quite simple (effectively a cantrip), so even though each major rune is a bit complicated you never need to know more than one, unless you invest in feats.

In effect the charges here function like spell points from the variant rules in the DMG, so it would be easy to imagine a rune crafter class that could have a pool of charges to allocate to runes each day based on the spell point system, with rune effects for major runes going from 1st-5th level, and some additional epic runes or something for higher level effects that all would just be 1/day (kind of like warlock mystic arcanum). If you kept the item you could imbue energy (charges) into it and activate as one action, or you could choose to just imbue energy and not activate, which would let someone else activate it.
