# dwarves redux

## Tim Sackton — November 17, 2019 at 8:50 PM

Tweaked again to use charges and be more item-y: https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LtFg6GJLYThfAVQlu80/-Ltw71jWsFcgvazJsLhM

A few notes:

1. Everything is now useable by anyone who has the item and knows the word of command to activate it.
2. Everything uses charges; level-gated effects are now restricted by having the number of charges you can imbue an item with increase with a fraction of your level. Charges also mean some less powerful effects can be balanced by requiring fewer charges to use. However the balance is a bit tricky here so the number of charges per effect and the number of charges you can imbue in a rune are subject to change.
3. Need something better for Rune of Valor, single person immunity to fear doesn't feel that exciting given that the passive effect is advantage on saving throws against fear (immunity is better but it feels kind of redundant). Could use a different passive effect or a different active effect, or make it an aura or something (although then becomes very powerful and also kind of redundant with rune of calm). Or come up with an entirely different rune that is appropriate to the ruler caste.
4. There are a few runes that either have different effects depending on the kind of item you inscribe, or one effect that requires a particular item. I think this is flavorful but might be annoying in play.

Not sure when I will next play with Isaac's friends but probably will give this at least an initial playtest then. If you have any more comments would be interested.
