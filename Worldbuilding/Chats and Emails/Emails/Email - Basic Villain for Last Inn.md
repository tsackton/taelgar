# Basic Villain for Last Inn

## Mike Sackton — August 9, 2022 at 6:30 PM

Here's what I have:

There is a dwarf who has become corrupted via magic runes to be a servant
of the Goddess of the End (the offspring of G’Zab and Jinnik, two of the
Void Minds). He is totally hollowed out – there is nothing left to save,
and the original dwarfs mind is completely gone, replaced by a terrifying
aberration that wants to summon a hideous monster to eat the world.


Any thoughts from a lore perspective? I was figuring that the dwarf
stumbled on some curse in the deep somewhere that ended up opening some
kind of void-mind conduit that let him be posssed (and that by the time of
the adventure, the original dwarf is dead and gone, eaten in some fashion
by the Goddess of the End)
