# climate map

## Tim Sackton — February 10, 2021 at 1:16 PM

I made a climate map of a big chunk of Taelgar, using colors and definitions from: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Köppen_climate_classification

I would be very curious as to your impressions. I am a little uncertain about South Cymea through the eastern edge of the Garamjala to the "big desert" and along the Green Sea. This is totally un-created land so figuring out climate will help place what is there.

I am also a bit unsure of parts of the west coast. Generally should be wet winters and dry summers, with some places getting even rainfall, but the distribution of Mediterraen vs other climates is a bit unclear.

For Sembara, I basically followed China through to Manchuria / Siberia, which I think works. Cymea is kinda like Japan, with south Cymea more like south China and Sembara more like the Korean penisula and north. How this intersects with the Plaguelands is a bit uncertain to me.

Between the two deserts is also a bit uncertain -- plausibly this could be one big desert but for various reasons I don't want that. There are mountains there which could influence things, of course, but I'm not sure what is there is correct.

The dashed lines are the equator, 25 degrees north, 45 degrees north, and 70 degrees north.
