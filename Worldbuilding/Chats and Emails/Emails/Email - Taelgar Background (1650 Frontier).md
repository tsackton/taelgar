> Climatically I have this as Bsk - cold, semi-arid climate, kind of like the dry parts of Colorado perhaps with a touch of interior Spain.

My rough idea on climate is something like the following:

* The prevailing winds are the westerlies, coming over the mountains, which
are dry and cold, moderated somewhat (pre-Plaguelands) by summer winds from
the east/Green Sea that used to bring sporadic rain mostly in the form of
drenching thunderstorms. Today the summer winds bring ash-storms more often
than rain, although occasionally massive thunderstorms will blow out of the
east and catch things on fire
* Winter is cool-to-cold, depending on elevation, with the lower lying
areas occasionally dipping below freezing and the higher elevations
frequently getting into the 20s/30s especially overnight. There is little
precipitation in the winter due to the rain shadow of the <CentralMountains>
* Spring is characterized by rushing rivers and snowmelt and rapidly rising
temperatures and the occasional soaking rain. Most of the rain in the
region falls in spring
* Summer is characterized by high temperatures and
thunderstorms/ash-storms, and the threat of hail, lightning, etc especially
at lower elevations
* Autumn is characterized by drying out rivers, moderate temperatures, and
is the best time to travel

There are large day-to-night temperature variations and even in the hotter
summers the nights can be cool. It is often very windy, with swirling winds
from the mountains in winter and fall and ash-storm winds from the
Plaguelands in summer. Everyone in the region is thankful in the
mid-to-late-fall when the ash winds stop.

Elevation & Vegetation

* Aurbez is mostly at 1500-3000 feet, with some settlements higher. Higher
elevations support alpine forests of pine, juniper, and spruce which also
especially cluster around the river valleys.
* The March of Andonne is along the plains, a flatter, lower
elevation area, rarely over 1000', and is more scrubland with few tall
trees, scattered grasses, thorny shrubs, and so on.
* Southern Maseau is similar to Andonne, but as you move north the
ash-winds disappear and the summer rains fall more heavily

Food & Water
* Aurbez is mostly a land of herders, with some small-scale grain
cultivation, and a significant hunting culture. Water is mostly found from
the rivers, from which there is some limited irrigation
* Andonne doesn't really support adequate food resources, and much of the
food is imported, although there is some small scale herding mostly of
goats. Along the northern banks of the western Andonne there is some
irrigation fed-argiculture

Thoughts?
