# revamped elves

## Tim Sackton — June 24, 2021 at 12:48 PM

With the long break between sessions and the fact that for once I have planned ahead a bit, I've been looking at mechanics some more over the past few weeks. One thing I started doing was going back to all the homebrew Taelgar races, which I designed largely before I had run much/any D&D 5e, and am looking at revising them.

My main goals with these revisions were: 1) get rid of fixed racial ASIs, which feel increasingly anachronistic and pointlessly constraining; 2) get rid of subraces in favor of flexible options in the main race package, since I find the whole idea of subraces doesn't really fit with the Taelgar ethos about races, especially the Elder races; 3) make racial magic feel more distinct and unique, and 4) update the lore where possible.

Here is the revised elves document: https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LubwNH3wBO1-n9L8MLu. Summary and design logic is below.

For lore, I added some of the details about the cycle of generations we worked out. For mechanics, I removed the subraces, gave them a floating +2 / +1 ASI, and then added three traits to the base elven package that give options to choose from. First, Elven Experience that reflects their long life and lets you pick three tools/languages/weapons (max one martial) proficiencies. Second, Firstborn's Gift which is basically a choice of either Fey Craft or Mask of the Wild, essentially one option for Elmerca and one for Aldanor. Third, Elven Ritual Magic, which is a list of options that are all effectively level 1 or level 2 ritual spells.

The idea is that elven racial magic is due to their constant close connection to the plane of magic, and I also wanted there to be fairly diverse choices since I think elven racial magic should reflect a wide range of styles. So mechanically, I wanted a variety of choices and also to avoid anything that was limited use (X times per long or short rest). Naturally this inclines towards ritual magic, which I think works well both for flavor and mechanical balance. So the flavor is that to draw on the innate connection with magic takes time and concentration, even though you can do it at wil, and the mechanics are basically a mix of at will cantrips or cantrip-equivalent effects, plus a more powerful effect that requires a ritual to invoke. I also added a racial feat that is basically a variant version of the ritual caster feat, for fun.

As I've designed it right now, the only impact on Delwath would be that you'd get a few additional language or tool proficiencies, since you used your racial ASIs efficiently anyway and both Fey Craft and Fey Sight (now Ritual of Elmerca's Sight) remain options. I am curious about your take on the flavor, especially, and the lore around elven racial magic (and conceivably you might be interested in the ritual caster variant feat at some point), but if you have suggestions for options to add to either the Firstborn's Gift trait (effectively cantrip-level at-will powers) or the Elven Ritual Magic trait (at the power level of 1st or 2nd level ritual spells) I'm open to ideas.
