# Taelgar Campaign

## Tim Sackton — May 3, 2020 at 3:44 PM

Hi all,

Here is the Dunmari campaign idea fleshed out in a bit more detail. If this sounds awesome to all of you, I'll send around some more details about the Dunmari, the area, how other races fit in, and a few other mechanical/background details to consider for creating characters.

If you want to play something different (something more "dungeon of the week", for example, or something more linear and less mysterious), definitely let me know, there are a lot of variants on the basic setting of the Dunmari frontier I can sketch out. I promise I won't be offended.

Here is it, the Dunmari Campaign sketch:

### Bowed but Unbroken

Many, many generations ago, the Dunmari people rode north from their ancestral homelands, conquering the scattered kingdoms and small settlements left behind after the fall of the Drankorian Empire. The Dunmari eventually settled much of the broad, fertile plain between and mountains and the sea. Dunmar grew rich and powerful off the trade passing through its land between Sembara and Chardon, and the agricultural wealth of its lands. Until the great calamity struck, and Cha'mutte rose. The Dunmari cavalry bore a heavy toll in the battles that followed, and Dunmar was broken. Now, the Dunmari eke out a living on the margins of their former kingdom, still a proud people but much reduced in material circumstance.

### Unsettled Powers and Ancient Lands

While the Dunmari tend their flocks and rebuild, slowly, over generations, a threat grows around them. The fertile plains of Dunmar were once the heart of the Drankorian Empire: the lands themselves have been twisted by calamity twice over, and are soaked in magic. Strange things happen to those who ride out into the wilderness, and those who do return cannot speak of what they've seen. The Dunmari have known this land intimately for generations, and secrets are hidden in this history. Whether they should be revealed or left forgotten, who can say.

### Treasure and Empire

While Dunmar defends its eastern frontier against the aberrations of the wild, on its western frontier the Chardonian Empire looks to Drankor. Forces in Chardon dream of glory and reclaiming the lost knowledge of magic that they believe is their birthright. While the Dunmari lay claim to the mysteries of the land as their heritage and birthright, in the quest for knowledge and pursuit of the dream of empire, what boundaries will Chardon be willing to cross? And who does the legacy of Drankor belong to?

### A Time for Heroes

In a corner of Dunmar, a group of companions find themselves thrust into adventure. As the mystery of the wilds and the Drankorian plains slowly reveals itself, our heroes must find a way to uncover the truth and confront the past and future to determine the fate of Dunmar, and perhaps the world.
