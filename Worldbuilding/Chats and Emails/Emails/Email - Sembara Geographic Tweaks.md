# Sembara geographic tweaks

## Mike Sackton — January 21, 2021 at 9:03 PM

Tim,

I've been playing around with Sembaran geography and climate and such, and was wondering what you thought about the following tweaks and climate bits.

Three major highland regions -- these would be areas were much of the land is > 1000', in some cases more than that, but significant enough to impact climate, etc.

Northern Highlands -- from Zovcka across the map to the eastern mountain range, pretty much in a straight line.

Western Highlands - from the central mountains to the line formed by the Wistel (or so) in the south, and then a line from the Aine Hills to Braebine Hills and then curving back to the west to connect to the spur of the central mountains

Volta Highlands - a patch of high land connecting the two little hill systems in Vostok near the Volta.

Mostreve Hills - stretching across the bottom of the map.

The idea is to set up a distinct climate and ecoregion between Zovcka which is really northern and the rest of the highland kingdoms. And this geography also makes Breva kinda interesting -- it is squished in between the 'real' western highlands, the Volta River, and the northern highlands. Might make it into a bit more of a distinct place -- more of a 'lowland' highland kingdom, but also not really bordering Sembara proper, more of a mix of Northlands + Highlands. Ardlas and Lavnoch would be more truly highland kingdoms, 100% in the western highlands, very similar culturally and economically.

Ecoregions--

Northern Highlands, not heavily forested. More like mixed steppe + forest.

Big stretch of northern forest (think Siberia/Canada) from northern Breva across the map to the east. Widens after it passes the Volta Highlands.

Western Highlands, more forested than not, but also a lot of cleared land for sheep pasture and whatnot, and more second growth forest.

Coastal Vostok haven't quite figured out, but probably temperate forest but also where most of the people live and so fairly cultivated. Not sure if you have thoughts.

Volta Highlands, heavily forested. Possibly somewhat difficult to pass through, making Vostok itself a bit harder to get to from Sembara than it looks on the map, and making Tollen a bit more culturally 'Sembaran'.

Main Sembaran (incl Tyrwingha) plain is agricultural with broadleaf forest here and there until you get to the very western bits (western marches) where it is drier and more of a scrubland.

Mostreve Hills - ??

South of Mostreve is plaguelands. But the little patch along the west mountains, I was thinking is these days very very dry, as in the summer the winds blow off the ash of the plaguelands. Australian Outback/Mexican drylands type place. (But before plaguelands, a lot nicer)

Thoughts?

Yeah, I think it does make sense to see Tollen as not particularly "Vostokian". In fact I would think that the small area of coast technically in Vostok but southwest of your light green line would really be basically hinterlands of Tollen and not controlled by any Vostokian government.

On Cymea: yes, the power base of Cymea is really to the east and south, The idea is that the line of hills that run up the coast sort of through the west are kind the central Italian hills -- not a major barrier to movement, exactly, but enough so that influence spreads more easily along the coast. I kind of imagine something like medieval Venice with a power base to the southeast, which culturally and politically dominates the islands and some of the coast, and is really the 'heir to Drankor' in at least self-image. On the inner green sea coast -- agree this needs a name -- you have small city states with much less power, and perhaps some piracy, and with few good harbors not as big a naval power base as it seems.

But in general I see the inner Green Sea as really Tollen. I think Tollen probably controls the straights, and thus access of trade to Sembaran markets. Tollen is maybe more culturally 'northland' than 'Cymea' and so tends to have close economic ties with Sembara, and keeps Cymea's influence in the inner green sea less.

Actually, thinking about this, it would probably make sense for some parts of western Cymea to really be under Tollen influence more than anything else.
