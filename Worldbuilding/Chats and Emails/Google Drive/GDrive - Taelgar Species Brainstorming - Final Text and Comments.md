### Species in Taelgar

Generally speaking, non-human species in Taelgar are quite distinct from humans. They have unique magic, culture, and biology that makes them significantly different from “humans with funny ears.” While there are some cosmopolitan cities, and some non-human species live more frequently with humans than others, kingdoms, laws, and culture have significant human / non-human divides. A halfling in the human kingdom of Sembara would be a halfling first, a Sembaran second, if at all, at least in most cases. But with everything, exceptions abound, and playing against type is a time-honored fantasy trope.

#### Types of Species

The world of Taelgar is only about 6000 years old, and no sentient species have evolved, which means that all sentient beings in Taelgar were created in some way by magic, usually by divine intervention directly. An old but useful typology of species is [here](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1JR_xwNXgvupmXcCT_RJbMJ4xD5EDEFwoom8C8IkRR2c/edit).

**Elder species** (need a new name), who were created by embodied gods:

2. Stoneborn

3. Lizardfolk

4. Dwarves

5. Halflings

6. Orcs

**“Divine” species** (need a name), who were created directly or indirectly from the energy of the Void itself, either by Arha and Malik or by the Three Despairs/Void Mind:

1. Humans

3. Goblins

4. Bugbears

**“Magical” species** (need a name), who are the descendants of the children of Arha and Malik, the archfey, titans, great wyrms, and elder elementals:

1. Fey of various flavors (fairies, fae, changelings, satyrs, harengon, etc)

2. Centaurs (fey descendants but unusual history)

3. Kobolds (dragons)

**“Unusual” species** (those that don’t fit elsewhere):

1. Gnomes (sundered from dwarves, share distant ancestry but have forsaken the Baharazel)

2. Kenku (paradox of time travel created when Aerin, the first kenku, traveled into the Long Breath, the moment out of time when the embodied gods were shaping the elder species)

3. Merfolk and various other underwater creatures (currently unexplained origins)
