[2026-09-13 10:38 AM] rsulfuratus: btw here are my dm notes for the entrance to Orenlas


    ## Paths of Dreams

    Entrance to Orenlas challenge. Steal a bit from Korok forest - the party will have to land the skyship and proceed on foot; from the air, the forests of Orenlas just look like impenetrable mist. There is no way to see a clearing, and no way to tell where one could safely land. Crashing will be met with force.

    Focus on the heartland of Orenlas, the dense forest, roughly 140 miles east to west and 80 miles north to south, on the southeastern side of the lake and mountains. This is where Erelion is, in the center of the forest, although distance is not what it seems in Orenlas, either, and paths and roads don't necessarily travel only through the material world.

    "As you approach the edge of Orenlas, the world around you seems to subtly shift. The air becomes thick with a soft, luminescent mist, casting a dreamlike veil over everything. The trees stand tall, their trunks ancient and gnarled, yet their leaves shimmer with an ethereal glow, as if kissed by starlight. The forest's boundary is not marked by walls or gates but by an almost imperceptible transition, where the ground gently changes from the familiar to a trail blanketed in silver-blue moss. This trail beckons you, yet there is an unspoken understanding: to walk this path is to share your very essence with the ancient spirit of forest, that still resonants with the magical energy of the Age of Creation. The boundary between dreams and reality here is porous, and the forest waits, ready to respond to the intentions you carry in your heart."

    As the party walks into the forest, should feel it plucking at their dreams. Everyone is here for good reasons, so won't reject anyone, but won't necessarily give good or clear information - that depends on rolls and skill.

[2026-09-13 10:38 AM] rsulfuratus: Basic idea is that as they proceed on the path, can use skills to influence outcome of the visions and dreams, the more they connect with the elven dreaming, the more useful the visions they see are. Delwath has advantage on all rolls.

    The basic framework is that characters can try to interact and shape visions, and if they do well might see things in them, depending on what they perceive / dream. Run it kind of like the Hall of Stories, but instead of memories, this is dreams, hopes, motivations, wishes.

    As they walk, feel the dreams lingering and probing. Ask about desires and wishes, allow skill checks to learn and shape the visions they receive.

[2026-09-13 10:39 AM] rsulfuratus: here are my Erelion notes


    ## Erelion

    Emphasize the weird things growing on trees (gold? cloth from insects? You walk into an elven city, and it isn't oriented around trade and commerce the way human cities are. There are strange trees, and a vaguely feywild feel, and gardens of gemstones just beginning to bud from stalks and bright silk butterflies emerging from cocoons that are spun from silk that is harvested for clothes.)

    Pass through gate, see what they are looking for. People look at them strangely. They'll probably be kind of confused and uncertain. If they strike out on a definite direction, let them, otherwise greeter arrives.

    A few people they might meet in Erelion:

    `**`Caretaker Erdhin** (ER-dheen, try to pronounce with the tap /r/, e.g. like fast tt or dd)

    "That was long ago, even as the elves count, six cycles or more. There are few now left who lived in those days, much less remember them."

    Other names:
    (male)
    **Cirdore** (*KEER-doh-reh*, again with the last reh tapped if possible)

    (female)
    **Iridris** (*ee-REE-drees*, tapped /r/ if possible)
    **Nerwenye** (*NER-wen-ye*)

    Basic ideas for people:
    - an elf Delwath knows, who fought in the Great War, from his `*`ka*; escaped the Battle of [[Urlich Pass]] into Sembara, returned to Orenlas during his first `*`mela*, now settled here
    - some kind of hospitality person, not that there are inns exactly, but there probably are facilities for visitors and someone to feed them
    - someone curious about stories of their skyship, perhaps a historian of sorts, or a poet inspired by the legends of the ship
