# 2026-10-10 - Dwarven Refugee Wizard and Chardon

[2026-10-10 05:09 PM] Deciusmus: Basic idea for a character....

Dwarven wizard, grew up in a traditionalist dwarven settlement (Labkhandur Azrath - naming -- lots of dwarven settlements are called Labkhandur (thing) basically meaning "memory of labkhans...") nestled somewhere in the Sentinels, isolated and isolationist, trying to recreate the olden times. If not idyllic, he had as good a childhood as one can as a dwarf after the Godfall, and as his magical talents became evident, he decided to go to Chardon to try to earn money, resources, etc to support his community, maybe even learn some powerful magics, etc.

Chardon was disillusioning, but not a total disaster. At first, it was a struggle to get in the door anywhere -- but he was already say a 2nd or 3rd level wizard at this point, and he found a series of teachers and positions that would accept a dwarf. But it was always a struggle to learn more, and increasingly he became disillusioned with the idea that there was a path to success in the Chardonian Confederacy.

Now a relatively powerful transmuter (https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/2190886-wizard#Transmuter) he has lost any real hope of gaining legitimate wealth and status to protect his home community, and increasingly is willing to take whatever positions and jobs he can find that will increase is magical knowledge and give him access to better tools and technologies and skills.

His world shatters when he hears from home: disaster has befallen Labkhandur Azrath.

[2026-10-10 05:23 PM] Deciusmus: The disaster...

A hobgoblin mining camp (well, a slave camp guarded and ruled by hobgoblins) 50 miles north of Labkhandur Azrath was struck and the hobgoblins badly hurt by the Kharaz Azarak -- a dwarven fanatic group that believes that death in righteous battle against those who killed the Baharezeal provides a chance to find the Heart of the Mountain.

The hobgoblins retaliate, and a company of battle mages marches in all directions, seeking revenge and bringing retribution.

Labkhandur Azrath had the bad luck to be on their warpath, and 10% of the population was executed by hellfire. But that wasn't the real disaster.

Bahrâ Nâdum -- memorial stones and carvings to the Bahrâzel are not uncommon in some traditional dwarven socities. But hobgoblins increasingly find these offensive, and often claim they are symbols or markers for fanatic groups like the Kharaz Azarak. So they demanded the dwarves destroy their stones. But the dwarves of Labkhandur Azrath resisted, and so the hobgoblins retaliated by burning the entire valley to the ground, enslaving the healthiest and strongest of the remaining dwarves, and leaving behind a ruin.

And so the people of Labkhandur Azrath become refugees

[2026-10-10 05:26 PM] Deciusmus: My character learns of this and leaves Chardon, full of both guilt and rage. He learns where the bulk of the survivors are (the refugee camp where the game begins) and makes his way there, looking for justice, or if not justice, revenge
