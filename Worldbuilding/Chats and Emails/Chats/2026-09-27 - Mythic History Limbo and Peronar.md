[2026-09-27 02:00 PM] rsulfuratus: ### Backstory

    Long ago, before the multiverse was created, the primordial cosmos was place of impossible change, where time did not operate in a linear fashion and cause did not precede effect. Fundamental forces - order, chaos, growth, death, and many more - warred (in some metaphysical sense) for supremacy but nothing could truly thrive.

    Into the primordial cosmos stepped the Divine Presence. They sundered the cosmos into the multiverse during an event called the Riving, creating the Material Plane in the process. During the Riving, the fundamental planes of chaos (Limbo) and order (Mechanus) were created, isolated and trapping the primordial forces of order and chaos into their own realms and leaving the Material Plane balanced.

    In Limbo, the ancient beings from before the Riving who had "fought" for chaos to sweep across the cosmos became the chaos beasts. Tied to the plane of chaos itself, they were forever changing, growing into massive fronts and then decaying into fragments and splinters, in an endless cycle of change. In this way, they could not threaten the material plane, for they had no permanence, no stability for motive and power to grow and be shaped.

    This changed when Rai and the monks attempted, and failed, to close the wound between Limbo and the Material Plane, created by Cha'mutte's death. When Rai and the monks were pulled into Limbo, some of the material plane was pulled in too, and some of Limbo was ejected. This planar essence solidified into the Relic of Reshaping, which brought stability. Some time later, a passing chaos storm, a chaos beast at the height of its power where normally it would start to decohere, was caught by the relic. It stabilized, and remembered. It remembered the Divine Presence exiling it and its kin from the Material Plane, it remembered its desire to not just wallow in chaos but bring it to the world. It remembered, and began to plan.
