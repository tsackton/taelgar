## E138 — “(no subject in export)”

- **From:** cos@cs.brandeis.edu (Reply-To; From header absent)
- **Date:** March 16, 1998, 8:30 PM EST

Trying to come up with a possibility for someone who might reasonably
appear and disappear in different parts of the campaign, kept coming
into conflict with possibilities for someone who was actually *at* the
academy.  There are a few ways to integrate these two, but none of
them feel very real to me so I wasn't able to come up with much of a
character around any of them.  It seems to me that if I'm going to be
there only on certain occasions, Mike would probably use me more as a
plot device than as a player-character, and that's just fine with me,
because I'm mainly interested in getting a chance to role-play rather
than actually play a campaign.  I think I should be someone from the
city outside the academy.

My favorite concept of a character so far: A male, about 22 years
old.  Comes from a reasonably successful family background, but did
not settle on a vocation or focus in life.  Instead, he's tried a lot
of different things and met a lot of different people.  He's used the
mobility granted by his background to switch professions on occasion,
to travel to neighbouring cities on occasion, and tends to "context
hop" a lot.  He's got a very wide range of knowledge while lacking
depth; knows enough about many professions/crafts to dicuss them
intelligently with their practitioners, but not to actually *do* them
well on his own, usually.  He's the same with people, generally
preferring to meet someone new than to deepen an existing
acquaintance.  As a result of this lifestyle, he tends to have an
eclectic, if shallow, set of connections, and often shows up in the
oddest places.

Brynn's character would have to be an exception to his usual tendency,
since he knows her very well.  My thought was the he met her at some
tavern/inn/restaurant sort of place, where he was probably working for
a while.  Just to get a feel for that kind of work and to become
familiar with different kinds of people, you know, the same kind of
motivation that makes some people in our modern world put off college
for a semester and work as a cab driver.  For some reason, she caught
his attention and he tried to pick her up.  What came of that?  Hard
to tell without knowning what she is like, which is what I was trying
to do the other day :)  Perhaps they did date for a while, but then
realized that having a relationship just wasn't her cookie (ooh, look,
I'm using Brynnterms!) so they scaled it back into something less than
that.  Brynn, help out with this part?

What he might have done with the past few years of his life, kind of
depends on what this world is like and what there is to do in it, so
Mike would determine that.  Good possibilities would be things like
having signed on as a sailor on some trading ship for a year, having
started an apprenticeship with a craftsman but then dropped out,
having been a performer of some sort (i.e. an actor), or having joined
a military or guard sort of outfit for a temporary stint.  He's not
very good at sticking to any one thing, and keeps trying something
else.  Mike, help out with this part?

Does this sound workable?
  -- Cos
