## E050 — “Re: Taelgar: Chardon”

- **From:** Tim Sackton
- **Date:** March 12, 1998, 1:16 AM (archive timestamp; no Date header)
- **To:** Mike Sackton
- **Cc:** Dave Schwartz, David Kong, Eric Rosenbaum

At 08:14 PM 3/11/98 -0500, Mike Sackton wrote:
>Quick question on what Tim said:
>Time frame: are you speaking from end of 3rd, beginning of our campaign,
>or early 4th?
>

I'm speaking from the end of the 3rd age.  In the fourth age, the Chardon renaissance probably will spring back redoubled.  With Irebor and Rohan in ruins and much of Sembia drained of resources by the war, Chardon is one of the biggest relatively undamaged areas there is.  It will continue growing in importance during the fourth age.  Particularly since I expect Chardonians will start to explore the seas in earnest.  Also, I wouldn't be surprised if Chardonians attempt to reestablish contact with the South.  Also, Chardon will grow into a country in earnest.  They are one of the few places that will have resources to expand early in the 4th age.  Everything else will be too involved in recouperating.

-Tim

## E058 — “Re: When (Taelgar Time) did the campaign end?”

- **From:** Tim Sackton
- **Date:** March 13, 1998, 1:43 AM (archive timestamp; no Date header)
- **To:** Mike Sackton
- **Cc:** Dave Schwartz, Dave Kong, Eric Rosenbaum

At 02:05 PM 3/12/98 -0500, Mike Sackton wrote:
>On Thu, 12 Mar 1998, Tim Sackton wrote:
>
>> As soon as Cha'mutte died, the fourth age began.
>>
>
>Tim, yes, I know. However, what I was wondering was when in the year did
>the Fourth Age begin? The changing of an age is not a magical thing,
>right? It simply begins where the old age was. So what season does the 4th
>age begin in? What day, what month?
>

Well, ages exist in two fashions.  In one sense, they are defined by dragon politics.  I haven't really worked this out entirely yet, but it's basically when a dragon dies/looses.

In another sense, they define turning points in history that are not easily categorized or even recognized at the time.  Generally there are large periods of chaos surrounding the death of a dragon.  I mean, Rohan is in chaos, basically.  Sembia narrowly averted total disaster.  This next question is me being a total idiot, but I don't think you ever did anything about the plague, did you?  Sorry, it's been a while and I haven't thought about Taelgar at all until recently.  The last few weeks of the campaign (game time) are a little fuzzy.  And the hundreds of thousands of hobgoblins massed to the north didn't just suddenly vanish.  All that happened was they lost their magical supply of food.  In short, Taelgar will not be a nice happy fuzzy place immediately.  The next few generations will be really tough.  The course of history will be changed.  In 100 years, historians will look back and say "that was the turning of an Age."  And some well-respected historian will put an exact date on it, and that'll become the official reckoning.  The official reckoning generally isn't used though, except in scholarly works or by the government.  The common people tend to date things according to local calendars.  This is all subject to change though.  What do you guys think?

-Tim
