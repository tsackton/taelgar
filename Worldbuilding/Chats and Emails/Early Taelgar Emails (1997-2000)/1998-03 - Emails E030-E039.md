## E031 — “Re: Half-Elves”

- **From:** David Schwartz
- **Date:** March 10, 1998, 3:12 PM EST
- **To:** Mike Sackton
- **Cc:** Tim Sackton, Dave Kong

I say, there is no mixing of the races.  An elf is an elf, a human is a
human.  It's like a man and an ape having sex.  They are different
species, so I say that human, or ogre, or anything else sperm just can't
fertilize an elf, or any other different species' eggs.  So no
intermixing.  Otherwise, what's to stop, say, dwarves and halflings from
getting it on and making dwarlings.
So that's my stand, no mixing of the races they are different species, not
just differen types of the same species.
As for the wild talent person; wouldn't the newly restored Academy, after
just being reintroduced to true magic and gemstone magic and all that. be
actively seeking out those people with the spark?  (or whatever)  Wasn't
that what Rai was kind of doing as we wandered around?
I'd say that if indeed the person was able to do true magic and was not
training, very quickly they'd do something stupid, like overdraw or
whatever, and then their heads would explode.

Yeah.  So that's my take:  no half-elves, no wild talents.

But lots of Kenku, yeah, lots of kenku.  And mockers.  Yeah.

-Dave

see the person they are communicating with.  This isn't so bad though, it
works really well when you're flying around and there's nothing to get in
the way of your sight lines.  Kenku need line of sight because nobody
knows how to contact others by finding conciousnesses or whatever- they
need a physical location to target on in order to reach the mind.  In
other respects, howver, kenku telepathy is much like halfling telepathy.

beyond simple communication.

Umm, gray man stuff.  Right.  See, the thing with gray man is that not all

this ritual ceremony thing, the details of which are a closely gaurded

certain icons of vorshitaal are used to transform the person.  A couple of
things happen when you do this:  the gray man gets his conciousness in a
new and different spot on the astral plane.  The gray man also gets a
totally new body, a sort of proto-kenku with no true form.  See, most
kenku shapeshift and come back to their real form, most kenku find it
annoying to stay shapeshifted in one form that is not their own for more
than say a month or two.  But gray men have no true form, sure, they have
a few faces which they use out of habit, but they have no actuall form to
which they muyst return.  The form which they inhabit, as well as the
power to hide conciousnesses comes directly from eru- that's why it's not
