Now, some comments on the geography and population outside of, but bordering, G.C. -- first, to the west of course is open ocean and the Shantu.  The pennisula is occupied to a certain extent by a large, unihabitable forest.  To the east, I would say that G.C. stretches a good distance towards the mountains, and then gives way.  A few ideas for what it gives way to: unihabitated land, a few small pseudo-G.C. city-states, New Volkrad, bandits/barbarians/nomadic tribesmen, a Bjornlings type society, a bunch of ghost cities from the Drankorian Empire.  Something off of one of those suggestions would be a starting point.  To the north, G. C. extends some distance, and then I like the idea of it giving way to a host of various sized duchies, etc, possibly collected under a leader but not necessarily.  Sometimes there could be a leader, sometimes maybe not.  These would be people who were originally a blend of Drankorian and northerner stock, in the very nothernmost ends of the Drankorian empire at its greatest extent.  They would have been spared the worst of the plague, but would have been cut off from all outside civilization.  Gradually contact would have been re-established with Charon, but soon after invaders would have come from somewhere and set up Amn (perhaps Kenku?).  Now, in the F.A., these people would be a mix of the invaders, the original stock, and refugees from farther north from the war.  Could be a really interesting place to set a campaign.  To the north of them are several hobgoblin-occupied territories (northern Amn and Volkrad), then wilderness, maybe or maybe not inhabitated by nomads, tribesmen, etc.  Note on hobgoblins: under Cha'mutte they would have been "civilized" to a great extent, but would still be cruel at heart -- more on this later.  Finally, to the south in the coastal mountains, and to the west on the inhabitable parts of the pennisula, would be a culture of very different ethnic stock, perhaps one that was never conquered by Drankor.
