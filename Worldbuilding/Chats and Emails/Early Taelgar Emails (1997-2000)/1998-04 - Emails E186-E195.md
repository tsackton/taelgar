## E188 — “Re: A rather long bit about Taelgar”

- **From:** Joshua M Glaser
- **Date:** April 6, 1998, 11:59 PM EDT
- **To:** Mike Sackton

>How old are you?
  24 years old, born May 23rd

>What do you look like?
  about 5'9, with short brown hair, a short beard and grey eyes. I tend to
wear a lot of brown clothes ( the dirt stains are less obvious that way).
When I'm out of the city I usually wear an old cloak I bought a few years
ago. I'm quite proud of my boots actually, they're these big black leather
thigh-length boots that cost me nearly two months pay. My gloves are rather
nice too, soft black leather with these silver bands that I found last year
out at a dig in the mountains.

>What are your favorite places to be?

	I particularly like to be outdoors down in the dirt exploring,
particularly in the mountains where the air is clear and civilization is
far away. I wish I could stay up there more often but I have to spend a lot
of time in the city as well, working at the Academy and occasional
freelance jobs trying to earn or raise the money to be able to go out and
explore.

>What would you/do you do for a living?

	I often work at the Academy researching archeological artifacts and doing
other assorted jobs in and around the city. My expeditions are what I
really think of as my living, the other jobs are simply ways to get money
during the seasons when it is impractical to be wandering the countryside.

>What is your goal(s) in life?

	To explore, basically. In particular I'd love to discover something
entirely new and important, especially something related to dwarves or any
other unknown peoples who live/lived in an underground society.

>How do you feel about death?

	I don't know, I suppose death really doesn't bother me as much as it does
most people. I believe death is simply a return to the earth, as all things
have died before, so will we die in the future. I mean, I spend my time
searching through the ruins of civilizations that have died and left their
remains. Who am I to think that we are any better than they were?

>What do you think of your parents?

	I actually don't think of my parents that much, I mean, they're nice
people, there's nothing unusual about them. Every now and then I send them
letters and visit during the holidays when I can, though my lifestyle means
I'm often far from home.
>Who is the most important person/thing/idea/&lt;blank> to you?

	Exploration and the search for "new" discoveries is probably the most
important thing for my, my life is based around this search and I'm never
happier than when I'm discovering a new ruin or a new artifact.

>What is your favorite food?

	I'm really not picky about food, it's often hard to find much out in the
woods and the mountains, so I've gotten used to eating anything that's
around. I suppose I like potatos quite a lot, particularly freshly dug and
cooked whole.( So they still have that slightly earthy taste).

>What is your religious/philosophical view on life?

	I don't belong to any particular church or formal religion if that's what
you mean. I suppose I sort of believe in the superiority of the earth
itself. I mean, we just walk around on the surface with our wars and our
lives, but the earth and the plants simply are. While I don't solidly
believe in this, I like to think that there is some kind of life energy
flowing around the world, keeping everything going and becoming a part of
everyone and everything, and returning to the earth when it dies.
>What do you do in your spare time?

	Actually, I've started to try to learn to draw and paint, as I always want
to bring the landscapes from the mountains back with me to the city. It
gets so dull and artificial in the city sometimes, and my paintings help to
keep me happy.

>What sort of person would make a good companion/friend/lover?

	Someone who also likes to travel, and to be outdoors searching through
ancient ruins. Also someone who can appreciate the beauty of the landscapes
and of life, I've met too many people who care simply about finding things
merely to become famous and rich rather than for the simple love of the
search and the life.

>Are you/have you ever been in love?

	I was in love once, 3 years ago, with a woman who was also an adventurer
like myself. Unfortunately, she was not quite the same as I am, she was too
much the glory hound, she couldn't appreciate beauty simply for beauty's
sake. We were close friends for a time, until I realised that our outlooks
were too incompatible, as I was always taking my time and sitting in the
dirt while she ran about always looking for bigger better treasures and
trying to puff up her importance whenever we brought anything back to the
city.
>What do you think about love?

	I try not to think about love too often, as the thoughts often make me
rather depressed, wondering whether or not I'll ever meet the right person,
wondering how important it is to me. I try to convince myself that love is
not that important, that I'm happy by myself. But sometimes that just
doesn't work, and I let myself realise how important love really is to me.

>What sort of mythical beast would you most like to meet?

	I'm not really sure, never thought about it before, maybe a griffon?
>If you could change one thing about yourself, what would it be?

	I'd like to be able to deal with people better; as I spend most of my time
away from normal society I tend to have problems dealing with people when I
am in the city for any long period of time.
>What does your room look like?

	A mess, basically, I've got stacks of old books sitting on shelves, on
tables, even on the floor at the moment. Various artifacts and stones lie
here and there, scraps of paper litter the desk. There are spider webs in
the corners (been meaning to clean those up but hey, at least they catch
the other bugs). One corner is reserved for painting in, so the floor (the
walls too to be honest) is splattered with assorted colors, I sort of like
the green. Five or six of my favorite paintings hang on the walls, with a
few more leaning on the shelves.
>What would your dream vacation/journy be?

	To spend time in and about the Khazad mountains I think, I was there once,
and it was the most beautiful place I can remember [I don't remember how
far away they actually are, if they're close then some other random
mountains].
>If you could have any one thing, what would it be?

	Something dwarvish, I suppose, I'd really love some kind of magical item
that could help excavating the earth or some such.
>What do you think of the other members of the current group?

	I've met Rylar before, nice girl, really strong in her studies at the
Academy. It's nice to have someone else from the Academy around, I know I
can trust her. Daren seems an interesting man, a bit forceful and pushy but
that's what's expected in a knight, and he is a good leader. His faith
seems rather powerful, I just hope it doesn't get us into trouble. I'm not
quite sure what to make of Balor, he's rather fearless but also kind of
impulsive. I know he'll do his best but I'm somewhat wary of his
trickyness, I'm not completely sure I can trust him yet. Sinis seems to be
a good man, and if he was taught by ????? then he should be reliable. He's
a bit headstrong though, and seems to be a little too eager to kill.

>Do you think we have enough combat?  Yes, more would go too slowly.
>Do you think I am trying to make the game too role-playing oriented? Not
really, it's nice to have actual motives and reasons for doing stuff,
otherwise I tend to get distanced from the game and begin to stop caring
about what's going on.
>Do you think you are being challenged enough? Too little? about right for
the moment, especially since we're still figuring out the system and our
characters. There should probably be a few more hobgoblins though, when
there's about an equal number of us and them it's really not an equal fight
considering our level of magic.
>What do you think of the NPCs? good, when important they have
personalities, when unimportant they don't which is really good.
>Are you having fun? Yes
