>How old are you?

>What do you look like?

(This will also include some personality notes).  Daren is a man of
averidge hight and good build.  He has hazel eyes and short, dark hair.
He tends to hold himself tall and streight.  He can often be seen staring
off into the horizon when not occupied, as if looking for something.  He
switches sometimes between being pensive and quiet, to being very
overbearing and loud when he feels things are going in a bad direction.
He often likes to be incharge, since he is not always sure what other
people will do when not under guidence, and tends to feel responsable
when things go bad as a result.  He has a very well defined sense of the
way the world should be, and does his best to accomedate the beliefs of
others, although it is sometimes difficult, when they chose to do things
he considers to be morally questionable, or dishonrable.

>What are your favorite places to be?

Daren is an explorer, possesed of a strong sense of wonderlust, he likes
to travel, and especially liked to be in the mountains near the Barrony
of High Horn in Sembia.  Mountain ranges always seemed to have a sense of
mystery about them to Daren, so he likes to be in the mountains, this fit
well during his training with the Ogre, when he spent that time living
with them in the mountains.

>What would you/do you do for a living?

Daren is a knight of Sembia and takes his duty's very seriously.  On the
other hand, he strongly believes in the ability of Sembia to defend
itself, and therefor believed that it was time to go into the world, and
to try and make it a safer place for people in general, who do not live
in the protected realm of Sembia.

>What is your goal(s) in life?

Daren has yet to look past his sense of duty for any long term goals,
which is partially his reason for traveling to Chardon.  In Sembia, he
saw his existence as being little more then yet one more of the mass of
armed men protecting Sembia, and wanted to go out and make a difference.

Daren believes in some fation that the armed forces in general are
insoficiant for trying to make the world saffer, since they generally
stick to there home country and dont venture out save in war.  Thus,
Daren hopes someday in the future to found an order of his own, of people
like himself, who are willing to go out into the world and risk there
lives to protect others.

>How do you feel about death?

Daren believes in the afterlife, and believs it to be a place where those
of good heart will live in peace with there friends and loved ones.
There, also, in heaven he can finally 'meet' Aru, and perhaps serve him
there.  (You will have to elaborate on some of the religouse belief's in
Telgar, such as "is there a war in heaven between the god's?" and things
like that)

>What do you think of your parents?

Daren holds his father, whom also was a knight in the highest respect.
The two had some disagreements, Daren believed it was better to go into
the world and find a place that needed him, while his father believed it
was there duty to stay and defend Sembia.  They are on good terms
however.  Daren's mother always encouradged him in his choices, and so he
feels somewhat closser to her then to his father.  Daren has two
brothers, one older who is a knight, and a younger one in training, and a
younger sister.
Father:  Sir Galahad of house Galador
Mother:  Moira (origionaly of house Terdan)
Brother(age 19):  Galen
Brother(age 15):  Jared
Sister(age 16):  Serina

>Who is the most important person/thing/idea/&lt;blank> to you?

The TWO most important people in Daren's life are his love Sarah, and his
friend Kyle (explained later on in detail in the section on friendship)

>What is your favorite food?

If they have pasta on Taelgar, then tourtalini, if not, then I suppose
lentil soup.

>What is your religious/philosophical view on life?

Daren believes that those with who are strong or gifted in some way have
a responsability to protect those weaker then themselfs, he believes this
is what Aru wishes of humanity.  He values honesty and trust above almost
all esle (thats why he gets so pissed at Dan's guy sometimes).

>What do you do in your spare time?

Daren tends to daydream allot when he is alone, and has a very active
imagination.  Normally he is happy just being with his friends and
interacting with them, which represents the one regret he has about
traveling away from Sembia, since all his friends are so far away now.

>What sort of person would make a good companion/friend/lover?

The key is honesty and a good heart.  because Daren values trust so much,
he is only really willing to call someone a friend once he knows they are
honest.  HE looks for them to have some sense of right and wrong, people
who will try and help those in needs.  Daren is slow to make friends, but
values those friendships intensly, and would give his life for a friend
without a second thought.  He would never lie or betray one for any
reason, it would be completely against his nature.

Daren has a number of friends back in Sembia who are in variouse walks of
life, some are commoners, others are nobles like himself, or in the armed
forces.  The person he considers his truest friend is Sir Kyle of house
Grey Falcon(same age as Daren) who also joined the knight hood, but
remains in Sembia.  THe two are like brothers, and Daren does hope to
visit Sembia every now and then to visit him.  (Feel free to use this as
a plot hook at some point, just dont kill him off or make him a priest of
evil or something rediculous)

>Are you/have you ever been in love?

Daren for some time was in love with Kyle's sister, Sarah, who is a year
older then Daren.  The two felt similarly about eachother, and she wished
the same things for the world that Daren does (although she is more of a
realist, and sees Daren's views as overly idealistic at times, but in an
affectionit sort of way).  The two promised to wait for each other when
Daren left.  (Character note:  Sarah has been trained as a priest of
Aru's aspect of healing).

>What do you think about love?

Daren views love as the highest of commitments, a bond that should never
be betrayed.  It is sacred, and to be charished above all else.  (Yes, he
tends to be overly sapy, romantic, and idealistic, and nieve, but thats
his personality)

>What sort of mythical beast would you most like to meet?

Daren has always been fascinated be storys of Gryphons, being majestic
and beutifull, he would love to meet one.

>If you could change one thing about yourself, what would it be?

Daren wishes he could get more insite into people.  He is often
frustrated by subtleties of life which escape him sometimes, and wishes
he could be more sure what those around him were going to do, or what
they were thinking.

>What does your room look like?

Daren has kept his room at home very clean, and has it decorated with a
number of paintings featuring some of the more heroic feats and battles
performed by the variouse knights of legend, whom Daren hopes to emulate.

>What would your dream vacation/journy be?

To travel into the deep misty mountains, in cool weather.  The sort of
weather where the sky is a light grey and the air feels like its about to
rain but never does, giving a sense of mystery and awe to the whole
afair.  And there, at the end, to finally meet a Gryphon...

>If you could have any one thing, what would it be?

I always thought the "sun sword" in D&D was a really cool concept.

Thunor:  Daren likes Thunor, but hasent seen enogh open personality

so Daren feels a certain fair of good will towards him.  Daren would like
to get to know him better.
Balor:  Balor is something of a disturbing mystery to Daren.  He is
sneaky and not open about his intentions, and therefore Daren doesnt
trust him completely.  They have now saved eachother enought times during
combat that Daren isnt worried about him betraying anyone, but Daren also
believes that Balor is not always honest to Daren in what he does.
THerefor, Daren doesnt fully trust him.
Rylar:  Rylar is so far the person in the group that Daren feels the
least concern about.  SHe has been open, helpfull, and put herself at

Sinis:  To soon to tell, cuts down surrendered foes :-P  NOt sure what to
