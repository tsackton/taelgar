## E196 — “Taelgar Stuff”

- **From:** Mike Sackton
- **Date:** April 25, 1998, 4:38 PM EDT
- **To:** Taelgar Discussion Group, Dave Schwartz, Eric Rosenbaum, Mike Sackton, Tim Sackton

How's it going with you guys?

No one seems to have said anything about Taelgar for a while. I'm starting
to get some really cool things going in the campaign, but I'm also
starting to wonder if my campaign will diverge from "real" Taelgar or
not... Here's a few questions:

Legends of ancient holy swords... I was thinking that there could exist a
sword made before time, created by Aru (does anyone prefer this
spelling?), maybe somehow related to the temple. Also, although not holy,
I was wondering about Derik III's sword, and thinking it might be cool if
it were a lost artifact of sorts.

Elementals... Last session a critical success on a summoning spell
summoned a earth elemental with a crude stone crown, calling himself
"Brother to the Mountains"... I was thinking he was some sort fo elemental
leader, maybe related to the ogre firstborn, or perhaps some sort of
elemental king.. what's the deal with elementals anyway?

Faires...any thoughts on them, other than where they live?

Mocker question: Would the mockers hunt down one of there own to test him?
(maybe someone they were testing for greater knowledge/responsiblity)

Chardon: thoughts about the Academy, harvestfest, "graduating" from the
Academy, the political side of the Academy, politics in Chardon in
general, etc.

Well, that's it for now. I don't really expect much in the way of answers,
but it would be nice :). Is everyone still interested in doing a lot of
work on Taelgar over the summer? And when are you all done with finals?

Mike
