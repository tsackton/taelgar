## E008 — “Re: Taelgar”

- **From:** Tim Sackton
- **Date:** October 27, 1997, 1:26 AM (archive timestamp; no Date header)
- **To:** Dave Schwartz, Mike Sackton, Eric Rosenbaum

I don't have time to get to comments actually about Taelgar (but I'll do that eventually, like, say, tues).  I do have a few general comments however.  First, we need to get Kong in on this, or at least I'd really like to.  He was instrumental in giving me ideas in the first years of the campaign, and I'd really like to get him involved in further work.  Eric and Schwartz, you guys both had closer relationships to him than I did, maybe one of you should broach it (although at some point I'm going to get around to giving him a call and seeing what's up).  Second, although worrying about the way the web page is all well and good, we have to have substance before we design appearance, although I think Eric has some pretty good ideas for eventual appearance of the page.  Graphic design will be a problem.  I know a little about photoshop, Schwartz probably knows more, but neither of us (I think) know enough to create good pictures from scratch.  We will have access to a scanner though, so we could resort to Eric and/or Schwartz and/or Kong drawing stuff to be scanned and modified in photoshop.  Anyway, I've still got reading to do for my 9:00 class, so I really have to go.  I'm glad other people are starting to get into this.

-Tim
