# October 1997 — emails E001–E004

## E001 — “Taelgar”

- **From:** David Schwartz
- **Date:** October 25, 1997, 2:19 PM EDT
- **To:** Mike Sackton
- **Cc:** Dave Kong, Eric Rosenbaum, Tim Sackton

	Hey guys, I was just looking at the taelgar web page and thing to myself.
We *really* should write up Taelgar.  it's a great, diverse world, with
new races, different racial attributes than the usual, an interesting and
(mostly) developed history.  It has a new form of magic, the dreamworld,
various planes, interesting personalities.  It's a cool place and would be
a great place to adventure in.
	So here's my proposition:  Over Christmass break, we all take one
weekend, a full weekend (or maybe two days in the middle of the week, the
important thing is we have to set a largish chunk of time and stick to it)
and during that time we all get together and write down as much as we can
about Taelagr.  We can talk amongst ourselves, finish hashing out rules
and plotlines and ideas that never got fully developed and get all this
down on paper.  If we acually sit down and work onj this stuff, we can
accomplish an enormous amount.
	I'm starting to miss Taelgar, Nick's place and very time I hear
"Fly" I chuckle to myself.  Anyhow, one weekend will get an enormous
amount out of the way.
	As a side note, I'm having increasingly brilliant ideas of how to
do Taelgar as a *really* cool website, or, better yet, a cd-rom.
	Well, one wekend will accomplish a lot.  Tell me what you think.

Dave
