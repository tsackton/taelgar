## E302 — “Taelgar the Fourth Age, Part 1”

- **From:** Mike Sackton
- **Date:** June 2, 1998, 11:50 AM EDT
- **To:** Dave Schwartz, Eric Rosenbaum, Mike Sackton, Tim Sackton, Dave Kong

The campaign began with a rather forced meeting on the road between the
four original characters, and then a battle between them, two rangers
(side note: The rangers have, by 153 F.A., begun to spread beyond the
Sembia area that the retreated to at the end of the third age. The
organization is more oriented towards exploration and rediscovery than
service, as it was in the 3rd age. Within Sembia the primary goal is
similar to that of a park ranger, or the Rangers from Tolkien. Outside of
Sembia however, the group becomes more "free" and tends to do things like
exploring the edges of the maps, seeking lost communities and such from
the battles (not so much anymore, as it is unlikely that any will be
found). Some traveller's aid is provided, but again, mainly in Sembia--
The two relevant rangers were both natives of Sembia, who had traveled
West to explore various passages between Sembia and Chardon, and to begin
to establish something of a prescence in the remoter northern areas of
Chardon).

Descriptions of characters:

Daren Galador:

A Knight of Sembia. Has a fiance Sarah (a healer), and a good friend Kyle
(a knight as well). See forthcoming email about Daren.

Balor:

A Mocker and mage. Native to Chardon. See Balor email.

Rylar:

A mage of the Academy. Very good friends with Belist (the son of the Baron
of Highhorn, currently studying at the Academy). See Rylar email.

Thunor:

An archaelogist and earth mage, studying at the Academy. See Thunor email.

And how they got to the hobgoblin battle:

Daren had been travelling westward for a long while, exploring. He left
Highhorn shortly after being knighted, and eventually found his way
towards Chardon. Somewhere along the way, he had a run in with some
hobgoblins, who almost defeated him, when magical fire came to his aid and
drove them back. North of the encounter spot, Daren was attacked by
another band of hobgoblins, where he lost his sword (which he had won in
the king's tournament for new knights--dwarven make, very nice
sword). He fell unconscious, but his horse continued riding south.

Rylar was visiting her parents, who live about 2.5 days north of Chardon,
and was on her way back, when she saw Daren, unconscious, on his horse,
moving toward her. She was travelling with Thunor, who had found the
remanants of an old road, and had followed it to some degree, but wanted
to return to the Academy for supplies and suchness before following it any
further.

Balor had been sent into the wilderness by the Mockers, mostly as a test.
He saw some hobgoblins, and started tracking them south. He is, after all,
impulsive. These hobgoblins were following Daren, and the caught up with
him soon after he ran (unconscious, though) into Rylar and Thunor.

Battle ensued, during which the two rangers (Trith and Luxor -- both
female) revealed themselves as existing, and fought. 6 hobgoblins
defeated.

More to follow

Mike

## E308 — “Taelgar Guide- 1st Part”

- **From:** Mike Sackton
- **Date:** June 21, 1998, 8:06 PM EDT
- **To:** Taelgar Discussion Group, Dave Schwartz, Eric Rosenbaum, Mike Sackton, Tim Sackton

Really Basic Stuff:

Taelgar is a large world. The "known" world is primarily temperate.  The
world itself is probably twice the size of Earth. There is one moon,
proportionally the same size as the Earth's moon. Taelgar does not exist
in a solar system per se.  There is only the one planet, and the one moon.
Beyond the moon is the sphere of fixed stars, which do change as Taelgar
rotates.  The Sun is a star, around which Taelgar orbits.

Calendar:

The Taelgarian calendar is very similar to the modern day calendar, with
the following changes:
(Note: This is the human calendar. Halflings use it, but all the other
races have different methods of keeping time, and only use the human
calendar when necessary for clarity)

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September,
October, November, December are the 12 months.

Every 7 days is a week, the days of the week being Monday, Tuesday,
Wednesday, Thursday, Friday,  Saturday, Highday.

The following five feastdays are outside of any month:
Midsummer- between the 30th of June and the 1st of July.
Yule- 3 days between the 30th of December and the 1st of January
[Samhain]- between the 30th of October and the 1st of November

Every 4 years is a leap year, during which a day is added between
Midsummer and the 1st of July.

The years are currently reckoned from the end of the third age. In
Sembara, Sembarin reckoning is still used (years since the founding of
Sembara), and in Dunmar years are reckoned since the return of the King's
Horse.  The 3rd Age ended in 621 Sembarin reckoning, or 1257 since the end
of the Second Age.
The current date is: 153 F.A.,  774 S.R., 79 R.H.
