# October 1997 — emails E012–E016

## E012 — “Taelgar Stuff”

- **From:** Mike Sackton
- **Date:** October 28, 1997, 8:34 PM EST
- **To:** Tim Sackton, Dave Schwartz, Eric Rosenbaum
- **Cc:** Dave Kong

Okay this might get long (I am procrastinating from studying for a latin
test after all):

First a few things that only have some bearing on Taelgar itself:  an mp3
of "Fly" exists on my roommates computer. He has a powerbook, so I am
having slight difficulties getting it onto my computer, but it will happen
soon, and then I can give it to anyone who wants it.  Secondly, I am going
to install linux on my computer, and, with any luck, setup a web server and
a irc server.  This will be a good place for development of taelgar, IMHO.
I can setup a taelgar channel on the irc server for actual conversationally
hashing out ideas, and there will be no quotas on webspace (1M gifs are
fine, in fact, 20 of them are fine).  The only disadvantage to this, is,
until next academic year, Brandeis will not have ethernet, but LocalTalk,
which means bandwidth will be low, and therefore the web site will be slow.
 I never envisioned the website, however, as a place to actually present
Taelgar to the world.  In theory it could become such, and a reasonably
well done website could be helpful in convincing SJG to actually buy the
book. So, although fun, I'm not sure the ideas for the cool aspects of the
webpage (Nick's place, etc) are ever going to be implemented. I am happy to
do such, if ppl want me too, but unless requested I'm not going to. On a
completely random side note, several friends of mine from Brandeis and I
are thinking of getting an apartment near Boston next year instead of
living at home. Any of you guys interested?

### Taelgar itself:

### The rangers:
   As far as the Rangers are concerned, I did develope (develop? what's the
correct spelling?) several ideas about the organization and its purpose,
but those were never actually implemented in the world, because the Rangers
were always looking after some major crisis.  For Tim: I imagine them
something like the B5 Rangers, yet slightly more oriented towards a
paticular country (Sembia) and oriented slightly less toward the truely
unusual.  Actually, I'm not sure how fantastic to make the Rangers, nor how
powerful. My current vision is a moderately powerful organization, that
performs many of the functions of the Tolkien Rangers, with its scope
limited to Sembia and some areas of Rohan.  Probably they would explore as
well as protect, and thus older Rangers would know a lot about areas of the
world, but the organization itself would be limited to Sembia.

### Timeline:
  I think the final form should definetly be early Fourth Age.  Writing
before then would elimanted the thrill of actually writing up what we know
firsthand, and would set the world at a very volitle time, thus forcing
further campaigns from this source material to deviate from the "real"
Taelgar or to plunge into worldwide chaos.  Aftermath can be lots of fun.
I don't have too many ideas about the Fourth age, yet I definatly think
that it should not be comprable to Tolkien's Fourth Age, where the humans
became the power and the other races basically disappeared.  The sense of
the fantastic should be just as strong as ever. Maybe even a renisaunce
(I've never been able to spell that word :))? I think that the four of us
should be, ideally, not longer active, which was pretty much set up at the
end of the campaign. It seems that Kenku, Sembia, Chardon, Rohan would be
the major powers, and possibly a return of a focused Council of Mages or
something.

I'm taking Latin, so I can help with the languages thing if ppl want me
too... as in I can help with things based on Latin.

That's about all I'm going to say for now. I got distracted and lost my
Taelgar train of thought.

Mike Sackton
msackton@cs.brandeis.edu, msackton@iname.com
Brandeis University Class of '01
