## E122 — “(no subject in export)”

- **From:** David S Kong
- **Date:** March 16, 1998, 2:30 PM EST

All,
	All of this Taelgar stuff that's going down is pretty kick-ass-I can't
say much 'cause I've got four friggin' tests this week, but I've got a few
comments.  Firstly, Rai, the big jade statue that he is, is still active in
some capacity, I'd imagine-so, he might have somehow been involved in all that
stuff that happened in the first hundred years of the fourth age, as would
Kyr and Aerin, I would guess. Also, what's Taliesin doing these days, and what
is the status of the council? anyone putting that together? By the way, all
you fruitcakes, it's T-A-L-I-E-S-I-N; spell it out! There should be some sort
of scramble for chaylite control-after all, the rediscovery of chaylite along
with the Dwarven rune magic should entail something. Oh yeah, and all of you
should also know that Rai's got a kid out there somewhere (yeah, that's right,
Rai got some before the age ended and his trouser snake turned into a precious
stone), so that could be something you (Mike) could use. Also, Mike, any plans
on the dragon conflict for this age, if any? I really think that there should
be some more behind the scenes mystical stuff going down-this is a fantasy
campaign, after all. Well, everything seems pretty cool so far-and Mike, as
cool as these new characters may be, you realize, of course, that nothing that
they ever do will be an infintesimal fraction of the coolness of the shit that
we did, and that they shouldn't even try, because if they do, they'll fail
miserably. So there. That's my two cents.

		-Kong
