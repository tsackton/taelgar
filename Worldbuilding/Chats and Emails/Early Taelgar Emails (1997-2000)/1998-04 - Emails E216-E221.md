	You are all exhausted after your journey, and the unexpected results of your plans. The defeat at the hands of the hobgoblin leader still tastes, bitter, and the long session with the base commander you underwent did not lessen your weariness. The fi!
ve of you crawl off to sleep, easily drifting into unconsciousness.

		*			*			*
	You feel a presence enter the room, something hunting for you, it's entire being focused on destroying you. Without thinking you leap to your feet, and lightning bolt flies from your fingers at the present, only to be absorbed, to no effect. If anyth!
ing, the being seems to grow, to enlarge into a terrifying monster, with huge fangs, red glowing eyes, and a neblous body. It comes closer...

&lt;sinis>I pull out my sword, and call out "Halt, Creature of Darkness. Be gone!"
&lt;sinis>I cast Flaming weapon on my sword

	As you beginning to speak you feel the rest of the world getting further and further away, and if you are being drawn into some nebulous dimension with only this being for companionship. Everything goes dim, except for the presence before you, which !
begins to glow with an eerie white-green light, and your sword which burns with a dim red glow. The creature steps forward to you, a twisted smile on its face, showing only pointed fangs, sharp and fearful, and a gleem of desire in its eyes. It hisses!
. "Come with me."

&lt;sinis> I follow it, at a safe distance (~20 ft.), never lowering my guard.  I am
&lt;sinis> watching for any other presences.

It walks through misty corridors and twisted passageways, with every few
feet turning back to make sure you are still following. You catch glimpses
of horrible images down branching corridors--women being raped, men dying
in spurts of blood, a caravan burning its guards screaming as they burn to
a crisp, an ugly beast with huge earthern legs and a mishapen head
tormenting an old man--and soon the being comes to a chasm, in the depths
of which you can see lava flowing, coughing up huge spurts of flame. Your
sword goes out. There is no bridge across the chasm, and the way back
seems misty and unclear. The creature turns to you and holds out a clawed
hand, as if for you too grasp it. It points across the chasm, and hisses,
"Our destination is this way. Take my hand, and I will lead you across the
chasm." Looking at his hand it seems to be reaching for your very soul,
trying to grasp it, imprison it. You feel a chill, and a sense of
foreboding and doom comes over you, as if taking his hand would spell some
incredible doom. Behind you, the way back into the misty corridors is
faint and hard to make out, but still visible. It seems to be shrinking,
cutting off your only way back.

&lt;sinis> I ask him "How do I know I can Trust you"

It's face splits into a wide grin, pointy fangs showing. It mumurs, "Of
course you can trust me, my friend. Your old master sent me." Its eyes
glow bright red in pleasure, and it beckons. "Come with me, friend..." Its
voice is slippery and oozing, but raspy in a strange way. Something like
the cross between a used car salesman, and a computer generated voice.

&lt;sinis> "Who was my Old master?"

"Alconius, of course."

&lt;sinis> "That tells me nothing.  If you are from my former teacher, you would know more than that.  Explain yourself"

It mumurs, "Time is running short. Will you follow me, or nay?" You look
down into the chasm and see that the coughs of flames are more violent and
pronoucning, and soon the enter chasm will be filled with flame. Behind
you the corridors have almost vanished into a white wall of fog. Where do
you go?

&lt;sinis> I cast Resist Fire, and breathe fire... I am ready to cast Body of flames
&lt;sinis> at a seconds notice.  "I accept your offer" I walk over to him.

You walk towards the sickly whitish-green being, and he holds out his
hand. You take his hand, he smiles, and giggles, like a giddy schoolgirl.
He steps forward, leading you above the flames, as the two of you walk
through the air. You feel cold, like some part of you is being encased.
You step onto the other side of the chasm, you hear a quite distinct
click, like a key turning in a lock, or a door closing softly. Then all
goes black, although you remain aware. A vague pain begins to spread
throughout your body, becoming more and more intense, until your only
thought is one of extreme agony. You try to scream, to move, but you are
frozen.

		*			*			*

The agony continues for what seems like forever.  Things start appearing
in the blackness. First, is a pair of eyes, peering in, but they seem to
neither see nor hear you. Second, you see a hand, reaching towards you. As
it gets closer, the pain eases, but a dread spreads over you, as if it is
going to crush your very being into oblivion. You find that be
concentrating on the pain, the hand retreats, but the agony leaves you
unable to do anything else. The hand approaches again. Do you allow it to
come closer?

&lt;sinis> No, i'm waiting to see if anything else happens.

The pain intensifies and you fall into a deeper blackness, seemingly
beyond every seeing light again. You begin to forget what sunlight is
like, and what grass, and wind, and all the other simple pleasures of life
are. An eternity passes. Eventually, something blue and fuzzy begins to
appear on the horizon. It looks like a pond, and it seems like it is
slowly filling with water. A sense of peace seems to be emanating from the
pond. But before you can do anything, a wave of fire destroyes the pond,
and the agony continues. Years pass. Your mind begins to vanish. The pond
returns, but every time it comes near, it is destroyed. You manage to
sneak nearer to the pond, but cannot actually reach it before it is
destroyed. Your life is nothing more than a continual attempt to reach the
pond, only to fail as the fire sweeps through the pond. And yet, you are
making progress. Every time, you get closer to the pond. And finally, you
are within reach of it, when you feel the fire beginning to form. What do
you do?

&lt;sinis> I will cast extinguish fire.

The wave of fire sweeps toward you, a huge wall of searing redness. Your
spell seems to not have slowed it one bit, but neither are you burning to
a crisp. It passes through you, and your senses vanish, overloaded by the
power of the fire. Yet you can sense it, seeking something, attack
someone, not yourself. It circles, and moves in to attack something,
something pleasent, something cool, something . . . life-like.  You feel
the other presence strike out at the fire, as if to attempt to destroy it.
You can feel the fire, reeling in pain. It gathers itself, and prepares .
. . something.

What do you do?

&lt;sinis>what i want to try to do is to create a barrier, or something simaler that holds back the fire

You grab out at the magical engeries you use to create cold, and begin
incanting the ritual words and gestures to realize this hypothetical
cold in the real world. You try to direct the energy to form a barrier
between the fire and the water, but instead of forming a barrier, the
water seems to be drinking of the cold. You feel yourself getting weaker.
You begin to feel pain again, real bodily pain. You hear the bable of
voices echoing in your head, none coherent. You feel faint. The water
drinks more of your enegry. The fire waits. What do you do?

&lt;sinis> I stop casting cold, and try casting create fire, just a small one, as a test.

The pain snaps away, the voices vanish. You are back in the darkness. The
fire flames to life, and swoops down upon the water, which retreats, away.
The fire then turns to you, and speaks: "Thank you, my servant" It
engulfs you, but you feel no pain, no discomfort. The roaring of the
flames is intense, and the redness seeps through everything, making it
difficult to think coherently. Your mind wanders. And then, a booming
voice emanating from the flames speaks: "Now awake!"

	*				*				*

You awaken, screaming in pain. You are lying on a table, next to an
unconscious man wearing blue robes. It is sunny out, and you can hear a
bird chrip once. Then you pass out.
