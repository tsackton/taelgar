## E029 — “Taelgar blurb (warning: bad writing ahead :) )”

- **From:** Mike Sackton (Reply-To; From header absent)
- **Date:** February 24, 1998, 1:59 AM UTC

Taelgar. Home to ragged beggars in the west slums of Chardon, to
finely clad merchants plying their wares between new founded nations,
to ancient wizards who remember the Great War (better name?), to
swift-winged kenku the messenger birdmen, to the nomadic halflings
liked by all, and to cruel and cunning hobgoblins seeking revenge for
their losses. Ravenged by the Great War Against Cha'Mutte, the nations
of the world seek to recover their losses, and regain their former
glory. All the while the evil servants of Cha'Mutte, and those simply
seeking fortune in the reborn world foray into civilized territories,
raiding and pilaging. Armies are weak, for the Great War took many. It
is a time of opportunity, of danger, of great risk and great gain.
Forget rules. Forget dice. Experience Taelgar.

I know that sounded a lot like a bad commerical, but it is vaguely
along the lines of what I want to do. Forgive it, I wrote it at 2am on
very little sleep. Comments much appreciated (and no, I didn't give
Taelgar a bad name by actually showing that to anyone :) )

Mike
