# Early Taelgar emails, 1997–2000

The archive contains 423 messages arranged by their recorded dates. These emails preserve discussions from their time; they do not establish current Taelgar canon.

## At a glance

- **1997:** Gathering material from the original campaign and planning a written setting and website.
- **1998:** Fourth Age drafts, questions about peoples and magic, campaign memories, and play correspondence.
- **1999:** Magic, culture, economy, Sembara, and setting history.
- **2000:** Cosmology, politics, religion, and questions about knowledge and scholarship in Taelgar.
