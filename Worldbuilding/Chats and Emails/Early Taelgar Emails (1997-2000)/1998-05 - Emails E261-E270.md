>2.  Increasing trade ties with the hobgoblin nation would mean that the
>hobgoblins would become increasingly depenadant on external trade for
>their existence.  the hobgoblins would find that they got goods from the
>rest of he wrold that they could get nowhere else.  They couldn't fight a

>of interdependance would arise, the nation would be unable to exist
>without the other cpuntries around it- just as Chardon would be unable to
>exist as itis  without, for example, the islands to the west and Rohan.

would this ever actually happen?  i don't know much about history (like in
the song) but did we do a whole lot of trade with the soviet union during
the cold war? no.  why?  we didn't particularly LIKE them, and they didn't
like us.  who the hell likes hobgoblins, whether there's actually a war on
or not (well, let's see, hobgoblins did kill my grandparents. . .but they
make such great cheap TVs!).  Besides, what would they actually produce?

>Ok, I'm sure you have comments and thoughts on the above, and I may have

>the "bad guys" shouldn't necessarily apply.

good point.

n the other hand, there probably still are a whole bunch of hobgoblin
>tribes wandering around, maybe even some immigrating to the nation and

they probably don't all like the idea of a big centralized civilization,

>I like liontaurs.  I really like them.  Pop them in some deserts or some

speaking of which, don't forget about the lava worms.  I love those.
