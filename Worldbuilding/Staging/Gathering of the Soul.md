# Gathering of the Soul

The Gathering of the Soul is a term used in [[Mos Numena]] funerary theology for the miracle by which the soul of the dead is drawn from the [[Plane of Souls]] into the [[Land of the Dead]]. In Sembaran and Tyrwinghan traditions, this passage is understood as a birthright of the human condition and the counterpart to the [[Gifting of the Soul]] at birth.

The Gathering is not usually treated as something that human prayer or ritual must cause. The rites of mourning, remembrance, and intercession are instead directed toward the soul's dangerous journey through the [[Land of the Dead]] and toward the divine realms beyond. For this reason, Sembaran tradition holds that the Eight can gather a soul even when no body can be recovered.

## Notes

Sources reviewed: [[Sembaran Funerary Traditions]], [[Tyrwinghan Funerary Traditions]], [[Drankorian Funeral Traditions]], [[Land of the Dead]], and [[Cosmology - Open Questions]].

This is best treated as a theological term, not a fully settled mechanical explanation. Drankorian, Sembaran, and Tyrwinghan traditions agree that the soul's transition into the [[Land of the Dead]] is generally automatic or divinely assured, while the metaphysical details of how soulstuff crosses the barrier remain unresolved.
