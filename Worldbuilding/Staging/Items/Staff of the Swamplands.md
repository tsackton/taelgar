# Staff of the Swamplands

The Staff of the Swamplands is a magic staff carried by [[Samso]] and used by the [[Silver Tempests]] for river travel. Its recorded magic summons a portable boat or raft, making it useful for moving a party through swamps and along rivers.

## Silver Tempests

- (DR:: 1748-02-19): After leaving [[Nymthrax|Nymthrax's]] lair, the Silver Tempests reached the [[Barzhen]], summoned a portable boat from the staff, and began traveling downriver. **Source:** [[Great Library Session Notes - Arc 4]].
- (DR:: 1748-09-17): During the investigation into [[Urkabi|Urkabi's]] disappearance, the Silver Tempests traveled from Urkabi down the [[Zurrua]] on Samso's summoned boat and camped along the river before continuing toward [[Castrella]]. **Source:** [[Great Library Session Notes - Arc 5]].
