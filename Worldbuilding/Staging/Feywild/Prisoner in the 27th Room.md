# Prisoner in the 27th Room

The Prisoner in the 27th Room is an unidentified being confined within the central cold-iron chamber of the [[27th House]]. The surrounding structure moves the chamber through space and time to prevent the being from growing through its walls.

Listeners recorded the Prisoner's voice under various names. As the Child, it describes learning words, time, and the world from a teacher. As the Dreamer, it describes mortals as dreaming souls and claims to perceive their desires and possible futures.
