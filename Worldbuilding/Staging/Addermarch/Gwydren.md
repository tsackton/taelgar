# Gwydren
*(GWID-ren)*

Gwydren is a long-lived fey of the [[Thornweald]] who knows its old paths and tends its largest, oldest trees. He was a friend of [[Sweetwillow]] and warned her to leave her grove as corruption spread through the forest.

%%^Campaign:none%%
## DM Notes

A weary woodland keeper, wary of humans, who wants the forest restored. He can be found among the ancient trees across the river. The party heard of him in [[Addermarch - Session 21]] but has not yet met him.
