# Sweetwillow

Sweetwillow was a dryad of the [[Thornweald]], whose sunny grove centered on a large weeping willow. Her friend [[Gwydren]] warned her to flee across the river as corruption spread through the forest, but she refused to leave. She was later killed by a swarm of death butterflies.
