# Thornweald
>[!info]+ Information
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Thornweald is an ancient forest domain of the [[Feywild]], ruled by [[Morlaith]], the Briarheart. It lies congruent with the hills and river valleys of western [[Addermarch]], west of [[Twilight's Grace]] and east of [[Duskmire]].

It is a realm of brambles, old paths, and hidden hollows. Morlaith maintains no court or manor; travelers who keep the old courtesies may pass where the careless are taken, changed, or lost.

%%^Campaign:none%%
## DM Notes

- The forest does not follow ordinary geography. Paths offer relative safety; straying risks becoming lost, sometimes permanently.
- Morlaith's domain has shrunk, leaving forgotten crossings behind. Its former extent remains uncertain.
