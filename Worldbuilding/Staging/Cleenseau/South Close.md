# South Close

The South Close is a comparatively dry, prosperous neighborhood of [[Cleenseau]] between the [[River Gate of Cleenseau|River Gate]], [[Essford Manor]], and the [[North Gate of Cleenseau|North Gate]]. It contains [[The Crossroads Inn]] and [[Smith's Way]].
