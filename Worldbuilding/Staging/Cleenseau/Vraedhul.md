# Vraedhul

Vraedhul, the Burning Shadow, was a dragon born from Cha'mutte's death clutch after the [[Great War]]. She built a lair around a weak point to [[Limbo]], learned to shape its chaotic magic, crafted cursed objects, and spawned [[Zaurmath]] and [[Krauzeth]]. Her lair was destroyed during the high tide of Limbo in DR 1720; whether she died or remains trapped in Limbo is uncertain.
