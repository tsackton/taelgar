# Vraedhul

%% Burning Shadow dragon who shaped Limbo’s chaotic magic %%

Vraedhul, the Burning Shadow, was an immensely powerful dragon drawn to the site of her lair by its connection to [[Limbo]]. She used Limbo's capacity to bend order to infuse objects with extraordinary properties.

Her craft centered on cultivating strange and cruel possibilities in existing things. Precious objects and living people could both serve as raw materials. She developed transformations that fascinated or amused her, treating the possessions and lives of others as material for her creations.

She delighted in crafting cursed objects and sending them into the world. Those afflicted sometimes sought her help, and she would sometimes remove a curse in exchange for a hefty payment of gold and gems.
