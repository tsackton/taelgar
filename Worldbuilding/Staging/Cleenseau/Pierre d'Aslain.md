# Pierre d'Aslain

Pierre d'Aslain is [[Isabeau D'Aslain|Isabeau d'Aslain's]] brother and the husband of [[Cece d'Aslain]]. At [[Damien Montrichard|Damien Montrichard's]] request, he raised a militia of about sixty people and led them north against the undead caves in DR 1720.
