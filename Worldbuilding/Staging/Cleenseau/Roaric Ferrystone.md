# Roaric Ferrystone

Roaric Ferrystone is an elderly, semi-retired blacksmith originally from the [[Ferrystones]] of [[Rinburg]]. He helped establish a smithy in [[Asineau]] and remained there to train [[Samuel Cordwaner]].
