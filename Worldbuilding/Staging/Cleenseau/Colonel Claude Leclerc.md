# Colonel Claude Leclerc

Colonel Claude Leclerc commands the South Watch of the [[Army of the West]]. During the undead rising of DR 1720, he unofficially allowed [[Ida Rosfeld]] to return to [[Cleenseau]] despite the army's confused orders.
