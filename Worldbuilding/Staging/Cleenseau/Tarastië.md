# Tarastië

Tarastië is a small weasel-and-raven gryphon who was formerly bonded to one of [[Ianto|Ianto's]] victims. After helping to bury the dead, she attached herself to [[Celyn]] and later accepted a bond with him in [[Twilight's Grace]].
