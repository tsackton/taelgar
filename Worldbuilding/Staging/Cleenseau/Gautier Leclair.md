# Gautier Leclair

Gautier Leclair is an innkeeper in [[Peydon]]. He maintained a facade of normality while living under the [[Midnight Lady]]'s surveillance.

His inn stood near the river, and [[Violet]] worked there as a serving woman. During the [[Cleenseau - Session 22|investigation of Peydon]] in March DR 1720, the hag appeared in a glass to interrupt the heroes' questions to the innkeeper. The frightened inn staff withdrew into the back when [[Viepuck]] openly taunted her.
