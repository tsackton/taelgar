# Ysabeau Daverre

Ysabeau Daverre is a daughter of [[Emeric Daverre|Émeric Daverre]], lord of [[Peydon]]. During the [[Midnight Lady]]'s control of the town, Émeric feared that his grant of sovereignty over “the lands of his blood” had also placed Ysabeau in the hag's power.

During the [[Cleenseau - Session 23|investigation of the Daverre household]], Ysabeau sought a private way to speak with the heroes and was upset when she learned the specifics of her father's bargain.
