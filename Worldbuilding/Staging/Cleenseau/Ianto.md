# Ianto

Ianto the Red was a redcap who ruled the [[Fortress of Ianto the Red]], enslaved goblins, and collected body parts, armor, and other trophies from his victims. The party killed him and recovered [[Greymalkin|Greymalkin's]] wings from his vault.
