# Snikket

Snikket Half-Tooth is a goblin formerly held at the [[Fortress of Ianto the Red]]. Transformed into a goat, he accompanied the party through the [[Grey Mists]] to [[Twilight's Grace]], where [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]] granted him freedom to choose his shape within her realm.
