# Roland Vaissier

Roland Vaissier is the captain of [[Emeric Daverre|Lord Émeric Daverre's]] household guard in [[Peydon]]. He resumed command after the death of the [[Midnight Lady]].

During the [[Cleenseau - Session 24|liberation of Peydon]] in March DR 1720, the [[Heroes of Cleenseau]] roused him from a drunken stupor and helped him return to duty. He accompanied them to arrest [[Edouard of Peydon]], the ferryman who had assisted the hag, and later received the surviving fugitives recaptured by the heroes.
