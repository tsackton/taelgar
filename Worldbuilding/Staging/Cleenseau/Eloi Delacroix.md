# Eloi Delacroix

Eloi Delacroix is a charismatic and well-liked miller in [[Peydon]] who runs the mill with his more distrustful brother, [[Marc Delacroix]]. He joined the celebration after the Midnight Lady's defeat and thanked the party with a loaf of miller's bread.
