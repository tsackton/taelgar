# Rose Stonebridge

Rose Stonebridge was a leader of resistance to hobgoblin occupation during the [[Third Hobgoblin War (Sembara)]]. She formerly carried [[Silverthorn]], whose recovered rapier and sheath later passed to [[Celyn]].
