# Émeric Daverre

Émeric Daverre is the aging lord of [[Peydon]] and the father of [[Ysabeau|Ysabeau Daverre]]. During the undead rising he surrendered his youth to the [[Midnight Lady]] in exchange for protection for the village and his family, leaving his hair white and his authority weakened until the [[Heroes of Cleenseau]] broke her control.

His bargain granted the hag sovereignty over “the lands of his blood,” wording he feared also placed Ysabeau in her power. The hag drained his vitality through a poppet in her dollhouse, leaving him exhausted and ill. He disclosed the bargain to [[Viepuck]] through a private telepathic conversation during the [[Cleenseau - Session 23|investigation of the Daverre household]].

After the hag's defeat in March DR 1720, Émeric smashed the glass through which she had watched his household, joining [[Cleenseau - Session 24|Peydon's celebration of its liberation]].
