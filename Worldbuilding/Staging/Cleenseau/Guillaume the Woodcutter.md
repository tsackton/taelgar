# Guillaume

Guillaume is a woodcutter in [[Peydon]]. Despite his fear of the [[Midnight Lady]], he gave the party guarded information about her movements and lair.

When the [[Heroes of Cleenseau]] [[Cleenseau - Session 22|visited him in March DR 1720]], he was making charcoal in a workshop with shuttered windows and no glass. He bore recent scars and flinched at direct mention of the hag. Speaking of her as an “old dog,” he indicated that she had injured him and warned that her wounds healed as soon as they were inflicted. He urged the heroes to leave before she spotted them.
