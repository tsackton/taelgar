# Lyra Corvin

Lyra Corvin is a royal inquisitor and a powerful abjuration and divination wizard who specializes in exposing falsehoods and hostile magic. Acting for [[Elaine II]], she confirmed [[Malach|Malach's]] defeat, took [[Isabeau D'Aslain]] into custody, and offered Robin stewardship of [[Asineau]].
