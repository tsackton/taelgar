# Edmund Hartwell

Edmund Hartwell commanded the Enst Regiment of the [[Army of the West]]. On January 8, 1720, he ordered its detached garrisons to march to [[Dunmore]] under General [[Ilsa Lemoine|Ilsa Lemoine's]] orders.
