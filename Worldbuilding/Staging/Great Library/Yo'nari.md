# The Yo'nari
*(yo-NAH-ree)*


The Yo'nari were an ancient proto-[[Deno'qai]] people and among the first humans to settle the northwest coast and interior mountains of [[Apporia]]. Before the [[Downfall Wars]], most of the people vanished into [[Cairn Dor]] with their queen, [[Yeshara]], leaving ruined settlements and standing stones behind on the [[Material Plane]].

## Early history

The Yo'nari belonged to the early Northros-speaking migrations from which the later Deno'qai descended. They maintained frequent contact with the fey and called the [[Feywild]] *Nazil-Dimu*. Their surviving sites used monumental stonework and wolf imagery, including parallel rows of wolf-headed plinths, a central longhouse, and the trilithic [[Cleaver-Stone]].

Yeshara's growing fear of dreams divided the Yo'nari. Some fled as she outlawed sleep rites and imposed prolonged vigils, while those who remained participated in a midwinter rite intended to sever their connection to sleep and the [[Plane of Souls|Plane of Consciousness]]. The rite instead created Cairn Dor and carried Yeshara and her followers into the new realm.

## Cairn Dor

The native population of Cairn Dor is the continuation of the Yo'nari people, transformed by long residence in the [[Shadowfolds]] and by Yeshara's doctrine of eternal waking. The ordinary inhabitants were called the *Nurim-Dor*, or People of Eternal Waking, while the [[Shemra Azem]], or Guardians of the Vigil, formed a distinct military order sustained by captives held as dreamers. The distinction is important: the Yo'nari were the people of the realm, not simply another name for Yeshara's army.

Over the following millennia, the Shemra Azem repeatedly raided Apporia for dreamers and periodically emerged in open war. These conflicts included the [[Apporian Shadow War]], the [[War of the Severed Dreams]], and the [[War of the Dark Rift]]. Long intervals between them reflect the time needed to gather new dreamers and rebuild the army after each defeat.

The largest surviving Yo'nari settlement in Cairn Dor was [[Eshlem]], with smaller villages and scattered agricultural homesteads around it. Their culture retained wolf-marked boundaries and the old fear of fey influence, but life under Yeshara had become organized around avoiding sleep and honoring the sleepless soldiers who supposedly protected the population.

## Remains

In DR 1748, the [[Silver Tempests]] found the ruins of a Yo'nari settlement in a ravine beyond the old Drankorian fort guarding the Cleaver-Stone. The site contained a longhouse, rows of wolf-headed plinths, and murals showing Yeshara leading her people beneath a trilith as part of the rite that created Cairn Dor.

Later that year, the company entered Cairn Dor and encountered the surviving Yo'nari population. When Yeshara was defeated, the inhabitants of the realm fell asleep. They remained asleep when Cairn Dor emerged from stasis in DR 1752, and their later fate is unknown.
