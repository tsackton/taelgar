# Evensong Spire

Evensong Spire is a fortress of immense towers intertwined with the materialized colors of the [[Sunset Gate]] at the western edge of [[Twilight's Grace]]. [[Sir Caldris|Sir Caldris, the Watcher of the Evening Song]], keeps watch there.
