# Khevaris Stoneweaver

Khevaris Stoneweaver was a geomancer who studied planar access to the [[Elemental Plane of Earth]]. His writings describe passages from the [[Ethereal Plane]] to the Plane of Earth as gray curtains that look like shimmering stone.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- Khevaris is described as a great geomancer.
- He studied planar access to the Elemental Plane of Earth extensively.
- According to his writings, the most direct way to reach the Plane of Earth is through the [[Ethereal Plane]].
- His writings describe passages to the Plane of Earth as gray curtains that look like shimmering stone.
%%
