# Saria

Saria was a conjurer of [[Cymea]] whose letters describe time passing strangely in the [[Feywild]]. She wrote of studying there for what seemed like a year, only to return before sunrise on the same night she had left.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Feywild]].
- Saria is described as a conjurer of [[Cymea]].
- Her letters speak of a year of study in the Feywild.
- The year in the Feywild seemed to last only an evening in the Material Plane.
- When she returned, the sun had not yet risen on the night of her departure.
%%
