# Bella Stormwind

Bella Stormwind was a halfling sailor of the [[Green Sea]] in the years before the [[Great War]]. She was known for a tale in which a typhoon carried her ship into a strange tropical sea and singing dolphins guided it back.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- Bella is described as a halfling sailor.
- She traveled the [[Green Sea]] in the years before the [[Great War]].
- She was known for a tale of being swept into a typhoon.
- In the tale, her ship emerged into a strange tropical sea with clear blue water and no land in sight.
- She sailed for weeks, following dolphins who sang in a strange tongue.
- The dolphins guided her ship into a storm that returned her to the Green Sea, miles from where she vanished.
