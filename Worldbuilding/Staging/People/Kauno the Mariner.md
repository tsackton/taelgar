# Kauno the Mariner

Kauno the Mariner was a legendary sailor in the myths of the Skaer. One tale says he followed the song of an ancient whale into a storm and found himself on a dark, still sea before eventually returning to the Green Sea.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- Kauno is described as a legendary sailor in the myths of the Skaer.
- One tale says he sailed single-handedly into a violent storm.
- In the tale, he followed the song of an ancient whale.
- In the heart of the storm, he found himself on a dead calm sea that was dark, cold, and smooth.
- The tale says a wind filled his sails without touching the water.
- He sailed for months following the whale and listening to its songs before returning to the Green Sea.
