# Elenwen

Elenwen was an elven poet whose writing is cited in discussions of the [[Feywild]] and its uncertain geography. She described the Feywild as a place shaped by memory and dream rather than ordinary law.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Feywild]].
- Elenwen is described as an elven poet.
- Her writing is quoted in a discussion of the Feywild's shifting geography.
- The quoted idea is that the Feywild shapes itself to memory and dream rather than to logic or law.
- The same passage connects her words to speculation that Feywild geography may reflect what once was, or what will be, on the [[Material Plane]].
%%
