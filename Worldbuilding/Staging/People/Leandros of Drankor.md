# Leandros of Drankor

Leandros of Drankor was a Drankorian natural historian whose catalog [[On the Mysteries of the Infinite Depths]] describes leviathans and other deep creatures of the [[Elemental Plane of Water]].

His work is a major source for later accounts of immense creatures in the Plane of Water, including [[Gazankoa]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[On the Mysteries of the Infinite Depths]].
- Leandros wrote [[On the Mysteries of the Infinite Depths]], a Drankorian natural history treatise from the Drankorian Era.
- The work is a catalog of leviathans and other deep creatures of the [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- It describes colossal squids the size of cities, endless serpents, shadowy unidentified forms, and [[Gazankoa]].
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- Later accounts cite Leandros's catalog for the Plane of Water's deep creatures.
- Leandros described Gazankoa as a being of unending hunger, with tentacles stretching for miles and a presence that seems to warp water.
