# Avariel

Avariel was an elven philosopher associated with the multidimensional web model of the [[Plane of Magic]]. She coined the term [[Ley Lines]] for the strands of magical connection by which arcane power reaches the [[Material Plane]].

Avariel argued that the Plane of Magic exists in a higher dimension than other planes and contacts all other planes in ways that ordinary planar geometry cannot fully model.
