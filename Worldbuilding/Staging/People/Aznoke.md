# Aznoke

Aznoke was a lizardfolk mystic and the author of [[The Endless Currents]], an epic poem about the Eightfold Essences. The poem describes physical, fundamental, and cyclical energies, and speaks of life and death as flowing currents within a larger natural cycle.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[The Endless Currents]].
- Aznoke wrote [[The Endless Currents]], a lizardfolk epic poem.
- Aznoke is described as a lizardfolk mystic.
- The poem describes the Eightfold Essences.
- The poem presents life and death as flowing currents within the natural cycle.
- Source: [[Energy Realms]].
- The name also appears as Aznoké.
- Aznoke describes the Eightfold Essences as physical energies, fundamental energies, and cyclical energies.
- The physical energies are Fire, Air, Earth, and Water.
- The fundamental energies are Magic and Spirit.
- The cyclical energies are Life and Death.
- Aznoke speaks of the Plane of Blossoming Currents as the source of vitality.
- Aznoke speaks of the Plane of Still Waters as a realm where life slows, death cleanses, and renewal is prepared.
- Source: [[Positive Energy Plane]].
- The Positive Energy Plane is identified with Aznoke's Plane of Blossoming Currents.
- Source: [[Negative Energy Plane]].
- The Negative Energy Plane is identified with Aznoke's Plane of Still Waters.
- Source note issue: [[Energy Realms]] uses "she" for Aznoke, while [[Positive Energy Plane]] uses "he"; no gender is set here because the sources conflict.
