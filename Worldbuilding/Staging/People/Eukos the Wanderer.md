# Eukos the Wanderer

Eukos the Wanderer was a Chardonian bard from the early DR 1500s. His travel writings described the lands north of Lake Valandros before the [[Great War]], including now-destroyed settlements and civilizations.

Eukos also recorded material from the [[Deno'qai]], including one of the few known versions of [[The Tales of Ma'qaar]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Research from Kassi]].
- Eukos was a Chardonian bard from the early DR 1500s.
- He belonged to the last generation before the [[Great War]].
- He wrote a travelogue about the land north of Lake Valandros.
- The travelogue is one of the best descriptions of several now-destroyed settlements and civilizations.
- Those places include [[Volganis]], [[Amani]], the [[Ausberg Empire]], and the [[Republic of Varjatta]].
- Eukos spent time among the Deno'qai tribes.
- He traveled north along the mountains as far as the [[Blackwater Fens]] and beyond, but stopped short of [[Pandar]].
- He described Deno'qai tribes as living in hidden villages in deep forests along the mountains.
- Source: [[The Lore of the Feywild]].
- Ulfgar Frostbeard used Eukos's unpublished notes in [[The Lore of the Feywild]].
- Those notes preserved one of the only recorded versions of [[The Tales of Ma'qaar]].
- Source: [[The Tales of Ma'qaar]].
- Existing timing notes place Eukos in the early DR 1500s.
