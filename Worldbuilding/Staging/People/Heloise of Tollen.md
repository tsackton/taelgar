# Heloise of Tollen

Heloise of Tollen was a theological cosmologist primarily associated with the study of the [[Plane of Creation]]. Her calculations suggested that the Plane of Creation may be an infinitely dense mass of [[Soulstuff]], impenetrable to anything except the [[Divine Presence]].

Heloise also popularized a folk theory that [[Arborea]] arises from the fundamental desire of creation to live.
