# Emmeline of Fellburn

Emmeline of Fellburn was a priest whose account of returning from death is cited in descriptions of the [[Land of the Dead]]. Her account describes a twilight shore, sinking sand, a current carrying souls, and a force pulling her back.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Land of the Dead]].
- Emmeline is described as a priest.
- She wrote about her experience returning from the dead.
- Her account describes walking across a sandy shore under a dark sky lit by twilight from below.
- Her account describes sinking into finer and finer sand until she was carried by a current.
- Her account describes other souls drifting nearby but not close enough to reach.
- Her account describes a brighter light ahead and a stronger current before she was pulled back.
