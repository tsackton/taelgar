# Calenya

Calenya of [[Orenlas]] was an elven poet who wrote frequently about the marvels of the [[Feywild]]. A nearly complete translation of Calenya's works is preserved in [[The Lore of the Feywild]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[The Lore of the Feywild]].
- Calenya is described as an elven poet of [[Orenlas]].
- Calenya wrote frequently on the marvels of the [[Feywild]].
- [[The Lore of the Feywild]] contains a nearly complete translation of Calenya's works.
- Source: [[Feywild]].
- Calenya described the physical structure of sunset made solid on the western edge of the Green Sea.
