# Breda Mistweaver

Breda Mistweaver was a cloud giant mystic whose legends of the [[Feywild]] were preserved in [[The Lore of the Feywild]]. Those legends were spoken to an unnamed scholar from [[Amani]] and were thought lost forever during the [[Great War]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[The Lore of the Feywild]].
- Breda is described as a cloud giant mystic.
- Breda told legends of the Feywild to an unnamed scholar from [[Amani]].
- [[The Lore of the Feywild]] preserves the only complete copy of those legends.
- The legends were thought lost forever during the [[Great War]].
%%
