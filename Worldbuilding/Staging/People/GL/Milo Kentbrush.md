Milo Kentbrush was a human adventurer whose ghost lingered in flooded caverns near [[Suwi]]. He had sought to kill the aboleth [[Ithu’rax]], but was killed before reaching it.

Milo's story is brief but stark: an overconfident would-be aboleth-slayer reduced to a sobbing ghost, still tied to the weapon he had brought for the deed. His body and ghost marked the point where the rescue of [[Dinia]] stopped being only a pursuit of kidnappers and became a descent into something older and stranger under the lake.

The [[Silver Tempests]] recovered his arrow of aberration slaying from the caverns, and Milo followed them, bound to the arrow as they continued toward Ithu'rax's lair.

## Campaign Interactions

- (DR:: 1748-08-24): The Silver Tempests found Milo's body and ghost while exploring the flooded caverns near Suwi.
- (DR:: 1748-08-24): Milo's ghost made clear that he had meant to kill Ithu'rax, but had been ambushed by slime servants before reaching the aboleth.
- (DR:: 1748-08-24): The party recovered Milo's arrow of aberration slaying, and Milo followed them while tied to the arrow.
