# Moondweller
>[!info]+ Biographical Info
> A [[Stoneborn|stoneborn]] (he/him)
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Moondweller is a [[Stoneborn]] guide associated with a glacier village near [[Vangebekkr]] in the high [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]]. He is remembered as a solitary figure meditating in the Stoneborn village while unnatural cold spread through the dwarven southlands.

Moondweller's importance was practical rather than political: he understood the glacier, the frost giants, and the corruption moving through the ice. To the [[Silver Tempests]], he named the crisis plainly. The frost giants had once been trading partners, but some had fallen under the influence of [[Morgana Stoneclaw|Morgana Frostclaw]], who had taken control of the ice magic buried in the glacier. The way forward was to kill the hag, or at least free the ice magic from her control.

He belongs to the strange high-mountain world between the dwarven cities below and the frost giant stronghold above: remote Stoneborn villages, glacier paths, brutal cold, and old arrangements between peoples who survived in places most travelers could not cross.

## Campaign Interactions

- (DR:: 1747-12-14): The Silver Tempests reached a Stoneborn village after crossing the cursed glacier and found Moondweller meditating there.
- (DR:: 1747-12-15): Moondweller told the party that the frost giants had once been valued trading partners, that some had turned to evil, and that Morgana Frostclaw controlled the ice magic at the heart of the glacier.
- (DR:: 1747-12-15): Moondweller's guidance pointed the party toward [[Vangebekkr]], the frost giant stronghold at the center of the crisis.
