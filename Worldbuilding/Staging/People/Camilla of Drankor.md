# Camilla of Drankor

Camilla of Drankor was an ancient scholar of fey cosmology. Her writings preserve fey accounts of the [[Dreamworld]], the [[Plane of Souls]], the [[Shadowfolds]], and the rumored [[Nightmare Realm]].

According to Camilla, fey traditions treat the Plane of Souls as a pale echo of the Dreamworld rather than as the central spiritual plane assumed by many theologians.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Plane of Souls]].
- Camilla is described as a theologian.
- She is described as the most prominent ancient scholar of fey cosmology.
- According to Camilla, the fey believe the [[Plane of Souls]] is a pale, distant echo of the [[Dreamworld]].
- Source: [[Dreamworld]].
- Camilla's writings describe the Dreamworld as a plane of vibrant wildness where those of strong mind can shape the dreams of others.
- Source: [[Shadowfolds]].
- Camilla proposed that each Shadowfold domain is best understood as its own demiplane.
- She described Shadowfold domains as colored and shaped by the particular evils of the dread lord who made them.
- Source: [[Nightmare Realm]].
- Camilla wrote of dark extraplanar horrors that even the fey refused to name.
- She presumed those horrors must come from some nightmare place.
