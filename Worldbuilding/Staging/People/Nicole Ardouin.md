# Nicole Ardouin

Nicole Ardouin was a Tollish cosmological and theological writer of the DR 1600s. Her work focused on difficult or marginal planes, including the [[Far Realms]], the [[Nightmare Realm]], and [[Pandemonium]].

Nicole argued that good and evil are poor measures for understanding alien minds, and that some Far Realms might contain beings capable of bringing new knowledge to the world if communication with them were possible.
