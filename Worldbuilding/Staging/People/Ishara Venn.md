# Ishara Venn

Ishara Venn was a planar geographer best known for [[The Seams Between Worlds]], a systematic study of [[Elemental Vortex|elemental vortexes]] and other planar connections. Her work treats elemental vortexes as temporary points of contact created when a nearly pure expression of an elemental energy draws an Energy Realm close to the [[Material Plane]].

Later scholars often treated Ishara's theory as controversial or fringe, but her work remained an important point of reference for later studies of planar connections.

- Source: [[The Seams Between Worlds]].
- Ishara wrote [[The Seams Between Worlds]], a cosmological treatise on planar connections.
- The book note describes [[The Seams Between Worlds]] as a Drankorian cosmological treatise from the Drankorian Era.
- Ishara defines elemental vortexes as temporary loci of connection between the [[Material Plane]] and an [[Energy Realms|Energy Realm]].
- Her definition depends on the presence of a nearly pure representation of one of the Five Fundamental Energies.
- Source: [[Elemental Vortex]].
- Few planar geographers find Ishara's theories convincing.
- Elemental vortexes are considered a fringe idea among the [[Faculty of Metaphysics]].
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- Ishara is described there as a Tollish scholar writing from the [[University of Tollen]].
- She spent decades collecting tales and lore of portals to the [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- She was especially interested in theories of permanent portals in the extreme depths of the sea.
- She argued that a deep canyon in the [[Western Gulf]] might show signs of latent portals.
- She argued that stories of elemental vortexes at the heart of storms were too robust and consistent to dismiss.
- Source: [[Western Gulf]].
- Ishara claimed that connections to the [[Elemental Plane of Water]] could be found in deep canyons of the [[Western Gulf]].
- Source: [[Great Chasm]].
- Ishara claimed that the [[Great Chasm]] likely had no bottom and attracted elemental vortexes.
- Source: [[Research about Planar Connections]].
- The campaign research note contrasts Ishara's elemental vortexes with Emyr of Tafolwern's broader planar upwelling theory.
