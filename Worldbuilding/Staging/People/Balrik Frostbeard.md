# Balrik Frostbeard

Balrik Frostbeard was a planar geographer whose reports concern travel in the [[Elemental Plane of Earth]]. The reports attributed to him describe tremors and quakes that constantly reshape the plane's passages.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- Balrik is described as a planar geographer.
- Reliable reports collected by Balrik say that tremors and quakes constantly reshape the passages of the Elemental Plane of Earth.
- These shifts can create new vistas, collapse routes, reveal buried treasures, or trap travelers in darkness.
