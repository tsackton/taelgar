# Lyrannis of Aquilona

Lyrannis of Aquilona was a cosmological philosopher and natural historian best known for [[On Elemental Forms]]. Her work cataloged accounts of elemental beings, especially genies, and argued that creatures with a pure elemental essence are overwhelmingly associated with Fire, Water, Earth, or Air.

Her conclusions are frequently cited in debates over the number and nature of the [[Energy Realms]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[On Elemental Forms]].
- Lyrannis wrote [[On Elemental Forms]], a natural history treatise from the Drankorian Era.
- The work is described as seminal.
- Lyrannis was a cosmological philosopher.
- She cataloged folklore, myth, and observation concerning elemental beings.
- She paid particular attention to genies.
- She argued that creatures with pure elemental essence are overwhelmingly associated with Fire, Water, Earth, or Air.
- Source: [[Energy Realms]].
- Her work is a major argument for the traditional model of four elemental Energy Realms, or five if the [[Plane of Magic]] is included.
- She dismissed the existence of an [[Elemental Plane of Metal]] because of the lack of evidence for pure metal elementals.
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Metal]].
- Her dismissal of a plane of metal is cited in debates over that plane's existence.
- Source: [[Frostfell]].
- Lyrannis did not catalog powerful genies of ice or cold native to Frostfell.
- She discussed creatures of ice and snow that may dwell in Frostfell.
- She argued that Frostfell should not be considered a true elemental realm because it has no true native creatures.
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- Lyrannis recorded descriptions of earth creatures with boulder-like or strange forms, including xorn.
- She dismissed claims about mountain-sized creatures on a surface of the Plane of Earth as unsupported.
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].
- Lyrannis cataloged many denizens of the Elemental Plane of Fire, including the [[Efreeti]].
