# Cavarrio

Cavarrio was a Chardonian scholar who wrote [[Chronicles of Drankor]] in DR 1209. The work is considered a major history of the [[Drankorian Empire]] and a standard source on Drankorian chronology.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Chronicles of Drankor]].
- Cavarrio wrote [[Chronicles of Drankor]] in DR 1209.
- He is described as a Chardonian scholar.
- [[Chronicles of Drankor]] is an extensive history and chronicle of the [[Drankorian Empire]].
- It is typically considered the most comprehensive source on Drankorian chronology by the [[Faculty of History]].
- It is a standard part of the Faculty of History curriculum.
- Cavarrio divides Drankorian history into five distinct eras.
- Source: [[Vision of Govir's research]].
- Govir's colleagues suggested Cavarrio's Chronicles as a source on Drankorian history.
- Source: [[Vision of Ulfgar's research]].
- Ulfgar copied an annal of emperors from Cavarrio's Chronicles.
