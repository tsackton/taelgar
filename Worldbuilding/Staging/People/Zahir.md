# Zahir

Zahir was a sailor from [[Sulqat]] who became famous for surviving the destruction of his ship in a gale off the [[Mawakel Peninsula]]. He claimed to have spent seven years in service to a marid before returning.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Water]].
- Zahir is described as a sailor from [[Sulqat]].
- His ship was destroyed in a gale off the coast of the [[Mawakel Peninsula]].
- He became famous for surviving the event.
- He claimed he awoke on the strange shore of a shimmering island of brilliant white sand.
- He claimed the island floated in a mysterious sea.
- He claimed he returned after spending seven years in service to a marid.
