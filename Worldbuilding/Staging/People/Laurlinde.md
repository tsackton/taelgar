# Laurlinde

Laurlinde was a bard remembered in the elven epic [[Light of the Everglade]]. The poem says her voice and bearing won her a boon from the archfey known as the [[Cloudspinner|Queen of Sunset]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Light of the Everglade]].
- Laurlinde is a bard in the elven epic [[Light of the Everglade]].
- Her silver voice and noble demeanor won her a boon from the [[Cloudspinner|Queen of Sunset]].
- Source: [[Feywild]].
- Laurlinde is cited as an example of a tale in which a mortal's interaction with the fey is not merely cautionary.
