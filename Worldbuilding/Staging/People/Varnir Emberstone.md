# Varnir Emberstone

Varnir Emberstone was a dwarven loremaster and historian of [[Sentinel Range|Labkhan]]. In the preface to her history of Labkhan, she claimed to have learned impossible facts from the dao of the [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Elemental Plane of Earth]].
- Varnir is described as a dwarven loremaster.
- She wrote a detailed history of [[Sentinel Range|Labkhan]].
- In the preface to that history, she claimed to have learned impossible facts from the dao.
- The same source describes dao as inhabitants of the [[Elemental Plane of Earth]] with vast memories.
