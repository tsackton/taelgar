# Thardrum Deepseeker

Thardrum Deepseeker was an ancient dwarven scholar who wrote about the [[Far Realms]]. He claimed that not all Far Realms need be home to dangerous and evil creatures.

History does not record Thardrum's fate, but his work was rediscovered in [[Isingue]] before the [[Great War]].

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Far Realms]].
- Thardrum is described as an ancient dwarven scholar.
- He claimed that not all Far Realms need be home to dangerous and evil creatures.
- History does not record his fate.
- His work was rediscovered in [[Isingue]] before the [[Great War]].
- Nicole Ardouin later made a related argument that good and evil are poor measures for understanding alien realms.
