# Gaius Devarro

Gaius Devarro was a Chardonian metaphysical cosmologist and early scholar of the [[Faculty of Metaphysics]] in [[Chardon]]. He lived during the waning days of the [[Drankorian Empire]] and helped shape the later [[Standard Multiversal Model]].

Gaius wrote on [[Limbo]], [[Ley Lines]], [[Echo Realms]], and the broader structure of the [[Multiverse]]. His works include [[On Minds and Chaos]] and [[An Exploration of Magical Connections Between the Planes]].
