# Julius Ceptuso

Julius Ceptuso popularized a traditional model of the [[Energy Realms]] based on five fundamental energies: Earth, Air, Fire, Water, and Magic. In that model, each fundamental energy is associated with an Energy Realm.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Energy Realms]].
- Julius Ceptuso popularized a traditional model of the Energy Realms.
- The model postulates five fundamental energies of matter: Earth, Air, Fire, Water, and Magic.
- In the model, each of those energies is associated with an Energy Realm.
- Modern metaphysical cosmologists usually treat Magic as a fundamental multiversal force rather than as an Energy Realm.
- The five-energy model still influences the popular conception of the Energy Realms.
