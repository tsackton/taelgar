# Theophesus

Theophesus was a Drankorian scholar whose work helped define the concept of [[Echo Realms]]. He collected some of the first maps of the [[Feywild]] and proposed that the Feywild has a physical correspondence with the [[Material Plane]].

In later writing, Theophesus applied a similar idea to the [[Dreamworld]] and the [[Plane of Souls]], treating the Dreamworld as a metaphysical echo.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Echo Realms]].
- Theophesus is described as a Drankorian scholar.
- He historically developed the concept of Echo Realms.
- He collected some of the first maps of the [[Feywild]].
- He proposed that a mountainous region in the [[Material Plane]] would likely be echoed by mountains, or something like mountains, in the [[Feywild]].
- In later writing, he linked the [[Dreamworld]] and the [[Plane of Souls]] in a similar way.
- He considered the Dreamworld to be a metaphysical echo, analogous to the physical echo of the Feywild.
