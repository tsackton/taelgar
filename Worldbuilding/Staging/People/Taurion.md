# Taurion

Taurion is associated with [[Taurion's Manual of Golems]], a Drankorian magic book found in the [[Tower of the Colossus]]. The tower also held a fragile literary collection identified in campaign notes as Taurion's.

%% Known facts
- Source: [[Taurion's Manual of Golems]].
- [[Taurion's Manual of Golems]] is also named Taurion's Manual of Stone Golems.
- The manual is a Drankorian magic book.
- The manual was found in the [[Tower of the Colossus]].
- DM-only source notes describe the manual as both instructions for constructing a stone golem and Taurion's research notes on golems.
- DM-only source notes describe Taurion as an elven artificer active in Drankor from the mid-500s.
- DM-only source notes say Taurion died in battle defending [[Rostaure|Rostaurë]].
- Source: [[Tower of the Colossus]].
- DM-only source notes say Taurion lived in the Tower of the Colossus.
- DM-only source notes say Taurion was killed in the wars that led to the destruction of [[Rostaure|Rostaurë]].
- Source: [[Tower of the Colossus Treasure]].
- The treasure note identifies a fragile scroll collection in the tower as Taurion's literary collection.
- DM-only source notes say the collection was mostly Drankorian and Elvish poetry, plays, and novellas.
- DM-only source notes say Taurion was from the 28th ka and was born around DR 187.
- DM-only source notes say Taurion did not settle in the tower until around DR 500.
