# Iagharaz
*(aya-gar-az)*

Iagharaz was a white dragon whose lair near the headwaters of the [[Sulqat]] became the center of the [[Mawar Confederacy]]'s Endless Winter in DR 1708-1709. By the winter of DR 1708, the river had frozen for much of its length, damaging the salmon runs and threatening the inland settlements that depended on them.

## History

- (DR:: 1708): Iagharaz and three sorcerers established a lair in the pine forests near the headwaters of the [[Sulqat]]. By fall, the river had begun to freeze unnaturally.
- (DR:: 1709): The [[Sulqat]] failed to thaw in spring, and the first armed expedition sent upriver vanished. By midsummer, people along the river were starving or fleeing toward the coast.
- (DR:: 1709): A second Mawar expedition, joined by [[Wazir]] after reaching [[Aibard]], encountered Chardonian heroes who were hunting an ensorcelled dragon. The combined force defeated Iagharaz and ended the curse on the river.

The defeat of Iagharaz shaped Wazir's life as much as it changed the river valley. Wazir played only a minor role in the battle, but the Chardonian heroes' victory, and especially the attention of Galena, helped draw him away from Mawar and toward [[Chardon]] and the [[Great Library]].

## Open Questions / DM Notes

Sources reviewed: [[Wazir]], [[Worldbuilding/Chats and Emails/Emails/Email - Timeline]], and [[Worldbuilding/Chats and Emails/Emails/Email - Wazir Backstory II]].

The original staging note gave the name and pronunciation "Iagharaz (aya-gar-az)" and described Iagharaz as "a white dragon that laired near the source of the Sulqat in 1709, with some sorcerers. Hunted by Chardonian adventurers, and killed."

The timeline email says three sorcerers of unknown age or race established the lair with a huge white dragon in the summer of DR 1708. Wazir's backstory describes the creature as an "ensorcelled ice dragon" and makes the three Chardonian heroes central to the victory. The exact nature of the enchantment, the sorcerers' goals or experiments, and the identity of the Chardonian heroes other than Galena remain underdeveloped.

Wazir's PC note also includes a comment that the Endless Winter event may not ultimately be caused by an ensorcelled dragon. This note preserves the current staging premise but should be reviewed if that backstory is finalized differently.
