# Gifting of the Soul

The Gifting of the Soul is a term used in [[Mos Numena]] theology for the divine miracle by which a living being receives a soul at birth. Sembaran and Tyrwinghan traditions describe it as the counterpart to the [[Gathering of the Soul]], the miracle by which the soul of the dead is drawn into the [[Land of the Dead]].

Mos Numenan writers often describe the Gifting through river and rain imagery: the soul's journey after death flows back through the [[Land of the Dead|River of Souls]], while the gift of a soul at birth is imagined as divine rain falling into the world. In broader metaphysical writing, the idea overlaps with the claim that some sapient beings receive [[Soulstuff]] from beyond the [[Land of the Dead]] when they are born.
