# Rohan War Speech — Early Draft

> I have long dwelt in the depths of despair, long suffered in the clutches of madness whilst my kingdom fell to ruin. No longer. I am with you again. So now, people of Rohan, warriors courageous and bold, listen to me speak. For it is our most desperate hour, and we must fight.
>
> Our sorrows are great. Ralthir, my horse, greatest and last of the da-hirrim, is dead. Our people die by the thousands to the hobgoblins in the North and the red death in the East. Our crops go unplanted and untended. Our wives and children fall victim to famine, disease, and raiding parties. We must not let this continue. We will go to war.
>
> Our country yet has greatness in it. We will field the finest army this land has ever seen. From every down, every vale and mark, the spears will shine. The spears will shine with the morning sun, with hope, with victory. We will not fall to chaos or despair. We will not give in. We will fight! And we will win.
>
> So now Rohirrim, to horse! Up spears! We muster now, and ride on the morrow!
