# Original Taelgar Documents

Early Taelgar worldbuilding documents preserved as readable Markdown. These are historical development materials and do not establish current canon.

## Documents preserved here

- [[Guide to Taelgar - Early Player Guide]] — The fullest surviving early general guide for players, covering the setting, history, peoples, religion, magic, and geography.
- [[Guide to Taelgar - Cosmology, Magic, Races, and Religion]] — The most complete focused guide to early cosmology, magic, peoples, and religious ideas.
- [[Guide to Taelgar - History, Culture, and Geography]] — The most complete focused guide to early regional history, cultures, politics, and geography.
- [[History Notes - June 1999]] — Early chronology for Comor, Dunmar, and Chardon, with notes on magical education.
- [[Dating Systems and the Chardonian Academy - June 1999]] — Dating systems, the structure of the Chardonian Academy, and a brief cult concept.
- [[Early Worldbuilding Checklist]] — A compact outline of subjects the original collaborators intended to develop.
- [[Early Taelgar Price List]] — Coinage, measures, price ranges, and sample goods from an early game reference.
- [[Rohan War Speech - Early Draft]] — A draft address rallying Rohan during the war.
- [[Country Worldbuilding Template - 1999]] — The outline used to organize early country development.
- [[Tirith Campaign and Setting Notes - June 1999]] — Characters, conflicts, and setting details from a game centered on Tirith.
- [[Early Sembara History - June 1999]] — A draft history of the Sembaran monarchy through the early Fourth Age.
- [[Early Taelgar Calendar]] — A generated annual calendar with lunar phases, seasons, and astronomical events.
- [[Chardon Magic Spell List]] — An early working list of spells and prerequisites.
- [[Early Currency Notes]] — A short comparison of Sembaran, Chardonian, and Roharic currencies.
