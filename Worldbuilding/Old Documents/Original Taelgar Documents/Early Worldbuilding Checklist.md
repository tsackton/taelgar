# Early Worldbuilding Checklist

## Gods, Religion, Myth, and Prehistory

1. Create Eru and Melkor, including aspects, avatars, etc.
2. Detail the occurrences from before the First Age.
3. Create a religion for each race/culture/god:
   1. Myths: creation, firstborn, others
   2. Influence among culture
   3. Supernatural powers of clerics
   4. Organization and beliefs
   5. Interactions with other religions
   6. Other stuff I’m forgetting

## Races

1. Description (physical, mental, social, habitat, etc.)
2. Religion, culture, and society
3. History
4. Current status
5. Other stuff I’m forgetting

## History

1. Timeline
2. First Age
3. Second Age
4. Third Age
5. Current time

## Geography

1. Current status of nations, human or otherwise
2. Significant natural features
3. Unexplored territory: the South, the East, or the Western Ocean

## Exotica and Esoteric Stuff

1. Magic
   1. The regular everyday kind (extent, reputation, etc.)
   2. True magic
   3. Other weird magic
   4. Enchanted items of unusual strength and power
   5. Alchemy and other semimagical arts
2. Psionics
3. Monsters
   1. Dragons
   2. Faeries
   3. Extraplanar servants of good/evil
   4. Undead
   5. Werecreatures
   6. Other mysterious creatures, and the extent to which they plague Taelgar
4. Anything else unusual that does not fit the usual definitions
