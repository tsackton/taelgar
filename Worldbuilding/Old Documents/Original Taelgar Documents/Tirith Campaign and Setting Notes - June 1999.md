# Tirith Campaign and Setting Notes — June 1999

Four travelers: two Dunmari warriors, a southerner (Bellini tribe), and a Dunmari mage.

Liontaur religion—Erask (southern) is a mage, befriended by liontaurs after being driven north by Mongol-likes. His brother had a feud with a man, who was a mage, and was exiled because of the feud. The man returned illicitly (maybe over a woman?) and when the brother challenged the bad mage, the brother lost, then the bad mage fled. Erask set off in pursuit.

The Dunmari are part of the counter-religion (which believes in trinity, and also in E+M, no contradiction—see more elsewhere?). They left their home hunting hobgoblins, and encountered Erask along with a few liontaurs in the badlands south of the southern forests. They journeyed together for a time, and the liontaurs left at the forest. The other four pursued the bad mage.

Bad mage: has ensnared Sir Lothar the Black, partly at the behest of the bandits (who are working for the mind flayers, unknowingly)—the bad mage is being supplied and fed and so forth by the bandits, and doesn't want them to go away—but he is essentially a peaceable man. He regrets his earlier rage, to some degree, so he is unwilling to slaughter Sembarans on any sort of large scale.

Sir Lothar had a ritual spell cast on him—it took several days, and the sickness was his body attempting to fight it off. It basically makes him indecisive, untrusting, and a bit forgetful, and sluggish (he sleeps a lot more than he used to, for example). Also, it gives the bad mage clairaudience/clairvoyance with the right preparations. No control, though, except what can be arranged mundanely (which the bandits are working on somewhat—some of the Mockers are doing little odd jobs and such to help them out).
