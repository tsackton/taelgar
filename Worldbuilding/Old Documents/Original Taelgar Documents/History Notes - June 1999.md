# History Notes — June 1999

Hkarian way of teaching magic: very much apprentice-master relationship—apprentice, journeyman, master ranks come from here; you are an apprentice until master deemed ready to be journeyman; then wandered until felt ready to be master, then judged and accepted (or not). Drankor follows this system faithfully—mages used to be more common, so finding master wasn't terribly hard—and places of learning drew magicians.

FGE: 200–1214

Plague ends: 1234

Chaos basically till 1400 or so

Third Age ends: 2244

## Comor

Founded during the 2nd age.

Wasn't influenced much by Drankor, except maybe religiously.

1878–1880: war they lost: ended up losing N. Comor to Vasa—enslaved.

N. Comor is freed: 2244.

16 F.A.: North Comor is independent (war began 11 F.A.).

59 F.A.: Some North Comorians look longing to the past—south is prosperous, they are not.

90 F.A.: North Comor gov't starts taking action—emigration prevention.

115–160 F.A.: Skirmishes, war, and antagonism.

160–190 F.A.: Unsteady peace: north has lost ground and people, but south doesn't want to push the war (south is prosperous, north is not—north is poor, but emigration isn't large anymore). Most of war is fought in the mid mountains, which is high north pop density, low south pop density. So north isn't doing too well.

193–194 F.A.: South throws support behind Sembara, north doesn't. South accuses the north of being pirates. North severs diplomatic ties; south attacks.

209 F.A.: South controls all Comor, except for a few rebels and northern supporters hiding in the northern mountains/wastes.

## Dunmar

Counter-religion: liontaur's religion (ancient, based on a trinity: mother, father, daughter: mother—earth mother-ish, father—warlike, traditional male, daughter: warrior spirit).

Driven out of southern plains ~1500.

Head north—cross the desert, wandering—eldest religious magician goes out to call for aid and find a way out of desert—horse came, and led the people to Dunmar.

Founded in 1528.

Horse is lost 2243.

79 F.A.: Horse returns.

## Chardon

Academy founded a long time ago: 946 F.A.

Founded as a monastery-library. A place for the gathering of knowledge, not teaching.

1080s: Gathers enough of a reputation so that a steady stream of scholars come. Biggest and best library outside Drankor (recognized). Port town grows up a little, grows a bit bigger and takes on a few students in an unorganized fashion.

1120s: Becomes a place of teaching; premier academy outside Drankor.

After plague:

1244: Academy starts to send out messengers, starts a massive effort to prevent massive loss of knowledge; academy proper institutes effective quarantines, etc. Loses personnel to the plague, but itself not devastated.

1296: “Inner” Academy dates founded—mages organized, to preserve knowledge—Chardonian tradition (pseudo-Hkarian) begins.

By 1400: Chardon is the first post-plague city. Pop of 5,000 or so.
