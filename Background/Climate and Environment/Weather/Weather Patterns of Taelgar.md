# Weather Patterns of Taelgar

This catalog connects the seasonal climate model to sequences of weather in particular regions. The existing qualitative maps illustrate five episodes:

- Western winter approach.
- Eastern winter redevelopment.
- Settled summer ridge with eastern inflow.
- Western summer wet spell.
- Active Dunmari monsoon.

![[taelgar-connected-weather-overview-v1.png]]

These are possible weather configurations, not an annual climate map or measured seasonal averages. The winter pair illustrates phases of a possible sequence. Surface wind direction can differ from the direction a whole storm system travels; temporary E–NE winds over Sembara do not replace the broader winter westerlies. ## Western weather sequences

[[Western Ocean and Coast Circulation]] explains the mechanisms behind these local sequences; [[Climate of the Western Coast and Interior]] describes their regional setting.

| Pattern | Sequence and local contrast |
| --- | --- |
| Chardon summer fog | During some settled summer spells, a cool foggy or cloudy morning becomes brighter, followed by a cool night. Inland Chasa can be hotter and sunny, with little widespread rain. |
| Enchanted forest during summer drying | Mist and dripping foliage renew moisture within Crimson and Elderwood while neighboring valley fields and grassland remain dry. Streams can carry forest water outward without bringing a rainy season to the valleys. |
| Southern winter rain passage | Thickening cloud and rain give way to broken showers and a clearer interval. Rain increases toward exposed uplands; open Apporian shores can be rougher than the sheltered gulf. |
| Northern summer wet spell | Fine or drying weather is interrupted by maritime rain across the upper Coastlands and Valandros, strengthened on rising forest slopes. Chardon can remain comparatively dry. |
| Southwestern plains wet spell | Showers reach the Voltara approaches, followed by renewed drying. Deeper Erbalta receives patchier rain, while the highest Fiatara can receive snow. |
| Mawakel snow and thaw | Northern cold brings coastal snow showers; milder western weather follows with rain and thaw. Snow can linger in hills or sheltered routes, and incoming wet weather can begin as snow where cold air remains. |
| Emerald Bay monsoon pulse | Active west–southwest inflow brings wetter, sometimes squally weather to the southern bay and canal approaches. Quieter periods bring lighter or more easterly winds, while northern Apporia stays predominantly summer-dry. |
