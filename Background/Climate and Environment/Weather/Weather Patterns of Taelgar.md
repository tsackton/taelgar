# Weather Patterns of Taelgar

**Navigation stub — weather-pattern catalog pending.**

This catalog will connect the seasonal climate model to sequences of weather in particular regions. The existing qualitative maps illustrate five episodes:

- Western winter approach.
- Eastern winter redevelopment.
- Settled summer ridge with eastern inflow.
- Western summer wet spell.
- Active Dunmari monsoon.

![[taelgar-connected-weather-overview-v1.png]]

These are possible weather configurations, not an annual climate map or measured seasonal averages. The winter pair illustrates phases of a possible sequence. Surface wind direction can differ from the direction a whole storm system travels; temporary E–NE winds over Sembara do not replace the broader winter westerlies. Full individual captions and the clearer winter legend remain to be written.
