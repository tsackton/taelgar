# Climate Revisions Plan

Agreed editorial direction, 3 October 2026. Make the climate collection readable and useful by removing repetition, administrative commentary, and misplaced detail. Preserve the model's explanatory depth and the atlas's useful default predictions. The local `AGENTS.md` records the lasting rules.

## Scope and target

This revision covers [[Taelgar Climate Model]], the six physical model pages, [[Regional Climate Atlas]] and its five regional volumes, and [[Climate and Weather Constraints]], now at the top level of this folder.

These documents currently contain approximately 50,600 words. Aim to cut roughly two-thirds, chiefly through removing repeated accounts and audit prose. This is a collection-level goal, not a quota for each page. Preserve unique useful detail and the constraints' evidence and coverage assessments even where that requires more space.

Weather, biomes, applications, archives, and Gazetteer rewrites are outside this revision. The structure described in [[Climate and Environment]] remains the basis of the work. Only small navigation corrections to that landing page should be included when the revised headings and destinations are settled.

The documentation and plan are now in place. Revision work starts when requested. Review the constraints first and gather substantive physical issues into their appropriate model pages, so those issues are available when the physical-model review begins. Refresh constraint coverage as relevant model revisions are completed.

## Temporary review priority

Tim has carefully reviewed [[Taelgar Climate Model]]; the physical pages and regional atlas have received less review. For this revision, resolve conflicts among these accounts in favor of the reviewed continental account by default. Where it is silent, retain useful compatible detail from the other pages. Preserve its reviewed substance when moving regional detail into the atlas or shortening the continental prose.

This priority does not override retained constraints or newer explicit corrections from Tim. If a constraint or a substantive physical problem conflicts with the continental account, bring that specific issue to Tim rather than silently changing the evidence or the reviewed model. This is a temporary review priority for this revision, not a permanent document hierarchy.

## 3 Revise the physical explanations

## 4 Reduce the continental document to its proper scale

- [ ] Retain the broad thermal and moisture framework, summer and winter circulation maps, major regional connections, and shared concepts needed to understand them.
- [ ] Replace detailed regional tours and climate tables with short descriptions of continental contrasts and links to the appropriate detail.
- [ ] Transfer unique local climate descriptions, regional analogues, and useful Köppen discussions to their atlas destinations, preserving the reviewed continental version where other accounts conflict. Keep mechanism analogues with the physical explanations.
- [ ] Settle an atlas home for southern and distant climate descriptions before removing their current continental treatment. Preserve existing coverage without inventing additional regional detail.

## 5 Rewrite the regional atlas for use

- [ ] Use a consistent arrangement within the volumes: broad regional character, distinct local climates with one seasonal account each, and geographic variations. Keep the index concise.
- [ ] Present the current model's integrated regional predictions directly. Preserve the detail needed to choose a climate for a new place; do not turn entries into lists of established observations.
- [ ] Remove circulation arguments, source assessments, repeated seasonal descriptions, routine uncertainty language, and duplicated weather or application discussions.
- [ ] Incorporate useful regional material displaced from the continental and physical documents. Retain clear contrasts in temperature, rain, snow, wind, and landscape moisture.

## 6 Read the collection as a whole

- [ ] Check that a reader can find a regional default, understand its physical explanation, and trace a relevant constraint without reading several versions of the same account.
- [ ] Check that every original requirement and dated observation retains its meaning, and that useful geographic coverage and unique model content have survived.
- [ ] Update and verify constraint coverage links, the dated summary, and per-row assessments against the revised model. Keep actual coverage gaps easy to find and general model weaknesses in their physical-page homes.
- [ ] Check shared systems, maps, wikilinks, section links, and regional boundaries. Make the necessary small landing-page navigation updates, including the old Evidence-directory reference.
- [ ] Complete the root instructions' file, metadata, and scoped diff checks. Report the final word count and any remaining substantive decisions here or in the handoff, not in the constraints document.

The collection is ready for use when regional defaults are easy to find, mechanisms are understandable, and the evidence remains intact. Completion does not require numerical climate normals or an exhaustive account of undeveloped places.
