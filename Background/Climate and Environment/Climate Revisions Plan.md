# Climate Revisions Plan

Agreed editorial direction, 3 October 2026; workflow clarified 4 October 2026. Make the climate collection readable and useful by removing repetition, administrative commentary, and misplaced detail. Preserve the physical model's explanatory depth and the atlas's useful regional defaults. The local `AGENTS.md` records the lasting rules.

## Scope and editorial direction

The core collection is [[Taelgar Climate Model]], the six physical model pages, [[Regional Climate Atlas]] and its five regional volumes, and [[Climate and Weather Constraints]]. The original collection contained approximately 50,600 words. Aim to cut roughly two-thirds through removing repetition, audit prose, and misplaced detail; this is a collection-level goal, not a quota for each page. Preserve unique useful content and the constraints' evidence and coverage assessments.

Write concrete, connected explanations. State the causal system and its seasonal behavior once, with maps beside the explanation they support. Remove routine hesitations, evidence commentary, development history, and reminders about absent measurements. Keep consequential assumptions in the explanation and genuine unresolved physical problems briefly beside their mechanisms. Do not collect discarded audit prose into another appendix.

Regional descriptions belong in the atlas; weather sequences and applications belong in their respective pages. Moving approved material into these homes is part of completing each physical page. Broader development of weather, applications, or biomes, and rewrites of archives or Gazetteer notes, remain outside this revision.

## Order and approval stops

Complete stage 1, then review the roles of all six physical pages together in stage 2. After that, repeat stage 3 for one page at a time: detailed review, approval, rewrite and redistribution, verification, and a status update.

Stop after each review stage and present its concrete conclusions for approval before writing or advancing. For a page review, the approval covers its proposed outline, substantive model decisions, map corrections, deletions, and named destination edits. Once approved, implement those changes together immediately; do not wait for all six detailed page reviews or request the same approval again. Report the completed page and pause before beginning the next one.

Use subagents for substantial text reviews. Keep working notes sufficient to preserve decisions through context compaction; no separate review ledger or audit trail is needed.
