# Climate and Weather Constraints

**Maintained working baseline, accepted 26 September 2026; model coverage reassessed 3 October 2026.** The subsequent Voltara/Erbalta review adds Tim's clarification of relatively dry Voltara winters, limited snowfall and intermittent snow cover to K31 and reconciles the regional model and Gazetteer. Tim's current western-forest review revises K16 to adopt naturally mild, rainy winters and magical summer moisture in Crimson and Elderwood within a dry regional summer. Other accepted requirements and dated observations are unchanged. This register guides climate-model development; future revisions can follow further feedback and review.

This note records what the physical climate model must accommodate. Broad climatic requirements are separated from weather on particular dates. Crops, rivers, roads, festivals and vegetation are grouped as supporting evidence for climate; they do not each require another climate-model row or a downstream simulation.

## Reading the tables

- **Required** identifies the outcome the model must accommodate, at the stated geographic and seasonal scope. **Open** names the details or explanations not fixed by that requirement; it does not make the required outcome optional. **Preference** identifies a genuinely revisable outcome or design choice.
- **Basis** distinguishes played observations from adopted reference/worldbuilding requirements and preferences. An adopted requirement need not have appeared in play; a played observation need not describe every year or the whole region. Adopted targets retain their stated degree of commitment and can be reconsidered explicitly, rather than treated as fixed by play. These are reviewed interpretations, not blanket endorsements of every claim in the linked source.
- An open explanation does not weaken an established phenomenon. Conversely, a mechanism explicitly retained in a requirement—such as Dunmar’s monsoon—is part of that requirement. Unspecified detail remains undeveloped; this register does not fix parameters it never addresses.
- **Campaign weather** normally represents plausible, broadly typical conditions unless qualified. A single year can be exceptional and does not establish a fixed onset date, freeze date, rainfall season or event frequency. Prep and backstory examples retain their different standing.
- **Covered** means the model assessed on that date accommodates the required pattern. **Partial** means a meaningful retained element still lacks treatment. **Unmodeled** means no regional treatment addresses that requirement, even if circulation in a neighboring region exists. **Tension** would identify an active incompatibility; it is distinct from a gap.
- Coverage assesses [[Taelgar Climate Model]], the six reconciled regional physical models and the five [[Regional Climate Atlas|atlas volumes]] on 3 October 2026. Coverage links point to current explanations and regional detail. **Covered is a qualitative compatibility assessment, not numerical validation:** the mechanism may still have physical weak points identified in [[#Current consistency assessment]]. A covered weather example is possible within the model; the model need not generate that exact historical day.
- New physical model choices—such as the offshore ridge’s reach, Voltara’s rainfall calendar or the detailed operation of the adopted forest magic—remain model choices. They do not acquire independent source authority by appearing in this register.

The register contains **32 consolidated climatic requirements** and **22 weather groups**. Current broad requirements: **32 covered, 0 partial, 0 unmodeled, 0 tension**. Weather groups: **22 covered, 0 partial, 0 unmodeled, 0 tension**. No active incompatibility with a retained requirement was identified. The physical weak points and unresolved source conflicts below qualify this fit; deliberately unspecified quantities and calendars are not new constraints.

## Current consistency assessment

**Reviewed 3 October 2026.** All 32 retained climatic requirements and 22 weather groups are accommodated at their stated qualitative scope by the revised continental model, six regional physical models and five atlas volumes. Independent physical and atlas reviews checked the shared seasonal systems, neighboring-region boundaries and the 15 regional maps. The assessment below distinguishes physical weak points from source disagreements; neither is hidden by a **Covered** label.

The former coverage gaps now have explicit treatment: the Addermarch–Tyrwingha slope contrast, adjoining northern grassland target, Shrev tropical margin, Illoria, northern/central and southern Sentinel snow regimes, Blackwater seasons and local ice, northern Green Sea mountain glaciers/weather, and separate southern jungle settings. The newly covered dated examples are northern autumn mountain weather, Blackwater's February route, the Svinjo whiteout and Drankor's humid rain episode. No requirement, preference, source standing or dated observation was changed to obtain that initial coverage. The later Voltara/Erbalta revision explicitly adopts warmer continental summers and cold, relatively dry winters with limited snowfall and recurring thaws; K31 now records these winter bounds from Tim's review. Tim's current western-forest review separately revises K16 and its coverage to distinguish ordinary Mediterranean summer dryness from adopted elven moisture within Crimson and Elderwood, with a drier transition toward Highveil.

## Retcons, exclusions and source authority

- **Dunmar:** the early winter-rain, spring-bloom desert design is superseded by the monsoon model. Early “cold nights” mean a large day/night contrast, not necessarily absolute cold. Premonsoon “first clouds in months” means generally clear skies. November fertility does not extend the monsoon into November. Obsolete religious/historical rain accounts supply no modern rainfall calendar.
- **Sembara:** retain four seasons, the broad productive agricultural character and greater warm-season precipitation, but release the old strictly dry-winter design and numerical climate classes. Played weather establishes a broadly temperate character compatible with multiple models, without requiring maritime European conditions. Winter rain and snow remain possible, and regional details stay flexible. The separate coastal moderation requirement in K06 remains.
- **Green Sea:** [[Trading in the Green Sea]] controls sailing requirements. The older [[Green Sea]] overview is unreliable and creates no constraints. **Irrla is entirely excluded as a source of climate constraints**; its climate is to be determined by the model. Neither accepted trade routes nor apparent ecological clues restore local Irrlan constraints.
- **Magic and irrelevant environments:** the Plaguelands, Eastern Isles, Sea of Storms, Circular Island, Orenlas’s supernatural local weather, magical Chasm weather, dragon-lair conditions, Feywild/elemental scenes and explicit magical storms or cold are excluded from ordinary physical-climate requirements. This applies even to apparently ordinary Eastern Isles vegetation or weather. High-atmosphere cold and deep magical ice-worm habitat are irrelevant to surface climate. Ordinary northern glaciers and ordinary snow seen by scrying remain usable evidence.
- **Mixed cases:** Tawir’s basic forest/fog and Blackwater’s played February conditions remain qualified requirements. Possible magic is not a confirmed explanation or a reason to discard these observations. Crimson/Elderwood's separately adopted summer magic in K16 does not confirm magic in Tawir or Blackwater; the Forest of Nightmares remains unresolved.
- **Unreliable precision and brainstorming:** old Skaer seasonal sketches, precise Ket freeze dates and ice thickness, older proposed drier Forest of Dreams contrasts, obsolete central-steppe ideas and raw development calendars are not requirements. Nor are real-world analogies, an older climate map’s authority, or duplicated planetary/calendar assumptions. Accepted broad character is stated above.
- **Undeveloped or unlocated material:** the undeveloped Far North and distant eastern/northeastern coasts impose no constraints. Lavnoch contributes no separate requirement. Unlocated mountain snow, redundant elevation-only observations, rejected minor backstories, dubious noncanonical Etienne travel material and rejected historical weather anecdotes remain outside this register.
- **Navigation and chronology:** ship-specific capabilities do not constrain regional winds. The Emerald Song’s ordinary surrounding weather is retained, but canal magic and voyage duration do not set a natural wind speed. Wellby’s revisable Aurbez–Dunmar backstory crossing concerns the southern Sentinels, imposes no early pass-opening date, and supplies no evidence for central/northern winter crossings.

## Maintenance
