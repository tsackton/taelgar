# Magical Climate Departures

This catalog distinguishes adopted magical departures from possible explanations. Each entry records the ordinary climatic baseline, affected place and season, persistence, and unresolved details.

## Crimson Forest and Elderwood summer moisture

**Standing:** adopted by Tim during the western-forest climate review. This is a worldbuilding decision, not an inference that played forest scenes independently establish the mechanism.

**Ordinary baseline:** [[Crimson Forest]] and [[Elderwood]] occupy broad, low foothills with naturally mild, predominantly rainy winters. Winter and spring rain replenish soils and catchments. Summer ridge influence reduces ordinary rainfall, as in the neighboring Chasa and Chardonian valleys. Higher Sentinel slopes retain ordinary winter snow.

**Adopted departure:** ancient elven magic renews moisture through recurring summer mist, canopy drip, and damp soils within the surviving enchanted forests. Together with winter recharge and reduced water loss under humid shade, this sustains dense, productive forest through the regional dry season. It supplies moisture beyond what ordinary rainfall and storage would reliably provide. Dry farmland and grassland can lie close to damp forest; water leaving in streams does not require a rainy summer in those valleys.

**Scope and limits:** the effect recurs through summer drying in Crimson and Elderwood and becomes less characteristic toward Highveil. It does not establish magical winter warming or the same moisture regime throughout every former elven forest. The workings, strength, and precise boundaries remain open. Tawir's possible unusual influence, Blackwater's possible local warming, and the Forest of Nightmares' unresolved magical climate are separate cases.

[[Taelgar Climate Model#Valandros and the western forest belt]] incorporates this departure into the continental overview.
