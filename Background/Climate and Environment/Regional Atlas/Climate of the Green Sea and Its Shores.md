# Climate of the Green Sea and Its Shores

## Regional coverage

- **Shared sea:** the [[Green Sea]], [[Western Green Sea]], [[Eastern Green Sea]], and the seasonal circulation connecting their shores and sailing routes.
- **Northern shores and hinterlands:** coastal [[Vostok]], [[Skaerhem]], [[Ursk]], the [[Great Northern Forest]], the [[Ket]] hinterland, the [[Svinjo Mountains|Svinjo]] and [[Yengorza Mountains|Yengorza]] uplands, and the adjoining [[Far North]] transition.
- **Western and southern shores:** [[Cymea]], the [[Cymean Peninsula]], [[Plataca Hills]], [[Mistfold Mountains]], southern Cymean lowlands, and the [[Medju]] coast.
- **Maritime islands and eastern shores:** [[Irrla]], the [[Eastern Isles]], and the distant southern Green Sea peninsulas.

This volume keeps the shared Green Sea wind and sailing cycle together. Sembara, Tollen, and the inland effects of Western Gulf moisture belong in [[Climate of Greater Sembara and Aurbez]]. Interior Vostok belongs in [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]]; the Great Desert interior remains in the continental overview.
