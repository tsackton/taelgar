# Climate of Greater Sembara and Aurbez

## Regional coverage

- **Sembaran riverlands and uplands:** [[Sembara]], its heartlands and adjoining foothills, [[Addermarch]], and the [[Mostreve Hills]].
- **Western Gulf margin:** [[Tyrwingha]], the Sembaran gulf shores, [[Tollen]], and the lower [[Volta]].
- **Southwestern interior:** [[Duchy of Maseau]], [[March of Andonne]], [[Aurbez Plateau]], [[Salt Hills]], and the adjoining [[Upper Istaros]] lowlands, including historical [[Isingue]].

This volume follows Green Sea moisture inland and the transition toward the drier southwestern interior. [[Climate of the Green Sea and Its Shores]] covers the basin's shared circulation and Cymean shores. Zimkova, Pekul, interior Vostok, and Sentinel high country belong in [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]].
