# Climate of Greater Sembara and Aurbez

Greater Sembara has four distinct seasons: spring rain and rising rivers, warm humid summers, autumn cooling, and changeable winters with frost, snow, rain, and thaws. Maritime influence softens the Gulf shores; cold increases toward the northern interior and higher ground. Southwestward, moisture becomes less dependable, and the hotter Maseau and Andonne lowlands give way to Aurbez's dry, elevated country.

## Regional coverage

- **Sembaran riverlands and uplands:** [[Sembara]], its heartlands and adjoining foothills, [[Addermarch]], and the [[Mostreve Hills]].
- **Western Gulf margin:** [[Tyrwingha]], the Sembaran gulf shores, [[Tollen]], and the lower [[Volta]].
- **Southwestern interior:** [[Duchy of Maseau]], [[March of Andonne]], [[Aurbez Plateau]], [[Salt Hills]], and the adjoining [[Upper Istaros]] lowlands, including historical [[Isingue]].

This volume follows Green Sea moisture inland and the transition toward the drier southwestern interior. [[Climate of the Green Sea and Its Shores]] covers the basin's shared circulation and Cymean shores. Zimkova, Pekul, interior Vostok, and Sentinel high country belong in [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]].

## The main climatic contrasts

The Western Gulf moderates coastal temperatures and supplies moisture to the Sembaran riverlands. Rainfall is greatest in spring and summer, with recurring rain and snow during winter. Mostreve's exposed slopes receive more rain than sheltered southwestern districts. Broken ridges and lower gaps admit maritime weather inland, while the Salt Hills and mountain shelter toward Aurbez mark the transition to drier country.

## Local climates

### Sembaran riverlands and foothills

The central riverlands have warm, humid summers and changeable winters with cold spells and thaws. Spring and summer bring the most rain, supporting productive farmland and woodland. Snow lasts longer toward the northern interior and western highlands than in lower districts nearer the Gulf.

The [[Enst]] has a spring meltwater rise and another rise later in summer.

### Tyrwingha and the southern Gulf shore

Tyrwingha has warm summers and comparatively mild winters. Maritime moisture brings summer rain and recurring wet spells in the cooler months. Its fertile lowlands and wooded northern Mostreve slopes are wetter than the sheltered southwestern side of the hills. Wheat, vineyards, and timber characterize the region.

### Addermarch and the Mostreve Hills

Addermarch's lower valleys have warm to hot, humid, rainy summers and relatively mild winters with little lowland snow. Higher ground is cooler and snowier, with more difficult travel in winter and during the spring thaw. The sheltered Addermarch side of the hills is drier than the wetter, more wooded Tyrwinghan slopes.

### Tollen and the lower Volta

Tollen has moderately warm summers and winters that frequently alternate frost and thaw. Winter snow often melts between events, and rain falls in both warm and cool seasons. The main lower Volta normally remains open; sustained freezing is exceptional.

The Western Gulf is relatively sheltered from severe storms and retains open water through winter, although exceptional winters may freeze protected harbors. Tollen's low southern riverbanks and tributary mouths are prone to flooding, especially at high tide; see [[Hydrology of Tollen]].

### Maseau, Andonne, and the Salt Hills

The Maseau and Andonne lowlands have hot summers and cooler winters, with greater seasonal temperature contrasts than the immediate Gulf coast. Important spring rains give way to long dry summer intervals interrupted by thunderstorms. Dryness increases toward the sheltered plateau margins around Aurbez.

Summer easterlies carry ash from the Plaguelands into the Andonne country. Occasional severe thunderstorms bring hail, lightning, and flash floods. The Andonne plains are ranching country, with scrubby grasses and small dryland trees. Ash damages crops.

### Aurbez Plateau

Aurbez has warm summer days, cool nights, and cold, generally dry winters with episodic snow. Spring rains are important; summers are dry, interrupted by occasional severe thunderstorms. Its elevation keeps the plateau cooler than the adjoining lowlands.

Reliable streams fed by Sentinel snowmelt supply the plateau's watered corridors. Trees and small farms lie along these streams and around the upper [[Aursen]] marshes, while exposed hills carry drier scrubgrass. Higher in the southern Sentinels, winter snow closes the passes.

## Four seasonal patterns

### Winter

Cold spells alternate with thaws across the Sembaran lowlands. Rain and snow both occur, with more persistent snow toward the northern interior and higher ground. Tyrwingha and Tollen have milder winters than inland districts. Aurbez is colder and generally drier than the Gulf coast, with episodic snowfall.

### Spring

Increasing rain and mountain snowmelt raise rivers across the region. Lower valleys warm before the Mostreve uplands. In Maseau, Andonne, and Aurbez, spring is an important rainy season before summer drying.

### Summer

Summer is warm, humid, and rainy in the Sembaran riverlands. The immediate Gulf shore is cooler than the hotter inland lowlands. Maseau and Andonne have long dry intervals and occasional thunderstorms; Aurbez has cooler nights and occasional severe storms amid otherwise dry weather.

### Autumn

The Gulf shores retain warmth while inland districts cool more quickly. Snow returns to the highest routes.
