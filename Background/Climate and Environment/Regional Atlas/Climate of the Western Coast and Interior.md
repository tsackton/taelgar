# Climate of the Western Coast and Interior

## Regional coverage

- **Western seaboard:** [[Apporia]], [[Emerald Bay]], [[Gulf of Chardon]], [[Chardon]], the [[Coastlands]], [[Mawakel Peninsula]], coastal [[Mawar Confederacy]], and the [[Slate Sea]] coast.
- **Southern basins and forest margin:** [[Chasa River Valley]], [[Yeraad]] and its marshes, the southern [[Elderwood]] margin, and the [[Myraeni Gap]].
- **Lake and western forests:** [[Lake Valandros]], [[Tawir Forest]], [[Ainumarya]], [[Elderwood]], [[Crimson Forest]], [[Highveil Forest]], [[Forest of Dreams]], [[Blackwater Fens]], and adjoining northern western forests.
- **Northwestern uplands and plains:** [[Mawar Mountains]], [[Fiatara Mountains]], [[Voltara]], and the [[Erbalta Plains]].

This volume follows western-ocean access from coast to interior. The forested western Sentinel foothills belong here; the range's high country belongs in [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]]. The Nevos monsoon and the Chataan–Alta Tonaro transition belong in [[Climate of Dunmar and the Nevos]].
