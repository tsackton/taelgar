# Climate of Dunmar and the Nevos

## Regional coverage

- **Nevos shores and islands:** the [[Nevos Sea]], [[Illoria]], [[Darba]], coastal [[Western Dunmar]], and the maritime approaches toward [[Emerald Bay]].
- **Interior basins and deserts:** [[Dunmar]], the [[Hara Basin]], [[Tokra]], [[Karawa]], the [[Garamjala Plateau]], and adjoining [[Xurkhaz]] river lowlands.
- **Southern lowlands:** the [[Nayan Floodplains]] and the transition along and south of the [[Shrev]].
- **Monsoon-facing terrain:** [[Darba Highlands]], [[Rudhan Heights]], [[Copper Hills]], [[Yuvanti Mountains]], [[Chataan Mountains]], and [[Alta Tonaro]].

This volume follows Nevos moisture inland and the transition between monsoon-facing slopes and sheltered basins. Apporia's coastal profiles belong in [[Climate of the Western Coast and Interior]]; the adjoining Sentinel high country belongs in [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]].
