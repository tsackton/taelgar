# Regional Climate Atlas

These five volumes define regional coverage for later development. Current temperature, moisture, and seasonal overviews are in [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised]]. The groupings follow connected coasts, watersheds, and terrain; they cross Gazetteer boundaries and contain several climates each.

| Regional volume | Main coverage | Continental overview |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [[Climate of Dunmar and the Nevos]] | Nevos and Illoria, Darba, Dunmar, Hara, Garamjala, adjoining Xurkhaz, Nayan Floodplains, Chataans, Alta Tonaro, and Yuvanti. | [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised#Dunmar and the Nevos\|Overview]] |

| [[Climate of the Green Sea and Its Shores]] | Shared basin circulation, Cymea, Skaerhem, coastal Vostok, Ursk and northern forests, Irrla, Eastern Isles, and the Medju coast. | [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised#Green Sea and Its Shores\|Overview]] |
| [[Climate of the Sentinels and Northern Highlands]] | Sentinel high country, Zimkova, Pekul, and interior Vostok. | [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised#Sentinels and Northern Highlands\|Overview]] |

## Boundaries between volumes

- **Western forests and the Sentinels:** forested foothills belong with the western coast and interior; Sentinel high slopes, passes, and glaciers belong with the mountain volume. Mawar and Fiatara remain in the western volume, while the Chataans and Yuvanti remain with Dunmar and the Nevos.
- **Sembara and the Green Sea:** the Green Sea volume owns the shared seasonal wind and sailing cycle; Greater Sembara and Aurbez covers its inland climatic effects and the drier southwestern transition.
- **Vostok:** coastal districts belong with the Green Sea; interior plateaus belong with the northern highlands.

Drankor, the Great Desert interior, and Orekatu remain under [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised#Southern and distant regions|Southern and distant regions]] without separate regional volumes.
