## Boundaries 

- **Western forests and the Sentinels:** forested foothills belong with the western coast and interior; Sentinel high slopes, passes, and glaciers belong with the mountains. Mawar and Fiatara remain in the western note, while the Chataans and Yuvanti remain with Dunmar and the Nevos.
- **Sembara and the Green Sea:** the Green Sea note owns the shared seasonal wind and sailing cycle; Greater Sembara and Aurbez covers its inland climatic effects and the drier southwestern transition.
- **Vostok and the northern Green Sea mountains:** coastal districts and the Svinjo–Isenborg and Yengorza highlands belong with the Green Sea; interior Vostok plateaus belong with the northern highlands. Their ordinary snow and glacier mechanisms are discussed in [[Sentinels and Continental Weather Connections#Northern Green Sea highlands|the distinct northern Green Sea highlands section]], without treating them as Sentinel slope sectors.

Drankor, the Great Desert interior, and Orekatu remain under [[Taelgar Climate Model#Southern and distant regions|Southern and distant regions]] without separate regional volumes. [[Southern Tropical and Desert Circulation]] develops their physical setting and its uncertainties, together with the Shrev and Garamjala transitions at the edge of the Dunmar volume.

Use the linked physical models for pressure systems, moisture routes, and regional maps. The atlas gives qualitative expectations, not rainfall totals, fixed freeze dates, or guaranteed travel windows. [[Climate and Weather Constraints]] distinguishes accommodated requirements from unresolved physical tests and source conflicts.
