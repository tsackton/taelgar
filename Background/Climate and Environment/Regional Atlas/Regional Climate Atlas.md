## Using coverage labels

**Modeled** means a broad climatic treatment exists, not that temperatures or rainfall have been quantified. **Partial** means some relevant behavior is developed while important local or seasonal details remain open. **Unmodeled** means no adequate regional explanation has been assigned. The linked profiles contain the current local treatments and point to the physical explanations. Detailed mountain regimes and other stated gaps remain unmodeled.

For an undeveloped place, start with [[Taelgar Climatic Model]], identify the relevant regional profile and neighboring transitions, then consider elevation, exposure, and distance from the sea. Record any resulting local profile as a prediction and state what remains uncertain. A blank area or an atlas lookup entry does not by itself supply a climate.
