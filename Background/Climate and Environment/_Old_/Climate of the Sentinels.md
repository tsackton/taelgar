# Climate of the Sentinels

> [!note] Archived climate material
> Preserved for historical comparison. The current continental overview is [[Taelgar Climate Model Revised]]; [[Regional Climate Atlas]] defines the current regional coverage.

**Gazetteer coverage:** [[Sentinel Range]], [[Northern Sentinels]], and mountain country within [[Central Highlands]]. Western forest profiles remain in [[Climate of the Northwestern Interior]].

**Partial coverage — detailed mountain climate pending.** Existing cross-range mechanisms are explained in [[Sentinels and Continental Weather Connections]].

## Existing connections

Western slopes are broadly wetter and snowier, especially in winter, while summer Green Sea moisture reaches the Sembaran eastern foothills. The contrast varies along the range. Upper-air disturbances can cross the mountains while surface moisture is lost; the working winter explanation allows eastern redevelopment to draw a new supply of Green Sea moisture. Neither lowland climate should simply be extended across the range.

## Required outcomes and preferences

K12–K14 in [[Climate and Weather Constraints#The Sentinels]] preserve high-country glaciers and persistent high-elevation ice. Higher southern passes close in winter and seasonal snow melts in warmer months, with some high glaciers remaining. Exact snowlines, glacier extent, valley conditions and seasonal dates remain open.

The southern high country retains a broadly dry character as a revisable preference. Lower winter-crossable routes in the northern/central Sentinels are a separate, revisable worldbuilding preference with no support from play. Wellby’s Aurbez–Dunmar backstory crosses the **southern** Sentinels; its route and crossing dates are revisable. Arrival at Karawa for campaign start and avoiding Raven’s Hold are the narrative anchors. It supplies no evidence for northern crossings or a regular early southern opening.

## Remaining development

The detailed mountain synthesis must connect the distinct systems on both sides, including snow accumulation, exposure and glaciers, without prescribing new pass dates or treating the whole range uniformly. See [[Sentinel Range#Climate and Character]] for geographic context. The current western-forest treatment and E10’s dated autumn weather do not establish a complete northern mountain regime.
