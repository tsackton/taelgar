# History of Taelgar - Non-Canonical Ideas

This documents various non-canonical ideas and suggestions for historical events. None of these should be considered canonical, but additionally, no event here should disagree with canonical events. This is not a place for alternatives to canon, but rather for speculative or unsettled additions.
## The Riving

These proposals concern the primordial setting and the Riving together. Their explanations of origins and transformations are not an established sequence in linear time; distinctions between primordial existence and creation during the Riving remain unresolved.
### Collective origin of the fey

Rather than being created by primordial archfey, the diverse fey could have emerged from the transformation of a primordial essence during the Riving. This essence, understood by later fey as something recognizably fey, became many kinds of creatures of widely varying power. The discussion agrees on a collective origin associated with the Riving, but leaves this particular mechanism tentative.

### Planes as containment

Some planes could have arisen through the containment of powerful primordial forces during the Riving. In this proposal, the Divine Presence created Limbo as a prison for chaos, while an order-associated realm—provisionally called Mechanus—served a comparable purpose for order. This same idea could extend to some other potential planes ([[Arborea]], in one telling).

### A possible planar separation account

The [[Multiverse]] page preserves a possible story in which the primordial cosmos was an unformed sea of unfettered magical power. During the Riving, the Divine Presence tore reality apart, separating raw magical and creative energy from the Material Plane and limiting the power to reshape the world.

In this account, the Ethereal Plane stretched between the Material Plane and Plane of Magic, while the newly created Land of the Dead separated the material/soul world from the Plane of Creation and raw divine energy. The separation unleashed energy that even the Divine Presence could not fully control; its continuing resonances fueled the Echo Realms.

The proposal distinguishes three kinds of planar origin:

- The Material Plane, Plane of Magic, Plane of Creation, Astral Plane, and Ethereal Plane existed in some form before the Riving; the transitive planes were subsequently stretched or reshaped.
- The Energy Realms, Plane of Souls, Land of the Dead, and Echo Realms arose during the Riving in recognizable forms. Echo Realms might subsequently collapse, merge, grow, or split.
- Spiritual Realms arose during or after the Riving, each with its own creation story, spun from soulstuff and the Plane of Creation by Embodied Gods, later Incorporeal Gods, and humanity's primal beliefs.

This is a proposed mechanism and chronology, not established canon. Its specific list of primordial planes is left open by the main history, rather than adopted there. The canonical connection between the Riving and Energy Realms, and the Crown vision's intertwined primordial magic, do not establish the rest of this account.
