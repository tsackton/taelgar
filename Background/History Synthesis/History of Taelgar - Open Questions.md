# History of Taelgar - Open Questions

Questions accompanying [[History of Taelgar]], limited here to the period before humanity. “Source question” identifies a question already raised in the material. “Implied gap” identifies an unresolved boundary or omission directly exposed by those sources, not a newly proposed historical storyline.

## The Riving

These questions concern the primordial setting and acts of creation associated with the Riving. “Before” and “during” identify unresolved relationships, not positions on an established linear timeline.

### Primordial beings and ancient powers

**Source question — primordial origins.** What forms did the predecessors of dragons, giants, fey, and elementals take, and how do they relate to their later manifestations? The existence of a particular list of titans, great wyrms, archfey, or elder elementals is not settled. Later creature rules distinguish dragons and giants from the less-defined origins of fey and elementals.

**Implied gap — Cha'mutte.** Was [[Cha'mutte]] a "great wyrm" who predated the Riving, or something else.

**Implied gap — giant achievements.** When did the giants' reality-weaving craftsmanship, rune-supported skyships, and great artifacts belong in their history? The Giants page establishes these achievements in ancient days, but does not place them before or during the Riving rather than in later history.

**Source question — the order–chaos war.** Was the campaign-established war before the Riving or somehow during it? The opposing side was assumed by the players to be order/Mechanus, but remained poorly defined. The history must accommodate the conflict without silently resolving those gaps.

### Planar form and creation

**Implied gap — primordial planar form.** Did the Plane of Creation, or any other fundamental planes, exist distinctly before the Riving, or did their recognizable forms arise through it? The primordial cosmos was simpler than the present Multiverse, but neither its number of realms nor their arrangement is established. The Crown of Purity vision places the Plane of Magic intertwined with the primordial world; it does not settle whether this was already a distinct plane.

**Implied gap — planar chronology.** Which recognizable planes arose during the Riving, and which were separated or reshaped from something already present? The possible creation account in the Multiverse page proposes a chronology, but only the Energy Realms' connection to the Riving is explicitly established by the Primordial Cosmos page. The beginning of linear time and fixed Taelgar do not settle the origins of the Plane of Souls, Land of the Dead, or every Echo Realm.

**Implied gap — creation of the first peoples.** Were the elves, dwarves, halflings, lizardfolk, and stoneborn created in the Riving itself or after linear time began? Were their creations simultaneous? Their creators are established, but the main history does not settle the relationship among these acts of creation.

**Source question — Far Realms.** Did the first cracks to the Far Realms appear during the Riving, during humanity's creation, or on both occasions? This affects when the Sentient Ocean could have reached Taelgar.

### The Void Mind and the Three Despairs

**Source question — reconciling the simplified model.** The February 7, 2025 discussion explicitly agrees to drop the Three Despairs as a required metaphysical category, retaining the Void Mind and mysterious associated powers without canonical creation stories. Thark remains an Embodied God; Jinnik and Umidzka are described instead as malevolent forces, with the possibility that Jinnik and the Mad One are the same entity. The reference pages still assert the older hierarchy, stolen divine power, Thark's parentage through Umidzka and G'Zab, and the Mad One as Jinnik's separate embodied avatar. Which surviving descriptions should be retained as theology or legend, rather than literal metaphysics? G'Zab's place in the simplified model is not established by that discussion.

**Implied gap — avatar language in recorded lore.** Campaign records preserve inscriptions and cult testimony describing the Mad One as Jinnik's embodied avatar or proposing to summon Jinnik's avatar. These establish that the descriptions exist in-world, but do not independently establish two distinct beings. How should those descriptions relate to the possible identification of Jinnik with the Mad One?

### Aerin and the kenku Firstborn

**Implied gap — Aerin's creation connection.** Aerin became effectively the kenku Firstborn through a time-travel episode associated with their creation. Its placement in the primordial cosmos or the Riving, the mechanism of kenku creation, and whether his role makes him a god remain undefined. The episode does not establish a primordial kenku population.
