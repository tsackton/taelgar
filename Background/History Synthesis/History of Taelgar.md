# History of Taelgar

## Overview
The historical framework of Taelgar is organized around four major cataclysms that reshaped the world order. The immediate aftermath of each of these events typically resulted in a significant new order:

* [[Riving|The Riving]], when time began
* [[The Downfall]], when [[Hkar]] sunk and the world was reshaped
* The [[First Plague]] and subsequent fall of the [[Drankorian Empire]]
* The [[Great War]] and death of [[Cha'mutte]]

This document is meant to be fully and minimally canonical. Ideas and explorations are in [[History of Taelgar - Non-Canonical Ideas]] and open questions are in [[History of Taelgar - Open Questions]].

History is more developed the closer to the present day it is, and some regions have seen more detailed development than others, so this document is necessarily uneven. 
## The Riving

The [[Riving]] was both an event and an act of creation: a moment and an eternity, through which linear time began and Taelgar became a fixed place. Its primordial setting and acts of creation belong to a mythic, transitory existence, not an ordinary sequence of before, during, and after.

### Primordial existence

The primordial world was unfixed: powerful beings could reshape reality with a thought or gesture, and neither time nor causality followed the patterns familiar to mortal minds. Even a single, stable reality was uncertain. This mythic, transitory existence was not an earlier version of later lands and kingdoms, and remains in some respects unknowable.

The [[Primordial Cosmos]] was simpler and less differentiated than the present [[Multiverse]]. Its actual form remains undefined: it may have comprised one realm or several, without the separations familiar in later cosmology. The [[Plane of Magic]] was intertwined with this world, but whether it existed as a distinct plane is not established.

### Creation and separation

Through the Riving, the unfixed primordial cosmos gave way to a world whose history could unfold through successive years. Taelgar became a fixed place, but its landscape was not immutable: later cataclysms would reshape the lands themselves. The separation of the planes accompanied this creation, although the exact nature of the transition remains undefined.

During the Riving, the [[Divine Presence]] gave the [[Embodied Gods]] physical form, permanence, consciousness, and the power to create. They in turn created the [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]], [[Halflings]], [[Lizardfolk]], and [[Stoneborn]], although whether this was in the moment of the Riving or after the beginning of linear time is not established, nor is it clear if all of these creations were simultaneous.

### Surviving echoes

Echoes of ancient power survive in the later world. [[Dragons]] and [[Giants]] retain a connection to the extraordinary magic of the primordial cosmos, although whether they are descendants, transformed survivors, or creations of its powers remains undefined. The giants' ancient history includes mastery of weaving magic and reality together, rune-supported skyships, and artifacts of unparalleled beauty and power, although whether this is a story of the [[Primordial Cosmos]] is not clearly established.

Some [[Fey|fey]], and perhaps elementals, may likewise preserve a continuity with the magical powers of primordial existence. The legends and stories of these beings, along with those of giants and dragons, may preserve fragments of the universe before the Riving, although their relationship to primordial reality is not clearly understood.

Fragments of the Riving's ancient power also survive in the world. The [[Crown of Purity]] and [[Heartroot]] are known examples; other remnants may survive in forms not yet recognized.

### Canonical facts

* There were no mortals before the Riving. Primordial beings were highly magical, with an undefined variety of forms, including at least some predecessor form of [[Dragons]] and [[Giants]].
* These primordial beings were not gods in the sense of the later [[Embodied Gods]] or [[Incorporeal Gods]]. The [[Divine Presence]] and [[Void Mind]] belong to a different, primordial category; both came to be in some fashion, but their origins remain undefined.
* The [[Energy Realms]] were created in some way by the Riving. This connection does not establish a creation sequence for every plane of the present Multiverse.
* The [[Divine Presence]] and the [[Void Mind]] existed during the primordial period in some fashion
* In an ancient war, the forces of chaos were defeated and exiled to [[Limbo]]. Whether this occurred before time began or during the Riving, and the identity of their opponents, remain unknown.
