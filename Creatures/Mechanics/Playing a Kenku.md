# Playing a Kenku
*See also: [[Kenku]]*  
D&D Beyond Link: [Kenku](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/2043575-kenku)

Kenku are humanoid birds with feathered wings that sprout from their backs, giving them true flight. They favor high places and steady winds. Clifftop towns perch above sheltered harbors in the [[Eastern Green Sea]], while bands in the far north fly the summer thermals where the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]] meet the plains. Kenku are enigmatic and private, and few talk about their history or origin to outsiders. Nonetheless, kenku travelers and wanderers are not an uncommon sight around the Green Sea, and across Taelgar many common folk know of kenku from garbled stories and rumors of the [[Great War]].

## Creating Your Kenku Character

When you create your kenku character, first consider your appearance. Kenku appearances are as varied as kinds of birds. Many northern kenku resemble ravens, hawks, owls, or eagles, while island communities include kenku resembling gulls, terns, and songbirds. Consider your plumage, beak, eyes, and the shape of your wings when imagining your character.

Flight is an ordinary part of kenku life. Think about how it shaped your childhood and how you approach the world. Do you instinctively seek high places, scout from above, or judge a destination by how easily you can land there? Traveling with companions who cannot fly might introduce you to routes and places you would otherwise have passed over.

Next, consider the community you came from and your relationship with your people's privacy. Did you grow up in a secluded settlement, perhaps far away in the far north or the eastern green sea, or a small kenku community in a city such as [[Tollen]]? Or, perhaps you were raised among other peoples. Are you, like many of your fellow kenku, reluctant to discuss your home and family with outsiders? Or, perhaps you are eager to share your experiences with new friends and traveling companions. Your own feelings about travel, friendship, and belonging need not match those of the kenku who raised you.

A simple answer, if engaging with kenku lore is not of interest to you, is that you grew up among the kenku of the Far North, and follow the typical reluctance to share about your community and history.

### Kenku Names

Kenku typically use a single name, adding a byname when needed to distinguish themselves from someone else. Naming traditions vary widely between communities. You might draw inspiration from the examples below, invent your own name, or choose one reflecting the people who raised your character.

**Example Names:** Arvek, Eshko, Kavra, Orrek, Teshka, Veyru, Zheva

%%^Campaign:none%%

The islander names used in play were inspired by Sioux and related languages, using Google Translate. This naming inspiration need not define the kenku language or naming traditions elsewhere. ## Basic Traits

**Creature Type**: Humanoid  
**Size**: Your size is Medium.  
**Speed**: 30 feet  
**Age**: Kenku mature and age at the same rate as humans.  
**Languages**: You may select four [[Languages]] during character creation, which likely include Common and Kenku or another language reflecting your homeland. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.

## Kenku Traits

As a Kenku, you have these special traits.

#### Flight

You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You cannot use this trait if your Speed is reduced by an effect related to the weight you are carrying, such as wearing armor for which you don't meet the strength requirement, or dragging weight in excess of your carrying capacity.

#### Fortified Mind

You have Resistance to Psychic Damage.

#### Vocal Mimicry

You have unusual control over the timbre and character of your voice. You can change the sound of your voice, making yourself sound like someone different, though you have no special ability to mimic specific voices. You can also mimic natural sounds, such as bird calls, animal noises, rustling leaves, or thunder.

#### Disguised Form

When you reach character level 5, you can transform yourself with a Bonus Action, selecting one of the options below (choose the option each time you transform). The transformation lasts for a number of hours equal to your Proficiency Bonus, unless you choose to end it early (no action required). Once you transform, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

Here are the transformation options:

**Masked Mind.** While the transformation lasts, you cannot be targeted by divination magic, or perceived through magical scrying sensors.

**Masked Body.** You transform your appearance. You decide what you look like, including your height, weight, facial features, hair length, coloration, and distinguishing characteristics, if any. You can make yourself appear as a member of another race, though none of your statistics change. You also can't appear as a creature of a different size than you, and your basic shape stays the same; if you're bipedal, you can't use this spell to become quadrupedal, for instance. You can reshape or hide your wings.
