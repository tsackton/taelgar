# Playing a Centaur
*See more: [[Centaurs]]*
D&D Beyond Link: [Centaur](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/2236579-centaur)

Centaurs are a migratory people, traveling on long routes that can take years or even generations to complete. Some follow ancestral paths, while others seek novelty and new wonders. Proud and independent, many centaurs regard themselves as children of Taelgar itself, giving thanks to the world rather than worshiping gods. Their deep connection to the land gives them a reputation as guardians and prophets, sensitive to signs and portents that other peoples overlook, but sometimes gives them a reputation for arrogance and aloofness.

---
## Creating Your Centaur Character

When you create your centaur character, consider the community you came from. Did you grow up in a wandering band, following a familiar migration route or seeking new lands? Or did you live a more settled life, perhaps among other peoples? If you traveled with a band, what led you to leave? Some young adult centaurs leave their natal herd and travel, seeking another community, often after a period of independent wandering. Others might leave on a particular mission, to follow an omen, or simply because they became curious about the world beyond their usual paths.

Consider also your relationship with the land. Do you share the belief that centaurs are its children and guardians? Are you drawn to signs and portents, or does your connection find expression in hunting, gathering, craftsmanship, or another pursuit? Many centaurs are aloof toward the affairs of other peoples, but you can decide how much this outlook shapes your character and their friendships.

A simple answer if engaging with centaur lore is not of interest to you is that you grew up in a wandering band and left to see more of the world, intending to seek a new herd when you are ready to settle down. 

### Centaur Names

Centaur names are diverse, often sharing the lyrical sound of Sylvan. Different bands may follow different naming traditions. No single pattern is universal, and centaur names need not all follow these examples. For inspiration, you might draw on classical Greek names, choose one of the examples below, or invent your own.

**Example Names:** Althea, Anthea, Damaris, Ianthe, Leander, Lysander, Melantha, Nereus, Phaedra, Theron
**Named NPCs:** Tharandros, Omphale, Herasto, Theopheia

## Basic Traits

**Creature Type**: Humanoid
**Size**: You are Medium
**Speed**: 40 feet
**Age**: Centaurs mature and age at the same rate as humans. 
**Languages**: The wide wanderings of many centaurs have left them prone to learn languages quickly. You may select six [[Languages]] during character creation, which should usually include Common, Centaur, and often either Elven or Sylvan. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.

#### Equine Build
You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push or drag. In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for you because of your equine legs. When you make such a climb, each foot of movement costs you 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.

#### Sure Footed
You have Advantage on d20 Tests you make to avoid or end the Grappled or Restrained conditions, and on saving throws you make to avoid forced movement or being knocked prone.

#### Migratory Instincts
You come from a long line of migratory ancestors. As long as you are on the Material Plane, you always know which way is north. You have Proficiency in the Survival skill. 

#### Twist of Fate
You are deeply attuned to the tides of fate and can manipulate it in small ways. When a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself succeeds or fails on a d20 Test, you can use your Reaction to roll a d4 and add the number rolled to, or subtract it from, the total of the d20 Test, potentially turning failure into success or success into failure. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and regain all expended uses when you complete a Long Rest.
