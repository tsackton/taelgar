# Playing a Kobold
*See more: [[Kobolds]]*
D&D Beyond Links: [Kobold](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1026395-kobold) or  [Dragonborn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751435-dragonborn). 

Kobolds are a draconic people, often living on the margins of other species' realms. While some kobolds do survive by theft or servitude, many kobold communities forge their own unique path in the world. Kobolds can be found everywhere: living under the protection of a powerful dragon, making homes in a hidden warren or abandoned mine, building independent communities deep in the mountains far from others. 

Kobolds tell many stories about their origins, but none is universally accepted. Though many kobolds believe they were created by powerful dragons as servants, allies, and spies, and seek a power worthy of their devotion, many reject this story. Some communities adopt the gods and religious practices of other peoples, while others focus on tales of kobold independence and ingenuity. 

Though sometimes stereotyped as bandits and a nuisance, kobolds are a diverse and varied species.

## Creating Your Kobold Character

To create your kobold character, first consider what kind of community you were raised in. Some kobold communities serve a powerful patron, such as a dragon. Others survive by banditry and thieving, while yet others forge their own independent path, and a rare few live in the communities of other species. What was life like in your community, and how did you fit into it?

Also consider why you left home. Few kobolds live openly among humans, and kobolds traveling among the realms of humans and other species might  encounter suspicion from people who associate kobolds with banditry or dangerous dragons. Think about what drove you to leave your home, knowing you might face these challenges out in the world, and about how you respond to this reputation. 

Your relationship with dragons and religion can be as important or incidental as you like. Perhaps you believe kobolds were made to serve dragons, or that dragons have no claim on your life. You might be devoted to a god worshipped by your neighbors, carry an assortment of prayers learned during your travels, or take little interest in religious questions. Borrowed beliefs can be deeply sincere, and your views need not match those of your family or community.

### Kobold Names

There is no single naming tradition shared by all kobolds, and kobolds do not speak a single language. Many speak a dialect of Draconic, though this is not universal and you may have grown up speaking a different tongue. Consider your native language for inspiration, though kobold names need not follow any particular pattern.

## Basic Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Small or Medium. Most kobolds are Small.  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
**Age:** Kobolds hatch from eggs, but otherwise mature and age at the same rate as humans.  
**Languages:** You may select four [[Languages]] during character creation, which likely include Common. There is no separate kobold language; choose languages appropriate to your character’s upbringing and experiences. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.
