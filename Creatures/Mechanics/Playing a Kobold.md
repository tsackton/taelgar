# Playing a Kobold

*See more: [[Kobolds]]*

Kobolds are draconic people who make their homes in hidden warrens, abandoned workings, and the domains of powerful creatures. Most are small, but some are larger or have particularly pronounced draconic traits. Resourceful and accustomed to living alongside stronger neighbors, kobolds find opportunities through ingenuity, bargaining, concealment, and friendship. Some serve dragons, while others maintain independent communities or settle among other peoples.

Kobolds tell many stories about their beginnings, but none is universally accepted. Their religious traditions are similarly varied: a community might worship a dragon, adopt the gods of its neighbors, or combine inherited stories with practices gathered elsewhere. Some kobolds seek a power worthy of their devotion; others are content to make their own way.

## Creating Your Kobold Character

Consider where you grew up and what drew you into adventuring. Did your community live independently, serve a dragon, or depend on another powerful patron? Were you raised among other peoples? You might have left to seek wealth, practice a craft, investigate a mystery, help your community, or simply see what lies beyond familiar tunnels.

Your relationship with dragons and religion can be as important or incidental as you like. Perhaps you believe kobolds were made to serve dragons, or that dragons have no claim on your life. You might be devoted to a god worshipped by your neighbors, carry an assortment of prayers learned during your travels, or take little interest in religious questions. Borrowed beliefs can be deeply sincere, and your views need not match those of your family or community.

Few kobolds live openly among humans, and you will often encounter suspicion from people who associate your species with banditry or dangerous dragon masters. Think about how you respond to that reputation and how you came to trust your adventuring companions. You might meet suspicion with patience, humor, proud defiance, or a determination to let your actions speak for you.

For a simple starting point, your character could come from a small independent kobold settlement near the campaign’s starting region. You left to pursue an ambition or find work, and formed a friendship with another member of the party. The details of your community’s origins and beliefs can emerge during play.

### Kobold Names

There is no single naming tradition shared by all kobolds. Established examples include Drubgrak, Vikka, and Zibzig Sparkscale. You can use these as inspiration or choose a name reflecting the people among whom your character grew up. A descriptive byname might express a talent, ambition, or memorable experience.

## Basic Traits

**Creature Type:** Humanoid  
**Size:** Small or Medium. Most kobolds are Small.  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
**Age:** Kobolds age at the same rate as humans.  
**Languages:** You may select four [[Languages]] during character creation, which likely include Common and Draconic. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.

Kobolds lay eggs. They have no separate kobold language; choose languages appropriate to your character’s upbringing and experiences.

## Kobold Traits

Choose one of the following sets of species traits. Both represent kobolds in Taelgar.

For a typical small kobold, use the [Kobold](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1026395-kobold) species traits. If you choose this option, your Size is Small.

Some kobolds are larger than most of their kin and have more pronounced draconic traits. To represent such a character, use the [Dragonborn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/species/1751435-dragonborn) species traits. If you choose this option, your Size is Medium.
