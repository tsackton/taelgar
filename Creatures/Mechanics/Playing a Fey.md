# Playing a Fey
*See more: [[Fey]], [[Feywild]]*
D&D Beyond Links: see Fey Options, below

The fey, the natives of the [[Feywild]], are as varied as the realms they inhabit. Some resemble humans or elves, though often marked by unusual eyes or an otherworldly elegance. Others have wings, horns, fur, or feathers, or take entirely animal forms: the familiar satyrs have the legs of goats and the body of a human, but rabbit-folk, owl-folk, bear-folk, and others can be found across the Feywild. Some fey can change their appearance at will. Some are small, with fairy wings. 

The Feywild is a realm of magic and emotion, where time and distance do not follow mortal expectations. The sun does not move across the sky, but rather as a traveler moves east, the sun sinks lower and lower. Some realms are in perpetual afternoon sun, while others linger in eternal night. The archfey, the great powers of the Feywild, shape these domains by their own natures, so the weather, landscape, and dangers of each realm reflect the being at its heart.

## Creating Your Fey Character

To create your fey character, start with the form and magic you want them to have. Are you nearly human in appearance, or unmistakably a visitor from another plane? Do you have an animal form, wings, or the ability to change your appearance? Are you a small fairy, resembling a pixie? Use the options below to choose mechanics that suit your idea.

Then, consider why you are in Taelgar. Did you leave your Feywild home due to danger, curiosity, or a quest? If you have a homeland in the Feywild, consider whether this is an important part of your character's story. If so, work with your DM to choose or develop the fey realm you came from and your relationship to it and its archfey. Your homeland and your relationship to the power that shapes it can be important parts of your story, whether you still live there or have made a life among mortals, but these details do not need to matter if you do not wish them to.

Consider also how comfortable you are among mortals. Do you pass as one of them, or make little effort to hide your nature? How do the gifts, promises, and bargains of your home shape the way you treat your companions?

A simple answer if engaging with fey lore is not of interest to you is that you come from a peaceful realm of a minor archfey, or even from one of the interstitial spaces ruled by no archfey, and left the Feywild out of curiosity about the mortal world. You can begin with curiosity and a reason to adventure and join your companions, leaving the details of your homeland for later.

### Fey Names

For a name from the Feywild, use [[Languages#Sylvan|Sylvan]] as your starting point: a lilting, musical language whose names should sound lyrical. Many existing fey names draw on classical Greek; Polynesian or Hawaiian names can also provide inspiration. These are starting points for choosing a name. The fey are a diverse folk and you can invent a name with the sound you want, or work with your DM to choose one that suits your character's home.

## Basic Traits

**Creature Type**: Fey
**Size**: As specified by your chosen species traits.
**Speed**: 30 feet.
**Age**: The fey have an unusual relationship to time. Many fey do not appear to age; those that do age may age at unpredictable rates, or may appear to grow old as if they are human. However, most typical fey cannot persist on the Material Plane for more than roughly a human lifespan.
**Languages**: Your connection to the Feywild grants you knowledge of Sylvan. You may also select four additional [[Languages]] during character creation, which likely include at least Common. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.

>[!info] Feywild Native
> Even if you were born on the Material Plane to fey parents, your native plane is considered to be the Feywild. If you die, your soul will return to the Feywild, perhaps to be reincarnated or reborn, perhaps to fade away. If you are subject to an effect that banishes you to your native plane, you will be returned to the Feywild, where you may be trapped if you are permanently banished.
### Shared Fey Traits

#### Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
#### Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
#### Fey Nature
You do not need to sleep, and magic cannot put you to sleep. To gain the benefits of a Long Rest, you must spend 6 hours of meditation and rest. 

## Fey Options

All fey share the basic traits, above, no matter what additional mechanics you choose to represent your species, and all fey gain the Shared Fey Traits, above, as well. 

Where these rules differ from your chosen species traits, use the rules on this page. This includes using the Fey Flight rule below in place of any published flight trait.

In addition, to represent your fey character, consider one of the published species below. If none of these options feel right, work with your DM to homebrew an option.

### Fey Flight
You gain a Flying Speed equal to your walking speed. You cannot use this trait if your Speed is reduced due to the weight you are carrying, such as if you are wearing armor for which you don't meet the Strength requirements, or are dragging something heavier than you can lift. 

### Satyr Traits

#### Magic Resistance
You have advantage on saving throws against spells.
#### Mirthful Leaps
Whenever you make a long jump or a high jump, you can roll a d8 and add the number rolled to the number of feet you cover, even when making a standing jump. This extra distance costs movement as normal.
#### Words of Influence
Your words have power over those who hear them. You know the Friends and Vicious Mockery cantrips. For you, these cantrips require only Verbal components, which can appear to be normal conversation if you choose.
