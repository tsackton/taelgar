# Playing a Fey
*See also: [[Fey]], [[Feywild]]*
D&D Beyond Links: see Fey Options, below

The fey are the natives of the [[Feywild]], and are as varied and diverse as the realms they inhabit. Many fey resemble humans or elves, though usually with graceful features and often marked by unusual eyes or an otherworldly elegance. Others borrow features from animals, from familiar satyrs with legs of goats, to rabbit-folk, owl-kin, and other stranger and more unusual peoples. Some fey can change their appearance at will, shifting to whatever form and appearance pleases them. Some are small, with fairy wings. 

The Feywild is a realm of magic and emotion, where time and distance do not follow mortal expectations. The sun does not move across the sky, but rather as a traveler moves east, the sun sinks lower and lower. This leaves some realms in perpetual afternoon sun, and others lingering in eternal night. The nature of these realms is determined by the archfey who rule them, the great powers of the Feywild.

## Creating Your Fey Character

To create your fey character, start with your appearance. Are you nearly human, perhaps even able to pass as a human, or are you unmistakably a fey, perhaps with animal features? There is a wide range of folk who inhabit the Feywild: use the options below (Fey Options) for ideas, or work with your DM to invent something new. Most classic creatures of myth and fairy tale can fit in the Feywild somewhere. Fairies, satyrs, diverse beastfolk, changelings, and more are possible models. 

Then, consider why you are in Taelgar. Did you leave your Feywild home due to danger, out of curiosity, for a particular purpose, or something else? Where are you from, and is your homeland in the Feywild an important part of your character's story? If so, work with your DM to choose or develop the fey realm you came from and your relationship to it and its archfey. Your homeland and your relationship to the power that shapes it can be important parts of your story, but need not be central to your character's story if you don't want them to be.  

Consider also how comfortable you are among mortals. Depending on your form, you may be obviously marked as fey or you may be able to pass for a native of the Material Plane. Do you attempt to hide your nature, if you can? Are you open about your extraplanar origins? How do you react to the attention, whether curiosity, fear, or something else, of natives of the Material Plane? 

### Fey Names

For a name from the Feywild, use [[Languages#Sylvan|Sylvan]] as your starting point: a lilting, musical language whose names should sound lyrical. Many existing fey names draw on classical Greek; Polynesian or Hawaiian names can also provide inspiration. These are starting points for choosing a name. The fey are a diverse folk and you can invent a name with the sound you want, or work with your DM to choose one that suits your character's home.

## Basic Traits

**Creature Type:** Fey  
**Size:** As specified by your chosen species traits  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
**Age:** The fey have an unusual relationship to time. Many fey do not appear to age; those that do age may age at unpredictable rates, or may appear to grow old as if they are human. However, most typical fey cannot persist on the Material Plane for more than roughly a human lifespan.  
**Languages:** Your connection to the Feywild grants you knowledge of Sylvan. You may also select four additional [[Languages]] during character creation, which likely include at least Common. Like all characters, you may choose to replace one of these language proficiencies with a tool proficiency.

>[!info] Feywild Native
> Even if you were born on the Material Plane to fey parents, your native plane is considered to be the Feywild. If you die, your soul will return to the Feywild, perhaps to be reincarnated or reborn, perhaps to fade away. If you are subject to an effect that banishes you to your native plane, you will be returned to the Feywild, where you may be trapped if you are permanently banished.

### Shared Fey Traits

#### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

#### Fey Ancestry

You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

#### Fey Nature

You do not need to sleep, and magic cannot put you to sleep. To gain the benefits of a Long Rest, you must spend 6 hours of meditation and rest. 

## Fey Options

Where these rules differ from your chosen species traits, use the rules on this page. This includes using the Fey Flight rule below in place of any published flight trait.

In addition, to represent your fey character, consider one of the published species below. If none of these options feel right, work with your DM to homebrew an option.

### Fey Flight

You gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You cannot use this trait if your Speed is reduced due to the weight you are carrying, such as if you are wearing armor for which you don't meet the Strength requirements, or are dragging something heavier than you can lift. 

### Satyr Traits

#### Magic Resistance

You have Advantage on saving throws against spells.

#### Mirthful Leaps

Whenever you make a long jump or a high jump, you can roll a d8 and add the number rolled to the number of feet you cover, even when making a standing jump. This extra distance costs movement as normal.

#### Words of Influence

Your words have power over those who hear them. You know the Friends and Vicious Mockery cantrips. For you, these cantrips require only Verbal components, which can appear to be normal conversation if you choose.
