# Humans

Humans are, by far, the most numerous sentient species on Taelgar, living in all corners of the world, across diverse cultures. Humans were created by the High Gods, the creator divinities [[Divine Presence|Arha]] and [[Divine Presence|Malik]], and endowed with the divine spark, allowing human belief to empower the incorporeal gods.

- [ ] **Warning — coverage.established_fact_missing:** The paragraph gives no account of humanity’s original homeland, the dispersal underlying its cultural diversity, or its ordinary lifecycle. [[Playing a Human#Basic Traits|Playing a Human]] states the ordinary age range. These are central context for the most numerous playable species, rather than incidental campaign appearances. Bounded candidate: “Human traditions remember [[Hkar]] as humanity’s first homeland, lost in the [[The Downfall|Downfall]]. Successive migrations from Hkar, and later centuries of separation and intermingling, gave rise to the many [[Human Cultures of Taelgar|human cultures]] of the modern world. Their languages, customs, and religions vary: some preserve [[Northern Folk Religions]], while others follow [[Mos Numena]] or other traditions.” For a lifecycle section: “Humans reach maturity in their teens and often live into their seventies, sometimes longer.” Link [[Playing a Human]] for character creation; retain uncertainty about Hkar’s detailed ancient history and do not invent a single species-wide culture.
- [ ] **Warning — correctness.cross_note_conflict:** “Humans were created by the High Gods, the creator divinities Arha and Malik” presents two named creators without the qualification in its linked source. Candidate: “Humans were created by the [[Divine Presence]], known in the [[Mos Numena]] tradition as Arha and Malik, and endowed with the divine spark, allowing human belief to empower the incorporeal gods.” This is a proposed theological clarification, not an automatic lore change.
