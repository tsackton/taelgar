# Dragons

%% have some notes to incorporate %%

Rarely encountered, but often sought, dragons are creatures of myth and legend: powerful, unfathomable creatures of inscrutable purpose.

Folklore traces dragonkind to powers of the [[Primordial Cosmos]]. Older accounts call these powers great wyrms, but how they relate to the dragons of the [[Material Plane]] remains unknown. The extraordinary power of dragons may preserve something of that earlier magic without revealing whether they descend from primordial beings, were transformed by the [[Riving]], or arose in some other way.

%%^Campaign:none%%

Dragons are native to the Material Plane and have a canonical but undefined connection to a predecessor form in the Primordial Cosmos. The existence of particular great wyrms, their relationship to later dragons, and what happened to any such beings during the Riving are not settled.

An older, developed account has great wyrms arise from chaotic magical power and give rise to dragons as diminished echoes. This remains a possible story, not an established origin or a chronology of the pre-Riving cosmos. The related idea that dragons draw on the power or soulstuff of more than one plane is also possible, not fixed.
