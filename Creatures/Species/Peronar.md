# Peronar

The Peronar were a subculture of [[Elves|elves]] closely associated with the humans of later [[Hkar]] and the [[Drankorian Empire]]. They retained a distinct cultural identity from other elves, even when living in the same cities. Peronar lived alongside humans and other elves in Drankor and in Estemar, the City of Peace in [[Rostaure|Rostaurë]].

Peronar held a prominent place in Drankorian rule: the [[History of the Drankorian Empire#Rulers|first four emperors]] were Peronar, and later Peronar emperors ruled intermittently until Imrodel, the last.  

The [[Omnis Pura]] particularly targeted the Peronar, and Rostaurë sheltered Peronar refugees during the upheavals following the Drankorian Civil War. [[Apollyon]] subsequently carried out systematic massacres of Peronar; Arheste witnessed their slaughter both among Rostaurë's defeated defenders and in public spectacles in Drankor. 

[[Session 115 (DuFr)|Accounts from the last days of Drankor]] express fears that most, if not all, of the Peronar were hunted down and killed by [[Apollyon]] -- but at least a small population survived in some fashion, for [[Rai]], alive during the [[Great War]], was a Peronar and the the last of Imrodel's line. If any other Peronar survived to the modern era, they are well-hidden. 

%%^Campaign:none%%
One non-canonical possibility is that the Peronar began as elves who settled on Hkar during early contact with humanity, staying to teach and help rather than returning to the elven kingdoms -- perhaps driven by a [[History of Taelgar - Non-Canonical Ideas#Elven preparations that outlive their warning|premonition of danger]]. Prolonged exposure to Hkar's human [[Divine Spark]] -- or other unusual facts about [[Hkar]] itself -- might have altered their connection to [[Aldanor]] without severing their Firstborn ties or wholly changing their elven nature. They could have become free of the fertility cycle of the [[Elven Cycle of Generations|ka]], while also becoming shorter-lived and less firmly bound to the world. In this conception, they would be a distinct elven population with some more human-like characteristics, rather than the offspring of humans and elves.
