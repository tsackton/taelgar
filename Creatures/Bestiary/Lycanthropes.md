# Lycanthropes

Lycanthropes are people afflicted by the curse of lycanthropy, a curse that transforms them into beasts, often, but not always, against their will. Werewolves are the most commonly described form of lycanthrope, but not the only one. The curse of lycanthropy is associated with [[Jinnik]], the Mad One, who is claimed to be the source of the curse and often referred to as the patron deity of lycanthropes. 

Although feared, lycanthropy can be cured.

%%notes
Curse of Lycanthropy cannot be cured by simply a Remove Curse spell. Requires two of the following things:

-   Remove curse spell
-   An item of personal significance to the person who is being cured, as a reminder of their past life before Lycanthropy took them.
-   The blessing of a God
