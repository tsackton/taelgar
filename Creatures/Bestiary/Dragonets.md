# Dragonets

Dragonets are intelligent, winged creatures who resemble miniature [[Dragons|dragons]]. Typically about three to four feet in size, they have wings, dexterous hands, and legs. They fly with confidence and often carry themselves with a certainty of their own dominance and place in the world, despite their small stature.

Dragonets love fish and are excellent swimmers. They readily dive from the air into the water, play in and around the water, and swim beneath the surface with the ease. 

## Society and Competition

Dragonet society is organized less by fixed institutions than by playful competition. Prominent dragonets attract shifting entourages of companions and admirers, while ambitious challengers seek status by testing themselves against established stars. These contests can involve stories and songs, acrobatics, flying, diving, or almost any other skill that can be performed before an audience. Winning matters, but performing well matters too: a dragonet who challenges a famous champion may improve their standing even in defeat.

Dragonets can magically sense which competitor has the greater support of an audience.; this shared awareness makes the result of a contest clear to all participants and watchers. Though competition can be fierce, it is rarely a source of resentment, with winners and losers typically returning to friendly lives together until the next challenge.

Dragonets no structures that would appear to outsiders to be a permanent government. When a community must judge a serious crime or make a decision that affects everyone, it gathers for a more solemn use of the same audience-support magic. These proceedings retain the performative and collective character of ordinary competition, but their tone and consequences are much more serious.

## Treasures and Stories

Dragonets delight in shiny objects, but their appreciation is often as much a social game as a desire to possess something valuable. A glittering stone or polished trinket can become the center of an extravagant invented mythology, with dragonets competing to tell the most compelling story about its origin and significance. Teller and audience alike often understand that the object is not truly important: the pleasure lies in the story, the performance, and the shared pretense that this particular bauble is a relic of tremendous consequence.

## History
*Tentative, might change*

Dragonets are first known as inhabitants of [[Hkar]], where they were common in cities, particularly in urban centers of magic. Whether they were created alongside humanity or came to Hkar from elsewhere in its early history remains unknown.

After [[The Downfall|the destruction of Hkar]], dragonet communities scattered. Dragonets remained common in [[Drankorian Empire|Drankor]], while other groups fled into the [[Feywild]] or far to the east. The relationship between the refugees who entered the Feywild and the fey creatures now known as pseudodragons is uncertain. The fall of Drankor brought another catastrophe in which many dragonets died or fled, leaving their surviving communities widely dispersed.

## The Circular Island

By 1749 DR, a stable population of roughly four to five hundred dragonets lived on the [[Circular Island]], most of them among the ruined manors and towers around [[Alectia]]. Their ancestors had survived the fall of Drankor, and lived for many years under the authority of [[Ra'ghemdros]]. Some became true believers in the dragon's rule, while many others merely endured it or quietly sought a different future.

After [[Session 110 (DuFr)|Ra'ghemdros was killed]], the ancient dragonet [[Dimitaur]] took charge of what remained of her hoard and proclaimed himself Protector of the Forever Sunset. The political future of the island's dragonets remains unsettled.

## DM Notes

Dragonets canonically exist and have a history tied to Hkar and Drankor, but their metaphysical nature remains unresolved. It is not clear whether they are a distinct species, a kind of intelligent creature represented by the unusual population on the Circular Island, a transformation related to dragons, or something else. Their relationship to both dragons and pseudodragons is likewise uncertain. See [[Metaphysics of Creatures]] and [[Creature Rules in Taelgar]].

The social material above comes from a conversation with players and is treated here as the intended general dragonet culture. How completely that culture survived among the different dragonet diasporas has not been established.

For the Circular Island factions, encounter guidance, and provisional combat mechanics, see [[Dragonets - Last Jade]]. That packet models dragonets with modified draconian statblocks: a 40-foot flying speed, one size category smaller, no Death Throes trait, and Flyby.
