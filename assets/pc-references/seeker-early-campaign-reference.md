# Seeker — early-campaign character reference

This set represents Seeker in the **early Dunmar Frontier campaign**, anchored in the descriptions from sessions 1–6 and the visual choices made for this reference set. Seeker's appearance will change during the campaign; preserve these files as the early-campaign set and give later appearances separate filenames.

## Visual choices for this set

The starting point was Seeker C (image 3) from the revised three-variant comparison panel. The matching views preserve his face, warm intelligent expression, and three red beard braids, with these requested refinements:

- Long, loose red-auburn curls reaching the middle of his back.
- Slightly narrower shoulders and less upper-body bulk, while retaining adult dwarf proportions: a high-INT, low-STR scholarly spellcaster.
- No spectacles.
- Neutral charcoal and gray-taupe scholarly robes; the Tollen-red cloak belongs much later in the campaign and is excluded here.

The exact hair length, curl pattern, three-braid arrangement, and narrower build are visual decisions from this reference process. The plain ankle-length robe, simple belt, and worn boots extend the selected close portrait into a complete outfit. Those clothing details are design interpretations. The rear view follows the front view for consistency.

### Decisions where the sources differ

For this set, the user explicitly chose the **red beard from session 6**, despite the blond beard in session 1, and directed that **spectacles be removed** because they were not a reliable part of Seeker's description. These choices govern the images while the quotations retain the transcript wording.
