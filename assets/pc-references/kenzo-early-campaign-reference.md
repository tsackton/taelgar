## Approved visual anchor

- A young adult human man with a subtly thin, gently tapered face, healthy cheek softness, a softly defined jaw and rounded chin, lighter eyebrows, relaxed dark-brown eyes, and a small closed-mouth smile.
- Clean-shaven, with warm natural skin and realistic skin texture.
- Long black hair swept back into a high topknot, with a few fine loose strands.
- A lithe, lean, strong athletic build, with balanced shoulders and understated natural muscle.
- A plain dark slate-blue woven hooded robe, hood down, over a gray open-neck linen underlayer.
- Warm mottled brown backdrop, soft portrait lighting, and a realistic photographic style.

The user selected the younger portrait with lighter eyebrows, requested a closer resemblance to the supplied second facial reference, then approved the refinement with a subtly thinner face and a more lithe, strong build. That final approved portrait is the identity anchor for these views.

## Full-body design

The front view extends the selected portrait into a practical robe ending just below the knees, long sleeves, a matching blue cloth sash, plain charcoal trousers, and worn dark-brown leather ankle boots. The sash knot and hanging ends sit at Kenzo's left front waist. They remain mostly hidden from the rear; the down hood rests across the upper back.

The robe length, sash, lower clothing, boots, and rear construction are visual interpretations made to complete the reference set. The rear view follows the generated front view for consistent proportions, clothing, and hair. Both full-body images use a relaxed neutral stance with empty hands so the outfit is visible. This does not imply that Kenzo never carries a staff.
