## Visual choices for this set

- A small, compact **adult halfling**, with short limbs and neck, a relatively substantial head, broad hands and broad round-toed boots. His build is nimble within those short proportions.
- The selected lean, freckled face, sun-browned skin, hazel-green eyes, softly tapered ears and dark chestnut hair swept back from his forehead, with loose wisps at the ears and nape.
- **No beard, moustache or stubble.** A small, patient smile and the quiet manner of someone at home on the road, following the user's preference for less mischief.
- A low-crowned floppy olive-brown cloth hat with a twisted brown cord; a weathered forest-green homespun coat with hood down, hand-sewn edges and a wooden throat toggle. The full-body coat reaches mid-thigh.
- Plain brown studded leather beneath the coat, a simple belt, brown trousers and well-worn leather walking boots. The clothes are faded, rubbed and used, but maintained.
- A plain bow and quiver carried on the back over his **right shoulder**, and a plain sheathed shortsword at his **left hip**. The front and rear views retain those anatomical sides. All equipment is scaled to a halfling.

The user selected the portrait and the quieter wanderer direction, and explicitly emphasized that Wellby is a halfling. The short body proportions, coat length and back construction, trousers, boot design, exact ordinary weapon designs and equipment placement are visual extensions of the portrait. The green coat, wide-brimmed hat, deep tan, diminutive quick movement, studded leather, bow, quiver, shortsword and worn boots/clothes are supported by the early transcripts. No later magical gear is depicted.
