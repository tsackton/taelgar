This set represents Delwath in the **early Dunmar Frontier campaign**, anchored in the descriptions from sessions 1–6 and the approved visual choices below. Preserve these files as the early-campaign set; give later appearances separate filenames.

## Visual choices for this set

- A graceful, elegant adult male elf with a lean medium build, moderately narrow shoulders, a natural narrow waist, long balanced limbs and light athletic definition.
- The approved **B face from the elven refinements**: refined cheekbones, a tapered jaw, golden eyes, pointed ears and a quiet, subtly smiling expression. Completely clean-shaven, with no stubble or beard shadow.
- **Deep copper skin**, with rich russet-red and brown tones.
- The approved **A hair from the nonhuman hair studies**, refined to **approximately shoulder length**: silky **opalescent silver** with pale lavender, rose and ice-blue iridescence. Softly center-parted and swept back into a short low ponytail, secured with a plain dark tie at the center of the nape. The tie sits behind the head and is hidden in the front and close views, rather than appearing beside an ear. The tapered ends fall a little below the lower edge of the green neck wrap on the upper back. Its pearly luster is part of the selected fantasy appearance.
- A plain steel **chain shirt**, fitted naturally to his slender frame, with short sleeves and a hem just below the hips. Charcoal linen long sleeves underneath, a dark forest-green scarf with tucked ends, a simple dark leather belt, fitted charcoal trousers and plain dark-brown mid-calf boots.
- Relaxed, poised front and rear reference poses with empty hands. No additional armor or later-campaign equipment.

The face and opalescent hair were explicitly selected by the user. The graceful build and chain shirt follow the user's direction for this full reference. The exact shirt construction, underlayers, belt, trousers, boots and rear clothing details are visual interpretations used to complete the selected portrait. The rear view follows the generated front view for consistency.
