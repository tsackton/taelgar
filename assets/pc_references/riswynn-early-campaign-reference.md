## Visual choices for this set

- Gentle rounded face, warm eyes, freckles, and a small, patient smile.
- A compact, muscular adult female dwarf build: a stronger torso and arms, a less cinched waist, and less pronounced hips and hip-armor flare.
- Rich red hair with intricate smaller braids gathered into a low coiled bun, plus **one long hanging braid over her left shoulder**. That braid stays over the front of her body in the rear view.
- Carefully maintained **plate armor**, with polished steel, narrow bronze edging, curved panels, modest layered shoulders, and the selected central anvil motif.
- A mace in her right hand and a shield in her left, held low so the armor remains visible. The rear view preserves those same hands and equipment.

The user selected the gentler Riswynn C portrait and approved the subsequent full-body proportion corrections. The supplied PHB dwarf illustration guided the compact, muscular proportions. Riswynn's face, hair, armor design, equipment, and reference pose follow her selected images.

Plate armor and the forge-cleric direction were explicit user instructions. The exact facial design, braid arrangement, armor decoration, lower armor, backplate, and mace/shield designs are visual choices for this set. The full-body views extend details outside the selected close portrait.
