## Visual choices for this set

- A youthful, gently tapered face with leaner cheeks and a narrower, naturally defined jaw, warm eyes, freckles, and a small, patient smile. Her head is slightly larger relative to her finer frame, preserving her facial proportions and emphasizing her dwarven build.
- A compact, lithe, muscular adult female dwarf with a finer frame and less overall bulk: moderately strong upper arms and forearms, a slightly fuller athletic chest and torso, a natural waist, understated hips, and subtly slimmer thighs.
- Rich red hair with intricate smaller braids gathered into a low coiled bun, plus **one long hanging braid over her left shoulder**. That braid stays over the front of her body in the rear view. In the close portrait, the hair around her face and neck is secured into the braids, without loose face-framing strands.
- Carefully maintained **plate armor**, with polished steel, narrow bronze edging, curved panels, modest layered shoulders, and the selected central anvil motif.
- A mace in her right hand and a shield in her left, held low so the armor remains visible. The rear view preserves those same hands and equipment.

Following consultation with the player, the user approved a leaner revision of Riswynn's earlier reference: a finer frame and matching face, then slightly stronger arms, a little more chest and torso, and subtly slimmer thighs. This approved full-body front view remains the visual anchor for the set; the subsequent head-size refinement applies consistently to all three views. Her short dwarven stature, single hanging braid, armor design, equipment, and reference pose follow the selected images.

Plate armor and the forge-cleric direction were explicit user instructions. The exact facial design, braid arrangement, armor decoration, lower armor, backplate, and mace/shield designs are visual choices for this set. The current portrait and rear view extend the approved full-body front reference.

## Provenance

The set developed from the approved front image, `riswynn-early-campaign-balanced-build-v3.png`. A new close portrait and full-body rear view were generated with the built-in image_gen tool to match that front. The close portrait was subsequently refined to soften the lines around her eyes and tuck the two loose facial strands into the braids; this edit and its prompt are recorded in `portrait-refinement-v2`. The latest built-in image_gen refinement targets an approximately 7% larger head in all three views, with the established face, body, armor, equipment, and framing retained. Current files and prompts remain in the Taelgar Images project under `generated/riswynn-early-campaign-character-reference-v3`; the immediately preceding vault images and note are preserved in its `previous-vault-reference` subdirectory. Earlier matching-view and portrait refinements remain under `generated/riswynn-early-campaign-character-reference-v2`. The prior generation history remains under `generated/riswynn-early-campaign-character-reference-v1` and `generated/riswynn-early-campaign-build-studies-v3`.
