# Urtzabala
*(oor-tsah-BAH-lah)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Urtzabala was the vast wetlands, home to many large [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] communities, that covered much what is now the [[Western Gulf]], along with the lowlands that later became the [[Enst]] and [[Wistel]] river valleys.

The [[Ozabal]] and [[Latazaro]] swamps are surviving remnants of Urtzabala.
