The Istabor Alliance was a loose confederation of city-states, freeholds, and agricultural hinterlands in the fertile upper Istaros watershed. [[Isingue]] served as the cultural, spiritual, and magical center of this dense network of richly productive agricultural villages and towns. The region prospered both from its agricultural vitality and its position at the crossroads of trade between Cymea, Dunmar, and Sembara.

During the Great War, [[Isingue]] bore the brunt of [[Cha'mutte's Plague]], and was utterly destroyed. The Alliance collapsed, the land was devasted, and surviving refugees fled north and west as they could. Now, many of the descendants of these refugees call the [[Refounded Alliance of Aurbez]] their home.

## Codex Summary of Istabor Brainstorming

The Istabor brainstorming converges on a strong central concept: a prosperous, decentralized Isinguese confederation in the upper Istaros, with Isingue as its cultural and spiritual “first among equals.” It was bound together less by a central government than by shared water-magic, agriculture, trade, religion, and mutual defense.

### What the discussions broadly agree on

- **Political character:** The alliance consisted of self-governing city-states, towns, villages, and freeholds, with relatively little higher authority. The discussions invoke New England town meetings, the early Icelandic Commonwealth, and a Continental Congress as analogies. These describe a political mood—local rule, councils, and voluntary cooperation—not an established constitution. See [[2023-08-08 - Isingue]].
    
- **Isingue’s role:** Isingue was probably not a gigantic administrative capital. It was the cultural, spiritual, and magical center of a densely settled network of prosperous communities. Its importance came from the magically fertile and healing waters around the Istaros confluence, later connected more specifically to the Heartroot. The current canonical notes have adopted much of this framework. See [[Isingue]] and [[Istaros]].
    
- **Economy:** Agriculture was the foundation—grain, cotton, and other unusually productive crops supported by the magical watershed. Trade made the region richer still. The principal prewar axis was repeatedly imagined as Cymea and South Cymea → Isingue → Dunmar → Chardon, with an alternate northern route through Brovna and Urlich. Sembara was initially a comparative backwater. See [[2025-08-06 - Upper Istaros Region]] and [[Green Sea Brainstorming]].
    
- **Formation against Avatus:** The later Avatus discussions proposed that the existing communities rapidly consolidated into the Istabor Alliance in response to Avatus’s early expansion. Istabor stopped his advance south, using fortifications and possibly Heartroot-assisted defensive magic along the Andonne and Douren. This produced a long “watchful peace”: Istabor was strong enough to contain Avatus but neither able nor particularly motivated to conquer his closed, mysterious dominion. See [[2025-11-23 - Avatus and Hobgoblins]] and [[2025-11-24 - Avatus and Hobgoblins]].
    
- **Rangers:** One proposed Ranger tradition began as guardians of Isingue’s waters and the Heartroot. A separate Addermarian tradition arose from guerrilla resistance to Avatus. Beryl may have joined these traditions during the Great War. The current [[Rangers]] note treats both historical connections as canon, but still presents Beryl’s precise unifying role as one possible explanation.
    
- **Language and identity:** Isinguese developed as an eastern Drankorian-descended language, closely related to and possibly mutually intelligible with Cymean, but distinct from Sembaran. Later discussion makes the linguistic divide substantial enough to reinforce the separate identity of Isinguese refugees. The language is now dying outside a few communities. See [[2024-07-12 - Languages and Humanity]].
    
- **Other cultural possibilities:** A distinctive burial tradition was proposed in which natural decay returns something spiritually valuable to the land; cremation would not be sinful, but wasteful. This would fit the water-and-fertility complex and distinguish Isinguese communities in Maseau and Aurbez, but it remains brainstorming. See [[2024-01-27 - Funerary Traditions]].
    
- **Destruction and diaspora:** Isingue was besieged during the Great War and the alliance was destroyed in DR 1545. Refugees dispersed principally into Maseau and Aurbez, with other communities in Tollen, Sembara, Cymea, and possibly southern Cymea. Later diaspora discussions distinguish assimilated descendants, tradition-keeping communities, active supporters of Aurbez, and committed restorationists. By the modern period, the last group is concentrated mainly in Aurbez and Maseau. See [[Isingue Notes]].
    
- **The Refounded Alliance:** Aurbez was deliberately conceived as an attempt to recover something about Istabor: local independence, mutual aid, common defense, Ranger involvement, and association rather than fealty. It is better understood as Istabor’s ideological heir than as an unbroken continuation of its government. See [[2025-08-07 - Sembara History]] and [[Refounded Alliance of Aurbez]].
    

### Important unresolved or inconsistent points

- **Drankorian inheritance:** One strand presents Isingue as consciously rejecting Drankorian ritualism and imperial principles. Another calls it the inheritor of a long Drankorian tradition. These can coexist—Drankorian language and material culture combined with political and religious rejection—but that reconciliation has never been written explicitly.
    
- **Urban scale:** Isingue alternates between a modest “first among equals” surrounded by dense small towns and a “major urban center” presiding over a large cotton economy. Its regional importance is consistent; its actual size is not.
    
- **Founding date:** Earlier material leaves the alliance’s formation organic and undated. The November 2025 Avatus work makes it a defensive coalition formed during the first Avatus war, but proposed dates range from roughly DR 1250 to the early 1300s.
    
- **Government:** “City-state alliance,” “freehold confederation,” “mutual-aid society,” “commercial alliance,” and “defensive pact” all appear. They are compatible at the level of atmosphere, but no actual institutions, membership rules, councils, or division of authority were settled.
    
- **Borders:** Whether the [[March of Andonne]] belonged to Istabor, Maseau, or shifted between them remains explicitly unresolved. Aurbez was probably Avatus’s original heartland rather than part of Istabor, although Istabor may have expanded north after Avatus fell.
    
- **Avatus chronology:** The broad sequence converged on an initial war, a long watchful peace, northern resource wars, and final defeat by Derik I. Exact dates and Avatus’s motives changed repeatedly. Older brainstorming gives his defeat as DR 1429; later work uses DR 1427.
    
- **Great War aftermath:** Current lore puts Isingue’s destruction in DR 1545. An older Sembaran chronicle instead has a deadly mist advancing from former Istabor territory in DR 1551. That may describe a later expansion of the curse, but the text does not clearly distinguish the two events.
    
- **Terminology:** “Isinguer,” “Isinguese,” “Isinguen,” and several misspellings appear across the discussions. The emerging clean division seems to be **Istabor Alliance** for the polity, **Isingue** for the city, **Isinguese** for the language and culture, and probably **Isinguer** for ancestry or an individual—but the vault has not applied that consistently.
