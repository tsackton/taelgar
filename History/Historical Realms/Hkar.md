# Hkar
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Hkar was humanity's first homeland, an island in the [[Endless Ocean]]. The [[Divine Presence]] created [[Humans|humanity]] there with the [[Divine Spark]], through which human belief could create and sustain [[Incorporeal Gods]]. Though some later accounts treat Hkar as mythical, its loss and the migrations from it shaped the histories of peoples across Taelgar. Hkar sank during [[The Downfall|the Downfall]] in CY 4122.

## Belief and society

Early human life on Hkar was rich with miracles. Over many generations, communities developed varied beliefs and practices; no single later religion represents all of Hkar's history. The [[Chainshroud]], a cult from the early days of humanity, preached slavery and dominion before its defeat and the imprisonment of its god [[Sarqon]].

By the eve of the Downfall, Hkar had a highly magical urban culture, and worship of the [[Mos Numena Pantheon|Eight Divines]] had become dominant among its urban elite. Other traditions had also arisen there: worship of [[Kaikkea]] belonged to the mariner culture of Hkar before its departure.

## Departures from Hkar

The first major emigrants were the peoples later called [[Northerners]]. They settled along Taelgar's western coast, spread north beside the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]], and eventually crossed into the northern highlands and forests. Their traditions of small local gods developed with little sustained contact with Hkar. These movements need not have been one brief or uniform migration.

A southern migration and smaller departures, including that of the ancestors of the [[Dunmari]], also carried human communities beyond Hkar, though much of their early history remains undefined. The [[Mariners]] settled first around the [[Eastern Green Sea]] and spread west along its shores, carrying a pantheonic tradition distinct from Mos Numena. Their descendants developed new cultures rather than preserving an unchanged Hkaran identity. The [[Skaer]] are among those of Mariner descent, but the circumstances in which they became a distinct people are not settled. The Mariner-descended inhabitants of [[Tollen]] had founded the city by CY 3902.

Neither the timing of humanity's first contact with older peoples nor its relationship to the earliest migrations is fixed. Hkar continued to change while migrant communities developed beyond it.

## The Downfall and legacy

The [[Downfall Wars]] ended with Hkar's sinking and the reshaping of much of Taelgar. Refugees from Hkar founded [[Drankor]] in CY 4133, carrying traditions of magic and worship that became central to the [[Drankorian Empire]]. Those traditions were one inheritance from Hkar, not the whole of its human history.

%%^Campaign:none%%

The dates CY 3902 (Tollen), CY 4122 (the sinking of Hkar), and CY 4133 (Drankor) are established anchors. No precise date for humanity's creation, first contact with nonhumans, or the principal departures from Hkar is fixed. Whether Hkar already existed when humanity was created, where the southern migrants settled, and how widely its refugees dispersed remain open. The names Thalassar and Archon below occur only in this note's older brainstorming and are not established places.
