- [ ] **Warning — coverage.established_fact_missing:** The visible article omits the migrations and later civilization that make Hkar central to human history. [[Northerners]] describes the first migration, its west-coast settlement, and its departure before Hkar's later urban, arcane culture; [[Mariners]] records the later seafaring migration and its Eastern Green Sea settlements; [[Human Cultures of Taelgar]] identifies the urban magical culture of the Downfall refugees; and the public History section of [[Drankorian Empire]] establishes those refugees as Drankor's principal founders. Add a compact account, for example: “Long before the [[The Downfall|Downfall]], successive migrations carried human peoples from Hkar to other parts of Taelgar. Among them were the [[Northerners]], who settled along the western coast and spread north around the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]], and the [[Mariners]], who settled the [[Eastern Green Sea]] and spread west along its shores. Hkar's later society developed an urban magical culture associated with [[Mos Numena]]. Refugees from its destruction founded [[Drankor]], from which the [[Drankorian Empire]] grew.” This explains the homeland's lasting significance without assigning uncertain dates, inventing migration motives, or developing its deliberately open internal history.
