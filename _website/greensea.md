# The Green Sea Campaign

> [!image|hero]
> ![[halflings-under-full-sail.webp]]
> *A halfling crew runs before a strong wind under brightly colored sails.*

>[!important] The Story
>Our story begins with a shipwreck. Strangers are thrown together, by chance and fate, lost and stranded somewhere in the Green Sea. Our campaign begins with their story of survival, as they forge an adventuring party and find their way home. Our story continues with *their* stories, as they make their way back to safety, perhaps changed by their experience, and follow the adventures that took them to sea in the first place. 

## Welcome to the Green Sea

From the shores of the [[Eastern Isles|wandering islands]], full of magic and mystery between the [[Green Sea]] and the [[Outer Ocean]], to the desert sands beyond [[Medju]] and the spice cities, to the mangrove swamps and endless channels of the lizardfolk homelands of the Zingra, to the bustling markets of Talshan where traders from all over mix and mingle, to the great cotton estates of southern Cymea, to the storm swept fisheries of the [[Skaerhem|Skaer Islands]], to the closed ports and deep snowy winters of [[Ursk]], the shores of the Green Sea hold wonders and danger alike.

### A Tour of the Green Sea

#### Southern Cymea (1)

South of the faded maritime glory of Cymea, the great cotton estates thrive on Green Sea trade, while fortifying their western borders against the dangers of the Plaguelands. 

> [!image|hero]
> ![[south-cymea-cotton-estate.webp]]
> *Cotton fields beneath a fortified estate in South Cymea.*

#### The Pearl Coast (2)

Along the coast, stone-walled towns thrive on the pearl industry and the wealth of the hills of the interior. 

> [!image|hero]
> ![[stone-coast-walled-port.webp]]
> *A walled trading town on the stone coast.*

#### Imperial Glory (3)

Nestled against the mountains, a new imperial realm grows, remembering the glory days when Drankor protected all from danger. 

> [!image|hero]
> ![[imperial-city-harbor-terrace.webp]]
> *Copper-roofed halls and merchant houses rise above the crowded imperial harbor.*

#### Talshan, the Great Port (4)

On the tip of Cape Odrava, the great port of Talshan bustles day and night, welcoming traders, merchants, and travelers from across the Green Sea. 

> [!image|hero]
> ![[talshan-harbor-from-the-cloth-market.webp]]
> *From the cloth stalls of Talshan, the market steps lead past ochre trade halls to the quays. Across the water, another stair hangs in midair beside an island tower.*

#### The Land of a Thousand Channels (5) 

In the Zingra, winding channels lead through the mangroves to [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] villages. A traveler may spot a line of fish weirs among the reeds long before the first house comes into view.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[lizardfolk-village-at-daybreak.webp]]
> *Mist rises among the raised houses, fish weirs, and winding waterways of a lizardfolk village.*

#### The Spice City of Medju (6)

In [[Medju]], the spice lords have grown rich on goods brought by caravan across the desert. Sailors say a buyer with enough coin can find almost anything in the city’s markets.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[medju-1.jpg]]
> *Ships gather beneath the towers of Medju, where desert trade routes meet the Green Sea.*

#### The Republic of Svolhas (7)

The farms of [[Republic of Svolhas|Svolhas]] lie between the sea and the steep valleys of southeastern [[Irrla]]. Raids on the coast have driven the republic to build up its fleet.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[svolhas-river-gate.webp]]
> *A shepherd brings the flock through a river gate in the [[Republic of Svolhas]]. Beyond the grain fields, another fort watches the road toward the [[Reaver Coast]], where hobgoblin clans hold the eastern tip of [[Irrla]].*

#### The Reaver Coast (8)

From sheltered harbors along the [[Reaver Coast]], hobgoblin pirate clans raid merchant ships crossing the [[Green Sea]].

> [!image|hero]
> ![[reaver-coast-raiding-galley.webp]]
> *As the rowers pull the galley around the headland, a fire mage at the bow watches a merchant sail on the horizon.*

#### The Shoals of Irrla (9)

Shifting shoals reach far out to sea off western [[Irrla]]. A current can sweep a ship into shallow water while the island’s mountains are still a blue line on the horizon.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[western-irrla-shipwreck.webp]]
> *Surf washes through the ribs of a wreck on the western shoals of [[Irrla]]. Beyond the breakers, rain veils the highlands.*

#### The Closed Ports of Ursk (10)

On the northern shore of the Green Sea, [[Ursk]] allows foreign ships to trade at only a handful of ports. Sailors who make the crossing for the prized dragonhides of the northern wastes may go no farther than the docks and warehouses.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[praznitsky-harbor-and-the-closed-city.webp]]
> *Foreign crews bargain for furs and dragonhide on the harbor island of [[Praznitsky]]. Masked wizards patrol the single arc of stone between the noisy quays and the city’s shut gate.*

#### The Skaer Islands (11)

On the volcanic islands of [[Skaerhem]], fishing villages shelter in rocky inlets along the storm-swept coast. The [[Skaer]] still honor [[Kaikkea]], the ocean goddess their ancestors worshiped before the Downfall.

> [!image|hero]
> ![[pyhlla-village-church.png]]
> *A fire burns outside the church of Kaikkea in [[Pyhlla]], above the rocky shore.*

#### The Western Gulf (12)

West of the [[Straits of Cymea]], halfling ships and Tollish traders sail the sheltered waters of the [[Western Gulf]]. 

> [!image|hero]
> ![[tollen-fiskurth.png]]
> *Shoppers crowd the wet cobbles of [[Fiskurth]], the fishing ward of [[Tollen]], where the inshore boats land their catch at dawn.*
