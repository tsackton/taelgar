<!-- SLOT: combat.summary -->
**Giant Lizards at [[Gomat]].** Three giant lizards attack with unnatural persistence at [[Gomat|Gomat Oasis]], but the party kills them all and secures the camp.

**Skeletons at the Fort.** Four skeletons emerge from a recently disturbed mound outside the ruined fort and attack. The party destroys them before investigating the site.
