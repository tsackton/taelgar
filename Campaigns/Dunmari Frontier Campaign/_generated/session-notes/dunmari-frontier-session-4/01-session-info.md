- (DR:: 1748-03-27): The party continues traveling west toward [[Karawa]].
- (DR:: 1748-03-28): The party continues west towards [[Karawa]] through the [[Karawa Desert]].
- (DR:: 1748-03-29): The party reaches [[Karawa]] in the late afternoon, now swollen with people for the [[Festival of Rebirth]], after an uneventful journey. They report to Elder [[Kisa]] about the fort, and hear about the [[Summer of Red Storms]]. Then they speak to [[Ikram]], who identifies the petrified explorer as [[Arcus]] and directs them to the smith, [[Jasu]]. They speak with [[Jasu]] as evening approaches, and then meet the [[Charmhearts]] and overhear Ander Charmheart's cursed mutterings. After nightfall, they speak with [[Candrosa]] and learn about [[Shakun's Protection]].
