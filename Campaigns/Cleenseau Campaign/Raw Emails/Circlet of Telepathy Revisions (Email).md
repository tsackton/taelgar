## Fri, 9 Feb 2024 14:55:27 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Circlet of Telepathy, take 2

Chris,

Because it is starting to come up in game, and I thought a bit more about
the circlet of telepathy and actually read Detect Thoughts more carefully,
I wanted to revamp how it works a little bit, and give you a choice between
a few options.

To begin with the following features will always work:

*Telepathy*: You can, at will, communicate mentally with anyone you can
identify within 30'. Usually "identify" means "see", but if you know
someone well (i.e. Robin) you'd be able to establish communication even if
you can't see them - and maybe some other circumstances would allow it as
well. Once established, this lasts until you end it, even if you leave the
30' range. This does not require concentration, but does require a mutual
language, and only works on a single target at a time. This works basically
as a "voice in their head". This is effectively a spell with no material,
somatic, or verbal components, so it is not detectable easily. It takes an
action to establish communication, and a bonus action to send a message.
The creature you have established communication with can respond using a
bonus action as well. You can break off communication at any time. If the
target does not wish to hear your voice, they can, as an action, try to
block you by making a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. Once
blocked, you cannot communicate with someone until the block wears off (24
hours).

*Detect Thoughts.  *
You can, at will, attempt to probe someone's surface thoughts within 30' of
you. This takes an action, and requires concentration. While probing
someone's surface thoughts they are unaware that you are probing them. If
you wish, you can try to probe deeper. Once you are probing deeper, the
following occurs:
- the target knows their mind is being probed
- you can read their deeper emotional state, goals, etc
- the target can attempt, each turn, to block you by making a Wisdom saving
throw against your spell save DC
- if the target successfully blocks you, the target is immune to further
mind probes for 24 hours

You can maintain the detect thoughts on a target who leaves the 30' range,
however the further they go from you the harder it is to be secretive about
it, and once the target is more than 300' from you, or is out of your
sight, the target can make a Wisdom save against your spell DC each turn,
and on a success you are blocked and cannot probe their mind for the next
24 hours.
Note you cannot use this to scan an area. Also note it is not blocked by
any particular materials.

There are two variants of how this can work.
*Option 1 - A Spell*. In this variant casting detect thoughts is a full
spell with verbal and somatic components, and it lasts for a minute. The
target gets no save against surface thoughts, but gets a Wisdom save
(against your spell save DC) when you attempt to probe deeper.
Pros: No one can stop surface thought reading.
Con: You are clearly casting a spell and you can't easily do it in secret

*Option 2 - Psionics*. In this variant, you are not casting a spell, you
are just reaching out with your mind somehow. This is still a magic effect
(so i.e. dispel magic could get rid of it) but has no verbal or somatic
components, and it lasts until your concentration ends. You can cast it
entirely by thinking - no one knows you are casting it. In exchange, the
target gets a save when you first try to probe their surface thoughts, not
only when you probe deeper (but also, the target doesn't know you tried
even if they fail until you probe deeper). The target here would not get a
*new* save when you try to probe deeper (but would become aware that you
were probing deeper).
Pros: You can do this secretly
Con: But targets all get saves

Note: In this variant, technically the duration is unlimited but if that
ends up too powerful I would grant a new save every minute.

Which do you prefer? (I think option fits the character better and also
seems more fun)

*Suggestion. *Once per day, for a target of your detect thoughts, instead
of probing deeper, you can implant a suggestion in the target's mind. This
is an action, but this does not require words and does not require verbal
communication, and has no material component (it works even if you are
unable to share a language with the target, or don't want to speak out
loud). The target gets a saving throw (Wisdom, against your spell save DC),
and if the saving throw succeeds, know you attempting to implant a
suggestion. If the saving throw fails, they do not know you attempting to
implant a suggestion, and it grows and they follow it (as per the rules in
the *suggestion* spell). The suggestion lasts for 8 hours or until you stop
detecting the target's thoughts, whichever comes *latter*. Maintaining the
suggestion does not require concentration. If by some chance it does last
longer the 24 hours, the target gets a new save each day. You cannot regain
the ability to suggest until the prior suggestion has been completed.

Notes: I tweaked this, so that if the target makes the save you don't get
to use it again. But you do not need concentration.

Mike
