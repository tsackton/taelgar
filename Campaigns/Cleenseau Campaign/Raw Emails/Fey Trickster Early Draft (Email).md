## Sun, 10 Dec 2023 11:40:15 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Fey trickster

Here’s what I have so far:

3rd level:
* spell casting as arcane trickster, but you can choose from spells on the bard list, or with GM approval, the cleric and sorcerer lists. Spell casting stat is wisdom.

* you can cast misty step a number of times equal to your wisdom modifier without using a spell slot.

* when you use misty step you can use one of following features:

Distracting Step. Chose a creature within 5’ of the space you appear in. You have advantage on your first attack roll against that creature before the end of your turn.

Taunting Step. Creatures you chose within 5’ of the space you left must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or have disadvantage on any attack rolls they make against creatures other than you until the start of your next turn.

9th level:
You are immune to charm. You can cast misty step as a reaction when you take damage. Additionally, you gain the following option:

Disappearing Step. When you cast misty step you are invisible until the start of your next turn, or until you cast a spell or make an attack roll.

I’ll make up the 13th and 17th level benefits when it is closer to relevant.

Thoughts?
