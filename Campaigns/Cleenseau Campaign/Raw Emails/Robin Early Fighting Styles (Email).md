## Wed, 4 Jan 2023 13:38:56 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: New fighting styles

Dan,

What do you think of the following:

*Fighting Style: Guardian*
You are trained in defending an area and guarding your allies. You gain the
following benefits

   - Creatures provoke opportunity attacks when they attempt to move past
   you, even if they take the disengage action. [ "move past" isn't well
   defined, but the idea would be to say that someone turning and running
   would be able to disengage, but a creature trying to hit the wizard behind
   you even if they stayed in your reach you would get an opportunity attack
   on ]
   - If you hit a create with an opportunity attack, you may choose to
   reduce their speed to zero for the remainder of their turn
   - You may take the "Defend" action on your turn. When you take the
   Defend action, you may choose to forgo one or more of your attacks and
   instead gain extra reactions, one for each attack you forgo. You may still
   use your remaining attacks as if you took the attack action.

The idea behind Defend is that if you have 1 attack you can save it; if you
get 2 you could attack once and save one, or save two, or attack twice. Etc.

*Fighting Style: Protector*
You are trained at punishing enemies that dare attack your friends. You
gain the following benefits:

   - If a enemy attacks an ally within 5' of you, you may use your reaction
   to either
      - Impose disadvantage on the attack roll OR
      - Attack the enemy as an opportunity attack


   - You may take the "Defend" action on your turn. When you take the
   Defend action, you may choose to forgo one or more of your attacks and
   instead gain extra reactions, one for each attack you forgo. You may still
   use your remaining attacks as if you took the attack action.


*Feat: Shield Master*
You have mastered the use of a shield. You gain +1 to Dexterity or
Strength, and in addition:

   - If you aren't incapacitated
   <https://www.dndbeyond.com/compendium/rules/basic-rules/appendix-a-conditions#Incapacitated>,
   you can add your shield's AC bonus to any Dexterity saving throw you make
   against a spell or other harmful effect that targets only you.
   - If you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity
   saving throw to take only half damage, you can use your reaction to take no
   damage if you succeed on the saving throw, interposing your shield between
   yourself and the source of the effect.
   - If you have the Guardian fighting style you can use your shield to
   distract opponents when making opportunity attacks. You gain +1 to hit on
   opportunity attacks while you are wielding a shield
   - If you have the Protector fighting style you can use your shield to
   shelter allies from ranged or magical attacks. You may use reaction to
   impose disadvantage on an ranged attack as long the ranged attack would
   pass within 5' of your square and is not targeting you. Additionally, you
   may use your reaction to give any ally within 15' of you a bonus on DEX
   saves equal to your shield AC bonus, as long as you are also a target of
   the spell.

What do you think?

Mike

## Wed, 4 Jan 2023 16:27:13 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: New fighting styles

For what it's worth, I think you'd end up with a much more
satisfying character that is also aligned with what you want him to be able
to do by taking:

1st level:
* Human bonus stat bump
* Shield Master

2nd level:
* Guardian Fighting style

4th level:
* Protector Fighting style and/or something else depending on play

With this approach and my homebrew stuff you would end up with stats like:

STR 12 DEX 17 CON 14 INT 10 WIS 10 CHA 16
OR
STR 12 DEX 16 CON 14 INT 10  WIS 10 CHA 17

This is much better than your current
STR 12 DEX 14 CON 12 INT 11 WIS 10 CHA 16

Either way, it would set up to get either Cha or Dex to 18 at 4th level
with only a half-feat and then have a 20 @ 8th level.

(I'd probably let a bonus fighting style increase a stat bump so you could
take protector + a stat bump)
