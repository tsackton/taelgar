## Sun, 12 Oct 2025 15:33:31 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Interlude - Meta

Hi all,

My plan for managing the downtime over email:

Each Sunday and Wednesday I will send an email recapping a month. Each
email will include the events of the previous month and my assumptions for
what each character is doing in the upcoming month.

(Today I'll just send a email recapping the previous discussion)

If you want to do something other than what I state as the assumption (or
just add color/interest to what I state), let me know before the next email
is due. In general, for each month, I'm looking for a primary activity and
up to three secondary activities. If any activities seem relevant to your
class skills, feel free to include a roll and what skill or competency you
are using along with the role. But feel free to include any narrative you'd
like as well, either to the group, or just to me and I'll incorporate it
into the email for the next month.

Some months will include an adventure hook. If the hook seems interesting
and folks want to follow up on it, let me know, and we will just stop the
email threads and the next session will be the hook. If we end up without
anyone taking the hooks, next session will start with the Queen's arrival
in the region.

Mike
