# Random Celyn Things (Email)

Local archive of all 1 message body in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. Background details offered by Kiya for Celyn, not a played scene.

## Sat, 30 Mar 2024 18:31:48 -0400 — Kiya Nicoll <darkhawk@mindspring.com>

Subject: [Thantos] Random Celyn Things

While I was writing ridiculous amounts of backstory I started taking notes on Celyn’s Quirks as I thought of them and have compiled them into a list of things the party would have figured out or noticed by now, just for general amusement and roleplay fodder.  So throwing this out into the void for general amusement:

* Always orders wine rather than beer in taverns, then flicks drops of it on the floor before drinking any.
* Appears to be completely oblivious to situations for extended periods of time before coming out with a comment that makes clear he heard everything, actually.
* Makes random references to his past as a teenaged delinquent.
* Refuses to make eye contact for more than brief accidental moments.
* Unreasonably chill. Chill about nearly dying.  Chill about everything except like one thing.
* Not chill at all about the party trying to talk with a hostile fey. That’s the one thing.
* His shield always has a pair of not-matching birds on it.  The birds are different every day.
* Was so perky and amiable about the time he said “If anyone bothers me in the bath I’ll stab them!” that it took a few minutes after the fact for people to notice he took his sword.
* Cheerfully obsessive love for the shiny math rocks.  Not that he’s bad at dice games but winning is clearly not why he’s there at all, he is blatantly just as happy losing as he is winning.
* Drums on things with his fingers when distracted.  Also sometimes with random sticks.
* Speaking of random sticks, prone to picking up bits of nature here and there, many of which wind up attached to his drum until entropy destroys them.
* Sometimes distracted by the need to harvest bits of apparently random plants.
* Can sometimes be found drinking some sort of herbal tea. It does not smell great.
* Faintly baffled by temple hierarchies and structure at all times.
* Prone to casting a light spell whenever he’s doing non-emergency critical wound care.  For each patient.  He just pops it there for them like a nightlight or something.  They tend to shimmer randomly through a variety of colors.
* Slightly slow to emerge most mornings for no readily apparent reason.
* Says he learned all kinds of random things from the fey. Sometimes things he did not appear to know last week, despite a lack of any apparent fey to provide instruction in the area.
* Cuts his own hair at irregular intervals.  Produces uneven results.
* Responds to pretty much anything he encounters from other people with some version of, “Oh!  Okay!”
* Oddly uncomforable when out of his armour.  Nothing overt, just tension vibes.
* Really likes birds, though not in an informed naturalist way, just a “Yay! Bird!” way.
* Absolutely hates sharing sleeping spaces, which is not an issue in the Cleenseau manor, but may come up on the road. If he can’t find a place to sleep alone he will claim a corner and curl up grumpily with bedroll.  Possibly behind a chair.
* Much better dealing with kids than adults.  Kids can handle weird.
* Occasional comments about having been someone else once.  (I think this first came up talking to Izgil in like session 1, but presumably this other person was the one named after a bird!)
* Gently distracted by Things.  Quality of light shining through the window with the smudge on it.  That hawk’s hunting dive at that squirrel.  Wind sounds in the trees.  Children’s laughter.  Ants. Just, a wide variety of Things seem to hold his attention slightly more than one might expect.
