## Sat, 11 Oct 2025 12:35:57 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Asineau: Map

Hi all,

Here is a more detailed map of Asineau.


[image: Asineau - 2 (2).jpg]

Key features:
Each hex is 20 feet. The map is basically pointing north. The road along
the top is ancient and magically preserved, and very well made stone. The
manor house is to the right side. There is a small courtyard with some
trees and a watering trough for travelers. The riverbank is marshy and
rocky to the west, and then there are a series of 8-12' high banks, before
the sandy beach area with three docks. The cliffs then arise again to the
east of the beach. There is a small sandy beach beneath the cliffs to the
west, but it is a bit of a scramble to get to. The building to the far west
is the mill and bakehouse, which is up a hill from the town. North of the
road are farmlands. You can see a partial wall. This is in relatively
poor condition, except for the section to the far east (from the manor
house to the cliff near the river). The building in the center of the more
northerly cluster of houses is the temple to the Wyrdling. The cluster of
houses just south of the manor is all owned by the lord and is currently
where many of the families of the manorial staff and guards live. There are
four wells, plus the river, for water. Vegetable gardens are mostly marked,
and there are livestock (sheep mostly) that wander through the village. In
the spring during the shad runs, the beach area is crowded with drying
racks for fish, as well as the top of the cliff just to the west of the
harbor/beach.

Mike
