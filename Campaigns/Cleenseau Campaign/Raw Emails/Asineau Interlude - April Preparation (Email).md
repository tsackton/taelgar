- sometime in probably early April he will ask Viepuck if he would be willing to contact the halfling tailors who fled Veltor to see if they or someone in their extended family would be willing to come to Asineau to talk with him about clothes.  This has nothing to do with the green cloth if anyone asks.



Aside from that, his priorities are SimManor, resting, Robin, and pursuing his own interests depending on where the impulse takes him, which means his visible activity will include stuff like:
	- spending time at the Wyrdling temple learning the ins and outs of how that functions more thoroughly, whether it needs repairs, what sorts of things are asked of people there, what sort of specialist-to-the-Wyrdling things are in demand in the region, etc.
	- occasionally joining people spinning for an hour because spinning in a medieval-ish village economy is eternal and he does, in fact, know how to do it
	- climbing around on the cliffs between the town and the river, partly to figure out where fortifications can go sensibly both in a structural sense (for which he may grab Izgil to advise) and a social sense (which will not impede traffic to the river) and where the river-side gate thus must sensibly go— but also mostly because climbing things is Enrichment In His Enclosure honestly
	- rambling the countryside in the area to figure out what’s growing there, what’s forageable in the wild bits, what resources are present (such as potential clay pits), and otherwise familiarizing himself with the new home turf
	- he wants to contact Warin the Woodsman up in Valit and see if he can get trained in how to do tracking, which he can roll into the rambling the countryside if that works out for Warin
	- continuing to discuss stars and their connections with navigation with Izgil as opportunity presents
	- peering curiously at all the things, being diverted by tiny miraculous moments, and generally being a cheerful mooncalf who is integrating into “this village has found its madman”


Celyn’s notion of how to live in a village is very “I know this space and all its territory like the back of my hand” so basically he is working on bootstrapping up to that with power of his terrifying overflow of Attention.

Two notes:

(a) As I was discussing with Dan, some of the game mechanics for these
kinda suck (all the weapons and armor in the armory shouldn't vanish after
you use them, they are not paper weapons). Also, for various world-flavor
reasons, I don't want making magic items to be particularly easy or to make
it possible to have Asineau become a center of magic item production so
every house in the Cleenseau region has continual light or whatever. So I
will likely homebrew some variants of the game rules for most of these,
sticking to the spirit of the rules.

(b) You might want Izgil to have a research library, rather than an arcane
study. Either could work from a flavor standpoint, but the Library lets you
learn up to 3 true facts about a topic of interest. That is mighty
convenient and might be better than cast Identify, which Izgil can mostly
already do, from a game perspective.
