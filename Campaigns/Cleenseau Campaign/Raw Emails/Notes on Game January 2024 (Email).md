## Fri, 26 Jan 2024 17:54:02 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Notes on game

A couple notes:

There are vast and epic amounts of text to go over here. I will try and review it as best I can before next game, but some of these e-mail threads have gotten...well, epic, and with all the embedded additional information it's probably going to be some work to untangle what's going on.

My suggestion in regards to weapons in town (other people feel free to chime in here)...I don't really know how much some extra people having a sword or mace on hand is really going to up the level of local drunken violence. It's a medieval town, everyone almost certainly has easy access to a knife and a hand axe or hammer, so...in terms of people being stupid in a tavern, I don't know that the presence of a sword or two really ups the lethality much.

Permit folks to carry spears and bows. A bow is a standard hunting tool some folks will already know how to use. Spear is easy to learn at a basic level (point the spiky end at the thing you want to stay away from you) and good for small groups of people to say hold a doorway or alleyway against a clump of zombies or orcs or whatever trying to get past. Both are inconvenient to grab and cause too much trouble with if you're in a drunken brawl.

For close up "surprise" combat, it's probably best to get folks to practice using improvised weapons (again your knives, hammers, hand axes, etc.) because that's what most folks are likely to have on hand if more surprise zombies appear.

Daniel Walters
