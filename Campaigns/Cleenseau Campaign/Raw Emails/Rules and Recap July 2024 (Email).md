## Sun, 28 Jul 2024 22:57:36 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Rules & Recap

Hi all,

Thanks for the session. It was enjoyable. I'm glad Celyn finally got to
show off his fey lore. I did want to talk a little bit about rules.

I don't want rules to get in the way of anyone's fun, and I don't want
people to feel like I'm trying to take away stuff from their character. I
also really like making up custom rules/spells/items etc to better fit
character's flavor, fun and so on. But I do feel sometimes like the flip
side of a million house rules is everyone being a little bit willing to
adjust and tweak. I know in the moment it can be frustrating to not be able
to do something, and I hate getting into debates on rules or what I ruled
in the past during the game. But between sessions, if people can spare the
bandwidth, I'd love to try to rationalize and document the various house
rules that might only be in email.

I think Dice Cloud is the easiest way to do that, and I've made a start on
everyone's characters. Dice Cloud is also nice because I can automate
things (i.e. Kiya, we all forgot *again* that Celyn should have gotten 8
temp HP after the first time being hit).

I've also added some features from One D&D, almost entirely around giving
you more options or making it easier to recover resources (not much in the
way of just purely new features although there are a few little ones).

So... TL;DR if people have time it would be good to get all the various
custom things sorted on your Dice Cloud character sheet and switch over to
that for the next game.

Mike
