# Greymalkin Rules Discussion (Email)

Local archive of all 2 message bodies in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. DM and player discussion of Greymalkin's mechanics.

## Mon, 16 Mar 2026 11:58:56 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Greymalkin

Proficiency Bonus: +3



Saves: STR: +4 DEX: +1 CON: +2 INT: +0 WIS: +2 CHA: +0Hi Dan,

Here are my proposed changes to Greymalkin.

*Proficiency Bonus*
Currently +3. Going forward, it will match yours.

*Stats*
Currently
STR: 18 (+4) DEX: 12 (+1) CON:14 (+2)
INT: 10 (+0) WIS: 14 (+2) CHA: 10 (+0)

Going forward:
Increase 2 stats by 2, or 2 stats by 1 and 1 by 2.
Whenever Robin gains a new feat during level up, increase 1 stat by 1.
Max 20.

*Saves*
Currently, just stat based (i.e. all saves = the stat bonus)

Going forward: Choose either DEX, CON, or STR to get proficiency in.
If you chose DEX -- also add INT
If you choose CON or STR -- also pick either WIS or CHA

Whichever saves you pick, Greymalkin adds proficiency to.

*Skills*
Currently Greymalkin has a static bonus to Perception. Instead choose 2
skills, Greymalkin is fully proficient in them (i.e. gets stat +
proficiency bonus)

*HP*
Currently 40 and a complicated formula that gives him 10 extra HP every 4th
paladin level.
New: Exactly like a PC with d8 Hit Dice, calculated at Robin's level. (So
8 + CON + (5 + CON) * level -1)

*Attacks*
Psychic Slam. To hit = + prof + str. Damage = 1d8 + str psychic (existing)
New: Claw Rend. To hit = prof + str. Damage 1d6 + str slashing

*Bonus Actions*
Misty Step, 60 feet (existing) 1 usage per day. Improves to usage = Wisdom
bonus, min 1.
Rend (new). One prone target. Make a claw rend attack.

*Speed*
Gains a fly speed of 40' feet

*Life and Death*
Greymalkin becomes fully real, an actual being who cannot just disappear to
the Feywild and be resummoned. If he dies, he dies.

*Languages*
Greymalkin can speak Sylvan (which I think is how we've been playing him)
and has learned a limited amount of Common, which he rarely speaks. He will
gain additional competencies occasionally if you want him to pick up other
languages etc.

*Changes to Robin*
Robin gains the ability, once per day, to summon Greymalkin to your
location anywhere on Taelgar, even across planar boundaries. (basically a
limited form of teleport/plane shift). But he has no ability to disappear
from where you currently are or "escape" to the Feywild.

Robin gains the ability to communicate telepathically with Greymalkin to
any range, but not across planar boundaries.

Thoughts?

Mike
