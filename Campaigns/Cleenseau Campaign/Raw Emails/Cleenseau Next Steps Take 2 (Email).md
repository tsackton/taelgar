Hi all,

The next steps email I wrote last night might have been a bit open-ended.
Here is a simpler question:

What is the party doing *first*?

* Accompany the baroness to Embry (if you pick this, next session will pick
up pretty much right after we left off)
* Return to Cleenseau (if you pick this, I'll write an email summary of
what is going on back in the Cleenseau region and we can go from there)
* Something else (if you pick this, I'll need some idea of where you are
going)

If it helps make the decision, at the very least you can get a brief report
from Vahiya that says basically: the fey portal is palpably still present,
and Vahiya is pretty sure it still allows access, from Taelgar, to the
Feywild. But there has been no signs that it can open from the other side,
and nothing has come from the Feywild to Taelgar since the Duskhound in
February - and even then, it isn't totally clear whether the portal was
opened, or whether the Duskhound has an alternative way of getting through.

In short: you can still fall into the Feywild *from* Cleenseau Wood, but it
seems to be quite difficult to come from the Feywild *to* Cleenseau Wood
(at least for the moment).

Mike

## Mon, 6 Oct 2025 23:24:58 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Taelgar: Next Steps - Take 2

Ok, so here is my plan..

a) I'll send out a "leaving Veltor" recap email which will narratively tie
off the Baroness loose end and provide an hook for anyone who wants to add
in other conversations or actions before you leave Veltor (although feel
free to toss those out before I write the email as well)

b) Once I've written that, I'm going to send out a "1720 Spring in
Cleenseau" email, basically recapping the events that are happening in the
background of the world over the next 4 weeks or so. This will provide a
hook for you all to respond with what sorts of things you'd like to do in
the first couple of weeks of downtime, and we can go from there. I'm pretty
busy with work over the next few weeks but should be able to do some email
gaming nonetheless.

c) I'm tentatively planning for our Nov + Dec sessions to be a one-shot
style adventure, probably a dungeon crawl but maybe a wilderness adventure,
probably set in Tyrwingha but maybe set in Maseau. This adventure could
pretty easily kick off anywhere from May to August, so when and how it
starts will likely come out from the email back-and-forth in (b)

Mike
