Local archive of 5 message bodies, including quoted reply history. Proposed and revised College of Lore character features. This is a mechanics discussion, not an in-world event.

## Sun, 5 Oct 2025 22:05:47 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: College of Lore

Here is an idea to replace the bonus proficiencies (pick two of the three below):

Secret Knowledge
Once per long rest, when you make an Intelligence ability check, you may roll a Bardic Inspiration die and add it to the check. If you roll a 4 or higher on the bardic die a flash of insight accompanies the check and you learn one true fact about the subject of your check. You can regain the use of this feature by spending a Bardic Inspiration.

Impossible Insight
Once per long rest, when you or a creature within 30 feet makes an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw you mystically gain impossible knowledge: Use your Reaction to roll a d4 and add or remove it from the roll, possibly changing the outcome. You must use this feature after the roll is made but before you know the outcome. In addition you gain an impossible piece of knowledge about a nearby person or thing.

Dream Whispers
When you finish a long rest, roll a d20. On a 10 or higher, you gain a new secret in your dreams — a temporary spell or revelation: You learn one 1st-level spell from the warlock or wizard list. It counts as a bard spell for you and vanishes from your mind when you next rest.

If you roll a natural 20, you also receive an impossible vision — for the next hour, you can cast Detect Magic or Identify without components as a bonus action, as faint runes drift across your sight.


Mike

## Thu, 9 Oct 2025 13:24:33 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: College of Lore

How about this:

*Flash of Insight*
Whenever a 4 or higher is rolled on your Bardic Inspiration die for an
Intelligence ability check, whoever rolled the die learns one true fact
related to the subject of the ability check. This occurs whether or not the
ability check succeeded. The first time you make an Intelligence check
after a long rest you automatically roll your Bardic Inspiration die
without expending a Bardic Inspiration use.

Note: The true fact is not guaranteed to be useful. "The Gloomshaper has
hair" is a true fact. It just isn't necessarily a useful one. But the
better the end result, the more likely it will be that I will give you a
useful fact.

*Uncanny Knowledge*
Whenever you use your Cutting Words feature, or yourself or an ally uses a
Bardic Inspiration die on an attack roll which hits, you gain uncanny
knowledge of your opponent. You learn the resistances, vulnerabilities,
immunities, and strongest saving throw of the enemy who was hit (or who you
targeted with Cutting Words).

*Mystical Learning*
Note: Is this too complicated? It seems like it might be fun.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, roll d100 and add your bard levels. If you
get over 99, roll twice. I'll then make a chart with random spells, with
the higher numbers slightly better and the lower numbers worse (over 90 is
probably 2nd or 3rd level). (There are at least 50 first level spells, so
probably I'll make each two numbers a spell). Whatever spell you roll is a
bard spell for you until you Long Rest again.

Mike

## Sat, 11 Oct 2025 12:14:10 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: College of Lore

A simpler approach for *Flash of Insight * would be: Whenever you succeed
on an Intelligence ability check that uses your Bardic Inspiration (rather
than 4 or higher).

Actually that suggests a totally different idea, based on *Mote of
Potential* from College of Creation:

*Mote of Lore *

A D20 Test succeeds with the use of a Bardic Inspiration die a bonus effect
applies depending on the type of D20 Test:

*Attack Roll*: You learn the resistances, vulnerabilities, immunities, and
strongest Saving Throw of the enemy.
*Saving Throw*: You learn the DC of the save, and whether the ability has
additional uses.
*Intelligence Ability Check*: You learn one true fact about the target of
your check.
*Charisma Ability Check:* You learn one fear, hope, desire, or other
emotion about the target of your check.
*WIsdom Ability Check*: You learn one true intuition about the area,
person, or animal that is the target of your check.
*Constitution, Dexterity, or Strength Check*: You gain a flash of knowledge
about yourself, and may roll the Bardic Inspiration die twice and take the
better result.

This would replace the bonus proficiencies entirely.

So basically pick either:

Just *Mote of Lore *or *Uncanny Knowledge *and *Mystic Learning*.

(If you pick the second one, I will probably still give you random flashes
of knowledge when anyone adds a Bardic Inspiration on Intelligence checks,
but it will be more DM fiat than guaranteed.
