Subject: Crowther boys notes

I was gonna try to stat them fully but I decided that that was too much work, so here’s a sketch instead.  (Don’t know if any of this would help with Llew’s letter but I might as well write down how they are in my brain.)


The Crowther boys are about six months older than Celyn (autumn birthdays to his spring).

Ever since they hatched the plan to Someday Leave The Village the Crowther boys have leaned into their twin status, because they figured that it was a performance schtick nobody else could do (at least not without magic) and thus made them a more hireable act.  They were gently thwarted that Celyn could always tell who was who without problem, because they thought it would be funny if he didn’t.

Both of them are good-looking and charming, and neither is particularly inclined towards monogamy (but could probably agree to it in the right circumstances); both would be happy to have a primary partner and an assortment of flings as the impulse struck.  Llew’s relationships have tended to be longer-term than Ellis’s (aside from Celyn).  Llew is straight; Ellis is bi/pan.  Ellis is a bit of a dreamy-eyed romantic and really he would like a slightly troubled partner to take care of and be a reliable stable point for; if Llew settles down it’ll be with someone that matches his practicality.

They are both caring and generous people, though Ellis is more expressive about it than Llew.  Llew chafes a bit more at the expectations of village life than Ellis does; he is also aware that since he also has instrumentcrafting skills he has more options in the world out there than his brother does, but he remains ferociously loyal to his twin.  Ellis is enough more easygoing that people get startled when he suddenly goes “I will not do that” about something, while Llew will nope out of things that annoy him pretty directly.

The general consensus in the village is that they’re nice boys, but they’re not going to amount to much because they won’t knuckle down and do the work, though at least they’re not totally shiftless and go earn money in the market town on the regular.  Ellis is fully aware of this reputation and tries not to be bothered by it (it fuels his ‘when we make our fortune as performers’ fantasy life), Llew is mostly annoyed that people keep trying to get him to do work he doesn’t like and hasn’t worried about the social implications.



The Crowther boys are a pair of mirror twins (Ellis is the left-handed one, Llew the right-handed one; Celyn can spot the handedness tells and they never figured out this was a big part of what he twigged to) and thus have fairly similar physical stats; Ellis has better dex, Llew has better con.  While they’re both very pretty, Ellis has better charisma in practice because he is much better at people, where Llew can be a little gruff and snarky.  Ellis has reasonably high wisdom, where Llew has reasonably high intelligence.

Both of them are proficient in Performance and Persuasion.  Ellis has Insight and Slight of Hand; Llew likes knowledge skills.  Ellis is both a singer and plays the crwth (“Crowther” is derived from this instrument); Llew favors wind instruments like flutes and simple horns.  Llew is also proficient with woodworking tools and dabbles in instrument crafting.

They speak Tyrwinghan and Sembaran.  There’s an outside chance Ellis picked up a little Sylvan but he is not fluent.  If there are any songs in other languages that kick around culturally, Llew would want to learn those languages; Ellis is happy being phonetic.  (If there are cultural songs in Sylvan that ups the odds that both of them speak it substantially, though.)


So:

Ellis, very high charisma, notable wis/dex, everything else closer to average. He is stronger than Celyn but not by much (so like 11 if it’s enough for numbers).  Proficiencies include performance, persuasion, insight, sleight of hand.    Observant feat giving him expertise in Insight. Tool competencies in singing and crwth; same wine village competency as Celyn. (Eloquence Bard.)

Llew, high charisma/int, some dex and con, the rest closer to average.  Keen Mind feat, no opinion about what proficiency he picks up there.  Proficiencies include performance, persuasion, history, society. Tool competencies in flutes/recorders, simple horns, and woodworking tools; same wine village competency as Celyn.  (Lore Bard.)
