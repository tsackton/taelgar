# Celyn Familiar and Feats Discussion (Email)

Local archive of all 3 message bodies in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. Long-term character-build ideas; not established events.

## Thu, 25 Jan 2024 18:54:10 -0500 — Kiya Nicoll <darkhawk@mindspring.com>

Subject: Long term pondering: feats

I have been trying to figure out my feats plan for future Celyn (“what do I take to maximize his raw chaos?”) and aside from the Eldritch Adept at level 15 plan (I am amused by how Alter Self was nerfed a bit between 3.5 and 5) I have been eyeing Ritual Caster.

Ritual Caster needs 13 int or wis to take; if I took it for wizard would you want me to have the 13 int on it?  I’m assuming yes, that makes sense.  (But with the houserule of getting one statbump at feat levels could I take the int *with* ritual caster to make the qualification?)

I am contemplating Celyn with some sort of fey creature familiar, you see.  Because there must be more mayhem.  That would be potentially the 8 rogue/3 cleric feat point, so level 11; for the one I get at either 6 or 7 I want to nab a half-feat so I can bump both dex and wis to 18.


Yes I am choosing my feat outlay on the basis of “what will open the doors to the most potential for chaos”.  It is after all Celyn’s holy obligation.


- Kiya

## Thu, 25 Jan 2024 21:12:43 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: Long term pondering: feats

You want Ritual Caster to get Find Familiar? If you are interested in a fey
creature as a familiar, you also have a 13th level subclass feature for Fey
Trickster we haven't made up. That's further away though --- but some kind
of fey familiar ability feels right at home in the Fey Trickster
progression.

Alternatively you could take the RAW Ritual Caster for wizard, which should
really have a 13 Int, but would be fine for you to take the stat bump same
level as the feat-grant.

As a different alternative, a fey-flavored ritual caster feat could be
fun... something tied to summoning/conjuration ritual spells rather than a
specific spell list. A ritual "in" to the Feywild that lets you
entice stuff out of you... letting you cast Find Familiar, Unseen Servant
(but, *fey* unseen servant, so you know, be careful how you ask it do
stuff), and and maybe eventually Phantom Steed, Magic Mouth and a few
others.

Mike
