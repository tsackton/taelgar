# Paladin Changes (Email)

Local archive of all 6 message bodies in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. Proposed and discussed paladin rules for Robin.

## Sun, 10 Dec 2023 20:26:16 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Paladin changes

Dan,

Here are the changes to Paladin I'm proposing you take @ 5th level. Mostly
from the 2024 D&D Next Playtest.

1) Remove Divine Sense. Instead, you can use once use of your channel
divinity to detect any fiend, celestial, or undead within 60 feet. Lasts
for 10 minutes.

2) Remove Divine Smite. Instead, you have a set of smite spells always
prepared. Once per long rest you can cast a smite spell without using a
slot. The starting smite spells are:

*Divine Smite: *2d8 radiant damage + 1d8 per casting level above 1st.
Trigger: Bonus Action when you hit.
*Thundering Smite: *2d6 thunder damage + 1d6 per casting level above 1st.
Enemy makes STR saving throw or is pushed back 10' and knocked prone.
Trigger: Bonus action when you hit.

At 5th level you get:
*Shining Smite (2nd level spell): *2d6 radiant damage + 1d6 per casting
level above 2nd. Target loses invisibility, sheds bright light in a 5'
radius and attacks against it have advantage. Trigger: Bonus action when
you hit; lasts 1 min (requires concentration)

I also was thinking of tweaking your oath powers a bit. You rarely use the
battlefield control one and the oath of comfort technically requires people
to be below 1/2 health, which we missed in the game today. But broadly I
wonder how satisfying a power it is, because it doesn't scale with level so
it is going to feel a bit weak at 12th level when you still just get 1d6
healing.

Here are a few ideas:

Instead of Oath of Comfort here are a few ideas:

1) During a short rest, if your lay on hands pool is less than 1/2 full,
you can restore it to 1/2.

OR

2) You can cast *Healing Word *once, at level equal to 1/3 your paladin
level, without expending a spell slot. You regain your expended use on a
long rest.

OR

3) When you do damage to an enemy with a smite, you can expend one use of
your channel divinity to gain 2d8 + your level temp hit points, which you
can distribute amongst your allies (including yourself)  within 60' as you
see fit

We could also keep it how it is for now.


Thoughts?

Mike
