Subject: Gareth's Ring

A short interlude, but you might want to investigate.

Gareth, as I think I mentioned, was wearing, when he was captured, a small
ring:

[image: image.png]

Izgil investigated the ring, and determined a few things about it:

It has a single use, and loses its magic after it is used. As long as the
ring is touching your bare skin, merely thinking the command word causes
you to vanish and reappear anywhere within 60 feet.  This does not have to
be somewhere you can see. The magic also seems a bit hidden, it is hard to
tell exactly how it was made.

Izgil senses one thing about ring that strikes him as slightly odd though:
there is some hint of evocation magic about it that he can't quite place,
like maybe there is some hidden element to it. It seems like a live wire,
dangerous and sparking.

It does not require attunement.

Matt: You can research this further either by giving me ideas on what you
want to do, or just an Arcana check, or both.

Mike

As a small roleplay note:

In and around the discussion of Gareth himself, Celyn is inclined to have some conversations with Viepuck about the man’s clothes.  He’s clearly leaning in a little on Viepuck’s superior *tailoring* knowledge for the explanations, but there’s a lot of, “Look at that cut and the quantity of embroidery compared to Elizabeth’s clothes, how he’s got something that had more work put into it to reinforce his position of authority within his scam, his right-hand man was fancier than average but less fancy.”  This is entirely likely to ramble off into other directions about different ways of presenting authority with clothing choices, and the intersection of ‘display of wealth’ with ‘presentation of authority’, and such.

Basically: Celyn has a lot of rather well-informed opinions about how clothing choices shape social interactions and what making deliberate decisions about that can do for how interactions work for a man who dresses like a moderately well-off peasant.  This is knowledge that he thinks can be useful to Viepuck for purposes of social infiltration games, and is entirely likely to have at least one tangent into ‘oh yes this particular cut will make you look taller/more broadshouldered/whatever else might be appealing to a twelve-year-old who wants to be taken seriously’.
