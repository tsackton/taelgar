# Celyn Fey Trickster Discussion (Email)

Local archive of all 3 message bodies in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. Kiya and Mike's ideas for Celyn's subclass; not all proposals were necessarily adopted.

## Sat, 23 Sep 2023 14:47:42 -0400 — Kiya Nicoll <darkhawk@mindspring.com>

Subject: Poking at character subclass stuff for Celyn

Sending my notes on this now to at least get them not just in my head, meanwhile.





Feytouched Trickster - Arcane trickster variant with a fae focus, requires Fey Touched feat


Caster Stat: Whatever was boosted with Fey Touched (wisdom, for Celyn)


The Feytouched Trickster, rather than developing sorceric abilities straight out, is more warlock/circle-of-dreams druid/feywid wanderer ranger adjacent; the fey have decided to bestow on them more power, for whatever arcane, mysterious, or perplexing reasons the fey have to do that sort of thing.

Instead of the Mage Hand enhancements from Arcane Trickster, the Feytouched Trickster gets a Warlock invocation at 3rd level and another at 13th.  These cannot depend on pact features.

The fey favor transmutation, illusion, and enchantment spells, and at least half the spells the Feytouched Trickster knows must fall into these schools.  (This includes cantrips.) Spells known/per day progresses as Arcane Trickster.  Spells come from the wizard caster list plus the spells available to Archfey-patron warlocks.

I am poking at the ninth and 17th level rogue features.  I kinda want to nab the “Hidden Paths” feature from the Circle of Dreams druid for ninth - yes, it’s a level sooner, but it’s close, and because the Fey Touched feat already grants Misty Step 1/day a character would already have something of an aptitude with the fey paths built in.

I feel like 17 should be something cool and unique which means trying to come up with something sensible is tricky.  Also the odds of getting there are low so there is time to think about it.

(I stare wistfully at the 2nd and 6th level powers out of the Circle of Dreams druid but honestly those are learning into Celyn’s cleric rather than his rogue and also too much potentially stealing someone else’s schtick [laugh].)



Chris notes that fey stuff often involves charm powers or immunity to charm powers and misty step.  I have the misty step covered with the ninth level power, in theory.  Archfey warlocks get immunity to charm at 10th; feywild wanderer rangers get advantage on charm checks at 7th and can do funky stuff, and get to add wisdom to charisma checks at 3rd; circle of dreams druids have no particular charm effects bonus.

(Damn, feywild wanderers get some nifty and slightly overpowered stuff.  Add wisdom to charisma *and* a skill proficiency at third level?  Advantage vs. charm/fear *and* can charm/fear an enemy if someone else passes a charm/fear check at 7th?  Sheesh!  I feel like I’m trying to balance my rogue ideas to not be OP and then there’s this in published material.)

I don’t know where anything like that would go, if it does go.


Oh hey Beyond lets me build homebrew subclasses I will fuck around with that for a bit and at least get some of this pinned down pending your thoughts, being able to program it into Beyond will at least make the character sheet managing work if we sort it out.

## Sat, 23 Sep 2023 17:48:13 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: Poking at character subclass stuff for Celyn

Is the idea that cleric is intended to be kinda a small dip, more for
flavor, and so the core of the character is basically "Fey-Flavored Rogue"?

Look at the Archfey Patron warlock features from here:
https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/ua/ph-playtest7/tsgOb3llF22AL0nU/UA2023-PH-Playtest7.pdf?icid_source=house-ads&icid_medium=crosspromo&icid_campaign=playtest7.
They seem like they might be a good fit, although your fey-touched feat
gives you misty step already  - but honestly, maybe that is just +1 to
number of times you can cast.

It could work something like:

Fey Trickster

Spells as Arcane Trickster. (But note with 2 cleric levels you get better
spell progression than that as multiclass shares spell slots)
Level 3 power: As Steps of the Fey from the PDF above. (Replace Cha
modifier with Wis). Possibly tweak the step options a bit.
Level 9 power: You can cast Misty Step as a reaction + cast it as a touch
spell (similar to Druid Hidden step). Add one or two step options (maybe
Disappearing Step from the PDF above, maybe 2x range, to more exactly
mirror Hidden Path)
Level 13 power: Something with charm/beguiling. Maybe Beguiling Defense
from the PDF above. Maybe something like the Fey Wanderer 7th level ability
beefed up a bit. We have time to figure it out :)
Level 17 power: Who knows?
