## Fri, 21 Jun 2024 21:19:03 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Lunar Sorcerer Feature

Here's a basic idea, based around the idea of "you can manipulate lunar
magic more easily"

*Pick one from:*
* Any metamagic used for a spell with the word "Moon" in the name costs 1
sorcery point less than normal. You can apply this feature a number of
times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
OR
* You can apply any metamagic that would normally cost 1 sorcery point to a
"Moon" spell, even if you don't normally know that metamagic option. You
can do this once per long rest.

*Plus*
* You can expend a number of sorcery points equal to 1/2 the spell level
(rounded up) to be able to cast a spell in the "wrong" lunar phase  (still
requires a spell slot)
AND
* You can expend a number of sorcery points in the table below to cast
spell in the "right" lunar phase, without using a spell slot:

1st: 1
2nd: 2
3rd: 4
4th: 5
5th: 6

(This is basically 1 less than normal)

Thoughts?
