In general, although there are 3 armies (of the South, West, and North)
they are more organized around guarding the borders, rather than covering
incidents in any interior location. Usually this would be the
responsibility of the baronial guard, and various lord's household guards.
Why exactly everyone is saying "need more troops" is a bit unclear,
especially given the limits of sending. So there is no army that is
"supposed" to cover this area. That said, you might imagine either the
garrison at Cleenseau (of the Army of the West) or the garrison at Fellburn
(of the Army of the South) as being well positioned to address a problem in
the hills of Aveil. Of course the Fellburn garrison of the Army of the
South is a bit scattered, but Ida has been recruiting and has a pretty
strong army here.  At this point, between recruits, the remarkably few
losses from the undead, and the stragglers from the Army of the South (who
have mostly stayed, if I recall?), Ida has almost 140 troops, which is
significant. And many of these folks are veterans, at least a bit, at this
point (very much due to the party's efforts).

She could dispatch a force of 20 calvary or 20 calvary and 40 foot soldiers
pretty quickly, without stripping the garrison at Cleenseau bare. Or more
if you thought it was worth stripping the garrison. It's about 10-12 days
to Veltor for her troops, or a bit more than half that if it's just light
cavalry (and the party could probably even go a little faster than that, if
you rushed). If you convince them, the Army of the South stragglers are
another group that is somewhat cohesive - almost 20 soldiers. And it would
also be possible to recruit along the Great South Road between Dallet and
Rinburg, where you'd turn north. Although that slows you down.

Of course, if more undead arise at the *next* new moon there is a
concern, but there is actually 24 days until the next new moon. If you were
efficient that's actually just enough time to potentially be back in
Cleenseau, but you'd have to move quickly, and you'd have to know where to
fight, not spend a week finding the problem. Then there are also rumors the
king is raising a new Army of the Interior to try to deal with this - you
are not sure what to make of them, of course.

Mike
