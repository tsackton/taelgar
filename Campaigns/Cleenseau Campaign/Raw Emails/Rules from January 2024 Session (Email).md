Silvery Barbs is very powerful, but thematically I really like abilities that can be used for the sake of teammates and emphasize character support and make for cool rescue moments. It's the kind of skill I encourage use of in LARPs because rather than just doing damage it promotes the right kind of supportive sportsmanship. For that reason I'd prefer not to see its effectiveness reduced.

## Sun, 7 Jan 2024 22:38:47 -0500 — Artan Eter <artan.eter@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Rules stuff from last session

Per rules as written, we have already officially reduced its effectiveness considerably - it is currently a “impose disadvantage” spell, which is handy but still not a guarantee.  RAW it imposes disadvantage and then grants you advantage, which is much more potent.  It’s quite so powerful because it was being used on Robin with a 20 AC, so was turning 15% hit chance into like 3%, but that can only happen so many times.  If that was used on someone for a spell save (which were 13 for me during the fight) that still forces the reroll but there is much different math.  Part of the power came from where it was used.

Not overpowered, but also remember that opponents are not idiots and if we fight a group with a leveled caster then having them casting it regularly is also sensible.

I specifically didnt’ take that because it eats spell slots and sorcerers have many many and warlocks do not.  (And for the misty step feat, have a different variant and that is how I picked up goodberry).

Also, it is a lvl1 spell?  So technically if used as a reaction then next round the caster shouldnt’ be able to use another leveled spell because that is 2?  Leveled reaction spells confuse me.

## Mon, 8 Jan 2024 05:38:54 -0500 — Matthew Rand <rand.matt@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Rules stuff from last session

I'm hesitant to nerf the range as already there are a lot of actions that
put enemies outside of 60 feet. A spellcaster or archer at range is already
outside our ability to affect since it's range to the aggressor and not
range to the target. I think this was just a perfect scenario. We had door
to hold, a very tanky front liner and a compact space. If this was an open
field it would have been far less useful.
