Subject: Taelgar character

At a high level I think the basic idea of a kid with a mysterious “humans are weird lets investigate them” patron works and seems pretty fun.

A few notes/thoughts though…

First, are you sure you want to play a kid? The campaign timeline is likely to be in-game between 2 and 5 years. So if you start as a 9 or 10 year old you end up a young teenager at best. It’s inevitable that you will be revealed, likely relatively early in the game. That is, if you are hoping no one every finds out you are a kid… that’s unlikely. Disguise self works relatively well but it lasts an hour not a day.

Being a kid is totally fine with me and I’m totally happy to bend reality a bit and let you be a very precocious kid — but the world will mostly react to you as a kid.

Second, Cleanseau, where the campaign starts, is a medieval trading and garrison town of about 1200. That may not be big enough for your “urban rat” vision, but it would be pretty straightforward to come up with a reason why your character would have left a larger city and came to Cleanseau.

Third, I like the basic idea of the patron but would likely want to change a significant number of the specifics. Does that work for you? Or are you attached to the specific details you sent me? I think I can make the flavor work, but the patron would be somewhat more mysterious and non-interventionist than you’ve outlined. There is a specific magical order to Taeglar that I don’t want to just totally ignore that the patron would need to fit into…

Let me know what you think of the above…

Backstory:

Easy personal motivation for leaving is hearing of some of the adventurers who helped rescue him years ago and trying to find them.

——————

Viepuck doesn’t remember when his parents died, or what caused it.  He has some vague memories of them, selling…. something.  Though not sure if it was their shop or not.  He was told by the first orphanage that they were killed by a bandit.  He was taken in at that orphanage around age 3 and stayed there until around age 5, when funding fell through and they had to close, some children being passed on to another orphanage (rumored to beat the children more, some children, including him, wouldn’t go and fled to the streets).  He doesn’t know if the story is true, or if he has any other siblings or family.  An older boy, Tal (~8), took to him as a friend and older-brother-figure at the time.  They spent a hard several months before falling in with a small group of thieves and pickpockets.  It wasn’t good work or care, but it was a reliable place to sleep and some protection as part of the group.

After about 2 years with the thieves, several of the older ones were caught by the city watch and the group fell apart.  Viepuck got picked up by a new and better-funded orphanage that promised to pass children off to tradespeople or otherwise find them work.  Children moved through the orphanage with some regularity, many being gone within the year.  Tal got a job running messages and moved on, they ran into each other for a bit before he drifted off with the new life.

Eventually Viepuck was sent with a group of children to interview with a local wizard.  All of them were taken in, fed, bathed, dressed in uniform and brought to be instructed in assisting a ritual.  Then they were subdued by the wizard’s minions and restrained as impending sacrifices.  The wizard had been working to channel energy off some unknown entity in order to power some kind of object?  Or himself?  Viepuck wasn’t really sure and it didn’t matter.

Soon after the wizard opened the portal he began the sacrifices.  Children were slowly murdered while he chanted and then cast them into the portal.  It probably went on for hours.  The adventurers attacked while Viepuck was being prepared.  He still has part of the sacrifice-sigil as a scar on his back.  The wizard’s minions engaged them at first but the wizard was distracted.  He hesitated, casting a couple spells back at them.

Viepuck could only remember thinking, hoping for any way to escape, being willing to do anything.  It hadn’t been an easy life, but it was still early and there was still a chance of…. something.  Anything.  Just not being cast into some kind of magic portal.  It was about then that Viepuck’s mind cracked open slightly for a moment as something asked a question, or offered help, or…. something.  It was big, and beyond experience, but at that point anything to help and Viepuck could only thing yes, yes help, anything.

Back tentacles erupted from around him breaking the restraints and knocking the wizard and a minion away.  As Viepuck turned to flee he froze as an echo in his mind said NO/tyEt.  He went to help the others escape as well in the chaos.  One of the adventurers advanced on the startled wizard and shoved him into the portal.  Soon after the battle concluded.

Several of the rescuers had been killed.  While they assessed the damage and any surviving minions attempted to flee the portal slowly closed.  One of them came to Viepuck and helped free the other children, asking where they had come from.  Viepuck would take days to be able to understand words again so didn’t know what the surviving adventurers had said, or done.  During the chaos of cleanup he found a small apple-sized iridescent sphere near the portal, and got the mental push “yOurs, tAke, keEp” so snatched it up and with years of developed skill on the streets, disappeared into the disorder.

He later found out that most of the surviving children were taken back to the orphanage, which became sponsored by one of the adventurers after the colluding staff members were rooted out, disposed of and replaced.  “Job placement” slowed down but still continued.

That night Viepuck dreamed of his entire life, pre-birth to that moment dreaming, all shuffling together and interwoven with image-concepts of some large creature bent in ways he didn’t understand, and a sense of being pet on the head or some other awkward attempt at comfort?  At the end of this he understood that he had made a deal with this entity and now represented its interests (largely not defined yet) in this world, which it had just been introduced to.

Viepuck found another small band of thieves to join, but didn’t like them when one of them was drunk and tried having his way with another of the children.  He was killed rather quickly and the group scattered, Viepuck taking charge of the younger ones.  Eventually they made their way to another orphanage, with Viepuck hiding his powers mostly and the children who know helping cover for his occasional nightly excursions.  He needs to send dreams to his master and too much of that on any one person can damage them, so he will spend some nights on the streets or breaking into small homes, never with intent to harm residents, but in need of new dreams.

When he acquires money he will donate some to the orphanage anonymously.  He has taken to doing less pickpocketing and more street performance with a few of the other children, supplementing with illusions to help them do seemingly impossible tricks.  It pays well enough to keep them housed and fed.

Viepuck has always been in the city and never left.  It is home, all of it, and a creature unto itself, sprawling and incomprehensible and full of layers folding in on themselves, like his master.

The iridescent apple-sized sphere is an egg and a connection to his master, never is more than a few feet away.  If stolen or lost, has a sense of “that way” towards it but also headaches increasing in intensity the longer it is gone.  Will hatch to familiar.

Viepuck hasn’t tried to talk to Tal a month before the incident and they have largely fallen out of touch.  What would they say?  He thinks Tal is still in town somewhere.  Probably?

Viepuck has considered contacting the adventurers that helped save him.  Again though, to what end?  Though their minds must be filled with interesting lives, so perhaps his master would be pleased to learn of them.

When there is time alone, Viepuck often just hides somewhere.  Alone and hidden is quiet and safe and free of demands for the moment, calm.
