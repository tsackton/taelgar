Bastion-SimManor-Focused-on-people:

Here is my proposed revamp of the current Bastion facilities you have,
focused on introducing some better options and clarity on what you can do.

This does NOT introduce "morale" or "economy" but if we agree those should
be codified then I will think on how to do it.

General rules:

*Craft PC Item* is meant to give you a means to acquire equipment easily
and cheaply. It is NOT meant to generate gold or give you a means for
stocking an army or otherwise creating a large number of goods for
distribution. The rules are written very generously and simply for that
purpose and I trust you will stick to the spirit even though there is no
in-game reason why it should be easier to create plate armor for a PC than
to sell.

*Trade*: In general, I will reward more in-game and detailed descriptions
with small bonuses. For example, if I tell you there are a lot more
Tyrwinghans about, and you say: "This month the workshop will focus on
producing fine wine glasses to sell to the influx of wine-drinking
Tyrwinghans" that might be worth an extra 10-15% of the standard gold
amount.

*Smithy*
Bastion Actions
*Craft PC Item*: You issue the Craft order and craft one or more pieces of
equipment for use by a PC or named NPC. Any item that can be reasonably
made by a blacksmith or weaponsmith can be crafted. You must pay 50% of the
cost of the item for materials. If the total cost of all of the items is
less than 20 GP, you may take an additional bastion action this turn.

*Trade*: You issue the Trade order. Your smithy produces goods for sale.
You may spend up to 25 GP to acquire metal and similar materials and you
earn by 2x the cost of the materials.

Special Actions
*Mend*: After spending a Long Rest in your smithy, you may repair a single
piece of equipment as if the *Mending *cantrip had been cast on it.

*Workshop*
Bastion Actions
*Craft PC Item*: You issue the Craft order and craft one or more pieces of
equipment for use by a PC or named NPC. Any item that can be reasonably
made by your workshop tools can be crafted. You must pay 50% of the cost of
the item for materials. If the total cost of all of the items is less than
20 GP, you may take an additional bastion action this turn.

*Trade*: You issue the Trade order. Your workshop produces goods for sale.
You may spend up to 10 GP to acquire materials and earn up to 4x the cost
of materials.

*Sanctuary*
Bastion Actions
*Craft PC Item*: You issue the craft order and craft up to three holy
symbol's imbued with the power of the deity your sanctuary is dedicated
to.  You may take an additional bastion action this turn.

Special Actions
*Create Healing Charm*: After spending a Long Rest in your bastion you gain
a charm that allows you to cast *Healing Word* at 1st level. This charm
lasts 7 days or until used.
*Charm of Hope*: After spending a Long Rest in your bastion, you gain a
charm that grants you an bonus 1d4 on any Saving Throw and Attack roll you
make. The charm lasts 7 days or until used.

You may only have a single charm from your bastion at a time.

*Barracks*
Bastion Actions
*Recruit*: You recruit up to 4 additional guards for your bastion, subject
to the capacity limits of your barracks.
*Train:* Your guards train the populace in the arts of war. You gain the
equivalent of one bastion defender, which represents 5 peasants trained as
militia.

Special Actions
*Weapons Training*: If you have a proficiency with a specific weapon, you
may spend 21 days training with your guards and switch this proficiency to
a different specific weapon type.

*Armory*
Bastion Actions
*Trade*: You purchase weapons and armor for your guards and bastion
defenders. This costs 50 GP plus 50 GP per defender, or half as much if you
have a smithy. Well-equipped bastion defenders are more effective. A
"militia" defender (unit of 5) can be equipped for the same cost.
Alternatively, you can sell weapons and armor for the same cost you
purchased them for.

Special Actions
*Repair*. If you spend a Long Rest in your Armory, you can repair any
weapon or armor as if the *Mending* cantrip was cast on it.

(note: I'd like to think of some better armory options but haven't yet..)

*Stable*
Bastion Actions
*Trade*: When you spend a bastion turn trading, you order your stablemaster
to buy and sell animals. They can sell any animals in your bastion at the
start of the turn, or purchase available animals for sale. When selling an
animal, you can do so for 20% more than the purchase price. Note that the
actual animals that are available for sale is determined by me, it won't
always be the case you can buy and sell several warhorses or other fine
animals, although sometimes it will.

Special Actions
*Train*: Your stablemaster and stablehands spend their spare time training
animals. After a Beast that can serve as a mount spends 14 days in your
Stable, all Animal Handling or Riding checks made with respect to it have
Advantage.
Options would be Trade. Not sure if anything else makes sense?

*Library*
Bastion Actions
*Research*. You order your library staff to research a topic. You learn up
to three true facts about
some legend, event, or location, or a person of significance.

Special Actions
*Translate*. If you provide a written document in an unknown tongue, the
library can transcribe it with 7 days of effort. Note: some languages may
not be able to be deciphered by your library staff.

*Garden*
Type:
Your garden has a type: Food, Herbs, Luxury. You can also choose to have
two types or all three.

Bastion Actions
*Harvest.* You order your garden staff to harvest goods from your garden.
What you get depends on the type:

* Food: Food for one person for 100 days. If there are 2 types, this is
reduced to 40 days. If there are three types, this is reduced to 25 days.
* Herbs: Sufficient material to make a healing potion. If there are 2
types, a healing potion requires two bastion turns. If there are three
types, it requires 3 bastion turns.
* Luxury goods: Sufficient luxury foods and perfumes and other goods to
earn 30 GP. If there are two types, this is reduced to 10 GP. If there are
three types, this is reduced to 5 GP.

Special Actions.
*Recover*. If you spend a Long Rest in your Garden, your level of
exhaustion reduces by one more than normal.

This is still rough draft, welcome any feedback!
