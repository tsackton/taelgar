… okay I just rubberducked this a bit at Jen and there is a lot of overthinking going on here.  I can simplify!

The Garden produces a combination of food, healing supplies, and stuff for direct sale.  The food will go into the inn kitchen when there is an inn, and thus be indirect sale; the healing supplies go into an apothecary, and thus are indirect sale; the stuff for direct sale is direct sale.

Maybe we model the first turn as putting half the garden into inventory (rations and healer’s kits, effectively, for setup for other things), the rest for sale, and subsequent turns can either all be sale (direct or indirect)?  Or we could handwave startup costs  and just have it all be for sale, just more varied in what’s present than the straight-up rules as written.

## Tue, 28 Oct 2025 17:41:26 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Bastion Turn 1 - Quick Question(s) For Everyone

Here is what I'm going to have the Stable (Bastion) do, unless there are any objections.

Building a 10 stall stable (non-Bastion) is going to be around 10 gold. That will provide room for party mounts plus the 4 riding horses currently in the Stable which will be transferred there for town business. That calculation is based on 10 10x10 stalls plus generous room for a hallway suitable to large creatures and a couple extra rooms, and then I doubled all that to assume generous extra space for feed, equipment, etc. necessary to support a stable. That frees the Stable up for trading operations in the future. For turn 1:

The 4 riding horses will stay in the Stable for 14 days, which is the required time for them to gain the Advantage on Animal Handling checks upgrade.

I will take the 1000 gp loan from Catherine de Brune, and have the Stable purchase 2 War Horses (or general top quality horses, whatever you want to call them) for a total of 800 gp. Next Bastion turn these can be traded for a 20% profit. The goal will be to have the Stable trading the best quality mounts possible.

Note: I will respond to this shortly. Here's my plan:

The first bastion turn is basically through the end of May. This is
basically taking into account all of the time setting up buildings,
recruiting people, etc. Afterwards it is one turn a month (the rules is one
per 7 days but I think one/month is more realistic and suits the downtime
vibe better).

So the next bastion turn will be June, then July, etc. This means that you
will have 5 total bastion turns before the Queen's audience in October, to
the extent you want to plan out what you want to do. But some of these will
be after the next adventure starts.
