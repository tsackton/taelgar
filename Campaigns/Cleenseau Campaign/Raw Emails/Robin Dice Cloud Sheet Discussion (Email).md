## Fri, 26 Jul 2024 18:53:17 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Robin - New Character Sheet

Dan,

Here's what I made as a character sheet for Robin on Dice Cloud:
https://dicecloud.com/character/qspypSSq7fK9wPjZs

(If you join Dice Cloud I can transfer it to you.)

This is intended to do a much better job of capturing all the house rules
and customizations I've done than your D&D Beyond character sheet does, and
should be substantially the same with a few notes:

1) I did not add your Inspiring Speaker, as it is duplicative of
Persuasion. If you want to be expert-level at public speaking you can. You
have 4 unused competencies, of which I might suggest you take one for
Tyrwinghan language and three for whatever you want (one of which could be
Inspiring Speaker).

2) The whole new lay on hands thing is much more clearly laid out. You
should note the various actions on the "Actions tab" including Cure
Blindness/Deafness/Paralysis.

3) You still have one lay on hands power to choose. You can see the options
in Dicecloud by clicking on the "Lay on Hands Powers" slot on the Build tab

4) You picked Bless for your Fey touched spell on D&D Beyond, but you
already have that as a always-prepared Paladin spell. You might want to
pick something else.

5) Note the change I made to Divine Sense. It should feel more sensible
now. It detects the presence of any undead, even if you can't see them, but
doesn't pinpoint their location. Additionally, it lets you sense whether
any creature you can see is undead/celestial/fiend.

6) I haven't added items yet. Will do that when I get a chance.

Let me know what you think

Mike
