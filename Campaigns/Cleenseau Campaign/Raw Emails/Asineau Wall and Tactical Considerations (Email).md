## Wed, 15 Oct 2025 22:01:10 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: [Thantos] Taelgar: Asineau Wall and Tactical Considerations

I'm going to nerd out a bit on tactics/strategy here (I like thinking about this stuff), all input/ideas are welcome but I realize other people may not care.

A quick survey of the map shows the following:

Roughly 65 hexes of that need coverage, consisting of walls and three gates along the road (on the northeast and northwest corners of the town) and at the river. At 150 GP per hex that's 9750 GP baseline. Breaks down to:

42 hexes of landside wall/gates, 6300 GP (first priority)
23 hexes of riverside wall/gate, 3450 GP (after the landside sections are done)

That helps defend from any low-scale attack, i.e. bandits, hobgoblin raiding parties, piles of skeletons/zombies, fey dominated beasts, etc. In short, anything that's not some kind of supernatural opponent, fireball wizard, or a proper army with siege equipment. Yes, someone could shoot arrows from that hill to the west, but you can avoid that by going in your house, and unless they have the forementioned fireball wizard, fire arrows are Hollywood nonsense that are actually completely useless at something like trying to set a house on fire. At the very least it slows down any hostiles until some kind of help can show up.

Speaking of sieges...the village cannot stand siege against an actual force with siege equipment more advanced than ladders as it is because it is really badly placed with that hill and cliff (assuming I'm reading the map right) to the west, that's in the worst possible spot. If you can build anything that throws rocks and stick it on that hill, you own the surrounding area. The only way to do a proper full defense would be to put a fort or fortified tower on top of that hill. I'm assuming the land and road slope upwards going west or the topography doesn't make sense. If I've read the map wrong and the land slopes down going west, that changes the calculous.

But perhaps there's a solution I haven't thought of, and I'd be curious if anyone else thinks of anything (as said, this kind of stuff is fun for me).

Kind of a shame I can't design a castle or proper fort here...oh gods could I nerd out on castle design...maybe at a higher level? I'd...love to nerd out on building a castle...

Daniel Walters

Updating everyone, after a bunch of back and forth between myself, Matt, and Mike:

Assuming the following conditions:

The basic work being down now will create a wall effectively equivalent to #6 in the attached image, with Wall of Stone to be used later to reinforce
1 hex of work completed per day (could go a little faster then that on the completed sections but let's keep it simple)
Viepick willing to spend 3 days per week on good berries
Robin does his communal healing time
Izgil willing to contribute (let me know if the number of days he can devote are limited)
Greymalkin, Odo, and 2 guards spend a week and a half to retrieve 160 gp worth of timber, discounted at 50%

The following occurs in order of completion:

15 hexes/day of area without wall at a cost of ~94 gp (rounded and not worrying about coppers/silvers, assumes two hexes of basic fortified gates for the road at same cost as wall)
25 hexes/day of existing wall upgraded at a cost of ~57 gp
23 hexes/day of seaward wall (assuming 1 gate, likely remaining constructed as per #6 above) at a cost of ~145 gp

Total cost is 376 gp, work to be completed in 63 days from start or about two months
