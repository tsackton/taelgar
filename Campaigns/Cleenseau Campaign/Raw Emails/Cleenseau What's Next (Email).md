It is currently March 20, 1720 in Taelgar. The new Queen, Elaine II, has

a royal visit. So you have about six months at loose ends.

You have some options on what to do with this time, of which I've listed
what I see as the obvious ones below:

* You could rest, rebuild, settle into Robin's lordship, tinker, etc. This
would be done in email with maybe a brief recap at the start of next
session, and then next session would start with the Queen's royal visit.

* You could accompany the Baroness to Embry and assist in her trial

* You could investigate Cleenseau Wood, the portal to the feywild, and
follow up on what Vahiya knows. (You could do a lite version of this in
email via sending - this would be heading out to investigate yourselves)

* You could set off in some direction (see the map Sembara - TaelgarVerse
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara/>)
and see what happens. Obvious place to go would be Tyrwingha, but you could
also go south to the Duchy of Maseau, or really anywhere on the map.

In particular, if as *players* you want a bit of random adventuring
unconnected to a larger plot, the last one might be a good option. I can
figure out how to make it make sense to your *characters* in game, although
this works best if your characters have some interest in leaving the
Cleenseau region and if I have some idea of what type of adventure you'd be
interested in.

Izgil has several things that spring to mind with the time ahead:

1. Make very sure the Baroness is dealt with - if he feels their testimony
is needed he will feel obligated to accompany even if the guards seems
plenty competent and able to handle her danger-wise. If this seems
abundantly unnecessary and we can get confirmation from say Lenora or
someone of that fact, then Izgil will happily let this one go. He just
doesn't like to leave a job unfinished.

2. He has a fair bit of research, poking and tinkering he hasn't had time
to do. The Moon bracers are top of the list, but also the Ring and other
magical components that he would like to work with and consolidate as Mike
and I have discussed into item(s) that can grow with the character over
time. This need not take the entire 6 months, but I would like to advance
these things.

3. Probably not the highest priority, but still a nagging interest is a
visit to Tyrwingha. He has had magical contact with Ethelyn, and he doesn't
really know why. He wants to learn more about why she took an interest in
him, intervened on his behalf, and what he might be able to learn that
would be of use against future Gloomshaper incursions.

4. The "loose ends" mentioned above, in conjunction with investigating the
woods around Cleenseau all seem sort of wrapped up in his "jobs left
unfinished" mental bucket. We necessarily had to leave the area as things
Got Bigger, but there are certainly things to be sorted out there.

All of that said....

I would say in general it probably makes sense for the first thing we
officially do is travel back to Asenau and Robin to take his seat there and
sort out the messyness. We had already actually begun to do this and had
setup Asenau as a more central location to train and muster our local
militia forces and in so doing bring things back in order. But actually
having legal authority here will expand the scope beyond "order and
defense" to "long term management and sustainability".

Doing so will give those of us who have downtime priorities the ability to
do so, and set things up in a place where if/when we need to be gone we can
feel good about doing so.

After that we have a couple of options it seems on a smaller scale /
shorter adventure / one-shot type interlude before we have the meeting with
the Queen in the fall. As a player I wouldn't mind something of that
nature, having a lower stakes interlude before we resume the main plot
after we've all had a 2-3 month period of recovering etc. sounds nice.
