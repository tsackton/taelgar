For Celyn the game will open on an October evening as he finds his way into
Clenseau. It is unseasonably cold and the sky looks like rain, and he is
anxious to get somewhere warm.

There are four inns in Clenseau:

The Bandit's End: it appears boisterous and loud with laughter coming out
of a full bar. Outside the walls. Doesn't appear to have a large number of
rooms.

The Crossroads Inn: the largest and most elegant inn, set inside the walls,
looks cozy. A halfling establishment, although welcoming to others as well.

The Fox's Flagon: A three story inn that seems relatively quiet for its
size. It has a large stableyard that is mostly empty. Outside the walls.

The River's Blessing: A quiet place from which you can smell delicious
fried fish. Outside the walls, small. It has a local vibe.

Can you let me know which inn you'd pick for the night?

Mike
