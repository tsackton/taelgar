## Sun, 2 Aug 2026 18:17:52 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Magic Items

Hi all,

After the game today Matt, Chris, and I discussed magic items and Izgil's
crafting a bit. We basically decided that we'd use Izgil's crafting as a
means to provide in-game flavor for why certain iconic items of PCs keep
getting better. So starting from when you get to Orenlas, there will be an
opportunity for loot to start to be "upgrade materials" rather than things,
that Izgil can use to make things you have better.

To make that work, I'd like to know from each of you what items you want to
declare "iconic". These are the main items Izgil will be upgrading and they
are items that you think really fit with your character and that you would
not want to discard in favor of something better

You can have up to 2 iconic items per character, plus 1 for Greymalkin and
2 for the party in general. You don't have to any any iconic items, in
which case I will take it to mean you don't really feel attached to any of
your items and would be happy to toss them aside for better things.

Once an item is upgraded, it will always require attunement even if it
doesn't at the moment. You can also let me know what sort of items you'd
like to be iconic for your character, even if isn't something you have
(i.e. maybe Robin wants shield + hammer, or maybe hammer + armor?)

My assumptions are:

Robin: warhammer and he'd like either armor or shield
Izgil: gem of spell storing, bracers of the moon
Viepuck: circlet of telepath
Celyn: not sure. is silverthorn important?
Greymalkin: saddle from lenora
Party: ?? nothing jumps out

Let me know what you all think though

Mike

## Mon, 3 Aug 2026 20:06:04 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Taelgar: Magic Items

All,

A few points...

(a) the Lantern has a different mechanism for power-ups and wouldn't be
part of this calculation
(b) nothing about this changes attunement slots -- you still only have 3
(c) there is nothing here that says any of your current items *have* to be
"core" items

The vibe I am going for is more...

If Celyn found an elven rapier in some ruins outside Orenlas that was a bit
better than Silverthorn, would either Celyn or Kiya feel some regret at
having to give up Silverthorn, or would you just happily take the better
item and find a new home for Silverthorn?

If nothing you have found yet speaks to you as an item you want to have
forever, you don't have to use these slots on anything you have.

Mike

## Mon, 3 Aug 2026 20:33:40 -0400 — Matthew Rand <rand.matt@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Taelgar: Magic Items

I agree on the two items Izgil feels closest to. There are a few other
items I associate with him but they are more things that I'd like to
combine into those two items (like the Gem of Spell Storing) or which I
wouldn't be heartbroken to replace (Like his Shield of the Sibyl).

For everyone to be aware what I'm going for is making the crafting vibe
I've been going for with Izgil actually do something in game without
unbalancing things. This concept for me as a player will be very satisfying
as I will feel some payoff every time I can help your character upgrade one
of their pieces of equipment, even if in practice it's the same as if Mike
gave us a better Sword/Hammer/Wand etc.

I hope that makes sense.
