Subject: Gathering Intel

Currently we have confused the vulture-scout because it looks like Aeschara the opposing fey is marching on the bear-fey’s territory openly.  So that report will go back.  What that means…. Unsure.  He may get data that she’s dead, may not.  We may have a full-scale attack coming at us.  Or they could fortify teh position.

The harpies are terrifyingly dangerous, as if there’s 10 of them we will ALL fail charm-saves by raw attrition probability in just a couple rounds.  AOE is going to help us a lot here, as they are comparably fragile.  Fighting those AND a dusk hound will get us killed, so we need to break up his forces.  Apparently the “block your ears” strategy doesn't work.

Breaking them off into smaller groups is the best way to manage this, but how…. I think we might be committed to escorting back these people, which is going to cost us 1-2 days, and becomes a “best case we get attacked on the road by a straggler ambush party” thus decreasing the odds but putting the people in danger.

Otherwise we just stumble towards their camp?  If we could catch the harpies while roosting we might be able to AOE them that way?  But that is attacking in the dark with a party of 3 humans against fey, so Izgul will be fine and the rest of us have problems.

——

A point to our advantage is that Viepuck can cast (the mike-defined spell) Speak with Objects, which gives us 10min to interrogate the friendly-to-us newly-sentient looking glass item about any camp data we might want.

Does anyone have suggestions on what we should be asking?

* How many harpies are left?  Vultures?
Really I don’t have a good idea of what else to ask.

Part of the problem is the time/risk curve evaluation.

We know this guy’s strategy involves using the harpies to lure in humans for whatever the fuck he’s doing with them aside from enchanting them to run messages (and now we suspect he gets bonus points for fucking with Ethlenn’s preferred humans), which means finding and eliminating hunting parties as they loop out to do that is theoretically a way to whittle down the dangers.

But of course:
- he knows we’re out there again
- he doesn’t know if his stupid spell worked but Areschera turned us on him like he thought he was turning us on her, or if we completed our mission and are now here to fuck with him, or if some third thing that led to us discovering and eliminating a hunting party, but he knows we are willing to hit hunting parties
- he will therefore likely either up hunting party sizes to deal with the risk of our existence *or* refactor his plans to hunker and hope he can drag us all in, either of which will reduce his active efficiency but make encounters more dangerous

Further:
- danger to his captives probably increases if he thinks that he might lose them, so doing a long term war of attrition picking off groups is not tenable for purposes of rescue even if we weren’t concerned about what the Hag might pull off points-wise while we’re being a problem to this guy

This is not simple to balance.

I do genuinely hope there’s another hunting party going for these people because it would be good to whittle some more down, or that we can track down others in the area.  Trying to figure out how to strategize is fussy.

Relevant point:

- according to the glass item (used when not awake) Robin and Celyn are prime targets for whatever the hell this guy is doing, which means the sparkly boys are effective bait for *something*.  This is probably not usefully exploitable but it is worth keeping in mind.  Viepuck is uninteresting to the glass item, and may be able to exploit *that*; at the very least it means that he won’t be immediately given away by an Ethlenn Vibes Scanning Glass if disguised.

How many pieces of glass are there?  (Will give us an estimated number of team leader quality harpies.)
How many harpies, vultures, other forces, if it can speak to that.
What does it understand about its purpose?  What is it looking for?
Does the camp contain human prisoners?
Do the harpies roost directly in the camp?
Do the harpies work in shifts?
What did the harpy who was carrying this one do for the last 24 hours (since that is its awareness span)?  Did it conduct other attacks?
What humans did it observe over its accessible consciousness-span and what is their condition?

That should do for a start I think.
