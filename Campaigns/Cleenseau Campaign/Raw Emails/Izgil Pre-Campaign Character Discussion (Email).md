## Wed, 28 Dec 2022 18:32:24 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: Taelgar character

Matt,

For a lunar sorcerer, how would you feel about a power that actually does
change with the phase of the moon? So it would be somewhat out of your
control exactly when you had what?

Here is a bit of background on the moon:
====
Infused with the light of the Divine Spark, the moon is fully awake now,
though hardly aware in any normal sense of the word. And yet as it grows
brighter, it can feel the threads of the divine that tie the world below to
the spiritual realms, and it pulls, yearning for more, for a deeper
connection to the warmth and the glow of divinity. The realms of the gods,
as well as the realms of demons and devils and the primordial forces of the
world, all draw closer. As the moon reaches for these realms, it creates
the planar tides, the ebb and flow of the strength of connection between
these realms and the world of Taelgar.

The moon does not remember its past, although it has heard the stories, of
course, the conflicting tales and prayers and legends of its origin. Is it
a great evil, cast out in the earliest days of creation by Arhat and Malik,
cursed to forget in its great tomb, struggling awake for only a few days of
each month? Some tales claim so. Or, perhaps, a great hero of the Age of
Myth, born and living and dying during the Long Breath before time began,
rescued from death by Arhat and Malik, given and honored place in the sky,
but unable to be fully restored? Or some say, a hero and villain both,
granted the gift of forgetting. The moon hears these tales, and more,
washing over them, and yet they find they do not care to know the truth,
even in these moments of lucidity.
 =======

I haven't thought through the entire mechanic but I was thinking of at
least for the 1st level feature, giving you 2 bonus spells for each
level... but you don't get to pick which phase of the moon you are attuned
to. You would get the bonus spells for the specific phase of the moon. As
an alternative, I might give you 3 spells total, and one cannot be cast
during the full moon, and one cannot be cast during the new moon, and each
long rest you can pick 2.

Any thoughts? I find the lunar sorceror the most interesting but could do
something with any of the others if you want.
