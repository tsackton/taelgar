## Tue, 21 Oct 2025 22:27:31 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Meta: Next Session - Please Read

Hi all,

I'm running a bit behind on the whole "downtime narrative" email thread. I
definitely have plans to keep it up (I have a "May in Asineau" email
partially drafted).... but I am not so sure I will be able to move it along
fast enough to actually drop the various possible "mini-arc" hooks into the
email thread in time for you to pick one and me to prep it.

So I'm pulling back the curtains and just asking for your direct input.
Here are the three possible hooks I could throw your way:

(a) A letter from now-Major Ida asks for your help investigating a giant
who's been spotted out past the wall. The details are muddled - there are
reports of a squad being killed by this giant, but maybe actually the giant
was helping them and something else killed them. Some say the giant is
wreathed in flame, but Ida personally doubts this.

(b) Requests from Maseau, in the south, probably through Bathalzar and some
of the folks you rescued from the ogre way back in session 5 or 6 of some
kind of malignment whirlwind that's been terrorizing the villagers west of
Valarin (the capital of Maseau). But some folks say it is actually made of
dust, or smoke, or ash, or it is the very Plagueland itself rising up.

(c) A tale from Addermarch and Tyrwinghan that a dragon has moved into the
Mostreve Hills, on the borders of Tyrwingha, and one band of rangers has
already died trying to drive it off. No one really knows where it came
from. The Sembaran and Tyrwinghan armies are in somewhat of a state of
disarray and recovery, and Elaine II is apparently preparing troops to
fight it, but also dreading the cost and focusing more on protecting the
borders than killing the dragon. Also, no one knows what it wants or where
it came from. Is it a portent of trouble?

If you could let me know whether:

* You are most excited about one of these three ideas in particular
* You are happy with any of them, and I just should pick one and you'll go
along
* You really just like the idea of having our next session be the meeting
with the Queen and continue with the main plot

It would help my prep immensely.

Mike
