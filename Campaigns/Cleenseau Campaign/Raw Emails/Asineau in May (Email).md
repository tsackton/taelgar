# Asineau in May (Email)

May 1720 update on Asineau; the second mailbox message is a forwarded copy of the first, so the original is preserved once.

## Wed, 29 Oct 2025 22:41:55 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Asineau in May

May is another bright month, and the spring millet shoots up across the
fields like it has years of growth to make up for. The lambs are plentiful
and growing well, and roads are starting to fill with travellers. The 12 of
May comes, and with it the festival of the Empty Cauldron. This
commemorates the memory of an famine from just after the Great War, when a
old woman, it is said, boiled water in an empty pot for seven nights, each
night saying she was boiling the memory and hope of plenty. On the seventh
night, the Father heard her prayers and the pot overflowed with sustenance,
and the people survived the famine.

Traditionally this is celebrated in Beury, but on Robin's urging the
village welcomes all, and there are tents and games, archery contests and a
warlord combat, where Rinualt's team, in a shocking upset, wins the day.
The traditional celebration of this miracle is centered around remembrance
of the empty cauldron and the miracle of food, and the tradition is to cook
a massive cauldron of stew, a mix of winter storage and new spring shoots.
This is not generally a competition but a communal event, focused on the
sharing of food. Yvette
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/yvette/>, one
of the cooks from Essford Manor comes with a borrowed camp kitchen from the
garrison and sets up a dozen stewpots, and everyone in the community adds
ingredients to whichever they want. Somehow, the miracle of the day is that
the food always tastes good, despite the lack of direction, and everyone
talks about how it is even better shared across the wider community, and
nods sagely about the wisdom of the new lord of Asineau. Rosalind does not
attend, but Rinault does, as do several other of the Cleenseau merchants,
plus a great many people from Asineau and Beury and Valit. (It is a bit far
for large groups from Cleenseau to attend as without a horse it is a full
day's walk to get to Beury).

*News from Afar*
In general, the realm is doing well. Queen Elaine II is crowned in Embry to
much fanfare, The only dark spot is rumors from the south, along the
borders of the Plaguelands. There are rumors that the ash from the
Plaguelands has come alive and is spreading west, eating people. But it is
far to the south, and no news has reached Sembara of dangers to nearby
neighbors like Maseau, or Tyrwinghan, and after the recent chaos, there is
little appetite for believing these rumors.

Celyn does receive a letter from home, which broadly indicates all as well,
as well as a more curious letter from Llew. (KIya I will send it to you
separately). In other news, the new Baron is planning a visit next month,
and will expect to be hosted by Rosalind. But he also plans to spend
several nights in Asieneau with his newest vassal. The new castellan of
Valit, Alix Degwyn will be in his company, and he is partially coming to
install them.

Celyn also receives a letter from Mahaut, which is light on news but does
mention her plan to come west this season, and if the gods will it, to
expect them sometime this summer.

*Visitors*
Two notable visitors arrive this month: the Merriweather caravan and Lenora
and her two remaining companions.

The Merriweathers arrive in a caravan of three wagons, mostly full of bolts
of cloth and some tailored clothes, as well as some oddities like some fine
wooden carvings, apple brandy from Addermarch, and intricate ceramic
platters from Rinburg. The caravan is lead by Quent, an older woman missing
one eye, but with a keen fashion sense, who is mostly cheerful and keen to
hear all about Celyn and the party's life. Tobin, her younger cousin, does
much of the trading, and his niece Tamsin seems to be everywhere and
talking to everyone but doesn't have any clear role in the caravan. The
caravan is a bit of an event - the Merriweathers don't usually stop in
Asieneau - but not that unusual for the region, as halfling caravans often
stop in Cleenseau.

Lenora also gives you a set of items.

A necklace with four beads on it. Each bead is attuned to a specific one of
the Mos Numena and can create a magical effect. Once used, the bead
disappears. Lenora says there were once eight beads, but currently four
remain:
* The Bead of the Warlord: Casts *Bless *at 3rd level, which lasts for 1
minute but does not require concentration to maintain.
* The Bead of the Father: Casts *Shield of Faith, *which lasts for an hour
and does not require concentration.
* The Bead of the Wanderer: Casts *Aid*, at 4th level.
* The Bead of the Wyrdling: Casts *Beacon of Hope *which lasts for 10
minutes and does not require concentration.

Four potions:
* A Potion of Flying
* A Potion of Heroism
* A Potion of Greater Invisibility
* A Potion of Fire Resistance

A finely made leather saddle, that used to be Lenora's. She no longer uses
a saddle with her jet-black pegasus, but she hopes its magic will be
useful. It has the following effects:
* While riding, you cannot be dismounted against your will if you are
conscious
* Attack rolls against your mount have disadvantage
* Your mount has advantage on Dexterity saving throws

Additionally, once per day you can cast the *Haste* spell on your mount,
except that it does not allow your mount to take the Attack or Utilize
actions, it does not require concentration, and it does not introduce a
lethargy effect when the spell ends.

A shield painted with signs of the Sibyl. It functions as a +1 shield.
Additionally, if you fail a saving throw against a magical spell or effect
you can choose to succeed instead. Doing so shatters the shield into many
pieces, rendering it useless.

A twisted ring that seems to flicker in and out of sight, almost like it is
only partially in this realm. Lenora says she found it in the old wizard
tower, where Viepuck was due to be sacrificed. She long feared it was evil,
but she believes it is merely a conduit to the Far Realms, and makes it
easier for the magic of those realms to be pulled into Taelgar. It
functions as a Rod of the Pact Keeper +1 (except it is a ring) for any
warlock with a patron from the Far Realms.

I assume there is not much unique you are doing, but anything you want to
