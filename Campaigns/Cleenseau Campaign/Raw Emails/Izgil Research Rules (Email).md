Subject: Research rules

Hey Matt

I was thinking of how to best model Izgil’s research into the fey and here’s what I’m thinking..

Research is divided into 2 phases- info gathering and synthesis.

Info gathering
Each day make a roll against your research skill. If you succeed, add 1 success to your tally and the DC goes up by 2. Succeed by >= 10 and the DC doesn’t go up. Critical success counts as 2 successes and the DC doesn’t go up.

You can keep looking for info each day until you get 2 failures in a row.

Synthesis
Takes 1 day, single roll.

End result—

The number of successes during info gathering tells you how much info you have. The success or failure of synthesis determines how reliable the info is.

So for example if you had 10 successes for info gathering you’d have a lot of info, but if you failed the synthesis roll you’d have less confidence about what is right and wrong.

The more info you have the easier synthesis is

So how this plays out for the fey research:

You made three rolls. I don’t remember what they were exactly but I remember you rolled well but no crits.

The initial DC for fey lore would be 15 — it’s tricky subject around here. Let’s say you got one 10+ success and 2 normal, so you’d have 3 successes and the new DC is 19.

Next session will be zombie adventure so you won’t have a chance to continue your research, and your third roll was for spending the day researching which you haven’t actually done yet.

So you have 2 choices:

1) decide you want to synthesize what you have learned before the zombie adventure, use your 3rd roll as the synthesis roll, and spend the afternoon organizing your research. 2 info gathering successes is not that much but you’d have the info now
2) keep going with info gathering after zombie stuff and basically not have any info now

If you pick 1 you can always go back and do more research but you’d be starting over at 0 success

## Sat, 4 Nov 2023 19:37:50 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: Research rules

I forgot to include the table:

In general:

   -

   1-2 successes means some useful information was found, but rarely
   anything secret or esoteric and rarely all of the information that was
   available
   -

   3-4 successes means most of the mundane information is found, and one or
   two unusual facts
   -

   5-8 successes means that all of the reasonable mundane information to be
   found was, and if any esoteric or secret knowledge could be discovered, it
   probably is
   -

   9+ successes would usually mean some key piece of secret knowledge is
   discovered beyond plausibility, i.e. you discover a hidden scroll or
   something



It's a bit of a "play it by ear" as well, i.e. I don't know that I'd have
perfectly strict rules. But the above guidelines would help you decide when
"enough is enough" (but also, if not time limited, there is generally no
reason not to just keep looking until you get 2 failures).
