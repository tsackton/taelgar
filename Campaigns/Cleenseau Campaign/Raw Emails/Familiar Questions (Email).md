Familiar Related Invocation Notes

Voice of the Chain Master: You can communicate telepathically with your familiar and perceive through your familiar’s senses as long as you are on the same plane of existence.

Additionally, while perceiving through your familiar’s senses, you can also speak through your familiar in your own voice, even if your familiar is normally incapable of speech.

This is basically the thing you already mentioned for the infinite-range, minus the speak-through-familiar.  Weak as a full-cost invocation, but useful to have.  I guess the question is does the remote-voice-thing work as queried above.

—
Investment of the Chain Master
Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything
Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain feature

When you cast find familiar <http://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:find-familiar>, you infuse the summoned familiar with a measure of your eldritch power, granting the creature the following benefits:

The familiar gains either a flying speed or a swimming speed (your choice) of 40 feet.
As a bonus action, you can command the familiar to take the Attack action.
The familiar's weapon attacks are considered magical for the purpose of overcoming immunity and resistance to nonmagical attacks.
If the familiar forces a creature to make a saving throw, it uses your spell save DC.
When the familiar takes damage, you can use your reaction to grant it resistance against that damage.

This one is almost worth taking, but I’m not sure I want to go that high in Warlock….  (I think I would need to go to 7th)

Already has fly and swim speed.
Bonus action familiar attack is useful, but also there are many bonus action uses, so competes on action economy.
Attacks are magical is kind of not useful?  Would be minimizing direct attack.
Familiar uses your spell save DC is nice, as that scales over time.  Currently the 13 is the same, but eventually will be higher.
Resistance vs damage on a reaction is handy, especially for a not-replacable critter.

I don’t THINK I’m going to pursue this?  Just commenting.

——

Mostly long-term I want to ensure durability.  That could be done by gaining hit dice and adding my proficiency bonus to saves.  Or pulling some interesting things on the animal companion thing.   But I don’t want to overpower or overshadow people, just be interesting and not a constant “try to not get killed” item.

I could see the animal companion thing being useful for that, also for Dan’s horse.

Possibly get one Companion level per character class level?  That way things don’t get too much too fast?
