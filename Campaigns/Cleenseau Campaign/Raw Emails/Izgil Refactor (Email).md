# Izgil Refactor (Email)

Local archive of all 1 message body in this correspondence, including quoted reply history. DM's proposed changes to Izgil's mechanics; proposals are not evidence of implementation.

## Tue, 29 Oct 2024 19:27:14 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Izgil Refactor

Matt,

Izgil is one of the more complex characters to refactor because I'm not
really sure how he ended up with all of his features. I think there were a
few things we did wrong during character creation as I was trying to use
some playtest rules that I misread.

I don't want to take anything critical away, but I would rather try to get
Izgil a bit more inline with the 2024 rules as they landed plus my current
set of homebrew. Some of this will be to Izgil's advantage, but
other things won't be. Anything that you really care about that seems to be
going away we should discuss.

*Stats*
The rules I've been using for everyone else for stats is:
* Do normal point buy
* Take one score that is 8 or 9 and increase it to 10
* Add +2 to one stat
* Add +1 to another stat

Then for Izgil, at 4th and 8th level, add +1 to Int.
First level feats can't have stat bonuses. All feats after first level will
have a stat bonus.

Just mucking around, this either leaves Izgil with the same stats but you
can either drop Int to 18 and boost Dex or Con to 16, or leave Int at 19
and boost Dex or Con to 15 (or Wis to 13).

But you can play around on your own, either via Dicecloud (all the rules
are implemented) or via D&D Beyond, just by doing normal point buy and then
adding the pluses above.

*Proficiencies *
Izgil should have a total of 4 skill proficiencies - two from sorcerer and
two from general "everyone gets 2 proficiencies". Your existing character
sheet has five, and I'm not sure why.

*Competencies*
Izgil should (save for feats, see below) have a total of 11 competencies,
of which at least 3 have to be languages. Izgil currently has 19. I am
really not sure where they all came from :-)

I would consider letting you take 3-4 bonus artisan skill competencies just
to represent Izgil's long life and background (or, you could take a feat to
give you some).

*Feats*
All characters should have 1 origin feat from the 2024 list (
https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#OriginFeats) or I can
homebrew something. Izgil has 2 (Master Artisian, which is a playtest
version of Crafter, and Magic Initiate). I think that happened because I
miscommunicated or misread, and gave you both a standard campaign starting
feat AND a bonus feat for dwarf, rather than just a standard campaign
starting feat.

I think you should drop both of those and pick a new feat (which could be
Magic Initiate or Crafter, if you want to replicate one of the abilities).
Remember that the 2024 sorcerer gets notably more known spells. So you are
trading a feat that gives you 2 cantrips and a 1st level spell for 3 extra
sorcerer spells (at 6th level) and up to 7 extra (at 20th level), which I
think is fair trade (what I'm trying to say is... drop Magic Initiate and
then decide if you want to keep Crafter or take something else).

*Dwarf*
My brother and I refactored the Dwarf species. You can see the new rules
here:
https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/campaigns/mechanics/species/playing-a-dwarf/#basic-traits

I think the new version is better. The summary of the changes is:

+ Darkvision goes to 120 feet
+ Speed goes to 30 feet
+ New Feature: Bahrazel's Blessing which gives you a magical effect
- Dwarven Training

Tremorsense remains the same and rune crafting is tweaked to be easier to
use.

Dropping Dwarven Training though means you lose the ability to have Light
Armor + Shield, which I know is an in-game thing of your character. So
let's maybe discuss that one.

*Sorcerer*
The tweaks to sorcerer in 2024 are basically all improvements - more
spells, cheaper metamagic, and a few nice bonus things. You can read that
yourself, nothing really big changes here. You will have a couple of new
spells to pick.

Mike
