## Thu, 16 Oct 2025 22:21:52 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: April 1720 - Around Asineau

The misty grey of March and the cold of February gives way to spring. The
cold, dry winds from the mountains to the west shift, with the coming
spring, and the warm winds, pregnant with rain, start to blow from the
Green Sea. Spring wheat is planted, and the shad run is enormous. The smell
of smoking and frying fish permeates Asineau, and the river teems with so
many fish the fisherfolk barely have to work to fill their nets.

*News from Afar*
News comes to Cleenseau from many places, with halfling traders from the
south, military messengers riding to Dunfry, and many others. In general,
the talk everywhere is of the new Queen, Elaine II. Peace seems to lay upon
the land, and many openly hope that the troubles of the last years were the
work of the lich, the False Robert, as he is coming to be called. Although
much is happening across Sembara, locally, the biggest piece of news is
about the trial and conviction of the old Baroness, Isabeau d'Aslain (more
details about this in a separate email - short version: the High Court
found compelling evidence she was involved in the framing of Reginald
Rusebek, the previous baron, which was sufficient to convict her of
treason).

There are also rumors that the Queen intends to ennoble one of the earls of
Tyrwingha, one Cadfael, to be the new baron, who is said to have taken the
house name de Dufferin. Although this is not the most popular thing, there
are some who say that with the fey about, having an earl from Tyrwingha
seems like a wise move. This new baron is said to travel with a cadre of
warlocks from the Army of Mostreve.

*News from Nearby*
In local events, things are quiet. The arrival of the party and your new
lordship of Asineau is the biggest local event, and Lord Murtha of Beury
hosts you several times, and seems full of advice and offers of friendship.
Vahaiya, the elf ranger, has grown anxious to return home, and is planning
a departure from the region as soon, to try to reach home before the summer
rains. They tell you much of the local wood, and have some news of the fey
portal to report (I'll send this in a separate email as well).

Otherwise, the biggest local news is the question of the new captain of the
garrison in Cleenseau. Rosalind has been asked her advice between three
candidates, and she in turn asks if you have an opinion: Ancer Benthey
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/ancer-benthey/>
(the
nephew of her guard captain, Ames, who you might remember from his dice
games last November) (you don't know him at all); Sergeant Eveyln Totteridge
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/eveyln-totteridge/>,
who is good with horses and traveled was in the group of soldiers who came
with you to Aslain. Greymalkin likes her, and she seems comptent, if
slightly more comfortable with horses than people; Bertrand Leclerc
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/bertrand-leclerc/>,
the second cousin of the new general, and well-liked by the Duke of
Wisford. Bertrand was a sergeant in the Army of the South who ended up
briefly in Cleenseau, before you headed to Aveil to start the fey arc. But
your only opinion of him was competent but clearly ambitious.

*Events in Asineau*
Note: This is incomplete. I got a bit carried away. Still plan to write up:
Balthazar, Lizardfolk, Halfing Contact, Necromancy Place Contact

In Asineau, Robin settles into the lordship and the party begins to settle
in. There is much travel to Cleenseau, time spent at the inns talking,
diplomatic missions, and other goings on. Rather than trying to write a
short story narrative, here are the main highlights:

   - Celyn spends much time wandering the land and learning the ins and
   outs of the place. Clay is scarce, and although there are bits and pieces
   here and there, nothing that would allow establishment of a substantial
   pottery industry. On the other hand, Es*talios finds the deep part of the
   Enst has a beautiful sandy bottom. The current is strong, and it is in the
   middle of the 1200' wide river, but if there was a way to fetch it, it
   would be a great source of sand for glass making.
   - Celyn also takes Warin and sometimes Vahaiya (also a tracker) on many
   of his wanderings, and starts to learn the ins and outs of following
   footprints. He hears much of the daily goings on in Valit and manages to
   visit the fey portal site once or twice (in practice this just means I'll
   give you a briefing on the details when it comes up in game). Warin has
   much to say about Sabine de Brune, and many theories about her
   disappearance. He doesn't believe the story circulating that her health was
   poor and she returned to Eskbridge to die. He mentions a few times thinking
   that she had a mysterious visitor, this past fall, and looks at Celyn,
   wondering if he knows anything.
   - Izgil spends much time with Brot tinkering, but even more time
   establishing a (library/arcane study tbd) in (tbd in Asineau). He spends a
   lot of time there tinkering with magical items and experimenting very
   carefully with them. Sometimes he gets caught up and doesn't emerge for
   three days.
   - Robin spends much of his time learning the finances and people of
   Asineau, visiting and listening, healing and being seen. He is very popular
   with the villagers and there is a palpable pride about the village. People
   introduce themselves as "from Asineau, where the heroes live" with a deep
   sense of gratitude and civic pride.
   - Robin also establishes a cordial relationship with Catherine de Brune,
   who essentially agrees to return all land purchases and restore the manor
   to the status quo before Lorin Valbert started his schemes. She does have
   to expend some money purchasing some legitimately bought land to return it
   to the manorial books, and asks for only two things: a lifelong right to
   buy or sell goods in Asineau free of any market taxes or monopolies
   normally charged, and guesting rights at the manor (basically, to be
   invited to any interesting parties you have, or you, if you host the queen
   or whatever). Plus, the unstated assumption you will not speak of Sabine's
   involvement with the fey, at least in public. Finally, she offers financing
   for any projects you need, up to 1000 GP, on a 10 year term, with 2%
   interest a year. She also asks, separately from the deal, if Viepuck would
   be willing to attend at her house, occasionally, to entertain her guests,
   of course, only when it was convenient for him. She mentions it would be
   good for him. Educational and all.
   - Rosalind is grateful for your return, and dines with you regularly,
   providing advice as you wish. She is busy settling things in Cleenseau, and
   trying to resettle Tavoise, and in general recovering from her travels and
   losses, which now include her father. She offers you stone for your
   building project at cost, from the quarry across the river, which the
   Essford family controls. It is currently not active, but if you can advance
   her 100 gold she can have it up and running by early summer. [meta: this
   will reduce the cost of building a stone wall by 25 GP per hex]. She also
   suggests, if you need unskilled labor, you might organize a "healing day"
   at the Wyrdling temple in Asineau. She expects many folks would be grateful
   for their mothers/fathers/sisters/children to be healed and would volunteer
   some time. There is also Beggar's Way and you can usually hire half-a-dozen
   laborers for the cost of a good meal.
   - Eleanor thanks Robin for his efforts, but says she is at peace with
   her god and El has said her time is near, and asks only that he help her
   face her death without pain. She won't explain in more detail, but she
   seems at peace. She says that come fall, you should be looking for her
   replacement.

The biggest event though is one day in mid April when ten dwarves with six
cartloads of stone arrive in Asineau. One of them you recognize: Bolgrim
Ferrystone
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/dwarves/bolgrim-ferrystone/>,
the dwarf you healed in Aslain after he was attacked by the Hunter's bears.
He declares that the Ferrystone family is eternally grateful to you, and
that his cousin, Roaic Ferrystone, lately of Beury, has heard you are in
need of a smithy. Roaic has been retired for some time, but on hearing your
need, has volunteered to come and establish a smithy for you, if you could
only be so kind as to find him a suitable apprentice. The Ferrystones then
get to work building, and in two weeks have constructed an elaborate smithy
for you.

There is much feasting over this time, and the dwarves share many tales of
Rinburg and Aslain. They are full of rumors of the new baron, and are quite
pleased to hear that - as they say - he is bringing fifty soldiers from the
Tyrwinghan army including a cadre of warlocks and three knights of the
Riven. Everyone seems to think - what with the fey - this is very wise and
far sighted cultural integration.

(Hopefully some of you enjoyed reading all this! There is no specific need
to respond, in general I assume that your characters are mostly carrying on
with what they did in April. I'm assuming you bring in Samuel, Odo's
brother, to be the smith, and Odo, to be the guard captain, but that will
actually happen in May, so it will be the Sunday email. There is no direct
need to respond to these events, but anything you do want to respond to
feel free. Also if I missed anything, let me know - I know I haven't
written up the lizardfolk yet, nor what is going on with Bathalzar, nor
details of the halfings or queries about the necromancers from Viepuck's
sendings)

Mike

Negotiating with Catherine de Brune

Guesting rights at the manor. - Yes

Will not speak of Sabine's involvement with the fey, at least in public. - Yes, we don't do guilt by association.

Finally, she offers financing for any projects you need, up to 1000 GP, on a 10 year term, with 2% interest a year. - Appreciated.

She also asks, separately from the deal, if Viepuck would be willing to attend at her house, occasionally, to entertain her guests, of course, only when it was convenient for him. She mentions it would be good for him. Educational and all. - That would be up to Chris.

A lifelong right to buy or sell goods in Asineau free of any market taxes or monopolies normally charged. - This one I want to negotiate on. Robin is very keen on trying to encourage a feel of "everyone's in this together" in the town to improve morale, especially after the mess that was being made of things previously, which means ideally everyone's paying in something and it doesn't look like nobles are all just patting each other's backs. Alternate proposal:

She can sell/buy at no market tax until she has effectively recouped the funds she has spent to restore the manorial lands (based on what would have otherwise been paid at the market toll rate, which is currently set to "light"), plus once the market is established and goes to an "average" rate, and after she has recouped her initial investment, three years guarantee she continues to pay the "light" rate, which will give her some return on that investment. I'll also point out that her partnership with me in restoring the town is apt to make her a quite popular figure in the town and that the way I'm proposing things, she still gets a benefit while it's less likely to sully the looks of things with "oh, my house is still falling apart but look at that, the manor is giving sweet deals to the local landed rich, surprise surprise..."

Terms open to negotiation, but that's my proposal.

Daniel Walters
