# Heir to House Griffin (Email)

Local archive of all 6 message bodies in this correspondence, including quoted reply history.

## Mon, 03 Aug 2026 12:22:00 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: [Thantos] Heir to House Griffin

So as discussed last time, I was thinking about who to declare as an heir to Robin's House and lordship. Note I consider this is as much a meta OOG question as an IG question as if anyone else wants a direct in on the nobility stuff, I don't want to be hoarding it if others are so inclined, so if others have specific interest please say so. Anyway, here's my suggestions.

Viepuck - Has a good head for administrative and legal stuff, and Robin trusts him (as he does the rest of the party, obviously, he considers them all family at this point). Robin would consider him a good choice if he wants it (Chris, that would be up to you), but has the disadvantage from an IG perspective that in a 'rocks fall, everyone dies' scenario, Viepuck might well die along with Robin. Then there's the question again of Chris, do you specifically want this for your PC, or does a party friendly NPC make more sense? As said I want to give others as much (or as little) inclusion as you want.

Rene - Seems kind and well intentioned, would likely be benevolent and a good caretaker. Also has the advantage of already being part of the landed class which may smooth things. May well be more inclined and educated towards the position than my other suggestions, so this has some strong appeal.

Odo - Robin trusts him, he's already in a prominent public facing roll, and has dedicated himself to the same Order as Robin. He'd certainly trust Odo to take care of the town, no idea if he'd have any administrative talent (or desire) though.

The lady whose name keeps escaping me that Robin appointed as his second while captain of the guard, she seemed honorable and fiercely protective of others, which are qualities Robin likes. No idea of she'd consider herself well suited to the position, and he'd trust her to say so if we decided to make the offer, and assuming Rosalind doesn't want to offer her the same.

Greymalkin - Can a fey take a lordship? Greymalkin wouldn't want a lordship, but at the same time being a knight in his own right, I think would accept a 'should I fall, will you watch over these people?' request from Robin. A Griffin of House Griffin is also slightly hilarious, but suffers the same positional disadvantage as Viepuck in that he might get taken out in a 'rocks fall, everyone dies' scenario. Incidentally, especially with Greymalkin being a semi-PC now, I've considered he could actually be a backup character if Robin did die at some point.

Note that one of the chief qualities all of the above choices share is that Robin would tend to want to adopting as heir anyone overly...ambitious. He wants someone who would care about Asineau and its people, and feel a sense of duty towards them, not someone who's just looking to grab a title, even if they otherwise might be good at the job (and Robin does see it as a job and a duty, not just a privileged).

What do folks think?

Daniel Walters

You're right about Izgil. Even if OOG I wanted a title Izgil would be more
the court wizard to the Lord rather than a good Lord himself. He is putting
down roots here and is likely to be invested in the region for a long time
to come but he wouldn't be a good candidate himself. Although as he
is likely to live a long time (outside of rocks fall) a support position to
whoever is lord could make sense.

Of the options you presented I like Rene best. He's got the right training
and upbringing and has demonstrated bravery in the face of someone who is
wrong while being smart enough to keep his opposition under the radar. He
also doesn't seem ambitious and it would potentially be a good way to
ensure the family of the corrupt Baroness doesn't become ignominious and
has a path back to respect.
