## Sun, 2 Jun 2024 21:00:18 -0400 — Artan Eter <artan.eter@gmail.com>

Subject: [Thantos] Before we go north to Aveil

* should leave a letter for Rosalind - she asked us to stay until she got back, but we should note that we found fraudulent tax collectors for the war tax, so need to go check on that, as no news has come out of Aveil in some time.  Apologies this seemed urgent.

* any of the sincere guards can come with us if they want to actually get a job in tax collection, we first need to find the baron/etc.

* anyone else can be provided a job with Ida or others, or return home.

* whoever lost their horse in the skeleton attack can have Garret’s horse.

* seems like people are getting a good handle on how to fight as a group in hazardous situations.  Compliment Rinault on his work in the zombie attack and remind that what keeps people alive is fighting as part of the group.  (If he comments that we tend to ride off alone, we have spells, when he gets spells then go ahead…)

Anything I’m missing?
