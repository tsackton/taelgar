Subject: Faden and Robin

Dan,

I love the idea of just resetting Robin's timeline and having you play him,
but reviving Faden could also be fun.

Faden if I recall correctly was basically an orc-analogue with an elven
soul who came from a background where he didn't have much family or a
childhood or anything like that and learned most of what you knew from a
bunch of ghosts?

And who then ended up a healer-druid?

I'm also happy to work out a way to play Faden but his background would

## Mon, 19 Dec 2022 20:45:37 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: Re: Faden and Robin

Yeah, I'm still weighing my desires for each...I was just looking over some of my old character models a while back and remembered Faden and how much I liked the character. I like them both, so it's a hard choice.

In D&D terms he was a Bugbear (so, hyena esque) but you'd interpreted that as some sort of chimera race in that setting. I had actually forgotten where he learned his druid stuff, but it might well have been ghosts. And yeah, he ended up as a healer druid. The flavor of the character definitely depends on not being human as I was going for the "looks like a D&D monster despite being very gentle" flavor. The social interaction challenge was an intended part of the character design.

In his original background he escaped from a wizard who was making other creatures like him for some sort of servitor army or something. I don't recall a huge number of specifics beyond that other then him being friends with Matt's character in that setting who was an elven assassin or something, but any or all of that could change. He and Robin have some similar personality threads in that they're both very humble and gentle people by nature.

I'm genuinely torn between the two, my decision might well be effected by the how the rest of our current game fleshes out, but I'm definitely open to discussion and input.

Daniel Walters (He/Him)

## Mon, 19 Dec 2022 18:01:15 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Re: Faden and Robin

I left some comments on discord. But from a game-world perspective I can
make either work, but I'd need to do a bit of thinking/tweaking about how
to fit Faden's background in. But the non-human thing would definitely be
possible. But Robin would be very easy to fit in, I'd just have him find
the lantern and have it somehow send him to the beginning of the adventure.
He wouldn't be part of the Order of the Charitable Wanderer at the start
(as paladin gets oath at level 3) but I'd set up the first couple sessions
to have a payoff of joining the order when he got to level three, which
could be kinda fun.

But really either way totally works. If you are honestly completely
undecided though... Robin is less work for me and especially if Chris and
Matt are both playing spellcasters, might balance the party a little bit
better.

That said, I really liked Faden and..
(a) I wouldn't want D&D mechanics to overly influence the type of
characters people play so a back-line heavy party is totally fine and I can
make that work
(b) In a game intended to have a long arc, playing a character with a lot
of personal growth potential could be very fun
(c) I actually really really enjoy working with players to make up
backgrounds, so it being "more work" to make Faden really just means "more
fun stuff to do" :-)
