Subject: Last Session - Sanctuary

Hi all,

I couldn't think of a good way to make this obvious in game, but I thought
I should mention that the reason Izgil started to see the Dreamworld
telescope as dangerous and threatening, rather than interesting, was
because you cast sanctuary.

It is sometimes a struggle to think of the best ways of indicating that a
clever thing actually works in a subtle way, or that something you do cuts
off a potentially much worse path that you then never learn about.

Mike
