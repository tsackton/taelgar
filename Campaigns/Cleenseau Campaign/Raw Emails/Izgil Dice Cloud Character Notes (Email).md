Matt,

I created a version of Izgil here:
https://dicecloud.com/character/f4uzeQj5e6TrzSz6i

I think some of the math got a little confused because I couldn't quite
make this version match D&D Beyond. Here are the changes and notes:

1) Skills. Izgil on Beyond has 5 skill proficiencies: Arcana, Nature,
Religion, Investigation, and History. I'm not sure where they came from as
Sorcerer + Background should be 4 skills.

2) Competencies: Izgil has 19 competencies (mostly via a long list of
tools) on his character sheet, but I'm not sure where those came from. My
math says he should have 11 (+ dwarven training, which you might want to
use to get armor instead). I didn't select any on Dice Cloud as I'm not
sure what you want to do..

3) New abilities from One D&D. I added the following two new abilities

*Innate Sorcery*: 2 times per day activate "superpower" mode - you get +1
to spell save DCs and advantage on spell attacks, lasts 1 minute

*Sorcerous Restoration:* One per day, during a short rest you regain 1/2
your level sorcery points.

4) Dwarven Rune Magic. I think it was implemented on Beyond a bit wrong.
The idea is that you have 2 runes you can inscribe: the rune of exploration
or the rune of lore. But you can only inscribe one or the other. You can
see the notes on the Izgil sheet above. Basically it means you get the Calm
Emotions / Identity free spells from both, but you have to choose each day
whether you want the Lore or Exploration abilities. You can change which
one is active by in your Inventory dragging from Carried to Equiped the
runes. And remember you can give the rune to someone else to (i.e. if you
want Viepuck or Robin to have the ability instead of you).

5) Fey Touched spell. You took Bless, but Robin also has that. Not terrible
to have it twice, but you could consider something else. Comprehend
Languages, Gift of Alacrity, Command might all be good other ideas.

6) I've made Tremorsense and your Advantage on perception in the dark more
obvious. I think you often forget about these.

7) I've added a "lunar sorcery points" which is the bonus ones you get from
your level 6 feature and added metamagic versions for moon spells only that
draw from those points instead.

8) Don't forget about the Rune of Lore power where you can take 10 on a
arcana check...

Anyway, take a look. I haven't added all the items but it should be a lot
clearer about all the custom stuff Izgil can do.
