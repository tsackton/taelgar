Subject: Game opening

[ This is a slight change from the background I sent you before ]

The game will open for Viepuck on a crisp October evening as he comes up
the Great South Road from Rinburg to Clenseau.

He gets the strong sense that his egg needs heat, and lots of it, and in
Clenseau, ends up finding an eccentric dwarf named Brot. They live in a
hamlet outside of town -- Tavoise -- and seem to be quite willing to let
Viepuck bury a strange object in their wife's forge.

Viepuck gets the sense the egg needs some time in the heat, and that it
needs something else -- the blood of something strange and from far away.
He senses he might be waiting here a few days (or longer) for the egg to
hatch.

He has a couple of options for where to stay:

* He could find a barn of hayloft in Taviose, to be nearest to the egg
* He could sleep in the woods -- Taviose is on the eaves of a large wood
* He could sleep in one of the four inns in Clenseau, about a mile south of
Tavoise along a dirt track:

There are four inns in Clenseau:

The Bandit's End: it appears boisterous and loud with laughter coming out
of a full bar. Outside the walls. Doesn't appear to have a large number of
rooms. Popular with guards from the local militia.

The Crossroads Inn: the largest and most elegant inn, set inside the walls,
looks cozy. A halfling establishment, although welcoming to others as well.
Popular with the richer townsfolk for gossip and news, and halflings.

The Fox's Flagon: A three story inn that seems relatively quiet for its
size. It has a large stableyard that is mostly empty. Outside the walls.
Popular with caravans.

The River's Blessing: A quiet place from which you can smell delicious
fried fish. Outside the walls, small. It has a local vibe. Popular with
local farmers when they come into town to sell their goods at market.

Can you let me know where he is sleeping?

The game will actually open on a cold rainy morning the day after he buried
the egg in Brot's coals - Viepuck will be going to check on the egg, either
from town or from the hamlet, depending on what you pick for where to sleep.

Mike
