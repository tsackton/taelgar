# Cleenseau - Session 15

The party returns to [[Cleenseau]], reunites with Izgil, incorporates survivors from the [[Army of the South]] into local defenses, and prepares the garrison for further magical threats. A new-moon rising of undead is defeated, and the party later exposes [[Gareth of Tollen]] as a fraudulent tax collector and bandit.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-01-25): The party returns to Cleenseau and reunites with Izgil and the Rangers.
- Robin formally inducts [[Odo Cordwaner]] into the [[Order of the Charitable Wanderer]].
- (DR:: 1720-02-04): Two groups of undead rise at the new moon and are destroyed.
- (DR:: 1720-02-06): The party encounters and arrests Gareth's group on the road.
- (DR:: 1720-02-06): Gareth is convicted and sentenced to death; lesser accomplices are handled separately.
