# Cleenseau - Session 34

>[!info] Wondering the Grey Mists: in which the party crosses the Grey Mists and reaches Twilight's Grace

> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-10-28) to (DR:: 1720-11-16)*

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-11-05) - (DR:: 1720-11-15): The party crosses the memory-altering [[Grey Mists]], then accepts hospitality at the [[Camp of Horn and Feather]], recovers its memories, and rests after a night of contests and revelry.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [[Krat]] (goblin): rescued goblin who accompanies the party into the [[Grey Mists]] and becomes separated there.
- [[Garn]] (goblin): rescued goblin who accompanies the party into the [[Grey Mists]] and becomes separated there.
- [[Droog]] (goblin): rescued goblin cook who accompanies the party into the [[Grey Mists]] and becomes separated there.
- [[Grib]] (goblin): rescued goblin vault helper who accompanies the party into the [[Grey Mists]] and becomes separated there.
- [[Snikket]] (goblin): rescued goblin transformed into a goat who reaches [[Twilight's Grace]] and receives freedom of form within Ethlenn's realm.
- [[Tarastië]] (she/her, griffin): small female weasel-and-raven gryphon formerly bonded to one of Ianto's victims who bonds with [[Celyn]].
- [[Thyron]] (fey): fey border guard, storyteller, and sibling accompanied by a mountain goat.
- [[Kyron]] (fey): bold fey sibling who challenges [[Greymalkin]] and [[Celyn]] to races and dares.
- [[Myron]] (they/them, fey): quiet fey sibling who discusses alchemy with [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and is accompanied by a giant silver moth.
- [[Syron]] (fey): fey hunter, knife thrower, and former partner of [[Istarias]] who trades gossip with [[Celyn]].
- [[Lyron]] (fey): playful fey dancer accompanied by an extravagant peacock.
- [[Sir Caldris]] (fey): large fey knight titled the Watcher of the Evening Song who guards [[Evensong Spire]].
- [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]] (archfey): ruler of [[Twilight's Grace]] who explains the party's role and grants boons.



### Locations

- [[Fortress of Ianto the Red]] (building, in [[Feywild]]): the captured redcap fortress where the party gathers Ianto's trophies and tends the recent dead.
- [[Grey Mists]] (region, in [[Feywild]]): a memory-altering region of mist crossed on the journey east toward [[Twilight's Grace]].
- [[Camp of Horn and Feather]] (settlement, in [[Feywild]]): the five siblings' lightly fortified ridge camp, where the party accepts hospitality, recovers its memories, and rests.
- [[Evensong Spire]] (building, in [[Sunset Gate]]): a fortress intertwined with the materialized colors of the [[Sunset Gate]] and watched by [[Sir Caldris]].
- [[Twilight's Grace]] (extraplanar domain, in [[Feywild]]): Ethlenn's Feywild realm, where the party receives an audience and boons.
