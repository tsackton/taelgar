# Cleenseau - Session 34

The party leaves Ianto's fortress with the rescued trophies and crosses a region of memory-altering grey mist. After resisting several traps and recovering lost memories, they reach the border of [[Twilight's Grace]], accept shelter from local fey, and receive an audience with [[Archfey Ethlenn]]. Ethlenn restores Greymalkin and grants other boons.

## Timeline

- The party buries the redcaps' victims and takes custody of a small gryphon-like companion.
- In the grey mist, false refuges and compulsions strip memories from several party members.
- The party reaches the Sunset Gate and is escorted to Twilight's Grace.
- Ethlenn explains that the party completed a danger she had foreseen.
- Greymalkin is restored; the transformed goat receives freedom of form within Ethlenn's realm; Celyn accepts a bond with the small gryphon.
