# Cleenseau - Session 23

The party splits to investigate the [[Emeric Daverre|Daverre]] household while the Midnight Lady is distracted. They find that she is draining [[Emeric Daverre]] through a poppet and discover an inscription leading to a hidden refuge. Inside the Lady's dollhouse-like lair, they destroy her, though she curses Celyn as she dies.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-03-03): Celyn and Robin investigate Emeric's condition while Viepuck and Izgil search the Lady's house.
- The party finds the poppet linked to Emeric and an inscribed stone describing a hidden refuge.
- The inscription opens a path into the Midnight Lady's extradimensional lair.
- (DR:: 1720-03-04): The party destroys the Lady and her refuge; Celyn receives a dying curse.
