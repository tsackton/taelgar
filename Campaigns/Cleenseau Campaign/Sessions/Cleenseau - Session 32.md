# Cleenseau - Session 32

>[!info] Closing the Portal: in which a gazebo dies and tea follows
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-10-20) to (DR:: 1720-10-26)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, March 8, 2026*
> *[[Cleenseau]] and [[Duskmire]]*
> *Kiya's Recap: [Defeating the Gazebo before Teatime](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/827993.html)*


The party destroys the magic holding the [[Cleenseau]] portal open, escapes across [[Duskmire]], and agrees to rescue a captive from a redcap fortress.



## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-10-20): The party arms itself, enters the Feywild portal, fights through the thorn maze, and destroys the ritual knot that keeps the gateway open.
- (DR:: 1720-10-20) - (DR:: 1720-10-25): Five material-world days pass during a journey that feels like only a few hours before the party reaches a pine-forest cottage.
- (DR:: 1720-10-25): Questioning the goatboy reveals that Izar the Red holds wings that may be Greymalkin's, and dinner ends with the old lady quietly approving any move against him.
- (DR:: 1720-10-26): In the morning the party trades for the goatboy, takes directions to Izar's keep, and marches halfway there before pausing to plan how to help him.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- old lady of the pine cottage: host at the cottage.
- goatboy servant: former servant of Izar the Red.
- [[Ianto]] (redcap): redcap lord who holds the goatboy's brother and collects trophies from defeated fey.



### Locations

- [[Cleenseau]] (*Klen-sew*, town, in [[Manor of Cleenseau]]): the party's starting point before it enters and destroys the portal.
- [[Duskmire]] (extraplanar domain, in [[Feywild]]): the Gloomshaper's Feywild realm, crossed while escaping east from the ruined portal.
