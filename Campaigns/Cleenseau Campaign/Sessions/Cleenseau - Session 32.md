# Cleenseau - Session 32

The party crosses into the Feywild and destroys the thorn knot holding the Cleenseau portal open. Fleeing east, they accept tightly constrained hospitality from an old fey woman. Her goat-transformed servant reports that a redcap named Ianto holds wings that may belong to Greymalkin, and the party sets out for his keep.

## Timeline

- The party enters the portal under concealment and takes a cautious route through the thorn maze.
- Radiant magic and fire destroy the central knot and its guardian, closing the portal.
- The party reaches a cottage in a pine forest and accepts the owner's hospitality.
- The goatboy identifies Ianto's trophy room as the possible location of Greymalkin's wings.
- The party trades for the goatboy's freedom and begins the journey toward Ianto's keep.
