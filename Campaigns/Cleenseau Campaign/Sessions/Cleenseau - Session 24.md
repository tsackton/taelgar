# Cleenseau - Session 24

The party returns from the Midnight Lady's destroyed refuge and tells the [[Emeric Daverre|Daverre]] household that the curse is broken. They restore the guard captain to duty, capture the ferryman and surviving bandits tied to the Hag, and stabilize local authority. A proclamation then summons the heroes to [[Veltor]].

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-03-04): The party returns to the manor and confirms the Midnight Lady's death.
- [[Roland Vaissier]] resumes command and joins the arrest of [[Edouard of Peydon]].
- The party captures or disperses the remaining bandits associated with the Hag.
- (DR:: 1720-03-05): A messenger brings the Baroness's proclamation calling the party to Veltor.
