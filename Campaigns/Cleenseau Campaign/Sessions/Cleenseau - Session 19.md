# Cleenseau - Session 19

The party frees Sabine and Istarias, then climbs the keep's tower to rescue [[Damien Montrichard]]. They defeat a [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]], escape with Damien, and expose [[Areschera]] as the fey who murdered and replaced [[Marguerite Deschamps]].

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-02-21): Sabine and Istarias escape the keep.
- (DR:: 1720-02-21): The party reaches Damien's prison and defeats the Duskhound guarding him.
- (DR:: 1720-02-21): The party escapes across the widow's walk with Damien.
- (DR:: 1720-02-21): Areschera is confronted and killed; the real Marguerite is confirmed dead.
- The magistrate delays further proceedings pending the Baroness's return.
