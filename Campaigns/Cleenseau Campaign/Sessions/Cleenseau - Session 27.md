# Cleenseau - Session 27

The party enters [[Veltor]] while Malach's opponents prepare their separate attack. They negotiate with local authorities, investigate the Baroness's ties to [[Areschera]], and use disguises and magical misdirection to gather evidence. The Baroness's faction responds by accusing Viepuck and Celyn of murder and necromancy.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-03-19): The party enters Veltor and negotiates an oath under [[Marian|Marian's]] truth-compelling miracle.
- Viepuck infiltrates the town in disguise while the others consult the magistrate and steward.
- (DR:: 1720-03-20): The party presents evidence connecting the Baroness with Areschera.
- Scrying and locate-object magic produce escalating countermoves between the two sides.
- At sunset, the party is publicly accused and held for trial.
