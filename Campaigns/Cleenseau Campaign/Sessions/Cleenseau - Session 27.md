# Cleenseau - Session 27

>[!info] Paranoia in Veltor: in which the party gathers evidence and is accused of necromancy
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-03-19) to (DR:: 1720-03-21)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, September 7, 2025*
> *[[Veltor]], [[Granite Mug]], and [[Veltor Keep]]*
> *Source: [Original player recap](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/822514.html)*

Entering [[Veltor]] under strict magical restrictions, the party gathers evidence against the Baroness until her faction accuses them of murder and necromancy.



## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-03-19): The party enters [[Veltor]], gathers local information, and uses Viepuck's disguised infiltration to test the Baroness's increasingly paranoid response.
- (DR:: 1720-03-20) - (DR:: 1720-03-21): The party shares evidence of the Baroness's fey pact, endures magical countermeasures, and is publicly accused and detained for trial.

- [[Pierre the Guard|Pierre]] (human): guard who helps the party gather local information at the [[Granite Mug]].
- [[Brunna Marrek]] (human): proprietor of the [[Granite Mug]] who offers to arrange a meeting with Etiene.
- [[Primrose Merriweather]] (halfling): halfling tailor and intelligence contact warned about the Baroness.
- [[Corrin Merriweather]] (halfling): quiet halfling tailor and intelligence contact warned about the Baroness.
- [[Etiene des Ostieux]]: captain of the guard who questions Viepuck's disguised persona and is later jailed.
- [[Marius]]: gate guard who admits Viepuck's disguised persona to the keep.
- [[Hesta Brill]]: night staffer who feeds Viepuck's disguised persona.
- [[Victorine Rosseau]] (Sembaran): chief magistrate who receives the party's evidence.
- [[Emeric Daverre|Émeric Daverre]]: castle steward who receives the party's evidence.
- [[Isabeau D'Aslain]] (Sembaran): Baroness whose pact and reactions the party investigates.
- [[Rene d'Aslain|René d'Aslain]] (Sembaran): the Baroness's nephew, who sketches the false pendant.
- [[Esme d'Aslain]] (Sembaran): the Baroness's niece, who discusses fear and magical study with the party.
- [[Annette]] (human): acting chief clerk inconvenienced by the party's move into the former fey tower.
- [[Reginald Rusebek]] (*REJ-uh-nuhld ROOZ-bek*, Sembaran): former baron recalled for having Merriweathers executed on false pretenses.



### Locations

- [[Veltor]] (*Vel-tor*, fortified village, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): baronial capital where the party investigates the Baroness under increasing suspicion.
- [[Granite Mug]] (inn, in [[Veltor]]): town inn where the party gathers information from guards and the proprietor.
- [[Veltor Keep]] (building, in [[Veltor]]): seat of the Baroness, infiltrated by [[Viepuck]] and later used to detain the party for trial.
