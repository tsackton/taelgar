# Cleenseau - Session 35

>[!info] Between Crises: in which the party studies Ianto's trophies, returns to Clawyn for Viatela, and sets out for Orenlas
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-11-16) to (DR:: 1720-12-12)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, August 2, 2026*
> *[[Twilight's Grace]], [[Orchard Close]], [[Ash Court]], [[Twilight's Pool]], [[Silver Vineyard]], [[Clawyn]], and [[Tyrwinghan Hills]]*
> *Kiya's Recap: [The existential question of what to do when there isn't a crisis](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/831585.html)*

The party investigates Ianto's trophies in [[Twilight's Grace]], returns through the [[Silver Vineyard]] to spend [[Viatela]] in [[Clawyn]], and sets out for [[Orenlas]].

## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-11-16) - (DR:: 1720-11-27): The party spends an interstitial period in [[Twilight's Grace]], sharing a meal in [[Orchard Close]] and consulting scholars in [[Ash Court]] before using [[Twilight's Pool]] to locate the living unicorn whose horn was among [[Ianto|Ianto's]] trophies.
- (DR:: 1720-11-27) - (DR:: 1720-12-11): The party rests in the [[Silver Vineyard]], returns to mortal [[Tyrwingha]], and reaches [[Clawyn]], where it remains through [[Viatela]].
- (DR:: 1720-12-12): The party leaves [[Clawyn]], travels around the mountains toward [[Orenlas]], and lets a startled owlbear retreat without a fight.

### Locations

- [[Twilight's Grace]] (extraplanar domain, in [[Feywild]]): Ethlenn's Feywild realm, where the party begins the session and researches Ianto's trophies.
- [[Orchard Close]] (neighborhood, in [[Twilight's Grace]]): a hospitable orchard neighborhood in [[Twilight's Grace]] where the party shares a meal.
- [[Ash Court]] (neighborhood, in [[Twilight's Grace]]): a scholarly neighborhood in [[Twilight's Grace]] where the party investigates the trophies.
- [[Twilight's Pool]] (fey portal in [[Twilight's Grace]]): the realm's principal crossing to [[Tafolwern]], whose waters help [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] locate the unicorn.
- [[Silver Vineyard]] (fey portal in [[Twilight's Grace]]): a shifting portal between [[Twilight's Grace]] and the southern [[Tyrwinghan Hills|Tyrwinghan hills]] through which the party returns to the mortal world.
- [[Clawyn]] (*CLOW-in*, village, in [[Tyrwingha]]): Celyn's home village, where the party remains through [[Viatela]] before resuming its journey.
- [[Tyrwinghan Hills]] (line of hills, in [[Mostreve Hills]]): the region the party crosses after leaving [[Clawyn]] on the way toward [[Orenlas]].
