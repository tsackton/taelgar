# Cleenseau - Session 35

The party remains briefly in [[Twilight's Grace]], seeking information about the trophies recovered from Ianto. They identify leads concerning several remains and locate the living owner of a unicorn horn. The party then leaves through a vineyard portal, visits Celyn's home village during a festival, and begins traveling toward [[Orenlas]].

## Timeline

- The party questions fey scholars about Ianto's trophies.
- Magic establishes that the unicorn whose horn was taken remains alive beyond the Sunset Gate.
- The party decides to return to the mortal world and eventually revisit Orenlas concerning the dragon remains.
- A vineyard portal brings the party to southern [[Tyrwingha]].
- The party visits [[Clawyn]] and then resumes its journey toward Orenlas.
