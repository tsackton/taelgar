# Cleenseau - Session 20

After Areschera's death, the party searches the keep and finds evidence of fey infiltration, concealed magical items, and possible necromantic activity connected with [[Isabeau d'Aslain]]. Leaving Veltor, they defeat harpies and enchanted bears attempting to abduct Tyrwinghan travelers and agree to escort the survivors through the [[Maudor Hills]].

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-02-21): Areschera's and the Duskhound's remains are burned under religious supervision.
- The party searches the Tower of Records and the Baroness's rooms, recovering magical objects and evidence.
- The party questions [[Rene d'Aslain]] and learns of a ruin associated with necromantic research.
- (DR:: 1720-02-25): The party leaves Veltor and interrupts the abduction of Tyrwinghan travelers.
- A shard of black glass is found to identify people protected by [[Archfey Ethlenn]].
