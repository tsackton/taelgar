# Cleenseau - Session 17

In [[Aslain]], the party safely examines a cursed letter from the bear fey and investigates the confused messenger who delivered it. In [[Cranford]], they assist with the investigation of a poisoned noblewoman and establish that the principal accused is probably innocent. They also learn that false messengers are reporting the party's death.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-02-17): The party defuses the curse embedded in the bear fey's letter.
- The party investigates the messenger's disappearance and reports of destroyed Night Queen evidence.
- In Cranford, the party examines a magical poisoning and helps prevent a premature conviction.
- (DR:: 1720-02-19): Viepuck corrects false reports of the party's death; the group departs for [[Veltor]].
- Near Veltor, Robin detects an imminent ambush.
