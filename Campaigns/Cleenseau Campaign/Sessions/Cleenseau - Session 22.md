# Cleenseau - Session 22

After freeing the Hunter's captives, the party travels to [[Peydon]] to investigate the fey power known as the [[Midnight Lady]]. Viepuck impersonates [[Gareth of Tollen]] to question her associates, while the others investigate the cursed town. The session ends after a confrontation at her scrying well shows that ordinary attacks cannot permanently harm her.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-03-01): Survivors emerge from the Hunter's cave and are reunited with evacuees.
- The party follows evidence of fey bargains and travels toward Peydon.
- Viepuck infiltrates the Midnight Lady's network in Gareth's identity.
- The party identifies the ferry, manor household, and magical well as parts of the Lady's control over Peydon.
- (DR:: 1720-03-03): The party confronts her at the well but cannot make the damage last.
