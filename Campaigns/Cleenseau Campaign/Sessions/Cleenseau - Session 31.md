# Cleenseau - Session 31

>[!info] The Queen's Escort: in which the queen nearly takes wing
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-07-05) to (DR:: 1720-10-19)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, February 1, 2026*
> *[[Asineau]] and [[Cleenseau Wood]]*
> *Kiya's Recap: [Monty Python's Flying Royalty](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/826614.html)*


After settling the dragon's aftermath, the party prevents a fey ambush from abducting the queen and prepares to close the attackers' portal.



## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-07-05): The party settles the dragon's remains in [[Asineau]] and begins mapping the surrounding [[Fey Realms|fey realms]].
- (DR:: 1720-10-15) - (DR:: 1720-10-18): Royal retainers inspect [[Asineau]], and the party prepares to escort Queen [[Elaine II]] through the swamps.
- (DR:: 1720-10-19): The party saves Queen [[Elaine II]] from the Hunter's ambush and accepts her order to close Lord Umbraeth's portal.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [[Vahaiya]] (*va-HAI-ya*, elf): elven ally who carries the dragon remains home for crafting.
- [[Elaine II]] (Sembaran): queen traveling with the entourage under the party's protection.
- [[The Hunter]] (fey): fey ambusher who leads the attempt to seize the queen.
- [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]]: teleporting fey hound that briefly captures the queen.



### Locations

- [[Asineau]] (*Ah-zee-noh*, fishing village, in [[Manor of Asineau]]): the party's home during the aftermath of the dragon hunt.
- [[Cleenseau Wood]] (forest, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): the wooded road where the fey ambush strikes the royal procession.
