# Cleenseau - Session 31

After settling the dragon's spoils and Asineau's new kobold residents, the party prepares for [[Elaine II|Queen Elaine II's]] visit. [[The Hunter]] ambushes the royal escort and a Duskhound seizes the Queen, but the party rescues her, kills the Hunter, and runs down the fleeing hound. Elaine orders the party to close [[Lord Umbraeth|Lord Umbraeth's]] portal.

## Timeline

- Dragon remains are distributed and the party expands its map of nearby fey realms.
- Royal retainers prepare Asineau for the Queen's visit.
- The party escorts Elaine through the swamp road.
- The Hunter's servants attack and briefly abduct the Queen.
- The Hunter and Duskhound are killed; Elaine orders an immediate attack on the portal.
