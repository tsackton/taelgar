# Interlude: After the Siege of Fellburn

> [!info]+ Session Info
> (DR:: 1720-01-25)
> [[Fellburn]] and the [[Oracle of Hope]]
> by email: May 24th–June 1st, 2024

## Summary

After the siege, the party exchanges news with the victors, witnesses the succession of Fellburn, and follows several personal leads before returning north.

## Cast

- [[Jacques Bellemont]]: Duke of Wisford, who appoints Elise to govern Fellburn and prepares to travel to Embry
- [[Marcel Desrosiers]]: captain of the Duke's household guard, who briefs the party on the undead attacks
- [[Yolande Leclair]]: disciple of the Night Queen who explains the clergy's warnings and response
- [[Hubert Moreau]]: disinherited heir exiled from Wisford for abandoning Fellburn's people
- [[Elise Moreau]]: Hubert's sister, appointed Baroness of Fellburn
- [[Charlotte of Mahaut's Players|Charlotte]]: former companion of Celyn who delivers a cryptic message
- [[Hugh Cosson]]: actor traveling with Charlotte
- [[Lenora Belles]]: Night Queen paladin whom Viepuck thanks for saving him years earlier
- [[Raoul Boulain]]: missing Night Queen elder discussed by Yolande and Armand
- [[Omphale]]: centaur prophet near Windlea whom Armand had been seeking

## Locations

- [[Fellburn]]: city recovering from the defeated undead siege
- [[Oracle of Hope]]: ancient singing crystal above Fellburn where Celyn meets Charlotte
