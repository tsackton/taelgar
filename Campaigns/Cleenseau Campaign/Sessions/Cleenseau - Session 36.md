## People and Places

- [[Arianwen]] (she/her, human): human Ranger captain who holds the entrance stable while the rescue proceeds.
- [[Bryn]] (human): human [[Plaguelands]] scout recovered from the folded ravine.
- [[Eira]] (elf): elven emissary and healer recovered from the shelter beneath the glass rain.
- [[Maelona]] (lizardfolk): lizardfolk planar researcher found dead beside [[Taliesin]].
- [[Taliesin]] (human): human feywise pathfinder whose identity and memories are copied by the doppelganger.
- the doppelganger: psychic construct that copies [[Taliesin]] and attempts to end the rescue before the original is found.

### Organizations

- [[Rangers]] (army): a five-person embassy traveling east to seek advice and aid from [[Orenlas]].

- a wooded ridge in the [[Plaguelands]]: on the way to [[Orenlas]] where the [[Rangers]] hold open the unstable overlap with [[Limbo]].
