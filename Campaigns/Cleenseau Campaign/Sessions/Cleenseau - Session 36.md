# Cleenseau - Session 36

On the road toward [[Orenlas]], the party finds Rangers holding open a path into a pocket realm where belief alters reality and time. They enter to recover three missing Rangers, destroy manifestations of a shadow-weasel creature, expose a false copy of one Ranger, and escape with two survivors and one body.

## Timeline

- The party helps two Rangers defend the anchors holding open the pocket realm.
- Celyn, Robin, and Viepuck enter while Izgil remains outside with the Ranger commander.
- The rescuers defeat separated manifestations of a shadow-weasel and make contact with the missing scouts.
- Viepuck exposes one apparent Ranger as a constructed impostor, which the party destroys.
- The group finds the real Ranger and a dead companion, then escapes before the realm collapses.
