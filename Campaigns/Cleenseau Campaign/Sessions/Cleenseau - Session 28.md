# Cleenseau - Session 28

>[!info] The End of Malach: in which a false trial becomes the lich's final intervention
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-03-21) to (DR:: 1720-04-01)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, October 5, 2025*
> *[[Veltor]], [[Veltor Keep]], and [[Asineau]]*
> *Source: [Original player recap](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/823914.html)*

At their trial, the party exposes the lich behind the false charges and survives his final attack, after which [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] accepts stewardship of [[Asineau]].



## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-03-21): At the party's trial, exposing the lich behind the false charges triggers a deadly shadow attack that the party defeats.
- (DR:: 1720-03-26) - (DR:: 1720-04-01): A royal messenger confirms Malach's defeat, takes the Baroness into custody, and offers [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] stewardship of [[Asineau]].

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [[Thomas Dyerson]] (Sembaran): circuit magistrate who presents the indictment despite objecting to the trial's irregularities.
- [[Marian]]: Warlord cleric whose miracle compels truthful testimony at the trial.
- [[Etiene des Ostieux]]: captain of the guard who arranges the trial while refusing to turn against the party.
- [[Isabeau D'Aslain]] (Sembaran): Baroness who directs the false prosecution and later claims amnesia.
- [[Perette]] (Sembaran): the Baroness's chamberlain, who gives false testimony against the party.
- [[Malach]] (undead): lich exposed during the trial whose shadow manifestation attacks the court.
- [[Vivian]]: murdered guard whose death is used as the basis for the false charges.
- [[Lyra Corvin]]: royal messenger who confirms Malach's defeat and offers [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] stewardship of [[Asineau]].



### Locations

- [[Veltor]] (*Vel-tor*, fortified village, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): town where the party remains through the trial and its aftermath.
- [[Veltor Keep]] (building, in [[Veltor]]): site of the trial and Malach's final shadow attack.
- [[Asineau]] (*Ah-zee-noh*, fishing village, in [[Manor of Asineau]]): neglected village placed under Robin's stewardship at the end of the session.
