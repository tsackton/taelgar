# Cleenseau - Session 28

At the party's trial, Viepuck publicly identifies [[Robert I]] as the lich [[Malach]]. Malach responds through a summoned shadow that kills and reanimates bystanders, but the party destroys it. News later confirms Malach's defeat, [[Isabeau d'Aslain]] is taken into custody, and [[Elaine II]] offers Robin the lordship of [[Asineau]].

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-03-20): The party answers the necromancy charges under Marian's divine compulsion.
- Viepuck names Malach; a shadow manifestation attacks the court and raises zombies.
- The party destroys the manifestation and protects many of the witnesses.
- (DR:: 1720-03-26): A royal messenger confirms Malach's defeat and the accession of Elaine II.
- (DR:: 1720-04-01): The party reaches Asineau and Robin accepts responsibility for the manor.
