# Cleenseau - Session 21

The party escorts the Tyrwinghan travelers south while investigating the black-glass tracking shard. False reports that the heroes are fey impostors complicate their passage. During the night, [[The Hunter]] abducts Robin; the others follow to his cave, rescue captives, and force the Hunter to retreat.

## Timeline

- The party questions the glass shard and learns that related pieces are used to identify targets favored by Ethlenn.
- The travelers reach a safer route south despite hostile rumors in [[Maudorville]].
- During a night attack, Robin is carried away while Greymalkin fights the attackers.
- The party tracks Robin to the Hunter's cave, frees captives, and attacks the Hunter and his servants.
- The Hunter escapes after the party defeats his immediate forces.
