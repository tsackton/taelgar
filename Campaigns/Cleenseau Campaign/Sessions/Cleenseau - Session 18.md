# Cleenseau - Session 18

The party survives an ambush outside [[Veltor]] and investigates who hired the attackers. Inside the town, they find [[Damien Montrichard]] accused of the crime, [[Sabine de Brune]] under guard, and evidence that a shapeshifting fey has infiltrated the Baroness's household.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-02-19): Assassins and bandits ambush the party on the road to Veltor.
- (DR:: 1720-02-19): Baronial guards help end the fight and take surviving attackers into custody.
- (DR:: 1720-02-20): The party learns that a captured assassin died and Damien has been blamed for the attack.
- (DR:: 1720-02-20): The party meets Sabine and [[Istarias]] and identifies further signs of fey interference.
- (DR:: 1720-02-21): The party prepares to free the prisoners and expose the infiltrator.
