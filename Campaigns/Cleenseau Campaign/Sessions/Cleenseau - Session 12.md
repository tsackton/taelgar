# Cleenseau - Session 12

Celyn and Viepuck catch [[Lorin Valbert]] and recover most of the money and horses he removed from [[Asineau]]. After returning the resources to the regional defense, the party rallies local forces against a tower-like mass of bones and roughly sixty zombies approaching [[Cleenseau]].

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-01-12) to (DR_end:: 1720-01-13): The party catches Lorin, negotiates the return of public resources, and allows him to continue east.
- (DR:: 1720-01-14): Scouts report a large undead force approaching Cleenseau.
- (DR:: 1720-01-14): The party and local forces defeat the bone construct and its zombies before they reach the walls.
- (DR:: 1720-01-15): The party rides south after learning that the threat at [[Eftly]] has already been defeated.
