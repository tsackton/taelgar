# Cleenseau - Session 29

Robin begins rebuilding [[Asineau]] as a defended and economically viable settlement. When [[Vahaiya]] reports a dragon attacking the eastern borderlands, the party travels to Marwick, scouts the ruined uplands, and prepares an ambush with a ballista. Captured kobolds provide intelligence and some agree to assist against the dragon.

## Timeline

- Robin takes possession of Asineau and begins improvements to its defenses, workshops, and trade.
- Vahaiya brings news of a dragon attacking settlements near Marwick.
- The party scouts the destroyed summer villages and prepares a trapped battlefield.
- A kobold foraging party surrenders and describes divisions among the dragon's followers.
- After midnight, the dragon and five kobolds approach the ambush.
