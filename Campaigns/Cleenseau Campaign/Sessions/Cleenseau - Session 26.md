# Cleenseau - Session 26

>[!info] Delivering the Phylactery: in which the party crosses the old forest and guards a planar exchange
> *Featuring: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1720-03-12) to (DR:: 1720-03-19)*
> *On Earth: Sunday, July 27, 2025*
> *[[Cleenseau Wood]], [[Beldor]], and [[Aveil Ridge]]*
> *Source: [Original player recap](https://kiya.dreamwidth.org/816893.html)*

The party carries the phylactery through [[Cleenseau Wood]], survives several supernatural threats, transfers it for destruction, and defeats fiends displaced by the planar exchange.



## Timeline



- (DR:: 1720-03-12) - (DR:: 1720-03-18): The party crosses [[Cleenseau Wood]] with the phylactery fragment, survives several supernatural threats, and transfers it to the group preparing to destroy it.
- (DR:: 1720-03-18) - (DR:: 1720-03-19): Near [[Beldor]], the party guards the planar departure and defeats the fiends displaced into Taelgar.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [[Raoul Boulain]] (Sembaran): Night Queen elder helping lead the effort to destroy [[Malach's Phylactery|Malach's phylactery]].
- [[Lenora Belles]] (Sembaran): Night Queen paladin who secures the fragment in a protective locket.
- [[Annabelle]]: zealot warrior accompanying Raoul and Lenora.
- [[Lucien of the Night Queen]]: celestial-patron warlock accompanying Raoul and Lenora.
- the forest lights: intelligent lights that accept the party's promise to leave their home peacefully.



### Locations

- [[Cleenseau Wood]] (forest, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): old forest crossed while carrying the phylactery fragment to the rendezvous.
- [[Beldor]] (mining village, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): mining village used as the staging point for the planar expedition.
- [[Aveil Ridge]] (*Ah-vayl Ridge*, ridge, in [[Barony of Aveil]]): high ground near [[Veltor]] where the groups prepare the planar exchange.
