# Cleenseau - Session 26

The party carries the phylactery fragment through [[Cleenseau Wood]] to Raoul's companions. Along the way they defeat shadow creatures and a basilisk and avoid conflict with unusual lights. After handing over the fragment, they guard the allied party's planar departure and destroy the fiends displaced into Taelgar.

## Timeline

- The phylactery produces nightmares and shadow attackers during the journey west.
- The party kills a basilisk in magically silencing rain.
- A peaceful exchange prevents a fight with the lights surrounding the camp.
- The party transfers the phylactery to Raoul's allies.
- (DR:: 1720-03-19): The allies travel to destroy the fragments; the party kills the fiends that arrive in their place and moves toward [[Veltor]].
