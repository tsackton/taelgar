# Cleenseau - Session 11

The party heals survivors from [[Ida Rosfeld|Ida's]] battered garrison and leads a council that funds a larger regional force based at [[Asineau]]. In Asineau, Viepuck and Izgil convince [[Lorin Valbert]] that hostile fey are targeting him; he abandons the manor soon afterward.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1720-01-09): Ida reaches Cleenseau with wounded survivors.
- (DR:: 1720-01-10): Regional leaders fund an expanded garrison under Ida.
- (DR:: 1720-01-11): The party visits Asineau and deceives Lorin into believing the manor is under a fey curse.
- (DR:: 1720-01-12): Lorin disappears and renounces his claim to the manor.
