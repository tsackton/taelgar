# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party investigates Gentza's murder and connects her death to stolen flour and medical experiments.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->

After sharing their suspicions about François, the party uncovers the poisoning during the [[Festival of the Bridge]], pursues the fleeing bandits, and defeats them near an ankheg tunnel.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Erdu]] approaches Rosalind for help investigating the murder of [[Gentza]], a young lizardfolk woman from [[Ganboa]]. The party questions [[Izoko]], follows the evidence through [[Asineau]], examines Gentza's abandoned boat and medical work, speaks with [[Arnold the Miller]] and [[Mermin Stonebridge]], and learns of stolen flour before identifying François as a suspect.

The party shares its suspicions about François with Rosalind. During the [[Festival of the Bridge]], [[Istarias]]—still unknown to the party—warns [[Celyn]] not to eat the bread just before [[Viepuck]] recognizes the poisoning scheme. The party pursues François, [[Jerome]], Terry, and Robert from the celebration and defeats them near their hideout after an ankheg joins the fight.
