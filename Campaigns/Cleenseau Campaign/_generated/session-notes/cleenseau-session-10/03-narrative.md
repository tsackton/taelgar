# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
The party defeats animated armor and a hobgoblin wight, then destroys the green gem producing the plague-bearing mist.

The party reaches [[Cleenseau Bridge]], helps the remaining guards defeat a dozen zombies, and meets allies bringing news of other attacks.

The party recovers old treasure from the ruined fort and receives news from Rosalind by letter.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
Beyond the portal, the party fights decayed animated armor and an unnamed hobgoblin wight amid the white mist. They recover the [[Circlet of Telepathy]] from the armor. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] channels the Wanderer's power to destroy the green gem producing the mist, after which the infected rats die. The party then meets [[Greymalkin]] and rides toward [[Cleenseau Bridge]].

The party rides to [[Cleenseau Bridge]] and helps the remnants of the [[Lord's Guard of Cleenseau]] defeat a dozen zombies. At dawn, [[Erick Murtha]], [[Warin the Woodsman]], and [[Beatrix Thorne]] meet the party with reports of undead attacks elsewhere in the region. The group begins planning a regional defense council.

The party completes its search of the [[Ruined Fort (Cleenseau)|ruined fort]] and finds an old treasury containing valuables and the [[Warhammer of Vigilance]]. Later, it receives a [[Letter from Rosalind (January 6th)|letter]] from [[Rosalind Essford]].
