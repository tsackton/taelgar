# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party spreads out the ballista crew, the decoy, and the allied kobolds, then waits for the [[Dragons|dragon]] to enter the trap.

When the [[Dragons|dragon]] dives in, the plan frays into chaos, but the party brings it down and forces the surviving hostile kobolds to yield.

With the field won, the allied kobolds heap tribute on [[Greymalkin]], and the party uses the pause to slip through a back tunnel into the lair.

Inside the lair, the last kobold resistance collapses quickly, and the party spends the rest of the push clearing traps, claiming the [[Dragons|dragon]] loot, and deciding what to do with the carcass.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Plans that have clearly been in the making since the prior cliffhanger are finally put in place. [[Viepuck]] prepares to serve as the decoy, [[Celyn]] and the ballista hold a separate position, and the allied kobolds stay back while the group readies the magic and timing meant to bring the [[Dragons|dragon]] down.

The dragon's opening sweeps burn through key defenses and shrug off some of Izgil's magic, turning the encounter into a messy scramble across the prepared field. Even so, [[Celyn]] keeps finding openings, [[Viepuck]] continues the assault, [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] holds the line, and [[Greymalkin]] joins the aerial chase until the wounded [[Dragons|dragon]] can no longer escape.

Victory gives the party only a short breathing space. The allied kobolds decide [[Greymalkin]] must be the real leader, reward him with a belt buckle and more tribute, and then send the party on with what little extra information they can provide about the lair and its hidden way in.

The final lair fight barely has time to become dangerous before it is over, with [[Celyn]] and [[Viepuck]] dropping the kobold leader before she can show what she was meant to do. From there the action shifts straight into cleanup as the party loots the barracks, works through the trapped tunnel, and reaches the treasure at the far end.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The source begins by reestablishing who is present and what the party has spent its time preparing since the last break in play. [[Viepuck]] is set to impersonate a kobold, [[Celyn]] and the ballista are placed apart from the main cluster, and the allied kobolds are deliberately kept well away from the center of the coming fight. Everything is arranged around one blunt objective: survive the first pass, ground the [[Dragons|dragon]], and turn the battlefield into confusion before it can simply terrorize the party into collapse.

The first exchanges go badly enough to prove that the plan is only a plan once the [[Dragons|dragon]] actually starts moving. Fire tears through the field, the ballista fails to produce the hoped-for breakthrough, and the [[Dragons|dragon]] repeatedly resists the party's attempts to control the fight. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] stays between danger and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]], [[Celyn]] turns invisibility and mobility into brutal close strikes, [[Viepuck]] keeps applying pressure, and [[Greymalkin]] takes a fly potion so he can carry [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] into the air for the final pursuit. Once the [[Dragons|dragon]] crashes, the remaining hostile kobolds lose whatever nerve they had left, and the field ends in surrender rather than a second clean battle.

The aftermath is comic enough to keep the victory from settling into ordinary triumph. The allied kobolds, impressed by Greymalkin's flight, elevate him into a figure of command, shower him with gifts, and use him to cow a captive enemy. That burst of celebration ends quickly once the party hears what it needs to hear and moves on to the back entrance. [[Celyn]] scouts through the tight tunnel, [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] solves the worst bottleneck with dimension door, a few kobolds are quietly neutralized before they can raise the alarm properly, and the whole group manages to gather inside the lair with surprise still mostly intact.

Once the guards stirred by the tunnel disturbance come back, the last meaningful resistance of the session proves brief and one-sided. [[Celyn]] lands the decisive blow almost immediately, [[Viepuck]] finishes the leader, and the remaining defenders stop mattering as individual threats. The rest of the beat turns into a methodical advance through the lair's hazards: tripwires, spikes, a grease trap, and the front-door mechanism near the treasure all have to be understood or bypassed before the party can settle over the [[Dragons|dragon]] loot. By the end, triumph gives way to logistics, because killing a [[Dragons|dragon]] is easier than deciding what to do with one afterward.
