# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party gathers news, interrogates Gareth, and finds Odo's younger brother.

The party defeats fey attackers and bargains with [[The Hunter|the Hunter]].

The party brings the [[Ashcombe]] refugees to [[Aslain]].
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
[[Viepuck]] coordinates the party's allies, Samuel is found and escorted to Odo, and Gareth reveals his dealings with the [[Midnight Lady]].

After investigating disappearances near [[Maudorville]], the party defeats the Hunter's servants, negotiates a temporary truce, and evacuates [[Ashcombe]].

The party escorts Ashcombe's people to [[Aslain]] and receives a suspicious Sylvan letter apparently sent by [[The Hunter|the Hunter]].
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Viepuck]] uses sending magic to gather news and coordinate allies. [[Samuel Cordwaner]], [[Odo Cordwaner|Odo's]] younger brother, is found hiding in the local hills and escorted to safety in [[Essenmer]]. Before his execution, [[Gareth of Tollen]] describes his dealings with the [[Midnight Lady]], giving the party a new lead concerning the hostile fey active in the barony.

Traveling north through [[Essenmer]], the party reaches [[Maudorville]]. [[Wilhem of Maudorville]] discusses lunar lore with [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]], [[Antoine Chevalier]] hosts the party, and [[Susanna]] reports that [[Amelie]] and [[Anna]] are missing. While investigating in the [[Maudor Hills]], the party defeats harpies, strange bears, and a giant vulture, then follows the surviving fey to [[The Hunter]]. [[Celyn]] negotiates a temporary truce with [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] and his unnamed duskhound and learns that three fey powers are competing in the region. The party then persuades [[Ameline Evynwood]] to evacuate [[Ashcombe]].

The party escorts the people of [[Ashcombe]] to [[Aslain]], where they reunite with their cavalry escort. An innkeeper delivers a suspicious letter written in Sylvan and addressed to the party; they suspect that it came from [[The Hunter]].
