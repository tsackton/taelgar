# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party reunites and prepares [[Cleenseau]] for further attacks.

The party and local troops destroy two groups of undead raised at the new moon.

The party exposes and arrests a fraudulent tax collector.

Gareth is convicted and executed for his crimes.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
On the road between [[Dallet]] and [[Beury]], the party reunites with [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and the [[Rangers]] returning from the [[East Bog]]. [[Vahaiya]] remains near the fey portal while [[Tristan Vaudrillard]], [[Remille Vauclaire]], and [[Emilie]] continue north. In [[Cleenseau]], the party helps [[Ida Rosfeld]] incorporate survivors of the [[Army of the South]] into the local defenses. They train the garrison against magical threats, consult [[Brot Starsearcher]] and [[Diesla Starsearcher]] about defensive engines, correspond with [[Rosalind Essford]] and [[Susanne Garay]], and receive news concerning [[Lenora Belles]] and [[Raoul Boulain]]. [[Rene Dubois]], [[Claire of Fellburn]], and [[Bertrand Leclerc]] arrive with the southern soldiers. Es*tiasilos scouts [[Enford]], and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] inducts [[Odo Cordwaner]] into the [[Order of the Charitable Wanderer]].

At the new moon, zombies approach from the nearby wood while orc and ogre skeletons advance toward [[Cleenseau]]. The party divides the local forces, destroys both groups, and tests the town's new defenses. [[Rinault Essford]] joins the fighting.

While checking nearby settlements, the party encounters [[Gareth of Tollen]] and his crew collecting illegal taxes. [[Viepuck]] recognizes Gareth from the [[Lord Mayor’s Workhouse]], uncovers the fraud, and helps the party prevent his escape. [[Elizabeth of Cassen|Elizabeth of Cassens]] and other honest guards help secure the prisoners. The party returns the recovered money to [[Erick Murtha]].

In [[Cleenseau]], Gareth is convicted of banditry, fraud, and his earlier crimes and is executed. His principal accomplice is also condemned, while two lesser bandits are assigned to the local army after one gives evidence against him.
