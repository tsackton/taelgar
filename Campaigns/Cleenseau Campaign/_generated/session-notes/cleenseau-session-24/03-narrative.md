# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party reports the Midnight Lady's death and helps restore local authority in [[Peydon]].

The party arrests Edouard and detains a would-be informant attempting to reach the docks.

The party pursues the escaped gang, defeats those who resist, and returns the survivors to Roland.

The Baroness summons the party to Veltor.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
The Daverre household and the people of [[Peydon]] celebrate their freedom while Roland resumes his duties as captain of the guard.

An unnamed man offers evidence against Edouard while the party forces the fleeing ferryman back to shore and takes both men to the manor.

After the informant helps Edouard and other gang members escape, the party overtakes them outside [[Peydon]] and recovers the surviving fugitives.

An unnamed messenger delivers a proclamation praising the party's victories over the fey and calling them to the capital.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party returns to Daverre Manor and explains the Midnight Lady's death to [[Emeric Daverre]], [[Estelle Daverre]], [[Odile Daverre]], and the household staff. Emeric destroys the glass through which the hag had watched the manor. As word spreads, [[Peydon]] erupts into celebration. The party rouses [[Roland Vaissier]] and helps him resume command of the local guard. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] recovers the Mirror of Whispers, now a nonmagical but valuable mirror.

As the party accompanies [[Roland Vaissier]] to arrest [[Edouard of Peydon]], [[Celyn]] intercepts an unnamed man sneaking toward the docks. The man claims that he is willing to give evidence against Edouard. When the ferryman pushes away from shore and ignores orders to return, [[Celyn]] teleports aboard and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] walks across the [[Auberonne]] to drag the boat back by its mooring rope. The party interrogates Edouard and takes him and the purported informant back toward the manor, where [[Celyn]] notices Edouard trying to pass the other man a message.

The unnamed informant bribes someone to release Edouard and several other gang members from their temporary jail. Guided by Es*tiaslos, the party pursues the fugitives on borrowed horses. [[Brigitte Charpentier]], [[Tibault the Butcher]], and [[Noemie Vallier]] are killed after resisting. [[Pierrot the Weasel]] surrenders and provides information, including the identity of [[Mireille the Herbalist]], who is then arrested. The party returns the survivors to [[Roland Vaissier]]. The false informant is branded and expelled from [[Peydon]]. After completing his research, [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] finds that his lunar bracers have merged with his skin.

After the party finally gets a full night's sleep, an unnamed messenger arrives with the Baroness's proclamation. It praises the [[Heroes of Cleenseau|heroes of Cleenseau]] for destroying hostile fey and summons them to Veltor to receive their reward.
