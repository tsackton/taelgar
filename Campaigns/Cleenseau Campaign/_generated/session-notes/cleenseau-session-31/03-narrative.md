# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
Back in [[Asineau]], the party sorts dragon spoils, absorbs its new kobold followers into village life, and begins a more serious map of the surrounding [[Fey Realms|fey realms]].

Early in October, royal retainers descend on [[Asineau]], and what begins as hospitality planning ends with the party committing to escort Queen [[Elaine II]] through the swamps.

On the swamp road [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] springs his ambush with red-eyed elk and a [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]], but the party rescues [[Elaine II]], kills [[The Hunter|the Hunter]], and runs down the fleeing hound before it can warn [[Lord Umbraeth]].

In the aftermath, the party tells the queen that only closing the portal will meaningfully blunt the threat, and she orders them to ride on, sleep, and strike it in the morning.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Back in [[Asineau]], victory over the dragon gives way to practical cleanup as [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] sorts useful remains, [[Vahaiya]] carries off the best trophies, and Robin's new village keeps adjusting to life with kobold dependents and lizardfolk trade. At the same time, [[Celyn]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] turn their attention to a growing map of the Feywild, comparing what they know of Ethlenn, [[Lord Umbraeth]], Greymalkin's lost realm, and rumors from farther west.

Early in October, the queen's advance people arrive to determine whether [[Asineau]] can manage even a single night of royal hospitality. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Celyn]], and the others muddle through food, lodging, wine, and decorum, then settle on the more important work of riding out ahead and escorting [[Elaine II]] through the swamp road themselves.

The escort through the swamps turns ugly the moment the kobold scouts report red-eyed elk charging the baggage train, a sure sign that [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] is near. The fight escalates from a hard roadside melee into outright crisis when a [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]] grabs Queen [[Elaine II]], but [[Viepuck]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] improvise a wildly effective rescue, [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] is beaten into paste, and the last fleeing hound is hunted down before it can escape.

With the ambush broken, the party explains that killing the attackers does not solve the larger problem while [[Lord Umbraeth]] still has a working portal into the region. [[Elaine II]] accepts that answer at once and turns the next day into an explicit royal order: get her safely to Cleenseau, gather what they can, and close the portal before the enemy can reset.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
Back in [[Asineau]], the party is still living in the long shadow of the dragon kill. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] separates out the best pieces of the carcass for future magical work, while [[Vahaiya]] takes the marketable remains back toward elven lands to place them with a crafter who will eventually answer in kind. The kobolds who survived the expedition have now attached themselves to [[Greymalkin]], which unexpectedly helps Robin's project of making [[Asineau]] a more open and prosperous village, especially as the local lizardfolk youth and the human teenagers settle into a mix of rivalry and friendship around the new glassworks. Meanwhile, [[Celyn]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] begin the more strategic labor of assembling a real map of nearby [[Fey Realms|fey realms]] and their mortal correspondences, tying together what they know of Ethlenn's lands, Lord Umbraeth's reach, Greymalkin's conquered home, and word of another portal being destroyed in western Addermarch.

Early in October, [[Asineau]] receives the sort of visit that makes everyone's station suddenly feel very visible. A small corps of royal retainers arrives to determine whether the village can host Queen [[Elaine II]] for one night on her circuit through Aveil, and the answer is effectively yes, provided nobody expects luxury from the poorest village in the region. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] fields awkward questions about whether he has ever hosted an entourage before, [[Celyn]] quietly enjoys being able to produce Tyrwinghan wine from home, and the whole exercise turns into a crash course in how to stage a royal stop without pretending [[Asineau]] is anything it is not. By the end of the planning, the real issue is not manners but danger, so the party commits to heading out a day early, camping beyond the swamps, and escorting the queen's train through the dangerous stretch themselves.

The escort through the swamps goes bad exactly the way the party feared it would. The kobold scouts return with word of giant deer with the same red-eyed corruption [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] has used before, and within moments [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] is shielding the entourage, [[Viepuck]] is keeping a telepathic link open, [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] is tracking [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] by his spear, and [[Celyn]] is slipping into cover to strike from the side. The first exchanges are dangerous but manageable until a [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]] teleports into the royal ring and actually seizes Queen [[Elaine II]], turning the ambush into a desperate rescue. [[Viepuck]] solves the immediate problem by sending [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] into the melee with a Fly spell, and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] follows with a Dimension Door straight upward that hauls the queen clear before the abductors can extract her. With his prize lost, [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] rages, orders the [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]] to warn [[Lord Umbraeth]], and is promptly beaten to death. The hound nearly makes good on the order, but [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Greymalkin]] run it down, sweep [[Celyn]] into the chase, and stay on it long enough for the last warning to die on the road as well.

Once the road is briefly secure, the party has to explain the more uncomfortable truth: this victory has bought time, not safety. [[Celyn]] lays out that so long as Lord Umbraeth's portal remains active, the threat to Aveil and to the queen herself will keep renewing in one form or another. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] makes no secret of how exhausted he is by the entire business, but that does not change the answer, and [[Elaine II]] asks plainly whether the only real solution is to close the portal. When [[Celyn]] says yes, she immediately instructs the party to do it. That turns the end of the session into a hard pivot toward the next mission: get the queen safely to Cleenseau, stop briefly in [[Asineau]], see whether the research has yielded anything more, sleep, and strike the portal at first light before [[Lord Umbraeth]] can reinforce it.
