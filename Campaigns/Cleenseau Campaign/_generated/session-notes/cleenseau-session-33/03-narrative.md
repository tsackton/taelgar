<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party travels through the eerily shifting landscape toward the black stone peak that houses [[Ianto]], his redcaps, and the goblins forced to serve them. After resting in the eternal late afternoon, the group uses [[Snikket|Snikket's]] fading memories to establish a rough map and learn that the goblins fear one tunnel in particular. Near the fortress, they follow an old blood trail into the shallow valley below the hollow peak, leave [[Snikket]] concealed in a thicket, and prepare to draw out the guards.

[[Viepuck]] disguises himself as [[Snikket]] and draws the first guards into an ambush. After eliminating two successive patrols, the party climbs the outer rock and looks down into the fortress's central opening. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and [[Viepuck]] descend under feather fall, bombard the assembled redcaps, and escape before the defenders can respond. The party then divides: [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] clear the redcaps feeding in the butchery while the others descend into the courtyard and attack the throne room. [[Ianto]] charges into the fight and briefly knocks [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] unconscious, but the party restores him and kills the redcap lord along with his remaining defenders.

The party searches the captured fortress and finds a group of surviving goblins: [[Krat]], [[Garn]], [[Droog]], and [[Grib]]. They call for Snikket's brother, explain that [[Ianto]] is dead, and offer the uncertain survivors a chance to leave with them. In the trophy vault they recover Greymalkin's wings, a silver unicorn horn, a brilliant tail feather, intertwined remains from a stag and a wood-creature, wrought-black elk horns, and the rest of Ianto's collection of body parts, armor, and trophies.
