# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party brings the Hunter's surviving captives home and performs rites for the dead.

The party hears that [[Peydon]] has become isolated and oppressive under a mysterious woman's influence.

The party questions the people of [[Peydon]] while the [[Midnight Lady]] watches and bargains with them through reflective surfaces.

The party recovers the Midnight Lady's scrying mirror but discovers that she can heal herself through a hidden poppet.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
[[Avice]] and two adult survivors emerge from the abandoned lair, receive care from the party, and return to [[Ashcombe]] before the party departs.

Rumors in [[Houille]] describe Peydon's displaced market, hostile atmosphere, false protective charms, and a supposed ruined temple.

After refusing the Midnight Lady's bargain, the party follows guarded clues from Peydon's residents and locates a place beyond her sight.

After opening the bricked-up well, the party finds the Midnight Lady's mirror and fights her long enough to expose the source of her unnatural recovery.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
After [[The Hunter]] abandons his lair, the enchantments on his bears and ravens fade and the animals flee. [[Avice]], a young girl from [[Ashcombe]], emerges first, followed by two adult survivors in poor condition. The party gives them weirdberries, hears their basic account, and explores the cave. [[Viepuck]] returns the survivors to their village, and the party stays through the morning to perform funeral rites for those who died in the caves.

The party reaches [[Houille]], the village across the river from [[Peydon]]. Residents complain that [[Peydon]] has taken over the local market and describe the town as hostile and unnaturally quiet. The party hears of anti-scrying charms sold by [[Ameline Lefevre]], a girl now afraid of mirrors, and a supposed ruined temple in the hills. They spend the night before crossing the river.

[[Viepuck]] crosses the river disguised as [[Gareth of Tollen]] and questions [[Edouard of Peydon]], while the others prevent Edouard from placing [[Mara]] in the Midnight Lady's debt. At the inn, the [[Midnight Lady]] offers information about the Gloomshaper if the party leaves [[Peydon]] to her, but they refuse. She then watches and taunts them through glass, water, and polished metal. The party speaks with innkeeper [[Gautier Leclair]], [[Violet]], [[Guillaume the Woodcutter]], [[Marc Delacroix]], [[Eloi Delacroix]], and [[Almeric]]. [[Ysanne]] finally leads them into Mother's Grove, where the [[Midnight Lady]] cannot see them, and explains that her sheep refuse to approach the town square.

Guided by Ysanne's warning, the party opens the bricked-up well in Peydon's town square and retrieves the Mirror of Whispers, the focus that allows the [[Midnight Lady]] to watch through reflective surfaces. The [[Midnight Lady]] demands its return and attacks when the party refuses. Every wound inflicted on her vanishes when she disappears and touches a poppet inside a bone dollhouse. While the others keep her occupied, [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] heads toward [[Widow Aalis|Widow Aalis's house]] to investigate the lair behind her power.
