# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party carries the phylactery fragment through [[Cleenseau Wood]] and defeats shadows and a basilisk drawn across its path.

The party communicates peacefully with intelligent forest lights and delivers the phylactery fragment to the group preparing to destroy it.

The party guards the planar departure, kills the fiends displaced into Taelgar, and continues toward [[Veltor]].
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Nightmares and shadow creatures trouble the party as it carries the phylactery west. A basilisk attacks beneath magically silencing rain but is killed before the journey continues.

Viepuck's colored illusions convince a ring of intelligent lights that the travelers mean no harm. The party then meets Raoul, Lenora, [[Annabelle]], and Lucien and transfers the fragment into Lenora's protective locket.

After traveling through [[Beldor]], the groups prepare a trapped departure site near [[Aveil Ridge]]. Raoul's party leaves to destroy the phylactery fragments, and the party defeats the fiends that arrive in exchange.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party carries the phylactery fragment west through [[Cleenseau Wood]]. Its influence causes paranoia and nightmares, and shadows manifest around it during the night. Farther west, magically silencing rain conceals a basilisk, which badly wounds [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] before the party kills it. The party rests and continues west.

When intelligent lights encircle the camp, [[Viepuck]] responds with colored illusions and establishes enough communication to show that the party will leave at sunrise. The lights accept this and depart without violence. The party later meets [[Raoul Boulain]], [[Lenora Belles]], [[Annabelle]], and [[Lucien of the Night Queen]]. After the groups confirm one another's identities, Lenora secures [[Malach's Phylactery|the phylactery fragment]] inside her protective locket.

The groups travel through [[Beldor]] and prepare a defended site near [[Aveil Ridge]] for the planar exchange. When Raoul's party departs with the phylactery fragments, imps and larger fiends appear in their place. The party contains them inside the prepared trap, defeats them, and continues toward [[Veltor]].
