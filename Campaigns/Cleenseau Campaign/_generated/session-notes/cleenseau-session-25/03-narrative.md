# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
Raoul reveals Malach's impersonation of the dead king and assigns the party one fragment of the lich's phylactery.

The party travels toward [[Tharwa Fea]] while [[Viepuck]] monitors the Baroness's search through an [[Aslain]] innkeeper.

The party explores [[Tharwa Fea]], secures [[Malach's Phylactery|Malach's phylactery]] fragment, and escapes its guardian.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Raoul tells the party that [[Malach]] has impersonated King Robert for four years and divided his phylactery into three hidden fragments.

On the road to [[Tharwa Fea]], [[Viepuck]] arranges for the unnamed innkeeper of [[The Setting Sun|the Setting Sun]] to report inquiries about his alias.

After passing the academy's enchanted gate, the party finds the phylactery fragment above a hidden study and seals it inside a lead box.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party avoids the Baroness's messenger and meets [[Raoul Montagu]] in the hills outside [[Peydon]], accompanied by [[Julien Marteau]] and [[Sveta Dragunov]]. Within a magical shelter warded against observation, Raoul explains that [[Robert I]] has been dead for four years and replaced by the lich [[Malach]]. He believes [[Malach]] is responsible for both the recent undead waves and magic that detects discussion of him. After Raoul, the paladin [[Leonora]], and their allies nearly defeated [[Malach]], the lich divided his phylactery into three fragments and concealed them. Raoul asks the party to recover the fragment located within the [[Barony of Aveil]].

The party sets out on foot while [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] studies his rings and [[Viepuck]] coordinates with distant allies. [[Viepuck]] tells the Baroness that he will be at [[The Setting Sun]] in [[Aslain]], then asks the inn's unnamed keeper to report any guards asking after his alias, “Timmy.” A day or two later the innkeeper reports that guards came through because they believed the child might be in danger. The party eventually reaches [[Tharwa Fea]], a ruined magical academy beneath an ominous cloud.

At [[Tharwa Fea]], the party studies an enchanted gate that admits visitors according to what they seek. Beyond it, they encounter tormented statues, illusory ghosts whose attacks still cause harm, ruined studies, and a library concealed by magical darkness. A hidden chamber leads to a space saturated with despair, where [[Malach's Phylactery|a fragment of Malach's phylactery]] floats beyond easy reach. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] suppresses its magic long enough for [[Celyn]] to trap it in a lead box. As the party withdraws, a sword-bearing human shadow emerges from the cloud. They destroy it with radiant magic and contact Raoul for instructions.
