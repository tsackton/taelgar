# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party recovers Asineau's money and horses from its former lord.

The party and local forces destroy an undead host approaching [[Cleenseau]].

The party meets another band of heroes returning from [[Eftly]].

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Celyn]] and [[Viepuck]] pursue [[Lorin Valbert]] along the [[Great South Road]] and catch him at [[Champimont]]. With [[Isolde]] remaining as his retainer, Lorin agrees to return most of the money and horses taken from [[Asineau]] in exchange for safe passage across the river. [[Viepuck]] uses the [[Ring of Waterwalking (Cleenseau)|Ring of Water Walking]] to escort them across, while [[Rinault Essford]] is restrained from confronting Lorin. The recovered resources are returned to [[Ida Rosfeld]] for the regional defense. The party also resolves [[Catherine de Brune|Catherine de Brune's]] claim to land improperly sold by Lorin, while [[Celyn]] works with [[El]] and other defenders in [[Asineau]].

An unnamed lizardfolk messenger warns that an undead force is approaching [[Cleenseau]]. The party rallies local infantry, archers, and cavalry near [[Cleenseau Bridge]]. They defeat roughly sixty zombies and a mobile tower of bones before either can reach the town walls. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin's]] attacks break the bone construct apart, while [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] destroys much of the zombie formation and the party defeats the skeletons released from the shattered mass.

After resting, the party travels along the [[Great South Road]] toward [[Eftly]]. In [[Champimont]], they meet [[Vahaiya]] and a company of [[Rangers]] returning after defeating the monster at [[Eftly]]: [[Adra Brightwood]], [[Enzo Brightwood]], [[Damien Montrichard]], [[Emilie]], [[Remille Vauclaire]], [[Tristan Vaudrillard]], and [[Armand]]. The groups exchange information about the undead crisis and the threats affecting the region.
