# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party negotiates entry into [[Veltor]], gathers information at the [[Granite Mug]], and confides in the Merriweathers.

Disguised as an urchin, [[Viepuck]] delivers a false pendant to the keep and slips away after testing its night staff.

The party presents evidence against the Baroness, studies her response to a false magical search, and is accused and held for trial.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
[[Marian]] accepts carefully limited oaths governing the party's entry into [[Veltor]]. At the [[Granite Mug]] and the Merriweathers' shop, the party gathers local information and warns Primrose and Corrin about the Baroness.

[[Viepuck]] enters the keep disguised as a hungry child carrying a false pendant. [[Marius]] admits him, Etiene questions him, and Hesta feeds him before he escapes unnoticed.

As paranoia spreads through [[Veltor]], the party shares evidence of the Baroness's pact with trusted officials. They misdirect her attempt to locate Viepuck's false pendant, detect magical surveillance, and are then publicly accused of murder and necromancy.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party descends near [[Veltor]] and negotiates with [[Marian]], whose miracle can compel truthful answers at considerable personal cost. They swear not to misuse magic in the town and carefully answer questions about recent necromancy. At the [[Granite Mug]], [[Pierre the Guard|Pierre]] and proprietor [[Brunna Marrek]] help them take the measure of the local guard. The party later speaks at length with [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]], warning them about the Baroness and the larger danger without naming the lich directly.

[[Viepuck]] disguises himself as a younger, bedraggled urchin and reports to the gate with a false pendant. [[Marius]] summons [[Etiene des Ostieux]], who accepts the object and has the apparent child kept inside the keep. Night staffer [[Hesta Brill]] gives him food, after which he slips away while the staff are distracted.

By morning [[Etiene des Ostieux]] has been jailed, the Merriweathers have disappeared, and the town is searching for Viepuck's invented child as a suspected necromancer. The party informs [[Marian]] and presents its Sylvan evidence to [[Victorine Rosseau]] and [[Emeric Daverre|Émeric Daverre]]. [[Isabeau D'Aslain]], [[Rene d'Aslain]], and [[Esme d'Aslain]] become involved when the Baroness asks the party to locate the false pendant; [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] instead claims interference from a paired object while the others study her reactions. After detecting scrying, the party relocates to the former fey-occupied tower, inconveniencing [[Annette]]. At sunset the Baroness's faction claims they murdered guards and practiced necromancy. The party submits to public detention until the morning trial.

The party also recalls that [[Reginald Rusebek]], the previous baron, had members of the Merriweather family executed on false pretenses.
