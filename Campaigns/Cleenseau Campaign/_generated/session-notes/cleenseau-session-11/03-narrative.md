# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->

Regional leaders fund an expanded defense force based at [[Asineau]].

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Ida Rosfeld]] reaches [[Cleenseau]] with wounded survivors from the [[Army Garrison of Cleenseau]] and reports zombie attacks along the road. The party rides out to meet the soldiers and uses healing magic to restore the injured. [[Eveyln Totteridge]], a sergeant from the garrison, is among the regional defenders introduced during these events.

At a regional council in [[Cleenseau]], the party and local leaders agree to expand [[Ida Rosfeld|Ida Rosfeld's]] company to one hundred soldiers and establish its garrison at [[Asineau]]. [[Catherine de Brune]] contributes substantially to the required funding. [[Isolde]] objects on behalf of [[Lorin Valbert]], but testimony from [[Thierry]] and [[El]] establishes that [[Asineau]] has also faced undead attacks. That evening, Es*tiasilos steals a jewelry box from Lorin. At [[The Crossroads Inn]], [[Marigold Stonebridge]] thanks the party for recovering [[Rose Stonebridge|Rose Stonebridge's]] rapier and gives [[Celyn]] [[Silverthorn|its matching sheath]].

In [[Asineau]], [[Eleanor]] tells the party about the disappearances of [[Sarabeth Asa]] and [[Lucas Asa]]. While [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Celyn]], and [[El]] heal residents, [[Viepuck]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] convince [[Lorin Valbert]] that hostile fey are stealing his heirlooms and preparing to attack him. In [[Taviose]], [[Hugh Darrow]] and [[Phillipa Northwood]] agree to help train soldiers, the party visits [[Remy Darrow]], and [[Brot Starsearcher]] agrees to make barding. The party also receives a [[Letter from Rosalind (January 8th)|letter from Rosalind Essford]] with news of the [[Siege of Fellburn|siege of Fellburn]]. Reports from [[Ida Rosfeld]] and [[Duncan Rivers]] bring further news of [[Colonel Claude Leclerc]] and the wider undead attacks.

The party learns that [[Lorin Valbert]] has disappeared after leaving a document renouncing his claim to [[Asineau]]. His departure leaves the manor vacant as [[Ida Rosfeld|Ida Rosfeld's]] expanded garrison prepares to take up its new position.
