# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party searches the keep and finds evidence tying the Baroness to [[Areschera]] and [[Tharwa Fea]].

The party resolves the [[Cranford]] case and travels through [[Aslain]].

The party defeats fey servants attempting to abduct Tyrwinghan travelers.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
A search of the keep reveals Areschera's concealed magical objects, a ruin connected to necromantic research, and a contract authorizing her infiltration.

After warning an ally and settling the [[Cranford]] murder case, the party continues south through [[Aslain]] while identifying recovered fey objects.

The party intercepts harpies and enchanted bears, rescues two captives, and discovers how the attackers selected their targets.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
After arranging for the fey bodies to be burned, the party searches [[Veltor Keep]] with local officials and recovers several magical objects from [[Areschera]]'s rooms. In the Baroness's tower, [[Rene d'Aslain]] shows them drawings of [[Tharwa Fea]] and admits that he found the source book in [[Isabeau d'Aslain]]'s rooms. A concealed contract states that the Baroness granted [[Areschera]] leave to murder and replace [[Marguerite Deschamps]] in exchange for the safety of the people in the Baroness's tower.

The party leaves [[Veltor]] and contacts [[Raoul Montagu]], who warns that magical communications may be monitored. In [[Cranford]], the case involving [[Juliana Westby]] and [[Tobias of Cranford]] is resolved before circuit magistrate [[Thomas Dyerson]]. The party then travels through [[Aslain]], returning borrowed horses and continuing to identify the objects recovered in [[Veltor]].

While traveling along the [[Auberonne]], the party spots harpies and enchanted bears attacking an encampment. They defeat the attackers and return two captives to a group of Tyrwinghan travelers. A black-glass shard carried by a harpy reveals people protected by [[Archfey Ethlenn]], explaining how the attackers chose their targets. The travelers ask the party to escort them beyond the Hunter's territory.
