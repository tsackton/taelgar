# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party brings the Tyrwinghan travelers through the dangerous part of their route.

The party pursues the Hunter's servants and frees [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] before they can carry him away.

The party frees captives and forces [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] to abandon his lair.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
After learning more about the tracking shard and hostile rumors in [[Maudorville]], the party escorts the travelers beyond the Hunter's territory.

Harpies abduct [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] during an attack on the party's camp, but the others overtake and defeat them before they reach [[The Hunter|the Hunter]].

After scouting from [[Ashcombe]], the party attacks the Hunter's lair, defeats his servants, and drives him into retreat.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party questions the black-glass tracking shard and learns that five other fragments exist. In [[Maudorville]], [[Wilhem of Maudorville|Wilhem]] reports that messengers have again spread claims that the party was killed and replaced by hostile fey. Rather than alarm the village further, the party continues south and brings the Tyrwinghan travelers to a safer route beyond the [[Maudor Hills]].

The Hunter's servants attack the party's camp during the night, leaving Robin's weapons and armor behind while harpies carry him away under an enchantment. The others pursue by air, help [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] break free, and defeat the abductors. Evidence carried by the harpies indicates that [[The Hunter|the Hunter]] is preparing to leave once a prisoner arrives.

The party rests at [[Ashcombe]], scouts the Hunter's lair, and overhears orders for [[The Hunter]] to return to his own court. They attack, kill or disable the sentries, and free the captives held inside. [[The Hunter]] flees down the hillside, leaving a duskhound behind to delay pursuit, and escapes despite the party's efforts to stop him.
<!-- /SLOT -->
