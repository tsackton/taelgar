<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] returns to [[Cleenseau]] with rumors of chaos in the [[Army of the West]]. During [[Viatela]], the party learns that [[Eremon]] has been dreaming about a redcap. The following day, they investigate blight in [[Abigail Moss|Abigail Moss's]] orchard near [[Taviose]]. [[Rinault Essford]] is also present during the party's discussions.

During a [[Pyravela]] celebration in [[Cleenseau]], the party hears about [[Duncan Rivers]], an innkeeper whose ale has been disappearing under unexplained circumstances.

The party waits in fields near [[Taviose]] on the evening of January 1. Before dawn on January 2, they kill an unnamed redcap. An unnamed [[Duskhounds|Duskhound]] then attacks and wounds several party members before disappearing suddenly.
