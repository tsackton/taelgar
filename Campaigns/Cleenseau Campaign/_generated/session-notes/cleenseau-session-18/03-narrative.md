# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party defeats an ambush and discovers that Damien appears to have hired the attackers.

The party finds Damien accused and Sabine confined while [[Areschera]] controls access to both.

The party recruits local allies, tests Areschera's reactions, and locates Damien inside the keep.

The party smuggles Sabine and [[Istarias]] out of the keep and returns for Damien.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
The party defeats assassins and bandits outside [[Veltor]], takes prisoners, and finds evidence falsely implicating Damien.

Evidence surrounding the captured assassin is manipulated, Damien is secretly imprisoned, and Sabine and [[Istarias]] ask the party to help them escape.

The party builds support among Veltor's soldiers, temple, and halflings, then confirms that [[Areschera]] is concealing Damien inside the keep.

With Pierre and the Merriweathers' help, the party frees Sabine and [[Istarias]] before returning to the keep to rescue Damien.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
Robin's weapon warns him of an ambush along the wooded road outside [[Veltor]]. The party defeats two assassins and six bandits, accepting several surrenders and capturing one assassin. [[Marceline Dupont]] and her baronial horse arrive after the fighting and take custody of the prisoners. [[Viepuck]] reads the surviving assassin's thoughts and sees [[Damien Montrichard]] as the man who apparently hired him. In [[Veltor]], the party questions Damien and determines that he was not responsible.

The next morning, [[Marceline Dupont]] reports that the captured assassin supposedly escaped and died using lockpicks that [[Celyn]] is certain he did not possess. [[Areschera]], disguised as the Baroness's chief clerk, claims that [[Damien Montrichard]] confessed to arranging the ambush and blocks every attempt to see him. The party learns that [[Sabine de Brune]] is confined in the keep and visits her. [[Celyn]] recognizes her servant as [[Istarias]], the fey who previously warned him about poisoned food. [[Istarias]] explains that a duskhound checks on the prisoners and asks for help escaping.

At the [[Temple of the Warlord in Veltor|Temple of the Warlord]], the party consults [[Julien Marteau]] and receives a letter from [[Victorine Rosseau]] concerning the Cranford murders. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Celyn]] heal injured soldiers while [[Viepuck]] gains [[Marceline Dupont|Marceline's]] cautious support. [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] agree to hide and smuggle [[Sabine de Brune|Sabine]] if the party can free her. During a performance at [[Veltor Keep]], the party watches [[Areschera]] react to accounts of its fey adversaries. [[Viepuck]] later contacts [[Damien Montrichard|Damien]] and learns that he is hidden upstairs under the guard of a duskhound.

[[Celyn]] talks his way into [[Sabine de Brune|Sabine's]] rooms, disguises her as a peasant, and leads her and an invisible [[Istarias]] out of [[Veltor Keep]]. [[Viepuck]] takes Sabine's appearance, receives the guard's soup, makes the bed appear occupied, and slips away. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] recruit [[Pierre the Guard|Pierre]], whose admiration for dwarves makes him eager to distract the gate guard. [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] shelter Sabine and prepare to smuggle her from [[Veltor]]. [[Istarias]] privately tells [[Celyn]] that his small Feywild kingdom may ally itself with the Tyrwinghan queen. The party then returns to the keep to rescue [[Damien Montrichard]].
