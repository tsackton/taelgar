# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party discovers how the Midnight Lady's bargain and hidden refuge bind the Daverre family and [[Peydon]].

[[Celyn]] trades the drained [[Wand of the Witch's Curse]] for information about the Gloomshaper and access to the Midnight Lady's refuge.

The party destroys the [[Midnight Lady]] and escapes her collapsing refuge.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Investigations at Widow Aalis's house and Daverre Manor reveal the bargain draining Emeric and the portal into the Midnight Lady's refuge.

[[Celyn]] accepts a dangerous bargain while the rest of the party prepares to suppress the Midnight Lady's portal and attack her refuge.

With the portal suppressed, the party enters the refuge, overcomes its conjured defenders, and kills the [[Midnight Lady]] despite her dying curse.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party separates while the [[Midnight Lady]] is occupied with her recovered mirror. At Daverre Manor, [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Celyn]] find [[Emeric Daverre]] suffering from vitality drained through a poppet in the Midnight Lady's dollhouse. Emeric privately explains that he granted her sovereignty over “the lands of his blood” and fears that the bargain includes his daughter [[Ysabeau]]. [[Viepuck]] questions the household, including [[Beatrice Roisier]], while [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] investigates the hidden entrance beneath [[Widow Aalis|Widow Aalis's house]]. In the preserved chambers of the late [[Arnault Daverre]], the party finds an inscribed refuge stone connected to a concealed portal.

The [[Midnight Lady]] offers [[Celyn]] information about the Gloomshaper, a personal boon, and an end to her immediate threats in return for the [[Wand of the Witch's Curse]]. The party drains the wand's charges before [[Celyn]] accepts. She explains that the exiled Gloomshaper is attempting to hold open the [[Cleenseau Wood]] portal by sacrificing unusually potent Tyrwinghans. Once the [[Midnight Lady]] withdraws to examine the wand, [[Celyn]] rejoins the others and signals [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] to suppress the portal.

[[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] suppresses the portal while the [[Midnight Lady]] is inside her refuge, cutting her off from the poppets through which she restores herself. The party enters and fights through oozes, giant rats, grasping vines, and shambling mounds generated by the refuge. [[Celyn]] calls down holy light that kills the [[Midnight Lady]]. With her dying breath, she revokes his boon and curses him, but [[The Wyrdling|the Wyrdling]] shields him from the full force of the attack. The party recovers the emptied wand and escapes as the refuge becomes unstable.
