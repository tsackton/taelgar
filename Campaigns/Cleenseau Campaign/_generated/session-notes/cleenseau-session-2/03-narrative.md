# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party and its escorts follow the spider trail into [[Cleenseau Wood]] and rescue [[Remy Darrow]].

The party destroys the ettercap and its lair, recovers its victims, and meets [[Narrisa de Abadelle]].

The party returns with the survivors and dead, investigates the spider attack, and recovers silk from the lair.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Warin leads the party and a small group of soldiers into [[Cleenseau Wood]], where they rescue [[Remy Darrow]] and survive an attack by spiders and wasps.

Deeper in [[Cleenseau Wood]], the party penetrates a webbed lair, kills the ettercap, and finds captives, bodies, and evidence of repeated spider hatchings before Narrisa appears.

Back in [[Cleenseau]], the party identifies victims, follows up with local contacts, and revisits the lair to recover valuable spider silk.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Warin the Woodsman]] leads the party, [[Odo Cordwaner]], [[Beatrix Thorne]], [[Adam of Cleenseau]], and [[Marion of Cleenseau]] from [[Taviose]] into [[Cleenseau Wood]]. They catch a spider carrying [[Remy Darrow]] and rescue him, but a second spider and giant wasps attack, badly wounding Marion.

The party follows the spider trail through webs and brambles to a dead tree at the center of the lair. They defeat the spiders and ettercap, recover several living and dead victims, find a mysterious black substance and a record of repeated hatchings, and meet [[Narrisa de Abadelle]] when she appears from the Feywild.

The party returns to [[Cleenseau]] with the survivors and recovered bodies and meets [[Rosalind Essford]]. [[Viepuck]] discusses charity with [[Conrad]], the egg with Brot, and a possible spider-silk venture with [[Susanne Garay]]; the party consults Perrin and returns to [[Cleenseau Wood]] to recover silk from the lair.
