# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party finds [[Arianwen]] and a duplicate of [[Taliesin]] defending a flickering camp, destroys the shadow-weasels attacking it, and learns that [[Bryn]], [[Eira]], and [[Maelona]] remain trapped in an unstable overlap with [[Limbo]].

Leaving [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] with [[Arianwen]], the others enter the overlap, defeat a shadow-weasel whose body occupies several moments at once, and convince [[Bryn]] to perceive them.

[[Bryn]] leads the rescuers to [[Eira]], who holds a protective dome against a rain of glass while the surrounding landscape changes form.

[[Viepuck]] probes the duplicate's thoughts, exposes it as a doppelganger copying [[Taliesin]], and helps the party destroy it outside Eira's shelter.

As the overlap begins to burn, [[Celyn]] and [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] open a route to the real [[Taliesin]] and the body of [[Maelona]].

Carrying [[Maelona]], the group uses light, illusion, Ranger tracking, and Celyn's divination to reject false routes and escape.

The overlap collapses and ages the campsite by roughly six months; afterward, the [[Rangers]] explain the history of Robin's lantern and advise the party to be honest with the trees of [[Orenlas]].

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
On a wooded ridge in the [[Plaguelands]] on the way to [[Orenlas]], the party sees a camp flickering in and out of existence and rushes to help its defenders. After destroying the shadow-weasels attacking the rope, cairn, and cookpot that hold the camp stable, they learn that [[Bryn]], [[Eira]], and [[Maelona]] remain trapped in an unstable overlap with [[Limbo]].

[[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] remains outside to help [[Arianwen]] hold the entrance while the others follow the duplicate into the overlap. In a folded ravine they fight the separated head, torso, and feet of one shadow-weasel, then use one of Bryn's cairns to help the trapped scout recognize them.

Following [[Bryn]] deeper into the overlap, the party reaches [[Eira]] beneath a sheltering dome as glass falls from the sky. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Celyn]] help sustain the protection until the weather begins to clear, but the duplicate becomes increasingly insistent that the remaining Ranger must be abandoned.

The duplicate's effort to end the rescue makes [[Viepuck]] suspicious enough to probe its mind. The spell reveals a constructed copy drawing on the real Taliesin's memories, and the party forces it into the solidifying air outside the shelter before destroying it.

Destroying the doppelganger leaves the overlap unstable and the first imagined escape route fading. Viepuck's attempt to probe the realm provokes a new transformation, but Celyn's certainty and Es*tiasilos's altered form clear a path through the forming fire to the final fragment.

The reunited rescuers carry [[Maelona]] toward the entrance through a landscape that continues to rearrange itself. Light, deliberate belief, an illusory clear path, tracks, and a final divine warning reduce a series of false exits to the one route that returns them to [[Arianwen]].

Once the final group emerges, the overlap closes and the damaged campsite abruptly ages by about half a year. During the recovery, the [[Rangers]] recognize the history attached to Robin's lantern and give the party practical advice for entering [[Orenlas]] safely.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
Five days after [[Viatela]], the party follows a wooded ridgeline through the [[Plaguelands]] on the way to [[Orenlas]] and sees the flicker of a campfire. Then it is gone, and then it is back, like a strobe in slow motion. Estiasilos scouts ahead and reports a camp and danger, so the party runs toward it. [[Arianwen]] and what appears to be [[Taliesin]] are fighting giant, semi-amorphous shadow-weasels. The creatures repeatedly turn against a rope staked along one side of the camp, a stone cairn, and a cookpot over the fire. The party dispatches the shadow-weasels, and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] buries the rope with magical earthmoving to shield it from further attacks. [[Arianwen]] explains that the [[Rangers]] were caught in an unstable overlap with [[Limbo]] where belief can reshape reality and time behaves nonlinearly. They believe the rope, cookpot, and cairn are helping them hold the way open, but [[Bryn]], [[Eira]], and [[Maelona]] remain trapped inside.

[[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] remains with [[Arianwen]] to study the overlap with [[Limbo]] and help hold the entrance. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Viepuck]], [[Celyn]], [[Greymalkin]], and [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] enter with the duplicate. Ranger cairns lead them down a path and into a ravine, somewhat scattered. [[Bryn]] flickers between positions without perceiving the rescuers. A shadow-weasel attacks from three different moments at once: its head, torso, and feet appear separately, but an injury to one makes all three glow. The party defeats the parts separately and is left with [[Bryn]], who still cannot see them. [[Viepuck]] creates an illusion of one of Bryn's cairns and eventually talks the scout into listening to the party and concentrating on finding the other [[Rangers]]. That purpose draws [[Bryn]] back into their shared reality.

[[Bryn]] says it gets a little weird farther down the path. Around the curve, the rescuers find a shimmering dome in a clearing, with [[Eira]] inside it, as glass begins to rain from the sky. They charge into the dome; [[Celyn]] is injured by the falling glass, and the shelter wobbles with the effort of protecting the enlarged group. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] lends his strength to the belief that the protection will hold, while [[Celyn]] points out that with good luck the weather will break soon. It begins to clear. [[Eira]] warns that they should leave before the landscape devises something new to do to them, and the duplicate grows agitated and suggests abandoning the last missing Ranger.

After confirming that his telepathic tie to [[Arianwen]] is not essential, [[Viepuck]] convinces the duplicate that a mind probe will help everyone escape. The probe reveals that this is not [[Taliesin]], but something reading the real Taliesin's mind to produce a copy. [[Greymalkin]] decides that evisceration is the solution, and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] quickly explains the discovery to [[Bryn]] and [[Eira]]. [[Viepuck]] punts the doppelganger through the side of the shelter, where it sinks halfway into air with the consistency of sticky toffee. [[Celyn]] concludes, "Okay I guess it's stabby time while the guy's immobilized." The doppelganger lashes out with psychic attacks, but the party destroys it. It becomes a gray, slightly malformed corpse. Unfortunately, the way out that [[Celyn]] had imagined fades while his attention is elsewhere, and the ground outside begins sparking as though it is about to catch fire.

[[Viepuck]] considers the unstable overlap a mental construct and declares, "I MIND PROBE IT." His patron's attention touches the place. [[Celyn]] works to reestablish the passage toward the real [[Taliesin]]: he found it once, and he can find it again. [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] reacts to the contact by shifting through unfamiliar forms, then spins around and disgorges water across the forming fire, clearing a path in the direction [[Celyn]] knows they must go. They leave the worst of the weirdness and find a campsite containing a very confused [[Taliesin]] and Maelona's cold body. She bears no marks or injuries, suggesting that she was killed by psychic violence.

The party lifts Maelona's body and begins hiking out. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] provides light while [[Celyn]] lights a pebble from his pocket. They cross the worst of the unstable overlap with weaponized optimism, a general belief that they are getting out, and Viepuck's illusion of a clear path in a plausible direction. This returns them to the ravine, now with four exits. [[Bryn]] identifies one as an illusion, and [[Bryn]], [[Celyn]], and [[Greymalkin]] use tracks to eliminate another. With an either-or choice remaining, [[Celyn]] asks his god whether one way is safe and receives an unequivocal warning, so the group takes the other path and leaves the overlap.

The unstable overlap with [[Limbo]] collapses behind the group, and the already battered campsite promptly ages about half a year. Once everyone has recovered enough to exchange stories, [[Taliesin]] recognizes Robin's lantern as one that once belonged to [[Cedric Cyfenwid]] explains more of its history. The [[Rangers]] also give the travelers advice for entering [[Orenlas]] safely, including, "be honest with the trees." [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] answers, "That will not be a problem, I am very forthright." [[Celyn]] answers, "That will not be a problem, I am not forthright at all but I'm honest."
