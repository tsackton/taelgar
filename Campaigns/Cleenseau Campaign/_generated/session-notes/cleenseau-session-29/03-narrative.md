# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
[[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] accepts the lordship of [[Asineau]] and begins remaking the village into a stronger, richer home.

When [[Vahaiya]] warns of a dragon on the eastern border, the party rides to [[Marwick]] and offers to take up the hunt.

At [[Marwick]], [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] plans a dragon-killing ballista while the others scout the ruined uplands and learn the beast's habits.

Their trap catches kobolds instead, and the party turns the meeting into a surrender, an interrogation, and an alliance of convenience.

With the ambush reset and the friendlier kobolds in place, the party waits in the dark until the dragon begins its descent.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
[[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] takes possession of [[Asineau]] after the Baroness's fall, and the party throws itself into rebuilding the village around its new lord. New guards and craftspeople arrive, regional trade and festivals begin, and an agreement with nearby [[Ganboa]] supports mutual defense and the village's glassworks.

[[Vahaiya]] arrives in high summer with news of a dragon ravaging the border between Tyrwingha and Addermarch, and the party decides to intervene before the damage spreads further. At [[Marwick]] they find a castle already half-emptied by fear and learn just how badly the upland settlements have been mauled.

[[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] turns his recent study of siegecraft toward building a ballista suited for bringing down a fast-moving dragon. Meanwhile [[Celyn]], [[Viepuck]], and one of the Earl's surviving knights search the ruined villages, find signs of kobolds, and confirm when the dragon leaves the heights to hunt.

When the baited village draws in a kobold foraging party rather than the dragon, [[Viepuck]] and [[Celyn]] help force a surrender before bloodshed can really begin. The questioning that follows reveals rival kobold factions, the southern disaster that drove them north, and enough fear of the dragon that [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] can recruit the least loyal among them.

The party repositions for the night, sends Es*tiaslos near the lair as a watcher, and settles into a long silence broken mostly by talk of what might happen to the surviving kobolds afterward. Just after midnight, the warning comes that the dragon is moving with five kobolds in tow.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
Robin's acceptance of the lordship of [[Asineau]] turns the party's recent victory into a new civic responsibility. The party recruits guards and craftspeople, establishes smithing, workshop, garden, and glassmaking projects, and hosts the Empty Cauldron festival on May 12. The Merriweathers begin trading through the village, Lenora visits while traveling toward Medju, and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Celyn]] negotiate with [[Erdu]] and [[Unai]] in [[Ganboa]]: younger lizardfolk led by [[Izoko]] agree to gather glassmaking sand, while the two communities establish the basis for mutual defense. [[Asineau]] starts to look less like a burden inherited from [[Lorin Valbert]] and more like a place the party genuinely means to build.

That settled stretch ends when [[Vahaiya]] brings word of a dragon attacking the eastern borderlands and fears it may be tied to an older, more terrible draconic threat. With the [[Sembaran Army]] and the [[Rangers]] already stretched thin by the southern ash crisis, the party steps forward because few others can. Their ride to [[Marwick]] brings them to a stronghold stripped down for survival, where the local Earl can offer little more than hard-won intelligence about destroyed summer villages, vanished herds, and a predator that has already scattered the countryside.

At [[Marwick]], [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] applies everything he has been learning about fortification and siege warfare to the problem of dragging a dragon out of the sky. While he and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] work on the specialized ballista, [[Celyn]] and [[Viepuck]] head into the ruined uplands with one of the Earl's surviving knights to see what the wreckage can teach them. Their scouting reveals traces of kobolds around the dragon's territory, and a day spent watching from the ridge tells them when the creature leaves to plunder, giving the party the timing they need to turn theory into an ambush.

The carefully prepared trap does not bring the dragon first; instead it snags a small kobold party hoping to profit from the cow set out as bait. Viepuck's sleep magic, Celyn's sudden appearance, and Robin's sheer presence bring the standoff under control, and what might have been a skirmish turns into a long, unexpectedly fruitful interrogation. The kobolds explain that they were driven north by the ash-cloud horror in the south, that their dragon's followers are split between zeal, caution, and quiet panic, and that even loyal servants do not think the beast hunts the way the party expected. By the end of the exchange, [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] has bent that confusion into a practical arrangement: the least committed kobolds will stay close and help spring the trap when the dragon returns.

With the plan chosen, the party settles into the last tense stretch before battle. Es*tiaslos takes up the forward watch near the lair, the allied kobolds remain close enough to be useful, and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] is already thinking past the fight to whether the survivors might be brought back to [[Asineau]]. [[Celyn]], amused by how badly that would play with the local miller, has time to enjoy the thought only briefly. After midnight the quiet breaks: the dragon is coming down with five kobolds, and the trap is about to become a battle.
