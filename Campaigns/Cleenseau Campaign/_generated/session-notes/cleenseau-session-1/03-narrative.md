# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The four future companions spend the evening separately in and around [[Cleenseau]].

The travelers meet while defending [[Taviose]], then are welcomed as heroes and dine at [[Essford Manor]].

News of another spider attack wakes the party before dawn.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
[[Viepuck]] and [[Celyn]] stay at [[The Fox's Flagon|the Fox's Flagon]] while [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] camp along the [[South Watch Road]].

The four travelers fight together during the spider attack on [[Taviose]], meet local residents and officials afterward, and accept an invitation to [[Essford Manor]].

The party is awakened at [[Essford Manor]] by news that giant spiders have attacked [[Taviose]] again.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Viepuck]], who arrived in [[Cleenseau]] on October 17, and the newly arrived [[Celyn]] spend the evening at [[The Fox's Flagon|the Fox's Flagon]]. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] separately camp along the [[South Watch Road]] while approaching the region.

[[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] arrive as giant spiders attack [[Taviose]] and join [[Celyn]] and [[Viepuck]] in defending the hamlet. After the fighting, the four introduce themselves at Perrin Voclain's home, speak with [[Ysabel]] and the Starsearchers, and are welcomed as heroes. At [[Essford Manor]] they meet several local officials and first hear about the deaths and absences troubling the Essford family.

Before dawn, the party receives word of another spider attack at [[Taviose]] and begins deciding how to respond.
