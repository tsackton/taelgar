# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
Leaving Ethlenn's audience, the party shares a meal in [[Orchard Close]] while [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] debates pie with [[Pip (brownie)|Pip]] and [[Moss (brownie)|Moss]] and [[Viepuck]] discusses apple trees with [[Old Bramble]].

The party consults scholars about Ianto's trophies, learns that the unicorn whose horn was taken remains alive beyond the [[Sunset Gate]], and uses [[Twilight's Pool]] to locate it.

The party decides to return to the mortal world and rests in the [[Silver Vineyard]] before taking its shifting portal into southern [[Tyrwingha]].

The party reaches [[Clawyn]], stays through [[Viatela]], and then resumes the journey toward [[Orenlas]] without provoking a startled owlbear.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
As the party leaves its audience with [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]], they find themselves in [[Orchard Close]], an orchard with tables set among stone buildings. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] becomes deeply engaged in a debate about apple-pie construction with [[Pip (brownie)|Pip]] and [[Moss (brownie)|Moss]] before everyone settles in for a pleasant light meal with the locals. [[Viepuck]] talks about apple trees with the elderly satyr [[Old Bramble]], receives a couple of seeds, and mind-probes an apple tree. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], already uncomfortable among the fey, finds [[Dame Elowen|Dame Elowen's]] determination that he have a nice time distressing.

The party still carries a collection of trophies from the redcap's castle and wants to reunite them with their people, so they move on to [[Ash Court]], where a symposium runs more simultaneous conversations than anyone can follow. They drift into a discussion of magical creatures and then into a conversation about dragons. An unnamed fey scholar observes, "Oh, most people don't survive a conversation with a dragon, which one?" [[Celyn]] answers, "... well, he didn't survive the conversation with us...."

The scholars suggest possible provenances for two feathers: one may come from an allied region, while the other probably comes from a place they do not speak of. The party determines that the unicorn horn belongs to a still-living unicorn. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] tries to locate the rest of the creature, but the protections on [[Twilight's Grace]] obscure his spell; the unicorn is not there, but somewhere beyond the [[Sunset Gate]]. [[Viepuck]] tries to find the owner of the satyr hoof, but the spell fails less informatively.

Seeking help with the spell, the party finds [[Neris]], a naiad holding forth on the source of magic as an ocean. [[Celyn]] comments on innate and learned magic compared with opening the door to another's magic, using Ethlenn's warlocks as an example. [[Neris]] remarks to [[Celyn]], "He's not as smart as you are, huh?" She and [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] argue over magical theory at some length, and [[Viepuck]] draws out her explanation that the waters of a particular famous portal can penetrate the obscuring protections. When it emerges that [[Neris]] is not herself a spellcaster, [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] nearly loses himself laughing and has to take a walk.

After some pleasant wrangling, the party leaves the symposium and goes to [[Twilight's Pool]], where [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] learns where the unicorn is. They consider what to do next. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] would happily remain among the academic arguments and research, while [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] would like to be out of the Feywild. Restoring the stolen body parts matters, but is not urgent.

Confident that they can return to [[Twilight's Grace]] through [[Tafolwern]], the party decides to return to the mortal world and head toward [[Orenlas]], where they previously sent dragon remains and may be owed an interesting bounty. The nearest portal to [[Orenlas]] runs through the [[Silver Vineyard]]. It is common lore in Celyn's homeland that people go questing for the mortal side of this portal, never find it, and return roaring drunk. While the rest of the party takes a long rest, [[Celyn]] naps and then talks ferociously with the local fey about winemaking. The vineyard portal brings the party out in the hills along the southern border of [[Tyrwingha]].

When the party returns north out of the wilderness, they discover they are not far from [[Clawyn]], the village [[Celyn]] comes from. They go to [[Clawyn]], arriving not long before [[Viatela]], and remain through the festival. They do not stay for the later festival that tradition would demand, which would require another month and more; [[Olwen Bettws|Celyn's mother]] is disappointed. The day after [[Viatela]], the party heads around the end of the mountain range on the hike toward [[Orenlas]]. They startle an owlbear while traveling, but it sensibly runs away, and they let it go rather than indulging Greymalkin's desire for a romp and impromptu feast.
