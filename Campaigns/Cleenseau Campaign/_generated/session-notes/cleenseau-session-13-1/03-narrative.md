# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
Corrine and the party discuss healing, transformation, and their experiences of being understood.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
Corrine thanks the party for healing her and talks about her fear of unwilling transformation. [[Viepuck]] thanks her for changing his view of wizards, and [[Celyn]] shares a thought about his former name.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
After dinner, [[Armand]] goes to pray, and [[Odo Cordwaner|Odo]], uneasy with Corrine's unseen servant and still grieving [[Eftly]], goes to bed. [[Corrine Morphokamia|Corrine]] thanks [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and [[Celyn]] for healing her. The light of their lantern and their care have helped her face a fear that the undead eel's victims brought sharply to mind: unwilling transformation. She tells the party that she was born in a boy's body and came to understand her inner and outer selves as distinct. [[Viepuck]] checks on Odo, who says that sleep comes easily and that he will be ready for the next day. [[Viepuck]] then thanks Corrine; her courage and hospitality have changed his view of wizards. Corrine says she has long been comfortable with who she is, but has struggled with suspicion of her studies. She had hoped to learn something in the lake and found only darkness and death. [[Celyn]] responds with a memory of his old name and the birds he imagines carried it away. At the end of the exchange, he asks Corrine for a room where he can sleep alone. The source records his request, but no answer.
