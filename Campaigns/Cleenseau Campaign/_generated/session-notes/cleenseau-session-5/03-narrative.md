# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party scouts [[Wakog's Camp|Wakog's camp]], questions a captured orc, and joins forces with the escaped Narrisa.

The party provokes fighting within the warband, frees the prisoners, and defeats [[Wakog]].
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
With a locally gathered force supporting them, the party scouts the warband, learns about its rival factions, and finds Narrisa after her escape.

[[Viepuck]] turns the rival orc factions against one another while the party and [[Balthazar]] free the prisoners, after which the party and its allies defeat [[Wakog]] and the remaining [[Orcs|orcs]].
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
[[Guy de Varan]] guides the party toward [[Wakog's Camp]], supported by a force including [[Ysabel]], [[Perrin Voclain]], [[Warin the Woodsman]], [[Odo Cordwaner]], [[Agnes of Evis]], [[Jasper of Beury]], and [[Mabel of Cleenseau]]. The party captures an orc patrol member and learns that two rival factions follow [[Wakog]]. They later find [[Narrisa de Abadelle]], who has escaped the camp, and defeat the [[Orcs|orcs]] pursuing her.

[[Viepuck]] enters [[Wakog's Camp]] disguised as an orc and persuades one unnamed faction leader that the rival faction has betrayed them. As the [[Orcs|orcs]] attack one another, [[Balthazar]] cuts prisoners free and distributes goodberries while [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Narrisa de Abadelle]], and [[Celyn]] lead the rescue. The party and its supporting skirmishers defeat [[Wakog]] and the remaining hostile [[Orcs|orcs]], allowing the surviving prisoners to escape.
