# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
El watches [[Celyn]] work magic and remarks on how different it feels from their own miracles.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
After talking to the horses, [[Celyn]] notices El watching him. El calls his magic controlled, unlike the chaos they feel in their own miracles. [[Celyn]] says that what El saw was a miracle of the past. El points toward [[Viepuck]] and a young girl, then goes back to the temple.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
After [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] addresses the people of [[Asineau]], [[Celyn]] finishes talking to the horses, setting a search for a girl in motion, and notices [[El]] watching him. He looks back with curiosity. El's stare is fascinated rather than hostile: they have been watching magic at work.

“So controlled. My miracles shake with chaos...” El says, beginning to turn away.

“The miracle there is of the past, not today,” [[Celyn]] replies. He is willing to explain further if El asks, wanting them to understand that this magic is different from the miracles he performs now.

El studies him, then gently points toward [[Viepuck]], who is starting to talk to a young girl. When [[Celyn]] looks back, El has retreated to the temple.
