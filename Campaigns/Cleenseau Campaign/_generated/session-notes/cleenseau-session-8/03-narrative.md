<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party investigates [[Cleenseau|Cleenseau's]] history and the zombie plague. [[Walter of Cleenseau]], [[Wymar Essford]], [[Rinault Essford]], [[Jonathon Henwyn]], and [[Harold Stonebridge]] provide accounts of the town and the buried fort. At [[Tumbledown Farm]], [[Eliana]] helps [[Viepuck]] and [[Celyn]] trace the missing [[Cedric]] to the river. Outside [[Auloutte]], [[Gideon Thorne]] raises the alarm when [[Cedric]] and his unborn child are found dead and zombified. Rat tunnels connect the farm, a house near [[Beatrix Thorne]] and [[Jon Thorne]], and deeper buried passages. A Thorne teenager bitten by a rat appears to have resisted the magical infection. That evening, [[Marigold Stonebridge]] explains the history of [[The Crossroads Inn]], where [[Celyn]] finds another secret tunnel.

The party descends through the trapped entrance beneath [[Essford Manor]] into the buried fort. They avoid traps designed to contain the lower levels, defeat giant centipedes, and open a Ranger's [[Chest of the Forest Guardian]]. A swarm of animated bones and a skeletal necromancer then attack; after defeating them, the party recovers the [[Ring of Wounding]], [[Dwarven Ring of Sorcerous Restoration (Izgil)|Ring of Sorcerous Restoration]], [[Cloak of Concealment]], and [[Ring of Waterwalking (Cleenseau)|Ring of Water Walking]]. The session ends when they reach an entrance hall occupied by ghouls.
