# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party splints Greymalkin's recovered wings in place, gathers Ianto's trophies, buries the redcap's recent victims, and gains the company of [[Tarastië]].

The [[Grey Mists]] tempt and frighten the travelers with false refuges and compulsions, separate the goblins from the party, and strip away months of memories before [[Celyn]] leads the others onward.

The party meets five fey siblings guarding the border, establishes its bona fides, accepts their hospitality, and restores most of the memories lost in the [[Grey Mists]].

The siblings roast the creatures of their hunt and draw the party into gossip, wine, races, dances, wagers, stories, and alchemical discussion before everyone rests.

The party reaches [[Evensong Spire]], reports its news to [[Sir Caldris]], and travels by giant black swan to [[Twilight's Grace]].

Ethlenn explains that the party completed a danger she had foreseen, restores Greymalkin's wings, grants [[Snikket]] freedom of form within her realm, and bonds [[Tarastië]] with [[Celyn]].

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
After destroying [[Ianto|Ianto's]] fortress, the party reattaches Greymalkin's wings but cannot restore his control of them. They collect the principal trophies and the remainder of Ianto's collection, bury his recent victims, and meet [[Tarastië]], a small weasel-and-raven gryphon who had been bonded to one of the dead and attaches herself to [[Celyn]].

Traveling east through the [[Grey Mists]], the party encounters a cottage full of illusory gold, a false library, a compelling pasture, visions in the rain, and imagined attackers. The goblins become separated, and the accumulated magic removes memories from [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]], and [[Viepuck]], leaving [[Celyn]] to explain their journey and guide them out.

Beyond the mist, the party encounters [[Thyron]], [[Kyron]], [[Myron]], [[Syron]], and [[Lyron]], five similar-looking fey siblings serving as border guards. [[Celyn]] establishes that the travelers destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal, the siblings identify Robin's lantern with the vanished [[Cloudspinner]], and the party accepts an invitation to the [[Camp of Horn and Feather]], where Celyn's prayers restore the others' memories.

At the [[Camp of Horn and Feather]], [[Syron]] trades gossip with [[Celyn]] about [[Istarias]], [[Kyron]] challenges [[Greymalkin]] and [[Celyn]] to races, [[Lyron]] leads a dance contest, [[Thyron]] exchanges tall tales with [[Viepuck]], and [[Myron]] discusses alchemy with [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]]. After the revel, the travelers sleep and earn an escort to [[Evensong Spire]].

Escorted to the [[Sunset Gate]], the party enters [[Evensong Spire]], a fortress intertwined with the gleaming colors of sunset. [[Sir Caldris]], the Watcher of the Evening Song, hears their report of the destroyed portal and Ianto's death, then sends them onward to [[Twilight's Grace]] aboard a giant black swan.

In [[Twilight's Grace]], Ethlenn tells the party that [[Celyn]] and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] followed the path she had quietly set against a foreseen danger, while [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and [[Viepuck]] joined by chance. During the ensuing discussion of boons, she restores [[Greymalkin]], gives [[Snikket]] freedom to choose his shape within her realm, and offers [[Celyn]] a bond with [[Tarastië]] that both accept.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party gets Greymalkin's wings down from the wall and tries to reattach them. They can get the wings physically affixed and keep them alive with magic, but they remain dead weight outside Greymalkin's control, so the party splints them in place.

They collect the trophies in the hope of finding anyone to whom they might be restored, including a silver unicorn horn, a phoenix-like tail feather, wrought-black elk horns, intertwined remains of a stag and wood-creature, and the rest of [[Ianto|Ianto's]] collection. They bring what they can of a recent victim back down the blood trail, but find only the remnants of a traveler's camp rather than a village. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] digs graves while [[Celyn]] manages respectful care for the dead.

During the ritual work, [[Celyn]] notices a small creature spying from above and offers condolences. The creature, a weasel-and-raven gryphon named [[Tarastië]], had been the companion of one of the deceased. She receives a large, perfect pinecone and some sticks as grave offerings, then attaches herself to [[Celyn]] for security. The party sets out toward [[Twilight's Grace]] with [[Krat]], [[Garn]], [[Droog]], [[Grib]], and the transformed [[Snikket]].

The travelers see a wall of mist across their intended route and enter it, keeping roughly east by following the north-south ridgelines. They soon encounter a goblin-scale cottage that is obviously a trap, but the goblins charge inside to count a heap of coins. [[Viepuck]] begins trying to stop them with magic and repeatedly forgets what he can do. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] asks, "Weren't you going to cast an illusion?" [[Viepuck]] finally answers, "I can't cast illusions." [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] caves in the floor under the table, but the goblins only climb into the pit and complain that counting the gold is now more difficult. Meanwhile, [[Snikket]] tries to wander toward a pasture, and the party reluctantly leaves the other goblins behind.

A glint of moonlight appears through the mist just off the path. [[Celyn]] observes that "just off the path" is the most suspicious possible location. Izgil's moonlight reveals a library and compels him to approach it, but [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] holds the rope the others have looped around him until they leave its range. The lantern helps them see but impresses on [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] that using its daylight would betray their defenses. [[Viepuck]] attempts to probe the intelligence behind the mist and forgets he can do that.

In thicker mist, rain droplets show [[Celyn]] hills, fields, and people he might recognize if he looks more closely, but he refuses the temptation. [[Viepuck]] becomes convinced that [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] has vanished from around his neck until [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] shows him otherwise. The party crosses a path among possible barrows, ignores the sounds of an attack, and sees a four-armed headless monster rear from the mist; the last goblin flees back down the path. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] becomes convinced that a dragon or other flying creature will snatch someone, but asks [[Celyn]] to watch the sky and manages to keep moving.

Near the edge of the mist, the accumulated effect becomes clear. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] has lost a day, [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] roughly six months, and [[Viepuck]] about a year and a half, including his memories of [[Estiasilos|Es*tiasilos]] hatching. [[Celyn]] alone fully remembers why they are there. When [[Viepuck]] cannot remember [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] loses his temper, invokes the lantern's daylight, and marches toward the trees visible beyond the mist.

Lounging in a tree beyond the mist is a silvery-pale fey being who greets the party as approaching intruders. [[Celyn]] tries diplomacy before [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] can create an incident and explains that they destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal and therefore could not use it to go home. [[Celyn]] identifies himself as the person a particular squire of the court had been sent to watch, provoking laughter from someone in the background who says her sister will enjoy that gossip.

Outside the mist, the party meets several similar-looking fey who are clearly the border guard: [[Thyron]], [[Kyron]], [[Myron]], [[Syron]], and [[Lyron]]. They ask [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] how he acquired his lantern and identify it as a rare surviving vessel of the particular light of the vanished [[Cloudspinner]]. One sibling abruptly declares, "THIS IS BORING. Come to our campfire." [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] asks, "Is this an invitation of hospitality?" The answer is, "It is what you make of it," which the party accepts.

The nominal fort on the ridge is mostly a wall with a bonfire and tents behind it. The siblings return from their hunts and speak to the slain creatures as though expecting to hunt them again after the feast. [[Celyn]] attempts to restore the party's memories. His ritual prayer returns Viepuck's knowledge of his abilities, Izgil's memories through the fight with [[The Hunter|the Hunter]], and nearly all of Robin's missing day.

The creatures of the hunt are roasted, and every fey sibling seems to have an unusually large animal companion. Lyron's peacock struts at [[Greymalkin]] as if insisting its feathers are shinier. The siblings introduce themselves; their names are similar but their personalities are not.

[[Syron]], who has returned from hunting and once dated [[Istarias]], is glad to hear what he has been doing and to mock him. [[Celyn]] says, "He threw acorns at me." [[Syron]] replies, "Rather silly of him." [[Celyn]] explains, "Well, I was trying to decide which road to follow and I went down the one that didn't get acorns thrown at me." [[Syron]] concludes, "Rather wise of him!" She agrees that his nervous disposition may explain why a relationship with someone carrying at least twenty knives did not work out.

One sibling tells [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], "You're so serious." [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] answers, "These are serious times." The fey says, "You don't seem like much fun." [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] replies, "I can be plenty of fun, right now I'm just tired," after which the fey ply him with wine. [[Viepuck]] is invited to share stories but cannot remember the party's exploits and falls back on older standards.

The revel is full of dares and challenges. [[Kyron]] challenges [[Greymalkin]] to race his elk through the trees. [[Greymalkin]] wins, and the elk must compose an ode in his honor. [[Lyron]] challenges [[Celyn]] to copy her acrobatic fire-leaping dance. Her peacock blocks Celyn's view until [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] turns it invisible, which delights [[Lyron]]; [[Celyn]] copies the dance, then uses an illusory duplicate to set a dance for [[Lyron]] and another sibling to imitate. [[Kyron]] later challenges [[Celyn]] to race himself and laughs when the performance reveals the trick.

[[Thyron]] trades tall tales with [[Viepuck]]. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] notices that what [[Myron]] tosses into the fire is carefully disguised alchemy rather than herbs and begins an intellectual conversation with them. [[Syron]] throws knives while [[Tarastië]] occasionally tries to steal one. The party eventually sleeps beneath the unchanging last glimmer before sunset. When they wake, one sibling offers to escort them to the border because they won several wagers.

The travelers hike to the border and see the shimmering wall of the [[Sunset Gate]], the line between day and night. Rather than fragmented shards, it appears here as a solid wall of gleaming yellow-and-orange glass, with the fortress of [[Evensong Spire]] intertwined with it.

At the spire, the party reports that it brings news from the west. [[Sir Caldris|Sir Caldris, the Watcher of the Evening Song]], is fairly certain they have no news he does not already know, and [[Celyn]] agrees that this is likely. They report that they destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal and slew [[Ianto]]; the latter visibly does not concern him. [[Sir Caldris]] arranges their passage to the capital on a giant black swan, which carries [[Greymalkin]] in its beak because he does not fit on its back.

In [[Twilight's Grace]], [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]] says she is pleased that the party succeeded at the quest they did not know they were on. She had nudged several of her people toward a doom she foresaw, including the possible death of her chosen mortal queen. [[Celyn]] and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] were the ones who did not drift from their path; [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and [[Viepuck]] joined them through chance encounters.

The party discusses boons. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] asks for [[Greymalkin]] and [[Snikket]] to be restored. Ethlenn restores Greymalkin's wings. She will not revoke Snikket's transformation, but grants him the choice to take any shape he likes within her realm. [[Snikket]] becomes a tall human resembling [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] and runs away in embarrassment. Ethlenn offers [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] the future possibility of a vow of service and declines to explain why his research into the moon sometimes crosses her path. She declines Viepuck's request that she speak with his patron but grants him a personal boon.

[[Celyn]] asks Ethlenn to remove the curse placed on him by the Hag. She offers him the same freedom of form as [[Snikket]] but thinks it may not suit him. [[Celyn]] decides that his home is no longer in Tyrwingha and asks for something else. Ethlenn explains that [[Tarastië]] was once bonded and asks whether [[Celyn]] would accept such a bond. [[Celyn]] asks [[Tarastië]] whether she wants it; she agrees, and the bond is made.
