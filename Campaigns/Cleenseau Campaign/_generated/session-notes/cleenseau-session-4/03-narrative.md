# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
The party manages the aftermath of the poisoned bread and Viepuck's exposed identity.

With Ida ordered away, the party gathers regional aid against [[Wakog]] before Guy arrives from the warband's camp.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.intermediate -->
The party deals with the captured conspirators and Odo's dismissal before Vincent de Arban's investigation exposes Viepuck's identity.

Ida marches for [[Dunfry]] as [[Wakog]] advances, so the party gathers fighters from nearby settlements before [[Guy de Varan]] arrives after escaping the warband.
<!-- /SLOT -->

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
The party helps [[Rosalind Essford]] manage the aftermath of the poisoned bread and pleads with [[Ida Rosfeld]] after [[Odo Cordwaner]] is dismissed. [[Francois the Bandit|François]] dies by suicide on November 5, [[Jerome]] is executed on November 6, and François is buried on November 12. [[Vincent de Arban]] arrives while investigating Viepuck's letter and exposes Viepuck's identity, while a letter from [[Susanne Garay]] brings [[Viepuck]] substantial funds.

[[Quentin]] unexpectedly orders [[Ida Rosfeld]] to [[Dunfry]], and she marches away on November 27. As [[Wakog]] moves south, the party seeks aid from [[Beury]], [[Valit]], and [[Asineau]], gathering forces associated with [[Erick Murtha]], [[Sabine de Brune]], and [[Lorin Valbert]]. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] and [[Brot Starsearcher]] leave for [[Dunfry]] on December 2. On December 4, [[Guy de Varan]] arrives after escaping [[Wakog's Camp|Wakog's camp]] and reports torture and pillage there.
<!-- /SLOT -->
