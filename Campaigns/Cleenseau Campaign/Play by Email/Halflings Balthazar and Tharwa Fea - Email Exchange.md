An update on a few loose ends from April.

*Halflings*
At some point soon after settling into Asineau, Viepuck sends to the
Merriweathers, with Celyn's request for a visit. This is well-received. The
Merriweathers appear quite fond of the party, and although the 25 words of
Sending is short, Viepuck can send several, and you get the sense the
Merriweather's feel they owe you a debt. A trading party of Merriweather's
will certainly make it to the Cleenseau region sometime in the spring.

*Tharwa Fea*
Tharwa Fea is the name of the old arcane academy where the trapped souls
are. Viepuck asks a few contacts via Sending about it, but there is not
very much clear information. Lenora suggests it is likely possible to in
some fashion free the trapped souls, but these kinds of things tend to be
unique and really, to make progress, you'd have to return to Tharwa Fea and
learn more about who trapped the souls and how the place came to be haunted.

*Balthazar*
Viepuck makes contact with Balthazar. His egg has hatched and he is now
accompanied by a small glittery jellyfish-like creature that floats in the
air beside his head. It has two dozen tentacles that end in small flashes
of light, like frayed fiber optic cables, and swirling sparkles inside its
body. It occasionally turns into small wren, or a orange cat with one eye.
He introduces this creature as Ur`sta*glumph. Bathlazar is restless though,
he talks about leaving constantly, but something seems to be holding him
back. He has grown stronger, and gained some minor magic abilities, but he
still seems to struggle with trauma at times and hesitates to go off on his
own. But he says Ur'sta*glumph is growing restless and needs new places -
high mountains, or deep seas, ancient forests, or faraway lands. He wants
some advice from Viepuck, if you have any to give.
