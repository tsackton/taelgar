(a) West, towards Dunfry. This is the opposite direction from Embry (where
he is from) and towards the western army
(b) South, towards Valarin. This would be riding through Bandit's Way and
past where Wakog's camp was, and leaving Sembara entirely.
(c) East, towards Embry. This would mean riding through Beury (~3 miles
down the road) and then down the road through the "haunted" section then
onward to Rinberg.
(d) North, towards Cleenseau Wood which stretches for 100+ miles without
any settlements.

The second question is, when did he leave. He was clearly gone at sunrise
this morning (~7am) but if he left at say 6am, he only has a 5 hour head
start, and catching him would be a lot easier than if he left at midnight
