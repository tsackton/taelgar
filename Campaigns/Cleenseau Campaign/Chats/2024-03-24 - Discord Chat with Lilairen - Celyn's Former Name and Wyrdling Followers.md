    Here's how I would pronounce the Welsh-inspired name Llinos in Tyrwinghan:
    
    Hlin-oss
    
    Breaking it down:
    
    Hlin - Like the "hl" consonant blend in "hlen" but stronger aspiration
    oss - Rhyming with "moss"
    
    Using Welsh pronunciation:
    
    Ll - Makes a harsh aspirated "hl" sound to start the name
    inos - The "inos" has an "oss" type vowel sound
    
    So putting it together:
    Hlin-oss
    
    The stress is approximately even across both syllables.
