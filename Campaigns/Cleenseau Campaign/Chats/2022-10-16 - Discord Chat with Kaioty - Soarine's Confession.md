# 2022-10-16 - Discord Chat with Kaioty - Soarine's Confession

%% Source: Discord channel 692424122751254578. Discord DM with .deciusmus and kaioty. %%

[2022-10-16 10:50 PM] Kaioty: when you get a sec, can you check this for world continuity etc?

[2022-10-16 10:50 PM] Kaioty: Soarine is very pensive for a while. Doesn't engage in revelry or immediate action. If asked he can be convinced to do things but he will be very lost in his own mind for a bit.
    
    Eventually when an opportunity arises (probably when Roe pulls the group together privately away from gossip to discuss the errand he wishes to run), Soarine will finally heave a sigh. 
    
    "I need to tell you all something. I may well be sealing my own fate but someone needs to know what I know and why. Someone impartial who will do the right thing no matter what it costs them" and he looks at Robin when he says this. "If the right thing when I've said my piece is to stop me before...something... worse happens... I won't resist. I have no desire to become part of the problem we tried to solve here today."
    
    "Everything I told you was true. I was a retainer of the duchy near here which owes most of its livelihood to the trade of the abandoned dwarven mines to the East and North. I had a lowly position as I was not high ranking in my family lineage. One day I went into one of the mines, some workers had gone missing and I was to find a sign of them if I could or confirm their deaths for their loved ones if I couldn't. But I had volunteered for the mission, because I had heard this particular mine held some kind of secret treasure nobody had ever found. As a lesser son of a lesser house I admit my own avarice. I would inherit nothing, and in an effort to make my own way in the world I sought opportunity in my duties."

[2022-10-16 10:50 PM] Kaioty: .
    
    "What I found there was a treasure of some kind though I know not now whether it was blessing or curse. I should clarify that before this time I was not a spellcaster. I knew nothing of magic or how to wield it. I was simply a wisp of a lad who was nimble enough and smart enough to to get into trouble but not always to get out of it. The ones I was sent to find I found crushed by a cave-in, and as I investigated another seeming cave-in happened. I lost consciousness for a time. I have...fragments of memories while I was out. Mostly things I have tried to forget or discounted as fancy since then. When I awoke I was covered in dust and one of the boulders from the prior cave-in had protected me from being crushed though I had some lump on my head. I managed to crawl out with the tale. But I also brought with me power I had never before possesed. It took me time to master it, and the learning is really what drove me from the Duchy. I was no good as a retainer while I was recovering and when I did I was too preoccupied. I quarreled with my family and left."

[2022-10-16 10:51 PM] Kaioty: .
    
    "Since then I have earned what coin I could helping people on the road, while always trying to understand more about what happened to me down there. And now I think I know." He will take off his outer coat and pull up his sleeve on his right arm, exposing a band around his arm that is greyish in color, and looks very similar to the glimpse we saw around the head of the Thing that had been the Dwarf in that other dimension. "Every time I summon the power to cast a spell I have always sensed I was exerting my will on some outside force to bend it in the direction I wished. I always assumed it was that way for all. Wielding the power of creation after all. Now I think I just have managed to avoid succumbing to whatever is on the other side of these marks. That affinity has been a tool for us here today, it allowed me to learn more than I think IT wanted me to. But it's also clearly a vulnerability. I do not wish to become whatever that dwarf had by the end. I don't feel on the cusp of losing control but I have no idea what separates him from me other than he lost, and I haven't...yet."
    
    "So that is my tale. And perhaps Roe was right to mistrust me after all. And if you decide I'm too much of a danger to be allowed to live...I understand. I might even agree."
