[2025-10-05 12:25 PM] Deciusmus: brief overview of the justice system:
    
    The typical trial or criminal inquest goes:
    
    * Accusation or discovery of a crime, typically brought by a victim, a bystander, or the sheriff/guard/etc is brought to the magistrate
    * Inquest - the magistrate investigates, sometimes with staff
    * Indictment - if there is evidence, the magstriate writes a formal indictment. At this stage the accused is generally imprisoned
    * Trial - a formal trial is brought the magistraate is the prosecutor and judge, generally. The accussed is allowed to speak on their behalf and the evidence against them is presented publically

[2025-10-05 12:26 PM] Deciusmus: During the trial there is a bench of honorables. a group of usually 2 or sometimes 4 peers who participate in the sentencing and judgement of guilt
