# The Shared Vision of Home

*A transcript excerpt from the first session of [[Dragonets Campaign]], played September 17, 2026. Narration by David Kong (DM).*

## The Embrace

**DM:** As you all hug the egg, the patterns on the egg begin to glow, and all of you experience, again, this… it's hard to describe it other than, like, the most sacred experience you've ever had in your lives. Like, this is, like, a very, very powerful, sacred moment.

And as you touch it, the egg… begins to…

**Zeno:** Whoa.

**DM:** —glow. And light up. And these eight points of light start to appear across the egg, as all of you are touching it, holding it, and just feeling this deep, deep sense of being moved by this embrace of each other, but then also the embrace of the egg.

And as you touch it, and as it lights up, you feel also, in some weird way, that your consciousness is merging together. Like, you're hugging it as kind of four distinct entities, but the longer you hug it, the more that it feels like you're actually just one entity.

And as you transition into this feeling of, ‘Oh, shit. We're not four things, but we're actually one thing,’ your consciousness starts to fade and shift.

## A Sky on Fire

**DM:** And where once you were sitting on the palm of the Wanderer holding an egg, you're no longer sitting on the Wanderer and holding an egg. You're someplace else now.

You're feeling something, but it's actually darkness. And it's something you haven't felt in a very, very long time, if ever. And a sense of despair. And a sense that there's no hope. A sense that whatever good was in the world, and whatever was light, and whatever was shining, and bright, and laughter, and family… This is the opposite of that.

This is dark. This is despair.

And you're starting to see now, stretched out, this night sky with the skies on fire. And it's burning. Raging fire. Deep, deep, dark fire.

There's still a few points of light in the sky, but you can see them slowly going out, one by one by one, as you watch.

And as you get closer, you realize that there's a figure on the ground, lying on the ground. And as you get closer and closer, collectively, all of you kind of getting closer to this scene, you realize that lying on the ground, there's a dragonet there.

And the dragonet is broken, is wounded severely. And it's a purple dragonet. It's an older one. You've never seen this one before, huh? But it's got some silvery fur, and it's got some amethyst-looking skin and scales.

But one of its wings is folded back on itself, broken. And it's lying in a pool of blood. Deep, deep, dark blood.

And as you get closer to this dragonet, you realize it's dying. And not only is it dying, it's gonna die very, very soon, that these are his last breaths.

And as you get closer now, you're now inside the dragonet. **You are the dragonet, and you are dying.** And you're experiencing the last gasps and breaths.

## Home

**DM:** And as you, the dragonet, lying on the ground, dying, looking at the sky on fire and these lights going out, you can see in the distance small little sparks start to fly out of the sky. These bright points are going out, but then these streaks of light are flying around. And you see one of these streaks of light flying closer and closer and closer to you. And you know you're gonna die soon. You know any moment this could be your last breath.

But as it gets closer, you feel to yourself a little bit of, ‘I need to stay alive, at least to see what this light is.’ And the light gets closer and closer, and closer and brighter, and brighter and brighter.

And finally the light comes, and it consumes you completely.

And you are no longer the dragonet. You are no longer whatever you were. But you feel, as that light touches you, this word in your head, and this sensation, because it's a beautiful sensation. It's just like the sacred sensation you just felt hugging the egg together, holding each other.

And there's a word that echoes in your collective mind's eye. And it's **home**.

And you feel, as this light envelops you and this feeling of home envelops you, suddenly these images start to appear. And it's images of a man with a jade hand, and you're feeling yourself getting scratched on the chin. Oh, you fucking love this, getting scratched on the chin so much. This is the best shit ever. You feel yourself getting scratched on the belly. You're like, oh, this is the best shit ever.

And you see other figures too. Other friends, other beings that you've called family and have felt the sensation of home.

You see an island. And you see a perfectly circular island. And you see all of these other dragonets flying around, and the clutches, and the eggs, and this deep, deep sense that this is home.

## The Return

**DM:** And as you get closer to the island, now you're traveling there. You actually feel that you are flying through the air.

And before—sorry—as you're experiencing these images of home, you travel through a space of just unimaginable geometry, and you can't even talk about it as a physical place. But just light, energy, patterns… You feel an incredible force as you're moving through this.

And then you come out. And you see before you this sea, this vast, vast sea in this night sky. And you're hurtling towards that sea, over the sea, and then you see a storm in the distance, this weird vertical storm that's just hovering.

And then you crash into the storm. It's silent inside the storm.

And then finally, you get closer and see these eight statues and the Wanderer's palm. And you land, and you crash into that statue.

And as you now, who were once four dragonets, and then one dragonet, and then the light, you now merge back into this space and into this egg.

And the four of you wake up. And you're lying on the ground next to this dragon egg, or next to this glowing egg.

And all of the lights from the egg, as you wake up from this set of visions, slowly start to fade and darken. And the egg itself goes dark.
