<!-- SLOT: items.treasure -->
- Mysterious egg: No longer glowing by morning; its earlier vision and gifts remain central to the friends' investigation.
- Ancient food machine: A dormant roadside mechanism that responds to Zeno's music and the friends' imagined food, with increasingly confused results.
- Food-machine creations: Fish-filled potatoes, clam-studded tuna bones, and metal spaghetti-like strands; edible samples are arranged on sticks and fish ribs.
- Oru's mechanical artifact: A round temple find with tiny moving parts, newly ticking and more responsive near Zeno; its purpose and any transfer of ownership remain unknown.
- Statue of [[The Wanderer|the Wanderer]]: Admits the friends and Roska through its eye, activates around the four friends, and ultimately shines with light and a heartbeat-like pulse.
- Eight seats in the Wanderer’s heart: Dragonet-sized seats in the presumed heart chamber; each friend is drawn to one, leaving four empty.
- Energy-signature device: A glowing device studied by the two observers in the closing reveal; never encountered by the player characters.
