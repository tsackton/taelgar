<!-- SLOT: cast -->
- Grandpa Jim the Cloud: A cloud long acquainted with Tony; reports the island's changes and criticizes Tony's lack of useful work.
- Paul: Tony's cousin, also called Paulie; describes Joey receiving imagined salmon from the food machine.
- Xena: Meets the friends at the food machine and asks Heska about his fishing.
- Ilse: Oru's messenger, who offers restaurant advice and asks to join the venture before returning to her duties.
- Ilse's supervisor: An unnamed shaman who interrupts Ilse's business discussion and recalls her to the summons.
- Oru: A familiar elder shaman and former restaurateur; investigates the egg and awakening mechanisms, then waits outside the Wanderer's eye.
- Roska: A skilled fighter who reports the reopened eye, enters with the friends, and is evacuated after the guardians wound him.
- Four guardian statues: Immense figures inside [[The Wanderer|the Wanderer]]; fire on Roska, then become peaceful once he is removed.
- Glenda: Tony's seagull familiar; assists during the confrontation, and her infatuation prompts his thoughts of finding her another partner.
- Two unidentified humanoids: Observers shown only in the closing vessel scene; interpret four signatures as Firstborn and discuss the resistance, without meeting the player characters.
- Kaz: Zeno's uncle, a monk in the mountain monastery recalled from childhood.
- King of the Hermit Crabs: The golden-shelled crab whom Tony remembers granting his misunderstood wish and inadvertently giving him his name.
- Dying purple dragonet: The unidentified dragonet whose experience the friends recall from the egg's vision.
- Joey: According to Paul, received salted salmon after imagining it at the machine; no independent special ability is established.
