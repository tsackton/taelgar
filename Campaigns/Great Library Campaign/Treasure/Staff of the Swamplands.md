# The Staff of the Swamplands

The Staff of the Swamplands is a dark-wood magic staff recovered by the [[Silver Tempests]] from [[Nymthrax's Hoard|Nymthrax's hoard]] in the [[Blackwater Fens]], and later carried by [[Samso]]. Nymthrax displayed it as the centerpiece of a collection of lizardfolk treasures.
