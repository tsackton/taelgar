# Chronicles of Zadkai's Return and the Scouring Wind

After returning from [[Cairn Dor]], the [[Silver Tempests]] discover that several years have passed, and the world has changed....

## Adventure 21: Airion's Secret Lair

### Session 64

The [[Silver Tempests]] make their way to a refuge and hall of stories that [[Brelith]] and [[Dinia]] have established on the western slopes of the [[Fiatara Mountains]], following the oligarch takeover of [[Voltara]], where they have a relaxing dinner and long discussion with [[Brelith]] and [[Dinia]]. Over the course of the next couple of days, the group learns of the events of the past few years, while they were asleep in [[Cairn Dor]], and learns more about each other as well. [[Mabist]] and [[Blue]] try lily pad cakes for the first time, to mixed results. 

After a few days of relaxation and restoration, [[Silverstorm]] arrives, his massive wingspan briefly blocking the sun as he flies over the refuge, seeking to speak with the [[Silver Tempests]]. From [[Silverstorm]], the party learns a little of the politics of the [[Elemental Plane of Air]]: [[Zadkai]]'s master, [[Azravan]], Duke of the Scouring Wind, leads a faction who believes that all humanity must be erased from the multiverse as punishment for the sins of the wizards who enslaved [[djinn]]. [[Orumei]], Duke of the Returning Wind, wants to prevent this war. Having learned that [[Zadkai]] is seeking some kind of hidden research or magic of [[Airion|Airion the Mistspeaker]], hidden in his [[Airion's Secret Lair|secret lair]], [[Orumei]] has asked [[Silverstorm]] to find what [[Zadkai]] is seeking and bring it to them. Silverstorm has asked the party to help, figuring that stealth is an asset and a roc with a three-hundred-foot wingspan will struggle to be stealthy.

The party agrees to help, and asks Dinia if she knows anything about [[Airion's secret lair]]. She tells a tale of a mountain in [[Tawir Forest]], that was called Bald Mountain long ago, but acquired an impenetrable cloud that hovered over the summit, and gained the name [[Mount Graybane]]. This, she says, must be [[Airion]]'s lair. As she finishes her tale, [[Brelith]] completes a casting of _Legend Lore_, which reveals the same information. 

Departing the [[Fiatara Mountains]], the [[Silver Tempests]] fly on [[Silverstorm]]'s back to the edge of [[Tawir Forest]], where they camp for the night. The next morning, using _Wind Walk_, they travel quickly over the forest to [[Mount Graybane]], noting that the [[Airion's Floating Tower|floating tower]] presumably occupied by [[Zadkai]] has landed near the mountain.

Attempting to fly as mist through a supernatural storm, [[Adrik]] and [[Blue]] are tossed by the wind and slammed into the ground, forced to shift out of mist form. [[Adrik]] manages to run out of the storm, while [[Blue]] huddles close to the rocks, calling for help with her thaumaturgically-enhanced voice. With no way to see [[Blue]], it is only [[Adrik]]'s stone sense and storm affinity that hints at her direction and allows the party to regroup. 

Afterward, they proceed into the storm on foot, tied together by ropes. The wind makes walking difficult, and the climb has some challenging moments, but the real difficulty comes as the rain grows and the party must scale a steep boulder, slick and running with water. [[Samso]] uses his [[Staff of the Swamplands]] to push the water clear of the rock, simplifying the ascent. Even so, the weaker members of the party need help to reach the top: Samso by shapeshifting to a giant lizard, better suited to climbing, and Blue by simply being dragged to the top, battered by the winds.

Near the summit, the [[Silver Tempests]] make a final push through lightning and thunder, and finally attempt to dodge loose rocks that come flying down the mountain, dislodged by the storm. Adrik's stone sense gives some warning, though ultimately he decides to simply absorb the blow of the rockslides, deflecting them out of the path of the less robust party members below.

We end as the party suddenly enters the quiet at the top of the mountain, at the eye of the storm. Standing a short distance away is a large domed building, clearly the entrance to something, the door smashed completely open. 

### Session 65

We begin as the [[Silver Tempests]] enter the domed building, the entrance to [[Airion's Secret Lair]]. The doors have been smashed open, as if by a hurricane, and the interior of the building -- a large, domed room about 60 feet in diameter -- has clearly been ransacked and destroyed. The only obvious exit is a spiral stairway descending from the center of the room; the party immediately descends below. The descent is strange, and grinding noise seems to shift the staircase somehow, but the party safely arrives in a small, square room below. One wall contains a heavy metal double door, glowing with heat. One wall contains a moving, animated tapestry depicting a sky with clouds. One wall contains a gemstone mosaic depicting a tunnel leading into the wall, ending in a distant point. The final wall has a silver mirror frame, covered in runes, surrounding a vault door with four elemental keyholes. Shattered pieces of the mirror lay on the ground, and between the stairway and the metal door is a stone-rimmed pool of still, cold, water. After some fruitless investigation, long debate, and realizing they probably need to find four elemental keys to enter the vault, the party steps through the tapestry into a vast expanse of sky, distant clouds blowing across the horizon. With [[Blue]] and [[Mabist]] under the effects of Blue's fly spell, and [[Adrik]] riding [[Samso]] in giant-bat form, the party flies across the sky, quickly noticing a distant group of humanoids approaching, and also that the exit is nowhere to be seen. 

The humanoids turn out to be a [[djinn]] leading a small elemental scouting party, who cannot find the exit. Though they are allies of [[Zadkai]] and laugh at the party when they admit to being enemies of the [[Scouring Wind]], the djinn agrees to give the party two diamond gemhearts -- but not the elemental air key -- in exchange for help finding the exit. The party goes along with this initially, even though they also have no idea how to exit; eventually [[Samso]] notices a glitch in the distant clouds that may be a clue, but doesn't reveal this to the djinn. Soon, the party just decides to attack before their fly spell ends. The battle is quick, as Mabist strikes the djinn with a dramatic blow enhanced with eldritch force, and Adrik manages to jump to the djinn and strike it several times while clinging to its back despite its attempt to flee. With the djinn dead, Adrik falls as well, grabbing the diamond gemhearts and the air elemental key, and is chased by Samso who gathers him. 

The party then turns to searching for the exit. The glitch is a clue, but it takes a while for the [[Silver Tempests]] to realize that the door, camouflaged by an illusion, is moving away from them at a constant rate. Once Blue and Adrik teleport some distance away, they realize the glitch is in between them and the rest of the party, and quickly are able to close in on it and grab the door frame, passing through back into the vault room.

The air gemhearts contain an essence of elemental air that allows not only flight, but allows one breath to last for an hour, opening up access to the deep pool of water. With [[Samso]] able to hold his breath for some time, and [[Mabist]] able to breathe water, the two air gemhearts let Samso and [[Blue]] also descend safely through the cold water column. Sinking, the party passes through several hundred feet of increasingly cold and frigid water, until they reach a frozen ice cap at the bottom. An ice cave is visible on the other side. [[Adrik]] and [[Mabist]] attempt to shatter the ice, resorting to [[Thunderbrand]] and _Booming Blade_, which draws attention. Two ice sharks, and a larger, tentacled ice creature, come to investigate, seeking warm-blooded food, and attack. Samso shifts into an orca, and Mabist finishes shattering the ice, which then leads to the [[Silver Tempests]] and the ice sharks, but not the larger creature, tumbling into the cave below, which fills with water, though the ice plug quickly begins to refreeze. We end as the sharks are driven off, dead or badly wounded, and the party swims through freezing water of the ice cave to a tunnel, leading slightly up, where they can stagger, wet and cold, out of the water onto solid ice.
### Session 66

We begin as the [[Silver Tempests]] continue through the glacial tunnels in the ice realm within [[Airion's Secret Lair]], making their way through a passage that resembles a roofed glacial crevasse, with walls of blue ice and a dangerously slippery floor. Despite their haste, the party makes it safely to a gash in the floor, opening into a large chamber below. Using the flight granted by the air gemhearts, the party easily flies down into the cave. 

The ice guardian, a massive serpent of ice, has heard their approach and waits hiding on the ceiling. When the party lands in its cave, it attacks, smashing its tail into the ground, sending a cloud of freezing ice erupting around the party, quickly followed by a blizzard that batters the group before they can establish themselves, and blasts of ice that freeze the party into place. Adrik can endure the punishment, but the guardian’s sweeping attacks leave the rest of the party restrained, bloodied, and staggering. 

The party fights back, led by Adrik's blows with Thunderbrand and helped by Samso’s draconic spirit. Mabist, struggling to get into position, summons a field of tentacles that keep the guardian immobile, as to resist their effects it is forced to solidify itself in ice. When the guardian summons a giant snowman, Samso holds it off in giant lizard form, Blue catches both enemies in a _Fireball_, and Mabist, finally getting into melee, follows with a fierce series of greatsword attacks. Several heavy blows, including a critical hit amplified by _Eldritch Smite_, do substantial damage to the guardian.

But the serpent survives the assault. After a series of blows from Adrik leave it badly wounded, but not dead, it unleashes a devastating eruption of frost, overwhelming the already wounded party. Blue, Samso, and Mabist all fall, leaving only Adrik to face off against the guardian. With his companions down around him, he strikes the killing blow with Thunderbrand, and then rushes to revive Samso with a healing potion.

Samso manages to heal Blue, but Mabist succumbs to their wounds before Samso reaches them. Luckily, Samso is able to restore him with _Revivify_, using the last of the party’s diamonds for the spell. Meanwhile, an ice gemheart falls from the guardian’s chest, with the elemental key wrapped around it, and the entire ice complex begins to melt. The party grabs the gemheart and key, and hurries back through the tunnels, escaping up the water column to the vault room.

Back in the vault, the party takes a breather, and then turns to the hot metal doors leading into the fire realm. Mabist drinks a potion of fire giant strength and uses the ice gemheart to gain resistance to fire. With this combination of strength and protection, they pull the massive doors open.

Beyond the doors, a passage of dark stone leads to a large circular chamber. A pool of lava fills the center, with fire elemental dolphins jumping and playing in it. Golden runes cover the left hand side of the floor, channeling lava that flows out of the pool through complicated circuits before it disappears into the walls, near one of four exits that lead away from the chamber. 

After a brief look around, including Samso's realization that the runes are some kind of energy control mechanism, the party leaves via the further away of the two right-side doors. This leads through a room with a stuck door and a channel of lava; Adrik leaps across the lava and forces the door open. The session ends as the [[Silver Tempests]] step into the next room, the lair of the iron golem that holds the fire key. 

### Session 67

We begin as the [[Silver Tempests]] confront the fire guardian, an iron golem standing in a waterfall of lava, in [[Airion's Secret Lair]]. A lava river runs through the chamber, and a blazing gorgon aids the guardian. Combat breaks out immediately. The Iron golem heals while in the lava, and the party struggles to push it out of the river, but Blue's _Psychic Lance_, Adrik's warhammer, and Mabist's sword eventually lead to victory. With the iron golem dead, the party recovers the fire key and a fire gemheart, and then returns to the central chamber to explore.

Behind one door is a chamber where lava has overflowed from the Plane of Fire and magma elementals rest in a pool of lava. The party and the elementals exchange a series of wary glances. The Silver Tempests slowly back out and close the door, avoiding another fight. Behind another door -- locked this time -- is a private study apparently used by [[Airion]]. Most of its contents have decayed over the centuries, but an enchanted chest anchored to the floor holds Airion's notes on the workings of the lair, which the party takes. 

In the final room, containing lava runes and some complex elemental mechanism, the party finds a fire elemental trapped within a magic circle. It scrapes the walls where lava pours down into carved runes, maintaining a complicated runic machine. The party spends some time trying to understand the mechanism while the elemental pleads to be freed. Eventually they break the circle, releasing the elemental and shutting down the machine, which exploration reveals is somehow connected to the storm around the mountain. Before leaving, the elemental glances toward the back wall, drawing the party's attention to a secret room. After working out how to open it, they find more fire gemhearts stored within.

Returning to the main vault chamber, the party rests while [[Samso]] reads Airion's notes, learning that once a magical summoning mirror -- now smashed -- had allowed Airion to summon the four elemental keys needed to open his vault. Without an intact mirror or the command word the party has been forced to fight each of the elemental guardians to retrieve the keys. They now have three -- fire, water, and air -- and only need the earth key to access the vault. 

So, they next turn to the earth tunnel, where loose dirt continually replenishes itself when disturbed, frustrating any attempt to dig through. Small cracks provide another route: using the fire gemhearts to take smoke form, the party drifts through the earth. Shortly, the tunnel clears of dirt, and then comes to a three-way fork. The party chooses the right-hand tunnel, ascending steeply following a twisting corridor into a large chamber, with four massive stone pillars descending into the floor. 

A war band of air elementals allied with [[Zadkai]] is taking a short rest there. Still in smoke form, the party approaches stealthily and surprises them. The djinn leading the war band shouts for someone to escape and warn Zadkai that other enemies have entered the lair, but the party manages to track down and kill every fleeing enemy, preventing news of their infiltration from reaching Zadkai.

Exploring the chamber, the party discovers that the stone pillars can slide upward. Two have earth gemhearts attached to their lower ends. Raising the pillars also reveals holes leading down into a massive geode. Able to fly in smoke form, the party descends into the giant crystal structure below.

We begin as the [[Silver Tempests]] descend in smoke form into the massive geode beneath the earth tunnels of [[Airion's Secret Lair]]. At its center stands a great crystal holding the earth key and an earth gemheart, protected by swarms of minions. Combat begins with the party still flying about a hundred and fifty feet away, as the crystal fires a powerful blast across the chamber.

[[Mabist]] uses _Dimension Door_ to bring [[Adrik]] into the fight against the crystal, while [[Samso]] turns into an earth elemental, drops onto it, and then slips underground. The crystal proves immune or resistant to most of their attacks, leaving thunder, bludgeoning, and force as the useful ways to hurt it. [[Blue]] instead focuses on the minions, clearing groups with _Fireball_, which does nothing to the crystal itself. Samso also fights the minions, some of which follow him underground, while two gem oozes hidden among the crystals join the battle.

The crystal summons more minions every round, and the party takes steady damage while Adrik and Mabist chip away at it. Near the end of the fight, a blast knocks Blue out of the air; she falls and loses consciousness. Adrik and Mabist finally destroy the crystal, which explodes in a shower of piercing shards, further endangering Blue. Samso, still in earth elemental form, is able to heal her with _Cure Wounds_. With the earth key recovered, the party now has all four keys.

Badly battered, with Blue nearly out of spells and Adrik out of rages, the party debates resting. But leaving the earth tunnels requires the smoke form granted by the fire gemhearts, and they cannot afford to let that expire while they linger. They decide to return to the vault and open it, ready to shut and relock the door if anything dangerous waits inside. The four keys cause the shimmering metal door to slide open, revealing [[Airion]]'s treasury.

The vault is full of magical experiments: worktables covered in diagrams, measuring instruments, and objects held in open frames, with a great gemstone-studded diagram of the elemental planes on one wall. A brass-and-crystal astrolabe stands beside Airion's private notebook, its nested rings turning around a tiny opening onto blue sky. The party gathers the astrolabe and notes, a flying carpet, an immovable rod, a fire essence shard, and a frost brand sword, along with the coins, ingots, and two star sapphires. They do not yet know the sapphires' value, and leave the gems embedded in the wall diagram alone.

Deciding they can summon [[Silverstorm]] immediately if they need to escape, the party heads upstairs. The storm has cleared from [[Mount Graybane]], following their earlier shutdown of the lava-powered machinery. [[Zadkai]] is approaching in [[Airion's Floating Tower]], still about a quarter of a mile away, with djinn scouts coming to investigate. The party boards Silverstorm at once, and the roc climbs high into the cold sky, outpacing the tower. After searching above the clouds for a time, Silverstorm dives into the top of a great cloud and emerges on the [[Elemental Plane of Air]].

There is no ground below them, and no visible sun. Instead, the sky brightens and darkens, with a glow to the east in the morning and to the west in the evening. Silverstorm carries them through a largely empty stretch of the plane, with little sign of life. They travel through the afternoon, sleep on the roc's back, fly for another full day, spend a second night on his back, and continue until late afternoon on the third day.

Their destination is [[Orumei]]'s hidden refuge, a forest growing on clouds within a bank of fog. Silverstorm navigates the mist and lands on one of the great puffy cloud trees sheltering the forest below. Moss drips with fog, and birds, flying squirrels, and other animals move among the trees, the first such creatures the party has seen on the plane. A palace of clouds stands in a clearing. From outside it looks soft and indistinct, but stepping into its courtyard brings the party into clear skies and solid walls.

An unnamed guard leads them to a private meeting room, where Orumei explains the recent history of [[Sulmana]]. The city was ruled by the Dukes of the Four Winds: the Returning, Vanishing, Brittle, and Singing Winds. After Zadkai returned to the plane, he urged [[Azravan]] toward war against humanity. Azravan proclaimed himself Duke of the Scouring Wind, deposed the Duke of the Brittle Wind, and claimed a place among Sulmana's rulers. As Zadkai became more active and the [[Scouring Wind]] grew stronger, Azravan drove Orumei out, and Sulmana became the cult's base.

An awkward, scholarly djinn wizard takes charge of the astrolabe and Airion's notes for study. Orumei invites the party to enjoy the pleasures of the court for a few days while their assistants work out what the discoveries mean. We end with the party safe at the refuge, able to rest and recover, with the next step against the Scouring Wind still to be decided. The party levels up this session.
