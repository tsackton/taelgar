#### Evidence for the opening dates

The Nantucket Memorial Airport reported 0.00 inches on July 8–11, 0.28 on July 12 and 0.00 on July 13–14. July 8 and 9 had clear daytime skies. July 11 had dense morning fog and midday mist. July 12 had heavy rain around 9 a.m. and more light rain that evening. July 13 and 14 cleared after early fog. A second gauge, Nantucket 3.8 WNW, reported 0.03, 0.01, 0.01, 0.01 and 1.40 inches for the observation periods ending July 8–12, respectively; no reports for July 13–14 appear in that series. Gauge totals alone do not date a daytime shower. [Island rain gauges] · [Airport observations]

Tim adopts four island sessions on July 8, 9, 11 and 12, with Kate joining on July 12. The weather supports the two later indoor games. Session 5 follows on July 27. [Roscoe] · [Return ferry]

August 10 still fits either road session around the movie. August 11 had a 1:30–5:30 p.m. whale watch. August 17 had a 1–7 p.m. anniversary gathering in Sturbridge and August 18 had a 2–8 p.m. wedding. A weekday evening or weekend morning remains possible. No calendar entry identifies #7 or #8.
