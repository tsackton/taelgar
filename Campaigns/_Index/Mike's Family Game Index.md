### Mike’s Family Game · session log

Campaign code **mikefam**. Tim recalls two sessions of Mike’s Sembara game in 2021.

| Session | Played locally | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wednesday, July 21, 2021 **(probable)** | Nantucket. Tim identifies the [BUSY block on his work calendar](https://www.google.com/calendar/event?eid=Xzg0c2pnZGhrNnQyM2diOWw4NTBqaWI5azYxMjQyYjlwOG9zazZiOWo2Z3MzNmhoaThoMmplaGk2NzQgdHNhY2t0b25AZy5oYXJ2YXJkLmVkdQ) from 2 to 5:30 p.m. Eastern as the likely session. On July 26 he wrote that Isaac and Charli wanted to continue the game Mike had run on the island. [July 26 email](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/17ae4af058cb5578) |
| 2 (final) | Sunday, September 5, 2021 | The family calendar scheduled [D&D (Sembara/Mike)](https://www.google.com/calendar/event?eid=XzZjcWs2aGEzNjRyazhiOXA2c3IzOGI5azZsMWpjYmExNzRyamFiYTI2dDEzNGNpNTc0cjM0ZHBoODggaDJxaGswMTlvZTNhdmN0a3AwZ2FyZnNsYmdAZw&ctz=America/New_York) from 2:30 to 6 p.m. Eastern, followed by dinner with Mike, Dana and Charli. Tim identifies this as the finale and confirms that Kate and Dana Sackton both played. |

Mike ran the level 3 game for Tim, Isaac, Nathaniel and Charli Sackton. Tim played [Caerwyn of Abenfyrd](<../../Worldbuilding/Tentative/Caerwyn%20of%20Abenfyrd.md>). Kate played Zoe Kothashoy, a human rogue. Tim’s [August 6 email](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/17b1eb50fc935eea) links [her character sheet](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/55820805). Dana played an elf.

#### Evidence notes

The families’ [summer plans](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/17789609f596ea23) placed Mike and Charli on Nantucket through July 25. You can also date preparations from [Caerwyn’s July 12 backstory](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/17a9b8aedb26967a). On August 4 Mike shared [notes from the Nantucket game](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/17b1244b7d3c1509). The sharing email survives, but the linked document was unavailable during review.

The [vault campaign inventory](../Campaign%20Inventory.md) lists three sessions. An earlier reconstruction assumed two sittings on Nantucket. Tim’s revised count accounts for one island session and one September session. No separate date or account of a third sitting was identified.
