# Lost in the Feywild: People, Places, and Organizations

This page collects the named cast, recurring places, organizations, and books recorded across the seven episodes of [[Lost in the Feywild]].

> [!tip] Campaign home
> Return to [[Lost in the Feywild|the campaign landing page]], or begin with [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 01|Episode 1]].

## Player Characters

| Character     | Place in the party                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Kaito Min]] | An elven performer and opportunist who enters the adventure hungry for treasure and leaves the prison able to imagine happiness. |
| [[Edric]]     | A generous wanderer devoted to [[The Night Queen]], whose faith provides the moral compass for the party's decisions.            |
| [[Tarek]]     | A quick-handed Tollish traveler and salvager whose gathered items often become the clues the party needs.                        |
| [[Ayveen]]    | A hopeful Tollish adventurer and musician, whose patience and stories ground the party and help guide the prisoner home.         |
| [[Txarro]]    | A lizardfolk druid and protector whose animal forms and healing guide the party through the Feywild seeking a way home.          |

### Companion

- [[Mossfoot]] — Edric's stalwart but not especially brave pony, left at [[Dandelion House]] when the party entered the hidden root tunnel. First appears in [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 01|Episode 1]].

## Places

| Place | Role in the story | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Tollen]] | The shared home of the Tollen Misfits and the point from which their road west begins. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 01\|Episode 1]] |
| [[Varrow Forest]] | The old and increasingly trackless forest the party crosses with Alden to reach Dandelion House. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 01\|Episode 1]] |
| [[Dandelion House]] | A majestic, decrepit Drankorian manor whose inherited deed and hidden root tunnel draw the party into the adventure. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 01\|Episode 1]] |
| [[Feywild]] | The extraplanar realm beyond the root tunnel, where emotion, story, place, and time do not obey mortal expectations. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 02\|Episode 2]] |
| [[Twilight Kingdom]] | A silent realm of rolling hills, unfamiliar stars, distant houses, and a sun fixed just below the horizon. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 02\|Episode 2]] |
| [[Lord Endless Ending's Manor]] | A lantern-lit Feywild manor trapped in the repeated final moments of a party. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 02\|Episode 2]] |
| [[Twilight Promontory]] | The rocky overlook where a lone door first leads into the 27th House and where the collapsing prison finally leaves the travelers beneath the stars. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 03\|Episode 3]] |
| [[27th House]] | A vast Drankorian mechanism of shifting numbered rooms built around a cold-iron central prison and used to move its captive through space and time. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 03\|Episode 3]] |

## Books

| Book | Place in the story | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *[[The Shape of Possibility]]* | Menestheus's metaphysical and mathematical treatise about modeling possible futures and the story-like simultaneity of time. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 03\|Episode 3]] |
| *[[Brilliant Numbers]]* | Menestheus's mathematical treatise explaining the six-digit numbers used by the Arithrimos Lamperum to encode extraplanar weak points. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 03\|Episode 3]] |
| *[[The Shape of Discovery]]* | A brief philosophical treatise arguing that curiosity shapes possibility through the same irrational unpredictability as greed and games of chance. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 05\|Episode 5]] |
| *[[Allegories for the Initiated]]* | A collection of fairy tales and riddles whose meanings reveal secrets already known to initiated members of the Arithrimos Lamperum. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |
| *[[On the Science and Possibilities of Soul Bindery]]* | An Arithrimos Lamperum treatise advocating the use of captive souls to power liches, golems, and other devices; Txarro burns the library's copy. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |
| *[[Journeys of Souls\|Journey of the Souls]]* | An ancient Hkaran religious work about the passage of souls after death, represented in the prison library. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |
| *[[Journey of the Souls - A Refutation\|Journey of the Souls: A Refutation]]* | A five-volume refutation shelved immediately after *Journey of the Souls*. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |
| *[[The Riven Veil]]* | A theological and cosmological treatise by Marcion of Iridel, shelved among a larger collection of his works. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |
| *[[Zharuk Akhal]]* | A famous dwarven cosmological treatise on the Far Realms; an empty shelf records that it is out by special dispensation. | [[Lost in the Feywild - Episode 06\|Episode 6]] |

Episode 1 also mentions the party recovering a collection of unnamed old books from an ettin before the campaign began. The library of the [[27th House]] holds many other unnamed volumes; the listening-station notebooks are records rather than named books and are not included above.
