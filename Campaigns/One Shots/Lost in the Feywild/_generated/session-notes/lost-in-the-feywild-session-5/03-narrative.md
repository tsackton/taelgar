# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
We begin with the party in the fungal greenhouse, where, after some discussion, they decide that retrieving the glass key is the best route out.

Following [[Menestheus]], the party proceeds through the house, and finds a lecture hall with strange diagrams and a black painting. In the next room, [[Tarek]] uncovers a clock that tracks the prison.

Proceeding through the house, the next listening station contains [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Thirst|entries]] of a speaker named "Thirst" by the transcriber. The party resolves not to free it, and continues onward.

In the next room, an animal experimentation laboratory, mushroom men attack [[Menestheus]]. The party fights to rescue him, killing and burning both infected bodies.

Afterward, [[Tarek]] discovers white filaments growing inside [[Menestheus]]. Putting aside that problem for now, the party continues through the house, passing Thalestria's sealed office which resists every attempt to open it.

Proceeding, the party reaches another listening station, where the the [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Prisoner|transcriptions]] addresses [[Lord Hulda|Hulda]] and the party directly. Beyond, in a library, a clockwork figure raises its head to study them.

We begin with the party in the fungal greenhouse, weighing Hulda's betrayal against Lord Endless Ending's bargain. After discussion, the party chooses the glass key as its best route out. They decide to retrieve it, whether that means opening the prisoner's door or not.

Following [[Menestheus]], leaves the fungal greenhouse and crosses a parlor into a lecture hall where chalkboard diagrams depict a lesson about dandelions, and a black painting conceals a tear in the universe. In the next room, a clockworks workshop, [[Tarek]] takes an unfinished portable clock that changes numbers as the prison moves.

Proceeding through the house, the next listening station contains [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Thirst|entries]] of a speaker named "Thirst" by the transcriber. The Thirst notebook describes [[Prisoner in the 27th Room|the prisoner]] filling empty heads with its children, and Kaito hears it claim the party is helping it escape. The party resolves not to free it, and continues onward.

In the next room, an animal experimentation laboratory, mushroom men attack [[Menestheus]]. The party retaliates, seeking to rescue him, but [[Menestheus]] is dragged into a mouth-covered chest and nearly killed. [[Txarro]] heals him before [[Edric]] destroys the last creature and the party burns both bodies.

Although healing closes Menestheus's wounds, [[Tarek]] sees fungal growth inside his mouth and privately warns [[Txarro]]. Putting aside that problem for now, the party continues through more workrooms and an unfinished planar orrery. At a corridor of offices they find a door labeled for [[Thalestria]], but even Edric's enraged blows cannot break in, and the party moves on.

Proceeding, the party reaches another listening station, where the the [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Prisoner|transcriptions]] addresses [[Lord Hulda|Hulda]] and the party directly; they realize it is describing them in the present. Continuing quickly on, they enter a vast library where a porcelain clockwork figure raises its head to study them.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
We begin with the party still in the fungal greenhouse, discussing the journal entries they have read. After some discussion of their meaning, the party reviews the choices left by [[Lord Hulda|Hulda]], [[Lord Endless Ending]], and the prison at the center of the shifting house. [[Menestheus]] explains what little he knows about the order and its absent leadership while [[Kallipseis]] returns to work on her fungal golem.

The party remains reluctant to help either [[Lord Hulda|Hulda]] or [[Lord Endless Ending]], but the glass key still offers their clearest route out. They decide to retrieve it, whether that means opening the prisoner's door or not.

They follow [[Menestheus]] out of Kallipseis's fungal greenhouse, cross a parlor, and come into a lecture hall whose chalkboard diagrams the life cycles of dandelions and other wind-dispersed plants. An enormous black painting conceals a field of stars, a tear in the universe, and the textured form of a seed sprouting through the gap. [[Menestheus]] recognizes the image as exactly the kind of uncontrolled breech between worlds that [[Arithrimos Lamperum]] was created to stop.

[[Tarek]] also finds a book called "The Shape of Discovery," a brief treatise arguing that curiosity shapes possibility through the same irrational unpredictability as greed and games of chance. The party connects Kallipseis's description of infected people to Alden's pale, round appearance, surprising strength, and fear of fire.

In the next room, a clockworks workshop, [[Tarek]] finds and wraps an unfinished but functional desk-sized clock. Like the grandfather clocks in Dandylion House and the estate of [[Lord Endless Ending|Lord Endlessly Ending]], its numbers change as the party moves, apparently tracking the central prison room's position in space and time. Carrying the clock, the party reaches another mechanical listening room.

The notebook at the new listening station is titled Thirst. Its [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Thirst|entries]] describe the speaker filling bodies emptied of dreaming souls with its children, while those children search for the person who can set it free. The party identifies the children with the fungal infection and suspects that [[Prisoner in the 27th Room|the prisoner]] manipulated [[Alden]] and the suspicious deed that brought them to [[Dandelion House]].

When Kaito takes the listener's place, he hears [[Prisoner in the 27th Room|the prisoner]] say that it is full of dreams, can read the party, and knows they are helping it. It can see both the moving exit and a hole large enough for its children to pass through. The party resolves not to free it and continues onward.

The next room is an animal experimentation laboratory filled with cages, restraint devices, dead specimens, an infected ant colony, and humanoid remains. As [[Menestheus]] approaches the far exit, a hand reaches from a side passage and drags him out of sight.

The party begins to fight among the laboratory tables and specimen containers, with [[Menestheus]] already grappled by a mushroom man. [[Edric]] rages and charges the captor, Kaito attacks with Dissonant Whispers, and Tarek's approach awakens a second infected corpse that looks almost identical to [[Alden]].

The creatures retaliate with elongated fingers and mouths opening across their chests. One briefly catches [[Tarek]] while the other pulls [[Menestheus]] inward, wounding him badly as he falls unconcious. Ayveen's Silvery Barbs blunts the attack, [[Txarro]] becomes a tiger, and the party focuses its attacks on the two infected bodies.

[[Txarro]] restores [[Menestheus]] with Cure Wounds before [[Edric]] crushes the final mushroom man's head. Its death releases a cloud of seeds that [[Txarro]] breathes in. The party burns both bodies, which flare like dry straw.

After the fight, further healing closes Menestheus's external wounds, but Tarek's examination reveals white filaments growing inside his mouth. [[Tarek]] quietly warns [[Txarro]], and they decide not to tell [[Menestheus]] until they know whether anything can be done.

The party moves through a dead plant greenhouse, a picked-over armory, and an unfinished orrery that models the planes as connected, interlocking spheres. They then reach a corridor of offices belonging to [[Arithrimos Lamperum]], including a prominent office labeled for [[Thalestria]] which withstands all attempts to open. Weakened after raging and trying to smash his way through, [[Edric]] accepts the failure as a warning from the gods, and the party moves on.

In the map library, [[Menestheus]] recalls other [[Arithrimos Lamperum]] facilities and a captive creature that experienced its entire life simultaneously. The order could ask it questions about events that others would call the future, although [[Menestheus]] remains uneasy about whether it was a prisoner or a pet.

Beyond a fabrication lab, the party finds another listening station. The prisoner's looping voice in the [[Transcriptions from the Prisoner#The Prisoner|transcriptions]] addresses [[Lord Hulda|Hulda]] directly: the dragon-killer is bringing five friends, those friends are outside its room now, and they can hear it. The party realizes that [[Prisoner in the 27th Room|the prisoner]] is describing them in the present.

One door short of [[Prisoner in the 27th Room|the prisoner]], the party enters a vast library with books chained to its shelves. A human-sized porcelain and clockwork torso is anchored to a desk just inside. As the party enters, it raises its head, opens its eyes, and studies them.
