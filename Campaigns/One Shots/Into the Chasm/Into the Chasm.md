# Into the Chasm

> [!image|hero]
> ![[runic-plaform-in-the-great-chasm.jpg]]
> *Ancient machinery hangs in the depths of the Great Chasm, holding the planes in balance.*

Into the Chasm is a six-episode adventure about five strangers, a village trapped beyond the world, and the failing runic machinery hidden in the depths the [[Great Chasm]]. The game begins as an impossible storm casts the travelers into [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]], where their search for a way home leads them to a fogbound village in another dimension, and a frightened ruler who no longer trusts that the outside world is real.

## The Adventure

For months, strange weather pours out of the Great Chasm. A sandstorm brings summer heat in November; a rainbow shatters into glass; a freezing sheet of ice sweeps downriver. An expedition goes down to investigate and does not return. Then [[Kalima]], a terrified woman speaking Old Zimkovan, climbs out of the depths. When a whiteout storm sweeps her and five adventurers into the chasm, they find themselves together on an ice platform, with wolves guarding the bridges and a violent wind barring the way back. The only way forward is through [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]], where the group faces shifting passages, dangerous winds, and finally a great shaggy beast guarded by living snow echoes of each member of the party. 

Beyond the labyrinth lies [[Melusa]], Kalima's home: a village of ledge houses and floating islands, where the sun seldom sets and the villagers send sacrifices into the maze to bring it back when it does. Taking shelter with Kalima's family, the party learns of the strangeness of this place. The villagers endure, and [[Forquarion|Malquin the Vaqar]] watches from his Ice Palace, though a small group of rebels and dissidents hope for a better life outside of Malquin's grasp. Five strange gashes in the cliff, recently appeared, offer glimpses of other worlds. One may lead home—if the party can persuade Malquin to let them pass.

A strange meeting with Malquin himself results in a deal: the party can leave, if they return with proof the outside world is real. Their escape brings them back to the [[Great Chasm]], and to [[Eidrikas]], Ekko's missing mentor, holding a damaged runic mechanism together with his own body. Orcs have carried away its keystone. Recovering it means descending through floating stone, warped gravity, and ancient chambers full of danger. Restoring it should steady the planes, but may also close the way back to Melusa. With their own route home within reach, the explorers choose to return for those who want to leave. The machinery is restored, the willing villagers escape, and the rescued Melusans eventually find a new home.

## Along the Way

**[[Zeyfa's Labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** — Ice bridges, vanishing passages, and a beast waiting at the end of every route. Kalima has escaped once, but the wind has brought her back.

**[[Melusa]] and the Ice Palace** — A village in the fog, sustained by strange abundance and governed by sacrifice. Its people disagree about whether endurance is their only possible future.

**[[Great Chasm|The Floating Machinery]]** — Runic platforms and vast suspended chambers, where the laws of gravity change from room to room and an exhausted mystic holds back further damage.

**[[Kemeko Monastery]]** — The kenku stronghold above the depths, whose help makes the refugees' ascent possible.
