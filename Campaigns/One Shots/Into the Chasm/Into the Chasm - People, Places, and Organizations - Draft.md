# Into the Chasm: People, Places, and Organizations

This page collects the cast, places, groups, and important objects recorded across the six episodes of [[Into the Chasm]]. Descriptions follow the adventure through its ending.

## Player Characters

| Character | Place in the party |
| --- | --- |
| [[Finnan Oakstride\|Finnan]] | A halfling lore-gatherer whose music, magic, and Old Zimkovan help the party understand Kalima and Melusa. He rallies the willing refugees and seeks somewhere they can settle. |
| [[Jrain Fanlish\|Jrain]] | A traveler, messenger, and archer who scouts with her hawk Rala, guides the party through danger, and organizes the refugees' passage through the portal. |
| [[Eolo]] | A brash young kenku whose flight and psionic powers carry him toward trouble as often as away from it. During the evacuation, he receives the refugees on the narrow platform beyond the portal. |

### Other figures

- **[[Pakrinek]]** — A kenku warrior sent to protect Eidrikas's expedition. In [[Into the Chasm - Episode 04|Episode 4]], Eidrikas reports that the orcs killed Pakrinek.
- **The dead man in the labyrinth** — A sacrifice from Melusa whose answers to Finnan's magic offer the party its first account of the village. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 02|Episode 2]].
- **The damaged custodian** — A stone-and-metal construct that questions the party's authorization and cannot resume repairs while a threat remains. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 05|Episode 5]].
- **The older Melusan woman** — An elder who blames the strangers for the disturbance and persuades many listeners to remain. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 06|Episode 6]].

## Groups and Traditions

- **[[Vejo Vaikai]]** — Eidrikas's mystic community, which guards knowledge of the ancient mechanisms that stabilize the chasm's planar vortices. The mechanisms' builders remain unknown.
- **Zahara's dissidents** — A small circle of Melusans who question the elders' insistence on endurance and seek a way to leave. Their refuge is on one of the floating islands.
- **[[Kestavo]]** — The spirits tended by Justas and recalled in Finnan's lore. Their magic unsettles Melusans for whom the Kestavo have become little more than old stories.
- **[[Zeyfa]]** — The feared West Wind associated with the labyrinth and its sacrifices. The villagers' accounts of Zeyfa should be read as their beliefs; the adventure does not settle the realm's metaphysics.
- **[[Melua]]** — An old waterfall kestavo recalled through Finnan's ancestral memories, suggesting a connection with Melusa's distant past. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 02|Episode 2]].
- **The orcs** — The band that attacks the machinery, kills Pakrinek, and carries away the keystone. A lightning-shrouded orc and a Thark-marked God Caller are among the enemies the party faces in the lower chamber.

## Objects That Matter

- **Romil's glowing token** — Justas gives Kalima a light-enchanted token that steadies her in the labyrinth and becomes evidence, to her family, that the Kestavo are more than stories.
- **Malquin's necklace** — The token Malquin gives the party when he permits their passage through the guarded gash. They leave without another audience with him.
- **The keystone** — A curved, rune-carved arc taken from the mechanism. Its charm draws Eolo toward the chambers below; returning it to the broken circuit steadies the machinery and begins closing the portal to Melusa.
