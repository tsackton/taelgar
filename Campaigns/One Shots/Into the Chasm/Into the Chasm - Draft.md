Into the Chasm is a six-episode adventure about five strangers, a village trapped beyond the world, and the failing machinery beneath the [[Great Chasm]]. An impossible storm casts the travelers into [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]], where their search for a way home leads through living snow, a fogbound village, and the court of a frightened ruler who no longer trusts that the outside world is real.

## The Adventure

For months, strange weather pours out of the Great Chasm. A sandstorm brings summer heat in November; a rainbow shatters into glass; the sky turns black. An expedition goes down to investigate and does not return. Then [[Kalima]], a terrified woman speaking Old Zimkovan, climbs out of the depths. When a whiteout storm sweeps her and five adventurers into the chasm, they wake together on an ice platform, with wolves guarding the bridges and a violent wind barring the way back.

Beyond the labyrinth lies [[Melusa]], Kalima's home: a village of ledge houses and floating islands, where the sun seldom sets and the villagers send sacrifices into the maze. Her family offers shelter, but little hope of escape. The villagers endure, the dissidents search for a way out, and [[Forquarion|Malquin the Vaqar]] watches from his Ice Palace. Five strange gashes in the cliff offer glimpses of other worlds. One may lead home—if the party can persuade Malquin to let them pass.

Their escape brings them to [[Eidrikas]], Ekko's missing mentor, holding a damaged mechanism together with his own body. Orcs have carried away its keystone. Recovering it means descending through floating stone, warped gravity, and ancient chambers full of danger. Restoring it should steady the planes, but may also close the way back to Melusa. With their own route home within reach, the explorers choose to return for those who want to leave. The machinery is restored, the willing villagers escape, and the rescued Melusans eventually find a new home.

## Along the Way

**[[Zeyfa's Labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** — Ice bridges, vanishing passages, and a beast waiting at the end of every route. Kalima has escaped once, but the wind has brought her back.

**[[Melusa]] and the Ice Palace** — A village in the fog, sustained by strange abundance and governed by sacrifice. Its people disagree about whether endurance is their only possible future.

**[[Great Chasm|The Floating Machinery]]** — Runic platforms and vast suspended chambers, where the laws of gravity change from room to room and an exhausted mystic holds back further damage.

**[[Kemeko Monastery]]** — The kenku stronghold above the depths, whose help makes the refugees' ascent possible.
