# Into the Chasm: People, Places, and Organizations

This page collects the cast, places, and groups recorded across the six episodes of [[Into the Chasm]]. Descriptions follow the adventure through its ending.

> [!tip] Campaign home
> Return to [[Into the Chasm|the landing page]], or begin with [[Into the Chasm - Episode 01|Episode 1]].

| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Finnan Oakstride\|Finnan]] | A halfling lore-gatherer whose music, magic, and Old Zimkovan help the party understand Kalima and Melusa.                               |

### Other figures

- **[[Pakrinek]]** — A kenku warrior sent to protect Eidrikas's expedition. In [[Into the Chasm - Episode 04|Episode 4]], Eidrikas reports that the orcs killed Pakrinek.
- **The dead man in the labyrinth** — A sacrifice from Melusa whose answers to Finnan's magic offer the party its first account of the village. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 02|Episode 2]].
- **The damaged custodian** — A stone-and-metal construct that questions the party's authorization and cannot resume repairs while a threat remains. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 05|Episode 5]].
- **The older Melusan woman** — An elder who blames the strangers for the disturbance and persuades many listeners to remain. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 06|Episode 6]].

## Groups and Traditions

- **[[Vejo Vaikai]]** — Eidrikas's mystic community, which guards knowledge of the ancient mechanisms that stabilize the chasm's planar vortices. The mechanisms' builders remain unknown.
- **[[Kestavo]]** — The spirits tended by Justas and recalled in Finnan's lore. Their magic unsettles Melusans for whom the Kestavo have become little more than old stories.
- **[[Zeyfa]]** — The feared West Wind associated with the labyrinth and its sacrifices by the villagers of Melusa.
- **[[Melua]]** — An old waterfall kestavo recalled through Finnan's ancestral memories, suggesting a connection with Melusa's distant past. [[Into the Chasm - Episode 02|Episode 2]].
- **The orcs** — The remnants of the [[Ravager of Winter]]'s horde that attacks the machinery, kills Pakrinek, and carries away the keystone. A lightning-shrouded orc and a Thark-marked God Caller are among the enemies the party faces in the lower chamber.
