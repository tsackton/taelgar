# Narrative

<!-- SLOT: narrative.short -->
Months of ominous signs associated with the [[Great Chasm]] culminate in five days of black skies. Soon after, an unnatural whiteout storm drags [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]], [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]], [[Jrain Fanlish|Jrain]], [[Ekko]], [[Eolo]], and a strange Zimkovan woman [[Into the Chasm|into the chasm]].

They awaken on an ice platform floating in darkness, under the false sky of the shrieking white storm, and begin taking stock of one another, when [[Eolo]] approaches a frozen ice statue of a giant wolf.

His approach wakes the icebound wolves, and a desperate fight breaks out against wind-shades, chasm wisps, and darkness.

After the fight, with the platform quiet, the Zimkovan woman says they are in [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]], where her people hid from an old cataclysm. The only way out, she says, is to proceed deeper into the maze.

While the woman clutches Romil's token beside Finnan's illusory hearth, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] turns to the others and recounts her story, telling a tale of the ancient people before [[The Downfall]] who fled into the labyrinth. The strangers compare how the storm caught each of them; the woman adds that the West Wind sends her people into the labyrinth as sacrifices.

The woman introduces herself as [[Kalima]] and warns that every path leads to the beast. With no other options, the adventurers and [[Kalima]] set off across one of the bridges, into the labyrinth.

<!-- SLOT: narrative.long -->
For generations, the [[Great Chasm]] has split [[Zimkova]], an endless cleft of strange lights, impossible structures, gravity-defying winds, and unseasonable growths. As our adventure begins, months of ominous signs have issued from the [[Great Chasm]], bringing uncertainty and fear to the people of [[Zimkova]]. When the sky above the chasm turns black for five days, the [[Vejo Vaikai]] send an expedition of mystics protected by Kemeko warriors to investigate. They do not return; instead, a [[Kalima|strange woman]] speaking Old Zimkovan emerges from the [[Great Chasm]]. These events draw five adventurers -- [[Jrain Fanlish|Jrain]], [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]], [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]], [[Ekko]], and [[Eolo]] -- to the edge of the [[Great Chasm]], where a sudden and unnatural whiteout storm drags them and the Zimkovan woman down [[Into the Chasm|into the Chasm]]. As they fall through the blowing whiteness and plummet into the unknown, each struggles to hold on to their gear and whatever else they can save.

They awaken on an ice platform, apparently floating in a black void, the false sky of a shrieking white storm overhead. Dim blue light glows from inside the platform, revealing two bridges leading off into darkness and a large frozen statue of a wolf before each bridge. [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] plays a simple melody on his clay pipe, bringing light and clear looks to the platform. [[Eolo]] snaps to standing with both crossbows drawn, [[Ekko]] lowers a wing to restrain [[Eolo]] from panicked action, [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]] rises defensively with axe and shield, and [[Jrain Fanlish|Jrain]] regains her poise standing beside the [[Kalima|young Zimkovan woman]] she tried to protect during the fall. The woman is dressed in unfamiliar leathers and furs, terrified, and only speaks Old Zimkovan; when [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] sings to calm her, the wind nearly steals his voice, but the spell settles her. As he starts to question the woman, [[Eolo]] approaches one of the frozen ice statues of a giant wolf.

The wolf begins to move as [[Eolo]] approaches, gathering itself to pounce. [[Eolo]] tries a telekinetic shove and fires crossbow bolts, but the guardian gets the better of the exchange, knocking him down, biting into his throat, and leaving an icy shard pulsing in his neck. [[Ekko]] takes the shape of a polar bear, cracking the other wolf's shell of ice, while [[Jrain Fanlish|Jrain]] puts arrows into the first guardian. [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]] steps into the fight with axe, shield, and a conjured _Spiritual Weapon_. Finnan's music and calming magic keeps panic from breaking into unchecked fear. As the battle proceeds, wind-shades sweep in from the darkness: one terrible shriek drops the Zimkovan woman unconscious on the ice. The first wolf shatters, and chasm wisps spill out of it, drawn to light. They snuff Finnan's magical glow, and leave the party fighting by the platform's dim blue light, trying to shove [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] toward the edge. As [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]] and [[Eolo]] weather the wolves' bites and the party finally defeats the platform guardians, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] brings the Zimokvan woman back with a healing word.

After the last wisp is gone, quiet falls on the ice platform. With the last of his calming magic, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] turns back to the woman, asking for information in Old Zimkovan. Looking at the ice bridges, the woman realizes with despair that she is back in [[Zeyfa's Labyrinth]], from which no one escapes. Fighting her despair, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] coaxes old stories from her with music. She speaks of her people, who came generations ago to hide from a cataclysm that changed the earth; they found [[Zeyfa]] already waiting and demanding appeasement. The only way out, she says, shaking with terror, is to proceed deeper into the maze, towards the beast. Panicked, she collapses. Trying to exude calm, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] summons an illusion of a small hearth, and [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]] searches his charms for one of Romil's tokens, which he makes glow with the light of the [[Kestavo]]. Scared but steadied, the woman clutches the glowing token while singing an unfamiliar lullaby.

As the woman steadies herself by the illusory, but calming, hearth, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] turns from her to the others, introduces himself as [[Finnan Oakstride]], and retells her tale: before Drankor, when much of the land was [[Urtzabala|swamp]], as [[The Downfall]] reshaped the world her people fled into this place, and became trapped in its maze. The strangers then speak of their own journeys that brought them here, before the conversation turns to what the group knows of the chasm, Kemeko, the [[Kestavo]], and [[Zeyfa]]. When [[Jrain Fanlish|Jrain]] the woman asks what appeases the West Wind, [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] gently draws the her back, calming her confusion over what he is and then receiving the answer: when the sun sets and the new ones are old enough, the West Wind demands that one of her people be sent into its labyrinth as a sacrifice.

The conversation stretches into a short rest. [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] looks for magic, but apart from Justas's shield nothing on the platform or in the false sky gives a clean signature. Meanwhile the cloud presses lower, the wind howls harder, and the woman curls in on herself, saying the beast does not like waiting. The group tests the platform for means of escape, but are rebuffed. An object dropped straight down is blown violently back; even [[Eolo]] and [[Ekko]] leaping into the void only to learn that the updraft makes escape by flight impossible. With little choice, the party decides to brave the bridges, the maze, and the beast. When [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] asks, the woman introduces herself as [[Kalima]], and tells the party that all paths lead to the beast. She only escaped by luck, after finding an earthen tunnel and digging through it. [[Justas Rhostrin|Justas]] and [[Finnan Oakstride|Finnan]] argue that she is safer with them than left on the platform alone; she agrees to come if she can stay between them, and the group sets out across the bridge into the snowy maze.
