## Narrative

### Sunday, May 30th, 2 Robert I

The party continues down the wide road towards the river, hoping to find [[Gwydren]] among the great trees on the far side. After about an hour, they hear compelling drums from within the forest. [[Drou]] and [[Yvan Greenrabbit|Yvan]] resist the music, but [[Fazoth de Brune|Fazoth]] is charmed and begins wandering towards it.

Yvan tries to grab Fazoth but cannot stop him. Drou plays against the drums, distracting Fazoth enough for him to use _Misty Step_ to reach a nearby tree. Fazoth clings to the trunk even as his legs continue trying to carry him towards the music. With Drou's countermusic helping him resist, he finally breaks free of the enchantment. The party nearly loses the road in the confusion, but manages to find it again.

Farther along, they hear skittering above a fallen tree. They decide against investigating and continue towards the river.

The river is swift and about sixty feet across, with the forest overhanging its banks. Drou uses her _Staff of the Python_ to summon a giant constrictor snake, planning to have it ferry everyone across.

As they prepare to cross, two hidden displacer beasts attack. One lashes out with its tendrils and bites Yvan, nearly knocking him unconscious. Fazoth answers with _Witch Bolt_, but misses. Drou's _Dissonant Whispers_ and Yvan's _Scorching Ray_ wound one of the beasts as the party gets onto the snake and heads into the river.

The beasts follow them into the water, trying to flank the snake and its passengers. Fazoth's _Eldritch Blast_, Yvan's sorcery, and a second _Dissonant Whispers_ from Drou further wound the injured beast. The party reaches the far bank with one attacker badly hurt and the other still uninjured. Both beasts abandon the pursuit.

On the far side, the party takes a short rest while Fazoth sends his pseudodragon to scout the trees that rise above the canopy. Over the next two hours, it searches the great trees and finds a tent and fire: Gwydren's camp.

Gwydren stands about eight feet tall, with moose-like legs, a human-shaped torso and arms covered in light brown fur, and a head combining human and moose features. Broad antlers hung with moss rise above his head. His robe is made of plants, moss, and lichen, and he wears a green cloak woven with ferns.

He is suspicious of the visitors. The party talks about a fey acquaintance, explains their intention to return the Singing Tree's heart, and discusses [[Caradoc]], whom Gwydren knows as Aurenxis. Yvan makes a sincere appeal, while Drou gives an impressive performance supported by Yvan's illusions. They convince Gwydren that they can protect him, and he agrees to guide them.

During their conversation, Gwydren tells them that Caradoc is something of a famous figure, and recounts his adventures as a young man in the [[Feywild]]. Traveling under the name Aurenxis, Caradoc reached [[Sunward Reach]], a fortified realm between the nightmare realms of [[Harrow]] and [[Wend]] on the eastern, nightward side of the [[Sunset Gate]] and [[Morlaith|the Briarheart]]'s domain to the west. After a raid by Harrow and Wend led to the capture of a prominent knight of Sunward Reach, Caradoc ventured into [[Darkfall]] himself, freed the captive, and brought the knight back.

For the rescue, [[Calanthe|Lady Calanthë]] granted him a boon, represented by an item that could summon her aid. This is the vine-covered wooden wand Caradoc has since given the party. The rescue also marked him in the eyes of Harrow and Wend's agents. He now fears returning to the Feywild, where they might find him and hunt him down.

Gwydren serves a rich forest-vegetable stew, roasted carrots, and honeycomb for dessert. The party spends the night at his camp, taking a long rest.

### Monday, May 31st, 2 Robert I

The next morning, the baying of a wolf and the sound of hunting horns carry through the forest. The hunters serve the [[Lord Umbraeth|Gloomshaper]], another archfey seeking the heart to cause chaos. Gwydren urges the party to hurry and leads them towards a shortcut through Old Brindlethorn's sett. He knows how to navigate her extensive tunnels, but warns the party to be polite and leave her bedrooms alone.

Old Brindlethorn is a female badger as large as a cottage. She lies across the entrance, trapped in an enchanted nightmare. Her distress appears to be aggravated by the Singing Tree's corruption and the nearby fey heart, and she thrashes and claws in her sleep.

Yvan tries to push her or prop her up, but is struck by a flailing claw. Attempts to wake her also fail. Although she cannot be roused, the party realizes she can still sense what is happening around her. Drou plays soothing music, while Yvan uses _Prestidigitation_ to create the scent of fresh soil. Together, they calm her movements enough for Yvan to slip through with the heart, carrying it farther away from the sleeping badger.

Once inside, Yvan tries to sneak into her bedroom, but makes too much noise. Brindlethorn erupts into another burst of violent agitation, catching Drou with a claw. Despite this, the party and Gwydren all manage to pass the sleeping badger.

We end as the whole group enters Old Brindlethorn's tunnels.
