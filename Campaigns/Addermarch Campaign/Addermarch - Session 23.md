# Addermarch Campaign - Session 23

>[!info] The Heart Returned: in which the party restores the Singing Tree
> *Featuring: [[Yvan Greenrabbit]], [[Drou]], [[Fazoth de Brune]]*
> *In Taelgar: (DR:: 1715-05-31) - (DR_end:: 1715-06-03)*
> *On Earth: Sunday November 1, 2026*
> *[[Thornweald]]*

In the [[Thornweald]], the party emerges from [[Old Brindlethorn]]'s tunnels to face a massive enraged boar and several thorn trees bashing against a massive oak. After defeating the enraged trees and animals, the party meets Rumplewick, a small, impeccably dressed fey who lives in the oak and, grateful for their aid against the thorn trees, gives them tea and cookies and beeswax earplugs against the maddening song of the Singing Tree. Proceeding down the path, the party struggles against the song that attempts to turn them against each other, but finally returns the Fey Heart and ends the Singing Tree's destructive song. Exiting the Feywild through the [[Nightsong Roots]] portal, the party makes their way back to [[Caradoc]].

## Narrative

### Monday, May 31st, 2 Robert I

With [[Gwydren]] as their guide, the party moves quickly through [[Old Brindlethorn]]'s tunnels and emerges into a fight on two fronts. A massive boar, surrounded by stirges, blocks the path to the Singing Tree, while nearby thorn trees batter a large oak. The party engages with both groups of enemies at once. [[Drou]] dodges the boar's early attack, while [[Yvan Greenrabbit|Yvan]] sends a _Chromatic Orb_ bouncing between the trees and [[Fazoth de Brune|Fazoth]] summons his fey ally. The thorn trees get off several lashing vine attacks, drawing [[Yvan Greenrabbit|Yvan]] closer, and [[Drou]] is badly battered by the boar. But the combat turns when Yvan unleashes a devastating _Fireball_ that rips through the thorn trees with particular force. Fazoth's Eldritch Blast helps end the fight, though Yvan and Drou are left badly wounded.

With the engaged creatures destroyed, the party approaches the large oak and realizes they have saved the home of Rumplewick, a small, fancily dressed fey wearing a top hat. Grateful, he invites them inside for tea and cookies, complaining about the awful racket outside. This draws the party's attention to the faint echoes of the Singing Tree's song: listening to it causes Drou and Fazoth to turn on one another, drawn into the maddening anger of the song. Rumplewick drags them into his home and shuts the door, blocking out the noise, before they can seriously hurt each other. Inside the tree is a neat, well-maintained kitchen with a brass stove, a whistling tea kettle, and cookies in the oven. Over refreshments, Rumplewick introduces himself, thanks his rescuers, and expresses surprise at their willingness to face the Singing Tree. Before they leave, he gives them beeswax earplugs to make the approach safer.

The party follows the path for about half a mile. As they approach the Singing Tree, even the earplugs can no longer keep out its maddening song, its magical keening of anger and despair. Fazoth and Drou initially resist the song, but Yvan falls under its influence. Fazoth tries to approach the tree, only to be driven back by its dissonant echoes. Drou moves forward to help, casting a spell to deafen Fazoth against the tree's song, but Yvan turns a devastating _Witch Bolt_ on her; only her _Silvery Barbs_ twists fate and averts the blow that would have knocked her unconscious otherwise. Fazoth resists the song and draws closer, but before he can place the heart Drou casts _Shatter_ on top of herself and Yvan. Finally Fazoth manages to place the heart around the roots of the young sapling. 

The Singing Tree is restored. Its song changes from anger and despair to beauty, and the party leaves the Feywild through the [[Nightsong Roots]] portal. They make their way west to [[Haldrenn]], where they rest for the night. 

### Tuesday June 1st, 2 Robert I

The party leaves [[Haldrenn]] and travels leisurely back towards [[Caradoc]]'s cottage near [[Brantor]].

### Wednesday June 2nd, 2 Robert I

The party continues on their leisurely walk to [[Caradoc]]'s cottage near [[Brantor]].

### Thursday, June 3rd, 2 Robert I

The party arrives at Caradoc's cottage near lunchtime, having solved the mystery that began with enraged animals in [[Haldrenn]], led through the serpentine weapons, and ended with the recovery and return of the fey heart.
