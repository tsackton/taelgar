The party rescues Rumplewick, returns the stolen fey heart to the Singing Tree, and makes its way back to [[Caradoc]] after ending the tree's maddening song.

## Timeline

- (DR:: 1715-05-31): The party passes through [[Old Brindlethorn]]'s tunnels, defeats the boar, stirges, and thorn trees, and shelters in Rumplewick's home before restoring the Singing Tree and leaving through [[Nightsong Roots]].
- Return journey, date unconfirmed: The party travels back to [[Caradoc]]'s cottage near [[Brantor]].

## Cast of Characters

- **[[Yvan Greenrabbit]]** - A halfling wild-magic sorcerer whose fireball helps defeat the thorn trees and who returns the heart to the Singing Tree.
- **[[Drou]]** - Elven bard-monk, badly wounded in the fighting and later turned against her companions by the tree's song.
- **[[Fazoth de Brune]]** - Human warlock-alchemist whose summoned fey and spellwork help clear the path.
- **[[Gwydren]]** - The forest guide who accompanies the party into Old Brindlethorn's tunnels.
- **Rumplewick** - A small, fancily dressed fey in a top hat, rescued from the attack on his tree home; he offers hospitality and beeswax earplugs.
- **[[Caradoc]]** - The alchemist to whose cottage the party returns at the end of its journey.

## Narrative

### Monday, May 31st, 2 Robert I

With [[Gwydren]] as their guide, the party moves quickly through [[Old Brindlethorn]]'s tunnels and emerges into a fight on two fronts. A massive boar, surrounded by stirges, blocks the path to the Singing Tree, while nearby thorn trees batter a larger tree.

The boar waits to charge anyone who steps onto the path. [[Drou]] advances, but its first attack misses, leaving her on her feet and able to strike back. As the stirges join the attack, [[Fazoth de Brune|Fazoth]] attacks the thorn trees, hoping to draw them away from their target and into range of a fireball. [[Yvan Greenrabbit|Yvan]] follows with a _Chromatic Orb_ that bounces between the trees. Their lashing thorn vines entangle him and drag him closer, while the boar and stirges badly wound Drou.

Fazoth summons a fey ally, and Yvan's heightened _Fireball_ devastates the trees, whose vulnerability to fire makes the spell especially destructive. Drou retreats from the boar and stirges, and Fazoth kills the boar with _Eldritch Blast_. His blasts and summoned fey, together with Drou's spellwork and Yvan's fireball, bring down both groups of enemies. Yvan and Drou survive with barely any strength left.

The party has saved the home of Rumplewick, a small, fancily dressed fey wearing a top hat. Grateful, he invites them inside for tea and cookies. Before they enter, his complaint about the awful racket draws their attention to the Singing Tree's song. Listening to it causes several of the party to turn on one another. Rumplewick drags them into his home and shuts the door, blocking out the noise.

Inside is a neat, well-maintained kitchen with a brass stove, a tea kettle, and cookies in the oven. Over refreshments, Rumplewick introduces himself, thanks his rescuers, and expresses surprise at their willingness to face the Singing Tree. Before they leave, he gives them beeswax earplugs to make the approach safer.

The party follows the path for about half a mile. Within sixty or seventy feet of the Singing Tree, even the earplugs cannot keep out its magical keening of anger and despair. Fazoth and Drou initially resist the song, but Yvan falls under its influence. Fazoth tries to approach the tree, only to be driven back by its dissonant echoes. Drou moves forward to help, and Yvan turns a devastating _Witch Bolt_ on her; her _Silvery Barbs_ helps avert the blow.

Fazoth again resists the song and draws closer, but his summoned fey succumbs and attacks. Drou also falls under the tree's influence, casting a _Shatter_ that badly hurts both herself and Yvan. Amid the chaos, Yvan teleports into the tree's vicinity and places the fey heart back where it belongs.

The Singing Tree is restored. Its song changes from anger and despair to beauty, and the party leaves the Feywild through the [[Nightsong Roots]] portal.

### Return to Caradoc

The party travels back to [[Caradoc]]'s cottage near [[Brantor]]. The mystery that began with enraged animals in [[Haldrenn]], led through the serpentine weapons, and ended with the recovery and return of the fey heart is resolved. We end with the party back at Caradoc's, considering what comes next.
