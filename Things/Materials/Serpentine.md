# Serpentine
>[!info]+ Information  
> (material)  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

Serpentine is a pale green gemstone that is associated with necromancy and enchantment magic. 

It is most famously associated with [[Avatus]], ruler of the [[Dominion of Avatus]], whose desire for serpentine was a major underlying cause of the [[Serpentine Wars]] in the DR 1300s. Serpentine weapons, produced by [[Avatus]], played a major role in the [[Serpentine Wars]] between the [[Dominion of Avatus]] and [[Addermarch]]. Tracking and safely disposing these cursed weapons after these wars was an early formative task for the [[Rangers]], who recorded the [[Serpentine Register]] to document their efforts.
