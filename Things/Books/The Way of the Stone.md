# The Way of the Stone
>[!info]+ Information  
> An alchemical tome, written before DR 1600, by an unknown author  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

*The Way of the Stone* is a fragile alchemical tome written in Draconic. The book contains recipes for invulnerability, youth, immortality, and related elixirs. But all the recipes depend on something called the [[Prime Arcana]], which is only loosely described as a magical stone associated with transmutation magic. 

One extant copy was found on [[Grimstone]]'s corpse after the [[Labyrinth Prisoners]] killed him in his laboratory beneath [[Perdoli Manor]]; based on Grimstone's notes, he believed that the Prime Arcana could be made from the right petrified living victim, but the veracity of this statement is unknown.
