# On the Birth and Death of the Lesser Gods
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Dunmar|Dunmari]] theological treatise, by Esa  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

*On the Birth and Death of the Lesser Gods* is a work by Esa, a Lakan scholar who lived about seventy years before [[Kassi]] researched the text. Esa examines minor divinities that seem to arise and disappear, comparing several traditions that Dunmari theology groups uneasily under the category of lesser gods.

Much of the work considers [[Bhishma]], [[Shakun]], and [[Adra]]. Esa begins from the Dunmari teaching that such beings received divinity from [[Dunmari Religion|the Five Siblings]], who bestowed part of their power upon those who would otherwise have remained mortal.

Esa then compares three less easily classified traditions: the [[Kestavo]] of [[Sembara]], the tanshi of the northern Deno'qai, and the kadaa, or saints, of the Mawar. She treats the kadaa as demigods comparable to those of Dunmari tradition, citing [[Guzo the Mariner]] as a historical person elevated to divinity after death. She is more skeptical of the Kestavo, proposing that some may have been fey beings mistaken for gods, much like the fey guardians associated with [[Tyrwingha]].

The tanshi present Esa with a different problem. She accepts them as genuine gods, yet observes that many appear to have vanished as their worshippers were dispersed or destroyed. Drawing on accounts of the Drankorian conquest of the north and later devastation during the [[Great War]], the [[Blood Years]], and the hobgoblin wars, she asks whether a god can truly die when the community sustaining its connection to the world is destroyed.

Esa argues that divine energy cannot itself be destroyed. A god whose worship disappears should therefore be understood not as dead but as severed: a surviving divinity whose connection to the world has faded away. If belief creates or sustains that connection, she reasons, renewed worship might restore it. The work consequently imagines lost divinities wandering the outer planes, cut off from Taelgar but potentially capable of return.
