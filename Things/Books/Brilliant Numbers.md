# Brilliant Numbers
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Drankorian Empire|Drankorian]] mathematical treatise, written during the [[History of the Drankorian Empire|Drankorian Era]], by the [[Arithrimos Lamperum]]  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

*Brilliant Numbers* is a Drankorian mathematical treatise, introducing brilliant numbers: six-digit numbers that are each the product of two three-digit prime numbers. Most of the text is an index of several thousand brilliant numbers, arranged sequentially.

Within the [[Arithrimos Lamperum]] brilliant numbers served as encoded location keys: by memorizing the right factors, members of the order could record maps of extraplanar weak points with locations concealed in plain sight.
