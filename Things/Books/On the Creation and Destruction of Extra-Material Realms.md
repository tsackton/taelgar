# On the Creation and Destruction of Extra-Material Realms
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Drankorian Empire|Drankorian]] cosmological treatise, written during the [[History of the Drankorian Empire|Drankorian Era]]  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

A Drankorian book about the creation and destruction of realms in places like the Feywild and Shadowfell. No extant copies are known, but several fragments on the Feywild are preserved as commentary on a famous Drankorian play ([[A Fool's Fortune]]) about a hapless merchant who ends up in the Feywild.
