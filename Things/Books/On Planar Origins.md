# On Planar Origins
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Chardonian Empire|Chardonian]] cosmological treatise, written in the DR 900s, by [[Gaius Devarro]]  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

On Planar Origins was meant to be [[Gaius Devarro|Gauis Devarro's]] magisterial treatise on the origin of planes, drawing together several extant Hkaran and early Drankorian works, and reconciling them with the emerging [[Standard Multiversal Model]], but it was never finished. Nevertheless, it remains valuable for its terminology: it is widely credited with popularizing the term the [[Riving]], and for its practical discussions of observable impacts of the Riving within the modern [[Multiverse]].
