The Yo'nari were an ancient proto-[[Deno'qai]] people and among the first humans to settle the northwest coast and interior mountains of [[Apporia]]. Before the [[Downfall Wars]], most of these people vanished into [[Cairn Dor]] with their queen, [[Yeshara]], leaving ruined settlements and standing stones behind on the [[Material Plane]].

## History

The Yo'nari belonged to the early Northros-speaking migrations, from which the [[Deno'qai]], [[Vargaldi]], [[Zimka]], and perhaps others descended. They frequently encountered fey as they traveled across [[Apporia]], and called the [[Feywild]] *Nazil-Dimu*. Their surviving sites on the [[Material Plane]] use monumental stonework and wolf imagery, including parallel rows of wolf-headed plinths and the trilithic [[Cleaver-Stone]].

Yeshara's growing fear of dreams divided the Yo'nari. Some fled as she outlawed sleep rites and imposed prolonged vigils, while those who remained participated in a midwinter rite intended to sever their connection to sleep and the [[Plane of Souls|Plane of Consciousness]]. The rite instead created Cairn Dor and carried Yeshara and her followers into the new realm. 

## Cairn Dor

The native population of Cairn Dor is the continuation of the Yo'nari people, transformed by long residence in the [[Shadowfolds]] and by Yeshara's doctrine of eternal waking. The ordinary inhabitants, called the *Nurim-Dor*, or People of Eternal Waking, fear sleep yet must occasionally rest, while the [[Shemra Azem]], or Guardians of the Vigil, formed a distinct sleepless military order sustained by magic.
