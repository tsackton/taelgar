The Mariners are an ancient seafaring culture, originally from [[Hkar]], who left in a broad migration in the years prior to [[The Downfall]]. They settled first around the [[Eastern Green Sea]] and spread westward along the shores of the [[Green Sea]]. 

%%^Campaign:none%%

A few additional notes:
- Their language was the ancestor of the [[Languages#Eastros Language Family|Eastros languages]]
- They brought with them a pantheonic religious tradition distinct from [[Mos Numena]]. 
- Later cultural and religious schisms divided their descendants; the [[Skaer]] are one surviving people who trace their ancestry to the original migrants. The long-uninvented "monotheistic religion" is supposed to be a descendant of a Mariner schism. 

Nomenclature:

“Mariners” is intended as a modern scholarly exonym, perhaps Chardonian. Ancient Mariners and Hkaran Mariners can be used as a fuller form if needed. Their departure from Hkar is known as the Mariner Migration or, more traditionally, the Mariner Exodus, though few details other than its existence are invented. 

No shared endonym is invented, and it is not known whether the original communities used a single collective name (unlikely, but possible, especially in the original Hkaran incarnation). Eastros is a Drankorian scholarly name for the descendant language family, not intended as a recorded name for the original people (though it could be related to a Drankorian exonym). The historical name of the original Mariner language is unknown.
