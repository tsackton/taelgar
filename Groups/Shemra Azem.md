The Shemra Azem, also known as the sleepless, are a mysterious group, spoken of in rumors and legends as the ever‑vigilant army associated with legends of [[Yeshara]], the wolf queen. 

%%
Major DM notes in backlinks

Recurrent wars:

- During the [[Apporian Shadow War]] of DR 657–661, Drankorian armies forced their way to the Cleaver-Stone, closed the route into Cairn Dor, and built the [[~Drankorian Cleaver-Stone Fort~]] to prevent new raids. The fortress suppressed further large-scale incursions until the fall of the [[Drankorian Empire]].
- During the [[War of the Severed Dreams]] of DR 1191–1196, Yeshara's forces reappeared after the Drankorian fort had been abandoned. This was the first conflict in which their enemies were widely called the Umbral Covenant. The defenders damaged the soul compass used to find dreamers and again locked the Cleaver-Stone.
- During the [[War of the Dark Rift]] of DR 1584–1597, the Shemra Azem emerged through a separate route near [[Enderra]], destroyed the dwarven realm, and invaded northern Apporia. Their material army was eventually defeated, but the war allowed Yeshara's forces to recover the old Drankorian fort and regain access to the Cleaver-Stone and its soul compass.
- A new cycle began with the [[Lizardfolk Village Disappearances]] of the DR 1730s and 1740s. Yeshara's forces took entire communities, using their inhabitants as dreamers while preparing another army. This cycle was interrupted before it became an open war.

Details:
Waking dreamers caused the soldiers sustained by them to fall. However, it is also likely/possible that killing the Shemra Azem, perhaps only possible away from Cairn Dor, destroys the dreamers; this is needed to explain the disapperance of the dwarves of Enderra after the War of the Dark Rift
