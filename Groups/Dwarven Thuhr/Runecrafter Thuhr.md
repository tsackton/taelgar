# The Runecrafter Thuhr
*(ROON-kraf-ter THUR)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Dwarves|dwarven]] thuhr  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

The Runecrafter Thuhr is one of the seven traditional [[Dwarves|dwarven]] *thuhr*: institutions of kinship and obligation that bind dwarven society together across clan and kingdom. In dwarven tradition, it is associated with [[Karthel]], the Runecrafter among the [[Bahrazel|Bahrâzel]], and with the runic arts that are central to dwarven identity. Its traditions also encompass scholarship and the search for lost lore.
