The Farmer Thuhr is one of the seven traditional [[Dwarves|dwarven]] *thuhr*: institutions of kinship and obligation that bind dwarven society together across clan and kingdom. In dwarven tradition, it is associated with [[Oshkir]], the Farmer among the [[Bahrazel|Bahrâzel]], and with living things, growth, healing, and teaching.
