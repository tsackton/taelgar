# The Stoneworker Thuhr
*(STOHN-wer-ker THUR)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Dwarves|dwarven]] thuhr  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

The Stoneworker Thuhr is one of the seven traditional [[Dwarves|dwarven]] *thuhr*: institutions of kinship and obligation that bind dwarven society together across clan and kingdom. In dwarven tradition, it is associated with [[Tarbaka]], the Stoneworker among the [[Bahrazel|Bahrâzel]], and with the crafts of stone and the forge.
