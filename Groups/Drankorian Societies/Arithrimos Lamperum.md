# The Arithrimos Lamperum
>[!info]+ Information  
> A [[Drankorian Empire|Drankorian]] secret society  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

The Arithrimos Lamperum was a Drankorian society dedicated to cataloging and understanding [[Extraplanar Weak Point|extraplanar weak points]] and other kinds of [[planar connections]]. For many years, the order recorded these places, using brilliant numbers as encoded location keys: large numbers, each built from prime factors of the same length, that could hide precise maps in plain sight, undecipherable to anyone who did not know the factors.

In the waning days of the [[Drankorian Empire]], the order increasingly focused on closing what planar connections they could.

## Purpose and Culture

The Arithrimos understood the boundaries between planes as porous walls requiring continual maintenance. Its members described their work as "keeping the outside out and the inside in," and for centuries regarded extraplanar incursions as a greater danger than the Empire's mortal rivals. Their work was genuinely protective: they mapped weak points, repaired damaged boundaries, contained creatures that crossed them, and closed passages that could not safely remain open.

The order combined mathematical and metaphysical scholarship with strict secrecy and compartmentalization. Cartographers such as [[Menestheus]] memorized the factors needed to decode [[Brilliant Numbers|brilliant-number]] maps, while other specialists performed the work of containment. Listeners could be instructed to observe a prisoner without being told what it was; as [[Kallipseis]] put it, "secrecy is security." Even instruction within the order could take the form of riddles and fairy tales whose hidden meanings were comprehensible only to initiates, collected in works such as *[[Allegories for the Initiated]]*.

Its hidden bases combined scholarship, engineering, and ordinary institutional life. The [[27th House]] contained maps, chained libraries, lecture halls, listening stations, laboratories, workshops, chapels, kitchens, baths, and quarters, all built inside an immense prison-machine.

The conviction that this work protected the world also made the order capable of profound cruelty. At its worst, the Arithrimos treated secrecy as justification, prisoners as research material, and souls as a source of motive power. *[[On the Science and Possibilities of Soul Bindery]]*, written by an early member, enthusiastically proposed powering golems, automata, ships, and weapons with the pressure of souls prevented from reaching the afterlife. The same principle eventually powered the 27th House. The order thus occupied an uneasy place between necessary guardianship and a willingness to pursue that mission without regard for its cost.

## History

The Arithrimos operated for centuries, locating and maintaining weak places throughout the planes. It eventually traced strange roots crossing planar boundaries back to the being later imprisoned in the [[27th House]]. After early investigators were consumed, the order captured the being and constructed a shifting complex around it, using the souls it held to keep the prison moving through space and time.

In the waning days of the Empire, [[Thalestria]], then head of the order, believed the world they knew was ending. She redirected the Arithrimos from maintaining planar boundaries toward permanently sealing as many remaining breaches as possible: in her metaphor, the revel had passed its height, and their task was to lock the doors and extinguish the lights.

The order's independence, power, and resources eventually drew the hostility of [[Apollyon]]. [[Kallipseis]] publicly resisted demands made by the Empire, while Thalestria believed their mission was too important to endanger through open defiance. When the Arithrimos was disbanded, Thalestria blamed Kallipseis and withdrew most of the personnel from the 27th House. To keep its prison operating without the order's attendants, she tricked [[Lord Endless Ending|Lord Revel Beginning]] into a bargain that supplied an unending stream of mortal visitors.

Kallipseis remained behind and claimed leadership after Thalestria's disappearance or death. She regarded the continued presence of herself and Menestheus as sufficient for the order to survive, and devoted herself to creating a body that would allow her to leave and rebuild it.
