# Combat table with enemy rosters

150 recorded encounters across five campaigns. This includes solo and smaller-party encounters as well as the four-or-five-PC subset. Dragonets is excluded. Each session link opens the source combat extraction.

Enemy lists are draft annotations derived from existing combat records. Names and descriptive forms follow the played source. Counts appear only where supported, and labels can overlap when the record uses several descriptions for one actor. A repeated enemy in successive encounters remains on each encounter row. Summoned enemies and minions are included. Allied NPCs, party summons and constructs are omitted from enemy lists. Changes of allegiance are explained in the roster notes.

Time is total elapsed recording time, including internal pauses and discussion. The PC count includes roster characters with a recorded regular turn. The normalized column divides elapsed minutes by recorded rounds and that PC count. It is blank when no PC acts. Friendly duels, PC conflicts and environmental opposition are labeled explicitly. The observed fight and extended-action-cycle encounter remain in this full table and carry their existing qualifications in the analysis data.

The filterable data is in [encounters.csv](encounters.csv). Enemy annotations and source-record fingerprints are in [enemy-rosters.yaml](enemy-rosters.yaml).

Dunmari sessions 96, 107 and 135 and Mawar episode 1 lack timed combat extractions. Labyrinths session 2 has a known missing recording interval containing an additional fight. This table covers the surviving extracted encounters.
