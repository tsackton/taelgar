 Calcara is a small, rocky island off the coast of [[Castrella]]. %%^Date:1749%% As of DR 1749, the otherwise uninhabited island was home to Kazuro, a stoneborn hermit who lived in one of its caves. %%^End%%

%%
future site of Kenzo's ocean monastery 

General caves: "Looking for a cave? Calcara Island is full of caves. Some are big - big enough to shelter from a storm in a good-sized boat, even. There are hidden depths there, I am sure. ""

Kazuro's cave: He lives in a simple stone structure carved out of the limestone, in a cave. It is dark most of the time, although he seems to have cultivated some kind of bioluminescence that lights everything with a pale moonlight blue glow. The cave has a dock built and steps carved that lead up to a porch/deck, and Kazuro's house. It is too small for the large ship to enter. 

idea but not canon: 

Kazuro Been wanting to construct a monument / temple / meditation retreat on top of the island, overlooking the ocean. But it is cursed. When the Chardonian navy drove out the (Shadow Armanda), they pulled down a watchtower that had stood here. But the land is now cursed by the evil fiends, and I cannot build until it is purified.
