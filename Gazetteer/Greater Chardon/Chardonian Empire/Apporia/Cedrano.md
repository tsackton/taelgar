# Cedrano
*(KEH-dran-oh)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Cedrano is a coastal province in the [[Chardonian Empire]], one of the three Apporian Provinces, located on the northeast coast of the [[Apporia|Apporian Peninsula]], across the [[Gulf of Chardon]] from [[Chardon]]. 

This is a land of rocky cliffs and tiny fishing villages nestled in small harbors. The interior is almost entirely uninhabited, dominated by rough mountainous terrain, with the exception of the [[Kedron]] river valley, which is dotted with some small villages and gives the province its name. The rocky, rough terrain of Cedrano supports minimal agriculture, largely small-scale sustenance farming; its economy is supported by silver mines in the interior, especially along the Kedron, and the rich bounty of its coastal waters.

The only significant city in Cedrano is [[Castrella]], at the mouth of the [[Kedron]] river, though numerous smaller villages dot the cliffs.. 

Cedrano was largely overrun during the [[War of the Dark Rift]], and [[Castrella]] was besieged but never sacked. It was incorporated into the growing [[Chardonian Empire]] shortly after the end of the war, in DR 1599, but granted significant autonomy.
