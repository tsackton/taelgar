# Enderra

Enderra was a stoneborn realm that once existed in the interior mountains of the [[Apporia|Apporian Peninsula]]. It was destroyed during the [[War of the Dark Rift]].

%%^Campaign:none%%

Unclear if this refers to the mountains, the stoneborn realm, or both. Unclear if any stoneborn still live there. 
There was also a dwarven kingdom beneath the mountains. Its fate is not decided but likely largely destroyed. 

DM Note Review

- **Kazuro’s homeland:** Kazuro explicitly calls the central mountain land “Enderra.” He was a young Stoneborn adult when the War of the Dark Rift began, fought in it, and eventually left because the mountains had become inseparable from his wartime trauma. [[Session 99 - DM Notes]]. See more: [[Kazuro]].
- **Origin of the war:** One private account says dwarven miners broke ancient seals beneath Enderra, releasing Shadowfell energy that corrupted them. The resulting Umbral Covenant appeared to Enderrans as corrupted dwarves and other shadow beings operating through a planar weak point. Its true nature and connection to the Void Mind remained unknown.. [[Session 99 - DM Notes]] **note this detail has been superceded by invention around [[Cairn Dor]]**
- **But that origin is not fully settled:** Other notes preserve several possibilities—opening a rift, awakening something buried, or attracting a dark power—and suggest the Covenant may have wanted something hidden in the mountains more than it wanted Chardon. The relevant continuity review calls this part of the history particularly weak. [[Session 63 Background]]
- **Cairn Dor connection:** Enderra contains a portal connected to Cairn Dor. Actual play established dead dwarven dreamers from the Dark Rift era, a portal emerging somewhere in Enderra, and a dwarven iron golem guarding it; the party later escaped Cairn Dor through that portal. [[Great Library Session Notes - Arc 5]]
