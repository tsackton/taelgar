# The Bastion Quarter
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Bastion Quarter, often just called the Bastion, occupies the area near the [[Trade Gate (Chardon)|Merchant's Gate]] where the [[North Road|northern trade road]] passes through the [[North Wall (Chardon)|North Wall]], extending south and southeast towards the river and the [[River Bridge (Chardon)|River Bridge]]. 

The Bastion Quarter and the [[Summit Quarter]] together make up Precinct XIX.
