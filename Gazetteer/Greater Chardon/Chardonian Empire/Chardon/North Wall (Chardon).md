# The North Wall
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The North Wall comprises the fortifications, constructed during the [[Great War]] and the [[Blood Years]], that encircle Precincts XVIII and XIX on the [[North Bank]] of the [[Chasa]], enclosing the neighborhoods of [[Bastion Quarter|Bastion]], [[Summit Quarter|Summit]], and the [[Chalyte Levels]]. Built to defend Chardon against hobgoblin attacks from the north as part of extensive fortifications on [[Mount Nera]], the wall is far less important as a fortification in the modern era, when the North Road passes through it into the heart of the empire.
