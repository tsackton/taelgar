# Kylos Market
*(KEE-lohs MAR-kit)*

Kylos Market is a major market and busy plaza in Precinct X.

%%^Date:1749-05-01%%

On May 1, DR 1749, a wall of gray fog appeared in the market during the [[Pandemonium Incursion in Chardon]]. People who touched it vanished, and subsequent winds drove some city residents mad. The vortex closed weeks later, but a whispering wall of cracked stone remained. Since then, the market has been largely abandoned.
