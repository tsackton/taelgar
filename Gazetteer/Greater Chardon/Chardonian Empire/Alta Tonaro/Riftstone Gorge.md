# The Riftstone Gorge
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Riftstone Gorge is a deep, broken canyon system cut into the arid northern slopes of the [[Chataan Mountains]], beneath the towering bulk of [[Thundercrown]]. It consists of a wide central canyon, flanked by steep cliffs, with narrow slot canyons branching in all directories. Most of the year, this is a land of dust, with only the occasional sparse scrub and hardy trees clinging to shaded canyon bottoms. However, after a rain, the canyon floor comes alive with green and sudden wildflowers. 

The Riftstone Gorge is mineral-rich with chalyte and silver, and strategically important for the [[Chardonian Empire]]. In the early DR 1700s, it was the site of a major Chardonian defeat, the [[Reports on Chalyte in Alta Tonaro#Report of the Riftstone Gorge Disaster|Riftstone Gorge Disaster]], which ultimately led to the end of the chalyte boom in the area, and the gorge became a byword for the dangers of the deep chalyte reserves in the Chataans. %%^Date:1749-07-10%%Since the defeat of [[Dhrukmir]], the stone giant tyrant who claimed the northern [[Chataan Mountains]] for himself, the newly formed [[Imperial Chalyte Mining Company]] has begun to redevelop the rich chalyte veins in this area%%^End%%
