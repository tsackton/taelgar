# Yeraad
*(yeh-RAHD)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Yeraad is a wide, winding river that flows from the foothills of the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]], through the southern parts of the [[Elderwood]], and across the lowlands to the [[Emerald Bay]]. 

It is a generally slow and winding river, carring a great volume of water. The Yeraad valley is a broad, low-lying basin surrounded by higher ground; numerous small swamps, wet riparian forests, oxbows, small lakes dot the river basin. 

Its name derives from the old Northos name, meaning Green River.
