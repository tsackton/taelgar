# The Mount Graybane
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Mount Graybane is an isolated mountain rising from the center of [[Tawir Forest]]. Its lower slopes are wooded; its bare summit is completely masked by an unnatural storm that has sat, unchanged and unmoving, over the mountain for hundreds of years. Formerly called Bald Mountain, it acquired the name Mount Graybane after the impenetrable cloud settled over its summit. 

The entrance to [[Airion's Secret Lair]] stands at the summit, within the quiet eye of the storm.
