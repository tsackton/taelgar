# Lake Suwi

Lake Suwi is a small lake upriver from the coastal village of [[Suwi]], west of the [[Fiatara Mountains]] and near the [[~North Bay~]]. The woods along its shore are frequently logged by the nearby villagers of [[Suwi]], and the ruins of a castle overlook the lake from a rocky clearing.
