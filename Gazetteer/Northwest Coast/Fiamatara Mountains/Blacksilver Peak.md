# Blacksilver Peak
>[!info]+ Information

>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Blacksilver Peak is a jagged mountain in the northern part of the [[Fiatara Mountains]], with a significant glacier covering the summit. A large complex of Dwarven ruins lies beneath the peak.

%%^Campaign:none%%

Not canonically determined if the dwarven ruins in Blacksilver Peak connect to the dwarven ruins to the south, the [[Goldpeak Mines]]. The PCs never found a connection, but that doesn't mean one doesn't exist.
