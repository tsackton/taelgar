# Airion's Floating Tower
>[!info]+ Information

>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The floating tower of [[Airion|Airion Mistspeaker]]. For many years after the [[Great War]], it could be found on [[Blacksilver Peak]], in the [[Fiatara Mountains]].

%%^Date:1748-03-01%%
After the [[Silver Tempests]] explored the tower and confronted [[Zadkai]], it remained for some months, but by spring of DR 1748, reports from [[Dinia]] said the tower had vanished. 
%%^End%%

%%^Date:1752-05-01%%
By DR 1752, rumors suggested the tower had been seen again, around the [[Erbalta Plains]]
%%^End%%
