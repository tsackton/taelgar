# Guluppa-Sog
*(goo-LUP-pa sog)*

Guluppa-Sog, as the bullywugs call it, loosely meaning "the settlement by the southern still-water", was a small [[Bullywugs|bullywug]] settlement on the [[Zarnato]], upriver from the [[Fort Zarnato]]. It was settled by bullywugs from the area around [[Blackwater Fens]] fleeing [[Nymthrax]].

With limited food, its people started raiding Chardonian cattle from the [[Fort Zarnato]], which eventually drew the attention of the [[Silver Tempests]], who attacked the village during their investigation, killing many residents before making peace after learning that [[Nymthrax]] had displaced the community. After [[Great Library Session Notes - Arc 4|Nymthrax was killed]], the community abandoned Guluppa-Sog and returned north.
