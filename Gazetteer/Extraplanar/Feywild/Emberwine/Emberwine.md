Emberwine is a fey realm, south of [[Amberglow]], west of the [[Sunset Gate]], and east of [[Everlight's Bloom]]. It is a realm of revelry and dance, a place where music and wine flow freely. It is a land of rolling vineyards and gentle hills, bathed in the constant golden light of the late afternoon sun.

### Open findings

- [ ] **Warning — coverage.established_fact_missing:** The article identifies Emberwine as a fey realm but never names its ruler or court. [[Sunwine Hall]] identifies the Master of Revels as Emberwine's ruler and places his court at the hall, corroborated by [[Session 120 (DuFr)]]. Add the defining relationship, for example: “Emberwine is ruled by [[Lord Soven|the Master of Revels]], whose court is [[Sunwine Hall]].” The title and existing filename link avoid choosing among the ruler's differing recorded personal-name spellings.

- [ ] **Warning — coverage.established_fact_missing:** The visible account presents music, wine, and revelry without the established qualification that makes this particular revel distinctive. The main narrative of [[Session 120 (DuFr)]] records pleasure and sweetness with undertones of overripeness and strain, and the session's “Taking Flight” account states that overindulgence in fey wine gradually erodes memory. [[Count Vashan]] separately records a fey courtier drinking to forget. A bounded addition is: “The revel at [[Sunwine Hall]] continues without end. Descriptions of it note pleasure and sweetness with an undercurrent of overripeness and strain; among its fey revelers, prolonged overindulgence in the wine can dull memory.” This states a defining property rather than the party's visit. It does not generalize the effect to Material Plane natives: [[Ampelion River]] explicitly leaves those effects unrecorded.
