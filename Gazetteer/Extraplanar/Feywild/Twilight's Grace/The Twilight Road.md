The Twilight Road is an old and indirect crossing between [[Twilight's Grace]] and western [[Tyrwingha]]. On the Material Plane, it emerges in the countryside roughly two days’ walk east of [[Eskbridge]]. The Road is used by messengers, wanderers, scouts, and travelers who wish to pass between the worlds without the ceremony or scrutiny of [[Twilight's Pool]].
## In Tyrwingha

On the mortal side, the Traveler’s Road is a narrow, twisting path through lightly wooded country. It is overgrown with thorn, elder, hazel, and long grass, and in places moss covers the remnants of ancient paving stones. The path does not appear to lead anywhere important. It bends frequently, doubles back upon itself, narrows between leaning trees, and occasionally vanishes beneath roots and undergrowth before reappearing a few yards farther on. Its existence is broadly known.

Travelers sometimes report:

- hearing footsteps behind them when no one is present
- seeing wagon ruts where no cart could have passed
- finding weathered milestones whose inscriptions have worn away
- smelling woodsmoke from houses that cannot be seen
- hearing the faint notes of an old traveling song somewhere ahead

Near the crossing, two overgrown tracks braid together beneath a stand of leaning trees. A worn stone marker stands nearby, sometimes said to be an ancient shrine to [[The Wanderer]].

The Road is not a main thoroughfare and is rarely encountered by accident. Those who do find it often assume it is merely an abandoned local track until the country begins to change around them.

## In Twilight's Grace

On the Feywild side, the Road appears clearer but no straighter. Low stone walls, silver-shadowed hedges, and lantern-lit puddles line its course beneath an unchanging dusk.

The puddles reflect stars that are not visible in the sky. Grass whispers with the sound of passing travelers, and small offerings can sometimes be found beside the road at bends and crossroads.

Signposts occasionally appear along the way. Their directions are often contradictory, though those familiar with the Feywild insist that all of them are true.

The Traveler’s Road is not closely watched. It belongs less to Ethlenn’s court than to the idea of travel itself, and those who use it are expected to know how to behave upon an old road.

## Crossing

A traveler who remains upon the Road and continues forward without looking back may pass between the Feywild and the Material Plane.

Once the Road begins to change, turning back or stepping from the path usually returns the traveler to the side from which they began. Some stories claim that those who leave the Road entirely may emerge somewhere else, or at some other time.

On reaching Tyrwingha, the change is subtle. The light becomes flatter, the air colder, and the sounds of ordinary insects and birds return. The path becomes muddy and narrow, and after some further walking eventually joins a local road or reaches a small hamlet.
