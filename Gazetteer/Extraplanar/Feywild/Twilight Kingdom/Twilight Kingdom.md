# The Twilight Kingdom

The Twilight Kingdom is a [[Feywild]] realm, occupying the land west of Tollen and the Volta, near [[Varrow Forest]]. It is an interstial space in the Feywild, ruled by no single archfey, though it is a land of evening, just after the sun has set, of pale blue twilight at the horizon and darkness above. A river wends quietly through rolling grassy hills under the first few unfamiliar stars of twilight, and a road runs more of less north-south through the hills. The sunset, over the hills to the west, casts a barely-visible glow across the horizon; deeper darkness lies to the east. 

It is said that the Twilight Kingdom contains a "dozen dozen" homes, at least some of which are grand manors. 

It is also rumored that somewhere in the Twilight Kingdom is a strange bottomless well of darkness on a promontory, and a lord who watches the well hoping a child might emerge once again.
