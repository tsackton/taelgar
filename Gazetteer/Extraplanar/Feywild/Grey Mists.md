# Grey Mists
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Grey Mists form a magical defense along the western border of [[Twilight's Edge]] in the [[Feywild]]. Their illusions draw travelers from their intended route, while the mists erode memories until intruders may forget why they came—or where they meant to go.

%%^Campaign:none%%
### DM Notes
The mists aim to turn travelers aside or make them forget why they are heading this way. Temptations offer reasons to abandon the journey: gold, a promising library, a welcoming refuge, or glimpses of familiar people. Apparent dangers encourage retreat or a change of course.

Memory loss gradually weakens the purpose holding the travelers together. A destination may cease to matter, companions may become strangers, and eventually the journey itself may seem inexplicable. Keep the atmosphere damp, muffled, and disorienting, with distractions tailored to each traveler’s desires and fears.
