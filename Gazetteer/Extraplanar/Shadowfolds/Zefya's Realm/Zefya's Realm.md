A stub to hold the general world of the Into the Chasm extraplanar domain. Tentatively assumed to be a Shadowfolds domain, although [[Forquarion]] is not really consistent with typical lords of Shadowfolds domains. Some possibilities: 

- Forquarion is not the lord of the domain, and is just a dupe of some kind; the real lord is someone darker, more mysterious, and not introduced in the game
- Zefya's Realm is a strange kind of Shadowfolds domain with an atypical creation story, perhaps associated with some great evil committed by the ancestors of the people who live there, or some other dark event, that incidentally swept up Forquarion
- Zefya's Realm is not actually a Shadowfolds domain, and is instead some kind of other extraplanar realm, perhaps a unique type of demi-plane created somehow out of the Downfall 

Other answers are also plausible here.
