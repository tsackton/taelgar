Zeyfa's Labyrinth is an icebound maze associated with [[Zeyfa]], the cruel West Wind of Zimkovan legend. [[Kalima]] describes it as a prison whose paths lead toward a beast. Her people, she says, fled an ancient catastrophe into or near this place and appease the West Wind by sending sacrifices into the labyrinth. Although she fears that no one escapes, she had previously found an earthen tunnel and dug her way out before being swept back in.

The entrance encountered in DR 1730 was a floating ice platform suspended in darkness beneath a white storm. Frozen wolf guardians, wind-shades, and chasm wisps attacked there. A violent updraft prevented descent into the void, while narrow ice bridges led to a snow-packed chamber with four branching tunnels. Kalima warned that the labyrinth was unsafe to sleep in and that the way out lay farther in, toward the beast.

On DR 1730-01-25, the [[Chasm Explorers]] killed the shaggy white beast in its black-stone chamber. The chamber changed to earth and granite, revealing an exit toward [[Melusa]] ([[Into the Chasm - Episode 03]]).
