# Sarren Kir

Sarren Kir, the Fort of the Sleepless, is the hill fort and royal residence of [[Yeshara]] in [[Cairn Dor]]. It stands on a rocky hill above the [[Wolf Queen's Stillings]] and serves as the principal stronghold of the [[Shemra Azem]].

The approach to Sarren Kir passes through dense fog and a trilith marked with wolf motifs. Beyond the trilith the fog gives way abruptly to clear skies and the guarded approaches to the fort. [[Yeshara]] rules from within the stronghold, while [[Shemra Azem]] patrols control the surrounding roads and countryside.

The fort also supports Cairn Dor's system of captive dreamers. In DR 1748, human prisoners awaiting confinement as dreamers were held there.
