The Imperial University was one of the three great universities of [[Drankor]] in the late Drankorian Empire, alongside [[Valtrius University]] and the [[University of the Blessed Waters]]. 

%%^Campaign:none%%
### DM Notes

**Vibe**:
- The premier institution of the Empire, focused on philosophy, theology, governance, and theoretical magic.
- Elite; a key propaganda tool during Apollyon’s reign.

**Ruins Description**:
- Sprawling marble courtyards, now cracked and overgrown.
- Grand lecture halls partially collapsed, with faded mosaics of the Eight Divines.
