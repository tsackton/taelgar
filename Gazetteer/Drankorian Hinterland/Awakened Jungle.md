# The Awakened Jungle
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Awakened Jungle is a coastal jungle, south of the [[Garamjala Plateau]] and west of the ruins of [[Drankor]], inhabited by a variety of talking animals and plants. Though far from all creatures of the jungle are sapient, numerous communities of plants and animals form societies of within the jungle, including [[Session 105 (DuFr)|Elvish-speaking fruit bats]] and [[Session 106 (DuFr)|coastal howler monkeys]], held together by the wisdom of the trees, informally led by the eldest tree, known as [[Canopy]]. The inhabitants of this jungle have no sense of the outside world, rarely, if ever, interacting with humanoids, going about their lives in isolation from the world, with care, compassion, and cooperation. 

%%^Date:1749-03-01%%
In the spring of DR 1749, the jungle was struck by a wave of dangers and misfortune, associated with [[Seeds of Chaos]] fragmenting from the prison Cha'mutte constructed to hold Apollyon, as it dissolved. These fragments of Limbo corrupted the Awakened Jungle, leading to chaos elemental attacks and sickness, until the [[Dunmar Fellowship]] [[Session 106 (DuFr)|healed]] [[Canopy]] and broke the hold of Limbo over the jungle. 
%%^End%%

%%^Campaign:none%%

The original creation of the Awakened Jungle, more or less, dates to when a [[Jade Piece of Rai's Hand]] landed in the jungle after the [[Awakened Soul Disaster]] in DR 1718. The largest trees awakened first, followed by some animals. While the animating magic faded with time, the awakening was permanent: the descendants of the awakened animals retained the speech and intelligence of their parents. This creates a dichotomy: while many of the older trees remember awakening (including [[Canopy]]), most animals were born awakened. 

The basic vibe of the jungle is loose social collectives, often but not exclusively organized along species lines, with trees as kind of the elder statesmen of the jungle. The only canonical faction explored in depth in play is the fruit bats, though others clearly exist. 

In DR 1749, as Apollyon began to destroy the prison Cha'mutte had trapped him in, the force of this destruction started scattering chaotic Limbo energy; the prison itself was constructed of Limbo energy by Cha'mutte. A fragment of this, something like the [[Seeds of Chaos]] associated with Cha'mutte, corrupted Canopy, one of the elder trees, and spread, through the roots that connect the trees of the jungle. When the Dunmar Fellowship destroyed this corrupting influence, they created space for the jungle to begin to heal.
