# Shrev
*(SHREV)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

One of the rivers of western [[Dunmar]], flowing from the [[Yuvanti Mountains]] to the [[Nevos Sea]]. Normally a placid river, during the summer monsoons it regularly overflows its banks, nourishing the dry savanna of the southern Nayan Floodplains. 

For much of the reign of the [[Nayan Dynasty|Nayan Samraats]], the Shrev has served as a practical southern boundary of Dunmari influence, and often as a fortified defense against strange monsters and evil magics emerging from the jungles to the south.
