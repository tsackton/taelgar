Nardith, meaning “new Ardith,” is a dwarven kingdom in the [[Yuvanti Mountains]], founded by survivors of [[Ardith]] after the [[Great War]]. Newly risen from the plains of [[Dunmar]] when the refugees arrived, the mountains proved easy to mine and rich in minerals. Nardith is now a prosperous realm, trading with [[Dunmar]] and [[Chardon]].

The Nardikhun, as the people of Nardith are called, bear the memories of the loss of their ancient homes and the flight across the open land, and many remain concerned with the fate of their lost heritage. Although some Nardikhun feel called to recover heirlooms or explore their ancestral homeland, few wish to abandon the prosperity of their new home to face the dangers of Ardith. Indeed, many Nardikhun have taken to exploring the deep hidden secrets of their new home, and as such [[Traveler Thuhr|Travelers]] are common among those born in the Yuvanti Mountains. 

The capital and largest city of Nardith is [[Tharn Todor]], a vast underground settlement home to most of the Nardikhun. Smaller settlements stretch north towards [[Darba]], along the Dwarven long road leading northwest from Tharn Todor. While there is some settlement to the southeast, few dwarves live beyond the [[Ragath Dor]], and the mountains around the [[Hara Gorge]] are considered dangerous.

According to its founding tradition, Tharn Todor was built where refugees discovered the [[Chalice of the Runepriest]] flowing with salt water in a chamber already prepared beneath the mountains. The Chalice was later carried north by an expedition seeking survivors in the ruins of Ardith, and was lost when the expedition failed to return.
