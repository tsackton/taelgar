# Am'khazar
*(ahm-KAH-zahr)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Am’khazar, roughly “rich-iron-mountains,” is one of the [[Dwarven Kingdoms|five dwarven kingdoms]] of [[Sentinel Range|Labkhan]], located north of [[Ardith]] and west of [[Khatridun]]. Its deep mines and the gemstone trade of [[Zarkandur]], the City of Gems, are central to its wealth.

## Culture and Economy

Am’khazar has long been a quiet, wealthy kingdom, drawing from the mineral wealth of the [[Sentinel Range|Labkhan]]. Before the [[Great War]], it was never as powerful as [[Khatridun]], and in the years since it has not risen to the heights of [[Nidzahar]]. But its people, the Am’khun, have remained prosperous, traditionalist, and thriving. They trade raw ore, processed metals, and polished gemstones cut by the renowned gemcutters of [[Zarkandur]], throughout the other dwarven realms, and beyond. 

%%^Date:1747-12-16%%
Despite the peace and prosperity of Am'khazar, it has experienced trials and turbulence. In DR 1747, unnatural cold spread into southern Am’khazar, forcing the abandonment of [[Tumukhar]]. The cold was caused by [[Morgana Stoneclaw|Morgana Frostclaw]], a hag wielding ice magic from beneath the frost giant stronghold of [[Vangebekkr]]. The curse ended when the [[Silver Tempests]] killed her in December 1747.
%%^End%%
