Ardith was the southernmost of the [[Dwarven Kingdoms|five dwarven kingdoms]] of [[Sentinel Range|Labkhan]], lying south of [[Urlich Pass]]. Once rich and prosperous, it grew wealthy through trade with [[Drankorian Empire|Drankor]], and later [[Dunmar]], the [[Istabor Alliance]] and others. It was overrun during the [[Great War]]; most surviving dwarves fled south across Dunmar to the [[Yuvanti Mountains]], where they founded [[Nardith]]. All that remains of Ardith are ruins, lost mines, and forgotten roads in the deep.

Ardith’s abandoned halls and mines still contain considerable wealth: forged steel, weapons, jewels, and rich seams of ore. Its former cities remain connected by long roads beneath the mountains. Yet, these ruins are extremely dangerous, overrun by the aberrations and monsters of the deeps. Large expeditions to the lost halls of Ardith frequently meet disaster.

## DM Notes

Most discussion concerns expeditions into Ardith, related to [[Roe DoTorka]] and Ausson's Crossing background:
- Successful expeditions tend to be small groups of two to five, careful about stealth, short stays underground, and varying their entrances and routes between visits. Large parties attract whatever hunts in the depths; the original account describes parties of twenty as very rarely surviving even a week underground.
- The long roads that once connected dwarven cities also allow aberrations to travel through the ruins and hunt intruders.
- Much of Ardith’s wealth remains unmined. Surviving drains, ladders, pumps, and elevators make deep ore seams accessible; these workings are an important part of what explorers seek, alongside finished weapons, jewelry, and other treasures.
- Expeditions have different motives: Nardikhun recovering family heirlooms, adventurers with ties to other dwarven communities, treasure hunters, and miners seeking ore.
- Some humans of [[Ausson's Crossing]] and the [[Aurbez Plateau]] respect dwarven ownership of finished treasures but consider extracting unmined ore legitimate, even when using dwarven machinery.
- Some Nardikhun feel called to their lost homeland, but most have found prosperity in the Yuvanti Mountains and have little desire to face Ardith’s dangers.

**Geography Still to Resolve:** The later [[2025-11-24 - Avatus and Hobgoblins|discussion of Avatus and the hobgoblins]] favors major population centers farther south, with the Aurbez mines as frontier resource colonies. The older mining account places some nearby mines closer to Ardith’s ancient heart. The precise extent of the kingdom and the position of its heartland remain unsettled; the later discussion’s possible history of sealed entrances is also provisional.
