The dwarven realms of the [[Sentinel Range]], known as the Five Kingdoms, have a long, deep history. Dwarven legends and tales say that once, all the land under the mountains was part of the great Dwarven empire, stretching well over a thousand miles from the southern edge of the Sentinels to the cold, snowy mountains of the Far North. The heart of this vast empire was in the area that, in the current day, is just south of the devastation around the ancient [[Urlich Pass]]. 

Though [[The Downfall]], when [[Hkar]] fell and great wars were fought in the depths underground below the mountains, reshaped this great empire, the Dwarves of the Sentinels survived, and thrived, and while the [[Drankorian Empire]] was being established to the south, the Five Kingdoms were growing below and among the [[Sentinel Range]]: [[Ardith]], [[Am'khazar]], [[Khatridun]], [[Nidzahar]], and [[Fahnukan]].
![[sentinel-kingdoms-labeled-draft.png|200|right]]
## Ardith
Though now destroyed, [[Ardith]] remains prominent in dwarven memory and is still reckoned as one of the five kingdoms, though its people now largely live in [[Nardith]]. It was a rich, powerful, and prosperous kingomd in the southern Sentinels, south of [[Urlich Pass]], [[Ardith]] that grew rich on trade with [[Drankorian Empire|Drankor]], and later with [[Dunmar]]. The kingdom was destroyed at the end of the [[Great War]], leaving ruins, lost mines, and forgotten roads beneath the mountains.

## Am'khazar
North of [[Ardith]] and west of [[Khatridun]] is the kingdom of [[Am'khazar]], whose name means roughly “rich-iron-mountains.” [[Am'khazar]] has always been the center of mineral wealth in the [[Sentinel Range]], with deep mines and a rich trade in gemstones from [[Zarkandur]], the City of Gems. Its prosperity rests largely on trade with other dwarven kingdoms, and its people maintain a strong attachment to traditional ways.

## Khatridun
North of [[Ardith]] and east of [[Am'khazar]] is the kingdom of [[Khatridun]]. Khatridun was once the crown jewel of the Five Kingdoms, centered on the great city of [[Azrathun]], among the oldest Dwarven cities in the world. Until the [[Great War]]came, and [[Khatridun]] was devastated, and [[Azrathun]] was destroyed. Now, a remnant of this ancient kingdom survives on the border of [[Sembara]]. Once rich and prosperous on the trade between [[Sembara]] and [[Chardon]], [[Khatridun]] now looks inward to Dwarven history and the legends of the past.  

## Nidzahar
Though once a relatively minor backwater, since the [[Great War]] [[Nidzahar]] has grown to be the largest and richest of the Five Kingdoms, known widely across the [[Western Green Sea]] and even as far as [[Chardon]] as the source of expertly crafted and technically complex goods. [[Nidzahar]] is the most technologically oriented of the Five Kingdoms and has the most extensive surface settlements, including extensive trade connections to the human inhabitants of [[Zimkova]]. 

## Fahnukan
In the far, cold north, [[Fahnukan]] is the northernmost and most isolated of the Five Kingdoms. Even many dwarves speak of Fahnukan as a strange place, and for many generations there has been little contact between Fahnukan and the other dwarven realms of the Five Kingdoms. 

## Nardith
The realm of [[Nardith]], founded by survivors of [[Ardith]], is often considered one of the Five Kingdoms, the successor realm to Ardith, though it lies in the [[Yuvanti Mountains]], south of [[Dunmar]]. It was founded by refugees and survivors of [[Ardith]] in the years after the [[Great War]], and has become very prosperous with rich mines and trade with [[Dunmar]] and [[Chardon]]. Many of the people of Nardith retain a strong concern for the heritage lost with their ancestral home in the [[Sentinel Range|Sentinels]].

Historically, the southern kingdoms ([[Ardith]] and [[Khatridun]]) were the center of power, the oldest/richest kingdoms, dating back to even before the founding of [[Drankor]]. [[Am'khazar]] to the northwest got by on trading mineral wealth, while [[Nidzahar]] was a bit of a hippy backwater (such as things exist in dwarven society), and [[Fahnukan]] was even then seen as strange.

[[Nidzahar]], in contrast, looks to the [[Humans|human]] world and innovation, building on its artisan and craft-based traditions to push technological innovation. Throughout this, the kingdom of Am’khazar (the most traditional ‘dwarfish’ kingdom) chugs along on tradition and mining, while the weird, isolated [[Dwarves]] of the north remain weird and isolated. 

Elsewhere in the world, of course, there are other dwarven kingdoms, but historically the five kingdoms of the [[Sentinel Range]] have been the center of dwarven culture and the largest population centers of [[Dwarves]].
