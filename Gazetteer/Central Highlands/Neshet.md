Neshet is the largest village of the [[Te'kula]], in the [[Elderwood]].

For many years, it was hidden from [[Mezzar|Grimbaskal]] in a demiplane created by [[Aasimti]] through a [[Jade Piece of Rai's Hand]]. It returned to the [[Material Plane]] in DR 1748, following Grimbaskal’s defeat.

The main town/village of the Te'kula looks superficially like other Deno'qai villages -- a large wooden building/meeting room, in front of a clearing, with the tanshi tree/altar in front. But unlike other villages, there are not signs of many gods here, and the tree/altar is decorated in jade and clearly dedicated to Rai/Aasimti.
 
The village, more than other places in the Elderwood, has collected refugees who want to escape the world for various reasons. Time seems not to pass here -- although it does -- the cares of the outside world are far away. Over the past 15 years, the worry about Grimbaskal has faded, and few are scared now of him. But also, some debate about whether reentering the world -- even if Grimbaskal is dead -- is desirable. There is a great deal of peace and quiet now that people fear may be lost, especially given the rumors of the troubles among the other tribes and with Chardon (although some may suspect that Grimbaskal is behind a lot of this).
