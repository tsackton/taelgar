# Ainumarya
*(EYE-new-mar-ya)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Ainumarya is the original elvish name for the great forest on the west side of the [[Sentinel Range]], derived from the Elvish for "home of the first gods." It was once a major homeland of the [[Elves]], before the [[Great War]] and the [[Conclave War]] that followed devastated its forests and left its elven settlements largely abandoned. The fate of many of its former inhabitants remains unknown.

%% little canonical information exists for Ainumarya, room for invention. %%

The surviving stretches of forest are known, from south to north, as the [[Elderwood]], the [[Crimson Forest]], and the [[Forest of Dreams]]. Within the [[Crimson Forest]] and [[Elderwood]], broad low foothills have mild, predominantly rainy winters and seasonally drier summers, though with persistent summer mist and moisture, keeping them lush beside dry valleys. Farther north and on higher slopes, winters become colder and snow lasts longer.
