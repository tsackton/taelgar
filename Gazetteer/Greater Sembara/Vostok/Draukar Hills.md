# The Draukar Hills
*(DROW-kar)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Draukar Hills, northwest of Tollen and east of Breva along the [[Volta]], are a forested, difficult-to-cross highlands of jagged cliffs, waterfalls, and steep ravines.
