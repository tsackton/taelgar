# Lake Sova
*(LAYK SOH-vah)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Lake Sova is a lake in north-central [[Vostok]], within the [[Kem]]–[[Niva]] drainage system.

%%
Geographic clarification:

- Lake Sova is the lake immediately above the letters “ST” in “VOSTOK” on the regional map. An older source identifies it as the “middle lake.”
- It is distinct from [[Lake Pekul]], the larger lake farther north near the mountains.
- The map does not label the individual upper channels, so it does not establish whether the lake lies directly on a reach locally called the Kem.
- The streams entering and leaving the lake may have other, not-yet-established local names.
