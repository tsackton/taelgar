>[!info]+ Information

The South Vostok Hills lie along the [[Green Sea]] coast east of [[Tollen]], stretching north toward the [[Draukar Hills]]. Their difficult terrain separates the forests of [[Vostok]] from the rest of [[Greater Sembara]].
