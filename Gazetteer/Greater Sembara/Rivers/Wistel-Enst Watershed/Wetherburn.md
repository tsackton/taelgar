# Wetherburn
*(WETH-er-burn)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Wetherburn is a brook flowing from the [[Aine Hills]] into the [[Wistel]], one of the more prominent small tributaries draining the hills. Its upper reaches run beneath wooded slopes, with mossy stones along the banks. Often shallow and unnavigable, it is known for deep pools rich with brown trout. Spring rain and thaw swell its waters, while winter ice often forms along its banks and across quieter pools.
