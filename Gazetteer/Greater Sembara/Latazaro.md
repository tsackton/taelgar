# Latazaro
*(LAH-tah-zah-roh)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

Latazaro is a region of swamps and wetlands on the borders of [[Tyrwingha]] and [[Western Cymea]], home to substantial [[Lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] communities. The lower [[Llanfen]] runs along its edge, and it is further fed by numerous streams descending from the rocky hills at the western end of the [[Mistfold Mountains]]. Many lizardfolk settlements of Latazaro trade with the surrounding lands of [[Western Cymea]], and commerce and travel between Latazaro and the coastal towns of this region are common. The swamp is marked by warm, humid summers and mild, rainy winters, with only occasional frost. 

Along with [[Ozabal]], Latazaro preserves a remnant of [[Urtzabala]], the vast ancient wetlands that once occupied much of the present [[Western Gulf]] and neighboring lowlands.
