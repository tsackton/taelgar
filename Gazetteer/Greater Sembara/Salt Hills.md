# The Salt Hills
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Salt Hills are a spur of the [[Sentinel Range]] separating the [[Duchy of Maseau]] from the [[Aurbez and the Andonne|Aurbez and Andonne region]]. Several salt mines in the hills are controlled by the Duke of Maseau, and deposits of low-quality iron are also found there.
