# The Oracle of Hope
*(OR-uh-kul of HOHP)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Oracle of Hope is a twisting, tree-like crystal structure atop [[Fellbain]], overlooking [[Fellburn]]. Pilgrims climb the hill and wait among its crystalline swirls, though no one can predict when it will sing: sometimes hours pass between songs, sometimes a week. Its wordless music is said to bring comfort and hope, though each listener seems to find something different in it. The summit’s open, grassy slopes and broad views make the wait feel quiet and exposed beneath the sky.
