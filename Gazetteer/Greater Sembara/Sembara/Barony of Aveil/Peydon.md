# Peydon
*(Pay-dun)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> pop. 498  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

A manorial village along the [[Auberonne]] in [[Barony of Aveil]], it is about 15 miles north of [[Rinburg]] over the fields, and 25 miles by road. 

In early DR 1720, Peydon came under the control of the hag from [[Duskmire]] called the [[Midnight Lady]] after [[Emeric Daverre]] bargained away authority over the village in return for protection from the [[Undead Attacks in Sembara]]. The [[Heroes of Cleenseau]] killed her in March, and the village began recovering from her bargains and curses.
