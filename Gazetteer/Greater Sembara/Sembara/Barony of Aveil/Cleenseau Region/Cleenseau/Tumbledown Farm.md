A tenement owned by [[Geoffrey Save]] in [[Underhill]]. It has about fifteen single-room apartments, which tend to be cold in winter and hot in summer, in four stories clustered around a small and dingy courtyard.

%%^Campaign:clee%%
There are several storage cellars in the northern and western sections which zombie-plague infested rats travelled through. These tunnels eventually connected to the ruined forts underneath Cleenseau.
