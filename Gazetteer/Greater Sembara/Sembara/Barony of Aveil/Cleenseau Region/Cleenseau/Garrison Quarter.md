# Garrison Quarter
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`  
>> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Whereabouts")`

The Garrison Quarter is [[Cleenseau]]’s wealthiest neighborhood, clustered around the [[Army Garrison of Cleenseau|army garrison]] in the east of town. Many of its larger houses were converted from former garrison buildings in the 1660s, when [[Reginald Essford]] encouraged wealthy residents to settle through generous leases and land grants.
