# Aben Garrison

The Aben Garrison is an [[Army of Mostreve]] post near the headwaters of the [[Aben]] in the [[Tyrwinghan Hills]]. It houses the Red Camp of the Color Guard and serves as a gathering place for [[Rangers]].
