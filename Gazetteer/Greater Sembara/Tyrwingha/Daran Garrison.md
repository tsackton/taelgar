# The Daran Garrison
*(DAR-an GAIR-ih-sun)*
>[!info]+ Information  
> `$=dv.view("_scripts/view/get_Affiliations")`

Daran Garrison is an [[Army of Mostreve]] post near the [[Daran]]’s headwaters in the [[Tyrwinghan Hills]]. Together with [[Maerwyn Garrison]], it guards the gap between the hills and the [[Maerwyn Mountains]]. It houses roughly a hundred soldiers drawn from Tyrwingha’s three Guard Companies.
