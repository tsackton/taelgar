#### Short
With the orcs defeated, the party finds the stolen keystone amid the black pillars of the ruined engine hall.

#### Long
Our session begins with the last orc dead; the party can finally take in the vast hall beneath Eidrikas's platform. Six black pillars—apparently neither stone nor metal—descend through the floor, surrounded by hatches lit from below with an unsteady purple-blue glow. Broken gears, rods, and stone lie scattered across the chamber, and the stolen keystone rests fifty feet away: a jagged arc of black stone banded with silver runes and dark channels. Though the open hatches suggest another level below, the keystone is the piece Eidrikas needs.

#### Short
Eolo moves to take the keystone, but as he picks it up, he is caught in its charm, which pushes him to enter the hatches and descend below. Finnan breaks the lure with his magic, and the party carries the wrapped stone back to Eidrikas.

#### Long
Eolo moves to take the keystone, but as he picks it up, he is caught in its charm, which pushes him to enter the hatches and descend below. When he tries to toss the stone to Justas and fly below, the party quickly reacts: Finnan's _Calm Emotions_ spell snaps Eolo free of the compulsion. Jrain wraps the stone in a scavenged cloak, allowing Ekko and Eolo to carry it safely back to Eidrikas. Still gritting his teeth as his body completes the broken runic circle, Eidrikas confirms that replacing the missing arc should steady the planes and close the chaotic portals. However, he agrees to hold on for another day or two, so the party can return to Melusa and attempt to rescue any villagers they can. The Chasm Explorers leave the keystone with Eidrikas, rest until stars appear above the chasm, and prepare one last return to Melusa.

#### Short
Leaving the stone with Eidrikas and the repair unfinished for the moment, the party enters Melusa for one last rescue, only to emerge into a swarm of white portal wisps.

#### Long
Leaving the wrapped keystone with Eidrikas, the party crosses the portal in quick succession. They emerge into a swarm of dozens of glowing wisps that surround Eolo and Justas, and sting Jrain and Finnan as they arrive. Everyone sprints away, batting the creatures aside until the swarm yields and gathers again around the portal. Only then can they see how much has changed: the forty-foot ice wolf lies shattered across the plaza, a spike shoots from the metal crack, ash billows from another opening and coats the wreckage, and no guards remain. Beyond the salmon pool, the village still stands, but the Ice Palace is dark and its bridge is gone.

#### Short
After escaping the swarm, the party finds Zahara sheltering with a few dissidents, and learns that a vast fist from the ash portal destroyed the palace guards and ice wolf. With Eidrikas unable to delay the repairs indefinitely and their escape route likely to close afterward, the party decides to gather anyone willing to flee at the salmon pool in half an hour.

#### Long
Rather than explore the silent palace, the party hurries toward Iskander's side of Melusa and sends Rala to check Zahara's island refuge. In a cramped house, they find Zahara and a handful of dissidents, huddled around the body of another dissident, killed while trying to run past the swarm of portal wisps alone. Zahara tells them the news: the cracks worsened after their departure, growing more chaotic, and that a fist the size of a house burst from the ash realm, smashed the guards and the ice wolf before it vanished. No one has seen Malquin since, and attempts to enter the palace have failed. Iskander is already gathering others who want to try to leave, but fear of the wisps, who have already killed one person, has trapped them. With Eidrikas unable to delay the repairs indefinitely and their escape route likely to close afterward, the party decides to gather anyone willing to flee at the salmon pool in half an hour, then lead them through the wisps in one protected rush.

#### Short
Finnan carries the warning through Melusa, rallying as many as he can, but most of the village listen instead to an older woman's counsel and remain behind. Only a few dozen villagers gather to leave.

#### Long
With the departure set for half an hour, Finnan turns his fiddle into a town crier's drumbeat and calls the villagers from their houses, promising safe passage through the portal and an escape from Melusa. Ekko changes into a polar bear and fishes from the salmon pool, giving the willing an unmistakable landmark. Nearly fifty people gather to hear Finnan, but a stooped older woman accuses the strangers of bringing the curse, and insists that a capricious god is better than an absent one. Most look to her and the dark palace instead of moving toward the portal, though Iskander and Kalima arrive with thirty-five willing refugees. The party considers confronting Malquin, but they do not know whether that might provoke disaster for those who stay, or even whether life outside will indeed be better. Rather than take a risk on a dangerous visit to the palace, they decide to lead out those few who have chosen to go.

#### Short
Once the evacuation begins, Justas, Ekko, and Finnan's summoned fey spirit hold off the wisps, while Jrain sends the willing refugees through the portal to Eolo. After the last villager passes through with Justas and Finnan, Eidrikas restores the keystone.

#### Long
With thirty-five people waiting, Eolo crosses first to receive them on the narrow platform while Justas ties the party's rope around his waist, raises his shield, and walks into the swarm protected by his _Spirit Guardians_. Finnan sends a tricksy fey spirit blinking through the wisps in bursts of darkness, and Ekko's polar bear draws off any that drift back toward the portal. The first frightened group bunches at the opening, so Jrain waits for each wave to clear before sending the next. On the far side, Eolo catches the refugees and seats them in a tight row above the chasm. The wisps batter Ekko, break apart against Justas's radiant guardians, and finally overwhelm Finnan's fey spirit, but not before the last group crosses safely. Justas stays behind until only a few lights remain; his final call draws one more young villager from the crowd, and Finnan waits for them both before following. Once everyone is packed onto the platform, Eidrikas sets the keystone into the broken arc. The runes blaze, the platform steadies, and the portal begins to peel from the wall.

#### Short
With the portal closing, the refugees crowd Eidrikas's platform and stare into the real Great Chasm, as the  party turns to getting the refugees safely to the surface. Eidrikas and the kenku of Kemeko Monastery organize lifts to the surface, while Finnan seeks a village where Old Zimkovan is still spoken and the newcomers can make a home. We end as the rescued Melusans eventually settle, bringing the expedition to a close.

#### Long
As the portal slowly peels away from the cliff wall, the refugees crowd the floating platform and look out into the Great Chasm, whispered questions passing among them. Eidrikas flies over to join them and points out more room on the great machinery below, now safe enough for a camp, though this does not solve the real problem: thirty-odd people will soon be hungry and tired, and the surface is a mile or more overhead.

The Chasm Explorers and Eidrikas they work out a rescue. The Kemeko Monastery can send kenku to help, and Eidrikas can carry four people at a time transformed into a giant swan. Flying everyone out will be safer than searching for underground passages possibly used by the and Stoneborn to originally reach this place. Finnan goes up with an early group to find somewhere the refugees can settle, while Justas stays to offer reassurance and help with the later lifts. They hope to find a northern village with room for newcomers, and elders who still speak Old Zimkovan; Jrain shares any memories and leads that might help.

Over the next few days, the rescue takes shape. He also shares what he knows of the Great Chasm with the Chasm Explorers: three ancient mechanisms of inkaya stabilize the planar vortices beneath the chasm, their builders unknown even to the Vejo Vaikai, who guard the secret of their existence. When this one was damaged, planar instability was the result. With the machinery restored, the party brings its expedition to a close. The rescued Melusans eventually find a place to settle, and the Chasm Explorers are free to turn toward whatever comes next.
