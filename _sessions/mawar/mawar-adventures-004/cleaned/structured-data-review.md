## Combat

### Steam creatures and armored fire warrior at the misty pool

Nerissa’s Faerie Fire outlines two of four steam creatures; Durandal warns Ander before the regular cycle.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The party kills one steam creature. Ander’s readied strike interrupts another’s approach before its breath; the fire warrior arrives, fails to capsize the canoe and attacks, prompting Sentinel. |
| 2 | Two more steam creatures die, with exploding-steam consequences. The warrior punctures and sinks the canoe despite Ander’s intervening Sentinel hit. |
| 3 | Ryu’s surrender demand fails. Water hinders Ander; the warrior trades attacks, provokes opportunity attacks and Sentinel, and retreats shouting toward the tunnel. |
| 4 | Nerissa’s fire is ineffective. Ryu’s attack breakdown is unclear; Ander reaches shallower water and later lands a Sentinel critical hit. |
| 5 | The warrior refuses again and invokes his unnamed master. Ander kills him after Ryu’s spear hit; armor shrapnel deals Ryu 26 and Nerissa 13 piercing damage. |

The warrior is dead and the canoe lost. Three steam-creature deaths are explicit; the fourth’s individual fate and Ander’s final explosion damage are unspoken.

### Fire mage and captive dragon turtle beneath the vegetation dome

The mage’s opening three blasts miss Ryu through cover; the source later confirms this volley preceded the regular cycle.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Nerissa’s Dissonant Whispers is partly resisted. The turtle breaks the roof, exposing Ryu; the mage hits him once. Ryu and Blu loosen the arrow without removing it. |
| 2 | Nerissa removes and drops the arrow. Fireball injures the riders; Ryu’s Absorb Elements corrects his damage from 11 to 5. His animal speech fails to reach the turtle. |
| 3 | Nerissa speaks Draconic and wins a promise of protection. Ander crashes down, attacks repeatedly and breaks concentration. The turtle helps, but the mage incapacitates it and Nerissa; Shield blocks Ryu’s spear. |
| 4 | The incapacitated pair cannot act. Ander misses against Shield; another Fireball hurts Ander and Ryu, with Absorb Elements again reducing Ryu’s damage. Blu wakes the turtle. |

Chaessenth is free and the party survives. Blu’s grasp-damage bookkeeping conflicts (5 versus later 9), and Ander’s critical/combined attack totals remain qualified in the detailed combat record.
