#### Short
The party travels to the black peak containing Ianto's fortress and prepares its attack.

#### Intermediate
The party follows the shifting landscape toward Ianto's hollow mountain fortress, using Snikket's fading memories to understand its rough layout. They camp below the peak before approaching the guards.

#### Long
The party travels through the eerily shifting landscape toward the black stone peak that houses [[Ianto]], his redcaps, and the goblins forced to serve them. After resting in the eternal late afternoon, the group uses [[Snikket|Snikket's]] fading memories to establish a rough map and learn that the goblins fear one tunnel in particular. Near the fortress, they follow an old blood trail into the shallow valley below the hollow peak, leave Snikket concealed in a thicket, and prepare to draw out the guards.

### recap-002 | Assault on the Fortress

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-10-28
- Time: day
- Locations: Fortress of Ianto the Red
- NPCs: Ianto
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: Ianto, redcaps

#### Short
The party defeats the outer guards, strikes the fortress from above, and kills Ianto and the remaining redcaps.

#### Intermediate
Viepuck's disguise draws the outer guards into a series of ambushes before the party climbs above the fortress. Aerial spell attacks scatter the defenders, after which the group divides to clear the butchery and throne room and Robin kills Ianto.

#### Long
Viepuck disguises himself as Snikket and draws the first guards into an ambush. After eliminating two successive patrols, the party climbs the outer rock and looks down into the fortress's central opening. Izgil and Viepuck descend under feather fall, bombard the assembled redcaps, and escape before the defenders can respond. The party then divides: Robin and Izgil clear the redcaps feeding in the butchery while the others descend into the courtyard and attack the throne room. [[Ianto]] charges into the fight and briefly knocks Robin unconscious, but the party restores him and kills the redcap lord along with his remaining defenders.

### recap-003 | The Trophy Vault

- Kind: aftermath
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Date: 1720-10-28
- Time: day
- Locations: Fortress of Ianto the Red
- NPCs: Krat, Garn, Droog, Grib, Snikket
- Organizations: none
- Items: Greymalkin's wings, silver unicorn horn, phoenix-like tail feather, intertwined stag-and-wood-creature remains, wrought-black elk horns, rest of Ianto's trophy collection
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party frees the surviving goblins and recovers Greymalkin's wings and Ianto's other trophies.

#### Intermediate
With Ianto dead, the party searches the fortress, finds the surviving goblins, and offers them safe passage. The vault contains Greymalkin's wings and a collection of distinctive trophies taken from defeated fey.

#### Long
The party searches the captured fortress and finds a group of surviving goblins: [[Krat]], [[Garn]], [[Droog]], and [[Grib]]. They call for Snikket's brother, explain that Ianto is dead, and offer the uncertain survivors a chance to leave with them. In the trophy vault they recover Greymalkin's wings, a silver unicorn horn, a brilliant tail feather, intertwined remains from a stag and a wood-creature, wrought-black elk horns, and the rest of Ianto's collection of body parts, armor, and trophies.
