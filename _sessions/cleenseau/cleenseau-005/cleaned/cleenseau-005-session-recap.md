- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-002

#### Short
The party provokes fighting within the warband, frees the prisoners, and defeats Wakog.

The party and its supporting skirmishers defeat [[Wakog]] and the remaining hostile orcs, allowing the surviving prisoners to escape.
