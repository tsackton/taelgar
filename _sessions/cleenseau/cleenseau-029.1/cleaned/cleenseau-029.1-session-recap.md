#### Short
The Merriweathers visit Asineau, where Celyn seeks clothing advice for his role as Robin's consort.

#### Intermediate
Quent brings the Merriweather caravan to Asineau with cloth, tailored garments, and other goods. Celyn speaks to the family away from the wider village about a wardrobe that can represent Robin's consort while still feeling like his own clothing.

#### Long
The Merriweathers arrive in Asineau with three wagons of cloth and tailored clothes, along with carved wood, Addermarch apple brandy, and Rinburg pottery. Quent leads the caravan, Tobin does much of the trading, and Tamsin moves among the visitors and villagers. Their stop is notable because the family does not usually trade in Asineau.

Celyn takes the opportunity to talk with them about clothing, away from the ears of the village at large, and encourages Robin to join him. He asks what they know of his earlier discussion with the Merriweathers in Veltor. His ordinary wardrobe deliberately presents him as a respectable but unremarkable farm boy. As one of the Heroes of Cleenseau and Robin's consort, he now needs clothes suited to public occasions without losing his own identity or pretending to be someone else. He treats that public role as a matter of morale as well as presentation.

He wants to understand the class signals of the clothes they design, and asks about cut and fit around his shoulders and hips, contrasting colors, a good shirt for travel, better binding options, and garments that leave him free to move. He also considers how Robin might use clothing to signal when he is acting as lord. The correspondence records Celyn's requests; it gives no Merriweather answer or finished wardrobe.
