#### Short
With Ida ordered away, the party gathers regional aid against Wakog before Guy arrives from the warband's camp.

Izgil and [[Brot Starsearcher]] leave for Dunfry on December 2. On December 4, [[Guy de Varan]] arrives after escaping Wakog's camp and reports torture and pillage there.
