# Ganboa Negotiations

Play-by-email exchange about Asineau's relationship with [[Ganboa]]. Authored turns appear in sent order; repeated quoted replies and mail footers are omitted. Complete email archive: [[Ganboa Correspondence (Email)]].

### Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com> — Tue, 28 Oct 2025 21:48:56 -0400

Robin approaches the lizardfolk of Ganboa with diplomacy and respect,
asking about sand-gathering from the Enst, fishing rights and shared
fishing responsibilities, and greater involvement in the region, from both
an economic, cultural, and festival perspective.

The community at Ganboa seems divided, with two different spokepeople: Erdu
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/lizardfolk/erdu/>, a
some cranky and human-skeptical elder, and Unai
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/lizardfolk/unai/>, an
herbalist.

Erdu feels the lizardfolk have first rights to the entire Enst, by ancient
right, and is somewhat skeptical that Sembara really deserves much. He says
that the monarch didn't do the first job of a monarch and keep the
hobgoblins away (this region was overrun during the hobgoblin wars 70 years
ago), and thus, lost his respect. He appreciates Robin's talk of defenses,
but mutters grumpily about human gods and their festivals and doesn't seem
interested in participating in anything other than some mutual defensive
aid planning. When invited to the festival, he shrugs dismissively, but
does invite Robin to speak to the others in the village. There are several
in the village like Erdu, including a few reclusive types who don't even
come to talk to Robin.

Unai is much friendlier. Her student, Gentza
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/lizardfolk/gentza/>,
was the one who's murder the party solved, and she has a deep gratitude for
this. She is gentle and tactful, and although she is clearly somewhat
reserved, she is interested in coming to some of these regional festivals.
She introduces Robin and Celyn to a few of the younger generation,
including Izoko
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/lizardfolk/izoko/>,
Gentza's boyfriend, who are in general happy to help collect sand, at least
in an informal and occasional way.

At the end, you end up with a half dozen lizardfolk, led by Unai,
interested in coming to some of the regional festivals, but certainly no
interest in _hosting_ a festival or having a lot of outsiders tramping
through the village. You also have a clear understanding with Erdu about
the benefits of mutual defense arrangements, and an open invitation to talk
further on this topic. Erdu seems to think Robin's worries about keeping
the place safe are well-placed and much appreciated, although he doesn't
want to leave the village or participate in the wider economy. Finally, a
bunch of the younger lizardfolk, lead by Izoko, become your sand
harvesters, in general willing to haul sand in exchange for either fine
food (the local snails are particularly appreciated, and easier to buy from
Beury than procure in the immediate Asineau/Ganboa waters), good rope and
nets, or gold. They seem just as interested in bartering though as money.
You can certainly support their wants with the output of the glassworks, or
you could dedicate some percentage of your garden to harvesting food
luxuries and use that to fund them. Or other ideas are possible as well.

None of the lizardfolk seem remotely interested in any more formal or wider
economic integration though - the idea of harvesting fish for sale, for
exagettmple, or otherwise getting more entangled in the market economy is
not that interesting to them. You get the sense they highly value their
self-sufficiency, and also their leisure time, and they are not that
interested in working harder to earn gold. Of course if there was a famine
or something they'd help, but you aren't going to convince them to pool
labor in any meaningful way.

### Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com> — Wed, 29 Oct 2025 12:58:56 +0000

We will welcome all involvement from the folk who expressed interest, and Robin will express gratitude towards those who are willing to trade and also to Erdu for being open to coordinating on defense.

A few points he will present to Edru (and others):

First - He apologizes for whatever misfortune has occurred in decades past, but will try and emphasize that Erdu isn't negotiating with 'Sembara' right now, he's negotiating with Robin, and Robin is committed to trying to achieve good relations now. He hopes he and his companions' actions during the last year count for more than words alone might. If there have been past failings, he'd like to try and correct them.

Second - Robin understands that Sembara and the lizard folk have different views on rights to land and the river and such, he knows these differences can be difficult to reconcile, but he wants to work to try and replace tension with cooperation to the greatest extent possible. He knows this isn't simple, it will take time and wont be solved with one conversation, and he's committed to doing the work if Erdu and others will work with him. He will also be rather open on his opinion that the last lord was an ass and he's determined to make things better.

Third - While he is interested in trade (and is very grateful for the sand arrangements), he will explain that he's as much concerned with general cooperation and friendliness as he is with economics. It's his belief that if there is another crisis or attack of some kind, be it an invasion or just a mundane drought or something, it will be far easier to help each other survive if people already know each other and have been working together to some degree. People with working relationships will be better able to assist each other when bad times hit, and will be better at standing together against any foe that pose a threat. So, in Robin's mind, these things are also about defense.

Fourth - He understands Erdu isn't interested in 'human gods', but Robin will emphasize that these festivals are not really religious festivals. They coincide with religious celebrations because that's a time people are already gathering together and so there was a desire among the lords to combine these things, but the festivals frankly are more about people getting to know folks from the other communities and encouraging friendly cooperation, which again is about defense.

Also, frankly, everything has kind of sucked for the past year, and he'd like people to have some fun. He'd feel good about the folks in Ganboa joining in that, he wants to raise folks spirits a bit. He will even be a little personal and briefly explain a little of his own relationship with the wonderer and that he doesn't spend a lot of time preaching and such or even really in formal prayer, mostly he does what he can to make the world somewhere he wants to live and trusts the Powers That Be know what's in his heart.

Fifth, specifically on defense, Robin is proposing the following:

The people of Ganboa in case of attack have right of sanctuary within Asinau's walls. Period. There's no request for exchange here, Robin is just offering this 'free of charge' as it were. You're our neighbors, we have your back, full stop.

Mutual aid - (we don't need to get into the nitty gritty of this), but basically an agreement to assist each other in case of disaster. Flood, fire, drought, etc. He understands the desire to be self-sufficient and he's working on numerous projects in Asineau to make them more self-sufficient as well, but it's easier and safer to be self-sufficient when you know someone's going to help you back up if something bad happens you can't handle alone. We all do what we can to be the best we can, but it's easier to survive together.

Defense pact - An attack on Asineau or Ganboa is an attack on both. Part of what he's hoping from the festivals is some informal training (via the tournaments) involving people from the different towns fighting in mixed groups. If there's a chaotic attack from hobgoblins or whoever, and you're caught away from the people you know for whatever reason, he wants folk to feel comfortable being able to just fall in with whoever is around and that's a lot easier if you already know people and have spared/trained/worked with them a little. That will make it much more effective if, in the case of attack, either Ganboa or Asineau needs to come to the other's defense, which he's hoping Erdu will be open to.

All in short, we're neighbors and we should have each other's backs.

While a lot of this is kind of directed at Erdu, Robin is happy to negotiate with anyone and as stated is quite happy to be working with Unai and the younger lizard folk.
