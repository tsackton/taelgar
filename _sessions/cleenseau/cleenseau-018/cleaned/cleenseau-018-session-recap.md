#### Short
The party survives an ambush outside Veltor, takes prisoners, and learns that the attackers were apparently hired by Damien.

#### Long
Forewarned by Robin, the party survives an ambush by two assassins and six bandits outside [[Veltor]]. [[Marceline Dupont]] and a troop of baronial horse arrive after the fighting and take the survivors into custody. While reading the captured assassin's thoughts, Viepuck sees [[Damien Montrichard]] as the apparent employer. After reaching Veltor, the party speaks with Damien and establishes that he did not hire the attackers.

### 1720-02-20

- Timeline Segment: timeline-002
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-02-20)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-002, recap-003
- Locations: Veltor, Veltor Keep, Temple of the Warlord in Veltor
- NPCs: Marceline Dupont, Damien Montrichard, Areschera, Sabine de Brune, Istarias, the duskhound, Victorine Rosseau, Julien Marteau, Primrose Merriweather, Corrin Merriweather, Pierre
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Combat Beats: none

#### Short
The party discovers that Damien and Sabine are being held inside a government increasingly controlled by hostile fey.

#### Long
The next morning, [[Marceline Dupont]] reports that the surviving assassin supposedly escaped and died using lockpicks that Celyn knows he did not possess. [[Areschera]], disguised as the Baroness's chief clerk, announces that [[Damien Montrichard]] has confessed and refuses the party access to him. The party visits [[Sabine de Brune]], who is confined under guard, and Celyn recognizes her apparent servant as [[Istarias]]. At the [[Temple of the Warlord in Veltor|Temple of the Warlord]], they confer with [[Julien Marteau]], exchange messages with [[Victorine Rosseau]], heal soldiers, and arrange with [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] to smuggle Sabine out of Veltor. A performance at [[Veltor Keep|the keep]] confirms Areschera's hostility, and Viepuck contacts Damien and learns that a duskhound is guarding him in a hidden part of the keep.

Celyn escorts [[Sabine de Brune]] and an invisible [[Istarias]] out of [[Veltor Keep]] while Viepuck disguises himself as Sabine to conceal the escape. [[Pierre the Guard|Pierre]] distracts the gate guard at Robin and Izgil's request, and [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] shelter Sabine for the journey out of town. Istarias tells Celyn that his small Feywild kingdom is considering an alliance with the Tyrwinghan queen. With Sabine safe, the party returns to the keep to rescue [[Damien Montrichard]].

#### Short
The party defeats an ambush and discovers that Damien appears to have hired the attackers.

#### Intermediate
The party defeats assassins and bandits outside Veltor, takes prisoners, and finds evidence falsely implicating Damien.

#### Long
Robin's weapon warns him of an ambush along the wooded road outside [[Veltor]]. The party defeats two assassins and six bandits, accepting several surrenders and capturing one assassin. [[Marceline Dupont]] and her baronial horse arrive after the fighting and take custody of the prisoners. Viepuck reads the surviving assassin's thoughts and sees [[Damien Montrichard]] as the man who apparently hired him. In Veltor, the party questions Damien and determines that he was not responsible.

- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-02-20

#### Short
The party finds Damien accused and Sabine confined while Areschera controls access to both.

#### Intermediate
Evidence surrounding the captured assassin is manipulated, Damien is secretly imprisoned, and Sabine and Istarias ask the party to help them escape.

#### Long
The next morning, [[Marceline Dupont]] reports that the captured assassin supposedly escaped and died using lockpicks that Celyn is certain he did not possess. [[Areschera]], disguised as the Baroness's chief clerk, claims that [[Damien Montrichard]] confessed to arranging the ambush and blocks every attempt to see him. The party learns that [[Sabine de Brune]] is confined in the keep and visits her. Celyn recognizes her servant as [[Istarias]], the fey who previously warned him about poisoned food. Istarias explains that a duskhound checks on the prisoners and asks for help escaping.

### recap-003 | Allies in Veltor

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Date: 1720-02-20
- Time: day
- Locations: Veltor, Temple of the Warlord in Veltor, Veltor Keep
- NPCs: Marceline Dupont, Areschera, Victorine Rosseau, Julien Marteau, Primrose Merriweather, Corrin Merriweather, Damien Montrichard
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party recruits local allies, tests Areschera's reactions, and locates Damien inside the keep.

#### Intermediate
The party builds support among Veltor's soldiers, temple, and halflings, then confirms that Areschera is concealing Damien inside the keep.

#### Long
At the [[Temple of the Warlord in Veltor|Temple of the Warlord]], the party consults [[Julien Marteau]] and receives a letter from [[Victorine Rosseau]] concerning the Cranford murders. Robin and Celyn heal injured soldiers while Viepuck gains [[Marceline Dupont|Marceline's]] cautious support. [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] agree to hide and smuggle [[Sabine de Brune|Sabine]] if the party can free her. During a performance at [[Veltor Keep]], the party watches [[Areschera]] react to accounts of its fey adversaries. Viepuck later contacts [[Damien Montrichard|Damien]] and learns that he is hidden upstairs under the guard of a duskhound.

### recap-004 | Sabine's Escape

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-004
- Date: 1720-02-21
- Time: day
- Locations: Veltor, Veltor Keep
- NPCs: Sabine de Brune, Istarias, Primrose Merriweather, Corrin Merriweather, Pierre, Damien Montrichard
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party smuggles Sabine and Istarias out of the keep and returns for Damien.

#### Intermediate
With Pierre and the Merriweathers' help, the party frees Sabine and Istarias before returning to the keep to rescue Damien.

#### Long
Celyn talks his way into [[Sabine de Brune|Sabine's]] rooms, disguises her as a peasant, and leads her and an invisible [[Istarias]] out of [[Veltor Keep]]. Viepuck takes Sabine's appearance, receives the guard's soup, makes the bed appear occupied, and slips away. Robin and Izgil recruit [[Pierre the Guard|Pierre]], whose admiration for dwarves makes him eager to distract the gate guard. [[Primrose Merriweather]] and [[Corrin Merriweather]] shelter Sabine and prepare to smuggle her from Veltor. Istarias privately tells Celyn that his small Feywild kingdom may ally itself with the Tyrwinghan queen. The party then returns to the keep to rescue [[Damien Montrichard]].
