- Locations: Peydon, Daverre Manor, Auberonne

#### Short
The party restores local authority in Peydon, arrests the ferryman, and pursues the escaped remnants of the hag's gang.

#### Long
After returning to [[Daverre Manor]], the party tells [[Emeric Daverre]], [[Estelle Daverre]], and [[Odile Daverre]] that the Midnight Lady is dead. They help [[Roland Vaissier]] resume his duties and arrest [[Edouard of Peydon]] at the [[Auberonne]]. An unnamed man offers to inform against Edouard but later helps the remaining gang members escape. The party pursues the fugitives, kills several who resist, and returns [[Pierrot the Weasel]] and [[Mireille the Herbalist]] to Roland's custody. News from elsewhere warns that the duke has been condemned for treason and is withdrawing toward Fellburn with [[Rosalind Essford]]. Izgil discovers that his lunar bracers have merged with his skin, while the recovered Mirror of Whispers has lost its magic.

## Recap

### recap-001 | Peydon Celebrates

- Kind: aftermath
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-03-04
- Time: day
- Locations: Daverre Manor, Peydon
- NPCs: Emeric Daverre, Estelle Daverre, Odile Daverre, Roland Vaissier
- Organizations: none
- Items: Mirror of Whispers
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party reports the Midnight Lady's death and helps restore local authority in Peydon.

#### Intermediate
The Daverre household and the people of Peydon celebrate their freedom while Roland resumes his duties as captain of the guard.

#### Long
The party returns to Daverre Manor and explains the Midnight Lady's death to [[Emeric Daverre]], [[Estelle Daverre]], [[Odile Daverre]], and the household staff. Emeric destroys the glass through which the hag had watched the manor. As word spreads, Peydon erupts into celebration. The party rouses [[Roland Vaissier]] and helps him resume command of the local guard. Izgil recovers the Mirror of Whispers, now a nonmagical but valuable mirror.

### recap-002 | The Ferryman and the Informant

- Kind: arrest
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-03-04
- Time: day
- Locations: Peydon, Auberonne, Daverre Manor
- NPCs: Roland Vaissier, Edouard of Peydon, the unnamed informant
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party arrests Edouard and detains a would-be informant attempting to reach the docks.

#### Intermediate
An unnamed man offers evidence against Edouard while the party forces the fleeing ferryman back to shore and takes both men to the manor.

#### Long
As the party accompanies [[Roland Vaissier]] to arrest [[Edouard of Peydon]], Celyn intercepts an unnamed man sneaking toward the docks. The man claims that he is willing to give evidence against Edouard. When the ferryman pushes away from shore and ignores orders to return, Celyn teleports aboard and Robin walks across the [[Auberonne]] to drag the boat back by its mooring rope. The party interrogates Edouard and takes him and the purported informant back toward the manor, where Celyn notices Edouard trying to pass the other man a message.

### recap-003 | The Gang's Escape

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Date: 1720-03-04
- Time: night
- Locations: Peydon
- NPCs: Roland Vaissier, Edouard of Peydon, the unnamed informant, Brigitte Charpentier, Tibault the Butcher, Noemie Vallier, Pierrot the Weasel, Mireille the Herbalist
- Organizations: none
- Items: Izgil's lunar bracers
- Enemies: Edouard of Peydon, Brigitte Charpentier, Tibault the Butcher, Noemie Vallier, Pierrot the Weasel

#### Short
The party pursues the escaped gang, defeats those who resist, and returns the survivors to Roland.

#### Intermediate
After the informant helps Edouard and other gang members escape, the party overtakes them outside Peydon and recovers the surviving fugitives.

#### Long
The unnamed informant bribes someone to release Edouard and several other gang members from their temporary jail. Guided by Es*tiaslos, the party pursues the fugitives on borrowed horses. [[Brigitte Charpentier]], [[Tibault the Butcher]], and [[Noemie Vallier]] are killed after resisting. [[Pierrot the Weasel]] surrenders and provides information, including the identity of [[Mireille the Herbalist]], who is then arrested. The party returns the survivors to [[Roland Vaissier]]. The false informant is branded and expelled from Peydon. After completing his research, Izgil finds that his lunar bracers have merged with his skin.
