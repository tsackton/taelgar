Subject: Taelgar character

There are four good cities I could set Viepuck—

Chardon: very far away, magical, large and important. Known as the center of a growing empire, and the home to the largest and most important by far magical university.

Picking Chardon would set Viepuck to be very far from home and with a foreigner vibe.

Tollen: a few weeks journey by boat and horse. On the borders of Sembara, the kingdom the campaign starts in. Known as a place with many different odd religions and an independent streak.

Picking Tollen is the city with the most people and oddness nearish to the campaign stomping grounds.

Embry: the capital of Sembara. An important city but mostly because it is the capital of Sembara.

Embry is a good choice if you definitely want to end up back in your hometown during the campaign, but want to keep the complexity of your current story. There would be some good plot hooks for you with this choice.

One of the small cities near Cleanseau: Dunfry, the garrison town along the Western Wall; Valarian the capital of Maseau to the south, filled with refugees; or Rinford, an important trade town along the same river as Cleanseau.

Any of these choices would necessitate simplifying his background a bit but the essential points would still work.

If you could pick one I’d be able to help you with starting languages and set up your background in the world.

What do you think of the following custom warlock patron, flavored on the
Great Old One

*The Alien Mind*
Your patron is an alien mind from the Far Realms, who seems fascinated to
learn and observe the human world. You sometimes get the sense of it
watching the world through your eyes, although describing what it feels
like is impossible. Sometimes it seems like an endless void just beyond
your perception, and your toes tingle like when you climb too high, as if
you mind fall into the void. Other times it seems like a fractal pattern,
and you worry if you look at it too closely you will get lost in the
intricacies as it unfolds. Other times, it seems like it isn't there at
all.

The bonus spells are the same as the Great Old One bonus spells, and I
haven't figured out the 6th/10th/14th level powers. But the first level
powers are:

*Project Disguise*
You can summon the psionic energy of the alien mind to project a different
appearance to all sentient creatures (Int 6 or higher) within three hundred
feet of you. You may choose to exclude some creatures from this projection,
or include only some specific ones. This psychic projection lasts as long
as you wish, and requires only that you be conscious. Effects that prevent
telepathy or mind reading block this projection. Any creature that uses an
action to investigate you closely must make a Charisma save against your
spell save DC, and if successful, the project vanishes (for that creature
only). Although the projection is not real, creatures will manifest their
own beliefs as to how to interact with you, and so, for example, would not
try to push their hand through the projected part of you, as their brain
would not believe their hand could go through your body.

However, the more people you are projecting to, the harder it is to project
an exact disguise and the more you must depend on each creature's own
thoughts generating the disguise for you. Therefore, if you projecting to
more than 6 people, it is likely that if they recount what they see it will
be subtly different. This can be useful of course -- makes it harder for a
good description of your disguise to get out -- but it also means you are
more likely to give people a reason to investigate you closely if they
realize that several of them are seeing different things.

*Probe Mind*
You can channel your patron's alien presence into someone's mind. This
opens them up to your patron's investigation. You may choose, when using
this ability, one of the following two effects:

* Examine Memories. The target's memories are laid open for examination, by
your patron and the target. This grants the target advantage on their next
Intelligence check.
* Examine Emotions. The target's emotions are laid open for examination, by
you and your patrion. This grants you advantage on the next Charisma check
you make to influence the target.

You may use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus.
You regain all uses after a long rest.

=====================

I'd keep the familiar pretty similar to what you wrote. I think the
mechanic of having to cast a cantrip although I can see how it makes sense
will not be that fun to play in game. I tried to replace the flavor with
the Probe Mind ability above.

I was considering adding something to Probe Mind like... if you probe
someone's mind to examine memories twice on the same day, they must make a
DC 10 Charisma saving throw or become lost in a trance, enthralled by the
examination of their own thoughts. Each time after the second, the target
must make a new saving throw, the DC increasing by 2 each time.

A target in a trance is incapiciated and does not eat, drink, etc. They
will eventually die of thirst. They may make a new saving throw every 24
hours to escape the trance. Additionally, damage equal to half their hit
points automatically ends the trance.
