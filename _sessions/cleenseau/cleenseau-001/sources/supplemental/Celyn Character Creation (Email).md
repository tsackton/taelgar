# Celyn Character Creation (Email)

Selected adopted character background and setting decisions from Mike Sackton and Kiya Nicoll's January 2–9, 2023 correspondence. The initial Tyrwingha primer and Kiya's cultural proposals were part of character development; the later GM replies distinguish adopted details from suggestions.

Kiya established that Celyn came from a vineyard-working family in a small Tyrwinghan village, had a difficult adolescence, followed local fey customs, and took the name Celyn after a transformative experience associated with the Wyrdling. She described his trusting intuition and accepting help from both the fey and his god. These details are developed in [[Celyn]] and the later stories about him.

Mike supplied Celyn's approach to the campaign: in the summer of 1719 he reached [[Rinburg]] after following a series of intuitions and apparent fey signs. After a few months there, talk of Cleenseau's bridge drew him upriver, and he arrived in Cleenseau on a rainy night in late October. Kiya accepted this. She also established that he enjoys dice games, win or lose, without staking more than he can afford.

The correspondence distinguishes the Eight Divines from the fey in Tyrwinghan practice. Mike accepted the general idea of local customs and taboos concerning fey places and offerings, while rejecting a proposed blending of the gods themselves with fey identities. He said storms belong to the Wildling, not the Wyrdling, and that the Wyrdling is a patron of people who feel out of season, including transgender people. He described a possible Mother-temple custom involving antlers gifted by an elk-fey as an illustration, not a documented Clawyn rite. Relative status among the gods and the Wyrdling's special place in Tyrwingha were left unsettled.

Early suggestions about Celyn's class, age at the campaign start, and specific fey customs are character-design discussion. Later character and campaign records govern the final details.
