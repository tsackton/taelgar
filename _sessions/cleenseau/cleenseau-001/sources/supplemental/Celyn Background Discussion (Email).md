# Celyn Background Discussion (Email)

Selected character and setting clarification from Kiya Nicoll and Mike Sackton's September 23, 2023 correspondence. Kiya's complete account of Celyn's miracle is preserved in [[Celyn's Miracle Story]].

Mike accepted the account but suggested that Celyn's village mentor be called a **temple steward** rather than a boundary-keeper, and that tending relations with fey should not be described as worshipping them at shrines. The work could include caring for a grove, leaving customary offerings, cleaning stones, and performing songs or dances at particular places and times. Mike described local attitudes as a mixture of appeasement, caretaking, and duty toward Ethlenn's subjects. These were editorial suggestions to the story, not a replacement text from Kiya.

Mike explained that temple stewards maintain buildings and arrange community events, whereas priests have personal connections to the gods and do not form an organized hierarchy. He described the Tyrwinghan tradition that a subject may renounce human allegiance and seek a place in Ethlenn's court. The tradition does not establish that any Clawyn resident did so.

Kiya suggested that Clawyn's principal temple might be dedicated to the Wildling; Mike said that suggestion could work. The established [[Clawyn]] note identifies its temple as dedicated to **the Mother**, which Mike has now confirmed as canonical. The Wildling suggestion was not adopted. Kiya also said she would revise her miracle story for another player; no revised version is contained in this archive.
