## Recap

### recap-001 | The Flickering Camp

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: beat-001
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Plaguelands
- NPCs: Arianwen, the doppelganger
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none

#### Short
The party finds Arianwen and a duplicate of Taliesin defending a flickering camp, destroys the shadow-weasels attacking it, and learns that Bryn, Eira, and Maelona remain trapped in an unstable overlap with Limbo.

#### Intermediate
On a wooded ridge in the Plaguelands on the way to Orenlas, the party sees a camp flickering in and out of existence and rushes to help its defenders. After destroying the shadow-weasels attacking the rope, cairn, and cookpot that hold the camp stable, they learn that Bryn, Eira, and Maelona remain trapped in an unstable overlap with Limbo.

#### Long
Five days after [[Viatela]], the party follows a wooded ridgeline through the [[Plaguelands]] on the way to [[Orenlas]] and sees the flicker of a campfire. Then it is gone, and then it is back, like a strobe in slow motion. Estiasilos scouts ahead and reports a camp and danger, so the party runs toward it. Arianwen and what appears to be Taliesin are fighting giant, semi-amorphous shadow-weasels. The creatures repeatedly turn against a rope staked along one side of the camp, a stone cairn, and a cookpot over the fire. The party dispatches the shadow-weasels, and Izgil buries the rope with magical earthmoving to shield it from further attacks. Arianwen explains that the Rangers were caught in an unstable overlap with Limbo where belief can reshape reality and time behaves nonlinearly. They believe the rope, cookpot, and cairn are helping them hold the way open, but Bryn, Eira, and Maelona remain trapped inside.

### recap-002 | Bryn and the Three-Moment Predator

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: beat-002
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: none
- NPCs: Bryn, the doppelganger, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: three-moment shadow-weasel

#### Short
Leaving Izgil with Arianwen, the others enter the overlap, defeat a shadow-weasel whose body occupies several moments at once, and convince Bryn to perceive them.

#### Intermediate
Izgil remains outside to help Arianwen hold the entrance while the others follow the duplicate into the overlap. In a folded ravine they fight the separated head, torso, and feet of one shadow-weasel, then use one of Bryn's cairns to help the trapped scout recognize them.

#### Long
Izgil remains with Arianwen to study the overlap with Limbo and help hold the entrance. Robin, Viepuck, Celyn, Greymalkin, and Es*tiasilos enter with the duplicate. Ranger cairns lead them down a path and into a ravine, somewhat scattered. Bryn flickers between positions without perceiving the rescuers. A shadow-weasel attacks from three different moments at once: its head, torso, and feet appear separately, but an injury to one makes all three glow. The party defeats the parts separately and is left with Bryn, who still cannot see them. Viepuck creates an illusion of one of Bryn's cairns and eventually talks the scout into listening to the party and concentrating on finding the other Rangers. That purpose draws Bryn back into their shared reality.

### recap-003 | Shelter Beneath Glass Rain

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-003
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: none
- NPCs: Bryn, Eira, the doppelganger, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Bryn leads the rescuers to Eira, who holds a protective dome against a rain of glass while the surrounding landscape changes form.

#### Intermediate
Following Bryn deeper into the overlap, the party reaches Eira beneath a sheltering dome as glass falls from the sky. Robin and Celyn help sustain the protection until the weather begins to clear, but the duplicate becomes increasingly insistent that the remaining Ranger must be abandoned.

#### Long
Bryn says it gets a little weird farther down the path. Around the curve, the rescuers find a shimmering dome in a clearing, with Eira inside it, as glass begins to rain from the sky. They charge into the dome; Celyn is injured by the falling glass, and the shelter wobbles with the effort of protecting the enlarged group. Robin lends his strength to the belief that the protection will hold, while Celyn points out that with good luck the weather will break soon. It begins to clear. Eira warns that they should leave before the landscape devises something new to do to them, and the duplicate grows agitated and suggests abandoning the last missing Ranger.

### recap-004 | The Doppelganger

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: beat-004
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: none
- NPCs: Bryn, Eira, the doppelganger, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: the doppelganger

#### Short
Viepuck probes the duplicate's thoughts, exposes it as a doppelganger copying Taliesin, and helps the party destroy it outside Eira's shelter.

#### Intermediate
The duplicate's effort to end the rescue makes Viepuck suspicious enough to probe its mind. The spell reveals a constructed copy drawing on the real Taliesin's memories, and the party forces it into the solidifying air outside the shelter before destroying it.

#### Long
After confirming that his telepathic tie to Arianwen is not essential, Viepuck convinces the duplicate that a mind probe will help everyone escape. The probe reveals that this is not Taliesin, but something reading the real Taliesin's mind to produce a copy. Greymalkin decides that evisceration is the solution, and Robin quickly explains the discovery to Bryn and Eira. Viepuck punts the doppelganger through the side of the shelter, where it sinks halfway into air with the consistency of sticky toffee. Celyn concludes, "Okay I guess it's stabby time while the guy's immobilized." The doppelganger lashes out with psychic attacks, but the party destroys it. It becomes a gray, slightly malformed corpse. Unfortunately, the way out that Celyn had imagined fades while his attention is elsewhere, and the ground outside begins sparking as though it is about to catch fire.

### recap-005 | The Last Fragment

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-005
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: none
- NPCs: Taliesin, Maelona, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
As the overlap begins to burn, Celyn and Es*tiasilos open a route to the real Taliesin and the body of Maelona.

#### Intermediate
Destroying the doppelganger leaves the overlap unstable and the first imagined escape route fading. Viepuck's attempt to probe the realm provokes a new transformation, but Celyn's certainty and Es*tiasilos's altered form clear a path through the forming fire to the final fragment.

#### Long
Viepuck considers the unstable overlap a mental construct and declares, "I MIND PROBE IT." His patron's attention touches the place. Celyn works to reestablish the passage toward the real Taliesin: he found it once, and he can find it again. Es*tiasilos reacts to the contact by shifting through unfamiliar forms, then spins around and disgorges water across the forming fire, clearing a path in the direction Celyn knows they must go. They leave the worst of the weirdness and find a campsite containing a very confused Taliesin and Maelona's cold body. She bears no marks or injuries, suggesting that she was killed by psychic violence.

### recap-006 | Finding the Way Out

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-006
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: none
- NPCs: Bryn, Eira, Taliesin, Maelona, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Carrying Maelona, the group uses light, illusion, Ranger tracking, and Celyn's divination to reject false routes and escape.

#### Intermediate
The reunited rescuers carry Maelona toward the entrance through a landscape that continues to rearrange itself. Light, deliberate belief, an illusory clear path, tracks, and a final divine warning reduce a series of false exits to the one route that returns them to Arianwen.

#### Long
The party lifts Maelona's body and begins hiking out. Robin provides light while Celyn lights a pebble from his pocket. They cross the worst of the unstable overlap with weaponized optimism, a general belief that they are getting out, and Viepuck's illusion of a clear path in a plausible direction. This returns them to the ravine, now with four exits. Bryn identifies one as an illusion, and Bryn, Celyn, and Greymalkin use tracks to eliminate another. With an either-or choice remaining, Celyn asks his god whether one way is safe and receives an unequivocal warning, so the group takes the other path and leaves the overlap.

### recap-007 | Six Months at the Camp

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-007
- Date: 1720-12-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Plaguelands
- NPCs: Arianwen, Bryn, Eira, Taliesin, Maelona, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: Rangers
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The overlap collapses and ages the campsite by roughly six months; afterward, the Rangers explain the history of Robin's lantern and advise the party to be honest with the trees of Orenlas.

#### Intermediate
Once the final group emerges, the overlap closes and the damaged campsite abruptly ages by about half a year. During the recovery, the Rangers recognize the history attached to Robin's lantern and give the party practical advice for entering Orenlas safely.

#### Long
The unstable overlap with Limbo collapses behind the group, and the already battered campsite promptly ages about half a year. Once everyone has recovered enough to exchange stories, Taliesin recognizes Robin's lantern as one that once belonged to [[Cedric Cyfenwid]] explains more of its history. The Rangers also give the travelers advice for entering [[Orenlas]] safely, including, "be honest with the trees." Robin answers, "That will not be a problem, I am very forthright." Celyn answers, "That will not be a problem, I am not forthright at all but I'm honest."
