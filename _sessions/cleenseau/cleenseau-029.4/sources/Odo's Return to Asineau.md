### Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com> — Tue, 14 Oct 2025 17:11:32 -0400

Odo is doing well. He is still in Tavoise, with his young brother Samuel
(who you helped find hiding in the hills south of Ashecombe). He is
thrilled to talk to the party, and tells you about his life.Tavoise is a
small hamlet, less than 30 people, and hasn't really recovered from the
spider attacks. He speaks fondly of his fiance (Abigail Moss
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/abigail-moss/>)
but also is not sure he is ready to settle down. He talks excited about
some scouting he has done with Vahaiya in the Cleenseau Wood and seems
eager for a new challenge or responsibility outside of the hamlet. Vahaiya
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/elves/vahaiya/>has
talked about their weariness and eagerness for their home (Orenlas
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/istaros-watershed/orenlas/orenlas/>,
a month's journey to the east), and Odo says they have invited him to come
visit the elves when they return home. Which excites and also slightly
terrifies him. But also, he feels some responsibility for his brother (who
is just 15, and still recovering from his month-long sojourn in the
wilderness hiding from zombies). Odo says his brother often wakes in the
night with terrible nightmares, and he is torn. Abigail wants to get
married and have a family, and he just isn't sure what to do with himself
next. Abigail says she will wait for him, but he is nervous. As you might
remember from Odo's backstory
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/campaigns/cleenseau-campaign/stories/asides-and-diversions/the-destruction-of-eftly/>
he
had a sweetheart in Eftly when he left 3 years ago to join the army, who
said she would wait for him, but didn't.

### Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com> — Tue, 14 Oct 2025 17:40:39 -0400

Odo would be thrilled to move to Asineau with Abigail and Samuel and be
your guard captain (filling the role of "hireling" for your barracks).
Although he is still torn about traveling and adventuring, working for the
four of you would be a massive honor for him so he would jump at the
chance. Especially with the idea that maybe some opportunities to see parts
of the world would come along while in your company. His brother Samuel was
heading to Champimont
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/champimont/>to
apprentice to a blacksmith when the dead awoke. He is still interested in
apprenticing as a blacksmith, and has a friendly if hesitant relationship
with Diesla
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/dwarves/diesla-starsearcher/>,
Brot's wife. He has never really been able to connect with Brot, who is
probably a touch autistic and has a hard time connecting with people who
don't speak the language of craft and metal. But of course he would need a
mentor, he doesn't know blacksmithing *yet*.



He is a bit on the old side for an egg, but he did spend quite some time
with Es*tiasilos. He doesn't have one yet, but who knows....



The Wyrdling is the deity of the mad and of hope and dreams (as well as
luck, chaos, and fate). The Mother
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/cosmology/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-mother/>
is
the goddess of peace and healing (among other things). The Night Queen
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/cosmology/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen/>
is the goddess of sleep and compassion (but is more commonly the goddess
who guides the transition from life to death, and the defender against
undeath). Any of the three could potentially bring peace to Samuel.
Although also remember the tradition in Sembara is to ask one god to
intercede with other gods, i.e. "Wyrdling, beg the Night Queen on my behalf
for restful sleep and wash away the terror of the awoken dead".  Or
"Wyrdling, bringer of hope, bring the hope of healing to the Mother, she
who grants healing and peace"

### Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com> — Tue, 14 Oct 2025 19:37:23 -0400

There honestly hasn’t been much need. Despite the all chaos from the fey, since the undead risings in January there really hasn’t been any dangers in the Cleenseau region.

When false rumors were going around that the party was dead and/or turned traitor Odo spoke against that and cautioned people to not panic. That helped a bit.

But in general he was appointed by Rosalind to be the main eyes / guard in Tavoise and he’s pretty much been keeping to that. The forays into Cleenseau Wood with Vahaiya have been part of that and they (Vahaiya) was certainly inclined to trust him partly due to his position and recommendation from Robin.

But remember his knighting isn’t really an official position. He’s a member of the order but that doesn’t really come with any income or lands or anything like that, just a bit of prestige.
