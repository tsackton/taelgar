# More Notes and Questions (Email)

Selected setting and character clarifications from the January 24–25, 2024 correspondence. This is a briefing and discussion, not a played exchange. The proposed recruitment and loot decisions were left for later play.

On January 24, Mike Sackton said the entire River Patrol and half the Bridge Patrol had left Dunfry on December 26, about 45 soldiers in all. Ancer Benthey remained in Dunfry; Evelyn Totteridge returned and survived the January 6 zombie ambush. He estimated that almost two dozen soldiers had been killed, leaving about 25 in Cleenseau, with high morale and good unit cohesion under Ida and Evelyn despite the losses. These were contemporary estimates; [[Army Garrison of Cleenseau]] also records later, differing estimates of the force.

Mike also said the Lord's Guard's ten-person houseguard had gone with Rosalind. Six of the eight sheriffs remained after two died in the fighting, including Ysabel. Beatrix appeared to be stepping into Ysabel's place; Robin headed the guard by Rosalind's command. [[Lord's Guard of Cleenseau]] still mixes pre- and post-attack staffing and leadership.

Chris Kelly suggested recruiting willing Wakog survivors for the local guard. Mike replied that recruitment should be resolved at the next game. Dan Walters described Robin as wearing colorful but practical clothing without jewelry or precious-metal adornment; Robin would prefer to sell or trade the elaborate saddle with the matching armor rather than use it himself. No sale is established by this exchange.

The GM identified the ornate saddle as part of a set with the plate armor, shield, and hammer. The Ranger chest and necromantic ring are described separately in [[Chest of the Forest Guardian]] and [[Ring of Wounding]].
