More precise information here:
* The entire River Patrol and half the Bridge Patrol left Dunfry on Dec
26th (~45 soldiers). Ancer, as the Sgt of the Bridge Patrol, stayed behind.
Evelyn (who you have not met in game, but probably have seen around town
say during Pyrevela or Vitalae, or just in some of the November downtime)
headed back to Cleenseau, and survived the zombie attack.
* They were ambushed by a large number of zombies on January 6th, about 3
days west of Cleenseau. Almost 2 dozen were killed. Ida reports there were
at least 60 zombies (to her 45 soldiers).

So that means the garrison is currently massively understaffed -- about 25
soldiers compared to a normal company size of almost 60. Also somewhat
shell-shocked. Skirmishes certainly happen against hobgoblins, orcs, etc
out west, but this type of thing hasn't happened in recent memory, and
being outnumbered so badly - and by zombies that won't die easily as you
keep hacking and hacking at them - is not normal experience. But Ida and
Evelyn are both good leaders and the garrison is holding up fairly well,
high morale and good unit cohesion despite the loses.

Outside of the garrison, is the Lord's Guard:
https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/lord-s-guard-of-cleenseau/
That's much more badly staffed. The entire houseguard (10 people including
Ames) went with Rosalind. The sheriffs remained (8 people, including
Ysabel) but two of them (including Ysabel) died in the recent fighting.
Beatrix is showing her will/strength though and looks like she is trying to
fill Ysabel's shoes. (And of course, Robin is the head of the guard, per
Rosalind's command).

You have less information on both the random townsfolk -- some of whom
would definitely have weapon skills - and the people in the other villages.
But you were able to raise a decent force for the battle against Wakog, and
a lot of those folks are still around. But recruiting can be one of the
first things to discuss in game next session.
