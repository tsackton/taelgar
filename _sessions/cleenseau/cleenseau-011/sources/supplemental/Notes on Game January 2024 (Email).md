# Notes on Game January 2024 (Email)

Selected GM clarifications and player intentions from the January 24–26, 2024 correspondence. Questions and proposed actions below are not evidence that the actions occurred.

Mike said Erick Murtha had asked the party to contact [[Ganboa]]. He assumed they had done so and planned for a lizardfolk representative to attend the next council meeting; the session record governs the meeting itself. Mike's description of [[Balthazar]]—his caution, curiosity, reluctance to accept help, care for an unusual egg, and life among other Wakog refugees in Underhill—is already preserved on Balthazar's page. Viepuck proposed teaching and befriending him; this correspondence does not resolve those proposals.

Mike identified [[Conrad]] as a frequent morning visitor to Essford Manor and a confidant of Viepuck. He said Conrad admired Anselm's strength but had also shared reservations about Anselm's ambition. [[Eremon]] was with Rosalind, had noticed the simultaneous illnesses of Wymar and other border lords, and was concerned by them. These were the party's contemporary impressions, not proof of a wider cause.

There was no kingdom-wide ban on carrying weapons inside towns. The restrictions in Cleenseau were imposed locally by its lords; members of the Essford household, including the heroes and Rinault's companions, could carry arms. Artan and Dan discussed relaxing the restriction and training additional defenders, but Mike left any order to Robin for later play. Mike also described an irregular crown-supported postal service reaching Cleenseau; he did not guarantee a letter's security.
