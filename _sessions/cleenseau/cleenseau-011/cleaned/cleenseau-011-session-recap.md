# Session Recap

## Session Header

- Title: Cleenseau - Session 11
- Desc Title: Organizing the Region
- Tagline: in which the party builds a defense and removes Asineau's lord
- One-Sentence Summary: The party organizes a regional defense force, recruits local support, and frightens [[Asineau|Asineau's]] ineffective lord into abandoning his manor.

- Scope: session
- Session Number: 11
- DR Date: 1720-01-09 to 1720-01-12

### 1720-01-09

- Timeline Segment: timeline-001

#### Short
The party rides out from [[Cleenseau]] to heal wounded soldiers arriving with news of further undead attacks.

#### Long
[[Ida Rosfeld]] reaches [[Cleenseau]] with wounded survivors from the [[Army Garrison of Cleenseau]] and reports zombie attacks along the road. The party rides out to meet the soldiers and uses healing magic to restore the injured. [[Eveyln Totteridge]], a sergeant from the garrison, is among the regional defenders introduced during these events.

### 1720-01-10

- Timeline Segment: timeline-002
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-01-10)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Locations: Cleenseau, The Crossroads Inn
- NPCs: Ida Rosfeld, Eveyln Totteridge, Erick Murtha, Blanche, Harold Bybet, El, Thierry, Catherine de Brune, Rinault Essford, Lorin Valbert, Isolde, Marigold Stonebridge, Rose Stonebridge
- Organizations: Army Garrison of Cleenseau
- Items: Silverthorn
- Combat Beats: none

#### Short
Regional leaders fund an expanded defense force based at [[Asineau]], while the party begins undermining its obstructive lord.

#### Long
At a regional council in [[Cleenseau]], the party and local leaders agree to expand [[Ida Rosfeld|Ida Rosfeld's]] company to one hundred soldiers and establish its garrison at [[Asineau]]. [[Catherine de Brune]] contributes substantially to the required funding. [[Isolde]] objects on behalf of [[Lorin Valbert]], but testimony from [[Thierry]] and [[El]] establishes that Asineau has also faced undead attacks. That evening, Es*tiasilos steals a jewelry box from Lorin. At [[The Crossroads Inn]], [[Marigold Stonebridge]] thanks the party for recovering [[Rose Stonebridge|Rose Stonebridge's]] rapier and gives Celyn its matching sheath.

### 1720-01-11

- Timeline Segment: timeline-003
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-01-11)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Locations: Asineau, Taviose
- NPCs: Eleanor, Sarabeth Asa, Lucas Asa, Isolde, Lorin Valbert, El, Hugh Darrow, Phillipa Northwood, Remy Darrow, Brot Starsearcher, Rosalind Essford, Colonel Claude Leclerc, Duncan Rivers
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Combat Beats: none

#### Short
The party heals residents, deceives Asineau's lord into fearing a fey curse, and recruits trainers in [[Taviose]].

#### Long
In [[Asineau]], [[Eleanor]] tells the party about the disappearances of [[Sarabeth Asa]] and [[Lucas Asa]]. While Robin, Celyn, and [[El]] heal residents, Viepuck and Izgil convince [[Lorin Valbert]] that hostile fey are stealing his heirlooms and preparing to attack him. In [[Taviose]], [[Hugh Darrow]] and [[Phillipa Northwood]] agree to help train soldiers, the party visits [[Remy Darrow]], and [[Brot Starsearcher]] agrees to make barding. The party also receives a [[Letter from Rosalind (January 8th)|letter from Rosalind Essford]] with news of the siege of Fellburn. Reports from [[Ida Rosfeld]] and [[Duncan Rivers]] bring further news of [[Colonel Claude Leclerc]] and the wider undead attacks.

- [[Lorin Valbert]] (met): lord of Asineau whom the party convinces that hostile fey are pursuing him

- [[Eleanor]] (met): temple steward who recounts the disappearances of the Asa family

- [[Phillipa Northwood]] (met): woodcutter recruited to help train soldiers
- [[Remy Darrow]] (met): Taviose resident who shares a drink with the party
