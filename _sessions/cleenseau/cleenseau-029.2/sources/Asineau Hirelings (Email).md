# Asineau Hirelings (Email)

Complete local archive of the Asineau hirelings correspondence (five messages). Message bodies preserve quoted reply history.

## Tue, 28 Oct 2025 22:19:46 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Asineau Hirelings

Hi all,

In April and May, a number of people appear in Asineau, some that you know,
some that you do not, to settle into your bastion.

*Odo Cordwaner and family*
Odo
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/odo-cordwaner/>,
his younger brother Samuel, and his soon-to-be-wife Abigail Moss
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/abigail-moss/>
arrive
in late April, to take up residence as your new guard captain and
apprentice smith.

Odo: Guard Captain (barracks leader)
Samuel: apprentice smith (along with Roic Ferrystone)
Abigail: she doesn't have an immediate role, although will want some
purpose. She is a orchardkeeper and pig farmer by trade, she could
certainly help out with the garden. Alternatively, she would be happy to
serve as a household servant or cook.

*Roaric Ferrystone*
As mentioned, the Ferrystones appear and build a smithy in gratitude, and
Roaric, a semi-retired blacksmith from Beury, agreed to stay on and train
Samuel.

*Brot and Diesla Starsearcher*
Attracted by the growing dwarven presence and Izgil in particular, Brot and
Diesla relocate their workshop (with the help of the Ferrystone's) to
Asineau. They agree to serve as the masters of your workshop.

*Bastien*
At some point in early May, a haggard looking man appears on the manor
doorstep, asking after you. He says he heard you were the heroes who
defeated the zombie menace, and he thanks you for that, and asks if you
need guards. He says he was part of the Army of the West, but he mustered
out in the recent reorganization, and he needs something new to do with
himself. As you talk to him, it becomes clear that he is grieving, and
before long you hear his story. His husband, Cedric, was killed by those
zombie creatures, and died all alone in the river. Bastien was away to the
west with the Army, and doesn't really forgive himself for not being home.
He is struggling and wants a place to struggle in that understands. He is
hoping El can help him deal with his grief, and that maybe there could be a
place for him in Asineau.

*Betsy Throne*
Another late April arrival is Besty Throne, just 23, who has been with
Rosalind's guard for a year or so. But her cousin is now in charge, and she
wants to be somewhere where she isn't always being judged as not as good as
her cousin. Plus, she says, the captain of Rosalind's guard, Ames, isn't a
good teacher. It seems that perhaps she has other reasons for wanting to
leave Cleenseau but she isn't super open about them. She is hoping for a
guard position.

*Mabel*
Mabel
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/mabel-of-cleenseau/>
comes
from Beggar's Way, the poor rundown part of Cleenseau, and appears at some
point, asking you to remember her work scouting for you during the battle
against the orcs, and wondering if you would be willing to take her on as a
guard and train her.

*Avelina*
Towards the end of May, an older woman, maybe around 50, appears in
Cleenseau, asking after Brot and Diesla and Roaric and Izgil. It seems she
is a smith from Cleenseau, specialized in weaponsmithing, who had injured
her shoulder and right arm in the recent troubles, and can no longer easily
work a smithy. She has given her weaponsmithing shop over to her journeymen
and hoped to spend some time with the dwarves before figuring out what to
do next. But if you had a position for her, she'd love to stay... [meta:
she'd be a good armoury master]

You also have three existing guards: Matias
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/matias/>,
Connor
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/connor/>, and
Elbeth
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/elbeth/>.
Although you could fire any of them if you want.

If you hire all these guards and don't fire any, that is 7 of your 8
guards. I can leave the last one unnamed or make up an 8th person.

Mike

## Wed, 29 Oct 2025 12:23:53 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Taelgar: Asineau Hirelings

I see no reason why we wouldn't want to take on the new folks.

At some point Robin will have a sit down with the original three guards (and I'd ask if Celyn or Viepuck can be there to gage reactions), and basically, he will say this:

Asineau was a mess, but Robin puts most of the blame with the person who had the most authority, i.e. the old lord. As far as he's concerned it's a new day and everyone in town is getting a fresh start. If any of them have done anything that might get them pulled in front of a magistrate, he encourages them to tell him now. Otherwise, basically the new rule is, 'don't be an asshole.' We're here to protect and take care of the town, don't be an asshole. We're trying to make this a place people are proud to come from, don't be an asshole. If there's a problem, come to him, one his companions, or whoever is acting in his stead if the party is off adventuring, but in short, we want this to be a good place to live. If everyone can be on board with that, there shouldn't be any issues (between Robin and the old guards).

Daniel Walters
