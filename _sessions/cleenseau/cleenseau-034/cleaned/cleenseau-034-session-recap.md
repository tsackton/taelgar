### recap-001 | The Fortress Aftermath

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-001
- Date: 1720-10-28 to 1720-11-05
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Fortress of Ianto the Red
- NPCs: Krat, Garn, Droog, Grib, Snikket, Tarastië, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: Greymalkin's wings, silver unicorn horn, phoenix-like tail feather, intertwined stag-and-wood-creature remains, wrought-black elk horns, rest of Ianto's trophy collection
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party splints Greymalkin's recovered wings in place, gathers Ianto's trophies, buries the redcap's recent victims, and gains the company of Tarastië.

#### Intermediate
After destroying [[Ianto|Ianto's]] fortress, the party reattaches Greymalkin's wings but cannot restore his control of them. They collect the principal trophies and the remainder of Ianto's collection, bury his recent victims, and meet Tarastië, a small weasel-and-raven gryphon who had been bonded to one of the dead and attaches herself to Celyn.

#### Long
The party gets Greymalkin's wings down from the wall and tries to reattach them. They can get the wings physically affixed and keep them alive with magic, but they remain dead weight outside Greymalkin's control, so the party splints them in place.

They collect the trophies in the hope of finding anyone to whom they might be restored, including a silver unicorn horn, a phoenix-like tail feather, wrought-black elk horns, intertwined remains of a stag and wood-creature, and the rest of [[Ianto|Ianto's]] collection. They bring what they can of a recent victim back down the blood trail, but find only the remnants of a traveler's camp rather than a village. Izgil digs graves while Celyn manages respectful care for the dead.

During the ritual work, Celyn notices a small creature spying from above and offers condolences. The creature, a weasel-and-raven gryphon named [[Tarastië]], had been the companion of one of the deceased. She receives a large, perfect pinecone and some sticks as grave offerings, then attaches herself to Celyn for security. The party sets out toward [[Twilight's Grace]] with [[Krat]], [[Garn]], [[Droog]], [[Grib]], and the transformed [[Snikket]].

### recap-002 | Across the Grey Mists

- Kind: travel
- Beat IDs: beat-002
- Date: 1720-11-05 to 1720-11-15
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Grey Mists
- NPCs: Krat, Garn, Droog, Grib, Snikket, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The Grey Mists tempt and frighten the travelers with false refuges and compulsions, separate the goblins from the party, and strip away months of memories before Celyn leads the others onward.

#### Intermediate
Traveling east through the Grey Mists, the party encounters a cottage full of illusory gold, a false library, a compelling pasture, visions in the rain, and imagined attackers. The goblins become separated, and the accumulated magic removes memories from Robin, Izgil, and Viepuck, leaving Celyn to explain their journey and guide them out.

#### Long
The travelers see a wall of mist across their intended route and enter it, keeping roughly east by following the north-south ridgelines. They soon encounter a goblin-scale cottage that is obviously a trap, but the goblins charge inside to count a heap of coins. Viepuck begins trying to stop them with magic and repeatedly forgets what he can do. Izgil asks, "Weren't you going to cast an illusion?" Viepuck finally answers, "I can't cast illusions." Izgil caves in the floor under the table, but the goblins only climb into the pit and complain that counting the gold is now more difficult. Meanwhile, [[Snikket]] tries to wander toward a pasture, and the party reluctantly leaves the other goblins behind.

A glint of moonlight appears through the mist just off the path. Celyn observes that "just off the path" is the most suspicious possible location. Izgil's moonlight reveals a library and compels him to approach it, but Robin holds the rope the others have looped around him until they leave its range. The lantern helps them see but impresses on Robin that using its daylight would betray their defenses. Viepuck attempts to probe the intelligence behind the mist and forgets he can do that.

In thicker mist, rain droplets show Celyn hills, fields, and people he might recognize if he looks more closely, but he refuses the temptation. Viepuck becomes convinced that Es*tiasilos has vanished from around his neck until Izgil shows him otherwise. The party crosses a path among possible barrows, ignores the sounds of an attack, and sees a four-armed headless monster rear from the mist; the last goblin flees back down the path. Robin becomes convinced that a dragon or other flying creature will snatch someone, but asks Celyn to watch the sky and manages to keep moving.

Near the edge of the mist, the accumulated effect becomes clear. Robin has lost a day, Izgil roughly six months, and Viepuck about a year and a half, including his memories of Es*tiasilos hatching. Celyn alone fully remembers why they are there. When Viepuck cannot remember Robin, Robin loses his temper, invokes the lantern's daylight, and marches toward the trees visible beyond the mist.

### recap-003 | The Border Siblings

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-003
- Date: 1720-11-15
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Camp of Horn and Feather
- NPCs: Thyron, Kyron, Myron, Syron, Lyron, Tarastië, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party meets five fey siblings guarding the border, establishes its bona fides, accepts their hospitality, and restores most of the memories lost in the Grey Mists.

#### Intermediate
Beyond the mist, the party encounters Thyron, Kyron, Myron, Syron, and Lyron, five similar-looking fey siblings serving as border guards. Celyn establishes that the travelers destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal, the siblings identify Robin's lantern with the vanished Cloudspinner, and the party accepts an invitation to the Camp of Horn and Feather, where Celyn's prayers restore the others' memories.

#### Long
Lounging in a tree beyond the mist is a silvery-pale fey being who greets the party as approaching intruders. Celyn tries diplomacy before Robin can create an incident and explains that they destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal and therefore could not use it to go home. Celyn identifies himself as the person a particular squire of the court had been sent to watch, provoking laughter from someone in the background who says her sister will enjoy that gossip.

Outside the mist, the party meets several similar-looking fey who are clearly the border guard: [[Thyron]], [[Kyron]], [[Myron]], [[Syron]], and [[Lyron]]. They ask Robin how he acquired his lantern and identify it as a rare surviving vessel of the particular light of the vanished [[Cloudspinner]]. One sibling abruptly declares, "THIS IS BORING. Come to our campfire." Izgil asks, "Is this an invitation of hospitality?" The answer is, "It is what you make of it," which the party accepts.

The nominal fort on the ridge is mostly a wall with a bonfire and tents behind it. The siblings return from their hunts and speak to the slain creatures as though expecting to hunt them again after the feast. Celyn attempts to restore the party's memories. His ritual prayer returns Viepuck's knowledge of his abilities, Izgil's memories through the fight with the Hunter, and nearly all of Robin's missing day.

### recap-004 | Revels at Horn and Feather

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-004
- Date: 1720-11-15
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Camp of Horn and Feather
- NPCs: Thyron, Kyron, Myron, Syron, Lyron, Tarastië, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The siblings roast the creatures of their hunt and draw the party into gossip, wine, races, dances, wagers, stories, and alchemical discussion before everyone rests.

#### Intermediate
At the Camp of Horn and Feather, Syron trades gossip with Celyn about Istarias, Kyron challenges Greymalkin and Celyn to races, Lyron leads a dance contest, Thyron exchanges tall tales with Viepuck, and Myron discusses alchemy with Izgil. After the revel, the travelers sleep and earn an escort to Evensong Spire.

#### Long
The creatures of the hunt are roasted, and every fey sibling seems to have an unusually large animal companion. Lyron's peacock struts at Greymalkin as if insisting its feathers are shinier. The siblings introduce themselves; their names are similar but their personalities are not.

[[Syron]], who has returned from hunting and once dated [[Istarias]], is glad to hear what he has been doing and to mock him. Celyn says, "He threw acorns at me." Syron replies, "Rather silly of him." Celyn explains, "Well, I was trying to decide which road to follow and I went down the one that didn't get acorns thrown at me." Syron concludes, "Rather wise of him!" She agrees that his nervous disposition may explain why a relationship with someone carrying at least twenty knives did not work out.

One sibling tells Robin, "You're so serious." Robin answers, "These are serious times." The fey says, "You don't seem like much fun." Robin replies, "I can be plenty of fun, right now I'm just tired," after which the fey ply him with wine. Viepuck is invited to share stories but cannot remember the party's exploits and falls back on older standards.

The revel is full of dares and challenges. [[Kyron]] challenges Greymalkin to race his elk through the trees. Greymalkin wins, and the elk must compose an ode in his honor. [[Lyron]] challenges Celyn to copy her acrobatic fire-leaping dance. Her peacock blocks Celyn's view until Izgil turns it invisible, which delights Lyron; Celyn copies the dance, then uses an illusory duplicate to set a dance for Lyron and another sibling to imitate. Kyron later challenges Celyn to race himself and laughs when the performance reveals the trick.

[[Thyron]] trades tall tales with Viepuck. Izgil notices that what [[Myron]] tosses into the fire is carefully disguised alchemy rather than herbs and begins an intellectual conversation with them. Syron throws knives while [[Tarastië]] occasionally tries to steal one. The party eventually sleeps beneath the unchanging last glimmer before sunset. When they wake, one sibling offers to escort them to the border because they won several wagers.

### recap-005 | The Watcher of the Evening Song

- Kind: travel
- Beat IDs: beat-005
- Date: 1720-11-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Evensong Spire, Twilight's Grace
- NPCs: Sir Caldris, Tarastië, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party reaches Evensong Spire, reports its news to Sir Caldris, and travels by giant black swan to Twilight's Grace.

#### Intermediate
Escorted to the Sunset Gate, the party enters Evensong Spire, a fortress intertwined with the gleaming colors of sunset. Sir Caldris, the Watcher of the Evening Song, hears their report of the destroyed portal and Ianto's death, then sends them onward to Twilight's Grace aboard a giant black swan.

#### Long
The travelers hike to the border and see the shimmering wall of the [[Sunset Gate]], the line between day and night. Rather than fragmented shards, it appears here as a solid wall of gleaming yellow-and-orange glass, with the fortress of [[Evensong Spire]] intertwined with it.

At the spire, the party reports that it brings news from the west. [[Sir Caldris|Sir Caldris, the Watcher of the Evening Song]], is fairly certain they have no news he does not already know, and Celyn agrees that this is likely. They report that they destroyed the Gloomshaper's portal and slew Ianto; the latter visibly does not concern him. Sir Caldris arranges their passage to the capital on a giant black swan, which carries Greymalkin in its beak because he does not fit on its back.

### recap-006 | Ethlenn's Audience

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-006
- Date: 1720-11-16
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Twilight's Grace
- NPCs: Archfey Ethlenn, Snikket, Tarastië, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: Greymalkin's wings
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Ethlenn explains that the party completed a danger she had foreseen, restores Greymalkin's wings, grants Snikket freedom of form within her realm, and bonds Tarastië with Celyn.

#### Intermediate
In Twilight's Grace, Ethlenn tells the party that Celyn and Robin followed the path she had quietly set against a foreseen danger, while Izgil and Viepuck joined by chance. During the ensuing discussion of boons, she restores Greymalkin, gives Snikket freedom to choose his shape within her realm, and offers Celyn a bond with Tarastië that both accept.

#### Long
In [[Twilight's Grace]], [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]] says she is pleased that the party succeeded at the quest they did not know they were on. She had nudged several of her people toward a doom she foresaw, including the possible death of her chosen mortal queen. Celyn and Robin were the ones who did not drift from their path; Izgil and Viepuck joined them through chance encounters.

The party discusses boons. Robin asks for Greymalkin and [[Snikket]] to be restored. Ethlenn restores Greymalkin's wings. She will not revoke Snikket's transformation, but grants him the choice to take any shape he likes within her realm. Snikket becomes a tall human resembling Robin and runs away in embarrassment. Ethlenn offers Izgil the future possibility of a vow of service and declines to explain why his research into the moon sometimes crosses her path. She declines Viepuck's request that she speak with his patron but grants him a personal boon.

Celyn asks Ethlenn to remove the curse placed on him by the Hag. She offers him the same freedom of form as Snikket but thinks it may not suit him. Celyn decides that his home is no longer in Tyrwingha and asks for something else. Ethlenn explains that [[Tarastië]] was once bonded and asks whether Celyn would accept such a bond. Celyn asks Tarastië whether she wants it; she agrees, and the bond is made.
