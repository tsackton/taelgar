## Recap

### recap-001 | Trial and Shadow

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-03-21
- Time: day
- Locations: Veltor, Veltor Keep
- NPCs: Thomas Dyerson, Marian, Etiene des Ostieux, Isabeau d'Aslain, Perette, Malach, Vivian
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: shadow manifestation, zombies

#### Short
The party exposes Malach at its trial and defeats the shadow manifestation that attacks the court.

#### Intermediate
At the sham trial, the party uses Marian's divine compulsion to answer the charges truthfully and publicly identifies King Robert as Malach. A shadow manifestation attacks the court and raises fallen bystanders as zombies, but the party destroys it.

#### Long
[[Thomas Dyerson]] presents the indictment while [[Etiene des Ostieux]], [[Marian]], and the local nobles assemble for the trial. [[Perette]] repeats the false accusation that the party murdered [[Vivian]] and practiced necromancy at the Baroness's direction. Marian invokes the Warlord's power to compel truthful testimony, and Viepuck eventually declares that King Robert is [[Malach]]. A shadow manifestation then tears open the air above the court, attacks the witnesses, and raises fallen bystanders as zombies. The party destroys the manifestation and protects many of those present; afterward [[Isabeau D'Aslain|the Baroness]] claims amnesia, and the authorities begin treating the accusations against the party as a manufactured case.

### recap-002 | A New Lordship

- Kind: aftermath
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-03-26 to 1720-04-01
- Time: multi-day
- Locations: Veltor, Asineau
- NPCs: Isabeau d'Aslain, Lyra Corvin
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Lyra confirms Malach's defeat and asks Robin to accept stewardship of Asineau.

#### Intermediate
Queen Elaine's messenger, Lyra Corvin, arrives with troops to take the Baroness into custody and confirms that Malach's reign has ended. Robin accepts the offered lordship of Asineau, and the party travels there.

#### Long
The party remains in [[Veltor]] while the court recovers and the Baroness's conduct is investigated. On March 26, [[Lyra Corvin]] arrives on behalf of Queen Elaine with troops prepared to take the Baroness into custody. She confirms that Malach has been defeated and asks Robin to accept responsibility for [[Asineau]]. Robin agrees, and the party reaches Asineau on April 1 to begin the work of restoring the neglected village.
