## Mon, 4 Mar 2024 18:08:22 -0500 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Next Thing

Hi all,

So in order to help me plan the next session a little bit, could I get some
feedback on what you want to do next?

I will send around an email with more role-playing details of Vahiaya and
their companions, but just to remind everyone of the current situation:

* The large town of Rinberg has some type of lake monster or other problem
near it
* The even larger town of Fellburn is under siege by 1000+ or so skeletons,
and the Army of the South, which was supposed to be defending the town is
nowhere to be found
* The Duke of Wisford has won (or lost) a large battle against skeletons to
the north
* You have murky news of some more troubles to the west, where the Army of
the West is based, but no real details
* You still believe that there is a pocket of zombies lurking around the
bog between Beury and Dallet
* You haven't heard anything from Rosalind in a while, as she rides towards
Embry
* Vahiaya is very interested in the portal to the Feywild you found, and in
exploring the Cleenseau Woods to see what types of information they could
find about fey activity. Is it related to all the undead? Or not?
* There is still work to do in Cleenseau region around helping out with

In particular, I do need to do some prep work for the next session which
will be somewhat different depending on exactly what you want to do.

To help you along, here are a couple of what-seems-to-me obvious
possibilities, but these should not be seen as limiting what you do --
there are no right answers and if you want to do something random you
certainly can:

* Return to Cleenseau and either (a) help organize defenses, or (b) explore
the fey portal with Vahiaya, or (c) explore the bog and try to hunt down
zombies (or any combination).
* Ride for Rinberg and see if you can do something about the lake monster
* Ride for Fellburn and gather information and scout the situation
* Spend some time on the road between Rinburg and Dallet trying to deal
with any small-scale fallout - lots of people here potentially need help

Wilder things you could do:

* Go to Embry in pursuit of Rosalind
* Go north looking for the Duke
* Go looking for the Army of the South
* Go west looking for information about the Army of the West

If your plan is to return to Cleenseau, I have lots made up there and you
don't have to be more specific than that, but if it is anything else, it
would help to have some guidance as to what you are going to do.

Mike

## Mon, 04 Mar 2024 23:39:56 +0000 — Dan Walters <arcadinal@protonmail.com>

Subject: Re: [Thantos] Taelgar: Next Thing

A few initial thoughts:

On a strategic level - If this lake monster at Rinburg can be slain and the siege at Fellburn lifted, we could create a rather strong stable block in south of the duchy, which would likely have ambient benefits to ongoing efforts just about everywhere else in the duchy. It also potentially allows us to open the road towards Embry which could be important.

I'm dubious about clearing the bog as a job for us. That sort of thing could take weeks or months, and with undead possibly wondering in from other regions it could in fact be a basically endless task. I feel this is best accomplished by organizing patrol groups that escort folks and keep the roads clear. If a more significant force of undead or something nasty like a caster is identified, we could go deal with it.

Investigating the fey portal absolutely has merit, and we could potentially close off a point of danger close to home. That said, I feel like there's a lot of potential global benefit to be had if we could free Rinburg and Fellburn. If we were able to slay the lake beast and than say muster a force sufficient to harry the seiging zombies enough to allow the defenders to help us destroy them, we'd make a pretty big splash. It would also put us in a much better position with heightened reputation to deal with things like trying to address problems with the army or even potentially whatever is going on in Embry.

The "Heroes of Clenseau" reputation does a fair bit for us right now, but (thinking bigger here) "liberators of the south" might encourage people with authority to actually listen to us.

Daniel Walters
