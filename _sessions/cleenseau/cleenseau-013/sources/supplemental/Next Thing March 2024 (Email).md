# Next Thing March 2024 (Email)

Selected situation briefing and agreed direction from the March 4–7, 2024 planning correspondence. The later session records establish what the party actually encountered.

Mike described reports of a threat at [[Lake Rin]], hundreds or perhaps a thousand skeletons besieging [[Fellburn]], uncertain news about the Army of the West, and remaining zombies near the East Bog. He stressed that the number at Fellburn came from Rosalind's letter and that contrary rumors were unsubstantiated. The size of a separate skeletal cavalry force near Dunmore was unknown. Fellburn lay about 140 miles east of Cleenseau; Dunmore was about 210 miles northwest.

The players broadly chose to travel east toward Rinburg and Fellburn to gather information and help where possible, while asking Vahaiya and the Rangers to return toward Cleenseau to help with the bog threat and later fey investigation. Mike accepted this as the preparation direction and expected the next session to begin on the morning of January 16, 1720. Artan also proposed sending introductions to Rinault, Ida, and Murtha, and reminding Perrin that Eleanor needed further Goodberries. This thread does not establish that those messages were sent.
