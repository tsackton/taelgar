## Recap

### recap-001 | Rescue from the Tower

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-02-21
- Time: day
- Locations: Veltor Keep, Merriweather's Tailor Shop
- NPCs: Damien Montrichard, Sabine de Brune, Istarias, the duskhound, Marceline Dupont, Victorine Rosseau, Isabeau d'Aslain
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: the duskhound

#### Short
The party frees Damien and escapes after defeating the duskhound guarding him.

#### Intermediate
After ensuring Sabine and Istarias are safe, the party returns to the keep, kills Damien's fey guard, and carries Damien out across the tower roof.

#### Long
With [[Sabine de Brune]] and [[Istarias]] safely outside the keep, the party returns for [[Damien Montrichard]]. They climb the tower, defeat the duskhound guarding him, release him from his chains, and escape across the widow's walk to [[Merriweather's Tailor Shop]]. Damien reports that [[Areschera]] had been using the duskhound to communicate with other fey. The party informs [[Marceline Dupont]] and [[Victorine Rosseau]], who delays further proceedings until [[Isabeau d'Aslain]] can return.

### recap-002 | The Shapeshifter Exposed

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-02-21
- Time: evening
- Locations: Veltor Keep
- NPCs: Areschera, Marguerite Deschamps, Victorine Rosseau
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: Areschera

#### Short
The party confronts and kills Areschera after she attempts to enchant them over dinner.

#### Intermediate
At a tense dinner in the keep, Areschera attempts to use magic on the party, provoking a fight that ends with her death.

#### Long
After resting, the party joins [[Areschera]] and [[Victorine Rosseau]] for dinner at the keep. The food is enchanted, and Areschera attempts to cast a spell on Viepuck when the party refuses to eat. A fight follows, and the party kills her. Her death exposes the fey shapeshifter who murdered and replaced [[Marguerite Deschamps]].
