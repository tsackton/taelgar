### 1720-01-25 to 1720-02-03

- Timeline Segment: timeline-001
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-01-25) to (DR_end:: 1720-02-03)

- Beat IDs: recap-001

- Items: none

#### Short
The party reunites, incorporates soldiers into Cleenseau's defenses, and prepares the town for another undead rising.

#### Long
On the road between [[Dallet]] and [[Beury]], the party reunites with Izgil and the Rangers returning from the [[East Bog]]. [[Vahaiya]] remains near the fey portal while [[Tristan Vaudrillard]], [[Remille Vauclaire]], and [[Emilie]] continue north. In [[Cleenseau]], the party helps [[Ida Rosfeld]] incorporate survivors of the [[Army of the South]] into the local defenses. They train the garrison against magical threats, consult [[Brot Starsearcher]] and [[Diesla Starsearcher]] about defensive engines, correspond with [[Rosalind Essford]] and [[Susanne Garay]], and receive news concerning [[Lenora Belles]] and [[Raoul Boulain]]. [[Rene Dubois]], [[Claire of Fellburn]], and [[Bertrand Leclerc]] arrive with the southern soldiers. Es*tiasilos scouts [[Enford]], and [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]] inducts [[Odo Cordwaner]] into the [[Order of the Charitable Wanderer]].
