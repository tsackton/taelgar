## Timeline

### 1720-02-17

- Timeline Segment: timeline-001
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-02-17)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-001, recap-002
- Locations: Aslain, The Resting Ox, The Setting Sun, Night Queen Temple (Aslain)
- NPCs: The Hunter, Bolgrim Ferrystone, Kazak Ferrystone, Vondal Ferrystone, Fin Merriweather, Callie Riverstone, Emma Marston, the messenger, the innkeeper
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Combat Beats: none

#### Short
The party defuses the Hunter's cursed letter and investigates the messenger and the destroyed Night Queen temple in [[Aslain]].

#### Long
At [[The Resting Ox]], the party safely examines the Sylvan letter delivered for them and confirms that [[The Hunter]] embedded a curse intended to make them forget the location of his lair. They heal [[Bolgrim Ferrystone]], who was injured when strange bears attacked the Ferrystones, and investigate the confused messenger who delivered the letter. [[Fin Merriweather]] directs them to [[Callie Riverstone]], who is sheltering the still-charmed messenger. Meanwhile, [[Emma Marston]] shows them evidence recovered from the burned [[Night Queen Temple (Aslain)|Night Queen temple]]. With help from [[Kazak Ferrystone]] and [[Vondal Ferrystone]], the party concludes that the supposed necromantic chamber was a recent and structurally implausible addition rather than part of the original temple.

### 1720-02-18

- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-02-18)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Locations: Aslain, Cranford
- NPCs: Callie Riverstone, the messenger, Juliana Westby, Tobias of Cranford, Lambert Talwrey, Piers of Houille
- Organizations: none
- Items: none

#### Short
The party confirms that the messenger was charmed by the Hunter's harpies, then travels to [[Cranford]] and learns of two murders.

#### Long
Before leaving [[Aslain]], the party visits [[Callie Riverstone]] again and examines the messenger hidden in her workshop. They determine that the Hunter's harpies charmed him and sent him to deliver the cursed letter. The party then travels to [[Cranford]], where [[Juliana Westby]] asks them to investigate the deaths of [[Lambert Talwrey]] and [[Piers of Houille]]. Witnesses accuse her surviving suitor, [[Tobias of Cranford]], of committing both murders.

### 1720-02-19

- Timeline Segment: timeline-003
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-02-19)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-004
- Locations: Cranford
- NPCs: Juliana Westby, Tobias of Cranford, Lambert Talwrey, Piers of Houille, Thomas Dyerson, Ida Rosfeld, Rinault Essford
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Combat Beats: none

#### Short
The party finds evidence that Tobias was framed, corrects false reports of its death, and detects an ambush near Veltor.

#### Long
[[Thomas Dyerson]] arrives from [[Veltor]] to investigate the Cranford murders. Celyn identifies magically created poison in the evidence but finds none in [[Tobias of Cranford|Tobias's]] room, while Viepuck's mind-reading and Robin's lantern support Tobias's claim of innocence. Dyerson agrees that the case is not proven and delays judgment while he gathers more evidence. Before leaving, Viepuck learns from [[Ida Rosfeld]] that a false messenger reported the party dead in the undead-infested caves; he corrects the report and assures [[Rinault Essford]] that the party is alive. About two miles from Veltor, Robin detects an impending ambush.

## Recap

### recap-001 | The Hunter's Letter

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-02-17
- Time: day
- Locations: The Resting Ox, Aslain
- NPCs: The Hunter, the innkeeper
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party safely defuses and reads the Hunter's cursed letter.

#### Intermediate
At the Resting Ox, the party identifies and neutralizes a curse embedded in the Hunter's Sylvan letter.

#### Long
At [[The Resting Ox]], the innkeeper gives the party a letter delivered for “the Tyrwinghan paladin riding a Thing, his straw-haired companion, his page, and their dwarven friend.” Celyn notices Sylvan sigils and treats the letter as a magical trap. Working together, the party identifies and neutralizes its curse before Celyn reads it. [[The Hunter]] gloats, threatens the party, and attempts to make them forget the location of his lair.

### recap-002 | Evidence in Aslain

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-02-17
- Time: day
- Locations: Aslain, The Setting Sun, Night Queen Temple (Aslain)
- NPCs: Bolgrim Ferrystone, Kazak Ferrystone, Vondal Ferrystone, Fin Merriweather, Callie Riverstone, Emma Marston, the messenger
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party investigates an injured dwarf, the missing messenger, and the burned Night Queen temple.

#### Intermediate
The party connects the messenger to the Hunter and finds evidence that the necromantic chamber beneath Aslain's burned Night Queen temple was recently constructed.

#### Long
The party heals [[Bolgrim Ferrystone]], who was badly injured when strange bears attacked him and his companions before the cavalry drove them off. At [[The Setting Sun]], [[Fin Merriweather]] directs Izgil and Celyn to [[Callie Riverstone]], who knows where the messenger has gone. Robin and Viepuck speak with [[Emma Marston]] about the destruction of Aslain's [[Night Queen Temple (Aslain)|Night Queen temple]]. At the ruins, [[Kazak Ferrystone]] and [[Vondal Ferrystone]] help examine the cramped ritual chamber and determine that its stonework is recent, poorly built, and inconsistent with the older temple. Emma sends word to Veltor asking that the surviving Night Queen clergy not be judged before a fuller investigation.

### recap-003 | The Road to Cranford

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-003

- Time: day

- NPCs: Callie Riverstone, the messenger, Juliana Westby, Tobias of Cranford, Lambert Talwrey, Piers of Houille
- Organizations: none
- Items: none

#### Short
The party identifies the messenger's enchantment and reaches Cranford amid a murder investigation.

#### Intermediate
The party confirms that the Hunter's harpies charmed the messenger, then arrives in Cranford and agrees to examine the case against Tobias.

#### Long
Before departing [[Aslain]], the party returns to [[Callie Riverstone]], who has hidden the confused messenger in her workshop. Viepuck and Izgil determine that the Hunter's harpies charmed him and sent him to perform errands. The party rides north and stops in [[Cranford]], where [[Juliana Westby]] is preparing to judge her favored suitor, [[Tobias of Cranford]], for the murders of [[Lambert Talwrey]] and [[Piers of Houille]]. Suspecting another fey deception, the party agrees to investigate.

### recap-004 | Not Proven

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: recap-004
- Date: 1720-02-19
- Time: day
- Locations: Cranford
- NPCs: Juliana Westby, Tobias of Cranford, Lambert Talwrey, Piers of Houille, Thomas Dyerson, Ida Rosfeld, Rinault Essford
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party finds evidence that Tobias was framed and continues toward Veltor.

#### Intermediate
The party persuades Thomas Dyerson to delay judgment against Tobias, corrects false reports of its death, and discovers an ambush near Veltor.

#### Long
When [[Thomas Dyerson]] arrives from [[Veltor]], the party argues that the testimony against [[Tobias of Cranford]] may have been manufactured through fey magic. Celyn identifies magically created poison in the cup used to kill one victim but finds no poison in Tobias's room. Viepuck reads Tobias's thoughts while Robin's lantern rewards truthful answers, and both support his claim of innocence. Dyerson accepts a verdict of “not proven” while he continues gathering evidence. Before leaving [[Cranford]], Viepuck learns from [[Ida Rosfeld]] that a messenger falsely reported the party dead in the undead-infested caves. He corrects the report and contacts [[Rinault Essford]] as well. About two miles outside Veltor, Robin's weapon warns him that the party is about to be ambushed.
