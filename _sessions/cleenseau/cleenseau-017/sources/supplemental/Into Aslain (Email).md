[Background: Over the course of two and a half long days, the party escorts
the villagers of Ashcombe down out of the Maudor Hills and across the
fields to Aslain, and then enters Aslain with some fanfare. To speed up the
start of the next session, I'm going to outline the people and events you
learn about as you enter the town, so you can decide who specifically to
talk to during our in-game time. If anyone wants to run any encounters over
email beforehand, we can, but I don't necessarily want to do so if not
everyone has the bandwidth to participate. The information below is what
you learn/know collected from (a) the people of Ashcombe, who you spend
some time with on the road and (b) Evelyn Totteridge
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/eveyln-totteridge/>,
the sergeant you brought with you from Cleenseau and the leader of your 10
troops. She has been anxious about you, and has been sending a rider out
each morning to look for you. This rider spotted you about 4 hours before
you reached Aslain, and Evelyn rode out to talk to you)]

*One Note About The Journey*
During the journey to Aslain across the hills and light forests, Celyn has
a high enough passive perception to have noticed a vulture, very high up,
always tailing the group.

*The Zombie Caves*
The town of Aslain has been central to the effort to provide troops to the
rangers trying to clear out the guerilla caves. In particular, you learn a
couple of things:

   - Pierre d'Aslain, the brother of the baroness, organized a militia of
   about 60 people and went north, shortly after Damien
   <https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/damien-montrichard/>
came
   through, apparently at Damien's request
   - Phillip Evynwood, the younger son of Ameline (the lady of Ashcombe),
   is a relatively wealthy timber merchant with strong connections to Rinburg,
   and is helping to raise a second militia at Pierre's request. He has been
   repeatedly asking Sergeant Totteridge for a commitment to join him and
   wants to leave Aslain immediately (he has recruited about 20 more people,
   but they are not well equipped or trained, and the 10 troops under
   Totteridge would be a huge boost)
   - Pierre's pregnant wife, Cece d'Aslain, is also still in Aslain with
   her young son, and has met with Evelyn twice to try to convince her to go
   north immediately to help her husband.

Evelyn wants to know your orders for her. You could also obtain
introductions and meet with:

   - Phillip Evynwood (intro from his mother, Ameline, the lady of
   Ashcombe, and also from Sgt Totterridge)
   - Cece d'Aslain (intro from Sgt Totterridge)

*The Ashcombe Refugees*
The villagers of Ashcombe are tired and scared, but through your leadership
have mostly not panicked. But there are a lot of questions as to what to do
next and concerns about how not to starve. Most of the winter food supplies
were left behind in Ashcombe. The leading figures in village are:

   - William Forester, one of the main lumberjacks, and effectively the
   steward/"union boss", who is still upset about his missing husband. He is
   caring but a bit headstrong.
   - Ameline Evynwood, the lady of Ashcombe Manor. Her husband is dead and
   her sons have left home to gain experience and connections in the wider
   world. She is sharp and dedicated to her people, but somewhat parochial and
   seems a bit overmatched by the circumstances
   - Alban, crippled and missing a leg from an accident several years ago,
   but still a repository of village wisdom, if tending a bit towards
   bitterness about his injury. He is the temple administrator to the village
   temple to the Wildling.
   - Rufus, Alban's adopted son and apprentice. A transman, from one of the
   small villages along the Enst, who doesn't speak about his family
   background. Learning rapidly from his adopted father and clearly the next
   generation of local knowledge, although some say he has a dark anger about
   him sometimes. Unclear if he has the temperment to take over the temple
   administrator, but  Alban doesn't have a better candidate (yet).

In general, the refugees are split between three groups:

   - Ameline, who has family and connections in Aslain, wants to beg
   charity from her connections and the d'Aslain family, and hunker down in
   Aslain and the surrounding villages (offering up some day labor) until the
   party declares it is safe to go back to Ashcombe
   - William, who has connections to the Masons Guild in Rinburg, is
   arguing the best course of action is to take as many people as are willing
   to go to Rinburg, recruit allies/militia in Rinburg, and then be ready when
   the party calls to retake Ashcombe. Leaving behind in Aslain only a small
   group who feel they can't make the journey.
   - Alban doesn't like the idea of trying to all stay in Aslain - he
   worries a lot about whether there is food for everyone if this drags out
   for months - but feels Rinburg is too far. He thinks they should scatter to
   a number of small villages along the road. Maudorville and several other
   unnamed ones (although he is clearly happy to take your advice to stay away
   from Peydon)

Rufus is quiet in the debates about what to do, but clearly a number of
villagers respect his opinion, especially the young families. Any advice
the party wants to provide would be gratefully taken, although it is also
reasonable to mostly stay out of the Ashcombe internal debates. You don't
have to involve yourselves in every decision in the world. You could also
have conversations with any of these people.

   - Old Alban, in particular, knows a lot about the woods around Ashcombe,
   and could answer any questions you might have about the lay of the land
   - William, in particular, has made it his business to know all about the
   people who have gone missing, and can provide insight/answer questions you
   might have about them

*The Night Queen Temple*
Evelyn also tells you about the attack on the Night Queen temple. As a
reminder, the Old Town of Aslain is now in ruins, but there is a small
community still living there around the still active temple to the Night
Queen. On February 12th (so 4 days ago, the day you were in Maudorville), a
group of soldiers - about 15 - came into Aslain, led by Marceline Dupont,
the baroness' Captain of the Horse. She had information about nefarious
activities happening underneath the old temple. Apparently, her
investigation bore fruit because the next day (the 13th, when you were on
the road towards Ashcombe)  the temple was destroyed. Evelyn doesn't have
much information about this, other than the news it happened, but she says
that Emma Marston, the magistrate of Aslain, was heavily involved and is
leading some investigations that came out of these events. She also says
the temple ruins are open, and you could go visit and investigate
yourselves. There are apparently some remnants of the small community still
hanging around the ruins.

You could:

   - Go investigate the ruins
   - Speak to Emma Marston (although you have no one to give you an
   introduction, that isn't necessarily much of an issue)

Marceline, the captain of horse for the baroness, left town shortly after
the destruction of the temple. You could ask for more information about her
whereabouts as well, although who would know is not immediately clear.
[Meta: This investigation we definitely should save for in-person]

*The Bear Attack*
Evelyn does have one sad bit of news to report: on the road north, they
were attacked by two ferocious bears with black and white fur about a day
after you split up. It was lucky, she says, because in fact they came upon
the bears as they were savaging two dwarfs (Bolgrim and Kazak Ferrystone)
on the road. Her soldiers charged - to save the dwarves - and one of her
troops was pulled from his horse and mauled to death. Bolgrim Ferrystone
was uninjured, but Kazak is pretty badly hurt, and although he has
stabilized, healing at some point would help get him back on his feet.

Evelyn can answer any questions about the attack, or provide an
introduction to Kazak and Bolgrim, who are recuperating at one of the
Ferrystone clan houses.

*The Fey Letter*
The messenger who brought the letter from the fey to Aslain was disoriented
and is missing, although no one seems to know who he is. You've been told
that his brightly colored leather shirt was pretty unique, and that a
halfling named Fin Merriweather might have some ideas at least where the
shirt came from.
[Meta: This investigation we definitely should save for in-person]

*Other People*
In addition to all of the above, you have an:

   - introduction to Seraphina d'Aslain (the Elder), the mother of Jean-Luc
   <https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/jean-luc-d-aslain/>,
   and cousin to both the baroness and the current Lord of Aslain, the young
   girl, Seraphina d'Aslain (the Younger) - who you understand is about 10 or
   so.
   - met Christiana Winchelsea, the temple steward of the large temple of
   the Mother in town, is at the forefront of the welcoming committee that
   greats your arrival in town, and is generous with her praise of your escort
   of these poor refugees to town, and asks you come to her if you need
   anything
   - met Owain Tuvar, the secretary and keeper of the purse for the
   d'Aslain family, also part of the welcoming committee, mostly quiet
   although he made a point to introduce himself to Izgil and Robin. He
   murmured something about being very interested in news from the south

There are also two families you've heard of:

   - The Ferrystones, a dwarven clan, a group of whom are staying at the
   Resting Ox. They are important in Rinburg, and might have some useful
   information to share (and see Bolgrim and Kazak above)
   - The Merriweather's, a halfling family in the area, who are apparently
   the family the old lord killed (Reginald Rusebek) to cover up his
   embezzlement. Fin (mentioned above) is one of them

*Messengers*
Finally, remember you are originally here partly to try to find out why no
news has been coming south. Although Evelyn has no specific information
about this, you could reach out to any of these potential contacts above to
discuss it.

[Meta note: In any of the situations below where there is an info-gathering
