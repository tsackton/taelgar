### recap-002 | The Buried Fort

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-01-07
- Time: morning
- Locations: Essford Manor
- NPCs: none
- Organizations: none
- Items: Ring of Wounding, Dwarven Ring of Sorcerous Restoration (Izgil), Chest of the Forest Guardian, Cloak of Concealment, Ring of Waterwalking (Cleenseau)

#### Short
The party enters the fort, defeats its undead defenders, and recovers a Ranger's cache.

#### Intermediate
The party descends into the buried fort, overcomes traps and undead defenders, and recovers several magical items from a Ranger's chest.

#### Long
The party descends through the trapped entrance beneath [[Essford Manor]] into the buried fort. They avoid traps designed to contain the lower levels, defeat giant centipedes, and open a Ranger's [[Chest of the Forest Guardian]]. A swarm of animated bones and a skeletal necromancer then attack; after defeating them, the party recovers the [[Ring of Wounding]], [[Dwarven Ring of Sorcerous Restoration (Izgil)|Ring of Sorcerous Restoration]], [[Cloak of Concealment]], and [[Ring of Waterwalking (Cleenseau)|Ring of Water Walking]]. The session ends when they reach an entrance hall occupied by ghouls.
