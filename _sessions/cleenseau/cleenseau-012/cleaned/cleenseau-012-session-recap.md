#### Short
The party catches Lorin, recovers most of Asineau's money and horses, and returns the resources to the regional defense.

#### Long
Celyn and Viepuck pursue [[Lorin Valbert]] along the [[Great South Road]] and catch him at [[Champimont]]. With [[Isolde]] remaining as his retainer, Lorin agrees to return most of the money and horses taken from [[Asineau]] in exchange for safe passage across the river. [[Rinault Essford]] is restrained from confronting him. The recovered resources are returned to [[Ida Rosfeld]] for the regional defense. The party also resolves [[Catherine de Brune|Catherine de Brune's]] claim to land improperly sold by Lorin, while Celyn works with [[El]] and other defenders in Asineau.
