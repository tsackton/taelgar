# Military Stuff (Email)

Selected setting clarifications and proposals from the February 4–March 5, 2024 correspondence. Proposed tactics, training, and scouting are not recorded here as completed actions.

Mike Sackton described Ida as a capable military leader who respected the party and would consider their advice. He explained that Fellburn lies outside the Barony of Aveil, in the Duchy of Wisford. The duke, Jacques Bellemont, held considerable local authority, while the army had its own royal command. The Army of the South, about 400 strong, was supposed to be based at Fellburn, but Ida did not yet know its disposition during the undead crisis.

Ida advised gathering information before any move toward Fellburn. She could send riders; the party's news was already several days old. What the party had heard suggested local dead rising in poorly directed groups, rather than a single coordinated army. Fellburn's old battlefields and unconsecrated mass graves offered a possible reason for a larger concentration there, not a confirmed cause.

Mike said Robin's healing had restored about ten soldiers to fighting strength, raising an estimate from 30 to 40. News of the healing was spreading and likely to improve morale. Dan proposed offering healing to gravely ill or wounded people from allied towns and encouraging militia training; later he suggested contacting Rinburg and Fellburn and adding clubs or hammers to training. The correspondence does not itself resolve those proposals.
