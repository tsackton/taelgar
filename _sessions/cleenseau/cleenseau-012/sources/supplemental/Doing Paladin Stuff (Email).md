# Doing Paladin Stuff (Email)

Setting clarification from Mike Sackton's February 5, 2024 replies to Dan Walters. Dan proposed training Odo or Beatrix as paladins; the exchange did not establish that either began such training or gained divine magic.

Mike described magic as rare in Sembara. His rough estimate was that, among roughly 15 million people, no more than about 15,000 could cast spells, most of those only minor magic, and perhaps a dozen could cast spells of the fifth level or higher. He said access to magic was not generally something one could teach, though a divine blessing could arise later in life. Sembara regarded organized study of arcane magic with suspicion; schools existed in Tollen and Chardon.

Divine miracles require a connection to a god. Temple administrators in Sembara are generally not themselves considered especially close to the gods, while divinely inspired people often stand outside an organized priesthood. The Mos Numena divines are understood more as broad archetypes than as personalities with human family drama. Mike described them as generally on humanity's side against the world's dangers; the tradition has no doctrine of original sin or impure thoughts.

Mike thought Odo or Beatrix might grow into more important martial roles, but said ordinary training would not itself make either a paladin. He left open the possibility of a later religious connection.
