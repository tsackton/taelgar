# Celyn Childhood and Worldbuilding - Correspondence (Email)

Kiya Nicoll's March 21, 2024 authored piece is preserved in full as [[Celyn's Musings on Magic]]. This source retains the distinct discussion that followed it.

Mike accepted the piece and clarified the difference in Celyn's experience of magic: fey magic feels like active work, something he had to learn to touch and shape; miracles of the Wyrdling feel like a gift passing through him, which he guides rather than creates. Kiya confirmed that this matched her intent. Mike also suggested that the earl's claim on Clawyn wine might have begun as labor on particular vines and later become a share of the bottled output. Kiya described that development as plausible. Neither proposed an exact present-day division of the vineyards.

The story's references to Ampelos, childhood theft, and Celyn's thoughts during the ride remain in the authored piece. The GM comments do not replace its wording.
