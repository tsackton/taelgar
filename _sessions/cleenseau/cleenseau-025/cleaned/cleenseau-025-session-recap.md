## Recap

### recap-001 | The King Is Dead

- Kind: revelation
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-03-05
- Time: morning
- Locations: Peydon
- NPCs: Raoul Montagu, Julien Marteau, Sveta Dragunov, Malach, Robert I, Leonora
- Organizations: none
- Items: Malach's phylactery
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Raoul reveals Malach's impersonation of the dead king and assigns the party one fragment of the lich's phylactery.

#### Intermediate
Raoul tells the party that Malach has impersonated King Robert for four years and divided his phylactery into three hidden fragments.

#### Long
The party avoids the Baroness's messenger and meets [[Raoul Montagu]] in the hills outside Peydon, accompanied by [[Julien Marteau]] and [[Sveta Dragunov]]. Within a magical shelter warded against observation, Raoul explains that [[Robert I]] has been dead for four years and replaced by the lich [[Malach]]. He believes Malach is responsible for both the recent undead waves and magic that detects discussion of him. After Raoul, the paladin [[Leonora]], and their allies nearly defeated Malach, the lich divided his phylactery into three fragments and concealed them. Raoul asks the party to recover the fragment located within the Barony of Aveil.

### recap-002 | Journey to Tharwa Fea

- Kind: travel
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-03-05 to 1720-03-12
- Time: multi-day
- Locations: The Setting Sun, Tharwa Fea
- NPCs: the unnamed Setting Sun innkeeper
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party travels toward Tharwa Fea while Viepuck monitors the Baroness's search through an Aslain innkeeper.

#### Intermediate
On the road to Tharwa Fea, Viepuck arranges for the unnamed innkeeper of the Setting Sun to report inquiries about his alias.

#### Long
The party sets out on foot while Izgil studies his rings and Viepuck coordinates with distant allies. Viepuck tells the Baroness that he will be at [[The Setting Sun]] in [[Aslain]], then asks the inn's unnamed keeper to report any guards asking after his alias, “Timmy.” A day or two later the innkeeper reports that guards came through because they believed the child might be in danger. The party eventually reaches [[Tharwa Fea]], a ruined magical academy beneath an ominous cloud.

### recap-003 | The Phylactery Fragment

- Kind: exploration
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Date: 1720-03-12
- Time: day
- Locations: Tharwa Fea
- NPCs: none
- Organizations: none
- Items: Malach's phylactery fragment
- Enemies: illusory ghosts, a shadowy guardian

#### Short
The party explores Tharwa Fea, secures Malach's phylactery fragment, and escapes its guardian.

#### Intermediate
After passing the academy's enchanted gate, the party finds the phylactery fragment above a hidden study and seals it inside a lead box.

#### Long
At [[Tharwa Fea]], the party studies an enchanted gate that admits visitors according to what they seek. Beyond it, they encounter tormented statues, illusory ghosts whose attacks still cause harm, ruined studies, and a library concealed by magical darkness. A hidden chamber leads to a space saturated with despair, where [[Malach's Phylactery|a fragment of Malach's phylactery]] floats beyond easy reach. Izgil suppresses its magic long enough for Celyn to trap it in a lead box. As the party withdraws, a sword-bearing human shadow emerges from the cloud. They destroy it with radiant magic and contact Raoul for instructions.
