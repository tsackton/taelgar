### recap-002 | Robin's Abduction

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-03-01
- Time: before dawn
- Locations: Maudor Hills
- NPCs: The Hunter
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: harpies, enchanted bears, giant vultures

#### Short
The party pursues the Hunter's servants and frees Robin before they can carry him away.

#### Intermediate
Harpies abduct Robin during an attack on the party's camp, but the others overtake and defeat them before they reach the Hunter.

#### Long
The Hunter's servants attack the party's camp during the night, leaving Robin's weapons and armor behind while harpies carry him away under an enchantment. The others pursue by air, help Robin break free, and defeat the abductors. Evidence carried by the harpies indicates that the Hunter is preparing to leave once a prisoner arrives.

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-003

- Locations: Ashcombe, Maudor Hills
- NPCs: The Hunter, the duskhound
- Organizations: none

#### Short
The party frees captives and forces the Hunter to abandon his lair.

The party rests at [[Ashcombe]], scouts the Hunter's lair, and overhears orders for [[The Hunter]] to return to his own court. They attack, kill or disable the sentries, and free the captives held inside. The Hunter flees down the hillside, leaving a duskhound behind to delay pursuit, and escapes despite the party's efforts to stop him.
