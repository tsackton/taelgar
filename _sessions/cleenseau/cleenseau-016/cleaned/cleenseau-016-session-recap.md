### 1720-02-10 to 1720-02-12

- Timeline Segment: timeline-002
- Timeline Key: (DR:: 1720-02-10) to (DR_end:: 1720-02-12)
- Resolution: day
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Locations: Essenmer, Maudorville, Maudor Hills, Ashcombe
- NPCs: Wilhem of Maudorville, Susanna, Antoine Chevalier, Amelie, Anna, The Hunter, the duskhound, Ameline Evynwood
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Combat Beats: recap-002

#### Short
The party reaches Maudorville, defeats fey attackers, bargains with the Hunter, and evacuates Ashcombe.

#### Long
Traveling north through [[Essenmer]], the party reaches [[Maudorville]]. [[Wilhem of Maudorville]] discusses lunar lore with Izgil, [[Antoine Chevalier]] hosts the party, and [[Susanna]] reports that [[Amelie]] and [[Anna]] are missing. While investigating in the [[Maudor Hills]], the party defeats harpies, strange bears, and a giant vulture, then follows the surviving fey to [[The Hunter]]. Celyn negotiates a temporary truce with the Hunter and his unnamed duskhound and learns that three fey powers are competing in the region. The party then persuades [[Ameline Evynwood]] to evacuate [[Ashcombe]].
