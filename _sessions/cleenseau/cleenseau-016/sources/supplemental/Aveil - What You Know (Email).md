# Aveil - What You Know (Email)

Complete local archive of the Aveil - What You Know correspondence (8 messages). Message bodies preserve quoted reply history.

## Wed, 17 Jul 2024 14:21:30 -0400 — Mike Sackton <msackton@gmail.com>

Subject: Taelgar: Aveil - What you Know

The party is heading north to the Barony of Aveil
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil/>
to try to accomplish one or more of the following goals:

(a) Investigate the old guerilla caves along the Aveil Ridge, where Damien,
Tristan, and the other Rangers are currently trying to flush out a
skeletons and zombies
(b) Speak with the Baroness
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/isabeau-d-aslain/>in
Veltor and inform her of Lord Valbert's abandonment of Asineau and
potentially advocate for Robin / Anselm / someone else to take over
(c) Try to figure out why no news has come from the northern part of Aveil
in weeks.
(d) Relatedly, try to figure out what is causing the Baroness and her
baronial infrastructure to be failing to help the towns along the Enst
(Cleenseau, Champimont, etc).
(e) Investigate Gareth's story about the Midnight Lady in Peydon
(f) Look for Arnaud's centaur friend, Omphale, a seer who Armand was hoping
would help him understand the Night Queen's premonitions, near the village
of Windlea

You can also look through the background to see if there is anything else
you want to do:

* Gareth's Story
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/campaigns/cleenseau-campaign/stories/gareth-s-story/>
* Elizabeth's Story
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/campaigns/cleenseau-campaign/stories/elizabeth-of-cassen-s-story/>
* Viepuck's Sendings
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/campaigns/cleenseau-campaign/letters-and-other-writings/sendings/viepuck-feburary-9th-1720/>

Logistically (and the map here
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/assets/aveil.jpg> will help)
you will most likely be traveling north along the Auberonne
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/auberonne/>.
Along the road, it is basically, in a line north:
* 30 miles to Champimont along the Great South Road
* 30 miles to Lake Rin along the Great South Road

Turn north here, and then:
* 20 miles from Lake Rin to Peydon
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/peydon/>
(goal
e)
* 15 miles from Peydon to Aslain
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/aslain/>
(the largest town in the Barony, good place for info gathering for goals c
& d)
* 40 miles from Aslain to Veltor
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/veltor/>(the
baroness' seat, important for goal b, likely d as well)

From Aslain it is 40 miles west to Windlea
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/windlea/>,
where Omphale is rumored to live (goal f)
From Veltor it is 20 miles east to the guerilla caves, where the undead and
rangers are (goal a)

As far as travel times, the roads are not nearly as good in Aveil as the
Great South Road along the Enst. I can work out the exact travel times if
you need to know to make your decision, but you should assume 30 miles a
day if you are pushing your horses (but you can't keep that pace up
forever), and 10 - 20 miles a day if you are keeping a more sustainable
pace.

As you go, you will certainly pass through Dallet
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/dallet/>,
Champimont
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/champimont/>,
the ruins of Eftly, and you will be a couple hour detour from Rinburg.
These are all places where you could try to gather information. In
particular:
* in Dallet lives Jean-Luc d'Aslain
<https://msackton.github.io/taelgarverse1720/people/sembarans/jean-luc-d-aslain/>,
who as you might guess from his name, came from Aslain and might have
information about what its like
* Champimont is the kind of place rumors gather, and its a waystation for
travelers
* if you were trying to gather an army (even a very small one) Rinburg
might be worth stopping in

Finally, the current assumption is that you leave Cleenseau the morning of
February 10th. But you could delay a day or two or more to accomplish
things in town, if you want.

So from a starting next session standpoint, the most useful thing would be:

(a) anything you want to in town before you go  - lectures on fey,
gathering troops, instructions to Ida, etc
(b) your first planned destination in Aveil - Peydon? Windlea? Veltor?
Aslain? Somewhere else?
(c) your pace? Do you want to stop and talk to people or try to rush along
a bit?

For example, you could ride hard for the guerilla caves and then work
backwards. Or you could stop everywhere along the way, etc.

Just from an entirely meta standpoint, my goal would be to skip over the
travel by email and then start next session in Peydon, or Aslain, or the
banks of the Leandre River looking for Windlea, or the Aveil Ridge near the
guerilla caves, of the gates of Veltor, or whatever. But it would be good
to know which of those you are aiming for.

Mike

Assuming this is the goal, the first logical stop would be Aslain, to
gather information. You could rush past it on the way to Veltor, but it's
literally on the road and it's the economic and political heart of the
barony, so it would make sense to spend some time there.

There are a couple of ways you could get there (these all assume mounted,
riding relatively fast, but not racing with spare horses).

*Option 1: *Cleenseau - Champimont (spend the night, Feb 10) - Rinburg area
(visit Rinburg, spend the night, Feb 11) - Peydon or Brisbourg (spend the
night, Feb 12) - Aslain (arrive @ midday on Feb 13)
This lets you discuss/gather info/whatever in Rinburg, and you could either
spend the night in Peydon (of the Night Witch) to investigate, or ride past
Peydon to Brisbourg (a small village on the road) if you wanted to avoid
Peydon for now.

*Option 2: *Cleenseau - Champimont (spend the night, Feb 10) - turn north
at Chantefleur, skipping Rinburg to Maudorville (small village on the road
near Maudor Brook, spend the night, Feb 11)  - Aslain (arrive evening on
Feb 12)
This gets you to Aslain sooner and therefore wherever you go past Aslain
sooner.

You are also welcome to plan your own route, you can go ~30 miles on the
Great South Road (which, like some US highways, runs East - West despite
its name) or ~20 miles on the Aveil Road (running North - South along the
Auberonne) each day.

A third option would be to try to rush to Veltor (the seat of the baron) as
fast as possible, which would look something like:

Cleenseau - Champimont (spend the night, Feb 10) -  Maudorville (spend the
night, Feb 11)  - Aslain (spend the night, Feb 12) - Cranford (river by the
bridge over the Cranmere, which is the unlabeled river on the map to the
east of the Auberonne, night of Feb 13) - Veltor (arrive afternoon of Feb
14)

But unless you are going to ride past Aslain without trying to talk to
anyone at all, it would be better to start the session in Aslain and let
you decide in game how quickly to leave in the morning.

All of these times assume "fast but reasonable" pace. You could improve
them if you decided to, for example, borrow 10 spare horses and switch
mounts, and feed the horses goodberries to heal their muscle strain. If you
are traveling with more than 4-5 others (i.e. if you gather any sort of
meaningful troops to come with you) your times will also slow down -
something like

Cleenseau - Dallet (night, Feb 10) - Essenmer (night, Feb 11) -
Maduorville (night, Feb 12) - Aslain (night, Feb 13)
(the party could ride ahead and visit Rinburg in this case, meeting the
troops at Madourville)

Another question:
What, if anything, are you doing before you leave Cleenseau? Either in
terms of leaving behind instructions or other similar actions, or in terms
of bringing other people with you?

Mike
