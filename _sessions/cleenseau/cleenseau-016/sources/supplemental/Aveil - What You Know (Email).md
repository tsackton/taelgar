# Aveil - What You Know (Email)

Selected travel and situation briefing from Mike Sackton's July 17–24, 2024 correspondence. The party was considering how to investigate the failure of baronial aid, speak with the Baroness, follow up on Gareth's account, find Omphale, and assess undead activity near the Aveil Ridge. These were possible goals, not evidence that every stop was made.

Mike described the road north from Cleenseau as roughly thirty miles to Champimont, another thirty to Lake Rin, twenty from Lake Rin to Peydon, fifteen onward to Aslain, and forty from Aslain to Veltor. Windlea lay about forty miles west of Aslain, and the old guerrilla caves roughly twenty miles east of Veltor. He advised a sustainable pace of about ten to twenty miles per day on Aveil's poorer roads; faster mounted travel could cover more for a limited time. The [[Barony of Aveil]] note already records the principal route distances.

Kiya favored understanding and stabilizing the baronial situation first. Mike suggested Aslain as a natural first information stop and outlined several possible routes, but no itinerary was settled by the correspondence. The session record governs the actual journey.
