# Uses of Sending (Email)

Selected in-world briefing from the June 21–26, 2024 planning correspondence. This thread did not contain the completed Sending conversations; those are preserved in the separate Session 16 inter-session source bundles.

On June 26, Mike Sackton identified the Rangers then missing as Damien Montrichard, Adra Brightwood, and Enzo Brightwood. Vahaiya was checking the Cleenseau Wood portal: it had clearly opened again briefly but was closed at that point. Odo wanted help finding his missing brother Samuel. These are the circumstances discussed before Viepuck's later Sendings, not evidence that every proposed contact occurred.

Viepuck proposed contacting Damien after speaking with Tal, then attempting to reach Samuel using Odo's memories and telepathy. Mike asked the players to finish the political messages and specify the experiment before resolving it. The later correspondence and session records govern what actually happened.
