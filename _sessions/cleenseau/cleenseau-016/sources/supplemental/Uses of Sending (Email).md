Some rules bits below. In-game answers later.

> On Jun 21, 2024, at 8:26 PM, Artan Eter <artan.eter@gmail.com> wrote:
>
> Does the person you’re messaging know who the message is coming from?

Yes, as long as they know Viepuck. This is part of the magic.

>  Can I make it seem to come from different people using Performance or Acting?
>

No, Sending isn’t verbal or even really language. You don’t need a common language to send the message. So it isn’t something you can manipulate with acting.

> Do the words have to be words, or can they be uttered sounds, moans, general audible things a trained voice-actor could do, or is this transmitting idea-concepts (I don’t know where this is going, I’m just trying to get parameters)?

It’s more idea-concepts. See above.

>  I can only send messages to people I’m familiar with - does the Circlet and some kind of Detect-Thoughts probing allow me to familiarize with a person based on another person’s memory?  “Think of the person you want to send do, imagine them, etc etc”.

In general, familiar means someone you have met and spoken with. However there are certainly clever things you could do with telepathy, mind-probe, etc to potentially, make it work in other circumstances. But it would be a delicate, rare type of thing that I’d want to play out.

>
> Does Es’Tiasilos having met (or observed) someone count as familiarity with someone?
>

There are perhaps some circumstances where Es*Tiasilos would be able to give you “familiarity” but it would be a bit more complex than just “has observed the person”.
