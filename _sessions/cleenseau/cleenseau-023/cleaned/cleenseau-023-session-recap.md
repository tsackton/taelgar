### recap-001 | The Daverre Bargain

- Kind: investigation
- Beat IDs: recap-001
- Date: 1720-03-03
- Time: evening
- Locations: Peydon, Daverre Manor, Widow Aalis's house
- NPCs: Midnight Lady, Emeric Daverre, Ysabeau, Beatrice Roisier, Arnault Daverre, the Daverre household staff
- Organizations: none
- Items: the Midnight Lady's dollhouse, the inscribed refuge stone
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party discovers how the Midnight Lady's bargain and hidden refuge bind the Daverre family and Peydon.

#### Intermediate
Investigations at Widow Aalis's house and Daverre Manor reveal the bargain draining Emeric and the portal into the Midnight Lady's refuge.

#### Long
The party separates while the Midnight Lady is occupied with her recovered mirror. At Daverre Manor, Robin and Celyn find [[Emeric Daverre]] suffering from vitality drained through a poppet in the Midnight Lady's dollhouse. Emeric privately explains that he granted her sovereignty over “the lands of his blood” and fears that the bargain includes his daughter [[Ysabeau]]. Viepuck questions the household, including [[Beatrice Roisier]], while Izgil investigates the hidden entrance beneath [[Widow Aalis|Widow Aalis's house]]. In the preserved chambers of the late [[Arnault Daverre]], the party finds an inscribed refuge stone connected to a concealed portal.

### recap-002 | A Bargain for the Gloomshaper's Secret

- Kind: negotiation
- Beat IDs: recap-002
- Date: 1720-03-04
- Time: morning
- Locations: Peydon, Widow Aalis's house
- NPCs: Midnight Lady
- Organizations: none
- Items: Wand of the Witch's Curse
- Enemies: none

#### Short
Celyn trades the drained Wand of the Witch's Curse for information about the Gloomshaper and access to the Midnight Lady's refuge.

#### Intermediate
Celyn accepts a dangerous bargain while the rest of the party prepares to suppress the Midnight Lady's portal and attack her refuge.

#### Long
The [[Midnight Lady]] offers Celyn information about the Gloomshaper, a personal boon, and an end to her immediate threats in return for the [[Wand of the Witch's Curse]]. The party drains the wand's charges before Celyn accepts. She explains that the exiled Gloomshaper is attempting to hold open the Cleenseau Wood portal by sacrificing unusually potent Tyrwinghans. Once the Midnight Lady withdraws to examine the wand, Celyn rejoins the others and signals Izgil to suppress the portal.

### recap-003 | The Midnight Lady's Refuge

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: recap-003
- Date: 1720-03-04
- Time: day
- Locations: Midnight Lady's refuge
- NPCs: Midnight Lady
- Organizations: none
- Items: Wand of the Witch's Curse, the Midnight Lady's dollhouse
- Enemies: Midnight Lady, oozes, giant rats, shambling mounds

#### Short
The party destroys the Midnight Lady and escapes her collapsing refuge.

#### Intermediate
With the portal suppressed, the party enters the refuge, overcomes its conjured defenders, and kills the Midnight Lady despite her dying curse.

#### Long
Izgil suppresses the portal while the Midnight Lady is inside her refuge, cutting her off from the poppets through which she restores herself. The party enters and fights through oozes, giant rats, grasping vines, and shambling mounds generated by the refuge. Celyn calls down holy light that kills the Midnight Lady. With her dying breath, she revokes his boon and curses him, but the Wyrdling shields him from the full force of the attack. The party recovers the emptied wand and escapes as the refuge becomes unstable.
