# Asineau Interlude - April Preparation (Email)

Selected character intentions and GM briefing from the October 12–15, 2025 correspondence about the party's return to Asineau in early April 1720. The played [[April Around Asineau]] exchange and Session 29 recap govern outcomes.

Mike identified three immediate issues: Cleenseau's army garrison needed a captain, Eleanor was ill again, and Catherine de Brune wanted discretion about her cousin's fey involvement while offering help restoring Asineau. He expected Izgil to establish a workshop and research crafting, Celyn to help Robin and investigate the zombie plague, Robin to rebuild Asineau and strengthen local ties, and Viepuck to help. These were opening assumptions the players could change.

Dan outlined Robin's priorities: diplomacy with Rosalind, Ganboa, and Catherine; construction logistics; healing where welcome; a smithy, smokehouse, workshop, larger garden, and lasting defenses. Kiya described Celyn's intended temple work, local foraging and terrain study, tracking practice with Warin, and navigation discussions with Izgil. She wanted him to look for workable glassmaking sand, possibly through trade with Ganboa if the Enst supplied it. She also described his intent to send birthday cheese and a letter to his parents, buy green fabric for a later private purpose, and ask Viepuck about displaced halfling tailors. The thread does not itself confirm those purchases or contacts.

The later discussion assigned game facilities and possible helpers. Mike cautioned that magical-item production would remain uncommon even if the party developed workshops. The accepted physical work, recruits, and glassmaking arrangements appear in the played April exchange and Session 29 recap.
