# Asineau as Bastion (Email)

Selected in-world resource and settlement detail from the October 12–25, 2025 bastion discussion. Facility slots, prices assigned for game play, and level-based benefits were mechanics; the building choices were proposals until resolved in later play. Dan Walters's attached [[Asineau - glassworks shop.jpg]] is retained as a preliminary workshop concept, not proof of construction.

Mike described the manor as having about 100 gp in reserves and ordinary income sufficient to support itself. He estimated roughly 800 person-days of owed labor annually, about half already needed for the manorial fields. Existing facilities included a stable managed by Bertram Northwoods, an armory, the Wyrdling temple, and guard housing. The armory had equipment for ten guards; four riding and four draft horses were at the estate. These figures describe the return to Asineau around April 1720 and should not be read as permanent staffing.

The village had two main granaries: one above the mill holding about 2,000 bushels and a communal store near the Wyrdling temple holding a little over 1,000. The manor had a smaller yard granary and hayloft storage, together roughly another 1,200 bushels. Households also stored fish, roots, legumes, and some salted meat. Mike said there was effectively no spare grain reserve that spring; the spring fish run and dairy foods helped bridge the lean period before barley in late July or early August, millet later, and wheat by mid-September. Storage capacity is not the same as food in hand.

Mike said there was small-scale household weaving and pottery, but no full-time village weaver or major kiln. Clay occurred in small deposits along the Enst; most pottery was imported, while leatherwork was done in Cleenseau. Kiya and Dan discussed a garden, smithy, workshop, guard expansion, and durable wall. These were design discussions. The later [[April Around Asineau]] exchange and Session 29 recap establish which projects developed.
