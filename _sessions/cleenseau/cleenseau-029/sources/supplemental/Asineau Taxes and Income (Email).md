Subject: Asineau: Taxes, Income

SimManor - Part 2 of 3 (part 3 will list all the people).

As mentioned, the manor of Asineau is really mostly a subsistence
farming/fishing kind of place, and there are only a handful of people in
town with meaningful coin-based incomes (the ropemaker, a few of the boat
builders, the miller, the guards).

The manor is currently quite cash poor, and the manorial reserves of food
were largely spent supporting the militia. The current assets are:

   - About 100 GP in cash reserves
   - Food for the extended household for about a month and a half
   - A decent supply of arrows, bows, spears, and leather and chain armor,
   left by the militia when they returned to Cleenseau

The standard financial obligations of the manor are to pay the hearth tax,
in March, which is 1 GP / hearth, to the Baron of Aveil (or rather, their
tax collectors) who then remits it to the crown, and to pay an honorus,
effectively a tax due from the lord themself to their feudal lord.

When Robin was appointed lord, the official documents set the honorus at
100 GP / year, but Queen Elaine II declared the first two years'
honorus paid as thanksgiving for Robin's dedicated service to the people of
Sembara. By the time you arrive and take control of the manor, the hearth
tax has also been paid. So there are no taxes due in the short term. (The
nona, a 1/9th tithe of the harvest, is due to be sent - in kind - to the
crown in the fall).

As for income, the old lord's ledgers indicated a decent income, here is a
sample for 1718. It is up to you what of these income streams you want to
count on though, or whether you want to change them. This is more future
looking though, the immediate assets are only the cash reserves above.
