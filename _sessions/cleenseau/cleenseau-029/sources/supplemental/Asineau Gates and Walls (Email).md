# Asineau Gates and Walls (Email)

Dan Walters's October 24, 2025 gatehouse design was explicitly a **conceptual model** of a proposed wooden fortification, not a final plan or evidence of construction. His four attached views are retained as design references: [[Asineau gate tower.jpg]], [[Asineau gate front.jpg]], [[Asineau gate house.jpg]], and [[Asineau gate rear.jpg]].

The concept placed a ditch beside the road to channel attackers toward a gate, with a muddy bottom near the water table and timber reinforcing the ditch edges. A wall of heavy, partly buried logs and earth led to a gatehouse projecting forward. He envisioned overhead openings for defenders, a possible improvised barricade behind the gate, and flanking towers with arrow slits and upper firing positions. The interior side was deliberately less defensible if attackers seized the wall. Dan said the model's slope, stake angles, plank orientation, height, and width needed correction; his target wall height was around twenty feet, while the image showed about ten. He was also considering enclosing the gatehouse front.

On November 6, Dan clarified that the images represented only a roadside gate template. A riverside gate was intended, but he had not designed it. The later historical siege-weapon discussion in the thread was out-of-game commentary, not an Asineau construction decision.
