# Cleenseau Next Steps Take 2 (Email)

Selected campaign-state information from the October 6–8, 2025 planning correspondence. The options for the next adventure and the GM's tentative schedule were out-of-game plans, not events in Taelgar.

Mike Sackton relayed Vahaiya's report that the portal in [[Cleenseau Wood]] remained present and appeared passable from Taelgar into the Feywild. She had seen no evidence of passage from the Feywild into Taelgar since the Duskhound appeared in February, and was unsure whether that creature had used the portal at all. This is a report about conditions around March 20, 1720, not a claim that the portal stayed that way indefinitely.

Dan Walters asked whether the party had done anything about the soul-bound statues in the old elven school at [[Tharwa Fea]]. Mike answered that they had not. Dan said Robin wanted to address them; Artan Eter suggested returning to investigate. This thread does not resolve the statues' fate.
