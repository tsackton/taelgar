# Celyn's Inn Idea (Email)

Selected hospitality proposal and GM response from the October 17–22, 2025 discussion. This correspondence did not build an inn.

Dan said a welcoming inn would express Robin's devotion to the Wanderer through practical hospitality. Kiya suggested starting small, perhaps helping a displaced family operate it, while expecting little initial traffic or profit. Mike agreed that an inn suited the Wanderer and mentioned the established custom of a Wanderer's cot in temples and similar buildings; [[The Wanderer]] already records that custom.

Mike offered two ways forward: build a modest establishment immediately with money, or wait and develop a larger one later. Dan ultimately chose to wait for a better, separate inn and use the manor's great hall for guests in the meantime. The later played record governs whether and when construction began. Mike said an ordinary inn would likely support its keeper before producing substantial profit, and that Asineau's prosperity could grow only gradually within the region's economy.
