# Recurring Festival Idea (Email)

Selected festival proposal and GM response from the October 17–23, 2025 correspondence. A former Play by Email reading copy repeated the authored turns. Neither this proposal nor the mechanics discussion establishes that every proposed contest or annual fair took place.

Dan Walters proposed that Robin invite Cleenseau, Taviose, Auloutte, Valit, Beury, and Ganboa to recurring regional celebrations, with cooking, archery, mock combat, music, and a market. The purpose was cooperation, mutual defense, and trade. On October 23, Dan said Robin would personally invite the lizardfolk to the first festival.

Mike Sackton replied on October 21 that the proposal was generally well received, but that Robin and Cleenseau's residents would tie it to the existing holiday calendar. Some village festivals could open to the whole region, and some shared holidays could rotate among villages. He thought this might yield five or six regional fairs each year; the number was an estimate, not a settled schedule. Ritual holidays such as Pyravela and Viatela were ill suited to this treatment. The temples traditionally funded many feast days.

The first documented Asineau festival and Ganboa's involvement appear in [[Cleenseau - Session 29]]. The later record governs what occurred.
