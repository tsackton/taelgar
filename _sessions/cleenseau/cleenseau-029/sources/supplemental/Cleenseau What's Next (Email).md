# Cleenseau What's Next (Email)

Selected campaign-state information from the October 5–6, 2025 planning correspondence. Character intentions and proposed adventures below are not completed actions.

Mike Sackton set the in-world date at March 20, 1720. Queen [[Elaine II]] had asked the party to be in the Cleenseau region in October for her visit. The visit later appears in [[Cleenseau - Session 31]]. The guards taking the former Baroness into custody appeared competent; Mike said the party did not have to accompany her to ensure she was guarded.

The party discussed returning to [[Asineau]] for Robin's new lordship and a period of rest and rebuilding. Celyn proposed working with the Wyrdling temple, training with Warin in tracking, and learning navigation from Izgil. Izgil wanted to research his moon bracers and other magical items and eventually learn more about his connection with Ethlenn. These were stated interests, not established downtime outcomes.

Mike mentioned several unresolved leads: the awakened orb in the Cleenseau watchtower ruins, Narissa somewhere to the south as far as the party knew, and reports of an item sought by parts of the Army of the South south of the Enst. He presented these as possible leads, without resolving their truth or outcome.

Kiya Nicoll explained that Celyn recognizes his birthday by spring crocuses rather than a calendar date. Her suggestion that he might spot them on the journey home and send a gift to his parents remained conditional here.
