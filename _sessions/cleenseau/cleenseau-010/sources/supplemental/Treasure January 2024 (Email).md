There is a small community of experienced smith's in Cleenseau, and in fact
Brot's (remember them, the dwarf with the telescope) wife is an
accomplished smith. It would be pretty straightforward to find someone to
make barding, but they would need money for raw materials and for their
time. Ida (the captain of the local garrison) is back in town and would
certainly be happy to provide some funds for barding out of the garrison
coffers, in exchange for the plate and shield.

Four basic options for barding:

* Simple chain/leather barding, the equivalent of a chain shirt. AC 13 /
costs 200 gold / 7 days
* Heavier chain and scale armor, the equivalent of scale mail, AC 14 /
disadvantage on stealth / 200 gold /  7 days
* Well fitted leather and plate, covering vital areas,  the equivalent of
breastplate, AC 14 / costs 1600 gold /  60 days
* Well fitted leather and plate, covering most areas, the equivalent of
half-plate, AC 15 / disadvantage on stealth / 3000 gold / 120 days

If Izgil spends a couple of hours each day directing the activity and
casting mending and such you can reduce the time by 20%. You could also
hire extra smiths to help, each smith you hire costs 5 gold / day and
reduces the time by 30%. If you got Izgil to help and hired 2 smiths (at a
cost of 15 gold each) you could have either of the 200 gold options done in
3 days.

Ida will, by default, provide 1000 gold towards costs but no more than 3
smiths. You can try to convince her of the urgency to get more funding.

*Important Note*: Barding does not stack with natural armor. Doesn't mean
it isn't worthwhile, but your steed's AC will go up as you level. At 9th it
will be 13, at 13th 14, and at 17th 15. So at some point it becomes less
useful.
