[ gaming ] The existential question of what to do when there isn't a crisis

Published: 2026-08-02T19:44:00

A quiet session mostly setting up a bit of an interstitial period in game.

When we left off we had just had an audience with Ethlenn, the Archfey protector of Tyrwingha.


As we left our audience we found ourselves in an orchard with a bunch of tables set up in amongst some stone buildings, and Izgil got deeply engaged in a debate about apple pie construction with a couple of brownies before we wound up settling in for a pleasant light meal with various locals. Viepuck wound up talking apple trees with an elderly satyr (Old Briarthorn? Something like, it has fallen out of my head) and came out of it with a couple of seeds and having mind-probed an apple tree. Robin was uncomfortable because Robin has fey-related PTSD and thus dealing with a fey woman who really really wants him to have a nice time was distressing him.

Eventually we got information about other areas to go to and decided to move along. We still have this collection of trophies we got from the redcap's castle and we would like to reunite them with their people, so we went to the part of the area that was basically the forum symposium conversation running more simultaneous tracks than a Worldcon. Viepuck tried to start a conversation, which didn't work very well, but we did wind up drifting into the Magical Creatures animal discussion (Celyn tried to help find something sensible but Everything Is Simultaneously Shiny And Too Intellectual For Him so he did not, but Izgil managed to home in on this by sheer power of nerdery).

We segued in on a conversation about dragons— given we had a conversation with one. "Oh, most people don't survive a conversation with a dragon, which one?" "... well, he didn't survive the conversation with us...." Then we got some conversation in about the bits we found, including possible provenance for two feathers, one of which is potentially from an allied region and one of which is probably from a place We Do Not Speak Of. We determined that the unicorn horn belongs to a still-living unicorn, and Izgil tried to use the horn to locate the rest of the creature, but the protections on Twilight's Grace foxed his signal, basically. Not here, on the other side of the Sunset Gate. (Viepuck tried to find the owner of the satyr hoof we found but the spell fizzled less-informatively.)

We got tips on how to find the magic nerds who might help Izgil figure out how to tinker with his spell to get a better read and wandered around until we located the naiad who was holding forth upon the source of magic as being an ocean. Celyn dropped in with commentary on innate/learned magic versus opening the door to another's magic (he does both kinds) and made it comprehensible to the audience by commenting on Ethlenn's warlocks as an example of the latter. This conversation opened the way for Izgil to start a nerd duel with the naiad (who did snark to Celyn early on "He's not as smart as you are, huh?") but they fought over the theory of magic at some length, and Viepuck made a play that got her to explain that the waters of a particular famous portal would penetrate the obscuring stuff. When it came out that she was not, herself, a spellcaster, Robin near lost it laughing and had to take a walk. (Celyn feels that if Robin has the giggles that is a vastly superior state of Robin and his stress levels.)

After some pleasant wrangling we took our leave of the symposium and wandered to the central portal, where Izgil got information about where the unicorn was. We then tried to figure out what to do next. Izgil would love to hang out here and wrangle academic arguments and do research forever; Celyn would love to go to the part of the city where one has Random Social Encounters because he is pretty sure that giving fate and chance that sort of opening would do wonders for his obsessions; collectively we would like to restore these stolen body parts if possible, but that is not an urgent question. Robin would like to be not in the Feywild.

Given we are confident that we can return here by visiting Tafolwern from the mortal-world side given how much the Queen likes us, we decided that our next steps were to return to said mortal world and aim ourselves in the direction of the elves we had sent dragon parts to to see what they had done with them, as the custom would be that they would pay us for this bounty in some sort of interesting stuff. The portal closest to the elven realm of Orenlas runs through a fairy vineyard (and it is a common bit of lore where Celyn comes from that people go questing for the mortal-side version of the portal, never find it, and come back roaring drunk), which means that when the rest of the party took a long rest, Celyn had a nap and then nerded out ferociously with the local fey about winemaking.

The vineyard portal brought us out in the hills along the southern border of Tyrwingha and when we got back north out of the wilderness it turned out we were not hugely far from Clawyn, the village Celyn comes from. This meant going to Clawyn, turning up not long before a major festival, staying at least for the festival (but not the one it would be Traditional to stay for, which would require staying a month and change; Celyn's mother is Disappointed), and then heading around the end of the mountain range on our hike towards Orenlas.

We spooked an owlbear while hiking, but it sensibly ran away and we let it rather than indulge Greymalkin's desire for a romp and impromptu feast.
