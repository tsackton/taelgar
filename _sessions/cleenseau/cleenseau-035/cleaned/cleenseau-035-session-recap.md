Leaving Ethlenn's audience, the party shares a meal in Orchard Close while Izgil debates pie with [[Pip (brownie)|Pip]] and [[Moss (brownie)|Moss]] and Viepuck discusses apple trees with Old Bramble.

#### Intermediate

#### Long
As the party leaves its audience with [[Archfey Ethlenn|Ethlenn]], they find themselves in [[Orchard Close]], an orchard with tables set among stone buildings. [[Izgil Moonseeker|Izgil]] becomes deeply engaged in a debate about apple-pie construction with [[Pip (brownie)|Pip]] and [[Moss (brownie)|Moss]] before everyone settles in for a pleasant light meal with the locals. [[Viepuck]] talks about apple trees with the elderly satyr [[Old Bramble]], receives a couple of seeds, and mind-probes an apple tree. [[Robin of Abenfyrd|Robin]], already uncomfortable among the fey, finds [[Dame Elowen|Dame Elowen's]] determination that he have a nice time distressing.

### recap-002 | The Scholars of Ash Court

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: beat-002
- Date: around 1720-12-01
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Twilight's Grace, Ash Court, Twilight's Pool
- NPCs: Neris
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party consults scholars about Ianto's trophies, learns that the unicorn whose horn was taken remains alive beyond the Sunset Gate, and uses Twilight's Pool to locate it.

#### Intermediate
In Ash Court, the party joins a discussion of magical creatures and obtains possible provenances for two feathers recovered from Ianto's castle. Izgil confirms that the unicorn horn's owner is alive, then Neris explains how the waters of Twilight's Pool can penetrate the protections obscuring its location.

#### Long
The party still carries a collection of trophies from the redcap's castle and wants to reunite them with their people, so they move on to [[Ash Court]], where a symposium runs more simultaneous conversations than anyone can follow. They drift into a discussion of magical creatures and then into a conversation about dragons. An unnamed fey scholar observes, "Oh, most people don't survive a conversation with a dragon, which one?" Celyn answers, "... well, he didn't survive the conversation with us...."

The scholars suggest possible provenances for two feathers: one may come from an allied region, while the other probably comes from a place they do not speak of. The party determines that the unicorn horn belongs to a still-living unicorn. Izgil tries to locate the rest of the creature, but the protections on [[Twilight's Grace]] obscure his spell; the unicorn is not there, but somewhere beyond the [[Sunset Gate]]. Viepuck tries to find the owner of the satyr hoof, but the spell fails less informatively.

Seeking help with the spell, the party finds [[Neris]], a naiad holding forth on the source of magic as an ocean. Celyn comments on innate and learned magic compared with opening the door to another's magic, using Ethlenn's warlocks as an example. Neris remarks to Celyn, "He's not as smart as you are, huh?" She and Izgil argue over magical theory at some length, and Viepuck draws out her explanation that the waters of a particular famous portal can penetrate the obscuring protections. When it emerges that Neris is not herself a spellcaster, Robin nearly loses himself laughing and has to take a walk.

After some pleasant wrangling, the party leaves the symposium and goes to [[Twilight's Pool]], where Izgil learns where the unicorn is. They consider what to do next. Izgil would happily remain among the academic arguments and research, while Robin would like to be out of the Feywild. Restoring the stolen body parts matters, but is not urgent.

### recap-003 | The Silver Vineyard

- Kind: travel
- Beat IDs: beat-003
- Date: before 1720-12-11
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Twilight's Grace, Silver Vineyard
- NPCs: Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party decides to return to the mortal world and rests in the Silver Vineyard before taking its shifting portal into southern Tyrwingha.

#### Intermediate
Confident that Ethlenn's favor will let them return to Twilight's Grace through Tafolwern, the party chooses to leave for Orenlas. They rest in the Silver Vineyard, where Celyn discusses winemaking with the local fey, then pass through its portal into the southern Tyrwinghan hills.

#### Long
Confident that they can return to [[Twilight's Grace]] through [[Tafolwern]], the party decides to return to the mortal world and head toward [[Orenlas]], where they previously sent dragon remains and may be owed an interesting bounty. The nearest portal to Orenlas runs through the [[Silver Vineyard]]. It is common lore in Celyn's homeland that people go questing for the mortal side of this portal, never find it, and return roaring drunk. While the rest of the party takes a long rest, Celyn naps and then talks ferociously with the local fey about winemaking. The vineyard portal brings the party out in the hills along the southern border of [[Tyrwingha]].

### recap-004 | Homecoming in Clawyn

- Kind: travel
- Beat IDs: beat-004
- Date: 1720-12-11 to 1720-12-12
- Time: unknown
- Locations: Clawyn, Tyrwinghan Hills
- NPCs: Olwen Bettws, Greymalkin, Es*tiasilos
- Organizations: none
- Items: none
- Enemies: none

#### Short
The party reaches Clawyn, stays through Viatela, and then resumes the journey toward Orenlas without provoking a startled owlbear.

#### Intermediate
After emerging from the Silver Vineyard, the party travels north to Clawyn and remains for the Viatela homecoming festival. They then head around the mountains toward Orenlas, allowing an owlbear startled on the trail to escape.

#### Long
When the party returns north out of the wilderness, they discover they are not far from [[Clawyn]], the village Celyn comes from. They go to Clawyn, arriving not long before [[Viatela]], and remain through the festival. They do not stay for the later festival that tradition would demand, which would require another month and more; [[Olwen Bettws|Celyn's mother]] is disappointed. The day after Viatela, the party heads around the end of the mountain range on the hike toward [[Orenlas]]. They startle an owlbear while traveling, but it sensibly runs away, and they let it go rather than indulging Greymalkin's desire for a romp and impromptu feast.
