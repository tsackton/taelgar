# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 5
- Beat Coverage: 10/10

## scene-001 | Four Dragonets on Circular Island

- Beat IDs: beat-001
- Rationale: The opening introduces the island, the four characters, and their two existing friendships before the festival preparations begin.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Four Dragonets on Circular Island: Four dragonets introduce their friendships, talents, and private frustrations on the Circular Island before the Jubilee.

## scene-002 | Preparing Xena's Performance

- Beat IDs: beat-002, beat-003, beat-004
- Rationale: Recruiting Heska, rehearsing the duet, and trading for jewelry are linked parts of preparing Xena's performance; the visit to Zare stays within this broader preparation scene.

### Beats

- beat-002 | Xena Changes the Plan: Xena demands dazzling jewelry and insists that the reluctant Heska join her Jubilee performance.
- beat-003 | Heska's Reluctant Rehearsal: Sculpit coaches Heska through a successful whip-catching rehearsal and helps turn it into a duet with Xena.
- beat-004 | Pebbles for Jewels: Little Tony trades Edmund to Zare for jewelry after finding an unusually harmonious team of gems for Xena.

## scene-003 | Triumphs and Disasters at the Jubilee

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006
- Rationale: The opening ceremony, Heska's disastrous debut, and Zeno's rap-battle victory form the public festival sequence, before the friends withdraw for dinner.

### Beats

- beat-005 | One Family, One Disastrous Duet: The Jubilee opens with a ceremony of unity, followed by a disastrous duet that Xena salvages after Heska's whip strikes her.
- beat-006 | Zeno Faces Rosca: Zeno defeats Rosca in two rounds of a call-and-response contest and celebrates with his friends.

## scene-004 | Dinner and the Strange Silence

- Beat IDs: beat-007, beat-008
- Rationale: The shared meal is interrupted by unusual sensations, leading directly to an aerial search and the flight to the Wanderer; end at arrival before examining the egg.

### Beats

- beat-007 | The Feast Turns Strange: The friends share a meal above the festival until silence, music, wild magic, and a moving shadow interrupt them.
- beat-008 | Following the Silence: The group follows Little Tony's silence and Zeno's inner note to a glowing object above the Wanderer's palm.

## scene-005 | The Egg and the Meaning of Home

- Beat IDs: beat-009, beat-010
- Rationale: Keep the examination, embraces, shared vision, and temporary changes in abilities together as the encounter with the egg on the palm.

### Beats

- beat-009 | The Egg Says Home: Unable to open or read the egg, the dragonets discover that touching it evokes home and begins to illuminate its engravings.
- beat-010 | A Shared Vision of Home: Embracing the egg gives the four dragonets a shared vision and briefly heightened abilities before the Wanderer's eye lights blue.
