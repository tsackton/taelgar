## Four Dragonets on Circular Island
*beat-001*

**Date**: unknown
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Four dragonets introduce their friendships, talents, and private frustrations on the Circular Island before the Jubilee.
**Long Summary**: Eight immense statues shelter distinct dragonet communities on the Circular Island, whose annual Jubilee brings them together. Little Tony hears objects clamoring for attention and finds quiet on the Wanderer's palm; he recruits his friend Sculpit into a fictitious-girlfriend story to discourage Glenda, his infatuated former seagull familiar. Sculpit prefers coaching performers to taking the spotlight and needs costume materials for his star client. In the Dark Lady's neighborhood, Zeno pitches names for a comedy club while Heska quietly pranks him. Heska is a skilled hunter who feeds the community, famously catching a giant fiddler crab, while Little Tony prizes unusual finds, including an award-winning butt-shaped pebble.
**Location**:
- Circular Island: Introductions alternate between the Wanderer's palm and the Dark Lady's neighborhood.
  Note: The palm is a quiet refuge for Little Tony and Sculpit. Little Tony has also found that objects fall silent near the storm surrounding the island.
**NPCs**:
- Glenda the Seagull (mentioned): Little Tony's former familiar. She fell in love with Little Tony; he describes an invented girlfriend to discourage her, with Sculpit helping maintain the story.
- Xena (mentioned): Sculpit's unnamed star client in this introduction. Sculpit is anxious about finding materials for her performance costume; she is named in the following beat.
**Items**:
- Giant fiddler crab shell (mentioned): Heska recalls catching a crab twice a dragonet's size. Its shell has a rainbow pattern like crashing waves, and he has promised it as decoration once Zeno settles on a club name.
- Edmund (mentioned): Little Tony recalls a butt-shaped pebble that earned third place in a finding competition last year; he names the same pebble Edmund when trading it later.
**Organizations**:
- Dark Lady community (encountered): Heska and Zeno live in the neighborhood built into the statue's robes; Heska brings food for its communal meals.

## The Egg Says Home

**Date**: unknown
**Time Window**: night
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Unable to open or read the egg, the dragonets discover that touching it evokes home and begins to illuminate its engravings.
**Long Summary**: On the Wanderer's palm, Heska knocks on the egg with Mage Hand and tests it with his whip; its unfamiliar material resembles neither ordinary stone nor metal. No opening or thoughts are detected. Contact evokes warmth and belonging; Little Tony hears home, and the engravings near his embrace begin glowing.
