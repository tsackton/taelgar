## scene-001 | The Wanderer’s First Steps

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002, beat-003
- Rationale: The opening recap and character reflections establish the same cockpit situation in which the party learns coordinated control; the practice continues until an outside summons changes their objective.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Firstborn and Familiar Seats: An audience-only glimpse beyond the storm identifies four Firstborn signatures before the dragonets reflect on their new powers inside the Wanderer.
- beat-002 | Learning to Move Together: The dragonets learn that shared intent can move the Wanderer’s toes, but cannot yet control it away from their seats or summon a manual.
- beat-003 | The Wanderer’s First Steps: The group unlocks the Wanderer’s sight, walks it into the sea, and discovers how each dragonet contributes to its control.
