#### Short
Inside the Wanderer, Heska, Sculpit, Little Tony, and Zeno learn to move together, taking the ancient statue into the shallows as the islanders watch.

#### Intermediate
Heska, Sculpit, Little Tony, and Zeno discover that the Wanderer responds when their intentions align: first its toes move, then its eyes open to them, and finally it walks. With magic and encouragement helping them cooperate, they take the statue into the sea and experiment with its hands and underwater sight. After at least an hour of practice, they begin to understand their different contributions to its control.

#### Long
Beyond the storm, two travelers detect four unmistakable Firstborn signatures. In the statue’s heart, Heska, Sculpit, Little Tony, and Zeno settle into seats that feel strangely familiar. Their first attempts pull in different directions, but agreeing to wiggle the toes produces a shared sensation of stiffness and the statue’s first movement. Leaving their seats breaks their control, and no instruction manual appears when they try to summon one.

Little Tony suggests looking through the statue’s eyes. Working together opens that view, and Sculpit learns to switch between it and his own sight. Walking takes more practice: Enhance Ability and Zeno’s encouragement help turn tentative foot movements into a journey into the shallows, watched by an astonished Oru, Roska, and the other islanders. The Wanderer scoops up the seabed, peers beneath the water, and lifts a boulder. Over at least an hour, the four begin to recognize their roles: Tony translates their shared intention, Heska guides the body, Sculpit brings its power into use, and Zeno draws the parts into harmony.

#### Short
Oru calls them ashore to meet Aksha and Shai, who revere them as Firstborn; an offer of sanctuary becomes an emergency evacuation when the visitors detect an approaching dreadnought.

#### Intermediate
Oru interrupts their practice with news of a vessel that has fallen near the Dark Lady. Little Tony’s telepathy bridges the language barrier with its occupants, Aksha and Shai, whose reverence for the Firstborn meets the dragonets’ warm embrace, restaurant plans, and offer of a home. The travelers describe hardship beyond the storm and warn that the party’s awakening has drawn dangerous attention. When their scanner confirms an imminent attack, Oru sends the islanders into shelter and the four return to the Wanderer as a dreadnought emerges through the storm.

#### Long
Oru reaches Tony telepathically with news of something that has fallen from the sky near the Dark Lady. The four bring the Wanderer along the shore, but attempts to speak through it yield noises and gestures. At Oru’s insistence they park the statue and fly out through its eye, warning curious dragonets away from the dangerous interior.

The newcomers are Aksha, who piloted their small vessel through the storm, and Shai, a bearded man with a topknot. Tony opens telepathic communication, and the dragonets embrace them. The visitors weep, bow, and call the four Firstborn. Their urgent questions about what has changed on the island keep becoming tangled in accounts of performances, Roska, food, and the party’s proposed restaurant. Sculpit uses Minor Illusion to show the egg and the vision they shared; Tony asks the visitors to explain themselves in return. Hearing how hard life is beyond the storm, the dragonets offer them—and others like them—a home. The visitors call the island a holy place, overwhelmed by its abundance and the welcome.

Their warning soon overtakes the conversation: the dragonets’ awakening has made them detectable to terrible enemies. Shai retrieves a scanner, and its readings suggest they have only five or ten minutes. Oru tells Roska to get the islanders into the central temple; Zeno also suggests the underground Groundlings Cave monastery. Aksha and Shai return to their vessel while the party flies back to the Wanderer. A huge dreadnought breaks through the storm, carrying the same sense of despair the four remember from their vision.

#### Short
They fight the dreadnought and its escort fighters with the Wanderer’s growing powers, finally bringing the warship down with a leaping stone-maul strike, but the village is damaged and the visitors’ craft is shot down.

#### Intermediate
Aksha and Shai engage the dreadnought’s escort fighters while the dragonets combine their spells with the Wanderer’s newly discovered eye lasers. Sculpit’s mirror images and Zeno’s wall of sound protect the statue, Heska doubles its size, and Tony helps locate a weakness that lets their giant fist break the warship’s shield. Lightning, compelled gestures, and another punch disrupt the attackers and tear out the dreadnought’s pilot, but enemy fire damages the village and brings down their allies’ craft. At last the four coordinate a leap onto the warship, smashing it with Tony’s conjured stone maul before destroying the remaining fighter.

#### Long
Four escort fighters converge on Aksha and Shai’s vessel while the Wanderer faces the dreadnought. Sculpit catches a fighter in a wind-like Bigby’s Hand, Zeno strikes another with Synaptic Static, and Heska discovers the statue’s eye lasers. An enemy beam tears into the Wanderer’s shoulder. Tony disables one of the warship’s guns with Dispel Magic, then helps the collective laser strike land against its shield.

Sculpit casts Mirror Image over the giant statue and crushes the fighter in his grasp. Zeno covers its chest with a Wall of Force expressed as a roaring wall of sound, and Heska doubles the Wanderer’s size. A dreadnought blast devastates the village square and nearby homes. Following Glenda’s advice to be forceful, Tony questions the hostile ship and identifies a vulnerable point behind its central iris. The enlarged Wanderer drives a fist into that point and breaks the shield.

Sculpit’s lightning cuts through another fighter into the exposed warship, while Zeno draws a gun crew into answering the Wanderer’s gestures and sends its attack into the sea. Mirror images take cannon blasts, and Heska discovers a shield that can cover part of the statue. With Hunter’s Pounce, the four punch through the iris, pull out the unidentified pilot, and accidentally crush it in their hand. The dreadnought loses its bearings, but an escort shoots down Aksha and Shai’s already battered vessel, cutting off their voices as more fire strikes the village.

Sculpit restrains the remaining fighter and lashes the warship with lightning; Zeno again distracts its gunners. Heska discovers the Wanderer can jump, and Tony shapes Wall of Stone into a gigantic maul. This time all four synchronize perfectly. The enlarged statue leaps onto the dreadnought and smashes through its cockpit, sending the ship crashing onto the shore. The last fighter is quickly destroyed.

#### Short
Little Tony saves Shai but cannot revive Aksha; the party then rescues many captives and about twenty eggs from the exploding dreadnought, before Shai warns that they must leave the island.

#### Intermediate
Little Tony rushes to the fallen craft, where Revivify fails to restore Aksha but Healing Word saves Shai. Sensing hundreds of childlike beings inside the dreadnought, the party enters a specimen vault filled with unfamiliar captives and eggs resembling their own. With an explosion imminent, Sculpit teleports eggs into the Wanderer, Heska enlarges him to carry more, Tony builds a giant dragonet from wreckage to break open cages, and Zeno carries a captive to safety. They save many beings and about twenty eggs, though not everyone; after their escape, Shai warns that the enemies now know where to find them and they cannot stay.

#### Long
Tony flies straight to the travelers’ wreck, hoping to revive them. Aksha is dead, and Revivify has no effect; Shai is barely alive, but Healing Word restores him. Tony then senses hundreds of beings inside the fallen dreadnought, with the impression that they are children. Shai cannot explain why a warship would carry them, so the dragonets go to investigate.

They find a specimen vault holding unfamiliar creatures in cages and eggs that resemble dragonet eggs. The captives plead for help while Tony senses the reactor approaching an explosion. With only a few rounds to act, Sculpit begins teleporting eggs into the Wanderer; Heska enlarges him so he can carry more at once. Tony uses Summon Draconic Spirit to assemble a huge dragonet from the wreckage and break open cages. Zeno picks out a creature he finds especially endearing and carries it away.

The four rescue many captives and about twenty eggs, but cannot save everyone. They make their final escape as the dreadnought explodes. Returning to Shai, they hear his warning: their enemies know where they are, and will keep finding them if they remain. They cannot stay on the island.
