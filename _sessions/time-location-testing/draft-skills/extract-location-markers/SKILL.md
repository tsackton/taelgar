# Extract location markers — experimental draft

This uninstalled draft produces **one file containing a place dictionary and one marker list**. Read [the location-marker format](references/format.md) before authoring. It replaces the location A/B requirements in the old shared experimental contract. During this experiment all generated files stay under `_sessions/time-location-testing`; no installation or live-bundle writes. Existing pilot outputs and builders retain their old format and must not be overwritten as part of a new extraction.

Resolve the YAML-registered polished transcript and fingerprint source/YAML before reading. Preserve source-local UIDs and complete shared-UID turn boundaries. A time-marker file is optional; if referencing one, bind to its revision and verify that it uses the same session and transcript revision. Location extraction does not require a scene map, a time extraction, or a time assignment for every line.

Read the complete transcript sequentially, including combat, lore, and chatter, before calling extraction complete; reread truncated output. Use search as a supplementary checklist. Resolve accepted proposals, retcons, canceled detours, compressed returns, pronouns, and changes of viewpoint or actor group from context. Do not replace played evidence with a recap or private DM plans. Record any concrete source or reading limitation that affects the extraction in optional top-level `limitations`; do not include reading-window logs or review-status fields.

Author source-ordered markers of **presence**, **movement**, and relevant **observation**, with actors, concise interpretation, exact evidence, and supported structured fields. A journey is a movement marker at the scale narrated: five transcript lines covering 200 miles may yield one marker. Preserve stated endpoints, route/waypoints, mode, distance, duration, and completion without inventing daily positions or intermediate visits. Distance and transcript length do not determine marker count. Split when independently useful activity, actor changes, destination changes, or distinct movements warrant it; avoid a tactical trace of every step. A brief stop can stay inside the route. A meaningful stop can have its own presence marker linked to the journey without manufacturing daily travel legs.

A completed movement establishes arrival; do not generate a duplicate presence marker from that same evidence. Later descriptions of the same ongoing location can strengthen or clarify the existing record without creating another marker. Add a marker for a meaningful spatial or actor/context change, a distinct stop not already represented, or a return; repeated place mentions or substantial conversation alone do not require one. “Toward Tokra” establishes no arrival. Separate plans, interrupted travel, observations through thresholds, and magical views from physical presence. Track split actors independently; speaking in a passage does not prove a character is present, and different actors do not prove concurrency. Record observed subjects separately from observers only when their identity matters to interpreting the location observation; do not inventory everyone visible or build an NPC whereabouts record. Preserve corrections without silently choosing between unresolved contradictory accounts.

Use `reading` for the interpretation, quantity `basis` for why a value is supported, and optional `note` only for a material qualification not already expressed. Apply the same restraint to place and relationship notes. Do not repeat explanations across fields or put generic extraction instructions in the output; preserve meaningful location detail and evidence.

Use the place dictionary for identity and necessary physical context. Reuse local IDs for evidence-supported repeated references, including unnamed rooms and camps. Include only places needed to understand the markers; incidental route features can remain text. Optional typed place relationships describe supported enduring context, such as a library within a tower or a camp near a town. Route direction belongs on the movement marker; a ship's temporary harbor location belongs on a marker, never as its permanent geographic parent. Retain doorway/stair topology only when useful for interpreting movement, not to reconstruct an entire building. Do not invent coordinates, floor numbers, metric geometry, or relationships from unavailable map gestures.

For a vault identity, search filenames, names/aliases, and variants; read the candidate note and surrounding source passage. Preserve session-era labels. Similar names are not identity; modern geography does not establish played movement or session-era spatial relationships. Omit `vaultPath` when unresolved and record material ambiguity in a note. Do not create, rename, merge, or move canonical notes, or force every local place to have a vault match.

Recheck actor scope, journey granularity, arrivals, stops, returns, bypasses, magical transport, frame changes, and planned departures. Verify JSON structure, source hashes, exact quotations, whole-turn UID boundaries, place/marker references, and containment cycles. Ensure observations and plans generate no physical visits, begun travel generates no destination arrival, and mobile places have no permanent geographic parent inferred from a temporary stop. Structural checks do not certify semantic completeness. Existing A/B builders neither extract arbitrary transcripts nor validate this revised format.

Deliver `location-markers.json` in the agreed experimental output directory, with a concise handoff of material identity, actor, or route uncertainty. No separate visits, journeys, observations, plans, or spatial-graph collections; no mandatory HTML/table artifact. Preserve human corrections and stable IDs on revision; recheck dependent claims when evidence changes rather than replacing hashes to pass checks. Record uncertainty without stopping over incidental wording. Stop before NPC whereabouts publication, campaign-map construction, scene partitioning, time/YAML changes, canonical geography edits, or skill installation unless separately requested.
