## Review notes

- Opening u0001–u0013 recaps the previous adventure’s departure and is explicitly excluded rather than creating duplicate encounters; this also avoids resolving the uncertain ASR name {{Phoeba}} from a recap fragment.
- The unnamed sailor in the hold is retained as a specific conversational partner; no name or identity link to another crew member is invented.
- Excluded generic pirates (including the crow’s-nest combatant), unnamed crew/crowds, masked wizards as a group, generic monsters and hypothetical creatures. Their scenes remain available.
- The session note and manifest place arrival on 1748-10-12, but u0944–u0947 says eleven days since departure on September 30. October 12 is retained as the documented date, pending chronology review.
- Pre-lunch timing at Wahacha is relative to the explicit lunch at u1204; no exact arrival hour is asserted.
- Pearl Brightmoon is the first mate; Corrin Wildheart is the navigator. Canonical surnames are identity metadata, not claims that Wellby heard them spoken in these passages.
- Baxter’s dock record is mentioned: Wellby discusses his present companion with Makha, without directly acting on Baxter. Mentioned never means definitely absent.
- Some apparent NPC dialogue is labeled Wellby in the source (for example u1041 and u1171); excerpts preserve those labels and summaries rely on the surrounding exchange and session note.
- Rose is observed, not assumed to have conversed with Wellby simply because she is introduced and performs music.
- No next-morning scene is invented: the session ends while arranging that future meeting. No individual crew member is credited with language lessons in the voyage montage.
- Scene indexes belong to this extraction; canonical note hashes are included for identity resolution, but hidden/future lore from those notes is not included in interactions.
- Version 2 retest: full conversational encounters replace highlight-only bounds; original segmentation is retained for stable scene joins. NPC cards carry one session overview and a featured attributed quote where available.
- Makha’s seven former scene records become one continuous guided visit spanning scenes 14–20. Wella’s dinner conversation stays together through Corrin’s report and topic changes. Explicit idle presence now covers Baxter on deck and at introductions, and Wella remaining on deck.
