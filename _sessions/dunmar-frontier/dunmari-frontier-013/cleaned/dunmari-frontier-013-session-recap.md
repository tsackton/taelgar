#### Short
After breakfast, the party searches Raven’s Hold, finding old treasures, a recent weapons stockpile, and signs that the valley’s corruption originates near the river.

#### Intermediate
With the gnolls defeated, the party searches the barracks before freeing the captive animals. An abandoned nest yields valuables and a locket that awakens Wellby’s memory of a three-headed beast, while the intact building holds enough recent weapons for nearly a hundred soldiers. Kenzo opens a storeroom of old Blackened Claw supplies. As Riswynn examines the finds, Delwath traces the strongest extraplanar energy to the river crossing below.

#### Long
Finishing a late breakfast amid the cries of captive animals, the party decides to investigate further before releasing them into the corrupted valley. Seeker, Kenzo, and Riswynn search the ruined barracks, where old burn marks and a great abandoned nest suggest a former monstrous occupant. While Seeker studies the exposed rock, Kenzo and Riswynn recover a gold ring, a silver locket, two pieces of amber, a glowing ruby, and an elaborate silver helm. Below lie the gnolls’ filthy sleeping quarters. In the intact building, Wellby, Delwath, and Vola find Blackened Claw graffiti and a much newer stockpile of mixed weapons and armor, enough for eighty to a hundred soldiers; some pieces bear a bloodied wolf’s head.

When the groups reunite, Kenzo picks the storeroom lock that defeated Wellby, and Riswynn mends the matching broken key. Wellby recognizes the locket through shared halfling memory: its owner died roughly a century ago when a flying beast with dragon, goat, and lion heads attacked. Whether that beast also drove out the hobgoblins remains uncertain. The storeroom contains rusted armor, potions, sealed flasks, and a strangely well-preserved standard whose claw emblem seems to flex. Vola’s interest in the helm earns a sharp rebuff from Wellby, and she leaves to watch the valley. Delwath returns from his own examination with a more urgent discovery: the courtyard mists echo an extraplanar presence near the river. Seeker, meanwhile, suspects the local ley lines converge at the dwarven fort.

#### Short
They divide the magical finds and read records of the hobgoblin occupation, while Delwath reveals the messages he has seen in shadow.

#### Intermediate
Riswynn’s identification gives Seeker a gem containing a fire elemental, Delwath the Helm of Iron Will, and Kenzo the Spear of the War Leader; she takes a scroll of Lesser Restoration. Delwath reads records of a fertile valley captured by the hobgoblins after the Great War, then joins the others in gathering potions, armor, and the fear-inducing standard. He also shares a shadow message urging him to seek hidden truths, connecting its language with his long imprisonment.

#### Long
Riswynn explains the treasures’ powers as the group sorts its discoveries. The ruby holds a fire elemental and goes to Seeker; the Helm of Iron Will, which protects against mental attacks, suits Delwath better than Kenzo. Kenzo keeps the Dunmari Spear of the War Leader, whose projected voice can rally allies against fear. Delwath identifies an old spell scroll as Lesser Restoration and passes it to Riswynn.

Turning to the hobgoblin logbook and scouting papers, Delwath finds accounts of the Blackened Claw’s advance after the Great War. Raven’s Hold was then a fertile, defensible Dunmari stronghold; the hobgoblins took it and tunneled toward a dwarven fort whose original inhabitants were already gone. The papers offer no answer to how their own occupation ended. The party loads rusted half plate onto the floating disk and takes the illusion-enchanted standard and eight potions. Four are healing potions, three for Kenzo and one for Delwath; the others seem to provide concentrated nourishment.

Before they leave, Delwath admits that words appeared to him in the shadows, speaking of hidden meaning between darkness and light and of messages reaching where voices cannot. He is uneasy about the power that lets him read them. He says he remembers little of Shadow-kar, but recognizes the language of the realm where he was trapped around the time of Cha’mutte’s fall; he has been free for only six or seven months.

#### Short
Following the tainted water from the courtyard into the valley, they find an Abyssal summoning circle beside the obstructed river and decide to destroy it.

#### Intermediate
Delwath bottles oily, foul-smelling water with Mage Hand before the party follows its stronger magical source down to the bridge. Clean water falls into the pool above, but emerges below as a sluggish stream choked with red vines. Wellby spots the runes, and Delwath translates a command binding something for a month and a day. Another shadow message warns of a waiting desert wind. With the source apparently before them, Delwath prepares to scratch out the circle.

#### Long
The party first examines the courtyard pool, where mists collect beside the ruined forge. Delwath uses Mage Hand to fill empty potion vials with water bearing an oily sheen and the stench of rot, sulfur, and burnt flesh. Ideas about testing the contamination on animals give way to plans for their rescue, but the group wants to remove the danger before setting them free. With Vola, they descend the fortified switchback road toward the bridge.

Near noon, the contrast is plain: a clear waterfall feeds the pool upstream, while vegetation and mist obstruct the outlet and the river below crawls through bruised red vines. Wellby notices a ten-foot circle of runes on the bank. Delwath reads the Abyssal words: “I bind you. I call you. I command you. Enter and obey for a month and a day.” An iron-and-salt taste tempts some of them to drink, though none is compelled. Kenzo blows the horn and hears nothing; Riswynn says it silently summons help, but nothing appears. Wellby lights the white-quartz bridge with his driftglobe. In its shadows, Delwath reads a warning that the wind scouring the desert sands is waiting, recalling the black wind named on the iron staff. He decides to cut through the summoning runes.

#### Short
Delwath’s strike awakens a towering vine creature, which the party defeats when Wellby’s final arrow sends it into the river and releases the blocked water.

#### Intermediate
As Delwath scratches the runes, a towering vine creature rises from the river and strikes him, taking cold damage from his protective armor. While he curses it and Riswynn attacks, Kenzo knocks it down with a force blast and follows with staff and fists, fighting on after its spores poison him. Seeker disrupts the runes and excavates the bank behind it. Wellby’s carefully aimed arrows wear it down until his final shot drives it into the river, where the renewed flow sweeps it away.

#### Long
As Delwath scratches the runes with his shadow sword, a ten-foot humanoid tangle of vines and mud erupts from the water and slams him. Frost flashes from his armor in return. Delwath curses the creature, and Seeker turns over part of the runic circle. Vola fights alongside the group as Wellby plants his feet and shoots into its tangled body. Riswynn closes with Shatterstorm, while Kenzo knocks it prone with a blast of force and rushes in to strike. The creature rises and releases a cloud of spores, poisoning Kenzo and filling his mind with distant howling and barking. As Delwath’s sword and Wellby’s arrows cut into it, Riswynn touches it with Inflict Wounds, blackening and drying its vines. Seeker conjures glowing hedge clippers for his Vicious Mockery, and Kenzo keeps landing blows with his staff and fists despite the poison. With the creature faltering, Seeker digs away the riverbank behind it. Wellby spots the blackened wound left by his companions and places his final arrow there. The impact drives the creature back onto the collapsing bank and into the river. Water bursts through the obstruction, sweeping away its vines and mud as the mists begin to clear.

#### Short
With the river flowing cleanly, they cure Kenzo, erase the circle, and free the captive animals under the guidance of a lion carrying Wellby.

#### Intermediate
Riswynn cures Kenzo with the Lesser Restoration scroll while Delwath and Seeker finish destroying the runes. Ash with residual magic and an ivory scroll-case cap suggest how the summoning was performed, but its author remains unknown. The party returns to the animals, avoiding the still-tainted courtyard pool. Seeker uses Speak with Animals and Suggestion to enlist a dominant lion as their guide, and Wellby rides along as the freed captives make their way out of the valley.

#### Long
Riswynn immediately uses the Lesser Restoration scroll to cure Kenzo’s poison. Delwath and Seeker obliterate the remaining runes while clean water rushes downstream, carrying away some of the red vines and leaving others drooping and blackening. Examining the circle’s remains, Delwath finds residual magic in a small pile of ash beside an ivory scroll-case cap. Someone used a scroll here, perhaps two to four weeks ago, but the party finds no firm answer to who did it or how this summoning relates to the demon already slain in the castle. Vola explains that the orc served Agata Dustmother and spoke of her search for lost artifacts; Agata’s connection to the circle remains uncertain.

With the river safe, they return to the captives. The courtyard pool still carries its oily taint, so Seeker directs the animals toward the clean water beyond the gates. Using Speak with Animals and Suggestion, he persuades the strongest adult female lion to lead the others away from settlements. She meets his eyes, gives a slight nod, and roars. The party opens the enclosures and frees the three hyenas from their pits. Wellby climbs onto a lion’s back and accompanies the procession as it moves through the valley, making sure the animals get clear before returning.

#### Short
Returning through the dwarven outpost, they gain passage past its damaged guardian, recover adamantine, and ride south to an evening camp.

#### Intermediate
The party makes one last exploration of the dwarven caves, where a damaged stone guardian bars the forge. Seeker cannot shrink it, but Riswynn’s ancestral shield wins recognition and passage. Beyond lie wrecked mining equipment, a deep shaft, and a valuable piece of adamantine, which they load onto the floating disk. They leave the guardian at its post, quietly rejoin their horses with Vola in the afternoon, and ride south for several hours before camping.

#### Long
Back in the dwarven caves, Wellby leads the party toward the forge and mine, where a damaged stone statue still paces its post. It struggles to raise its axe and orders them to stop. Seeker tries to shrink it, but the spell’s energy seems to sink into its glowing chest without changing its size. Riswynn raises the Shield of the Brawnanvil Clan and declares her family’s connection to the outpost. The guardian recognizes “my dwarves” and steps aside.

Beyond it, the old workshop and mining equipment lie shattered around a shaft deeper than Riswynn can see. Kenzo considers jumping, then decides against it. Seeker recognizes a large lump of adamantine among the remaining metal, and they add it to the floating disk. The guardian’s battle damage and destruction below suggest an attack from the mines long ago, possibly by mind flayers, though the attackers’ identity remains uncertain. Leaving the guardian on watch, the party slips out through the secret passage with Vola. They reach their horses and supplies in the afternoon, then ride south for a few hours and make camp for the evening.
