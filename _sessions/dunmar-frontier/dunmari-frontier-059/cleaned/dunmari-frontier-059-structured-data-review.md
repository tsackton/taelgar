## Combat

### Fire wasps and corrupted ankylosaurus at the lava river

Enari obscures the magma dancer with fog, prompting initiative as the wasps turn toward the party.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Kenzo’s readied critical hit disrupts a wasp before it attacks Enari. Both wasps wound Enari; the dancer escapes into the woods. Enari hits back, and Izzarak releases his readied Guiding Bolt after adopting Dragon form. |
| 2 | Kenzo damages and disrupts the wasps. Izzarak’s fourth-level Erupting Earth kills one, then the charging dinosaur sprays magma. The surviving wasp poisons and paralyzes Kenzo; Enari kills it before striking the dinosaur and suffering fire backlash. |
| 3 | Kenzo remains paralyzed until Izzarak’s Lesser Restoration removes both poison and paralysis. The dinosaur misses, the dancer returns to the lava, and Enari steps back through a missed opportunity attack before landing a critical pike sequence for 40 damage. |
| 4 | Freed Kenzo kills the dinosaur with a 13-damage staff hit, taking 3 fire damage after resistance. |

Both wasps and the corrupted ankylosaurus are dead; the surviving magma dancer opens a wary conversation. The party has not crossed the lava river.
