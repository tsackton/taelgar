## scene-001 | Beneath Raven’s Hold

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002, beat-003
- Rationale: The tunnel approach, Wellby’s discoveries, and the route discussion establish the party’s position and next objective.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Through the Hidden Tunnel: Riswynn breaks the rusty lock and leads the climb through a rough tunnel toward Raven’s Hold.
- beat-002 | Wellby’s Scouting Report: Wellby reports traces of a former hobgoblin occupation and gnolls herding dogs under the watch of a small bat-like demon.
- beat-003 | Mapping the Way Forward: The party maps its uncertain position and debates scouting forward or returning to observe the valley and investigate an iron construct.

## scene-002 | Securing the Tunnels

- Beat IDs: beat-004, beat-005, beat-006
- Rationale: The suspicious noises prompt reconnaissance and a quiet attack on the undead; the cleared tunnels then provide safe lookout positions for recovery and an hour of watching. Everyone remains outside the towers throughout.

### Beats

- beat-004 | Delwath Scouts Invisibly: Invisible Delwath finds two undead in a prison and scouts exits overlooking the gnoll-held plaza and an inner tower.
- beat-005 | Clearing the Undead Prison: The party quietly kills the two undead hobgoblins and investigates the chamber’s use as a prison and execution room.
- beat-006 | An Hour on Watch: An hour of watching reveals gnoll guard changes, animal activity, strange noises near the towers, and firelight in the keep.

## scene-003 | Into the Towers

- Beat IDs: beat-007, beat-008, beat-009
- Rationale: The entry plan, Kenzo’s first crossing into the tower, exploration, discovery of Vola, and the guard fight form one continuous infiltration and rescue attempt.

### Beats

- beat-007 | The Empty Tower: Invisible Kenzo finds an empty tower that can shelter the party, while its abandoned rooms yield a magical scroll.
- beat-008 | Finding Vola: Kenzo discovers Vola imprisoned in the occupied tower, gives her a healing potion, and brings back a rescue plan.
- beat-009 | Fighting the Tower Guards: The rescue turns into a fight with an orc and a jackal-like shapeshifter, both of whom the party kills.

## scene-004 | Vola and the Relic Race

- Beat IDs: beat-010, beat-011
- Rationale: The aftermath of the guard fight focuses on Vola: recovering her equipment, dealing with the captive, withdrawing, and hearing her account of the relic hunt.

### Beats

- beat-010 | Freeing Vola and Withdrawing: The party recovers Vola’s equipment, deals with the captive guard, and returns with her to the empty tower.
- beat-011 | Agata and the Relic Race: Vola explains that the Society of the Open Scroll is racing Agata Dustmother to recover lost imperial relics.
