#### Short
The party follows a hidden passage toward Raven’s Hold and regroups to compare Wellby’s discoveries.

#### Intermediate
After Riswynn breaks a rusted lock, the party follows a rough tunnel and climbs into an empty storeroom. Wellby rejoins them with Blackened Claw coins and an unreadable logbook, describing severed heads in an old armory and gnolls herding dogs under the watch of a small bat-like creature. His report of an iron construct patrolling the dwarven ruins adds another possible route to investigate. Unsure of their position beneath Raven’s Hold, they debate returning to watch the valley or pressing forward until a nearby noise interrupts them.

#### Long
Kenzo cannot pick the rusted padlock barring the passage, so Riswynn breaks it with her warhammer. Beyond lies a rough tunnel, markedly cruder than the dwarven outpost. Seeker notices limestone giving way to harder rock as they travel roughly 1,000–1,500 feet eastward and spiral upward. Riswynn climbs a forty-foot shaft using old iron fastenings, then lowers Delwath’s rope from an empty storeroom above.

Wellby rejoins them with silver and copper coins bearing the Blackened Claw mark and a logbook that appears to cover three years, though nobody can read it. Dusty barracks and dried human heads displayed in an old armory point to a grim occupation after Raven’s Hold’s days as a Dunmari–dwarven trading post. Outside, he saw three gnolls herding wild dogs toward the gates while a small, clawed, bat-like creature watched from above. Whether it commanded the gnolls remains unclear.

Kenzo favors caution and proposes splitting up to watch the valley while others explore. Wellby also reports a mechanical iron figure patrolling a rubble-blocked part of the dwarven stronghold; Seeker recalls old stories of guardians powered by runes. Distance estimates, the map, and the changing bedrock help them place themselves near the keep, but the passage leading southwest remains puzzling. Seeker makes little figurines and proposes settling their choice with stone dice, just as a noise draws their attention onward.

#### Short
After scouting the exits, they quietly kill two undead hobgoblins and watch the keep until sunset.

#### Intermediate
Delwath scouts invisibly and finds two undead hobgoblins gnawing bones in a former prison, a blocked northern exit overlooking gnolls, and a southern gate facing an inner tower. To secure their position, the party ambushes the undead with arrows, spells, and Kenzo’s finishing blows. Manacles and headless skeletons suggest the room once held Dunmari prisoners. They then rest while watching both exits: gnoll guards change, animals cry out, and unidentified scrambling and growling come from near the tower. As sunset brings firelight to the keep, they regroup at the tunnels.

#### Long
Following the noise into a curving corridor, Delwath turns invisible and scouts ahead. Two decaying hobgoblins crack and gnaw bones in a chamber fitted with manacles and a stone platform. Farther north, a rubble-blocked gateway overlooks a plaza occupied by three gnolls; many animals can be heard beyond it. To the south, an unlocked iron gate leads across a short bridge to an upper door in an inner tower. Delwath leaves the gate shut and returns after about fifteen minutes.

The party decides to clear the prison so they can watch the keep safely. Invisible Delwath and Wellby creep ahead, and Kenzo throws a light into the room. Arrows from Seeker and Wellby, followed by Delwath’s Eldritch Blast, bring down the first undead hobgoblin. Riswynn’s Sacred Flame and further attacks wound the second before it rushes Delwath; Kenzo finishes it with staff and fist. Headless skeletons, hostile Dunmari inscriptions, and the apparent execution platform suggest that hobgoblins once held Dunmari prisoners here.

With the tunnels quiet, they take an hour’s rest while keeping watch. Kenzo and Wellby use the southern gate; Delwath, Riswynn, and Seeker watch through the northern rubble. Four gnolls eventually replace the original guards amid animal cries that suggest feeding. Near the southern tower, forty-five quiet minutes give way to scrambling, growling, and hissing from an unseen source. Firelight appears in the keep as the sun sets, and the party regathers without yet entering either tower.

#### Short
Kenzo then leads them into the towers, where they discover Vola imprisoned and overcome the orc and two jackal shapeshifters guarding her.

#### Intermediate
Delwath makes Kenzo invisible to scout the first tower, which proves empty and defensible. The party follows, finding abandoned hobgoblin furnishings and a hidden magical scroll. Across the gate passage, Kenzo discovers Vola bound and wounded; he frees her bonds, gives her a healing potion, and returns for help. Seeker’s Sleep drops one guard but alerts an orc and a second jackal shapeshifter. Kenzo’s light crystal dispels the orc’s darkness, and magical weapons overcome the shapeshifter’s resistance. Kenzo kills the fleeing orc, leaving the other guard asleep.

#### Long
Delwath tests the tower’s wooden door with Mage Hand, then makes Kenzo invisible. Kenzo crosses from the tunnels into an empty tower whose windows overlook a dead garden, sickly green pools, and wandering undead hobgoblins. An upper apartment bears the Blackened Claw mark and still holds a silver goblet set with red gems, armor, and a greatsword. Broken stairs hinder access from below, making the upper rooms a useful refuge. Kenzo brings the others inside. Delwath finds a magical scroll hidden in the desk, while Seeker senses gemstones and precious metals toward the ruined armory to the north.

Still invisible, Kenzo crosses above the gate to the second tower. An orc heads toward its quarters, while two apparently human guards stand near a badly wounded woman upstairs. One kicks her and threatens more abuse in the morning before both guards leave. Kenzo introduces himself, unties her, and learns her name is Vola. She has lost track of how long she has been held and wonders whether the Society sent him. He gives her a healing potion, then returns to arrange a rescue past the guard at the stairs.

Seeker’s Sleep drops that guard, but the thump alerts the orc and its companion, whose face shifts into a jackal-like snout. Wellby’s arrows and Seeker’s Heat Metal on the orc’s scimitar hurt it before it fills the room with magical darkness. Kenzo dispels this with his crystal’s Daylight, ending his own invisibility. His ordinary staff fails to hurt the shapeshifter, which wounds Seeker and breaks his concentration. Riswynn’s magical warhammer and Searing Smite can harm it, and Delwath finishes it with his pact sword. More arrows drive the wounded orc to flee, but Kenzo intercepts it and kills it with a flurry of blows as it promises vengeance. The remaining guard sleeps through the fight.

#### Short
With Vola freed, they withdraw to the empty tower and learn that her Society is racing Agata Dustmother to recover lost imperial relics.

#### Intermediate
The party binds the sleeping guard and returns Vola’s equipment. She warns that the captive can escape his bonds by changing shape, and they decide to kill him before withdrawing to the first tower. Behind a barred door, Vola learns of Arcus’s death and explains that both belonged to the Society of the Open Scroll, associated with Chardon’s Great Library. They sought imperial relics ahead of Agata Dustmother, whose nature and purpose Vola does not fully understand. Wellby questions Chardon’s claims to the relics as the group considers joining forces and continuing its investigation.

#### Long
The party knocks out and binds the last guard while Kenzo fetches Vola, now able to walk after the healing potion. A chest yields her daggers, hand crossbow, cloak, and other adventuring supplies. When Seeker asks whether their captive could change shape and slip his bonds, Vola is certain he could and urges them to kill him. Delwath summons a shadowy dagger for the task. They then take Vola back across the gate passage to the empty tower and bar the connecting door.

Vola remains guarded as introductions begin. She briefly shows sadness at the news of Arcus’s death and tries to hide it, later explaining that he, too, belonged to the Society of the Open Scroll. Associated with Chardon’s Great Library, the Society is seeking lost imperial relics before Agata Dustmother can acquire them. Vola learned part of this from the three servants the party has just killed. She believes Agata means harm, but cannot say exactly what she is or why she wants the relics.

Wellby is skeptical of Chardonian claims to the objects, while Vola defends her homeland’s interest in recovering them. Her knowledge of Raven’s Hold is limited: she never reached the keep, and the animal noises have grown over the last five days without revealing their purpose. At about seven in the evening, they discuss traveling together and continuing to investigate the stronghold.
