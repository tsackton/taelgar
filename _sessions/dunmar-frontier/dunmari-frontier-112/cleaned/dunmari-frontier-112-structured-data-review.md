## Combat

### Bizarro Delwath in the Sapphire Tower

The assault group teleports into the control chamber, four invisible; the return token must be removed before destroying the defender.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Wellby dazes the defender; Kenzo avoids the fire trap and pushes him off the controls. The simulacrum’s critical Banishing Smite succeeds despite Counterspell. |
| 2 | Wellby is restrained by trapdoor vines; Seeker identifies the lightning-channeling crystals without learning to operate the tower. |
| 3 | Wellby escapes. Readied rune and initial token grabs fail; the simulacrum takes the amulet, and Delwath destroys the defender. |

Bizarro Delwath disintegrates with his token removed. His threat about the ship remains a claim; Faldrick answers the subsequent check.

### The remaining doppelgangers by the docks

Six combatants arrive together by Dimension Door, Ladder flies ahead, and Wellby initially supports from the tower roof.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The hostile construct dies; Kenzo stuns Bizarro Wellby. Bizarro Kenzo stuns the simulacrum, then is incapacitated and loses his token. Riswynn takes Wellby’s token; her double dispels the simulacrum. |
| 2 | Slow affects the allied constructs. Dark Ladder breaks Seeker’s concentration, dropping Kenzo’s token; Delwath recovers it and destroys Bizarro Kenzo. The two Riswynns exchange fire and blindness. |
| 3 | Riswynn is incapacitated; Kenzo keeps pursuing his target. Delwath cures his own blindness and uses Scatter. Seeker distracts his double; Kenzo redirects Guiding Bolt into Bizarro Wellby, killing him. |
| 4 | Delwath blocks Bizarro Seeker’s escape and cures Seeker’s blindness. Seeker takes his double’s token; Riswynn destroys it. The angel knocks Seeker unconscious and inflicts a failed death save. |
| 5 | Wellby blinds Bizarro Riswynn. Seeker fails another death save but receives 70 HP from Heal. Kenzo’s successful ki reroll cancels the disease effect; the angel still deals substantial damage. |
| 6 | Wellby takes the last token. Attacks and Conjure Celestial trigger the double’s Death Ward at one HP and heal Kenzo/Delwath. She evades opportunity attacks and raises Wall of Fire, ending her angel. |
| 7 | Wellby flies above the wall and uses clockwork and bardic assistance to land the finishing Lightning Arrow. |

Bizarro Riswynn disintegrates; all five doubles are defeated. The party returns by Rainbow Bridge with the amulets, still in midmorning.
