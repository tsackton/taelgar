#### Short
Seeking a hidden approach to Raven's Hold, the party follows numbered Dwarven stones up a stream valley to a gorge marked with the word "enter." A rabbit tests the water without immediate ill effect, earning the name Taster.

#### Intermediate
With the centaurs riding south to warn Candrosa, the party turns toward the mountains to avoid the dangerous plains around Raven's Hold. Riswynn's maps suggest a hidden route into the dwarven outpost, and Seeker and Wellby find out-of-place stones marked with Dwarven numbers, apparently counting down the distance. The trail leads upstream to a steep gorge, where the final inscription says "enter." Before proceeding, Seeker gives stream water to a caged rabbit; it shows no immediate ill effects, and he names it Taster.

#### Long
The centaurs ride south toward Karawa to warn Candrosa, leaving the party on the road below the mountains. Raven's Hold lies beyond the neighboring valley, with a rotten, burning smell drifting from its direction. Wary of the insanity vines and reports of poisoned water, the party considers a concealed mountain approach. Kenzo asks where the strange creatures originate, and Seeker uses a pair of hypothetical maps to illustrate his theory of shifting boundaries, but the connection between the corruption, a portal, and the storm remains uncertain. Riswynn's maps offer something more practical: her great-uncle marked a hidden exit from the dwarven outpost near the Long Road.

Heading toward the stream west of Raven's Hold, Seeker notices a volcanic boulder out of place among the surrounding rocks. Faint Dwarven runes mark it with the number 2300. With Wellby's help he finds another stone, this one worked granite marked 2100, and the party follows the decreasing numbers upstream. Near the expected 400 or 500 mark, the stream enters a steep gorge; here the inscription simply says "enter." Finding no obvious trap or visible corruption in the water, Seeker offers some to a caged rabbit. It drinks without immediate ill effect, and he names it Taster.

#### Short
Leaving the horses behind, they follow the stream underground, where Riswynn frees a chain mechanism and drains the flooded passage. Beyond it, the party passes a suspicious lever and illusory rubble before Wellby opens a hidden door.

#### Intermediate
The gorge narrows into a tunnel too low for the horses, which the party leaves near the water. Farther in, the rising water forces Delwath to swim ahead; he finds a metal plate diverting a waterfall into their path and a chain set into the wall. Riswynn pulls the stuck mechanism free, draining the passage. A long ascent leads to a guard chamber, where they leave an unexplained lever alone and take a ladder onward. Seeker discovers that an apparent cave-in is an illusion, and Riswynn tests the floor with ball bearings before they cross. At the passage's end, Wellby finds a stone knob that opens a concealed door.

#### Long
They enter the gorge around noon, following the stream until the narrowing tunnel forces them to leave the horses behind. Seeker takes Taster along, while Kenzo lights the way with the cold flame of his enchanted spearpoint. The tunnel floor rises beneath a level ceiling until the passage is completely submerged. Delwath swims roughly sixty feet ahead against the current and discovers a diagonal metal plate directing falling water toward them, with a loop of chain set into the wall. Returning to report, he leaves Riswynn to try the mechanism. After a strenuous effort, she pulls the chain free; the plate slides down into the floor, redirecting the waterfall through a hole and draining their route.

Beyond the barrier, a long spiral ascent brings them to a guard chamber with a rusty lever and suspicious seams running across the wall and ceiling. They leave that mechanism untouched. Seeker instead pulls down a trapdoor, releasing an extending ladder, and they continue east. Apparent rubble blocks the way until Seeker's hand passes through it. Riswynn throws ball bearings through the illusion to check the floor, and Seeker crosses safely; from the other side, a matching false lever suggests the appearance of a deliberately collapsed passage. Eventually the tunnel ends at a blank wall. Wellby finds a small stone knob beside it and opens the hidden door.

#### Short
Beyond the door, they meet Inakara, the strange ruler of a cavern settlement, who demands fighting help in exchange for passage. She leads them to a ruined marketplace and urges them to kill the creature preying on her people.

#### Intermediate
The door opens beside two pools inhabited by unfamiliar dwarf-sized creatures. Their white-haired ruler, Inakara, hisses and speaks broken Dwarven, demanding help before she will grant the passage Seeker requests. Kenzo finds no clear sign of a trap, though he cannot establish that she is trustworthy. She leads them across the cavern to a ruined marketplace, warning only that the creature there eats her people and has a dangerous bite. Wellby lights two torches and throws one ahead as the party advances among sculpted stone formations, with Inakara's followers gathering behind them.

#### Long
The door swings open onto a raised platform overlooking two pools. Straw, refuse, and animal bones lie scattered about the cavern, and unfamiliar dwarf-sized creatures gather around the water. Their ruler, Inakara, has bedraggled white hair, long black nails, and gray-purple skin mottled like bruises. She hisses and licks her fingers and a chalky stone as she questions the strangers in broken Dwarven. Seeker offers peaceful intentions and asks for safe passage; she wants help first, and presses them to say whether they can fight. Kenzo recognizes the possibility of creatures from the deep earth but cannot identify their kind. His scrutiny suggests that Inakara wants something done, without settling whether she can be trusted.

Drawing away from Kenzo's light, Inakara leads them across the pools, tapping her staff to command the attention of roughly thirty inhabitants. She takes the party to an adjoining cavern, a vast ruined marketplace recognizable from Riswynn's map. Shops and other buildings stand around a stone dais and a garden of sculpted stalagmites. Inakara says the creature within does not speak, eats her people, and bites, but gives little useful advice about fighting it. Wellby lights two torches and throws one into the cavern. The party moves toward cover among the formations while the inhabitants gather behind them to watch.

#### Short
A roper disguised among the stones seizes Wellby and Delwath, but the party breaks free and discovers that it can barely move. Wellby lodges an arrow in its hide, Seeker heats the arrowhead, and ranged attacks finish it; Inakara then points out a door onward, leaving the party to make its own passage safe.

#### Intermediate
A tentacle catches Wellby, revealing a roper concealed among the formations. Its tough hide turns aside the first attacks, and its tendrils catch Delwath as it drags Wellby into a vicious bite. Seeker's inspiration helps Wellby escape; Delwath eventually teleports to a roof, and Kenzo severs the tendril that later catches Riswynn. Realizing the creature moves too slowly to pursue them, the party spreads out. Wellby embeds an arrow that Seeker heats with Heat Metal, while Delwath and the others attack from range until it falls. The inhabitants cheer and Inakara claims credit, then points across the cavern when asked for the promised route. The party has yet to test that passage.

#### Long
A tentacle lashes from the rocks and grabs Wellby: one of the apparent stalagmites is a roper. Delwath curses it with Hex, but his sword and Riswynn's Guiding Bolt fail against its hard hide, and another tendril catches him. Seeker offers Wellby an encouraging image of glowing calamari and cracks a tentacle with Thunderwave. Kenzo helps Delwath wriggle free long enough to add another curse, but the creature catches him again after dragging Wellby into its mouth for a vicious bite. Riswynn cannot break Delwath's restraint with her hammer. Wellby, aided by Seeker's inspiration, escapes, drops his torch, and retreats into cover.

Kenzo studies the creature with Soul Sight, while Seeker heals Wellby and investigates the rock garden. Small tendrils at its base and a displaced sculpture suggest that the roper can move, but only slowly. Delwath teleports free onto a nearby roof and turns to ranged attacks. When a tentacle catches Riswynn, Kenzo tears it apart with his Fist of Planar Power, freeing her as the others spread out.

Seeker proposes heating an arrow lodged in the roper, but Wellby's first glancing hit has left nothing embedded. A later shot sinks into its hide, giving Seeker a target for Heat Metal. He moves close enough to heat the arrowhead and then withdraws, while Delwath's crackling blasts and the party's other ranged attacks wear the creature down. Unable to close the distance, it finally falls. The watching inhabitants cheer, and Inakara announces her success. Asked for the promised route toward Raven's Hold, she points to a door across the cavern and tells the party to make its own safe passage. The way onward remains unexplored.
