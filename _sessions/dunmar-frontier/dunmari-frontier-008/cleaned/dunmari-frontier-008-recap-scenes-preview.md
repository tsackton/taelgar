# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 4
- Beat Coverage: 9/9

## scene-001 | Following the Hidden Route

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002
- Rationale: Choosing the concealed approach and following the numbered stones share one immediate goal: finding the dwarven entrance. Entering the gorge and leaving the horses marks the shift to underground exploration.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Choosing the Hidden Approach: The party chooses a concealed mountain approach to Raven's Hold after reviewing the centaurs' warning and Riswynn's maps.
- beat-002 | Following the Dwarven Stones: Seeker and Wellby follow numbered Dwarven stones to a gorge, where a rabbit tests the stream water and earns the name Taster.

## scene-002 | Through Water and Illusion

- Beat IDs: beat-003, beat-004
- Rationale: Draining the flooded tunnel and navigating the guard chamber, ladder, and false cave-in form a continuous exploration of the concealed entrance route. Opening the final door introduces an inhabited cavern and a new social situation.

### Beats

- beat-003 | Opening the Flooded Passage: After leaving the horses at the gorge, Delwath scouts a submerged barrier and Riswynn pulls its chain to drain the passage.
- beat-004 | Levers, Ladders, and Illusions: The party bypasses an unexplained lever, passes through illusory rubble, and opens a hidden door into the outpost.

## scene-003 | The Price of Passage

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006
- Rationale: The bargain with Inakara continues as she leads the party to the marketplace and explains the threat. Keep her demand, guidance, and the party’s preparations together; the first tentacle attack begins the fight.

### Beats

- beat-005 | Bargaining with the Cavern's Ruler: Seeker bargains with Inakara for passage, while Kenzo finds no clear sign that her request for help is a trap.
- beat-006 | Into the Ruined Marketplace: Inakara leads the party to a ruined marketplace and demands that they kill the creature preying on her people.

## scene-004 | The Roper in the Marketplace

- Beat IDs: beat-007, beat-008, beat-009
- Rationale: The ambush, escapes, discovery of the roper’s limited mobility, and burning-arrow tactic are phases of one uninterrupted fight. Its brief aftermath resolves the bargain only as far as Inakara pointing out the onward door.

### Beats

- beat-007 | Caught by Stone Tentacles: A roper seizes Wellby and Delwath, and the party struggles against its hard hide before freeing the wounded halfling.
- beat-008 | Breaking Free and Finding Range: The party learns the roper moves slowly, shifts toward ranged attacks, and frees Riswynn by severing a tentacle.
- beat-009 | Burning Arrow, Fallen Monster: An embedded arrow lets Seeker burn the roper with Heat Metal, and the party finishes it from beyond its reach before seeking passage onward.
