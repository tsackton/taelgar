### Stony tentacled creature in the dwarven marketplace

A disguised stony creature catches Wellby with a long sticky tendril before initiative.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Delwath and Wellby are caught; ordinary attacks fail, while Kenzo helps Delwath and Seeker inspires Wellby and damages a tendril with Thunderwave. |
| 2 | Delwath escapes and curses the creature but is caught again; Wellby takes a 14-damage bite, then escapes with inspiration. Kenzo reads its defenses; Seeker heals Wellby. |
| 3 | Delwath teleports to the roof. Riswynn is caught, then freed by Kenzo’s 17-damage strike; Wellby’s first arrow hits, and the party spreads out. |
| 4 | Four tendrils miss Riswynn. Wellby embeds an arrow for 12 damage; Seeker heats it for 12 more. The creature saves against disadvantage, and Kenzo readies assistance. |
| 5 | Delwath repositions and pulls the creature with an 11-damage blast; Riswynn’s Sacred Flame is saved against. The DM resolves another minute of ranged attacks. |

The creature cannot close the safe distance and collapses under ranged attacks and the heated arrow. Its defeat resolves the fight; the promised onward route remains unexplored.
