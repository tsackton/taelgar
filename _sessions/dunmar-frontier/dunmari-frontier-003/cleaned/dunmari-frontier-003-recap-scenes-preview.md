# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 5
- Beat Coverage: 11/11

## scene-001 | Tracks Into the Fort

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002, beat-003
- Rationale: The party's approach, the ankheg ambush, and the immediate investigation of its burrow form one continuous sequence before the party turns to the temple and towers.

### Beats

- beat-001 | The Abandoned Camp: The party investigates a recently abandoned campsite outside the ruined Dunmari fort and follows its owner's tracks toward the causeway.
- beat-002 | Ambush in the Fort: An ankheg erupts beneath Wellby during his reconnaissance of the fort, but the party frees him and kills the creature.
- beat-003 | Aftermath and Burrow: The party examines the ankheg's lair, discovers extensive animal traffic into the caves, and chooses to rest in the pentagonal building.

## scene-002 | Sanctuary and Spoils

- Beat IDs: beat-004
- Rationale: The party rests and investigates the fort's temple and towers in a distinct aboveground interlude that ends with the decision to descend below.

### Beats

- beat-004 | Temple and Towers: The party rests in the magically preserved Dunmari temple, recovers sacred objects, and finds an enchanted horn in a ruined tower.

## scene-003 | Stone Trails Below

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006, beat-007
- Rationale: The statue, cockatrice nursery, and cistern are successive discoveries in one continuous underground exploration following the obsidian trail.

### Beats

- beat-005 | The Statue and Coin: Underground, the party finds the petrified Arcus and a Chardonian coin that transforms into a floating disc.
- beat-006 | The Cockatrice Nursery: The party discovers a cockatrice nursery where hatchlings repeatedly petrify a trapped lion, then retreats to explore the other tunnel.
- beat-007 | Beneath the Cistern: The wider tunnel leads beneath the ruined cistern, where the party follows the old watercourse toward the source of the disturbance.

## scene-004 | Battle for the Obsidian

- Beat IDs: beat-008, beat-009
- Rationale: The skeleton assault and the emergence of the obsidian staff-bearer are two phases of the same uninterrupted combat.

### Beats

- beat-008 | Skeletons in the Dark: A wave of unnatural rage and three animated skeletons confront the party at the entrance to the southern cavern.
- beat-009 | The Obsidian Bearer: An undead hobgoblin wielding an obsidian-topped staff joins the battle, but the party overwhelms it and captures the staff.

## scene-005 | Breaking the Staff

- Beat IDs: beat-010, beat-011
- Rationale: The post-battle study leads directly to the decision and repeated attempts to destroy the staff, culminating after the overnight rest.

### Beats

- beat-010 | Secrets of the Staff: The party studies the malignant staff, connects it tentatively to Cha'mutte, and finds a flaw that may allow the obsidian to be destroyed.
- beat-011 | Shattering the Obsidian: The party shatters the obsidian, ends its maddening influence, and rests overnight before returning toward camp the next morning.
