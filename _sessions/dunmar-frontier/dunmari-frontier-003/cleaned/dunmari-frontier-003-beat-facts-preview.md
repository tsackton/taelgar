**Time Window**: midday

*beat-010*

**Time Window**: afternoon
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party studies the malignant staff, connects it tentatively to Cha'mutte, and finds a flaw that may allow the obsidian to be destroyed.

**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
