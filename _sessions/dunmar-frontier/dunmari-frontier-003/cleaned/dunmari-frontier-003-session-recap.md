#### Short
We begin on the causeway outside the [[Dunmari Fort (Gomat)|Dunmari fort]], where the party uncovers an abandoned campsite and tracks leading into the fort. Following the trail, the party survives an ankheg ambush and discovers dense animal tracks in the caves below, but they decide to find a safe place to rest before exploring further. 

#### Long
We begin on the causeway outside the [[Dunmari Fort (Gomat)|Dunmari fort]]. Searching the scene, the Dunmar Fellowship finds a campsite in the foundations of the outbuildings outside the fort. The camp itself was several days to several weeks old, and had clearly been disturbed by animals (signs of scattered food -- salt pork and dried fish and the like that had been partially eaten by animals). Whoever made the camp had left most of their stuff, except weapons and water, and gone somewhere. 

The camper's trail runs from the disturbed burial mound, across the causeway, and into the ruins, and the party decides to follow it into the fort. Inside, only three buildings still have walls: a central headquarters building, a five-sided temple (which retains a metal roof), and two towers guarding the entryway. In addition, there is an empty cistern, cracked at the bottom. 

The sounds of a large clacking and chittering creature from the central building draw the party in. [[Wellby]] begins sneaking quietly towards the walls to investigate, as the sounds go quiet momentarily. [[Delwath]] suddenly remembers tales of a large desert monstrosity that hunted by tunneling underground and ambushing prey, but before he can say anything, a giant insect, an ankheg, a cross between a centipede and a praying mantis, bursts from underground and grabs [[Wellby]] in its mandibles. Despite a few tense moments as [[Wellby]] is in danger of being eaten, [[Kenzo]]’s quick action to whack the creature across the mandibles frees [[Wellby]], and the ankheg is dispatched by [[Delwath]]’s longsword blow after being knocked prone by [[Seeker]] excavating the loose earth under its back legs. 

Inside the creature's lair, the party finds the remains of a lion and a natural passage marked by tracks of many animals, including lions, hyenas, lizards, moving in both directions. Wary, the group postpones descent and seeks shelter for a short rest.

#### Short
Their shelter proves to be an ancient and still consecrated temple to the [[Dunmari Religion|Five Siblings]]; they find Aagir's Everlight and other portable relics while they rest. A search of the gate towers also yields a magical horn among the ancient remains of a long-dead Dunmari scout, after which they return to the ankheg tunnel and descend below. 

#### Long
The pentagonal shelter proves to be a temple to the [[Dunmari Religion|Five Siblings]], its still-consecrated walls remarkably standing amid the fort's ruins. In this temple, the altar to [[Aagir]] seems the most important, and a small [[Aagir's Everlight|silver spearpoint statue]], engraved with a flame motif and burning with a fire that seems to have no source of fuel, still stands on the altar. This item radiates evocation magic, and magic is also threaded through the walls of the temple, perhaps giving them more stability and structure. While resting and recovering, a search of the wreckage inside the temple also turns up some heavier religious items, including bronze chalices, silver candlesticks, some amulets, and small statues. 

After their rest, they turn to search the two gate towers; one is choked with rubble, but Wellby finds an ancient Dunmari scout in the other and recovers a magical horn decorated with a running horse. With the fort's surface explored, they return to the passage beneath the ruined headquarters.

#### Short
Descending into the caves, they find the missing camper petrified and missing an arm beside a heavy Chardonian coin that unfolds into a hovering disc. The narrower branch leads to cockatrice hatchlings repeatedly turning a trapped lion to stone, so the party retreats rather than risk the nursery. They take the wider route beneath the cistern and follow the dry watercourse ahead. 

#### Long
Descending through the ankheg's passage -- a low, narrow crawl -- the party reaches a junction containing an extraordinarily lifelike statue of a [[Arcus|human man]], wearing a cloak and leather armor, with a dagger in one hand. One of the statue's arms is broken and lies on the ground beside a torch. Also on the ground is a heavy [[Floating Disc Coin|golden coin]], radiating transformation magic. [[Seeker]] flips the coin, and it turns into a floating disc that hovers 3 feet off the ground, following him.

From this cavern, the party explores the narrower of the two tunnels, which leads to a muddy nursery where cockatrice hatchlings and eggs surround a lion caught in a cycle of petrification and recovery. Unwilling to alert the young or their parents, the party backs away. They instead take the wider branch beneath the cracked cistern and follow its dry channel south.

#### Short
They emerge in a larger cavern, where a wave of unnatural rage signals an attack by three skeletons, soon joined by an undead hobgoblin wielding an obsidian-topped iron staff that radiates wave after wave of anger and violent rage. The party destroys the skeletons, and overwhelms their staff-bearing master. 

#### Long
The tunnel leads to a larger cavern, where a wave of unnatural rage washes over the party just as three skeletons drop from the ceiling and attack. Delwath holds the narrow approach while the others fight from behind him, destroying one skeleton with a Hex-enhanced blow as Seeker turns the floating disc into an improvised barrier. A rotting undead hobgoblin enters the fray, carrying a black iron staff crowned by a pulsing obsidian stone, sending waves of rage and dark energy through the cavern. The party finishes the skeletons and concentrates on their new enemy: Delwath curses it, Seeker and Wellby attack from range, and Kenzo closes through its defenses. Wellby's hidden shot leaves it badly wounded, and Kenzo strikes it down, but the fallen staff continues to radiate hostility.

#### Short
With the staff-bearer dead, the party links the obsidian to the animals' rage. Seeker finds a flaw in the obsidian, allowing Kenzo and Wellby to crack it, and Delwath to shatter it with a summoned shadowy blade. The violent influence fades and the party sets up camp in the temple to rest overnight before heading back west.

#### Long
After defeating the undead, the party realizes the obsidian staff must be the source of the abnormal beasts. Seeker finds a flaw in the obsidian, allowing Kenzo and Wellby to crack it, and Delwath to shatter it with a summoned shadowy blade. While the obsidian seems to resist non-magical damage, a few blows are enough for it to shatter, and calm to prevail, although at the cost of some damaging psychic backlash. With the obsidian destroyed, the violent influence fades. Peace and calm permeate the area, and the party spends a quiet, restful night in the temple.
