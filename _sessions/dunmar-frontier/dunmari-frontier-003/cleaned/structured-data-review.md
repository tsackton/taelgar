| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Sleep fails. Kenzo’s Flurry and Delwath’s blast hit; Wellby cannot escape, and the ankheg maintains its grip without further damage. |
| 2 | Seeker heals Wellby for 6 and drops the ankheg prone with Mold Earth. Kenzo frees Wellby instead of dealing staff damage, then lands one unarmed strike. Wellby disengages and lands a disadvantaged Sneak Attack; Delwath kills the ankheg. |
