#### Short
Hralgar asks the party to recover his missing eyes, warning that his brief control over the storm and its dangerous dreams is failing.

#### Intermediate
Chained in Stormcaller Tower, the blinded Hralgar believes that recovering his eyes would allow him to free himself. His captor Eudomes is long dead, and he doubts that destroying the iron sphere used in his binding would help. With his strength ebbing and the storm temporarily quiet, the party has only a short time to search before his dreams take hold again.

#### Long
Chained in the shattered upper chamber of Stormcaller Tower, Hralgar explains that his bonds drain his strength and his dreams shape the world around him. He believes that he could escape if his missing eyes were returned, but cannot tell the party where they have gone. Eudomes, the wizard associated with his captivity, has been dead for centuries. The iron sphere was used to trap his spirit, yet Hralgar doubts that breaking it would undo the binding. If they cannot free him, he asks them to leave him alive and spread word of his plight.

The Fox and Hunter is visible again, helping the party recognize the difference between the giant’s lucid moments and his dreamscape. Hralgar can ease the storm while he concentrates, but maintaining that control costs him effort. They decide to use the reprieve to look for his eyes and clues to his imprisonment.

#### Short
While Kenzo meditates with him, the others find signs of recent looting, surviving books, and letters describing his secret binding and Drankor’s fall.

#### Intermediate
Kenzo stays with Hralgar while the others search the tower. Three sets of footprints and recently removed fittings suggest earlier visitors; most of the library’s books are gone. Delwath studies the chained volumes that remain, and Wellby finds Nelawe’s Lyrics of a New Age. Farther down, Wellby and Seeker discover old letters celebrating Hralgar’s binding for the Occulta Ludum and warning of the magical plague that destroyed Drankor. The search also reveals a sealed bedchamber.

#### Long
Kenzo remains upstairs, meditating with Hralgar, while the others work their way down. Wellby and Riswynn find three sets of bootprints in the dust. Seeker estimates that the metal fittings stripped from five marble pillars were removed within the last month or two, and the giant bust has empty sockets where its eyes and crown ornaments should be. They suspect Chardonian explorers, although nothing yet establishes who the looters were.

Most loose books have vanished from the library. Three remain chained to the stone: Tales Before the Fall, written in Giant, and the Hkarian volumes Journeys of Souls and On the Ordering of Giants. Delwath reads while Seeker sketches runes; Wellby discovers Nelawe’s Elvish poetry collection, Lyrics of a New Age, beneath a shelf and gives it to Delwath. The books describe storm giants becoming storms and reading omens, with Drankorian marginal notes showing an interest in transformation, but offer no clear way to release Hralgar.

Beyond the library lies a wind garden of marble lattice, chimes, mineral benches, and shifting colors. Below it, Wellby and Seeker search three looted rooms and find letters and household accounts; a fourth door will not open. Riswynn finds little in the ruined ground-floor rooms. Reunited, they read one letter praising the binding of Hralgar on behalf of the Occulta Ludum and demanding secrecy. Another urges flight toward Chardon: a magical plague has struck, the emperor is dead, some Magistri have teleported away, and Drankor has fallen.

#### Short
Delwath enters the sealed room, discovers a mirror that shows its past, and escapes with recovered treasures when Seeker shrinks the door.

#### Intermediate
Delwath uses Misty Step to enter the untouched bedchamber, presumed to be Eudomes’s, but cannot open its door from inside. Among the coins and artwork he finds magical goggles, a mirror, and a stone held in a sculpted hand. The mirror shows silent visions of the room’s past without revealing a usable password. Seeker shrinks the door enough for the others to pass out the mirror and artwork and for Delwath to crawl free with the goggles.

#### Long
Delwath peers beneath the sealed door and spends his remaining spell slot on Misty Step, entering an intact bedchamber and study. Paintings, decorated vases, and coin pouches remain alongside purple-lensed goggles, a mirror, and a heavy sculpted hand holding a black stone. He throws the coin pouches out the window, but their ancient fabric breaks apart, scattering silver and copper below. Taking ten minutes to examine the room with fey sight, he detects divination magic in the mirror and goggles and transmutation in the stone.

When he asks the mirror how to open the door, it shows a man presumed to be Eudomes enchanting it and later speaking to open it. The vision is silent, leaving Delwath without the password; further images show a tall elf, a toddler, and an older man. With teleportation no longer available, he needs another exit. Seeker casts Reduce on the door, lifting its lower edge while its sides remain fixed. Through the gap they extract the mirror, paintings, and vases, and Delwath crawls out carrying the goggles. Wellby slips in for a hurried final search but finds nothing more before the opening closes.

#### Short
Their next attempt to dislodge the iron sphere provokes the water guardian, whose three steam creatures and three dust creatures they defeat.

#### Intermediate
Turning back to the iron sphere, Seeker heats its support while Riswynn chills it and pulls on a rope until it breaks loose. The water guardian responds with three steam creatures, followed by three dust creatures. Scalding bursts injure the fighters and dust blinds Delwath, but blades, arrows, Shatterstorm, and Riswynn’s spiritual hammer destroy all six attackers. The guardian survives beneath the water, and Kenzo continues meditating upstairs.

#### Long
With no sign of the eyes, the others return to the platform and try to break the iron sphere free. Seeker heats its support with Heat Metal while Riswynn repeatedly chills it with Shape Water and pulls on the grappling rope. Their second attempt succeeds, but the water guardian sends three imp-like steam creatures against them. Scalding breath wounds Seeker and Riswynn and breaks Seeker’s concentration. Delwath’s sword can hurt them, but the fire of Green-Flame Blade proves ineffective. Wellby brings one down with an arrow, releasing a burst of steam over the nearby fighters; Riswynn disengages and conjures a spiritual hammer.

As the steam creatures fall, three dust creatures join the fight and blind Delwath with a blast of dust. Kenzo hears the disturbance above but stays with Hralgar. Seeker withdraws and puts on his own goggles, while Delwath retreats long enough to clear his eyes. Wellby keeps shooting, and Riswynn attacks with Shatterstorm and Spiritual Weapon. Delwath cuts down one dust creature, Wellby kills another, and Riswynn finishes the last. All six summoned attackers are destroyed, but the water guardian withdraws alive beneath the pool. The loosened sphere still rests on the platform.

#### Short
When Hralgar’s control slips, they pull the sphere to the ground and flee through the darkening storm, tied together against the wind.

#### Intermediate
Kenzo hopes his meditation has built a bridge to Hralgar’s mind, but it has not freed him. The others gather their finds and delay over moving the sphere until the Fox and Hunter begins to fade. Kenzo warns them and leaves by the window. They pull the sphere off the platform, injuring Riswynn, Seeker, and Wellby; it lands undamaged and inert. After brief healing, they tie themselves together and follow Delwath into the storm, with only faint light from the driftglobe.

#### Long
Wellby finds Kenzo still meditating upstairs. Kenzo explains that he is trying to build a bridge to Hralgar’s consciousness, hoping to reach him even after he slips back into dreams. His presence seems to have eased the giant’s concentration, but has not loosened the binding. He elects to stay until danger forces him out. Below, Delwath and Riswynn gather the recovered treasures and scattered coins while Wellby and Seeker debate how to move the sphere.

The Fox and Hunter begins to fade. Kenzo warns the others that they must leave, then descends from the window as the wind rises. From below the platform, Riswynn, Seeker, and Wellby pull on the extended rope until the sphere drops toward them, injuring all three, Riswynn most severely. It lies on the rock intact and apparently inert. After healing, the party abandons the idea of resting, ties everyone together, and hurries away.

A lightning strike extinguishes their lights. Even the driftglobe’s Daylight produces only a weak glow through the darkness. Delwath leads them onward through wind, rain, and mist, but they cannot keep a straight course and lose track of how much time has passed.

#### Short
At dawn they emerge with the disk and mirror but without the coins, rejoin Vola, and conceal Hralgar’s plight before Riswynn rides west with her.

#### Intermediate
Dawn finds them on the mountain side of the storm, exhausted except for Kenzo, who can no longer reach Hralgar’s distant mind. The floating disk and mirror survive the escape, but the coins are lost. Circling back to Vola, they agree to keep their discoveries private while investigating whether Society explorers or Agata’s forces took the eyes. Vola describes a recent expansion of the Society’s collecting mission. Riswynn leaves westward with her to return family heirlooms to her clan, while the others prepare to rest and return to Karawa and Alesh.

#### Long
They emerge into dawn on the mountain side of the storm. Everyone except Kenzo is exhausted, and when he reaches for Hralgar’s mind it feels too distant to contact. The floating disk and mirror have survived, along with the goggles, paintings, letters, and poetry, but the silver and copper coins are gone. They spend a few more hours circling the storm, recover Riswynn’s grazing horse, and return to Vola and the remaining mounts. Vola confirms that only a day has passed.

Before reaching her, they agree to conceal what they found. The Society of the Open Scroll and Agata’s forces both seem possible leads to the missing eyes, but neither suspicion is proven. They intend to keep Vola as a contact while returning to Karawa to meet Alesh. Vola knows of no expedition into the new storm and plans to report back to Chardon about Agata’s activities. She describes the Society’s work as preserving knowledge, studying useful magic, and securing dangerous artifacts, adding that orders from above accelerated its collecting three or four years ago, including outside Dunmar; she does not know why.

Riswynn plans to take her family heirlooms back to her clan in the Yuvanti Mountains and warn her people of what they have learned. She mentions distant Brawnanvil relatives in Chardon and rumors that the Eternal Chalice of the Runepriest was sold to Chardonian traders. Kenzo deflects Vola’s questions about the storm, emphasizing its danger without revealing Hralgar. After agreeing to exchange news of Agata, Vola and Riswynn ride west together. The others prepare for a short rest before taking the road south toward Karawa.
