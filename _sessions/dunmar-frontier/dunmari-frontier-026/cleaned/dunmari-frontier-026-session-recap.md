#### Short
The party returns to a battered Karawa, where Candrosa admits concealing the loss of Shakun’s Heart and fabricating Delwath’s divine dream.

#### Intermediate
After five days in the desert, Kenzo, Wellby, Delwath, and Seeker return to find Karawa still under attack and refused aid by the Samraat. Candrosa receives them in private, confesses that Delwath’s supposed divine dream was his own magic, and acknowledges the missing Heart. As Kenzo challenges his secrecy, Seeker learns that the temple’s garden of living stone has stopped growing and the mesa itself is crumbling.

#### Long
Five days after leaving Pava and Avaras, Kenzo, Wellby, Delwath, and Seeker approach Karawa as the sun sinks beside the Red Mesa. A patrol of centaurs and Dunmari brings grim news: giant vultures, ankhegs, and skeletal gnolls have attacked the town, more defenders are dead, and Samraat Nayan Karnas will send no troops beyond the Hara River. In the temple, an exhausted Candrosa admits he has made a terrible mistake. Kenzo tells him that Bhishma revealed the disappearance of Shakun’s Heart, and Candrosa confesses that he used his staff to create the dream Delwath believed came from a god. His secrecy has been driven by fear, doubt, and a magic that makes some mysteries literally difficult to speak. Seeker asks about the weathered stone and learns of a garden beneath the temple where stone grows into the ingredients of healing powders and ointments. That growth has stopped. Kenzo demands an explanation for the information withheld throughout their dangerous travels, while Wellby tries to reassure the priest. Before telling the full story, Candrosa asks them to meet another traveler seeking the same answers.

#### Short
At the centaur camp, they meet Drikod, a stoneborn sent south to investigate disturbances in the mountains.

#### Intermediate
Candrosa leads them to Drikod, a taciturn stoneborn from Kunda in the Indalas. His village elders sent him south because nature in the mountains is becoming unsettled, and he has already helped defend Karawa. After introductions, the five return together to hear Candrosa’s account.

#### Long
At the centaur camp they find Drikod, an eight-foot stoneborn traveling with little more than a cloak and staff. His spare possessions contrast with the party’s dusty robes, elaborate armor, and crowded packs. Wellby offers a greeting in Stoneborn, Seeker introduces himself as a friend of Candrosa, and Delwath welcomes the rare company of a stoneborn. Drikod comes from Kunda, one of a network of villages in the Indalas mountains. His elders sent him to Karawa to investigate rumors and the growing disturbances he has felt in the wilderness. Since arriving after the attack on town, he has joined its defense and become familiar to the centaurs. Kenzo offers to spar with him as they prepare to return to the temple, where Candrosa is waiting to speak to them all.

#### Short
Candrosa reveals that Cintra took the Heart south to heal her daughter, and the party persuades him to share the secret with Tharandros.

#### Intermediate
With Drikod present, Candrosa explains that the Heart is a literal part of Shakun, sustaining the mystai’s protective magic. Cintra, the Supreme Oracle, took it to Shakun’s Wellspring with her sick daughter Jumi in early February; neither they nor the parties sent after them returned. The adventurers press Candrosa to tell Tharandros and agree to return the next evening for a ritual that will show them what words cannot explain.

#### Long
Candrosa describes the Heart as a piece of Shakun herself, amplifying divine magic and giving strength to the mystai who protect Dunmar. Cintra, the Supreme Oracle, had a daughter whose gifts appeared before she could speak. When Jumi began wasting away, Cintra believed Shakun was telling her to take the child and the Heart to a sacred grove at the southern end of the Red Mesa. She left in early February with two companions. Two later parties went to investigate, and none returned. Candrosa fears that Cintra and her daughter are dead, but believes the Heart is misplaced rather than destroyed and hopes it remains at the Wellspring. The grove lies beyond a narrow slot canyon reached by descending from the mesa. Angry at the months of silence, the party asks why the centaurs and Havdar have not been enlisted to help. They also distinguish the loss of Karawa’s protection from older troubles in the desert and mountains. Candrosa remains afraid of revealing sacred secrets, but agrees to tell Tharandros that night and, at Delwath’s suggestion, search his dreams for signs that outsiders know of the Heart. He offers a partial initiation the following evening: without the Heart he cannot complete the rite, but he can show them how its magic works.

#### Short
While awaiting the ritual, they gather clues from a hawk and the mirror, then spend the next day preparing in Karawa.

#### Intermediate
A hawk tells Wellby of skeletal figures lingering near the southern mesa, while the Mirror of the Past shows Kharsan’s broken glassware shattered by an enormous axe amid flames. The wielder is unseen, though the party suspects a fire giant. After another night under the driftglobes, Wellby commissions armor for Baxter, Delwath confirms that Tharandros has learned the truth, and Kenzo practices climbing the mesa.

#### Long
While the others settle into camp, Wellby calls a hawk and asks about the Wellspring and threats nearby. It cannot make sense of the precise destination, but shows him skeletal humanoids lingering for weeks near the southern end of the mesa. Delwath then presents the broken glass from Kharsan to the Mirror of the Past. They see the glass being made, days passing, a building burning, and an axe as tall as a person descending to shatter it. A flicker of flame on the blade suggests a fire giant, but they never see the wielder. Drikod judges that the destruction occurred in late spring or early summer and shares rumors of fire giants and other dangers reaching the mountains. The party keeps its focus on the Heart. They sleep with their driftglobes lit against the pursuing shadows, then spend the next day in town. Wellby arranges for horse barding to be fitted to Baxter in exchange for a map of their journey; it will take several days. Delwath confirms that Candrosa has spoken with Tharandros, who recognizes the missing Heart as the absence he sensed and describes the mesa as suffering. With no spare healing preparations available, they make do, while Kenzo practices bounding up the cliffs with his ring of jumping.

#### Short
The following evening, a shared vision reveals the mystai’s protective web tearing apart around the Heart’s empty place.

#### Intermediate
Candrosa leads them beneath the temple in pale robes marked with ochre crescents. After drinking a potion and lying beneath harmless blue flames, they pass through visions of Dunmar, destruction, rain, and their own memories. They glimpse the mystai joined in a web reaching across the land, its strands fraying around an empty center, before a presence seeming both woman and mesa asks for help.

#### Long
At dusk they return to Candrosa and three mystai. He warns that the ritual binds people through their shared experience of Dunmar, and he cannot know exactly what it will show outsiders. Dressed in pale robes and marked with ochre crescents, they descend through the temple, passing the entrance to the stone garden, into a cavern lit by blue torches. A masked mystai sets the pool ablaze with blue fire and offers each of them a thick potion. Candrosa lowers them beneath the water, where neither the absence of air nor the flames causes harm. Their vision sweeps across Dunmar, through an immense forest, fire and ash, a vast city dissolving into red flow, and cleansing rain. They become water, then cloud and rain again, before images of villages, fountains, lanterns, sea voyages, and seven stone thrones mingle with their sense of themselves. For a moment they see the mystai as points of light joined in a web stretching to the borders of Dunmar. Its center is empty, and the strands are tearing loose. Back on a cracking Red Mesa, they feel where the Heart should be. As darkness gathers, an impression of a woman who is also the mesa reaches toward them and whispers, “Will you help me?”

#### Short
They wake to shadow hunters killing the mystai and, caught without gear, use light and magic to destroy all seven attackers.

#### Intermediate
The shadows from Kharsan strike as the party wakes, killing two mystai and raising a new shadow from one victim. Seeker floods the cavern with magnesium light, Drikod hurls lightning, and Delwath gives Wellby a conjured bow. Even in the light, the attackers drain Seeker’s and Delwath’s strength. Candrosa’s protective bonds, Seeker’s healing, Kenzo’s planar strikes, and Delwath’s blasts finally destroy the remaining shadows.

#### Long
The vision breaks into screams. A shadow hunter drains a mystai before their eyes, and another shadow rises from her withered body; a second mystai falls as the attack continues. The party has left its armor and weapons upstairs. Drikod opens with lightning, destroying a shadow, while Seeker creates a floating magnesium fire in the pool to fill the cavern with dazzling light. Candrosa links Kenzo and Delwath with a protective bond, and Delwath conjures a magical bow for Wellby. Kenzo fights with his bare hands as spells and arrows strike through the shadows, but the bright light only hampers them: Seeker and Delwath are wounded and drained of strength. Another lightning bolt and Kenzo’s Fist of Planar Power reduce their numbers. Candrosa shields Seeker with Sanctuary and calls the surviving mystai back into the light, but a diverted attack leaves Delwath barely able to stand. Drikod conjures a Flaming Sphere, Seeker heals Delwath, and Candrosa casts Warding Bond. Kenzo pulls a shadow away from Delwath and finishes a foe with a punch; Delwath destroys the last two with Eldritch Blast. Wellby counts seven shadows gone and returns the conjured bow.

#### Short
With the pursuit apparently ended, they mourn the two dead mystai and prepare to seek the Heart, now able to perceive the frayed bonds it once sustained.

#### Intermediate
Candrosa grieves over the two bodies, each death another strand lost from the web. Kenzo offers to train with him, and the party discovers that the mysteries are now difficult to explain to anyone outside their shared experience. Candrosa found no sign in his dreams that the secret has been breached, though he cannot be sure. They discuss Grash, Cintra, and Agata Dustmother, then return upstairs after sunset to prepare for the funeral and the next day’s journey.

#### Long
Candrosa kneels beside the two withered bodies in tears. The party explains that the shadows followed them from Kharsan and believes all eight originally sent after them are now destroyed: two in the desert and six here, along with the seventh shadow raised from a dead mystai. For Candrosa, each death is another connection lost, with no way to initiate replacements while the Heart is missing. Kenzo offers to train with him when the time is right. Though they are not fully initiated, Candrosa welcomes them into the temple, and they begin to understand his difficulty speaking: they can discuss the web among themselves, but cannot readily imagine explaining it to someone who has not experienced it. Candrosa’s dreams showed no foreign intrusion, although he remains uncertain. The party tells Drikod about Grash’s search for artifacts and considers whether the Heart might be involved; Agata Dustmother’s possible role and Cintra’s reasons for leaving remain questions. Seeker recognizes the seven thrones as his own coming-of-age vision. As they return through the darkened cloisters, the movements of the mystai reveal faint connections between them, frayed and trailing into emptiness. They settle in for the funeral and leave the journey across the mesa until morning.
