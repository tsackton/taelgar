# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 6
- Beat Coverage: 12/12

## scene-001 | Lizards at Gomat Oasis

- Beat IDs: b01, b02, b03
- Rationale: The approach, battle, aftermath, and overnight rest are one continuous effort to secure the abandoned camp at Gomat.

### Beats

- b01 | Lizards at Gomat Oasis: At sunset, the party reaches Gomat Oasis and finds three giant lizards feeding among dead sheep and abandoned tents before committing to an attack.
- b02 | Battle at Gomat: The party battles and kills the three giant lizards at Gomat, whose relentless attacks reinforce the sense that the animals are behaving unnaturally.
- b03 | Night at the Oasis: After finding no magic or human remains at the damaged camp, the party considers the lizards' strange violence and spends an uneventful night at Gomat Oasis.

## scene-002 | Akan Returns to Gomat

- Beat IDs: b04, b05
- Rationale: The morning investigation leads directly into Akan's return, his account of the attack, and the recovery of his surviving family.

### Beats

- b04 | Tracks in Morning Light: Morning light reveals the scale of the attack and lizard tracks heading east, while a wounded rider approaches Gomat from the northwest.
- b05 | Akan Returns to Gomat: Akan returns to Gomat, recounts the lizard attack that killed four of his family, and recovers the survivors and their supplies before the party follows the creatures east.

## scene-003 | East into the Nashtkar

- Beat IDs: b06
- Rationale: Leaving Gomat begins a distinct travel situation centered on following the lizard tracks and finding further evidence of unnatural violence.

### Beats

- b06 | East into the Nashtkar: Following the lizard tracks east from Gomat, the party travels the ruined road through the Nashtkar and finds more animals killed with inexplicable excess violence.

## scene-004 | Crossing Daraar Canyon

- Beat IDs: b07, b08
- Rationale: Scouting, camping above, and crossing the canyon all concern the same physical obstacle despite the overnight date boundary.

### Beats

- b07 | Camping Above Daraar Canyon: The party reaches Daraar Canyon, scouts its hazardous descent, finds evidence of a concealed earlier camp, and sleeps uneasily above the western rim.
- b08 | Crossing Daraar Canyon: The party shelters Delwath's horse, descends Daraar Canyon, and finds many animal trails converging on the eastern slope, including tracks from giant lizards and hyenas.

## scene-005 | The Bloodthirsty Wilds

- Beat IDs: b09
- Rationale: The terrain east of the canyon presents a new situation in which pervasive animal aggression accompanies the approach to the distant fort.

### Beats

- b09 | The Bloodthirsty Wilds: East of Daraar Canyon, even ordinary insects seem bloodthirsty, and the party finds a poisoned giant hyena beside a dead giant scorpion while approaching a ruined fort.

## scene-006 | Skeletons at the Ruined Fort

- Beat IDs: b10, b11, b12
- Rationale: The fort survey, skeleton attack, and post-battle investigation form one continuous encounter at the same site with the same immediate goal.

### Beats

- b10 | Ruined Fort and Mounds: The party surveys a ruined Dunmari fort and the grassy and rubble mounds outside its gate until Kenzo senses undead nearby.
- b11 | Skeletons from the Earth: Four skeletons claw out of the rubble mound and attack, but the party destroys them in a brief battle outside the fort.
- b12 | Reading the Disturbed Ground: The party finds signs of recent digging around the skeleton mound, identifies the ruins as a Great War-era Dunmari fort, and enters the gate as the session ends.
