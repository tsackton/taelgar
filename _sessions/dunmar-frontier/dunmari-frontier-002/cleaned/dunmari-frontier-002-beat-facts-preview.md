## Akan Returns to Gomat

**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Akan returns to Gomat, recounts the lizard attack that killed four of his family, and recovers the survivors and their supplies before the party follows the creatures east.
**Long Summary**: The rider identifies himself as Akan and explains that four giant lizards attacked his extended family during the previous day's hottest hours. After learning that the party killed the remaining three, Akan retrieves the survivors while the group gathers the scattered belongings and finds his dead sister's holy symbol of Chidya.
