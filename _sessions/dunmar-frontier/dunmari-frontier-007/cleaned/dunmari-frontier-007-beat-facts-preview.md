## Tracking the Gnolls from Karawa
*beat-001*

**Date**: 1748-04-07
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party leaves evacuating Karawa, follows the attackers’ tracks to a junction, and chooses Raven’s Hold over pursuing a westbound gnoll band.
**Long Summary**: With fresh mounts and ten days of food, the party prepares to leave Karawa, adding oil for the dangerous vines and caged rabbits as possible bait. Seeker proposes using a runic die to choose their pace, but they adopt Delwath’s plan to track cautiously before riding hard. Beyond the main trade road, Wellby and Delwath find an eaten antelope and intersecting trails: a larger group toward the northeast, a smaller gnoll band toward the west, and the trail to Karawa. A split from a larger force is plausible, but the direction of travel is uncertain. Unable to warn the evacuees remotely or be sure of overtaking the westbound band, they choose Raven’s Hold.
**Location**:
- Karawa -> Gnoll trail junction north of Karawa (Dunmar): Tracking across the main trade road into the northern plains.
  Note: The party follows tracks; it does not encounter a new hostile group in this beat.
**NPCs**: none
**Items**:
- Oil (encountered): Acquired for a possible attack on the vines at Raven’s Hold; it is not used here.
- Caged rabbits (encountered): Small caged animals obtained as possible bait for the bloodthirsty creatures; later action identifies the bait as rabbits.
- Runic die (encountered): Seeker presents a six-sided stone cube as a proposed way to choose travel pace; the group instead reaches agreement.
**Organizations**: none

## Maps of Raven's Hold

**Date**: 1748-04-07
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: At their first camp, the party studies Riswynn’s maps and weighs possible approaches to Raven’s Hold.

At camp, Riswynn produces two maps drawn by her uncle. They distinguish the Dunmari fortress from the dwarven outpost across the valley and discuss the dangerous vines inside the outer wall. ## Breaking the Ambush
*beat-007*

**Date**: 1748-04-10
**Time Window**: night
**Combat**: yes (end)
**Main Enemies**:
- Gnoll ambushers: The same six-gnoll camp ambush spans beats 005-007; these are successive phases, not separate encounters.
**Combat Notes**: All six ambushers die: Kenzo kills two in one runic burst, Wellby finishes another, and Riswynn kills the fleeing last survivor. The party suspects this was the band Shani described, but does not independently establish that identity.
**Short Summary**: Kenzo’s inspired strike turns the fight, and the party kills all six gnolls before searching the bodies and completing its rest.
**Long Summary**: Kenzo charges from cover, strikes with his quarterstaff, and follows with a blow empowered by Seeker’s runic inspiration; the bursting Rune of Destruction kills two gnolls. Riswynn and Delwath engage another attacker, Seeker sends healing to Riswynn, and Wellby finishes that foe with an arrow. The bodies carry crude weapons, barbed arrows, and a goat-knucklebone necklace, but no treasure. Comparing the skilled archers with the more easily distracted attackers, the party recognizes that a frontal assault on a larger force would be dangerous and finishes its long rest.

  Note: The same camp as beats 005-006; the party searches the defeated ambushers and resumes sleeping here.

*beat-009*

**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: A fleeing centaur herd brings warnings of poisoned water, and Tharandros recognizes the shadow tethered to Delwath.
**Long Summary**: About two dozen tired centaurs approach the morning camp from the east. Their leader, Tharandros, addresses Delwath in Elvish and warns that the northern water has been sickened by demons, forcing the herd west. ## Tethers and a Message South
*beat-010*

**Date**: 1748-04-12
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Tharandros explains the spreading poison and planar tethers, then agrees to warn Karawa after hearing of its Dreamworld magic.
**Long Summary**: Kenzo asks about Raven’s Hold in Dunmari, with Delwath translating when Tharandros shifts into Elvish. Tharandros believes the poison spreads from Raven’s Hold but is unsure; it has grown from one stream ten days earlier to every watering place the herd visited yesterday. Kenzo switches to Drankorian to ask about the tether’s power. Tharandros describes echoes between worlds and perceives a cord Kenzo can pull; Seeker relates this to runic resonance and illustrates the idea with an illusion. Tharandros initially refuses Delwath’s request to carry a warning to Karawa, prioritizing his herd’s safety. Delwath’s appeal to its deeper magic and Wellby’s claim that people there walk into the Dreamworld persuade him. He promises to carry the message and asks them to heal the land. Kenzo splits a clod of earth with him as a token of shared purpose; the herd turns south while the party faces the choice of routes toward Raven’s Hold.
**Location**:
- Dunmar: Morning camp below the foothills near Raven’s Hold.
  Note: The conversation continues at the same camp as beat 009; the closing passage leaves the party’s route choice unresolved and does not establish entry into the fortress.
**NPCs**:
- Tharandros (encountered): Centaur leader. Speaks Dunmari with difficulty and also understands Elvish and Drankorian. Believes, without certainty, that the poisoned water spreads from Raven’s Hold. Agrees to take the warning to Karawa after hearing of Dreamworld travel, and asks the party to clear the water and restore the pastures.
**Items**:
- Split clod of earth (encountered): Kenzo breaks a piece of soil in half and passes part to Tharandros while promising to work toward clean land; no magical property is asserted.
**Organizations**: none
