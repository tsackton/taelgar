#### Short
Leaving Karawa as its people flee west, the party tracks the attackers north, chooses a direct course toward Raven’s Hold, and studies Riswynn’s maps at camp.

#### Intermediate
As Karawa evacuates, the party leaves with fresh mounts, provisions, oil, and caged rabbits for bait. Delwath and Wellby follow the attackers’ trail beyond the trade road, where tracks branch west and northeast. With no reliable way to warn the evacuees or overtake the westbound gnolls, they ride toward Raven’s Hold. That evening, Riswynn’s maps reveal a possible hidden route through the dwarven outpost, though finding and entering it remain uncertain.

#### Long
Karawa is packing for flight when the party gathers its mounts and supplies. Seeker suggests settling their travel pace with a runic die, but Delwath’s proposal wins agreement: follow the attackers’ tracks cautiously, then ride hard if they lead toward Raven’s Hold. Oil might help against the dangerous vines, and caged rabbits might distract the creatures. Beyond the ancient trade road, Wellby and Delwath find an eaten antelope and a confusion of tracks. A larger force may have divided here, sending smaller bands toward Karawa and the west, but they cannot establish exactly what happened. Pursuing the westbound gnolls offers no assurance of reaching them in time to protect the evacuees, and the party has no ready means of sending a warning. They commit to Raven’s Hold, cutting northeast across the plains under Wellby’s guidance. At their first camp, Riswynn unfolds two maps drawn by her uncle. The Dunmari fortress and dwarven outpost stand across the valley from one another; a hidden dwarven exit might offer a way around the known vine field inside the outer wall. Whether they can find it or enter through it will have to wait until they see the ground.

#### Short
Farther north, Shani’s fleeing herders describe a gnoll attack and an unmoving lightning storm; the party heals one of them and follows the road toward the attackers.

#### Intermediate
The next two days take them through rich but strangely empty grazing country. A distant dust cloud proves to be Shani’s herders, found by Wellby and an invisible Delwath while Seeker conceals the horses. Seeker heals a broken arm, and Shani explains that lightning fires delayed the group before six gnolls drove them south at the cost of two horses and many goats. She agrees to carry word toward Tokra. The party continues north along the road, hoping to find the gnolls, and camps in an unnaturally silent landscape.

#### Long
The plains grow greener as they ride north, but torn animal remains and nervous antelope replace the herders who ought to be grazing their animals here. On the third day, dust rises from a group traveling south along the road. Wellby and Kenzo’s shared hundred-gold spyglass offers only modest magnification, so Seeker shapes an earthen blind for the horses while Wellby and an invisible Delwath creep closer. They find riders, goats, and two injured people on foot. Wellby steps out to greet them; their spokeswoman, Shani, is startled to find anyone heading north. Seeker mends one herder’s broken arm with a quick image of glowing bones while Delwath watches the road unseen. Shani’s family was delayed by fires from a vast lightning storm that has remained west of Raven’s Hold for a week or more. Then six gnolls attacked at night, costing them two horses and a third of their flock before they escaped. The party tells her of Karawa’s evacuation and asks her to carry word of their journey toward Tokra. After the herders leave, Riswynn wonders whether the storm marks a rift between worlds. Raven’s Hold remains their destination, and Wellby urges them along the road to seek the gnolls before they can pursue Shani. By evening, even birds and insects seem absent around their camp.

#### Short
Six gnolls ambush them during the night, but healing, cover, and Kenzo’s explosive runic strike turn a punishing fight into victory.

#### Intermediate
Arrows strike Delwath on watch and wake the camp. Poison and barbs nearly bring him down before Riswynn heals him, while Seeker offers a rabbit on his floating disc as a brief distraction. The party fights from behind the rocks; Kenzo and Riswynn kill two gnolls, and Seeker burns another with Heat Metal. Kenzo then charges out with Seeker’s runic inspiration and kills two foes in one concussive burst. Wellby finishes another, and Riswynn stops the last as it flees. Shaken by the archers’ coordination, they rule out a frontal assault on any larger force and finish resting.

#### Long
An arrow catches Delwath during his watch, and his warning brings the others out of their bedrolls. The gnoll archers exploit the darkness, driving poisoned and barbed arrows into him until he has only four hit points left. Riswynn waits at the edge of his rock with Cure Wounds ready; he reaches her, receives the healing, and drops prone. Kenzo also learns the cost of standing exposed on a boulder. Seeker flicks his coin into the air, unfolds it into a floating copper disc, and serves up a rabbit as bait before taking cover and sending Kenzo a rune of inspiration. From behind the rocks, Kenzo slams a foe into stone with blue force, and Riswynn kills another with Sacred Flame. One gnoll takes the rabbit, bites its head off, and keeps moving. Kenzo finishes his wounded target with a quarterstaff before retreating to drink a healing potion. Seeker heats a buckle in the rabbit-bearer’s armor, making it howl and drop the carcass. As the survivors close in, Kenzo charges between two wounded gnolls. His follow-up strike releases the Rune of Destruction, and its concussive burst kills both. Wellby’s arrow finishes another attacker after Riswynn and Delwath engage it; when the last gnoll runs, Riswynn brings it down with holy energy. All six are dead. Their crude equipment and the apparent leader’s bloody goat-knucklebone necklace yield no treasure. More troubling is how effectively the archers fought: another, larger band could overwhelm them. They return to their watches and complete the interrupted rest.

#### Short
Afterward, a vulture reports movement at Raven’s Hold, and the party approaches the foothills with the distant storm to the west, camping where the wind briefly carries sulfur and decay.

#### Intermediate
In the morning, Seeker questions a vulture feeding on the gnolls. It has seen things moving at the fortress but little on the plains, and cannot say who occupies the hold. The party rides closer before deciding how to approach. By the following evening, the distant waterfall and outer wall are visible, with the stationary storm hanging to the west. They consider the road into the mountain pass, then make camp as a brief north wind brings the smell of sulfur and rotting flesh.

#### Long
When the scavengers arrive, Seeker takes time to speak with one in its own tongue. The vulture is much more interested in eating the dead gnolls than in reconnaissance, but it does describe moving things at Raven’s Hold and an otherwise empty plain. It cannot identify the occupants or give useful numbers, and it cannot fly at the storm. The party decides to get closer and judge the approach for themselves. As the northward ride continues, the great thundercloud hangs low to the west while the mountains, a distant waterfall, and the valley’s outer wall come into view ahead. The road climbs toward a low pass beside the valley, offering another approach to consider before committing to the wall. They camp below the foothills. At sunset, the wind turns north and carries a faint mixture of sulfur, burnt flesh, and decay across the silent grassland; then it fades, leaving them uncertain whether they can still smell it.

#### Short
The next morning, Tharandros’s centaurs warn of spreading poison and planar tethers, then turn toward Karawa with a message while the party prepares to approach Raven’s Hold.

#### Intermediate
About two dozen centaurs arrive from the east. Their leader, Tharandros, warns that poisoned water is driving them west and recognizes the shadow bound to Delwath. He suspects Raven’s Hold is the source of the contamination but cannot be certain. Through Elvish, Dunmari, and Drankorian, they discuss the Abyss and the power of contact between worlds. Tharandros initially refuses to detour toward Karawa, but Delwath’s appeal to its deeper magic and Wellby’s mention of Dreamworld travel persuade him. Kenzo shares a broken clod of earth as a pledge to help restore the land, and the herd turns south.

#### Long
By morning, another group is racing toward their camp from the east: about two dozen tired centaurs. Their leader, Tharandros, speaks first to Delwath in Elvish. The northern water is poisoned, he says, bound by a tether to the Abyss; the herd is fleeing west to find safe pastures. He attributes that realm to ancient human beliefs in punishment, sustained by nightmares. Delwath’s claim to experience of other worlds draws a more personal warning: Tharandros can see the shadow tethered to him, and fears it could grow to destroy both Delwath and his god. Kenzo joins the discussion in Dunmari, relying on Delwath when Tharandros turns back to Elvish. The centaur believes the poison spreads from Raven’s Hold, though he has not confirmed its source. Ten days ago he found it in one stream; yesterday it was everywhere the herd stopped. Kenzo’s use of Drankorian surprises him, and his questions draw an explanation of echoes between worlds becoming sources of power. Tharandros sees a cord Kenzo can pull; Seeker answers with his own idea of runic resonance and an illusory map. Yet the centaur’s first concern is his herd, and he initially rejects Delwath’s request to carry a warning south. Delwath points to the deeper magic remaining in Karawa, and Wellby finally catches his interest by saying its people can walk into the Dreamworld and return. Tharandros agrees to investigate and deliver the message, asking them in turn to clear the water and restore the pastures. Kenzo breaks a clod of earth and gives him half, promising their best effort toward that shared purpose. The centaurs wheel south toward Karawa. Ahead of the party lie the mountain road and the valley of Raven’s Hold, with their choice of approach still to make.
