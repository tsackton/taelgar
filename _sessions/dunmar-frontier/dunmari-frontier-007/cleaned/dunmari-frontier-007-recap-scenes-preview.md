## scene-001 | The Road to Raven’s Hold

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002
- Rationale: The user grouped departure, tracking, route choice, and the first camp’s map discussion into one opening journey-and-planning scene.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Tracking the Gnolls from Karawa: The party leaves evacuating Karawa, follows the attackers’ tracks to a junction, and chooses Raven’s Hold over pursuing a westbound gnoll band.
- beat-002 | Maps of Raven's Hold: At their first camp, the party studies Riswynn’s maps and weighs possible approaches to Raven’s Hold.

## scene-002 | Shani’s Warning

- Beat IDs: beat-003, beat-004

### Beats

- beat-004 | Shani's Warning on the Road: Shani describes gnoll attacks and an unmoving lightning storm, and the party heals a herder before continuing north to seek the attackers.

## scene-003 | Gnolls in the Night

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006, beat-007
- Rationale: All three beats are tactical phases of one uninterrupted camp ambush. Keep the victory, body search, and immediate aftermath with the fight.

### Beats

- beat-005 | Arrows in the Night: Gnolls ambush the sleeping camp, nearly bring down Delwath with arrows, and draw the party into a desperate defense around the rocks.
- beat-006 | Holding Cover Against the Gnolls: The party kills two gnolls and shifts behind cover while the remaining attackers press around the camp.
- beat-007 | Breaking the Ambush: Kenzo’s inspired strike turns the fight, and the party kills all six gnolls before searching the bodies and completing its rest.

## scene-004 | Vulture News and Sulfurous Winds

### Beats

- beat-008 | Vulture News Near Raven's Hold: A vulture reports movement at Raven’s Hold, and the party approaches the foothills through empty plains before smelling sulfur and rotting flesh at camp.

## scene-005 | Tharandros and the Poisoned Land

- Beat IDs: beat-009, beat-010
- Rationale: The warning about Delwath’s shadow, discussion of planar tethers, and negotiation over carrying a message are one continuous conversation with Tharandros and his herd.

### Beats

- beat-009 | Centaurs and Delwath's Shadow: A fleeing centaur herd brings warnings of poisoned water, and Tharandros recognizes the shadow tethered to Delwath.
- beat-010 | Tethers and a Message South: Tharandros explains the spreading poison and planar tethers, then agrees to warn Karawa after hearing of its Dreamworld magic.
