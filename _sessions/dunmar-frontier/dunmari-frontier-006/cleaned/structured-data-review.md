### Gnolls and maw creatures at the northern camp

Delwath materializes his sword as three gnolls and three maw creatures approach.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Seeker puts the leading gnoll to sleep and inspires Delwath. Wellby wounds another gnoll; Kenzo’s planar strike deals half damage after a successful Strength save. The enemies close in. |
| 2 | Heat Metal kills the first awake gnoll. Riswynn ignites a maw, which extinguishes the fire. Another bites Kenzo; Delwath wounds the second gnoll and Ander enters the battlefield. |
| 3 | Seeker heals Kenzo and excavates beneath the gnoll, which lands upright. The maws wound Kenzo again. Wellby and Kenzo finish one maw; Delwath kills the second gnoll. Ander chants to be eaten. |
| 4 | Seeker slows Ander and heals Kenzo. Riswynn destroys another maw. The last maw drops dodging Kenzo unconscious; he fails a death save. Delwath guards him and wounds the maw. |
| 5 | Riswynn revives Kenzo with 15 healing. The maw bites Delwath and Hex ends. Seeker’s restored turn creates frightening vines. Delwath spends inspiration to hit and adds thunder damage after a failed Constitution save; Ander then faints. |
| 6 | Seeker heals Delwath for 4; Riswynn kills the last maw with her warhammer. |

Initiative ends with two gnolls and all three maws dead; one gnoll remains asleep for roughly thirty seconds. Its capture and later execution occur outside the combat segment.
