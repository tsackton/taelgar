# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 5
- Beat Coverage: 16/16

## scene-001 | Plans and Mysteries

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002, beat-003
- Rationale: The party establishes the expedition plan and gathers information about Raven's Hold, Ander's affliction, the anonymous runebook, and the temple's strange stone. These inquiries form the first preparation scene, ending when attention turns to supplies and practical arrangements.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Preparing to Leave Karawa: The party prepares to fortify Shakun's temple and investigate Raven's Hold while Karawa plans to evacuate west.
- beat-002 | Raven's Hold and the Anonymous Gift: Wellby learns more about Raven's Hold and Ander's affliction, while Seeker finds that the donor of his runebook remains unknown.
- beat-003 | Living Stone and Northern Gods: Seeker examines apparently growing stone around the temple, and Candrosa directs Kenzo to Alesh for better knowledge of the northern ruins.

## scene-002 | Making Ready to Leave

- Beat IDs: beat-004, beat-005, beat-006
- Rationale: Buying supplies, arranging the rendezvous with Alesh, seeing her off, and fortifying the temple put the departure plans into practice. Alesh's historical information stays with her farewell, and the next day's construction closes the preparation sequence before the festival gathering.

### Beats

- beat-004 | Outfitting the Expedition: The party buys discounted healing supplies, receives a scroll for returned relics, and finishes equipping the expedition.
- beat-005 | Alesh's Southern Scouting Mission: Alesh leaves to scout south after arranging a roughly three-week rendezvous and sharing Raven's Hold's history as a dwarven trading outpost.
- beat-006 | Fortifying the Temple: Seeker spends most of the following day strengthening the temple with masonry and earth-shaping magic.

## scene-003 | Riswynn and the Festival of Rebirth

- Beat IDs: beat-007, beat-008
- Rationale: The pre-festival meal and the ceremony that immediately follows form the opening evening of Rebirth. Riswynn joins the party before they attend the shrine burning together; the westward embers close this evening before the next day's attack.

### Beats

- beat-007 | Riswynn Joins the Party: Ikram introduces Riswynn, whose quest for a Brawnanvil heirloom gives her reason to join the expedition to Raven's Hold.
- beat-008 | Jeevali Blesses the Westward Journey: Suma interprets the burning shrine's westward embers as Jeevali's blessing for the planned evacuation.

## scene-004 | The Attack on Karawa

- Beat IDs: beat-009, beat-010, beat-011, beat-012
- Rationale: The renewed festival supplies the opening context for one uninterrupted defense against the gnolls and mouth creatures. Ander's arrival and Kenzo's fall change the stakes within the same battle, which ends when initiative stops.

### Beats

- beat-009 | The Festival Comes Under Attack: The renewed shrine's blessing is interrupted by an attack from the north, and the party meets three gnolls and three mouth creatures.
- beat-010 | Force, Flame, and Closing Jaws: The party brings force and fire against the attackers, but Kenzo comes under pressure from the mouth creatures.
- beat-011 | Ander Enters the Battle: Ander approaches the mouths seeking to be consumed as the party struggles to keep Kenzo standing and destroys two of the creatures.
- beat-012 | Kenzo Falls and Riswynn Saves Him: Riswynn revives the fallen Kenzo, Seeker's image of vines stops Ander, and the party kills the final mouth creature.

## scene-005 | Aftermath and Departure

- Beat IDs: beat-013, beat-014, beat-015, beat-016
- Rationale: The captives, the creatures' possible origins, Havdar's report, and Kenzo's recovery are the overlapping consequences of the battle. The brief dawn departure completes the resulting preparations as a closing passage: the villagers head west and the party follows the tracks north.

### Beats

- beat-013 | Captives and Clues to the Hunger: The party fails to communicate with its captive, recalls possible planar origins for the mouths, and returns Ander to his family.
- beat-014 | Havdar Urges Immediate Evacuation: Havdar reports killing two more attackers and urges departure at first light as the village and temple prepare through the night.
- beat-015 | Kenzo Returns from the Void: Kenzo emerges from meditation with brilliant blue eyes, while Seeker collects a tooth and Delwath finds the attackers' northern trail.
- beat-016 | Karawa Empties at Dawn: At dawn, Karawa's people begin their westward evacuation while the party follows the attackers' tracks north.
