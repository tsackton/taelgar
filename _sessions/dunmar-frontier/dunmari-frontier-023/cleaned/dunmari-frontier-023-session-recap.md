#### Short
Kenzo returns from his initiation with Bhishma’s message: a missing stone heart must be restored so Shakun can reach Karawa again.

#### Intermediate
Supported by Seeker, Kenzo rejoins Wellby and Delwath in the dining hall, pale and exhausted. He recounts accepting the sacrifice that made him a Mystai of Bhishma, while Seeker stopped short of that final step. Afterward, Bhishma answered his question about Shakun with a message to recover something taken from Karawa, accompanied by a vision of a beating reddish stone heart. The party suspects Candrosa knows more, but neither the heart’s whereabouts nor the circumstances of its loss are clear. Kenzo rests and drinks a healing potion before they continue.

#### Long
Seeker brings Kenzo back to the dining hall, supporting him as he staggers toward the companions who have been resting among the dead manticores. They describe the initiation as a journey through Bhishma’s own story, ending with the choice to give their lives for Jeevali. Seeker declined; Kenzo accepted, returning weak but newly initiated into the Mystai of Bhishma. In the vision that followed, crystal butterflies formed Bhishma’s answer about Shakun: she remained with Bhishma but could not reach Karawa, because something that helped her reach the world had been taken. Kenzo saw a beating heart of reddish stone and was told to find the missing object and bring it back. The others consider whether it could be a focus of divine power, distinct from Kenzo’s jade fragment. Candrosa may know what happened, but their suspicions of theft, coercion, or concealment remain unproven. With several hours of daylight left, Kenzo spends hit dice and drinks a healing potion, while Seeker restores a spell slot with his pearl.

#### Short
While he recovers, Wellby searches the ruins; afterward, Kenzo’s ritual greeting wins the party passage through the tower’s ghostly guards.

#### Intermediate
Made invisible by Delwath, Wellby explores the dormitories and workshops, finding coins, a monk’s diary, and blank vellum prepared for enchantment. The diary ends with its unnamed author choosing to defend the monastery during the Great War. Reunited, the party examines the courtyard’s magical fountain and the Hall’s intricate carvings. A ward rebuffs Kenzo at a side entrance, so they try the tower instead. Four ghosts block the door until Kenzo gives Bhishma’s initiatory greeting. Recognizing a brother, they admit the party but can no longer remember their own names.

#### Long
While Kenzo recovers, Delwath makes Wellby invisible to search the outbuildings. The looted dormitories yield 24 silver and 160 copper coins, along with an unnamed monk’s diary describing the gathering danger of the Great War. Its last entry records the author’s decision to stay and protect the monastery’s work. In the workshops, Wellby finds the remains of paper-making equipment and a remarkably well-preserved blank sheet; Seeker recognizes vellum prepared to hold a spell. Kenzo keeps the diary. Wellby also examines a dry cistern beneath the well. After the rest, they inspect the courtyard fountain, where a polished petrified-wood figure produces clean water through conjuration magic despite the vandalism around it. Beyond, countless tiny carvings cover the Hall of Stories with scenes of Dunmari life. Its side doors are warded, and Kenzo narrowly avoids a burst of radiant energy when he tries one. The tower door opens freely, but four hooded ghosts stand in the way. Kenzo kneels and gives the sign of Bhishma’s initiates, explaining that they seek knowledge of what happened thirty years ago. The ghosts recognize him as a brother and let them pass, urging haste. Unable to remember their names, they can offer little else, and Kenzo promises to try to help them remember. The party crosses the tower’s dark ground-floor corridor toward the Hall.

#### Short
Inside the Hall of Stories, linked memories reveal an expedition to seal corruption with Rai’s help, until a despairing presence drives them out.

#### Intermediate
Among pillars carved with names, the party discovers a web of memories that can be navigated through associations with their own experiences. They witness a monk’s initiation, the last defense that created the ghostly guardians, and an audience with Dimitaur and a shadowed jade figure they associate with Rai. Further memories reveal opposition to the expedition and a route south to Drankor. A fearful mind intrudes when Kenzo recalls his own sense of purpose, imposing visions of Dunmar’s destruction. Seeker breaks through with a childhood memory of overhearing a debate, reaching the masters’ decision to travel east and seal extraplanar corruption with Rai’s promised help. The party compares this with Kenzo’s earlier visions of the injured jade hand, but cannot establish whether the portal closed or what became of Rai. As the Hall darkens and its despair strengthens, they leave.

#### Long
Within the Hall, high windows illuminate pillars covered with names. Seeker and Delwath glimpse skeletons along the walls before the room fades into a young monk’s memory of preparing for initiation. A moment from Seeker’s own childhood has drawn them into it; around them stretches a web of further memories, reachable through similar connections. Delwath recalls the battlefield where he alone survived, and the Hall answers with the monastery’s defense against an undead army. They experience a defender’s death and refusal to abandon his duty: rising as a spirit, he destroys the undead commander and charges four fallen companions to guard the monastery until the monks return.

Seeker’s recollection of Frankar’s forbidden divination leads to an apprentice accompanying senior monks into a vast domed chamber. Dimitaur flies from the shadows, a glimpse of jade appears, and a voice speaks words the apprentice cannot understand. Outside lies a monumental ruined city, apparently Drankor; the monks leave excited by the prospect of healing Dunmar. Kenzo recognizes the audience from an earlier vision seen from the opposite side and connects the figure with Rai. Delwath then searches through memories of regret and retreat, from dissent against Dasa’s ancient campaign to an older master warning that the new expedition could worsen the land’s corruption. Wellby’s childhood memories of travel uncover a father describing the road to Drankor: along a dry river from southwest of Bas Udda to the Hara, south through desert, then down cliffs and waterfalls toward forested ruins.

Kenzo’s attempt to recall the purpose he felt when Kaeso sent him toward the Lakan Mystai goes wrong. A battle against shadows becomes a vision of Dunmar’s extinction, and he suffers psychic pain. Another mind is caught in these terrors and draws them into its despair. Seeker counters it by remembering a childhood glimpse of his parents debating an important undertaking. The scene shifts to an apprentice eavesdropping on the masters: they mean to travel east, locate the center of corruption reaching from an evil realm, and seal it so the land might heal. Though some fear the consequences, most trust Rai to guide and assist them.

The party compares this discovery with Kenzo’s earlier mirror vision: purple light pulling a fissure shut, a dark lash striking toward a jade hand, then a separate image of jade falling onto desert ground. The missing interval leaves the outcome unknown. His dream of a jade-armed figure shattering raises more questions, including whether Chardonian agents might be seeking scattered pieces of Rai. No memory settles these theories, and the expedition’s masters never returned to preserve their own account. With about an hour until sunset, shadows spread across the Hall and the presence’s despair grows harder to ignore.

#### Short
Climbing the tower, they survive a lightning ward and recover scrolls of Tongues, Sending, and Speak with Dead.

#### Intermediate
Back in the tower, the party searches a writing room and recalls Saka as a possible surviving source of information about the expedition. Stone doors on the next floor conceal a lightning glyph, which injures everyone when opened. Beyond it, the mostly emptied library still holds three spell scrolls: Tongues, Sending, and Speak with Dead. They consider contacting Rai or questioning the skeletons but use neither spell. Passing through an empty council chamber, they follow faint rustling toward a bedroom at the top of the tower.

#### Long
Leaving the Hall’s oppressive memories behind, the party climbs the tower through a writing room with ink-stained desks and a few remaining blank scroll sheets. Kenzo recalls that Saka, an older master who declined to join the expedition, was last reported north and west of Tokra. They wonder whether she was the dissenting woman in the memory and whether finding her might yield answers. On the next floor, opening a pair of stone doors releases a lightning ward: Kenzo, Seeker, and Delwath each take fourteen damage, and Wellby takes seven. The protected library beyond has mostly empty shelves and hanging book chains, but a search uncovers scrolls of Tongues, Sending, and Speak with Dead. Seeker records them while the others consider a message to Rai or questions for the skeletons downstairs. With forty minutes of daylight remaining, they continue upward instead, crossing an empty council chamber and checking a plain wooden door behind which they hear faint rustling.

#### Short
At the top, Karmana describes the growing darkness, and the party plans to scout Kharsan before returning toward Karawa.

#### Intermediate
The bedroom holds Karmana’s ghost, recognizable as the defender from the Hall’s memory. Once satisfied that Kenzo has truly been initiated, he explains that Sajan, the former archivist, bound herself to the Hall during the Great War and became trapped in its nightmares. Karmana knows danger has increased since the expedition disappeared, with a further wave of darkness from Kharsan less than a year ago, but cannot explain its cause. His own purpose leaves little room for memories beyond the monastery’s defense. The party agrees to explore the temple before sunset, camp, then reconnoiter Kharsan and return toward Karawa via Agata Dust Mother. These are still plans when the session ends.

#### Long
Beyond the door, a ghost paces through a simple bed, his features blurred by years of fading memory. They recognize the monk who died defending the monastery. He questions Kenzo’s use of the order’s signs, then accepts that a new initiate has reached him at last. His name is Karmana, and protecting the tower and Hall now consumes his thoughts. He identifies the presence they encountered as Sajan, the former master of the archives, who bound herself to the Hall when the Great War and plague destroyed its community. Her snares caught intruders in nightmares, but she too became lost in them; the monks who later reclaimed the monastery could only try to comfort her with happier memories.

Karmana says the dangers surrounding the monastery have increased since the expedition left thirty years ago. He presumes it failed, but cannot tell whether it released something, attracted hostile attention, or simply removed those who had protected the land. Less than a year ago, a wave of darkness carrying the stench of a rotten ocean swept from Kharsan, where the danger now seems concentrated. He knows Grash’s name from the orcs below, but not how these events fit together. Saka went west before the others departed east, and Rai never visited the monastery. The order’s knowledge of souls and consciousness might explain why Rai wanted its help, though Karmana cannot recover the details. He permits Seeker to take a carved ivory box holding six candles, but has no remembered business beyond keeping the monastery safe.

Still in the tower, the party weighs these answers against the missing heart and the unresolved fate of Rai. They favor helping Dunmar before undertaking the long journey to Drankor. With half an hour until sunset, they settle on visiting the temple first, then camping and scouting Kharsan the next day before heading toward Karawa by way of Agata Dust Mother. Kenzo insists on reconnaissance without exposing themselves; Wellby is less willing to rule out a shot at Grash. The temple visit, rest, and journey remain ahead of them.
