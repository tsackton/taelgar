# Dunmar Frontier chronology review

First full assembly from existing structured transcript products. No session-note narrative was used to fill chronology gaps. The user subsequently authorized the combat index as a source for level backfill. Source JSON, YAML and transcript files are unchanged.

The timeline covers 136 of 139 session numbers, with 131 combat records, 28 mirror examinations and 19 level-up milestones. A combat record can be a continuation or a brief hostile exchange. Level numbers and missing milestones are backfilled from the combat analysis; award-date uncertainty is preserved.

The campaign begins at the authorized S1 YAML date, March 22, 1748. Subsequent dates follow narrated transitions and same-scene handoffs; compatible YAML checkpoints narrow uncertain passages. Contradictory checkpoints remain review issues. Actor clocks split during solo arcs and reconnect only at identified reunions.

The readable timeline groups adjacent interaction titles and retains source-backed movement entries. Every displayed row maps to event IDs and source UIDs in timeline.json. Titles summarize played conversations and discoveries; they are not independent proof of every in-world claim made in those conversations.

“By” places an uncertain event at its latest supported bound for sorting, not on an asserted exact day. JSON preserves both bounds. Adjacent-day alternatives use “or.” Only multiday journeys use visible date intervals. An unknown watch stays blank; a contradicted watch is marked disputed. Within a displayed date, session/source order preserves scene sequence; overlapping tracks remain independent.

Validation includes source fingerprint checks, source-record coverage, event/reference integrity, date ordering and journey-only intervals. This is a chronology review of existing extractions, with focused retained-evidence checks for disputed dates and special callouts; it is not a new complete reading or extraction of every transcript.

Priority review: R03 (Kharsan drift), R09 (August/September conflict), R17 (crafting return), R24 (Apollyon countdown), R29 (late research montage), and G01 (missing structured sessions).
