#### Short
While the rangers reclaim Isingue, the fellowship uses the Mirror of the Past to uncover the Ash Queen’s origins and watch the Heartroot build its first home.

#### Intermediate
As the rangers clear Isingue, the mirror shows the Ash Queen and a companion shattered into Limbo, then the Queen restored to consciousness by the Relic of Order. The recovered scimitar is identified as a weapon of dark flame. Beneath the city, another vision shows the Heartroot carving its own cavern with roots and water and growing pillars of stone. The party also learns how Cha’mutte’s plague and Rai’s later intervention led to its corruption.

#### Long
With ash no longer falling over Isingue, the fellowship helps the rangers secure the ruins. One evening aboard Vindristjarna, they hold the Ash Queen’s remains before the Mirror of the Past. She and a companion wielding a dark-flame scimitar appear amid a shifting landscape, destroying a metal city before a pursuing light breaks them into fragments. In Limbo, the Queen drifts as a mindless storm until the Relic of Order restores her form and consciousness. The fellowship interprets the ancient struggle as a war against Mechanus; with the relic now destroyed, her scattered storm has no will to invade the material plane. The scimitar can grant an extra action through a burst of fire that also burns its wielder. Taking the mirror into the cistern, they find the returned Heartroot already growing new filaments and pillars. Its past unfolds in roots directing water through the earth, hollowing a lake, and slowly growing stone columns into a home that predates Isingue. Kenzo spends his free time there, welcome in the Heartroot’s lair. Cha’mutte’s plague had struck this center of resistance and concentrated his magic here; his death tore the planar wound. The rangers saved a fragment of the Heartroot, while the remainder eventually became the ooze after Rai pushed more of Limbo into the material plane.

#### Short
Later, at Ikram’s in Karawa, the companions describe the lives they hope to lead and promise to help one another along the way.

#### Intermediate
Further mirror questions can wait. After bringing the monks of Bhishma to Karawa, the fellowship gathers at Ikram’s. Seeker intends to refit Vindristjarna and find his brother before applying to the Philosophers Guild. Kenzo has promises to keep in Apporia and Chardon, stories to preserve, and a growing interest in the Orchard Keeper. Delwath opens the sisila fruit privately and resolves to seek his people. Wellby plans to ride north to his family and elders. They offer each other help while remembering old debts and unfinished business.

#### Long
Leaving the bag, the Hkaran treasure, and other mirror investigations for another time, the fellowship gathers at Ikram’s after delivering the monks of Bhishma to Karawa. Seeker’s ambitions have grown beyond a quiet academic life. Before seeking membership in the Philosophers Guild, he wants to refit Vindristjarna for planar travel and find his brother, with Faldrak’s help. His hopeful estimate of a few months will probably mean at least a year. Kenzo wants a rest on the Apporian coast and to honor his promises to Kazuro: help finding an apprentice and building a monastery. Chardon’s Magistros still needs watching, the Heartroot offers a place to return to, and Amar can help him preserve the stories of their companions and those they have lost. Beyond these duties, the Orchard Keeper has shown him an emptiness deeper than anything he knew. Delwath, uncertain whether the elves want to be found, takes out the sisila fruit and cuts it open. Whatever he sees remains his own; seeking his people now feels like the next step. Baxter waits outside as Wellby plans the northern road back to his family, elders, and gatherings, where he hopes to add his experiences to their song. Mapping the Feywild can wait. They promise knowledge, strength, and guidance whenever needed, including help against Frankar. Their memories return to Riswynn and her crown, Agata under Caulaus’s guard, the freed Cloudspinner, Hralgar’s storm-cloud existence, and the people still trapped in stone or in the mirror. Those unfinished stories remain for another day.

#### Short
Their companions’ stories continue as the Ladder gains its freedom, rainbow-prism rings keep the fellowship connected, and Dimitaur commissions a more flattering history of the Great War.

#### Intermediate
The Salty Squid bartender and Kecha turn out to have been the kenku god observing the fellowship. Dunmar has divided, while Riswynn’s quest continues beyond this plane. Delwath permanently animates the sixth Ladder, which leaves with a medal, repair supplies, and the party’s thanks. Milo and Tilly have reached a southern port, and Seeker presents rings for sending messages and returning to Vindristjarna. Faldrak plans the ship’s protection for the Plane of Fire. A last call finds Dimitaur feasting on the Circular Island, bargaining over forge access and a “correct history” that gives him his due.

#### Long
The bartender at the Salty Squid who overheard the fellowship speaking of Rai was also Kecha, the curious kenku scout who later joined their ship. The secret god of the kenku watched them for a time, encouraged Faldrak’s adventures, and returned to the eastern isles. Elsewhere, Darba has become a Chardonian client state, Sura rules the central and eastern part of Dunmar, and Karnas retains the southern floodplains. Riswynn has taken companions to another plane, her work concerning Thark unfinished; the orc city thrives and honors her with statues, while the boulder ladder’s battlefield has become a memorial. The living Ladder receives a farewell of its own. Delwath makes it permanently animate with true polymorph, Seeker thanks it for its sacrifices, and Wellby supplies a medal, rope, needles, and a letter asking strangers to treat it kindly. After recalling the five ladders that came before, they send the sixth out to choose its own destiny. Their magical eye shows Milo and Tilly in a tropical southern port beneath golden domes. Seeker then produces four rings made with Faldrak from the rainbow prism, each allowing messages between the companions and a return to Vindristjarna. Faldrak’s next project is a regenerating ice shell for travel in the Plane of Fire, leaving the planar crossing to Seeker. On the Circular Island, Dimitaur is testing his promised immortality against an enormous fish feast and sees little reason to worry about anything except sadness. He demands rent for access to the forge, accepts prizes as an alternative, and becomes interested in hiring a writer to set the record straight about the Great War. After his dragonet advisers untangle the arithmetic, he agrees to pay a net hundred gold a day. The book must be in Common, and a statue should show him larger than Rai. The epilogue ends with the immortal ruler returning to his business.

#### Short
After play ends, the group reflects on the campaign and begins planning a short adventure in northern Sembara.

#### Intermediate
The players celebrate the ending and discuss rotating DMs, making characters together, and trying short adventures with different tones or simpler rules. Ideas range from Mawar and the Feywild to space opera and a Taelgar–Lexington crossover. Their retrospective favors combat with narrative stakes, clear time limits, and memorable NPCs, while questioning some skill checks and the size of Vindristjarna’s cast. Mike’s northern escape premise emerges as the likely next game, with a shared session zero and sixth-level characters.

#### Long
With the epilogue complete, the players compare the campaign with their high-school games and celebrate an ending that rewarded creativity as well as combat. They favor making new parties together during scheduled sessions, then playing short arcs with different DMs. Possibilities include more Mawar, a Feywild realm, stories before time, an Arcane-inspired alternate Taelgar, space opera, and fantasy characters arriving in Lexington. Rules discussions return to how much a group can learn quickly, whether dice should reward character skills or shape a story, and what makes a creative challenge satisfying. Looking back, they value fights with narrative stakes, tactical variety, clear time limits, and distinctive encounters; some routine combats and repeated skill checks felt less rewarding. Faldrak, the Ladder, and Baxter stand out among companions, while many other crew members were difficult to remember without regular interaction. They recall custom abilities, individual adventures, drawings, and Lego miniatures, and agree that Vindristjarna became memorable even without a finished interior plan. By the end, Mike is considering an escape adventure in a great crevice in northern Sembara, probably at sixth level. A shared session zero would establish the party’s purpose and how its expedition went wrong. Scheduling and participation remain open as the group makes departure arrangements and heads to bed.
