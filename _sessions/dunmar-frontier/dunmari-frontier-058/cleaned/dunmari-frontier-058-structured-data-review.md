## Combat

### Spirit troll in the Ashfields

A spirit troll surprises the party with necrotic shadows, frightening Rothfis and Merash.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Thror shocks the troll and withdraws; Rothfis ends his fear and heals. The regenerating troll bites Merash and misses both claws. Riswynn casts Spirit Guardians; Merash hits, retreats and uses Second Wind. Its proposed opportunity attack is canceled by Shocking Grasp. |
| 2 | After Lightning Bolt, Rothfis’s critical hit and flurry injure him through shadow backlash; the troll’s opportunity claw misses. Radiant guardians strip its shroud but do not stop this regeneration. It downs Rothfis and wounds Merash. Riswynn lands an opportunity blow, then misses; Merash’s maul and Menacing Attack leave it frightened. |
| 3 | Thror’s Shocking Grasp dissipates the troll. Rothfis rolls a natural-20 death save and returns with 1 hit point. |

The troll is defeated; the party continues toward the cave rather than resting in the exposed wastes.
