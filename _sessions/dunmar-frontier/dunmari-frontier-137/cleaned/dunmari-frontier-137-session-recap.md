#### Long

Delwath scries the city and sees only a maelstrom of ash, already spreading about fifteen miles in every direction. The urgency changes their travel plans: Seeker casts Wind Walk, and Faldrak continues behind them with Vindristjarna.

#### Short
At the storm's edge, Delwath and Kenzo discover a distributed ooze network centered on a trace of Limbo beneath a well.

#### Intermediate
Wind Walk brings them to the storm's edge, about fifteen miles from Isingue. Delwath sends an illusory double through the ash, then scries the plaza it discovers around a cracked well. Kenzo senses the ooze spread beneath the city, concentrated in the river and plaza around a Limbo-like nodule below the well; his own brief excursion establishes that the ash is poisonous.

#### Long
They race across the Plaguelands, passing a burned forest and ash-covered hills before Wind Walk ends at the storm's edge. Delwath sends a Mislead double running inward. With only moments left before it vanishes, the double reaches a broad clearing around a cracked well, where an ooze emerges and strikes harmlessly through it. Delwath then scries the same spot, making out an eighty-to-one-hundred-foot mass, ruined market columns, and signs of nearby river docks. Kenzo's communion reveals a network like a chaotic inversion of the Heartroot, with concentrations in the river and plaza and a trace of Limbo beneath the well. It has no apparent conscious will, and the unnatural storm is centered on the buried nodule. Kenzo tests the ash himself; it becomes choking and poisonous, though his immunity protects him.

#### Short
Wellby becomes a green dragon and carries Kenzo into the ruins, where Kenzo takes a crystallizing sample from the ooze's pseudopods.

#### Intermediate
To retrieve a sample safely, Delwath transforms Wellby into a poison-immune green dragon and remains behind to maintain the spell. Wellby carries Kenzo to a collapsed well, where astral Kenzo descends into a ruined cistern and encounters two purple ooze pseudopods. He stuns one, tears off a fragment that hardens into crystal, and escapes on Wellby; back outside the storm, the dragon transformation is dispelled.

#### Long
They decide to collect a sample before committing everyone to the fight. Delwath first proposes becoming a green dragon, then transforms Wellby instead so he can maintain concentration from safety. Wellby carries poison-immune Kenzo into the storm near sunset, and they find a collapsed well. Kenzo passes through the rubble in astral form and discovers a vast ruined cistern: fallen columns and ceilings have made the apparent underground passages into a maze. Two purple pseudopods attack. Soul sight distinguishes these smaller segments from the full organism, whose defenses include immunity to acid, necrotic, and poison damage. Kenzo stuns a pseudopod, grapples it, and tears off a piece that immediately hardens into crystal. He returns to Wellby, who flies him back to the others. The Glove of Crystalized Magic helps dispel Wellby's lingering transformation.

#### Short
The mirror reveals the ooze's origins, and the party feasts and rests before attacking its river connection.

#### Intermediate
The fragment proves dead and nonmagical, but the Mirror of the Past shows it originating in the cistern's violently altered water and stone. Two connected cores spread filaments through the water, slowly consuming it and corroding the city. Hoping to cut off this supply, the party chooses the river concentration as its first target; Seeker conjures Heroes' Feast with Wish, and they camp outside the storm.

#### Long
Back with the others, they examine the dull gray-purple crystal. It is dead and nonmagical, and Delwath's sword does not identify it as native to Limbo. The Mirror of the Past shows the cistern convulsing as stone melts and water solidifies, then a gelatinous mass forming beneath Isingue. Its core divides into two connected masses orbiting one another and throwing out filaments through the water. Over years the growth consumes the water and corrodes the surrounding stone, eventually reaching through a well. The party considers attacking the hidden center, but first chooses the river concentration in hopes of depriving the ooze of its water supply. At sunset Seeker uses Wish to conjure Heroes' Feast. They eat, prepare their protective magic and equipment, and settle into camp outside the ash storm for the night.

#### Short
The next morning, Seeker and Delwath become leviathans, and the party severs the river ooze's connecting tendril despite its stone armor and acidic nets.

#### Intermediate
The storm has reached their tents by morning. Seeker and Delwath take leviathan forms and advance upriver with Kenzo and Wellby flying above them. Wellby spots the river ooze's connection to the plaza, giving them a target, but it reinforces the tendril with stone and traps both flyers in acidic nets. Kenzo breaks free and cracks the connection with force damage while Seeker keeps Wellby alive; Delwath's renewed tidal wave finally severs it, and the river mass collapses into crystal.

#### Long
By morning the ash is lapping against their tents. Protected by Heroes' Feast, they approach along the river: Seeker becomes a spellcasting leviathan through Shapechange, Delwath takes the same form through True Polymorph, and Kenzo and Wellby fly above. The Isingue ooze withdraws onto the bank and attacks through the dense ash. Wellby spots a tendril running back toward the plaza, and the party concentrates its attacks there. Delwath's first tidal wave fails to sever it. Kenzo's blows force the ooze to reinforce the connection with material from its body, then armor it with stone that resists bludgeoning damage. Acidic nets restrain both flyers, leaving Wellby especially vulnerable. They discover that the ooze retaliates against whoever last physically damaged its tail, allowing Delwath to draw attacks onto his resistant leviathan form. Seeker's psychic magic forces a legendary resistance, and Kenzo uses force damage to widen the cracks before breaking free. Seeker heals Wellby and strikes the damaged tail; when Delwath's tidal wave recharges, the next blast severs the connection. The river concentration bubbles apart, drains away, and hardens into crystal.

#### Short
Following the crystallized trail, they reach a collapsed plaza where an enormous ooze is rising from the cistern around its hidden core.

#### Intermediate
As the city rumbles, they free Wellby from the crystallized restraints, heal him, and follow the remaining connection toward the well. Kenzo senses the ooze gathering there. The plaza has collapsed into the cistern, exposing a mass roughly four hundred feet across; with its nodule hidden inside, they consider how to reach it, but the next fight has not yet begun.

#### Long
The ground begins to shake as the river mass falls apart. The remaining restraints turn to crystal and are washed away; Seeker heals Wellby again, and Kenzo shares his potions. Old passages from the river into the cistern have collapsed, so they prepare to follow the crystallized connection overland. Kenzo senses that the remaining ooze is now concentrated at the well. They hurry onward while the leviathan forms last and emerge into the storm's clear center. The plaza has collapsed into a slope down to the cistern, and an enormous, multicolored ooze, roughly four hundred feet across, is climbing out. Its nodule is hidden somewhere inside. They consider sending the leviathans and astral Kenzo through the mass while Wellby stays farther back, with Forcecage among the possible ways to isolate the core. Play pauses before initiative or any of these plans is attempted.
