## Arrivals in Karawa
*b01*

**Date**: 1748-03-21
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Kenzo recognizes Karawa from his recurring dreams as giant hyenas attack, while Delwath hears villagers calling for the elf who has defended them before.
**Long Summary**: Since leaving Tokra, Kenzo has dreamed nightly of strange landscapes, especially a red sandstone mesa sheltering a clear oasis. He reaches Karawa and finds that exact mesa, temple, oasis, and village before cries for help reveal two enormous hyenas charging into town. In a parallel introduction, Delwath has spent several months lodging above Badya's teahouse, investigating the Temple of Shakun and helping during an earlier hyena attack; his morning is interrupted when villagers call for the elf again.
**Location**:
- Karawa: the western approach, the village edge, and Delwath's rooms above Badya's teahouse
  Note: The beat presents parallel introductory scenes within and immediately outside Karawa.
**NPCs**:
- Candrosa (mentioned): Speaker of Shakun. Kenzo is seeking him, while Delwath has met him during his inquiries into the temple.
**Items**: none
**Organizations**:
- Shakun Mystai (mentioned): Kenzo seeks its speaker and Delwath has spent months investigating its secret rites at the Temple of Shakun.

## Hyenas Brought Down

**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: yes (end)
**Main Enemies**:
- giant hyenas: Kenzo kills one; Wellby delivers the final strike against the other after the party disables it.
**Combat Notes**: The combat ends with both hyenas dead and the badly injured villager stabilized.

## Strangers Find Common Ground
*b05*

**Date**: 1748-03-21
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The strangers exchange names and histories while the bloody hyena corpses, Delwath's sense of distorted magic, and Seeker's ley-line research point toward a deeper mystery.
**Long Summary**: Kenzo, Wellby, Seeker, and Delwath introduce themselves and compare what drew them to Karawa. Seeker notes dried blood from earlier violence on a hyena and proposes tracking the beasts, while Delwath senses a distortion in the Weave around the scene. Under the teahouse awning, Seeker heals Kenzo and explains that magical lines converge within the mesa and Temple of Shakun; Wellby identifies himself as a mapmaker seeking the blank lands to the east, and the group begins recognizing overlapping interests in Karawa, old Dunmar, and the source of the attack.
**Location**:
- Karawa: Badya's teahouse and its shaded tables at the northeastern village edge
  Note: The conversation begins beside the hyena corpses and settles at the teahouse tables.
**NPCs**:
- Badya (encountered): teahouse worker. Delwath asks her to bring fresh food as the strangers recover and talk.
- Candrosa (mentioned): Speaker of Shakun. Kenzo and Seeker both reveal that they came to Karawa hoping to speak with him.
**Items**:
- Seeker's ley-line diagram (encountered): His star-shaped sketch places a convergence of magical lines within the Temple of Shakun and its mesa.
**Organizations**:
- Shakun Mystai (mentioned): Seeker describes the local mystery cult and its possible connection to the magical convergence beneath the temple.

## Karawa Asks for Help
*b06*

**Date**: 1748-03-21
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Beli heals Delwath and Kenzo before Kisa asks the strangers to investigate the escalating attacks and Candrosa reacts uneasily to the failure of Beli's magic.
**Long Summary**: Beli returns with Candrosa and Elder Kisa, heals Delwath and Kenzo with red ochre paste, and helps other acolytes carry the wounded villager away. Kisa explains that this is the third recent attack, that the beasts are growing stronger, and that the approaching festival leaves Karawa dangerously exposed; she asks Delwath and the travelers to investigate. When Delwath reports that Beli's divine magic failed and that he sensed a distortion in the Weave resembling the power of ancient elven masters, Candrosa turns pale, acknowledges mysteries he cannot discuss openly, and leaves to check on his acolytes while Kisa entrusts the problem to Delwath.
**Location**:
- Karawa: Badya's teahouse at the northeastern village edge
  Note: Karawa's leaders meet the party inside the teahouse after the attack.
**NPCs**:
- Beli (encountered): Shakun initiate and healer. She heals Delwath and Kenzo, then helps carry the wounded villager away.
- Candrosa (encountered): Speaker of Shakun. He is alarmed by Beli's failed magic and Delwath's description of the distorted Weave, but says some mysteries cannot be discussed openly.
- Kisa (encountered): elder and unofficial leader of Karawa. She explains the growing danger and asks Delwath and the travelers to investigate before the festival.
**Items**:
- red ochre healing paste (encountered): Beli applies the muddy temple preparation to close Delwath's and Kenzo's wounds.
**Organizations**:
- Shakun Mystai (encountered): The order's speaker, initiate, and acolytes respond to the attack and care for the wounded.

*b08*

**Time Window**: afternoon
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party follows the hyenas east-northeast until their tracks vanish in rocky badlands, then climbs for a view and chooses a moving dust cloud to the north as its next lead.
**Long Summary**: The group follows the giant claw prints east-northeast through blooming scrub for several hours, but the trail disappears when the terrain becomes bare rock. ## Alesh on the Old Road
*b09*

**Date**: 1748-03-21
**Time Window**: afternoon
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party intercepts Alesh on the old trade road, learns of missing herders and renewed danger around Gomat, and decides to push toward the oasis.
**Long Summary**: Leaving the canyonlands, the party discovers that the dust belongs to Alesh, a Dunmari scout returning from Gomat. She has seen nothing dangerous on the road itself, but reports missing herders, unsettling rumors among scouts and pastoralists, and fears that the old dangers of the Nashtkar may be returning. The blood on the hyenas and the line of their approach make Gomat the party's best lead; Alesh explains the route, identifies Akan and his family as friendly herders camped there, then rides on toward her own family while the group chooses a hurried journey to the oasis over returning to defend Karawa.
**Location**:
- canyonlands east-northeast of Karawa -> old trade road toward Gomat: intercepting Alesh northwest of the canyonlands and conferring beside the road
  Note: The party leaves the badlands, reaches the old road, and ends ready to follow it east toward Gomat Oasis.
**NPCs**:
- Alesh (encountered): Dunmari scout returning from Gomat. She shares reports of missing herders, warns about the Nashtkar, explains the road to Gomat, and then rides toward her family.
- Akan (mentioned): herder camped at Gomat Oasis. Alesh recommends that the party seek him and his family for information when they reach the oasis.
**Items**: none
**Organizations**: none

## Nightfall at Gomat
*b10*

**Date**: 1748-03-21 to 1748-03-22
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: After camping beside the old road, the party force-marches through the following day and reaches Gomat exhausted to find giant lizards feeding among silent tents.
**Long Summary**: The party follows the ancient road through land visibly twisted by past cataclysms, camps for a cold and quiet night, and resumes before dawn. They push through a full day of desert heat into the moonlit evening, leaving Kenzo, Wellby, and Seeker exhausted. At Gomat Oasis the tents stand silent and unlit; dead sheep lie near the camp, and three pony-sized lizards tear into the carcasses without yet noticing the party.
**Location**:
- old trade road east of Karawa -> Gomat Oasis: an overnight camp followed by a forced march through the desert to the oasis
  Note: The journey crosses into the following day and ends at the edge of the silent herders' camp.
**NPCs**:
- giant lizards (enemy): three pony-sized beasts at Gomat Oasis. They are feeding on dead sheep in the darkness and appear unaware of the party when the session ends.
