# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 5
- Beat Coverage: 10/10

## scene-001 | Four Travelers Meet in Karawa

- Beat IDs: b01, b02, b03
- Rationale: The separate arrivals flow directly into the shared hyena fight that brings the four travelers together.

### Beats

- b01 | Arrivals in Karawa: Kenzo recognizes Karawa from his recurring dreams as giant hyenas attack, while Delwath hears villagers calling for the elf who has defended them before.
- b02 | The Hyenas Charge: The four strangers converge at Karawa's edge and enter battle with two giant hyenas before Kenzo batters one with his staff and bare hands.
- b03 | Hyenas Brought Down: Kenzo kills one hyena while Delwath, Seeker, and Wellby combine magic, terrain, and a precise final strike to bring down the second and save a wounded villager.

## scene-002 | Aftermath and Introductions

- Beat IDs: b04, b05
- Rationale: The immediate aftermath, examination of the hyenas, and introductions form one continuous teahouse scene.

### Beats

- b04 | Aftermath and Introductions: Beli runs to fetch Candrosa while Delwath and Wellby move the rescued villager into Badya's teahouse and the four strangers begin examining the attack.
- b05 | Strangers Find Common Ground: The strangers exchange names and histories while the bloody hyena corpses, Delwath's sense of distorted magic, and Seeker's ley-line research point toward a deeper mystery.

## scene-003 | Karawa Asks for Help

- Beat IDs: b06
- Rationale: Kisa and Candrosa arrive and shift the situation from introductions to a formal request for investigation.

### Beats

- b06 | Karawa Asks for Help: Beli heals Delwath and Kenzo before Kisa asks the strangers to investigate the escalating attacks and Candrosa reacts uneasily to the failure of Beli's magic.

## scene-004 | Into the Badlands

- Beat IDs: b07, b08
- Rationale: The party forms, prepares, follows the trail, and enters the desert in one continuous outbound journey.

### Beats

- b07 | The Investigation Begins: Delwath recruits the other three travelers, who rest and prepare before Seeker examines the mesa and the group finds the hyenas' eastbound tracks.
- b08 | Tracks into the Badlands: The party follows the hyenas east-northeast until their tracks vanish in rocky badlands, then climbs for a view and chooses a moving dust cloud to the north as its next lead.

## scene-005 | The Road to Gomat

- Beat IDs: b09, b10
- Rationale: Alesh redirects the investigation toward Gomat, and the ensuing road journey ends with the discovery at the oasis.

### Beats

- b09 | Alesh on the Old Road: The party intercepts Alesh on the old trade road, learns of missing herders and renewed danger around Gomat, and decides to push toward the oasis.
- b10 | Nightfall at Gomat: After camping beside the old road, the party force-marches through the following day and reaches Gomat exhausted to find giant lizards feeding among silent tents.
