## Kenzo Enters the Dreamworld
*beat-001*

**Date**: 1748-03-30
**Time Window**: dawn
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Candrosa sends Kenzo into the Dreamworld, where a jade-armed figure tells him to seek stories of the Order of the Awakened Soul.
**Long Summary**: Candrosa wakes Kenzo before dawn and leads him to the summit of Karawa's red mesa, where she describes dreams that mirror the transformations of his jade hand. Using the Dreamweaver Staff, she sends him into the Dreamworld. Kenzo encounters a jade-armed robed man who tells him that he is not alone and should seek stories of the Order of the Awakened Soul. Kenzo returns able to perceive Candrosa's aura; she knows the order only from legend, wonders whether Shakun may be connected to Kenzo's experience, and asks the party to remain in Karawa until the scouts return and the council meets.
**Location**:
- Karawa: the summit of the red mesa overlooking the town
  Note: Candrosa first wakes Kenzo at Ikram's before leading him up the mesa.
**NPCs**:
- Candrosa (encountered): Shakun mystai guiding Kenzo's spirit journey. She uses the Dreamweaver Staff to send Kenzo into the Dreamworld and interprets what he reports without claiming to know its cause.
- jade-armed robed man (encountered): figure who addresses Kenzo in the Dreamworld. His identity is not established in the session.
**Items**:
- Dreamweaver Staff (encountered): Candrosa uses it to send Kenzo into the Dreamworld.
- Kenzo's jade amulet (encountered): Kenzo wears it during the spirit journey and discusses its connection to his jade hand.
**Organizations**:
- Order of the Awakened Soul (mentioned): The Dreamworld figure tells Kenzo to seek stories of the order.
- Shakun Mystai (encountered): Candrosa conducts the spirit journey as one of Shakun's mystai.

## Breakfast and the Awakened Soul
*beat-002*

**Date**: 1748-03-30
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The companions compare Kenzo's spirit journey with Seeker's coming-of-age vision and Delwath's two centuries of imprisonment in a gray false world.
**Long Summary**: At breakfast, the party reads Candrosa's letter asking them to remain in Karawa and receives word that Kenzo is atop the mesa. After joining him, they hear his account of the Dreamworld and the Order of the Awakened Soul. Seeker compares it to the star-filled void of his Dwarven coming-of-age ceremony. Delwath then reveals that touching a dark metal circlet after the Great War trapped him for roughly two hundred years in a gray recreation of the world, where he watched generations pass before awakening in the present. The party considers possible connections among these experiences, Shakun's silence, and the border's strange magic without reaching a firm explanation.
**Location**:
- Karawa: Ikram's breakfast table and the summit of the red mesa
  Note: The party moves from breakfast to join Kenzo on the mesa.
**NPCs**:
- Candrosa (mentioned): author of the letter asking the party to remain in Karawa. Her message explains that scouts and the council should provide more information.
- Karawa messenger (encountered): young rider carrying news of Kenzo. The messenger tells the party that Kenzo is on the mesa.
**Items**:
- Letter from Candrosa (encountered): The party reads Candrosa's request that they remain in Karawa.
- Dreamweaver Staff (mentioned): Kenzo explains how Candrosa used it during his spirit journey.
- dark metal circlet (mentioned): Delwath says touching it after the Great War preceded his two-century imprisonment.
**Organizations**:
- Order of the Awakened Soul (mentioned): Kenzo repeats the Dreamworld figure's instruction to seek stories of the order.
- Shakun Mystai (mentioned): The party considers Candrosa's role and Shakun's recent silence while comparing their experiences.

## Jasu's Spearpoints and Seeker's Net
*beat-004*

**Date**: 1748-03-30
**Time Window**: afternoon
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Jasu explains the magic of Arcus's silvered spearpoints and agrees to build Seeker a chain throwing net after the two settle on payment.
**Long Summary**: At Jasu's smithy, the party examines the two silvered spearpoints Arcus left behind. Jasu explains that their Great War runes carry abjuration and anti-undead magic and that no ordinary forge can melt or rework them, though they could still be mounted on shafts. Seeker then commissions a weighted chain throwing net of his own design. After negotiating the cost and persuading Jasu of his ability to pay, Seeker secures the smith's agreement to complete the net for payment on delivery.

  Note: The entire beat takes place during the party's visit to the smith.

**Items**:

## The Council Reaches Accord
*beat-011*

**Date**: 1748-04-04
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The council agrees to evacuate Karawa, fortify Shakun's temple, seek the Samraat, and send separate scouts north and south before reconvening.
**Long Summary**: Suma and Kisa endorse Kenzo's plan to preserve Karawa's people, while Candrosa accepts the evacuation but remains committed to the temple. Havdar agrees to muster riders, escort the refugees west, leave a small guard, and return to Karawa. Suma and Kisa will organize the retreat; Seeker will help fortify and seal the temple if necessary. The party chooses to investigate the known magical lead to the north and hopes first to make a concerted attempt to reach Shakun. After questioning whether Alesh is the right emissary to the Samraat, the council revises the assignments: Alesh and selected riders will scout south, while the council identifies an emissary more likely to win royal attention. Kisa declares the decision final and orders the retreat and missions to begin as soon as possible after the festival.
**Location**:
- Karawa: the village council chamber
  Note: The session ends as the council reaches agreement.
**NPCs**:
- Suma (encountered): Jeevali priestess agreeing to lead the evacuation. She argues that flight with Havdar's protection will keep the most people alive.
- Kisa (encountered): council elder organizing the agreed plan. She supports the evacuation, will help lead it, and declares the council decided.
- Candrosa (encountered): Shakun mystai remaining at the temple. He trusts the party's experience and agrees that the people should flee while the temple is fortified.
- Havdar (encountered): war leader escorting the evacuation. He will muster riders, protect those fleeing west, leave support for the temple, and later return to Karawa.
- Alesh (encountered): scout volunteering to investigate the southern danger. She proposes that a politically stronger emissary seek the Samraat while she leads a fast southern reconnaissance.
- Nayan Karnas (mentioned): Samraat from whom the council will request aid and a muster. The council plans to choose an emissary capable of winning his attention.
**Items**:
- silvered Great War spearpoints (mentioned): Delwath proposes bringing them when the party travels north.
**Organizations**:
- Karawa Council (encountered): The council unanimously adopts the coordinated evacuation, fortification, diplomacy, and reconnaissance plan.
- Shakun Mystai (encountered): They will remain in the temple while Seeker helps reinforce it.
- Havdar's Warband (mentioned): The riders will protect the evacuation and later regroup at Karawa.
