# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 4
- Beat Coverage: 11/11

## scene-001 | Dreamworld and Dark Memories

- Beat IDs: beat-001, beat-002, beat-003
- Rationale: Kenzo's private spirit journey flows into one continuous morning conversation on the mesa in which the party compares supernatural experiences and adds Ander's Raven's Hold account to its possible leads.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Kenzo Enters the Dreamworld: Candrosa sends Kenzo into the Dreamworld, where a jade-armed figure tells him to seek stories of the Order of the Awakened Soul.
- beat-002 | Breakfast and the Awakened Soul: The companions compare Kenzo's spirit journey with Seeker's coming-of-age vision and Delwath's two centuries of imprisonment in a gray false world.
- beat-003 | Raven's Hold Revealed: Wellby recounts Ander Charmheart's attack by a blood-red vine at Raven's Hold, giving the party another possible lead on the spreading transformations.

## scene-002 | Spearpoints and a Throwing Net

- Beat IDs: beat-004
- Rationale: The afternoon visit to Jasu introduces a new location and immediate goal: examining Arcus's spearpoints and negotiating Seeker's commission.

### Beats

- beat-004 | Jasu's Spearpoints and Seeker's Net: Jasu explains the magic of Arcus's silvered spearpoints and agrees to build Seeker a chain throwing net after the two settle on payment.

## scene-003 | The Festival of Rebirth

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006, beat-007
- Rationale: The gifts, staff-dancing tournament, rumor exchange, and axebeak race form the festival interlude while the party waits for the scouts, ending when Alesh and Havdar return with urgent news.

### Beats

- beat-005 | Gifts, Rumors, and Staff Dancing: Karawa honors the party with gifts, Seeker receives an enigmatic Dwarven book, and Kenzo wins the festival's staff-dancing tournament.
- beat-006 | Rumors Before the Axebeak Race: Delwath reports rumors of attacks, storms, missing travelers, and Chardonian interest while the party prepares for the axebeak race.
- beat-007 | The Axebeak Riding Contest: Wellby places third and Kenzo is thrown early in Karawa's axebeak race before the exhausted scouts return with urgent news.

## scene-004 | The Fate of Karawa

- Beat IDs: beat-008, beat-009, beat-010, beat-011
- Rationale: The scouts' reports, the party's questions, Kenzo's proposal, and the final assignments are one uninterrupted council meeting with the single goal of deciding how Karawa should respond.

### Beats

- beat-008 | Scout Reports Divide the Council: Alesh and Havdar deliver conflicting scout reports, leaving Karawa's council divided between defense, flight, seeking the Samraat, and striking the raiders.
- beat-009 | Weighing Karawa's Options: The party tests Karawa's options against its numbers, travel times, and defenses, then connects Arcus and Raven's Hold to the region's Great War ruins.
- beat-010 | Kenzo's Evacuation Plan: Kenzo proposes evacuating Karawa's people to Tokra, fortifying the temple, and delaying any attack until scouts and the Samraat's army reveal more about the threats.
- beat-011 | The Council Reaches Accord: The council agrees to evacuate Karawa, fortify Shakun's temple, seek the Samraat, and send separate scouts north and south before reconvening.
