### Mirror theft, pursuit, and battle at the ruined temple

Invisible theft removes the top and mirror; Kenzo is trapped by the turning mirror as the party wakes.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Readied responses reveal Marcella. She resists restraint and paralysis and escapes at hasted speed, still carrying the mirror. |
| 2 | Wellby flies after her and releases Kenzo. Kenzo deactivates the mirror and attacks; Marcella disengages and continues running. Delwath remains at the temple. |
| 3 | Wellby and Kenzo pursue; Riswynn’s accelerated pursuit and Guiding Bolt bring Marcella down. Kadmos appears at the temple with a demon. |
| 4 | Kenzo recovers the mirror; Delwath banishes the demon. Casian incapacitates Delwath, Seeker wakes him, Wellby strips and leaves Marcella, and Kadmos summons a ghost. |
| 5 | Bandits and Delwath’s shadow beast wound Kadmos. Casian’s Fireball kills all seven bandits and burns Seeker, who strikes Casian back. Marcella revives; the ghost and Kadmos attack Delwath. |
| 6 | The shadow beast misses and Riswynn misses Casian. The Wall of Fire hurts Kenzo and Riswynn; Seeker heals Delwath and Wellby shoots Kadmos. Marcella approaches; ghost and caster bring Delwath down. |
| 7 | Kadmos resists the mirror and halves Riswynn’s bolt. Casian seeks peace; Seeker revives Delwath and wounds Casian with locusts. Wellby leaves Kadmos at 1 HP, and Marcella negotiates. |

All five PCs survive; the seven bandits die. Casian pays 175 gold, Kadmos keeps the top, and the attackers agree to return straight to Chardon and leave the party alone. Marcella’s weapons are to be returned; her ring is returned in the subsequent private farewell. No further attack is resolved.
