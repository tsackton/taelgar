#### Short
Pursued by orcs from Kharsan, the party races back to its horses and escapes into the desert.

#### Intermediate
Beneath the bridge outside Kharsan, Delwath rejoins the others with broken glass and tools from the ruined city. With Grash’s shadows already sent to find them, they make for the horses: Delwath and Seeker invisible, Kenzo and Wellby on foot. Horns sound as orcs spot movement along the cliffs, forcing them to abandon stealth and run. Their head start is enough to reach the animals and mount up.

#### Long
Just before noon, the party shelters beneath the bridge over the dry Kharja. Delwath has returned from scouting with glassblowing tools and broken glass that might reveal something through the mirror, but Seeker’s report of Grash sending eight shadows after them makes escape more urgent. Delwath renews his invisibility and extends it to Seeker, while Kenzo and Wellby dash across the exposed ground to the cliffs. They try to slip through the rocks toward the horses, only to hear horns from the orc fort. A patrol moves out to investigate, and the party gives up stealth for speed. With more than half a mile’s head start, they reach the horses before the orcs can catch them. Seeker clears the protective berm, and everyone mounts up.

#### Short
Riding toward Havdar’s camp, they guard their nights with light and find extra water with a desert wren’s help.

#### Intermediate
With only two days of water, they ride northwest toward Havdar’s camp, choosing speed over a hidden trail. Their first night passes safely beneath a rocky overhang lit by drift globes, and Kenzo wakes fully recovered from the lingering effects of his death. After another day’s ride, Wellby scouts through a desert wren’s senses. Seeker translates its knowledge of a nearby seep into directions, then goes with Delwath to collect enough water to last until Havdar’s camp.

#### Long
Havdar’s camp offers water and a chance to warn an ally, so they head northwest rather than detouring to an oasis or riding directly to the stone house. The horses raise an obvious plume of dust as they cover fifteen to twenty miles. At sunset, Wellby finds an overhang sheltered on three sides; Seeker adds a barrier, and they keep the drift globes lit, judging the shadows a greater threat than distant orcs. Kenzo is spared watch duty and wakes free of the lingering effects of his death. Another thirty-mile ride leaves their water running low. While making camp, Wellby proposes fashioning a kefiyah and coaxes a desert wren close with seeds. Through its eyes he sees no pursuers, but he cannot make sense of its memories of water. Seeker uses Speak with Animals and a diagram in the sand to triangulate a seep half a mile away. He and Delwath visit it after dark, and Mold Earth turns the muddy trickle into a small well, adding half a day to their supply.

#### Short
On the second night, they defeat two shadows and three shadow dogs with bright light and magic, but lose two pack horses.

#### Intermediate
A shadow lunges at Delwath as he goes to wake Seeker around midnight, and the accompanying dogs’ howl frightens him. Wellby activates Daylight while Seeker creates magnesium for Delwath to ignite with Green-Flame Blade. The light hampers the attackers, but a shadow drains Delwath’s Strength and the dogs kill two horses outside its reach. Kenzo destroys one shadow with Fist of Planar Power; Wellby finishes the other. Delwath kills the larger dog, and Wellby brings a drift globe toward the remaining pair so the party can finish them.

#### Long
As Delwath approaches Seeker around midnight, a humanoid shadow darts through the edge of the light. A howl wakes the camp and fills Delwath with memories of the realm he escaped. A second shadow slips into Kenzo’s tent, where ordinary blows do little against it. Seeker conjures magnesium in the fire ring, Wellby commands a drift globe to cast Daylight, and Delwath’s Green-Flame Blade ignites a brilliant flare. The smaller shadow dogs flee toward the horses beyond the light, while their larger leader attacks Delwath. One shadow drains three points of his Strength despite his retaliating Shadow Rebuke. Kenzo uses Soul Sight and Fist of Planar Power to destroy the shadow in his tent; Seeker’s earthen hand wounds the other, and Wellby’s arrow finishes it. As the horses flee into the light, the dogs kill two pack animals. Delwath discovers that necrotic magic does nothing to the larger dog, but kills it with his weapon and regains some health. Wellby advances with the second drift globe, exposing the smaller dogs as Baxter engages them. Both are bitten, and Wellby is knocked down before Kenzo dashes over to help. Wellby shoots one dog, and the party quickly dispatches the last. With the attackers dead, they bandage Baxter and resume their watches, still uncertain where the other shadows Grash sent might be. When questioned about his healing, Delwath describes respite granted through his otherworldly connection rather than life stolen from his enemies.

#### Short
They reach Havdar’s deserted camp, replenish their water, and turn toward the stone house to report to the Mystai.

#### Intermediate
Delwath’s Strength returns by dawn, but without the pack horses the onward journey is slower. They reach Havdar’s camp after sunset and find an intact spring and an empty campsite. After a night with the remaining horses kept close inside the lights, Wellby finds tracks leading toward Karawa. Kenzo feels a duty to share his initiation and the monastery’s history with Pava and Avaras, so the party takes the two-day detour to their house.

#### Long
By dawn, rest has restored Delwath’s Strength. The horses cannot be replaced so easily: without spare mounts, the party must walk at intervals and push beyond sunset to reach Havdar’s camp. No bodies or signs of slaughter greet them, only a deserted campsite and a welcome spring. They arrange their defenses more carefully this time, keeping the horses against the canyon wall and placing the drift globes to cover everyone. The night passes without another attack. In the morning, Wellby finds that Havdar’s people left two or three days earlier, heading toward Karawa. Delwath favors following them, but Kenzo feels obliged to tell Pava and Avaras what he learned at the monastery, especially now that he has joined their order. His companions agree. After replenishing their water, they travel for two uneventful days toward the stone house.

#### Short
Over dinner, they share Kenzo’s initiation and the danger from Grash, and receive advice to seek Candrosa and Saka.

#### Intermediate
Pava welcomes them at sunset, and Amil tends the horses while Kenzo reveals his initiation. The hosts welcome news of the surviving Hall of Stories but cannot explain Grash’s treasure hunt or the old expedition’s purpose. They recommend Candrosa for the missing Heart of Shakun and Saka for the Mystai’s past. Warned about Grash, they discuss keeping Amil closer. The party stays overnight; Kenzo spends time with the elders, and Seeker gives them a small stone carving of the monastery.

#### Long
Pava rises to greet them as they approach at sunset, calls for Avaras, and sends Amil to tend the horses. Kenzo signals that he has joined their order and recounts the ordeal: Jeevali asked him to give his life, and he did. Over dinner, he and the others share the monastery’s memories, their discoveries about Rai, and Bhishma’s message about the missing Heart of Shakun. Pava and Avaras are glad that the Hall of Stories survives, but know little of Grash’s sought treasure or the purpose of the expedition decades earlier. They recall unfamiliar adventurers who passed east a month ago and suggest that treasure hunters from Chardon or the Yuvanti dwarves might know more. Delwath adds his people’s tradition of forests and elven cities that once covered the land. The Mystai direct questions about Shakun’s heart and the land’s protection to Candrosa in Karawa; for the order’s history, they recommend Saka, remembered as living north of Tokra. Grash’s activities trouble them, and they privately discuss keeping Amil closer. Kenzo spends the night talking with them as a fellow initiate, while Seeker carves a small stone likeness of the monastery as a gift.

#### Short
After staying overnight, they return through Bas Udda to Karawa, where Tharandros is waiting to speak with them.

#### Intermediate
Leaving the next day, they make the six-day journey back toward Karawa, passing the still-deserted ruins of Bas Udda and camping under bright light each night. No further shadows attack. They arrive on May 23 to find Havdar’s soldiers and the centaurs guarding the town together, with Ikram’s ruined tavern adapted into stables and a training yard. Welcomed back by the patrols, they learn that Tharandros wants to speak with them.

#### Long
The following day they leave the stone house and settle into a steady journey back across the desert. Wellby discourages the occasional giant vulture with arrows, and their nightly camps remain brightly lit, but no more shadows appear. Whether they have passed beyond Grash’s reach or simply escaped notice is unclear. Bas Udda remains ruined and deserted as they pass through, though the approach to the Red Mesa begins to feel like coming home. On the sixth travel day, May 23, they reach Karawa in the late afternoon. Havdar’s soldiers have made stables from the ruins of Ikram’s tavern and turned its courtyard into a training ground. Centaurs and soldiers patrol the town together and intercept the party to welcome them back. Havdar returned a few days earlier, they learn, and Tharandros is eager to speak with them. Their escort leads them into town.
