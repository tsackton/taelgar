Before play resumes, the table discusses souls and ways to portray other planes.

#### Intermediate
Before returning to Karawa, the players consider how different rules, maps and modes of play might make other planes feel unfamiliar. Discussion of souls and afterlives mixes the DM’s explanations with player theories and unanswered questions.

#### Short
At the Karawa campfire, the companions discuss Shakun’s silence and discover that Kenzo’s jade was once part of something larger.

#### Intermediate
At their camp in the ruined caravanserai, the companions reflect on what draws them into the desert and wonder why Shakun’s protection has failed. Kenzo reads fear and guilt in Candrosa’s behavior, while Delwath suspects the mirror is recharging faster near the temple. Seeker’s goggles show that Kenzo’s jade was broken from a larger object, and its magic reminds Delwath of the elven elders.

#### Long
At the ruined caravanserai on April 24, Kenzo remains intent on understanding his past, Delwath finds renewed purpose in confronting the desert threat, and Wellby welcomes the prospect of exploration. Seeker experiments with a small illusion of the planes as they talk. Their questions return to Shakun and Candrosa: Kenzo believes the priest is frightened and guilty rather than simply lying, but they do not establish what he knows. Delwath thinks the mirror may be drawing power more quickly near the temple, though it is not yet ready. Kenzo hopes it can reveal something through his jade necklace. Examining the jade with his goggles, Seeker sees that one surface was once inside a larger object; Delwath recognizes magic like that invoked by elven elders. They consider storing bulky treasure before departure, while Wellby seeks tack for his axebeak and Seeker plans another look at the temple.

#### Short
After preparing with Havdar, they leave Karawa on April 27 and spend two days riding toward Bas Udda.

#### Intermediate
Havdar plans to pass through Bas Udda and scout the orc threat farther east, fighting a smaller force but retreating from a large one. The companions secure their surplus possessions, finish their business in Karawa and prepare to use the recharged mirror. Kenzo slips away privately before departure, while Tharandros warns Wellby about the dangers of dealing with the fey; the mounted expedition then travels through April 27 and 28.

#### Long
Havdar visits the next morning with a plan to ride through Bas Udda, then turn east toward water near Alesh’s reported orc sighting. With about twenty warriors and the party, he expects to face fewer than a hundred orcs, but a larger force would require retreat; messengers will seek support in Tokra. Over April 25 and 26, the companions store their surplus possessions and prepare for the journey. Candrosa repeatedly seems on the verge of saying something but holds back, and Kenzo sends his mentor news about Shakun. Wellby’s axebeak tolerates a saddle but is not yet ready to carry him. By departure morning the mirror is charged, and Kenzo slips away alone to consult it; the private scene’s contents are not recorded. Wellby and Delwath then seek Tharandros. He warns Wellby that the fey may regard deadly poison as a joke or grant extraordinary wishes on a whim, while Delwath’s own question is handled privately. With those conversations finished, the party joins Havdar’s mounted force on April 27 and rides toward Bas Udda, continuing the journey through April 28.

#### Short
At Bas Udda, they find devastation and fight seven giant vultures, killing five and driving off the other two.

#### Intermediate
On April 29 they reach a burned settlement where someone has arranged the dead for funeral rites. Seven giant vultures threaten the expedition; after Delwath attacks, the birds seize Wellby and then lift Kenzo into the air. Seeker, Kenzo, Delwath and Havdar bring down three birds around the temple, while Havdar’s archers kill two more and the survivors flee.

#### Long
Bas Udda is far more thoroughly ruined than Karawa: the farmland has been deliberately burned, most buildings have fallen, and only the central temple and a few nearby roofs remain. Yet the temple has been cleaned and bodies carefully laid out, attracting seven enormous vultures. Delwath’s opening blast misses, and two birds swoop on Wellby. Seeker’s Shatter weakens them enough for Kenzo to finish both with staff and unarmed blows. A third vulture seizes Kenzo and lifts him off the ground. Havdar strikes it with a spear and calls for another attack, while Delwath teleports onto its back. Kenzo escapes the talons, finds his footing on the bird and kills it; he lands safely, while Delwath suffers a short fall. Havdar’s archers kill two more vultures, leaving two to escape. As Havdar establishes a watch, Seeker has already glimpsed a frightened young man sheltering inside the temple.

#### Short
Inside, Amil’s story of an elf rescued by his masters draws Delwath’s attention, and the party helps with the funeral rites before agreeing to escort him home.

#### Intermediate
The young man is Amil, a devotee of Bhishma working to put Bas Udda’s dead to rest. Seeing Delwath reminds him of an elf his masters, Pava and Avaras, rescued from the desert months earlier. Havdar’s warriors and the companions help recover the remaining bodies for sunset rites, then arrange to part company so the party can accompany Amil to his masters.

#### Long
Among the temple’s gathered icons of the Five Siblings, Amil explains that he has been recovering the dead, preserving their memory and helping their souls find peace. He serves Bhishma and studies with Pava and Avaras, who live in a stone house beyond the town. Delwath’s appearance startles him: his masters once found an elf in the desert, and Delwath suspects their story may concern him. His own recollections remain fragmentary—ruins, desperate thirst and a shepherd family. Havdar offers his warriors’ help, and Seeker’s shaping magic speeds the work of clearing collapsed buildings. At sunset Amil prays over the funeral pyres; the party leaves the dead’s possessions unlooted. They agree to escort him home after a final morning search. Havdar supplies horses and provisions, gives Wellby directions to his eastern camp and promises a warning if a large orc force appears. Amil knows nothing of Agata, but suggests asking his masters, whose ability to perceive souls is a gift he hopes someday to receive from Bhishma.

Leaving with Amil on April 30, they travel toward the stone house until Wellby discovers three ogres at a spring on May 2.

They travel through the next day and approach a canyon spring on May 2, where broken animal remains and oversized tracks warn of danger. #### Short
Wellby’s request for passage starts a chase, and the companions defeat all three ogres with spells, an earthen barrier and arrows.

#### Intermediate
The first ogre pursues Wellby as a prospective meal and wounds him with its chain, which Seeker heats while Delwath and Kenzo attack. After Wellby kills it, Seeker’s earthen wall obstructs the two remaining ogres and Kenzo catches and returns a javelin. Spells, melee blows and falling rock break their advance; Wellby lands all three final shots, with Seeker’s rune supplying the last burst of killing damage.

#### Long
The ogre treats Wellby’s apology and request for passage as an invitation to eat him. It chases him back toward his companions and lashes him with a chain. Delwath prepares protective magic and mirror duplicates, Kenzo climbs the canyon wall and attacks from above, and Seeker heats the chain and gives Wellby an inspiring rune. The ogre keeps hold of its burning weapon and wounds Wellby again; one sweep also destroys a mirror duplicate. Seeker raises a five-foot earthen wall across the passage, and Wellby kills the chain-wielder with an arrow through its eye. The other two ogres advance with javelins. Kenzo is grazed while deflecting one, then catches a later throw and hurls it back into an ogre’s shoulder. A charge stumbles at the wall, Seeker’s attempt to turn an ogre against its companion fails, and Shatter wounds both while breaking loose part of the canyon wall. Delwath presses forward with his blade, and Wellby kills the second ogre. Kenzo leaps onto the last with staff and fists; Delwath follows with his shadowy sword while Seeker heals Wellby. Wellby’s final arrow strikes with the aid of the earlier rune, whose explosive magic finishes the third ogre.

#### Short
They retrieve Amil and reach his masters’ house that afternoon, where the older of the two men recognizes Delwath.

#### Intermediate
With the ogres dead, they find Amil hiding farther back and leave the foul spring behind. A few more hours bring them to a stone house around four or four-thirty, and Amil runs ahead to announce their arrival. Two elderly men come out to greet them; the older, paler man’s face lights up at the sight of Delwath.

#### Long

The ogres’ camp offers only animal remains and refuse, with neither treasure nor evidence of recent humanoid victims. Amil urges them onward despite the heat, and after a few more hours they reach the stone house around four or four-thirty on May 2. A livestock pen, colorful shade awnings, a well and an outbuilding surround the two-story home. Amil runs ahead, and two elderly men in gray-silver robes emerge. The older, paler man visibly recognizes Delwath, but their conversation has yet to begin.
