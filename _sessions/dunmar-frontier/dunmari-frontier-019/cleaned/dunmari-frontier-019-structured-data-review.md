Two diving vultures wound and grapple Wellby before the regular initiative cycle.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Seeker wounds both diving vultures and Kenzo kills them. Another seizes Kenzo; Havdar grants his counterattack, the troop volley kills a fleeing bird, then Delwath teleports aboard. |
| 2 | Seeker enters the temple. Kenzo escapes the grapple and kills his captor; Delwath takes a small fall. Another troop volley kills a fifth vulture. |

### Three ogres in the canyon

Wellby’s retreat draws the chain-wielding ogre to the party; Delwath casts Armor of Agathys before initiative.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Kenzo climbs and uses Soulsight. Delwath creates duplicates. The chain wounds Wellby without knocking him out; Seeker heats it and inspires Wellby, who shoots from hiding. |
| 2 | Kenzo’s blast deals half damage after a save. Delwath’s readied strike lands before the chain sweep, which destroys one duplicate. Seeker keeps Heat Metal and raises a wall; Wellby kills the ogre. |
| 3 | Two more ogres arrive. Kenzo deflects one javelin down to 1 damage; the second misses. Delwath curses and blasts with an unclear result. Suggestion fails; Wellby misses. |
| 4 | The front ogre trips at the wall; Kenzo catches and returns its javelin. Delwath strikes it and burns its companion. Seeker causes a rockslide; Wellby kills the front ogre. |
| 5 | Kenzo jumps down and strikes twice; the last ogre misses him. Delwath wounds it, Seeker heals Wellby, and inspiration turns Wellby’s final shot into an explosive killing hit. |

All three ogres die. Wellby delivers the finishing arrows, with Seeker’s rune assisting the last.
