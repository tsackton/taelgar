#### Short
At Pava and Avaras’ stone house, Delwath reunites with the monks who rescued him and reclaims his shadowed armor.

#### Intermediate
Amil’s masters welcome the party and recognize Delwath, whom they once found delirious and dying of thirst in the desert. He cannot remember their help or most of his journey from Kharsan. Avaras produces his old elven breastplate, now altered by dark metal and billowing shadows, which the monks feared had contaminated him. Seeker examines it with the purple goggles while Delwath explains his acceptance of the shadow power. He puts the armor on and stills the shadows, easing the monks’ fears without discovering where its power comes from.

#### Long
Amil leads the party into his masters’ stone house, where Pava and Avaras offer tea and recognize Delwath with some hesitation. They found him about twenty miles east, nearly dead from thirst and muttering in a language they did not understand. After a day and part of a night under their care, he slipped away westward; he remembers neither the monks nor much of the journey from Kharsan. As they describe their calling to tend unburied souls, Kenzo explains his studies of the occult, Laka, and Shakun, and Delwath speaks of leaving his people to fight in the Great War. Avaras then brings out the cause of their concern: Delwath’s elven breastplate, with dark metal worked into its front and shadows gathering around it. Delwath recognizes the armor he wore before his disappearance, but the monks had removed it when they rescued him, fearing it was cursing or contaminating him. Pava recognizes elven workmanship but says the shadows do not feel elven. Seeker inspects the breastplate through the purple Goggles of Object Reading. Delwath demonstrates a shadow blade and explains that the power helped him escape the realm where he was trapped. He accepts it as part of himself, dons the armor, and quiets the shadows, leaving the dark inlay visible. His composure reassures the monks, though the origin of the power remains unexplained.

#### Short
With their suspicions eased, the monks reveal their order’s failed cleansing and warn of a new malevolent will in Kharsan.

#### Intermediate
More willing to speak, Pava and Avaras describe a secret attempt to cleanse the cursed land about thirty years ago that killed their order’s strongest initiates. None has since returned to the monastery near Kharsan, where the history of the Dunmari is preserved. They also report changes within the past year: an evil will seems to direct Kharsan’s hauntings, and eastern orcs who once lived quietly now fight each other and raid west. Five unfamiliar adventurers, apparently Chardonian, have passed toward the dry Kharja in search of imperial treasures and perhaps a lost library.

#### Long
Now willing to answer broader questions, the monks are dismayed to hear that Shakun’s protection has fallen, but cannot explain why. They call Agata Dustmother misery incarnate, yet remember her as a tormentor who kept to herself rather than seeking territory; a bargain behind her changed behavior is only a possibility. Avaras recounts how, about thirty years earlier, leaders of the Order of the Awakened Soul attempted a secret cleansing of the cursed land. It failed, killing the strongest initiates and leaving survivors such as Pava and Avaras to quieter work among the dead. The order’s monastery, about ten miles south of Kharsan at a site associated with Bhishma and Dunmar’s founding, holds the history of their people, but none of the order has returned since the disaster. Neither host has visited it himself. Before they found Delwath, they began sensing an evil will behind Kharsan’s old hauntings. Orcs who had lived quietly to the east have also begun fighting among themselves and coming west. Another recent intrusion was a party of five unfamiliar adventurers, apparently from Chardon, overheard heading toward the dry Kharja in search of imperial artifacts and perhaps a lost Drankorian library farther south. Kenzo’s descriptions establish that these were different people from Vola and Arcus. The monks have not seen them return.

#### Short
When the hosts leave to cook, Delwath describes the sorrowful presence he sensed through the armor, and the party decides to rejoin Havdar before seeking the monastery.

#### Intermediate
In private, Delwath says that touching the breastplate brought a fleeting sense of a presence full of sadness and loss, which he thinks may be the source of his magic and connected to his escape. The others consider links between his return, the monks’ disaster, Kharsan, and Shakun’s fallen barrier, but reach no firm explanation. They favor the monastery over confronting Agata or pursuing the distant library, first honoring their promise to rejoin Havdar and sharing the monks’ account of the orcs. Dinner follows, and Pava promises Kenzo a lesson before they leave.

#### Long
Once Pava and Avaras withdraw to the kitchen, Seeker asks what Delwath has learned about his own story. Delwath describes the presence he sensed when he touched the breastplate: powerful, sad, and full of loss, with no clear form or speaking voice. He suspects it is the source of his magic and may have helped him escape, but cannot recall enough to be certain. His companions consider whether his return, the failed cleansing, Kharsan’s new menace, and the collapse of Shakun’s protection might be connected, without finding evidence for a single explanation. They weigh going west to Agata against traveling east to the monastery; reaching the rumored library farther south would demand more supplies. The monastery offers a nearer prospect of answers, but they first agree to keep their promise to Havdar, whose camp lies on the way, and tell him that the eastern orcs had once lived peacefully. Amil and the two masters return with chicken and rice. Over the evening, Kenzo asks to come back for further study, and Pava offers to show him something in the morning before everyone turns in.

#### Short
After a morning of training, they set out across the desert, with Wellby guiding the journey toward Havdar’s camp.

#### Intermediate
The next morning, Pava teaches Kenzo to strike at an opponent’s consciousness, using Wellby as a sparring partner. Wellby learns to slip away from blows, while Seeker’s rune practice draws an enormous hand from the earth. They leave after the heat on May 3 and spend the next two days traveling northeast. Wellby’s axebeak becomes ready for short rides, and his growing skill with desert navigation carries them through the gullies. On May 5, he spots a giant scorpion in time to go around it before they continue toward Havdar’s camp.

#### Long
On May 3, Pava takes Kenzo and Wellby outside and demonstrates how to reach past a physical blow to strike at the energy of another consciousness. Kenzo learns the technique by stunning Wellby, who in turn becomes increasingly slippery, moving with attacks so that solid hits become glancing blows. Nearby, Seeker studies his runes and coaxes larger and larger hands from the ground until an enormous one bursts out around lunch. The party waits out the worst heat, then leaves around three in the afternoon, heading northeast toward the broken canyon lands north of the Garamjala Desert. Their first afternoon and camp pass quietly. On May 4, Wellby’s axebeak eats from his hand, follows him, and is tame enough to begin short rides; Baxter is suggested as a name but remains only a contender. Seeker still has Taster with him. Wellby uses the map and Havdar’s directions to guide another quiet day of travel. On May 5, he spots a giant scorpion lying in wait and leads the party around it. After resting through the midday heat, they continue through gullies that Wellby now navigates with growing confidence.

### recap-005 | The Battle for Havdar’s Camp

#### Short
Arriving during an orc attack, they strike the enemy flank and help save the camp, leaving one spellcaster alive for questioning.

#### Intermediate
Battle sounds stop them short of the camp. Seeker sends his sight ahead and finds roughly twenty or more orcs attacking Havdar’s soldiers from the canyon rim. The party climbs to flank the warriors, their leader, a fire mage, and an armored spellcaster. A fireball badly wounds several companions, but Delwath’s assault and Seeker’s earthen hand bring down the fire mage while Kenzo and Wellby cut through nearby warriors. Kenzo stuns the leader, Delwath wounds it, and Kenzo finishes it despite a phantom hand gripping him. Seeker restrains the final caster, allowing Delwath to knock it unconscious. The party then helps Havdar finish the orcs below; several Dunmari are down, with their condition still uncertain.

#### Long
Before they reach the camp, shouting and the clang of weapons carry down the canyon. Seeker casts his sight ahead with spectral goggles and sees roughly twenty or more orcs firing from the rim while Havdar holds the camp below and other Dunmari move to flank them. Delwath cloaks his armor in frost, and the party climbs the canyon wall to strike from the opposite side. An explosion among Havdar’s men makes the enemy casters the immediate priority. Kenzo opens against a caster with Fist of Planar Power, breaking its concentration, but ordinary warriors rush him and Wellby while a fire mage catches Seeker, Delwath, and Wellby in a fireball. Seeker answers with Shatter, and Delwath charges the ash-gray, flame-painted mage with his sword. Wellby is left barely standing as the fighting closes around them. Seeker’s earthen hand crushes the wounded fire mage; Wellby kills an adjacent orc and throws away his burning hat, while Kenzo finishes the nearby warriors and stuns their leader with blue ki. Delwath strikes the leader with Green-Flame Blade, burns the armored caster beside it, and blinks out of sight. The surviving caster traps Kenzo in a phantom hand, but Kenzo can still attack and kills the leader. Seeker’s stone hand seizes the caster, and Delwath reappears to knock it unconscious for questioning. With the other rim cleared by the Dunmari, the party turns its attacks on the remaining orcs below, helping Havdar’s men finish the battle. One captive lies unconscious in the stone hand. Several of Havdar’s soldiers are wounded or down, but the party cannot yet tell who is dead; questioning the prisoner must wait.
