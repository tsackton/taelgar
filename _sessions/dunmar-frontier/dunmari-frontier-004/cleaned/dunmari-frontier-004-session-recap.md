#### Short
The party leaves the quieted Dunmari fort with its relics and returns to festival-swollen Karawa.

#### Intermediate
After confirming that a creature dug the older tunnels beneath the fort, the party inventories the temple relics and other artifacts it recovered. Seeker sketches the petrified explorer before they leave him behind and make the calm three-day journey west to a Karawa transformed by crowds gathering for the Festival of Rebirth.

#### Long
The party wakes in the fort's intact temple to find that the oppressive tension has vanished from the surrounding land. Seeker determines that the underground passage was dug by a creature months or more before the recent trouble, then carefully sketches the petrified explorer while the group debates the cockatrice nursery and what to carry home. They record the Black Iron Staff's Draconic inscription and gather Aagir's Everlight, the Cha'mutte symbol, the Floating Disc Coin, the Horn of Silent Alarm, and the portable temple relics. Leaving Arcus's stone form sheltered at the fort, they cross the desert without incident and arrive three days later at Karawa, now swollen with travelers, camps, and trade for the approaching Festival of Rebirth.

#### Short
There, Kisa, Ikram, and Jasu identify the petrified explorer as Arcus and trace his interest in Great War artifacts.

#### Intermediate
They report their success to Kisa, who recalls the Summer of Red Storms and directs them to Ikram and Candrosa. Ikram identifies the explorer as Arcus, a Chardonian traveler seeking artifacts, while Jasu reveals that Arcus left behind two silvered spearpoints that resist every attempt to work them.

#### Long
The party reports to Kisa that it destroyed the apparent source of the animals' rage. Although the countryside has been quiet for three days, travelers still bring news of axebeak attacks, and the staff's Draconic inscription alarms her. She recalls the half-human, half-hyena raiders of the Summer of Red Storms, confirms that the man in Seeker's sketch was not local, and sends the party to Ikram. At the crowded caravanserai, Ikram names the stranger as Arcus, a reserved traveler who arrived four or five weeks earlier, paid in old Chardonian silver, and asked about artifacts before visiting Jasu. The smith remembers Arcus's interest in relics of the Great War and describes two silvered spearpoints left to be hafted: apparently new, yet impervious to heat, reforging, or ordinary tools. He agrees to show them to the party in the morning and recommends Alesh as Karawa's best authority on Dunmari artifacts.

#### Short
Their inquiries lead to the Charmhearts, where a disturbed Ander speaks joyfully of a master who will consume him and the world.

#### Intermediate
Moving through the festival encampment, they visit Bree Charmheart and her Sembaran halfling company. Bree grows guarded when Wellby asks about a sick companion, and the disheveled Ander soon emerges to declare that the master speaks endlessly to him and will consume both him and the world.

#### Long
Leaving Jasu, the party walks among the festival camps to the colorful wagons of the Charmhearts. Bree Charmheart welcomes the group around their cookfire and trades stories of Sembara with Wellby, but her warmth vanishes when he asks about a disheveled companion who has spoken into his mind. As she tries to end the visit, Ander stumbles from a wagon, points at Wellby, and says with an incongruously peaceful smile that the master will eat him soon. Once safely away, Wellby explains that Ander hears the voice without respite: the master promises joy, calls him to join, and intends to consume the world. The party cannot prove a connection, but the message sounds ominously like another manifestation of the power behind the Black Iron Staff.

#### Short
That night, Candrosa reveals that Shakun's ancient border protections are failing and begs the party to help defend Dunmar.

#### Intermediate
That night, Candrosa interrupts the party's vigil at the oasis and summons them into the Temple of Shakun. After testing their intentions with a Zone of Truth, he confesses that Shakun's magic no longer turns enemies from Dunmar's borders: Havdar has fought hyena-like raiders to the south, an endless lightning storm rages to the northeast, and Ander's voice suggests a third threat. Candrosa asks for their aid and secrecy, then gives each of them a vial of healing mud before sending them to rest.

#### Long
Delwath, Seeker, and Wellby settle beside the oasis to meditate while Kenzo remains at Ikram's drinking late into the night. Around eleven, Candrosa interrupts the vigil with news that demands an immediate conversation and sends Wellby to retrieve Kenzo, whose halfling sobering ritual with three eggs and a stick ends in a volley of thrown eggs. As they assemble, an armored rider later identified as Havdar gallops from the temple toward the encampment. Candrosa leads the party into a private side room, where the Black Iron Staff and their questions force him to confront what he has been hiding. After hearing their reasons for coming to Karawa, he casts Zone of Truth and asks whether they intend to aid the town. He then reveals that, since the aftermath of the Great War, Shakun's magic has hidden Dunmar behind misdirection and turned hostile creatures away from its borders—but that protection now appears to be gone. Havdar has returned from fighting half-human, half-hyena attackers to the south, an unending lightning storm has appeared to the northeast, and the news of Ander's voice deepens Candrosa's fear. Begging the party to serve as the mundane defense Shakun no longer provides, he asks them to keep the failure secret through the Festival of Rebirth and to speak with Havdar in the morning. Before sending them to rest, he gives each traveler a vial of healing mud as thanks and a token of trust.
