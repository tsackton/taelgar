## Audio Monologue Candidates

- ID: audio-001
  - Source Lines: u0498-u0517
  - Speaker: DM
  - Summary: The party returns to a Karawa transformed by festival camps, livestock, bright wagons, and a population two or three times its usual size.
  - Why Called Out: A sustained arrival narration that establishes the festival's scale and energy before the party begins its investigations.

- ID: audio-002
  - Source Lines: u0671-u0682
  - Speaker: Kisa
  - Summary: Kisa remembers the Summer of Red Storms and the two-legged hyena creatures that attacked Dunmar forty years earlier.
  - Why Called Out: A self-contained, ominous eyewitness story that foreshadows the danger later reported by Havdar.

- ID: audio-003
  - Source Lines: u1722-u1740
  - Speaker: DM (Ander and Bree Charmheart)
  - Summary: Ander stumbles from the wagon, recognizes Wellby, and peacefully announces that the master will eat him soon as Bree hurries him away.
  - Why Called Out: The session's sharpest tonal turn, combining narration, character performance, and the first direct evidence that the threatening voice persists.

- ID: audio-004
  - Source Lines: u2269-u2331
  - Speaker: Candrosa
  - Summary: Candrosa explains how Shakun's ancient magic protected Dunmar after the Great War, reveals that the protections have failed, and begs the party for help.
  - Why Called Out: The central revelation and longest emotionally coherent speech of the session.

- ID: audio-005
  - Source Lines: u2374-u2380
  - Speaker: Kenzo
  - Summary: Kenzo angrily challenges Candrosa for testing the party's loyalty after everything they have already risked for Karawa.
  - Why Called Out: A strong character moment that gives the reveal immediate interpersonal stakes and preserves Kenzo's frustration in his own voice.
