# Recap Scene Proposal

- Scene Count: 4
- Beat Coverage: 7/7

## scene-001 | Return to Karawa

- Beat IDs: beat-001
- Rationale: The party closes out its exploration of the fort, inventories the recovered relics, and completes the multi-day journey back to Karawa.

### Beats

- beat-001 | From the Fort to Karawa: The party inventories the fort's relics, leaves the petrified Arcus behind, and makes the three-day journey west to a crowded Karawa.

## scene-002 | Following Arcus's Trail

- Beat IDs: beat-002, beat-003, beat-004
- Rationale: The report to Kisa flows directly into a continuous afternoon and evening investigation through Ikram and Jasu, with the immediate goal of identifying Arcus and understanding his interest in Great War artifacts.

### Beats

- beat-002 | Kisa Hears Their Report: The party reports its success to Kisa, who connects current dangers to the Summer of Red Storms and directs them to Ikram and Candrosa.
- beat-003 | Stories at Ikram's: At the caravanserai, Ikram identifies the petrified explorer as Arcus and shares news about his movements, recent attacks, and the coming festival.
- beat-004 | Maps and Jasu's Clues: After comparing maps and theories about Arcus, the party learns from Jasu that the explorer left behind strangely resilient silvered spearpoints.

## scene-003 | The Voice among the Charmhearts

- Beat IDs: beat-005
- Rationale: The visit to the halfling encampment introduces a new group, location, and threat when Ander reveals the consuming master's voice.

### Beats

- beat-005 | The Charmhearts' Troubling Secret: At the Charmhearts' camp, a disheveled Ander points at Wellby and speaks blissfully of a master who will consume him and the world.

## scene-004 | Shakun's Failing Protection

- Beat IDs: beat-006, beat-007
- Rationale: The attempted vigil, Candrosa's interruption, and the private temple meeting form one continuous late-night situation culminating in his revelation and plea for help.

### Beats

- beat-006 | Meditation Interrupted: Candrosa interrupts the party's vigil at the oasis and insists on speaking after Wellby retrieves a very drunk Kenzo from Ikram's.
- beat-007 | Shakun's Protection Fails: Candrosa reveals that Shakun's ancient border protections are failing and asks the party to help defend Dunmar from threats now crossing its borders.
