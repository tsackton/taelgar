#### Short
At the Monastery of Bhishma, Delwath sees a warning in the shadows and Kenzo senses undead within the desecrated temple.

#### Intermediate
Leaving the hall of stories, the party approaches Bhishma’s temple as evening falls. Delwath sees an ominous warning in the shadows, while Kenzo drinks healing potions before investigating the looted entrance. Kenzo finds that its altars are no longer consecrated and senses undead below the chapel and beside the better-preserved sanctuary.

#### Long
Leaving the hall of stories as dusk approaches, the companions turn toward the red sandstone temple. Delwath alone sees a warning in the shadows and suspects that the being granting his powers is cautioning him. Still injured by the earlier lightning trap and recovering from his recent death, Kenzo drinks two healing potions before entering; Delwath prepares protective magic, and drift globes light their way. The entrance chapel has been looted, and Kenzo’s Detect Evil and Good finds its altars no longer consecrated, with undead roughly twenty feet below. Farther inside, sunlight from the high windows illuminates a less-damaged sanctuary and a statue of Bhishma smiling through tears. Kenzo senses more undead down a side corridor. His new obligations to the Order of the Awakened Soul do not require him to fight everything he finds, leaving the party free to investigate cautiously or withdraw.

#### Short
Three shadows retreat from the party’s light, but with sunset approaching, the companions withdraw to camp and rest.

#### Intermediate
Delwath makes himself and Kenzo invisible to investigate the side corridor. Kenzo’s footsteps attract a shadow, which stops at the sunlight; advancing with his drift globe drives it back and reveals two more. With darkness approaching, the party leaves the temple unexplored below and returns to camp, where Delwath hears distant armor during his watch but nothing approaches.

#### Long
Delwath makes himself and Kenzo invisible while Wellby watches the escape route. Kenzo’s noisy footsteps draw a faceless, cloaked shadow from the corridor, but it stops short of the sunlight entering the sanctuary. He advances with his drift globe, driving it back and revealing two more shadows against the walls. Rather than risk a fight at sunset while Kenzo is still recovering, the companions withdraw without attacking. They return to their camp about a mile south of the monastery, finding Baxter, the horses, and their supplies safe. The undead beneath the temple remain unexplored, and Wellby admits that the haunted place unsettled him. They keep camp quiet and let Kenzo rest. During his watch, Delwath hears a distant clink of armor that comes no closer; he passes the warning to Seeker, whose watch is quiet before Wellby takes over.

#### Short
At dawn, Wellby finds the orc corpses and captive missing from the monastery, with search parties closing in.

#### Intermediate
The next morning, the party considers scouting Kharsan before returning to Karawa to question Candrosa about the missing Heart of Shakun. Their map suggests that the orc fort might house a growing settlement, but its purpose remains uncertain. Investigating the overnight noise, Wellby finds the orc bodies and equipment gone and the bathhouse captive missing, then spots search parties approaching and returns to warn the others.

#### Long
At dawn, the discussion turns to scouting Kharsan before returning to Karawa and questioning Candrosa about the missing Heart of Shakun. Studying Wellby’s earlier aerial map, Seeker recognizes a resemblance between the oddly shaped fort and orc cliff settlements. Delwath’s recollection that young orcs live in narrow, dark spaces suggests a possible nursery, though neither its purpose nor a threat to Karawa is confirmed. Wellby goes to investigate the sound heard overnight. At the monastery, the manticore bodies remain, but the dead orcs and their equipment have vanished without drag marks. The bathhouse’s external bar has been removed, and the captive is gone. Through his spyglass, Wellby sees orcs searching toward the monastery and along the dry riverbed. He returns with the warning. The missing corpses lend weight to claims that Grash can restore dead orcs, but nobody has witnessed what happened here; meanwhile, the cliffs obstruct a direct escape west with the mounts.

#### Short
To avoid the patrols while still reaching Kharsan, they ride a long southern loop around the cliffs and camp west of the river.

#### Intermediate
After weighing ambushes, hiding, and magical scouting, the party adopts Wellby’s plan to ride south around the cliffs, then turn west and north toward Kharsan. Their water will last only four or five days, making eventual resupply necessary. They hear distant orc shouts as they leave but see no pursuit; pushing beyond sunset exhausts Kenzo and Seeker, and they make a quiet camp south of the road.

#### Long
The companions weigh fighting the patrols, hiding, scouting invisibly, and sending Baxter away as a decoy. Exposed terrain, limited magic, and the need to protect their mounts make these plans unattractive. They also have only four or five days of water, so the eventual journey back to Karawa will require resupply. Wellby proposes riding south until the cliffs end, then looping west and north toward Kharsan; the group packs and follows that route. Their mounts raise a visible dust cloud, and distant orc shouts carry behind them, but no pursuit comes into view. By about three in the afternoon they have ridden roughly twenty miles to the end of the cliffs and turn west, then north. Delwath urges another hour of riding after sunset. Kenzo and Seeker become exhausted, and the party finally camps about ten miles south of the road, west of the dry river. The night passes without pursuers, though Seeker struggles to stay awake on watch.

#### Short
Next morning, Wellby scouts Kharsan through a hawk before the party conceals its mounts and takes shelter beneath the city’s bridge.

#### Intermediate
A pre-dawn start brings them back to the road west of Kharsan. Wellby scouts through a hawk, observing new guards, frequent patrols, and a shadowed palace the bird refuses to enter. Seeker hides the mounts behind an earthen ridge, leaving Baxter with them, and the companions approach the bridge with invisibility and stealth. No alarm sounds, and Seeker prepares to spy on the palace while Delwath sets out for the northern ruins.

#### Long
Wellby wakes everyone before dawn on May 13th. Rest has cleared the riding exhaustion, and Kenzo feels closer to recovery. They ride north to the road and stop west of Kharsan, where Wellby befriends a hawk and surveys the city through Beast Sense. He sees additional guards, patrols of roughly six orcs passing about every half hour, and a total force he estimates at one hundred to two hundred fifty. The palace lies under a strange darkness that the hawk refuses to enter. Seeker uses Mold Earth to conceal the horses behind a ridge, with Baxter staying beside them. To get within range for Clairvoyance, Delwath makes himself and Seeker invisible while Kenzo and Wellby creep along the cliffs and dash across the final exposed stretch. Wellby feels conspicuous, but no alarm sounds. Beneath the old sandstone bridge, Delwath recalls seeing it in darkness from two perspectives at once, as though through a mirror. Seeker prepares his sensor while Delwath uses his remaining invisibility to explore the northern ruins.

#### Short
From there, Seeker witnesses Grash’s necromancy and preparations to attack Uzgash, while Delwath investigates eerie voices in the ruins before they regroup to plan their escape.

#### Intermediate
Using Clairvoyance and a Tongues scroll, Seeker watches Grash execute two orcs, drain them through his ring, and reanimate their bodies. Grash dismisses an armored shadow’s complaints about the fruitless search for magical treasure, sends eight shadows after the intruders, and uses Sending to tell an unknown recipient that his orcs will be ready to strike by fall and Uzgash will fall. Meanwhile, Delwath hears unreachable cries for help in the northern ruins and collects glassware and tools for later investigation. He avoids an approaching patrol and rejoins the others beneath the bridge, where they plan to leave west.

#### Long
Seeker’s sensor enters a darkened throne room, where skeletal Grash carries a jagged glaive and wears a black ring. With a Tongues scroll and alternating sight and hearing, Seeker follows his anger at the intruders. Grash beheads two kneeling orcs; his ring pulses red as their bodies drain and shrivel, apparently invigorating him. He later reanimates them and replaces their heads. An armored skeletal shadow reports five weeks without magical finds and urges moving on, but Grash demands harder work and orders zombies to search all night. He summons eight shadows, touches them with his ring-bearing hand, and sends them to drain the invaders and bring back their bodies. Seeker casts Clairvoyance again as Grash uses a Sending scroll: treasure still eludes him, but the orcs will be ready to strike by fall, and Uzgash will fall. Seeker cannot identify the recipient or hear the reply; afterward, Grash lashes out in anger. Meanwhile, invisible Delwath hears Dunmari voices pleading and coughing among the northern ruins. In a partly surviving glassblower’s shop, he collects broken lamp covers, one intact cover, and rusty tools, hoping to examine their history with the mirror later. Following one voice brings him no closer to its source. He hears an approaching orc patrol and returns to the bridge before encountering it. The companions compare discoveries and plan to leave west, still beneath the bridge when their reconnaissance ends.
