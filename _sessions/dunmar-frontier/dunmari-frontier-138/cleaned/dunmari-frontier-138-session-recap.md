#### Short
At Isingue’s collapsed cistern, the party attacks the corrupted ooze while Seeker dives inside in leviathan form.

#### Intermediate
A fragment of Limbo embedded in the corrupted Waters of Isingue must be destroyed before the cistern can be restored. Wellby’s arrows and Kenzo’s rushing staff attacks draw the ooze’s retaliation and reveal its shifting defenses. Delwath abandons leviathan form to strike with his weapon, while Seeker keeps that form, forces the ooze to expend a legendary resistance, and dives toward its heart.

#### Long
An enormous ooze fills the ruined cistern beneath Isingue’s collapsed central well, spreading tendrils across the surrounding ground. At its heart lies a crystallized fragment of Limbo: while it remains, water entering the cistern will produce another ooze. Removing or banishing the corruption offers no easy solution, and the creature begins attacking. Wellby fires from the riverside as Delwath and Seeker advance in leviathan form. Kenzo uses Vital Burst to rush into reach and land four staff blows, then evades the ooze’s attempt to engulf him. Its body changes with each attacker, thinning against arrows and hardening against bludgeoning blows. Delwath returns to his own form and cuts into it with a critical smite. Seeker instead uses Rune of Psychic Disruption to draw out a legendary resistance, then enters the creature’s mass.

#### Short
The ooze repeatedly swallows Kenzo and Delwath as Seeker reaches its crystalline heart and discovers that only thunder can harm its current form.

#### Intermediate
Seeker pushes through the ooze as its shifting consistency alternately eases and obstructs his passage. Outside, Kenzo and Delwath are engulfed, escape through magic, and are dragged back in, while Wellby keeps shooting under retaliatory fire. A Potion of Speed helps Kenzo break free, and Seeker finally reaches the central sphere. His leviathan blows cannot damage it: the fragment’s current form is immune to everything except thunder.

#### Long
Seeker swims toward the sinkhole while Wellby and Kenzo continue attacking. Their blows change the ooze around him, turning a passage through thin slime into a struggle against a stony mass. The creature engulfs Kenzo and Delwath, wrapping them in restraining filaments. Delwath becomes a cloud of tiny water insects to slip free and resume his attacks, but the ooze keeps pulling its opponents back inside. Wellby is caught by an acid eruption and dragged closer, and those within the creature endure its crushing pressure. Kenzo drinks a Potion of Speed, tears himself free, strikes, and flies away, only to be caught again; Delwath uses Misty Step to escape in turn. Seeker presses on in leviathan form and reaches the dense crystalline sphere at the bottom of the sinkhole. His attacks leave it untouched. Contact recalls Limbo to him, and he discovers that its present form can be damaged only by thunder.

#### Short
Delwath’s Scatter and smites weaken the ooze, but the core resists Seeker’s efforts to reshape it and Delwath falls unconscious.

#### Intermediate
Delwath uses Scatter to free himself and Kenzo, then lands a critical smite before the ooze drags him back. Seeker tries to turn the core into feathers, but cannot change it. The creature’s counterattacks leave Wellby barely standing and trigger Kenzo’s restorative magic. Delwath escapes once more and tries to finish the ooze; his attacks falter, his magical dodge fails, and a pseudopod knocks him unconscious.

#### Long
Unable to break the core by force, Seeker tries to exploit its connection to Limbo while the others fight to stay alive. Wellby drinks a healing potion and flies higher, but remains within reach of the ooze’s pseudopods. Kenzo struggles against its restraint, and Delwath is engulfed again. Delwath quickens Scatter to teleport both himself and Kenzo clear, then lands a critical smite that leaves the creature badly weakened. His retreat lasts only until another glob pulls him back. At the center, Seeker embraces the sphere and imagines it vanishing into feathers; both his attempt and his retry fail. The ooze continues its assault. Wellby survives a counterattack with almost no strength remaining, and a blow through Kenzo triggers magic that restores him to half his maximum health. Delwath uses Misty Step for one more escape and attacks, but most of his blows miss. His attempt to dodge the answering pseudopod also fails, leaving him unconscious on the ground.

### recap-004 | Shattering the Heart

- Kind: combat
- Beat IDs: beat-010, beat-011, beat-012

- Enemies: Corrupted ooze, Slime minions

- Image Size:
- Image Placement:

- Image Caption:
- Image Alt:

#### Short
Kenzo revives Delwath, who heals Wellby, while Seeker cracks the exposed core with thunder; Kenzo then turns it to glass and shatters it, destroying the ooze and its minions.

#### Intermediate
Wellby lands a critical shot before falling, and Kenzo’s next strike exposes the core as the ooze scatters smaller slime minions. Kenzo revives Delwath, who heals Wellby; Seeker then ends shapechange and uses his glove to turn Chain Lightning into thunder, cracking the sphere. Wellby falls again after shooting a minion, but Kenzo reaches the core, wills it into fragile glass, and smashes it. The implosion flings Kenzo and Seeker skyward. Kenzo can fly; Seeker animates Ladder to carry him safely down as Delwath heals Wellby and the ooze crystallizes.

#### Long
Wellby’s Arrow of Seeking tears into the ooze, but its answering pseudopod knocks him unconscious. Kenzo follows with another strike, and the creature’s mass bursts outward into smaller slime minions, leaving the crystal sphere exposed. The main ooze still lives, so Kenzo turns to rescue: he flies back to Delwath and administers a Potion of Greater Healing. Delwath brings Wellby up with Healing Word, recovers a spell slot, and withdraws. At the center, Seeker ends shapechange and uses the Glove of Crystalized Magic to convert a seventh-level Chain Lightning into thunder. Cracks appear in the sphere. Wellby shoots down a minion and is struck unconscious again, while Kenzo races back to the heart. Concentrating on its nature as a fragment of Limbo, he imagines it as fragile glass; the sphere changes, and his unarmed and staff blows smash through it. The collapsing ooze throws Kenzo and Seeker a thousand feet into the air. Kenzo’s astral flight keeps him safe, but he cannot reach Seeker in time. Seeker animates Ladder, which wraps around him and descends gently with the image of a superhero cape streaming behind it. Delwath heals Wellby in time to watch the landing. Around them, the ooze and its minions have turned to crystal, and ash begins settling from the sky.

#### Short
With the ooze defeated, the party rests in Aurbez, returns the Heartroot to Isingue, and helps the Rangers establish a camp as the waters begin to recover.

#### Intermediate
The party teleports to Aurbez to report the victory to Cateline Malras and the Rangers, then rests before bringing the Heartroot back. Kenzo clears a path for river water to reach the cistern, and the root spreads filaments that cleanse the water and begin restoring its old channels and pillars. More rangers arrive over the following weeks. By early October the camp seems stable, and the party is back aboard Vindristjarna considering mirror visions and the rescued monks’ return.

#### Long
After the battle, the party teleports to Aurbez and tells Cateline Malras and the Rangers what happened. Following a long rest, Seeker sends Kenzo and the Heartroot back to the cistern, then brings the others and five rangers to help. Kenzo settles the root into the water and clears rubble so the river can flow in. Through his connection to the Heartroot he senses relief as it expands, sending fine roots through the ruins to draw in water and cleanse the ash and contamination left by Limbo. The filaments begin recovering ancient channels and growing new pillars, although rebuilding the cistern will still require work. The party gains the impression that the cistern predates the city itself. They remain to protect the Heartroot as more rangers arrive and a camp takes shape. By early October, Isingue seems stable enough for them to regroup aboard Vindristjarna. They discuss further views in the Mirror of the Past and returning the rescued monks, leaving those plans for what comes next.
