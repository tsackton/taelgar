### Shadow hunters attack Vindristjarna in Tollen harbor

The party raises the ice globe and prepares flight and protections before the attackers breach it.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The party waits while the attackers chip through the ice. |
| 2 | Readied arrow, Synaptic Static and Slow punish the breach. Fear and spear damage end Delwath’s flight; Kenzo breaks Fear, the ladder kills a hunter, and Delwath banishes both mounts. Corrected hawk damage lands on Wellby. |
| 3 | Wellby kills a hawk. A mount returns and is blinded; Kenzo deflects a spear, takes Blight and shield backlash, destroys the shield and stuns the other hunter. Icicles, ladder attacks and Delwath’s attacks thin the enemies; hawks wound Wellby. |
| 4 | A blinded mount grapples Kenzo but cannot carry him far. Riswynn kills the remaining hunter; Kenzo kills his captor before needing to escape. The last banished mount and hawk are mopped up. |

All attackers are dispatched. Kenzo climbs back up the ladder, and Tollen’s waterfront is quiet at 2 a.m.
