The Fellowship spends two days in Orenlas preparing to enter Limbo and seek Rai.

#### Intermediate
In Orenlas, they update Arheste and the elves on recent events while preparing for planar travel. Delwath asks about the elves' movements, Wellby reaches the fly's memories of Agata, Seeker readies chaos metal and practices shaping stone, and Kenzo communes with the elder tree. #### Long

Wellby makes contact with the fly and finds that it still remembers Agata fondly; winning it over will require changing its opinion and showing it a new home with him. Seeker prepares chaos metal for Plane Shift and practices making stable stone shapes, while Kenzo communes with the elder tree. They consider their means of reaching Limbo and decide they can shift directly from Orenlas rather than flying to Isingue. Cutting a new permanent passage with the Wand of the Planes is discussed and set aside.

On September 16, Delwath Plane Shifts them into glass snow, and Seeker shapes a safe passage toward an orchard.

#### Intermediate
They arrive amid cutting glass snow rather than immediate safety. Seeker turns it into a soft shelter, while Kenzo's attempt to commune with the plane hurts him. #### Long
On September 16, Delwath casts Plane Shift toward a sanctuary safe for Kenzo. They arrive in a storm of cutting glass snow and take slashing damage before Seeker shapes a soft, cotton-like nest around them. Kenzo tries to Commune with Nature but takes psychic damage from Limbo. Wellby's seeking arrow points toward Rai, initially 47 distance units away. They emerge into a black expanse dotted with blue flames and send out an Arcane Eye. Wellby notices patches of air solidifying, warning that the landscape is changing. As darkness becomes stone, Seeker remakes their shelter as a child's drawing of a ship, with an open sea and clear sky contained around it. They sail through the stone while sketch-like stone birds drift overhead. Two Arcane Eyes find more stone; a third reveals two orderly rows of floating trees.

#### Short
At the orchard, they hide their faces, avoid names, and gather fruit carrying memories and visions.

#### Intermediate
A faceless keeper tends the trees in glass dirt and forbids faces and names. Kenzo meditates his face away, Delwath makes his silver, Wellby's covering merges with his skin, and the keeper helps Seeker. A spoken proper name briefly banishes Kenzo. Fruit reveals Rai's sanctuary, the monastery, Frankar with an efreeti, and Seeker's empty home; the keeper also remembers fleeing Cha'mutte. Each companion takes something intended to help the journey.

#### Long
The trees grow from a shard of glass dirt tended by a faceless, robed keeper. To obey the orchard's ban on faces and names, Kenzo draws on his meditations on the void, Delwath uses Alter Self to make his face silver, and Wellby covers his face, the covering merging with his skin as he enters. Seeker's restless mind defeats his attempt, so the keeper helps him. Kenzo accidentally names the Material Plane and is banished, returning with a warning that another mistake would threaten the island's stability. Thinking of a desired memory or vision brings fruit into being. The keeper's pomegranate shows an unrecognized map of Rai's sanctuary; Seeker's melon shows the monks at their routines, and a flaming apple shows Frankar and an efreeti approaching a great chalice of flame. A search for Cha'mutte's lair produces nothing, and the keeper explains that ruins cannot persist in Limbo without a will sustaining them. Wellby's banana sings the keeper's alien childhood melody, while Seeker sees his own home empty and hollow. Eating another fruit restores the keeper's memory of fleeing Cha'mutte, who wanted to use Limbo's power. Before leaving, Kenzo communes with a tree and receives chaos ki; Seeker takes glass dirt, Wellby a nut for an escape route, and Delwath fruit associated with finding home and the elves.

#### Short
There, they restore the fading monks through stories and shared roles, then follow them into the ordered monastery.

#### Intermediate
Grasping hands need stories of thresholds, disembodied faces need their emotions and memories, and half-formed monks need partners for prayer, teaching, and sparring. Each companion helps complete these interrupted identities. The recovered monks follow them into a cloister and grow more solid by resuming repetitive routines. Carefully preserving those patterns, the party approaches the central temple and finds a worn jade statue missing a hand.

#### Long
At the sanctuary's edge, the least human monks are grasping hands seeking stories of thresholds. Seeker recalls the door in Eudomes' tower, Wellby an early journey, and Delwath the Rainbow Bridge and escapes from danger, especially the Plane of Water. Beyond them are faces of emotion. Kenzo meditates with a laughing face until a father-and-child memory returns; Delwath uses Suggestion to draw out a grieving face's story of carving a death marker. Wellby's account of the Dunmari landscape brings back a joyful wedding memory. Seeker describes terrifying magical dangers so vividly that another monk recalls a fear of public speaking. The final circle contains half-formed monks acting out their roles. Delwath becomes a student to a teacher of survival in Limbo. With Bardic Inspiration, Kenzo joins a praying monk in advanced Bhishma communion. Seeker conjures chalk and a board for a lesson on Tokra history, digressing into architecture, tunnels, and their battle with the werewolves until the student solidifies. Wellby mirrors a sparring monk's movements, completing the last role. Their rescued companions follow them through the barrier into a sandy cloister, where sweeping, prayer, sparring, writing, and meal preparation gradually restore their bodies and souls. The party avoids disturbing the choreographed patterns and enters a smaller likeness of Drankor's Temple of the Eight Divines, its art replaced by Bhishma stories and Jeevali's resurrection. Rai's worn jade statue stands inside, missing a hand.

#### Short
Rai explains his divided vigil and needs help recovering a fragment of stability, while Kenzo returns his jade hand and gives him relief.

#### Intermediate
Rai speaks into their minds and learns that Dimitaur never delivered his plea for help. He says he must sustain the monks while resisting an ancient entity that is drawing Limbo and the Material Plane together. The party needs the fragment of stability controlling that struggle, whose runes Rai can no longer read. Kenzo returns the hand he has carried since childhood, and it fuses to the statue. Rai can guide them toward the fragment, but warns that its keeper can reshape the surrounding terrain and that his own strength is failing.

#### Long
A voice reaches them from the statue, asking whether Dimitaur sent them. Rai had asked for help, but the message never reached the Fellowship. He explains that he brought the Dunmari monks into Limbo while trying to close the wound at Isingue. Keeping them from fading now divides his attention with restraining an ancient entity that seeks to draw Limbo and the Material Plane together. Leaving would doom the monks, while abandoning the other struggle would let the entity prevail. Rai believes a nearby fragment of stability bears hyper-dense runes that must be read in the right order, something his failing eyes can no longer do. From his observations of Limbo's changing life, he suspects that a great chaos storm gained a fixed purpose and control of its form by passing over that fragment. The Fellowship discusses taking the fragment to the rift; killing its keeper may be unnecessary. Kenzo tells Rai how his parents found the jade hand and how he has carried it since childhood, and learns that Rai has seen him in their shared dreams. He unscrews the hand from his staff and places it on the statue's stump. It fuses in place, bringing a quiet sense of relief. Rai offers to guide them toward the fragment, but they will have to act alone once there. He warns that the entity can reshape its domain, that lapses in his own concentration have cost monks their lives, and that he cannot sustain the balance indefinitely.
