## The Silent Orchard
*beat-003*

**Date**: 1749-09-16
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party obeys the faceless orchard keeper's rules and gathers fruit containing memories, visions, and intended aid.
**Long Summary**: Two rows of fruit trees grow in glass dirt under a faceless robed keeper, who forbids faces and names. Kenzo hides his face through meditation; the keeper helps the distracted Seeker, Delwath uses Alter Self to turn his face silver, and Wellby's covering merges with his skin. Kenzo is briefly banished for naming the Material Plane and is warned against another mistake. The keeper supplies a pomegranate showing Rai's sanctuary, which they do not recognize. Seeker produces a melon showing the monastery, a flaming apple showing Frankar and an efreeti approaching a great flaming chalice, and a fruit showing his empty home. A fruit sought for Cha'mutte's lair yields nothing; the keeper says ruins cannot persist without a sustaining will. Wellby's banana recalls an alien childhood melody, and the keeper recovers a memory of fleeing Cha'mutte and his desire to use Limbo's power. Kenzo communes with a tree for chaos ki; Seeker takes glass dirt, Wellby an escape-route nut, and Delwath fruit to help find the elves.

  Note: The written notes call Wellby's covering a scarf; the recorded recap calls it a hat. The keeper's claims about ruins and Cha'mutte are attributed accounts.

## Rai's divided vigil
*beat-007*

**Date**: 1749-09-16
**Time Window**: unknown
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Rai explains his struggle to protect the monks and restrain an ancient entity, and Kenzo returns his jade hand before they discuss retrieving the fragment of stability.
**Long Summary**: Rai speaks telepathically from the worn jade statue and asks whether Dimitaur brought his plea for help; the party has received no such message. He says he brought the Dunmari monks into Limbo while trying to close the wound at Isingue and must now both keep them from fading and resist an entity drawing Limbo and the Material Plane together. He believes a fragment of stability bears hyper-dense runes that must be read in the correct order; his eyes no longer work. He suspects a primordial chaos storm gained a fixed purpose through that fragment, and can guide the party to it, though retrieving it need not require killing the creature. Kenzo explains that his parents found the hand and that he has carried it since childhood; Rai says he has seen Kenzo in the dreams. Kenzo returns the hand from his staff, and it fuses to the statue, bringing Rai relief. Rai warns that the entity can reshape its domain and that monks have been lost when his concentration faltered. The party discusses recovering the fragment to heal the rift, but neither retrieval nor departure is recorded. Closing remarks about apprenticeships and fruit trade are future possibilities.
**Location**:
- Limbo: Telepathic audience with Rai in the central temple
  Note: One continuous audience on September 16, also summarized in the earlier written notes. Rai's account of the entity, its origins, and the relic is explicitly his observation or belief; his perceived elapsed time differs from the Material Plane's.
