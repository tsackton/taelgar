## scene-001 | Dawn at the Gatehouse

- Beat IDs: beat-001
- Rationale: Morning routines and the approach of one patrol form a continuous observation-and-preparation scene.

### Beats

- beat-001 | Dawn at the Gatehouse: The party wakes in the gatehouse and prepares an ambush as gnolls approach with hyenas and a flying demon.

## scene-002 | The Gatehouse Ambush

- Beat IDs: beat-002, beat-003, beat-004
- Rationale: The ambush and defense of the gate form a continuous effort to draw the retreating gnolls back and prevent their leader from escaping.

### Beats

- beat-002 | The Ambush Drives Them Back: Wellby kills the flying demon, Seeker puts the hyenas to sleep, and the gnolls retreat under the party’s ambush.
- beat-003 | Kenzo Opens the Gate: Kenzo jumps down and opens the gate to lure the gnolls back, but their leader continues fleeing.
- beat-004 | Holding the Gate: Kenzo delays the gnolls at the gate and returns a hunter’s arrow as Wellby kills their fleeing leader.

## scene-003 | Kenzo’s Last Stand

- Beat IDs: beat-005, beat-006
- Rationale: The remaining fight turns on defeating the hunters and supporting the badly wounded Kenzo in the passage beneath the gatehouse.

### Beats

- beat-005 | The Hunters Fall: The party kills both gnoll hunters while Kenzo holds the entrance against spearmen.
- beat-006 | Kenzo Stands His Ground: With only two hit points left, Kenzo holds his position until the remaining gatehouse gnolls are dead.

## scene-004 | Pursuit to the Outer Plaza
