#### Short
At dawn in Raven's Hold, the party spots a gnoll patrol and prepares an ambush from the gatehouse.

#### Intermediate
After a night of disturbing dreams, the party’s morning preparations are interrupted by nine gnolls approaching the barred gate with three hyenas and a small flying demon. Kenzo and Delwath watch the patrol cross the foul courtyard while the others take cover in the gatehouse and ready an ambush.

#### Long
Morning sunlight falls across Raven's Hold after an uneasy night of bad dreams. Wellby packs carefully, Seeker sketches maps and considers an adventure book, and Riswynn checks her armor and the shield recording her family’s history while Vola oils her gear. From their watch, Kenzo and Delwath spot nine gnolls crossing the courtyard with three hyenas and a small batlike demon. The patrol skirts pools of foul water and lingering mist before reaching the barred gate. Delwath casts Armor of Agathys and drinks a healing potion as the party takes protected positions above the entrance.

#### Short
Wellby kills the demon, Kenzo draws the retreating gnolls back to the gate, and a distant arrow brings down their fleeing leader.

#### Intermediate
Wellby’s first arrow destroys the batlike demon, and Seeker puts the three hyenas to sleep while the party attacks from cover. As the gnolls withdraw, Kenzo leaps down and opens the gate to lure them back. Most charge toward him, but their leader and one follower keep fleeing. Unable to replace the bar, Kenzo holds the gate shut with help from Seeker’s inspiration until the attackers force it open, then catches and returns a hunter’s arrow. Above him, Wellby kills the distant leader with a shot of about two hundred feet.

#### Long
Wellby opens the ambush with an arrow that destroys the batlike demon in flame and smoke. Seeker lends him a spiraling rune of inspiration and puts all three hyenas to sleep. From the gatehouse, Delwath’s blasts, Riswynn’s magic and spectral hammer, and Vola’s psychic blades join the arrows raining onto the patrol. The gnolls fire back, wounding Vola, but begin to retreat from the defended position. Kenzo jumps from the wall, slows his fall with ki, and removes the gate’s heavy bar to tempt them back. Most turn and charge; their leader and one follower continue toward the outer fort. Kenzo swings the gate shut but cannot replace the bar, leaving him to brace it alone against repeated assaults. Seeker’s inspiration helps him hold long enough to delay several attackers before they finally force the gate open. A hunter’s arrow flies toward Kenzo; he catches it and hurls it back, badly wounding the archer, then fights beneath the covered passage. Meanwhile, Wellby follows the fleeing leader between firing positions and kills it with an arrow from roughly two hundred feet away. Its companion keeps running.

#### Short
The remaining gnolls nearly overwhelm Kenzo beneath the gatehouse, but he survives with help from above and finishes the fight.

#### Intermediate
The party brings down both gnoll hunters while Kenzo fights their spear-wielding companions at the entrance, catching and returning another arrow along the way. Repeated wounds leave him close to death beneath the passage, where his companions cannot easily see or heal him. Vola lowers a rope, Wellby climbs down and drops painfully to the ground, and Delwath attacks while hanging from the wall. Kenzo stays in the melee and kills the last warriors, emerging battered but alive.

#### Long
With the leader dead, the two gnoll hunters and the warriors around Kenzo remain dangerous. Delwath kills the hunter wounded by its own arrow, while Riswynn attacks the other with Sacred Flame and her spectral hammer. Seeker’s insulting illusions spoil an enemy shot; Kenzo catches another arrow and returns it before continuing to strike with staff and fists. The second hunter falls to Delwath, but spear thrusts leave Kenzo with only two hit points. Beneath the gate passage, he is hidden from much of the support above, including healing. Vola lowers a rope and grappling hook, yet Kenzo refuses to abandon the fight, remarking that near-death experiences offer spiritual growth. Wellby descends and drops the last fifteen feet, injuring himself on landing. Delwath sends a blast from his precarious position on the rope, and Riswynn directs her hammer against a foe she cannot see. Kenzo avoids the final attacks and brings down the remaining gnolls with his staff and fists. Leaning on his staff, he emerges with the observation that the fight was more difficult than he had expected; one gnoll is still escaping toward the outer fort.

#### Short
After securing the sleeping hyenas, the party pursues the survivor and kills it and three more gnolls in the outer plaza.

#### Intermediate
Wellby sets off after the fugitive while Seeker and Riswynn confine the sleeping hyenas in pits. Kenzo uses the last of his healing mud and joins the chase through the polluted courtyard. Near the outer plaza, the fugitive meets three more gnolls. Kenzo and Wellby kill the runner, and the others arrive in time to help defeat its allies, leaving the plaza quiet except for the captive animals nearby.

#### Long
Wellby immediately pursues the survivor while Seeker shapes pits beneath the sleeping hyenas and Riswynn helps contain them. Kenzo exhausts his red healing mud to recover from his wounds, then races after Wellby past the courtyard’s stinking pools. Delwath follows, with Seeker and Riswynn farther behind. Near the stables in the outer plaza, the fugitive joins three more gnolls. Kenzo catches it with his staff and fist, and Wellby finishes it with an arrow. The other gnolls attack as the party arrives in stages; Kenzo deflects a thrown spear, though his return misses. Delwath wounds one enemy for Seeker to finish with a crossbow bolt, Riswynn kills another with Guiding Bolt, and Kenzo closes with the last. By about eight in the morning, all four are dead, and the howling of captive animals is the only disturbance around the ruined horse statue and plaza.

#### Short
Searching the fort, they find starving captive animals and signs of corruption, then pause for breakfast before investigating the pools.

#### Intermediate
Seeker heals Kenzo and ritually speaks with hyenas among the dozens of starving predators penned in the stables. They describe capture and hunger but cannot explain why they are being held. Meanwhile, Wellby finds burned barracks, Riswynn discovers Blackened Claw symbols, crates of worn equipment, and a locked room, and Delwath keeps watch. The dead leader carries a fine bloodied spear marked with a running horse and a locket of foul, steaming liquid. The party debates whether corrupted water or flesh could transform animals into gnolls, but reaches no firm conclusion. Concerned about exposing the captives to poisoned water, they postpone releasing them and take a short rest and breakfast before examining the pools.

#### Long
Seeker heals Kenzo and spends ten minutes preparing to speak with the animals while the others scout. In the stables, roughly seventy to one hundred predators are crowded into about twenty-five stalls, held by chains, ropes, and rubble. Hyenas, jackals, wild dogs, and great cats are gaunt with hunger. Four hyenas near the front tell Seeker that two-legged beasts captured them at their home, several days’ travel away, and have been starving them. He offers rations, but their account supplies no explanation for their captivity. Kenzo sits beside him, meditating as he listens to the exchange.

Wellby explores the eastern building, finding filthy sleeping quarters beneath an upper floor ruined by an old fire. In the better-preserved western building, Riswynn recognizes Blackened Claw symbols and finds about ten crates, worn leather armor and shields, and a room secured with a chain and lock. Delwath watches the plains without seeing movement, and Vola rejoins the party. Searching the dead leader, Wellby recovers a well-made spear beneath its crust of blood; its running-horse design matches the horn they already carry. A metal locket releases acrid vapor when opened and contains bubbling liquid smelling of sulfur and rotting flesh. Wellby avoids the escaping contents, closes it, and brings the spear to Kenzo.

Wellby also drags a gnoll corpse toward the animals for food, and Riswynn proposes feeding and freeing them, but the party worries they would drink the poisoned water outside. Regretting that no gnoll remains alive to question, they compare the demon’s corruption of the antelope with Vola’s former captors and the name Agata Dustmother. Delwath recalls childhood tales of abyssal corruption spreading through land and flesh, raising the possibility that gnolls are transformed animals. Neither that idea nor the proposed roles of contaminated water and meat are established. They decide to examine the green pools before releasing the captives and gather in the warming plaza for breakfast and a short rest. The pools remain unexplored.
