## Captives and Clues
*beat-009*

**Date**: 1748-04-13
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party questions starving animals and searches the fort, finding a bloodied spear and a locket of foul liquid.
**Long Summary**: Seeker heals Kenzo and performs Speak with Animals as a ritual. The ruined stables contain roughly seventy to one hundred starving predators in about twenty-five stalls, restrained with ropes, chains, and rubble. Four hyenas say two-legged beasts captured them several suns from home and are starving them; they offer no clear explanation of the ritual. Wellby finds filthy barracks and old fire damage, Riswynn finds Blackened Claw symbols, about ten crates, worn equipment, and a chained, locked room, and Delwath sees no movement on the plains. Searching the leader, Wellby finds a blood-caked spear bearing the running-horse design of their horn and a metal locket containing steaming, acrid liquid. He avoids the escaping vapor, closes it, brings the spear to Kenzo, and drags a gnoll corpse toward the animals for food.
**Location**:
- Raven's Hold: Outer plaza, ruined stables, flanking buildings, lookout, and leader’s corpse
**NPCs**:

- Captive hyenas (encountered): Questioned by Seeker; describe capture and starvation without explaining the ritual.

## Corruption, Questions, and Breakfast
*beat-010*

**Date**: 1748-04-13
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party postpones freeing the animals, considers possible sources of demonic corruption, and rests before investigating the pools.
**Long Summary**: Riswynn proposes releasing and feeding the captives, but the group worries they might drink the valley’s poisoned water. They regret killing every gnoll before questioning one and review the demon, the jackalweres who held Vola, and the mention of Agata Dustmother. Delwath recalls childhood stories of abyssal corruption infecting land, flesh, and animals; he suspects gnolls could be infected beasts, without proof. The party considers both the water and the antelope carcasses the demon had blackened with its claws as possible means of transformation. Four poisoned antelope remain in the keep. They decide to investigate the nearby green pools before releasing the animals, take a short rest and breakfast in the plaza, and end the session with that investigation still ahead.
**Location**:
- Raven's Hold: Outer plaza and nearby stables; other parts of the fort discussed
**NPCs**:
- Agata Dustmother (mentioned): Named in recollection of Vola’s shapeshifting captors.
- Vola Forena (mentioned): Her former captors are discussed.

- Four corrupted antelope carcasses (mentioned): Still in the inner keep; considered a possible source of corruption.
