#### Short
After defeating the roper, the party finds a Pearl of Power, rests in the ruined tavern, and scouts the blighted valley.

#### Intermediate
Kenzo retrieves a pearl from the roper's acidic remains, and Riswynn identifies its power to restore spellcasting energy. In the ruined tavern, Wellby recognizes a faded hobgoblin banner scratched into the wall. Seeker attunes to the pearl during a short rest, after which Wellby looks outside and finds the valley hazy, its river dark and its plants corrupted.

#### Long
With the roper dead and Inakara's people watching from the passage, the party searches the ruined marketplace. Seeker senses valuable stone somewhere to the south and below. Kenzo examines the creature and pries a pearl from its acidic remains, sacrificing part of an old mage's staff in the process. Delwath detects transmutation magic, and Riswynn's ritual reveals that the pearl can replenish spent spellcasting power. Nearby, the abandoned tavern bears graffiti of a hand with three fingers burned to the bone. Wellby recognizes an old hobgoblin battle banner, though the writing remains unreadable. The market was deliberately sacked, but the party cannot establish how its current residents fit into that history. After roughly half an hour of investigation, they rest in the tavern. Seeker attunes to the Pearl of Power, restores some magic, and feels the pearl slowly drawing energy from the land. By mid-afternoon, Wellby is peering through the main entrance toward Raven's Hold. Haze hangs over black, slimy cattails, thorny bushes, dead trees, and an almost bloody river; he sees nothing moving. He returns, and the group turns back toward the vault.

#### Short
They win passage from Inakara with a promise of treasure and an intimidating display of magic.

#### Intermediate
Inakara insists their earlier bargain granted passage out, and bars further exploration. Kenzo senses her fear as well as her strength and magic. Riswynn promises a small share of treasure, while Seeker, Kenzo, and Delwath make themselves look dangerous. Inakara gives way, warning that she will watch them and that brain eaters may throw their bodies to the blind ones.

#### Long
Inakara rises to block their return through the inhabited cavern, insisting that safe passage out was all she promised. Speaking through Dwarvish, the party withdraws briefly to consider gifts, stealth, and threats. Kenzo's soul sight reveals that she fears them, despite her considerable strength and magical ability. Three attendants seem almost emotionless; a better-equipped fourth appears anxious and ready to fight. Riswynn offers a token of friendship from whatever they find, and Inakara becomes eager at the mention of the mirror doors she cannot open. Riswynn refuses to promise everything behind them. Seeker makes his fingers seem to stretch like roper tentacles, Kenzo strikes his staff with a tremor and a flare of torchlight, and Delwath summons a sword crackling with green flame. Inakara backs away and lets them through, but promises to watch and demands a share. As they pass, she mutters that something may eat their brains and toss their bodies to the blind ones.

#### Short
Seeker and Riswynn open the vault using keys visible only in the brass doors' reflections.

#### Intermediate
Heading down beneath the market, Kenzo shares a strange vision linking the residents to a mind flayer. Wellby detects an invisible follower, probably Inakara, and drives her back from the vault entrance. There, Seeker and Riswynn see keys around the statues' necks only in the polished brass reflections. Removing and turning both keys sends beams of light into the central door, opening the way.

#### Long
The party heads north past the pools to a circular chamber, where a dwarf statue bears a family resemblance to Riswynn. Kenzo describes a memory that was not his own: creatures like the residents following a tall figure with tentacles around its mouth. Together with Inakara's threats, it suggests a connection to mind flayers, though he cannot say whether one is nearby. The southeast passage descends beneath the market to three circular brass doors facing two armored statues. Wellby hears someone following invisibly, runs back with his torch, and demands a conversation. Muttering in broken Dwarvish retreats down the corridor; the group suspects Inakara. Wellby finds no mechanical traps in the antechamber. Seeker and Riswynn, unlike their companions, can see necklaces on the statues in the brass reflections. Watching those reflections, they feel and lift two keys from the statues, making keyholes appear in the side doors. Turning both keys through a full circle fills the room with light. Two beams join at the central door, which opens onto a descending passage. The party enters together, while the unseen follower seems to recoil from the brightness.

#### Short
Inside, Riswynn recovers her ancestral shield beside the remains of five dwarves and a mind flayer.

#### Intermediate
The undisturbed treasury holds five dwarven skeletons and an ancient mind flayer with its skull smashed open. Riswynn finds the shield that preserves her family's stories, and Seeker respectfully takes a warhammer inscribed with runes of thunder and stoneworking. Wellby and Kenzo open the remaining chests, recovering six potion bottles, two crystal globes, and Brawnanvil trade bars. Riswynn reserves the shield for her family and shares the other treasure.

#### Long
Dust lies thick in the treasury. Four dwarves have been carefully laid out, while a fifth sits slumped against the wall with a warhammer whose runes remain pristine. Beside them lies the shield Riswynn came to reclaim: a relic valued for the stories of her ancestors rather than its use in battle. Near the inner iron doors, a long-dead mind flayer has its skull smashed open. The arrangement suggests that a mortally wounded dwarf laid out the others before dying. Seeker studies the hammer, recognizing runes related to thunderous force and others resonating with stoneworking, though its full powers remain unknown. After discussing the dwarven practice of returning relics to their clans, he respectfully lifts it from the dead dwarf's grasp. Of eight stone chests, six are empty. Wellby opens one of the remaining two to find six bottles marked with runes for life, strength, and health, along with two crystal globes. Kenzo opens the other, revealing fourteen silver bars and four gold bars stamped with the Brawnanvil crest. Riswynn claims the shield for her people, agrees to share the other finds, and carries the metal bars as they turn to the inner doors.

#### Short
Beyond the vault, Delwath scouts a breached tunnel and brings back adamantine from a deep mine shaft.

#### Intermediate
The inner chamber is wrecked, and hammer marks suggest that its western tunnel was deliberately collapsed. Seeker clears a small opening, and Delwath turns invisible to scout beyond it. The tunnel ends at a deep vertical shaft. He uses Mage Hand to chip off an ore sample, lets his hammer fall while catching it, and returns without hearing an impact. Seeker identifies the sample as adamantine.

#### Long
The iron doors open onto a ruined chamber, where rubble blocks a rough tunnel leading west. Seeker shifts enough loose stone to peer through and finds hammer blows along the walls. Beyond the collapse, the passage looks less like dwarven construction than the work of a directed earthquake. The party suspects the ancient mind flayer entered this way and a defender brought the tunnel down behind it. Seeker's earlier sense of valuable stone still points farther west, so Delwath casts Invisibility and scouts several hundred feet down the steep passage. He finds no signs of recent traffic. The tunnel ends at a vertical shaft about fifteen feet wide, extending beyond his sight in both directions, with a dull metallic vein across the gap. Using Mage Hand and a hammer, he chips free a small sample. When it starts to fall, he catches the sample and drops the hammer; no sound of impact follows. Delwath returns, and Seeker identifies adamantine, a rare metal useful in unusually durable weapons and armor. The shaft is too far away to explain the original stone sense directly, though the same ore may lie higher in the mine.

#### Short
After securing the vault, they pay Inakara to guide them to a locked passage toward Raven's Hold and hear her warnings of returning mind flayers.

#### Intermediate
The crystal globes prove to be driftglobes, and Kenzo takes one before the party reseals the breach and closes the vault. Inakara accepts two silver bars and a promise of three more to lead them to a tunnel, though she refuses to go through it. Along the way she claims to be preparing for the mind flayers and dismisses the sightless ones as powerless. She leaves the party at a rusty locked gate, uncertain how her warnings connect to the valley's corruption.

#### Long
The globes are identified as sources of light that can also produce daylight once each day, and Kenzo takes one. Seeker reseals the rubble, the party pays respects to the dead, and they close the mirror doors; the keys vanish from the locks and reappear on the statues in reflection. Inakara promptly demands her share. Two silver bars and a promise of three more persuade her to guide them to a passage toward Raven's Hold, but she admits she has never passed its locked bars and refuses to lead them through. She names herself and predicts that the coming brain eaters will spare her while killing the party. Their new light makes her recoil as she leads them through the halls, past moving shadows and a northern passage she warns them to avoid. Asked about the sightless ones, she calls them powerless figures who sit and moan while she does everything. When the party names mind flayers, she says they are coming and that she is clearing the way for them. Kenzo senses fear and doubts that more silver would buy useful answers. At a rusty chained gate beneath the main entrance, with a tunnel running east beyond it, Inakara threatens to point the party out to the newcomers and vanishes. The gate remains shut. As play ends, the group weighs her claims against the old corpse and Kenzo's vision; an invading army or a connection to the portal and poisoned water remains speculation.
