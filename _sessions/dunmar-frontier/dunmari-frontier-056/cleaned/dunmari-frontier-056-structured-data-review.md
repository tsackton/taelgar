## Combat

### Six shriveled dwarves at the blocked clifftop cave

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Archers wound Riswynn. Thror misses and Merash lands two handaxes; a cold black sphere catches the party. Rothfis flies free and Riswynn dispels it. |
| 2 | Rothfis catches an arrow but misses the return. Thror kills one enemy; Merash’s corrected blast hits all five survivors. Rothfis kills another; shadow arms wound him and Riswynn, who gains inspiration. |
| 3 | Tipsy Sway redirects a missed sword into another wight; Life Drain then reduces Rothfis’s maximum by six. Thror’s Shatter and lightning kill two. Merash closes, Rothfis kills a fifth, and Riswynn misses. |
| 4 | Thror kills the last enemy with Shocking Grasp. The extra storm movement is denied for a cantrip. |

All six attackers die and their ghosts rise. The four dwarves survive; Rothfis’s six-point maximum-hit-point reduction lasts until a long rest. Tak speaks from the blocked cave afterward. The ghost on the distant prominence is only observed at the session cutoff.
