Delwath uses Dimension Door to rejoin the attack, breaks the guardian’s concentration, and kills it.

#### Short
Beyond a portal under the lava, they find the Cloudspinner and haul her still-sealed crystal prison into the Portable Hole.

#### Intermediate
After healing and smashing the scrying mirror, they recover ten Concordia Pyrae volumes describing fire magic and the villa’s history. Seeker displaces the lava to expose a locked portal, opened with the fiend’s key. Inside, shadowy bonds suspend the Cloudspinner’s crystal and drain the rescuers’ vitality. Once the bonds are cut, the exhaustion-proof ladder pulls the crystal out; they cannot open it, so carry the captive away inside the Portable Hole.

#### Long
Delwath’s Prayer of Healing gives the party a brief rest while the ladder destroys the scrying mirror. Ten fire-resistant Concordia Pyrae books survive in the chamber: seven research volumes and three chronicles, including an account of Apollyon and Cha'mutte fighting over the Cloudspinner. Beneath the lava basin, the party detects a concealed portal. Seeker dispels the magical heating and then uses Wall of Stone to displace the still-hot lava, releasing the clay construct as his concentration changes. The fiend’s key opens the way into a chamber of shifting shadows, where a woman lies suspended inside a smoky crystal held by four dark ropes. Delwath begins cutting the bonds, but the chamber drains his vitality; Wellby severs the final tether, and Seeker also suffers its exhausting effects. The animated ladder is immune to that exhaustion and can maneuver the heavy crystal in the gravityless space. It hauls the prison back through the portal. Unable to break the crystal itself, the party secures the still-imprisoned Cloudspinner in the Portable Hole.

#### Short
Taking refuge with Dimitaur, they rest and share a vision of Rai leaving Apollyon’s dagger in the Temple of the Eight Divines.

#### Intermediate
They teleport to Dimitaur’s summit shelter, where his warning and Milo and Tilly’s insistence on helping persuade them to rest and face the dragon. During their sleep, the completed jade hand reveals Rai catching Apollyon’s dagger before it reaches Apollyon’s tomb. #### Long
With the crystal recovered and Ra'ghemdros likely alerted, they teleport to Dimitaur at the summit temples. The party warns Milo and Tilly to stay clear, but the halflings insist on coming to hunt the dragon. With the spell slot needed for a simulacrum already spent, the group settles for an eight-hour rest beginning around five, guarded by Delwath’s lynxes. In a shared dream, the jade hand shows Cha'mutte’s death and Rai’s immediate journey to Drankor, where he catches a dagger just before it strikes Apollyon’s tomb. Holding it cuts Apollyon off from the souls that sustain him. Decades pass, and Rai moves to the Temple of the Eight Divines; over the years, Apollyon weakens while the net of Limbo woven into his prison spreads across the world. Rai finally leaves the dagger in the temple’s divine protection and goes to address that danger with the monks. The vision gives the party a place to search for the dagger, though it may no longer be there; their suggestion that Rai went to Isingue remains a conjecture.

#### Intermediate

The scar opens into sunrise around the horizon, and her corpse crashes through the Temple of the Night Queen. Wellby shoots down the last fleeing chimera. With another dragonet battle ending in the distance, Dimitaur announces the dragon’s death and calls everyone to the villa for rewards.

#### Short
They divide Ra'ghemdros’s hoard, leave Dimitaur as protector of Hkar, and sail back to Vindristjarna with Milo and Tilly.

After resting and bypassing the lair’s acid traps, they discover a vast hoard arranged like a museum. Dimitaur claims Apollyon’s throne as protector of Hkar and oversees treasure for the dragonets.
