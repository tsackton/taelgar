## Combat

### Fiend guarding the prison beneath the villa

Scatter places the party and ladder in the fiend’s room. Kenzo activates the claw; Aid, inspiration and the ladder’s water retaliation prepare the attack.

| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Seeker identifies the one-way scrying mirror. The fiend resists the ladder’s grapple and Wellby’s blindness; Delwath’s radiant sword blocks regeneration. Kenzo lands claw and psychic blows, and the fiend teleports. |
| 2 | The construct and ladder attack; whips pull Delwath into lava despite an earlier successful save. He climbs out and critically smites, but Wall of Fire and melee attacks knock him unconscious. Kenzo forces a Legendary Resistance with Stunning Strike and carries him out, costing a failed death save. |
| 3 | Seeker heals Delwath for 28. Wellby blinds the fiend, then suffers two legendary whips and a pull into the fire. Delwath returns by Dimension Door, knocks it prone, breaks the wall’s concentration and kills it. |

The fiend vanishes. The black iron key is described retrospectively and later opens the portal; the ladder subsequently smashes the scrying mirror.

Tilly’s Aid gives Wellby, Milo and herself +15 maximum HP, and she hands out two goodberries each and summons her draconic spirit. Seeker distributes inspiration; the initial stone construct and ladder are ready when initiative begins at 600 feet.
