| Round | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Kenzo’s attack is redirected into Wellby before he hits Fausto. Banishment fails; Fausto reveals the Cleansed and teleports. Glass guardians blind Kenzo and release lightning; Seeker heats a guardian while Wellby tries a stair button. |
| 2 | Kenzo clears his blindness and escapes with one eye, ultimately taking no falling damage. Delwath takes the other eye, stones and mace and casts Fly. Seeker survives the golems, escapes blind, and Wellby follows with the whistle. |
| 3 | Fausto’s psychic attack ends Fly. Kenzo’s attempted catch fails, leaving Delwath and Seeker unconscious after the fall. Failed death saves threaten them before Wellby revives Delwath with a potion. |
