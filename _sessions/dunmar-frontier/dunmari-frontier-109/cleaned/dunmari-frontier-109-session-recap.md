#### Short
After scouting the dragon’s territory, the party takes leave of Dimitaur and prepares to fly to Apollyon’s tower as ospreys.

#### Intermediate
From the Sibyl’s mountaintop temple, the party chooses Apollyon’s tower as its next destination. The previous evening’s Clairvoyance revealed dragonets and chimeras around the dragon’s ruined villa, while Kenzo sensed a fiend beneath it but no dragon. In the morning, he gives Dimitaur a statue as a farewell gift, and Seeker prepares Animal Shapes for a flight across the jagged coastal terrain.

#### Long
Sunrise reveals Apollyon’s intact black tower beyond the forests and rivers of Circular Island. The previous evening, Seeker had used three Clairvoyance charges to inspect the dragon’s territory: a ruined villa, a plaza, and thermal pools amid red-orange growth, with dragonets and four chimeras nearby. Kenzo had sensed a fiend beneath the villa but no dragon, leaving the party to wonder whether its lair lay beyond ordinary space. They choose to investigate the tower and its possible forge first. After breakfast, Kenzo gives Dimitaur a statue of Dimitaur himself, and the party prepares to depart. Kenzo detects no conscious beings at the tower, although constructs and traps remain possible. To cross the jagged coast, Seeker will turn them into ordinary ospreys, with the rope ladder to be animated nearer their destination.

#### Short
The tower guardians shoot them out of the air, forcing them to open the rooftop hatch and escape inside.

#### Intermediate
Flying west, Seeker briefly lands to animate the rope ladder and turn it into a fifth osprey. The tower’s stone guardians fire crystal darts as they approach, breaking several bird forms. Unable to find a normal opening mechanism in the rooftop hatch, Wellby uses the chime while Kenzo carries Seeker through. Delwath follows under fire and closes the hatch behind them.

#### Long
They fly west past statues of the Warlord and Wildling, pressing through an oppressive presence as the tower draws near. Seeker lands behind a boulder to animate the rope ladder, then transforms it into a fifth osprey for the final approach. Two stone guardians with metal fists stand below the spire. Their crystal darts strike just before the birds land, breaking several transformations and leaving Seeker injured by the fall. On the roof, investigation and magical senses reveal no straightforward way to open the handleless hatch. The party considers using the chime under Silence, but the guardians rise to attack before the ritual can finish; a tiny mechanical fly has been watching them. Wellby opens the hatch with the chime, Kenzo carries Seeker down, and Delwath follows after another dart hit, pulling the hatch shut over a chamber of manacles, an anvil, and descending stairs.

#### Short
Inside, Seeker identifies soulbinding manacles before the pursuing guardians force a battle on the stairs, where the animated ladder helps the party destroy them.

#### Intermediate
Seeker learns that the manacles feed sacrificed souls into a phylactery and help prepare a lich’s transformation potion. Before they can investigate further, the guardians approach from below. The ladder blocks the narrow stairs while Wellby repeatedly blinds the constructs and Kenzo attacks from the air. Their magical defenses frustrate Delwath’s spells, but concentrated physical attacks eventually bring both guardians down.

#### Long
The anvil shows no response to spilled blood, but the manacles carry powerful necromancy. Seeker resists their influence while identifying how they strip souls from sacrificed victims, feed a nearby phylactery, and contribute to the potion used to become a lich. The party considers consulting the mirror, only to hear the guardians coming up from below. They hold the narrow stairs with the animated ladder in front, Kenzo flying above it, and Wellby firing from behind. Delwath’s conjured shadow lynxes cannot harm the constructs, and an acid spell rebounds toward Kenzo, who deflects it. Sanctuary briefly protects the ladder while Wellby’s arrows repeatedly blind the guardians. Kenzo absorbs and returns their blows; Delwath drops into melee, and Seeker attacks in bird form. Kenzo destroys the first guardian, and Delwath’s final sword strike reduces the second to dust.

#### Short
With the guardians defeated, the mirror shows Apollyon forging a plain adamantine dagger to hold the souls used in his transformation.

#### Intermediate
After healing and salvaging two cracked cores and two mechanical flies, the party uses the mirror on the manacles. Kenzo’s question reveals a human Apollyon forging an unadorned dagger and feeding it sacrificed souls. Seeker gathers nearby adamantine scraps in the hope that a connection to the blade might help locate the phylactery.

#### Long
The party heals Kenzo and the battered ladder, then collects two cracked metallic cores and two mechanical flies from the guardians’ remains. Although the mirror will need seven days to recharge, they choose to question the fixed manacles while they can. Kenzo asks to see the phylactery. In the vision, a still-human Apollyon wears a crown and rainbow cloak as he hammers an iron-hilted dagger, layering it with inscribed adamantine. Repeated sacrifices in the manacles send souls into the plain blade, while blood gathers below and shadows from the crown contribute to the transformation potion. Knowing the phylactery’s appearance, Seeker searches the surrounding stone for adamantine. He finds scraps rather than the dagger and gathers about half a pound, hoping they might provide a useful connection to it.

#### Short
Descending through the ruins, they recover two magical books and bypass a room of moving metal before searching the living quarters.

#### Intermediate
The ruined library holds a crimson book of lich-making instructions and a second volume secured by a metal clasp. Seeker examines the dangerous crimson book before both go into the portable hole. Farther down, the party passes an Omnis Pura chamber and discovers metal scraps moving beyond a partition. They fly around that room through exterior windows and recover valuables from the residential floors.

#### Long
They leave the ritual chamber and descend past the terrace and decayed study into a ruined library. Two books have survived: a locked volume with a metal clasp and an unmarked crimson book. Seeker’s examination reveals that the latter is bound in blood-soaked human skin and contains instructions for becoming a lich. They use Mage Hand to stow both books in the portable hole. Below the library, an Omnis Pura meeting room contains imperial statues, but rattling metal beyond a partition gives them pause. Delwath’s Ghostly Gaze reveals scraps swirling in the next room. The party uses osprey forms to fly outside and reenter through lower windows, leaving that threat unexplored. Searching the residential floors yields coins, a silver pitcher, a gold platter, and three plain gold rings.

#### Short
They destroy the audience chamber’s skeletons, remove its throne as elephants, and rest before scouting the basement forge, where opening the door triggers a fireball.

#### Intermediate
Wellby awakens roughly forty skeletons, then seals their chamber with an immovable rod so Delwath can destroy them from outside with Sickening Radiance. A curse briefly forces Seeker to attack Delwath before a shadow leaves him. The party becomes elephants to push the jeweled throne into the portable hole, then rests before inspecting the lower forge. Delwath spots a void sword and invisible force hands beyond its door; Wellby’s attempt to unlock it triggers a fireball that burns Seeker.

#### Long
At ground level, Wellby opens the audience chamber and finds roughly forty skeletons beginning to rise around a jeweled stone throne. He shuts the doors and secures them with an immovable rod. Seeker and Delwath fly outside to reach the windows, but when they resume their normal forms, the crimson book’s curse forces Seeker to strike Delwath with Catapult. A black shadow leaves Seeker’s face, and he remembers none of the attack. Delwath’s Sickening Radiance destroys the trapped skeletons; Seeker heals him, and Kenzo confirms that the possessing influence is gone. The party rearranges the portable hole and becomes elephants to push the throne inside. Below the spoiled basement stores they find another locked door, then return upstairs for tea, biscuits, and a short rest. Back at the door, Delwath’s Ghostly Gaze reveals a forge, a floating void sword, and side rooms; See Invisibility exposes two force hands. They spread out before Wellby picks the lock. A fireball erupts: Wellby evades it, but Seeker takes the blast.

#### Short
The ensuing fight ends with the void sword, its four daggers, and both force hands destroyed, aided throughout by the animated ladder.

#### Intermediate
Seeker reanimates the ladder while Kenzo assesses the sword’s defenses. Delwath casts Sickening Radiance, but the sword passes through the closed door and the hands force their way after it. Wellby’s arrows and Delwath’s smite break the sword into four daggers. Seeker drops Animal Shapes to slow three of them, leaving the party without its flight home. With the ladder blocking and grappling the hands, they destroy every remaining defender.

#### Long
As the void sword and force hands engage, Seeker brings the ladder back to life and Kenzo uses Soul Sight to learn the sword’s extensive immunities. Delwath fills the forge with Sickening Radiance, and Kenzo shuts the door as they withdraw. The sword passes straight through it, stabbing Wellby and the ladder; the hands batter the door open. Seeker turns a psychic attack into fire to bypass the sword’s immunity, while Wellby lands a devastating arrow and Kenzo strikes at range with Lengau’s Claw. Delwath uses Scatter to move Seeker to safety and put himself beside the sword, then delivers a critical Eldritch Smite. The blade shatters into four attacking daggers. Seeker abandons Animal Shapes to slow three of them, and Wellby destroys the unslowed dagger. The ladder holds the passage and grapples a hand while Kenzo and Delwath finish the remaining blades. Wellby’s fiery arrows bring down both hands, including the last one after it shoves the ladder backward and knocks it prone.

#### Short
They recover treasures and decipher the forge’s costly enchantment process, then teleport back to Dimitaur, who burns the crimson lich book.

#### Intermediate
The forge’s stores yield a damaged silver rod, twenty boxes of magical powders, and three jeweled rings. The Tanshi tell Delwath that the forge draws power from its user and that instructions survive in Apollyon’s notes. Wellby opens the protected spellbook at the cost of a psychic blast, allowing Seeker to decipher the process: enchanting an item consumes the user’s highest spell slot until a successful daily recovery check. At sunset they teleport to the temple with the lich book in a lead box; Dimitaur promptly destroys it with fire.

#### Long
With the defenders gone, the party searches the forge and its side rooms. They find extensive metal stock and etching supplies, then open a stone chest containing a damaged silver rod with an organic spiral and star-like orb, twenty labeled boxes of magical powders, and three braided gold rings set with emerald, ruby, and sapphire. Kenzo senses no ley lines. Delwath consults the Tanshi, learning that the forge remains functional, draws power from its user, and has surviving instructions. Upstairs, the portable hole smells of blood from the crimson book. Wellby finds a glyph on the other book’s clasp, but two attempts to dispel it fail. He uses the chime from a distance and still suffers a powerful psychic blast, though he resists the lasting befuddlement. Inside are Apollyon’s backup spells and encrypted research. Seeker spends three hours deciphering the notes while the others rest, finishing around six. The forge combines etched metal and powders with magic channeled through its hand plate; it consumes the user’s highest spell slot, which ordinary rest cannot restore without a successful daily recovery check. They leave the forge unused. Seeker creates a temporary lead box for the lich book, and they teleport to the Sibyl’s temple at sunset. Asked to watch the book while they deal with the dragon tomorrow, Dimitaur instead orders everyone clear and burns it with a fireball. They report their discoveries and prepare to rest.
