**Date**: 1749-05-18 to 1749-05-19
**Time Window**: night
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: Dimitaur describes the island’s history and western ruins as the party weighs a dragon bargain and settles in for the night.
**Long Summary**: Dimitaur knows no reason Ra’ghemdros cannot leave the island and warns that an association with Rai would put him in danger. He describes dragonets traveling with the people of Hkar, the island itself being moved by ancient magic, and Apollyon’s old western tower above a forge, which he has never explored. The overnight rest carries the session into May 19.
