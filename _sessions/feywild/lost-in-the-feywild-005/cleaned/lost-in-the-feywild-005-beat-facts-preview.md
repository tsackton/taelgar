## Choosing the Glass Key
*b001*

**Date**: 1740-10-06
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: After reviewing the Dreamer notebook and their competing obligations, the party decides to seek the glass key while leaving the prisoner confined.
**Long Summary**: The party finishes discussing the Dreamer notebook's conflation of dreams and souls before the session recap returns them to Kallipseis's fungal greenhouse. After restating their appearances and motives, they debate whether they owe anything to Hulda or Lord Endless Ending, question Menestheus about the order and its absent leader, and consider the risk that taking the glass key could release the prisoner. They conclude that the key is still their best route out, but intend to remove it without opening the prisoner's door, and leave Kallipseis to continue work on her fungal golem.
**Location**:
- 27th Room: room 18, Kallipseis's fungal greenhouse and laboratory
  Note: The beat resumes exactly where the preceding session ended and closes as the party exits the greenhouse.
**NPCs**:
- Kallipseis (encountered): ancient dwarven gardener and self-described head of Arithrimos Lamperum. She returns to supervising construction of her fungal golem while the party plans its next move.
- Menestheus (companion): Arithrimos Lamperum mathematician and mapmaker. He answers questions about the order, Kallipseis, and the purpose of the prison complex before leading the party onward.
- Lord Endless Ending (mentioned): Fey lord tied to the bargain. The party remains divided over whether helping him is preferable to helping Hulda.
- Hulda (mentioned): original employer. The party treats his apparent betrayal as grounds to disregard their original agreement.
- the prisoner (mentioned): being confined in the central room. The party wants the glass key but does not intend to open the prisoner's door.
**Items**:
- Dreamer notebook (encountered): Its language about dreaming and souls drives the opening discussion.
- glass key (mentioned): The party decides to retrieve it as their best chance of leaving the complex.
- fungal golem body (encountered): Kallipseis continues supervising its construction as the party departs.
**Organizations**:
- Arithrimos Lamperum (encountered): Kallipseis and Menestheus are the two surviving members present, though Menestheus disputes treating her as his direct superior.

## Lessons and Clockworks
*b002*

**Date**: 1740-10-06
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The party studies a dandelion lecture and a black painting of a seed sprouting through a cosmic tear, then finds a portable clock that tracks the prison.
**Long Summary**: After crossing a parlor, the party enters a lecture hall whose blackboard diagrams wind-dispersed plants and whose enormous black painting conceals a star field, a tear in the universe, and the textured shape of a sprouting seed. Tarek also finds The Shape of Discovery, a treatise linking curiosity, greed, chance, and possibility, while the group realizes that Alden matched Kallipseis's description of an infected person. Menestheus identifies the painted seed crossing between worlds as exactly the kind of breach his order guarded against. In the next clockworks workshop, Tarek takes a portable space-time clock whose changing numbers track the prison's position, and the party reaches another listening station.
**Location**:
- 27th Room: parlor, lecture hall, clockworks workshop, and a mechanical listening room
  Note: The party advances through several adjacent rooms while following Menestheus's route toward the glass key.
**NPCs**:
- Menestheus (companion): guide and planar mapmaker. He explains that the painted seed resembles an uncontrolled breach between worlds and identifies the clocks as prison trackers.
- Alden (mentioned): Hulda's servant. The party connects his pale, round, unexpectedly strong appearance and fear of fire to the fungal infection.
**Items**:
- The Shape of Discovery (encountered): A brief treatise arguing that curiosity shapes possibility through the same irrational unpredictability as greed and games of chance.
- black star-field painting (encountered): Its nearly invisible center depicts a sprouting seed emerging through a tear between worlds.
- portable space-time clock (encountered): Tarek wraps and carries an unfinished desk-sized clock whose numbers change as the prison moves.
**Organizations**:
- Arithrimos Lamperum (encountered): The lecture hall and workshop belonged to the order, whose purpose included preventing growth through planar breaches.

## Thirst and the Animal Lab
*b003*

**Date**: 1740-10-06
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The Thirst notebook ties the prisoner to the fungal children, and the party's search of an animal lab becomes an ambush when Menestheus is dragged away.
**Long Summary**: The notebook titled Thirst describes the speaker filling empty heads with its children and being helped to find the person who can free it. The party interprets the children as the fungal infection, suspects the prisoner manipulated Alden and the false-looking deed that brought them here, and resolves not to free it. When Kaito takes the listener's role, he hears the prisoner claim it is full of dreams, can read the party, and sees both the moving exit and a hole large enough for its children. The group moves into an animal experimentation lab filled with cages, restraints, dead animals, an infected ant colony, and humanoid remains; as they approach the exit, a hand pulls Menestheus out of sight.
**Location**:
- 27th Room: mechanical listening station and animal experimentation laboratory
  Note: The beat shifts from interpreting the Thirst notebook to exploring the adjoining lab, where the ambush begins.
**NPCs**:
- Menestheus (companion): guide through the complex. He trusts Thalestria's judgment that the prisoner must remain confined and is dragged out of sight at the end of the beat.
- the prisoner (encountered): voice at the Thirst listening station. It communicates through the window while Kaito listens, saying the party is helping it and that its children can pass through a hole.
- Thalestria (mentioned): former head of Arithrimos Lamperum. Menestheus treats her decision to imprison the speaker as reason to keep it confined.
**Items**:
- Thirst notebook (encountered): Its transcriptions connect the prisoner, the fungal children, and the search for someone able to free it.
- infected ant colony (encountered): The dead colony shows fungal bodies bursting from the ants, like cordyceps.
**Organizations**:
- Arithrimos Lamperum (mentioned): The party debates whether the order understood the prisoner well enough to justify its confinement.

## The Prisoner Speaks
*b007*

**Date**: 1740-10-06
**Time Window**: morning
**Combat**: no
**Short Summary**: The prisoner addresses Hulda and her five friends through a listening station, and the session ends when a clockwork figure awakens in the final library before the prisoner's door.
**Long Summary**: In the map library, Menestheus describes other Arithrimos Lamperum facilities and an indefinitely confined creature that perceived its whole life at once and served as an augury. The party then passes through a fabrication lab into room 25, where the prisoner loops statements addressed to Hulda and declares that Hulda is bringing five friends who are outside its room and can hear it now. One door from the prisoner, they enter room 26, a massive library whose shelves include chained books. A human-sized porcelain and clockwork torso anchored to a desk raises its head, opens its eyes, and studies the party, providing the session's cliffhanger before the table briefly debriefs.
**Location**:
- 27th Room: map library, fabrication lab, room 25 listening station, and room 26 library
  Note: The party ends in the library immediately before the door to the prisoner's central room.
**NPCs**:
- Menestheus (companion): guide and former order mapmaker. He recognizes the map library as the kind of place where he trained and describes another facility's captive augury creature.
- the prisoner (encountered): voice heard in room 25. It directly addresses Hulda and announces that her five friends are outside its room and hearing it.
- Hulda (mentioned): person addressed by the prisoner. The party identifies him as the subject of the prisoner's statements about killing a dragon and bringing five friends.
- clockwork library figure (encountered): porcelain-faced construct anchored to a desk in room 26. It awakens, looks directly at the party, and tilts its head before the session ends.
**Items**:
- map library collection (encountered): Its drawers contain maps of Taelgar and abstract topological diagrams, but no obvious Feywild maps.
- room 25 listening notebook (encountered): Its entries accompany the prisoner's contemporaneous statements to Hulda and the party.
- chained library books (encountered): Some volumes in room 26 are physically chained to their shelves.
**Organizations**:
- Arithrimos Lamperum (encountered): Its map archive, fabrication lab, listening station, and library occupy the final rooms the party crosses.
