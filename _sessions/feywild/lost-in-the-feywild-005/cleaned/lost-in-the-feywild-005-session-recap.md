#### Short
The party decides to retrieve the glass key without freeing the prisoner.

#### Intermediate
After weighing Hulda's betrayal against Lord Endless Ending's bargain, the party chooses the glass key as its best route out. They intend to leave the prisoner confined and follow Menestheus from Kallipseis's greenhouse.

#### Long
The party finishes interpreting the Dreamer notebook's conflation of dreams and souls, then reviews the choices left by Hulda, Lord Endless Ending, and the prison at the center of the shifting house. Menestheus explains what little he knows about the order and its absent leadership while Kallipseis returns to her fungal golem. Concluding that the glass key offers the only clear path out, the party decides to retrieve it without opening the prisoner's door and leaves the greenhouse behind.

#### Short
Following Menestheus, they find evidence that the infection is spreading through planar breaches and take a clock that tracks the prison.

#### Intermediate
They cross a parlor into a lecture hall where a dandelion lesson and a black painting depict a seed sprouting through a tear between worlds. Tarek finds The Shape of Discovery and takes a portable clock that changes as the prison moves.

#### Long
They pass through a game room into a lecture hall whose chalkboard surveys wind-dispersed plants. A nearly black painting hides stars, a tear in the universe, and the textured form of a seed sprouting through it; Menestheus recognizes this as exactly the kind of uncontrolled planar growth his order was meant to stop. Tarek also finds The Shape of Discovery, which treats curiosity as an unpredictable force that shapes possibility. In the next workshop, he wraps and carries an unfinished desk-sized clock whose coordinates change with the prison, and the party enters another listening room.

#### Short
The next listening station ties the prisoner to the fungal children before Menestheus is dragged away in an animal lab.

#### Intermediate
The Thirst notebook describes the prisoner filling empty heads with its children, and Kaito hears it claim the party is helping it escape. They refuse to free it, but in the adjoining animal lab an unseen attacker pulls Menestheus out of sight.

#### Long
The notebook titled Thirst describes children placed in bodies emptied of souls and helpers searching for the person who can free the speaker. The party identifies those children with the fungal infection, suspects the prisoner manipulated Alden and the deed that led them here, and resolves to keep it confined. Taking the listener's place, Kaito hears the prisoner say it is full of dreams, can read the party, and sees both the moving exit and a hole large enough for its children. They move on through an animal laboratory filled with restraints, dead specimens, and a fungus-ravaged ant colony, until a hand reaches from a side passage and drags Menestheus away.

#### Short
They rescue him from two mushroom men, killing and burning both infected bodies.

#### Intermediate
The party attacks the mushroom man holding Menestheus as a second Alden-like corpse rises to join the fight. Menestheus is dragged into a mouth-covered chest and nearly killed, but Txarro heals him before Edric destroys the last creature and the party burns both bodies.

#### Long
The party enters initiative among the laboratory tables and specimen containers, with Menestheus already grappled by a mushroom man. Edric rages and charges the captor, Kaito attacks with Dissonant Whispers, and Tarek's approach awakens a second infected corpse that looks nearly identical to Alden. The creatures retaliate with elongated fingers and mouths opening across their chests; one briefly catches Tarek while the other pulls Menestheus inward, drops him to zero hit points, and inflicts a failed death save. Ayveen's Silvery Barbs blunts the assault, Txarro becomes a tiger and later restores Menestheus with Cure Wounds, and the party focuses down both enemies. Edric crushes the final mushroom man's head, releasing a cloud of seeds that Txarro breathes in, and the remains burn like dry straw.

#### Short
Afterward, Tarek discovers white filaments growing inside Menestheus, and Thalestria's sealed office resists every attempt to open it.

#### Intermediate
Although healing closes Menestheus's wounds, Tarek sees fungal growth inside his mouth and privately warns Txarro. They continue through more workrooms and an unfinished planar orrery, but even Edric's enraged blows cannot break into Thalestria's office.

#### Long
After the fight, further magic heals Menestheus's external wounds, but Tarek's examination reveals white filaments growing inside his mouth. He quietly tells Txarro, and they conceal the discovery until they know whether anything can be done. The party presses through a dead plant greenhouse, a picked-over armory, and an unfinished orrery modeling interlocked planes before reaching the order's offices. Thalestria's door has no visible lock and withstands repeated attempts to smash it open; weakened after raging, Edric accepts the failure as a warning from the gods and agrees to move on.

- Kind: beat
- Beat IDs: b007

- Locations: 27th Room
- NPCs: Menestheus, the prisoner, clockwork library figure

- Items: map library collection, room 25 listening notebook, chained library books
- Enemies: none
- Image:
- Image Placement:
- Image Render:

#### Short
They reach the final approach, where the prisoner addresses Hulda and her five friends before a clockwork figure awakens in the adjoining library.

#### Intermediate
In the map library, Menestheus describes another captive creature used by the order to perceive future events. The party then hears the prisoner announce that Hulda's five friends are outside its room, and enters a vast library where a porcelain clockwork figure raises its head to study them.

#### Long
They enter a map library where Menestheus recalls other Arithrimos Lamperum facilities and a captive creature that experienced its entire life simultaneously, allowing the order to question it about events others would call the future. Beyond a fabrication lab, room 25 contains another listening station, and the prisoner's looping voice addresses Hulda: the dragon-killer is bringing five friends, they are outside its room now, and they can hear it. One door short of the prisoner, the party enters room 26, a towering library with chained books. A human-sized porcelain and clockwork torso anchored to a desk lifts its head, opens its eyes, and regards the party as the session ends.
