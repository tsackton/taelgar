## Timeline

## Narrative

### Choosing The Glass Key

The party finishes interpreting the Dreamer notebook's conflation of dreams and souls, then reviews the choices left by Hulda, Lord Endless Ending, and the prison at the center of the shifting house. Menestheus explains what little he knows about the order and its absent leadership while Kallipseis returns to work on her fungal golem.

The party remains reluctant to help either Hulda or Lord Endless Ending, but the glass key still offers their clearest route out. They decide to retrieve it without opening the prisoner's door and follow Menestheus out of Kallipseis's greenhouse.

### Lessons And Clockworks

The party crosses a parlor into a lecture hall whose chalkboard diagrams the life cycles of dandelions and other wind-dispersed plants. An enormous black painting conceals a field of stars, a tear in the universe, and the textured form of a seed sprouting through the gap. Menestheus recognizes the image as exactly the kind of uncontrolled growth between worlds that Arithrimos Lamperum was created to stop.

Tarek also finds The Shape of Discovery, a brief treatise arguing that curiosity shapes possibility through the same irrational unpredictability as greed and games of chance. The party connects Kallipseis's description of infected people to Alden's pale, round appearance, surprising strength, and fear of fire.

In the next clockworks workshop, Tarek finds and wraps an unfinished desk-sized clock. Its numbers change as the party moves, apparently tracking the prison's position in space and time. Carrying the clock, the party reaches another mechanical listening room.

### Thirst And The Animal Lab

The notebook at the new listening station is titled Thirst. Its entries describe the speaker filling bodies emptied of dreaming souls with its children, while those children search for the person who can set it free. The party identifies the children with the fungal infection and suspects that the prisoner manipulated Alden and the suspicious deed that brought them to Dandelion House.

When Kaito takes the listener's place, he hears the prisoner say that it is full of dreams, can read the party, and knows they are helping it. It can see both the moving exit and a hole large enough for its children to pass through. The party resolves not to free it and continues onward.

The next room is an animal experimentation laboratory filled with cages, restraint devices, dead specimens, an infected ant colony, and humanoid remains. As Menestheus approaches the far exit, a hand reaches from a side passage and drags him out of sight.

### Mushroom Men

The party enters initiative among the laboratory tables and specimen containers, with Menestheus already grappled by a mushroom man. Edric rages and charges the captor, Kaito attacks with Dissonant Whispers, and Tarek's approach awakens a second infected corpse that looks almost identical to Alden.

The creatures retaliate with elongated fingers and mouths opening across their chests. One briefly catches Tarek while the other pulls Menestheus inward, drops him to zero hit points, and inflicts a failed death save. Ayveen's Silvery Barbs blunts the attack, Txarro becomes a tiger, and the party focuses its attacks on the two infected bodies.

Txarro restores Menestheus with Cure Wounds before Edric crushes the final mushroom man's head. Its death releases a cloud of seeds that Txarro breathes in. The party burns both bodies, which flare like dry straw.

### Infection And Locked Offices

Further healing closes Menestheus's external wounds, but Tarek's examination reveals white filaments growing inside his mouth. Tarek quietly warns Txarro, and they decide not to tell Menestheus until they know whether anything can be done.

The party moves through a dead plant greenhouse, a picked-over armory, and an unfinished orrery that models the planes as connected, interlocking spheres. They then reach a corridor of offices belonging to Arithrimos Lamperum, including a prominent office labeled for Thalestria.

Thalestria's door has no visible keyhole and withstands repeated attempts to smash it open. Weakened after raging, Edric accepts the failure as a warning from the gods, and the party moves on.

### The Prisoner Speaks

In the map library, Menestheus recalls other Arithrimos Lamperum facilities and a captive creature that experienced its entire life simultaneously. The order could ask it questions about events that others would call the future, although Menestheus remains uneasy about whether it was a prisoner or a pet.

Beyond a fabrication lab, the party enters room 25 and finds another listening station. The prisoner's looping voice addresses Hulda directly: the dragon-killer is bringing five friends, those friends are outside its room now, and they can hear it. The party realizes that the prisoner is describing them in the present.

One door short of the prisoner, the party enters room 26, a vast library with books chained to its shelves. A human-sized porcelain and clockwork torso is anchored to a desk just inside. As the party enters, it raises its head, opens its eyes, and studies them.
