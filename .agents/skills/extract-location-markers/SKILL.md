# Extract location markers

This skill produces **one file containing a place dictionary and one marker list**. Read [the location-marker format](references/format.md) before authoring. It replaces the location A/B requirements in the old shared experimental contract. Use an explicitly requested output directory; otherwise write `cleaned/<stem>-location-markers.json` in the existing session bundle. Preserve source transcripts and session YAML. Existing pilot outputs and builders retain their old format and must not be overwritten as part of a new extraction.

Resolve the YAML-registered polished transcript and fingerprint source/YAML before reading. Preserve source-local UIDs and complete shared-UID turn boundaries. A time-marker file is optional; if referencing one, bind to its revision and verify that it uses the same session and transcript revision. Location extraction does not require a scene map, a time extraction, or a time assignment for every line.

Read the complete transcript sequentially, including combat, lore, and chatter, before calling extraction complete; reread truncated output. Use search as a supplementary checklist. Resolve accepted proposals, retcons, canceled detours, compressed returns, pronouns, and changes of viewpoint or actor group from context. Do not replace played evidence with a recap or private DM plans. Record any concrete source or reading limitation that affects the extraction in optional top-level `limitations`; do not include reading-window logs or review-status fields.

Author source-ordered markers of **presence**, **movement**, and relevant **observation**, with actors, concise interpretation, exact evidence, and supported structured fields. A journey is a movement marker at the scale narrated: five transcript lines covering 200 miles may yield one marker. Preserve stated endpoints, route/waypoints, mode, distance, duration, and completion without inventing daily positions or intermediate visits. Distance and transcript length do not determine marker count. Split when independently useful activity, actor changes, destination changes, or distinct movements warrant it; avoid a tactical trace of every step. A brief stop can stay inside the route. A meaningful stop can have its own presence marker linked to the journey without manufacturing daily travel legs.

A completed movement establishes arrival; do not generate a duplicate presence marker from that same evidence. Later descriptions of the same ongoing location can strengthen or clarify the existing record without creating another marker. Add a marker for a meaningful spatial or actor/context change, a distinct stop not already represented, or a return; repeated place mentions or substantial conversation alone do not require one. For each `arrived` movement, quote the passage establishing that the listed actors reached the recorded destination. Later narration placing them there is sufficient; cite it in the same movement record. If the evidence ends with the actors approaching, use `underway`. Separate plans, interrupted travel, observations through thresholds, and magical views from physical presence. Track split actors independently; speaking in a passage does not prove a character is present, and different actors do not prove concurrency. Record observed subjects separately from observers only when their identity matters to interpreting the location observation; do not inventory everyone visible or build an NPC whereabouts record. Preserve corrections without silently choosing between unresolved contradictory accounts.

Use `reading` for the interpretation, quantity `basis` for why a value is supported, and optional `note` only for a material qualification not already expressed. Apply the same restraint to place and relationship notes. Do not repeat explanations across fields or put generic extraction instructions in the output; preserve meaningful location detail and evidence.

Use the place dictionary for identity and necessary physical context. Reuse local IDs for evidence-supported repeated references, including unnamed rooms and camps. Include only places needed to understand the markers; incidental route features can remain text. Optional typed place relationships describe supported enduring context, such as a library within a tower or a camp near a town. Route direction belongs on the movement marker; a ship's temporary harbor location belongs on a marker, never as its permanent geographic parent. Retain doorway/stair topology only when useful for interpreting movement, not to reconstruct an entire building. Do not invent coordinates, floor numbers, metric geometry, or relationships from unavailable map gestures.

Retain the larger setting as well as local detail. When supported, include the named manor, keep, settlement, region, or realm enclosing the played rooms or grounds, even when it needs no separate arrival/presence marker. Link local places to that setting with evidence-backed `within` relations, and retain a supported regional chain when useful. Revisit earlier unnamed references when a later passage identifies the enclosing site. Do not put every garden or crypt inside the manor building merely because it belongs to the estate: distinguish the estate, main house, and nearby grounds at the scale supported. `near`, `above`, route direction, and a vault directory are not containment evidence. If the parent or relationship is unresolved, preserve that limit in a place/relation note rather than inventing a hierarchy.

For a vault identity, search filenames, names/aliases, and variants; read the candidate note and surrounding source passage. Preserve session-era labels. Similar names are not identity; modern geography does not establish played movement or session-era spatial relationships. Omit `vaultPath` when unresolved and record material ambiguity in a note. Do not create, rename, merge, or move canonical notes, or force every local place to have a vault match.

Actively check vault identities for enclosing named settings and significant destinations; do not stop at room-level dictionary entries or treat `vaultPath` as dispensable when a match is verified. A canonical note can resolve the identity of the transcript's unnamed manor without making its modern geography a transcript fact. Keep the transcript wording as the label where needed, record the verified `vaultPath`, and explain any consequential naming qualification in `note`. Place/relationship `evidence` remains transcript evidence. Do not import vault-only containment or current whereabouts into those fields; a useful vault-only orientation can be attributed explicitly in a place `note` with its vault source and temporal limit, rather than presented as played geography. No new movement marker follows from a dictionary parent or vault match.

For every arrived movement, read the quoted endpoint and check each listed actor against that place at that point. If only a scout reaches the cliff lip while companions remain on the path, limit the arrival to the scout and the lip. For a round trip, quote the return as well as the departure. Keep the outing together unless a distinct stop or actor change warrants a separate marker. Recheck stops, bypasses, magical transport, frame changes, and planned departures. Verify JSON structure, source hashes, exact quotations, whole-turn UID boundaries, place/marker references, and containment cycles. Check that a reader can identify the supported enclosing setting, that verified major-place links were retained, and that containment has its own evidence rather than being inferred from a vault identity. Ensure observations and plans generate no physical visits, begun travel generates no destination arrival, and mobile places have no permanent geographic parent inferred from a temporary stop. Structural checks do not certify semantic completeness. Existing A/B builders neither extract arbitrary transcripts nor validate this revised format.

Deliver the location-marker JSON in the requested output directory or the bundle location above, with a concise handoff of material identity, actor, or route uncertainty. No separate visits, journeys, observations, plans, or spatial-graph collections; no mandatory HTML/table artifact. Preserve human corrections and stable IDs on revision; recheck dependent claims when evidence changes rather than replacing hashes to pass checks. Record uncertainty without stopping over incidental wording. Stop before NPC whereabouts publication, campaign-map construction, scene partitioning, time/YAML changes, canonical geography edits, or skill installation unless separately requested.
