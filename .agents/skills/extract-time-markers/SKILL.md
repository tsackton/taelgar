# Extract time markers

Extract **evidence of time passing within the session's events, and dates or times that anchor those events**. Each record is source UID → inferred time or duration → evidence. Read [the time-marker format](references/format.md) before authoring. It supersedes the time A/B requirements in the older shared experimental contract. Use an explicitly requested output directory; otherwise write `cleaned/<stem>-time-markers.json` in the existing session bundle. Preserve source transcripts and session YAML. Existing pilot outputs and their builders use the old format and must not be overwritten as part of a new extraction.

Session events include actions played out, downtime resolved, journeys summarized, and events established retrospectively during the session. They need not occur in transcript order. An opening date does not establish that everything narrated afterward happens after that date. Include dates, clock times, time-of-day cues, elapsed intervals, rests, overnight transitions, compressed travel or downtime, and temporal transitions into and out of scenes narrated out of chronological order. Retrospective statements can establish or clarify session timing.

For every candidate, identify **which session event or interval it dates, measures, or connects**. Mentioning time during an event is insufficient: the statement must establish something about that event's timing. Exclude unrelated historical dates, character-history durations, cosmology, philosophical discussion, jokes, and hypothetical future activities. Do not retain excluded material merely to classify it. Preserve every qualifying assertion separately, including repetitions and corrections; apply that preservation rule only after deciding whether the assertion concerns session time.

A proposed duration, travel estimate, or spell's maximum duration does not establish elapsed time. Use it only as supporting evidence when subsequent narration establishes a relevant timing constraint, and label any inference. An explicitly expired spell can establish elapsed time; merely casting an hour-long spell cannot establish that an hour passed. A later action does not by itself prove an earlier forecast was accurate.

Resolve the existing session YAML and its registered polished transcript. Record source/YAML fingerprints before reading. Preserve source-local UIDs and whole polished-turn mappings; session + source revision + UID identifies evidence. Do not substitute a recap or use recording runtime as world time.

Read the complete transcript sequentially, including combat, lore, and chatter, before calling extraction complete; reread truncated output. Search is a supplementary checklist, not a substitute for reading. Look for dates, bare clock answers, time-of-day cues, rests, sleep/waking, elapsed intervals, compressed travel, and frame changes. Resolve question/answer pairs, accepted proposals, corrections, and retrospective confirmations using surrounding speech. A smaller output does not justify a narrower reading method. Record any concrete source or reading limitation that affects the extraction in optional top-level `limitations`; do not include reading-window logs or review-status fields.

Author one marker per qualifying session-time assertion in source order: its source UID/range, inferred time or duration, and exact evidence. Preserve separate qualifying assertions even when they describe the same event, repeat a value, occur in adjacent turns, or share a UID. Opening morning and a later approximate clock are separate markers; sleeping overnight and a later next-morning date are separate markers. A single narrated six-day interval can be one marker, but a separately stated endpoint date remains another. Supporting context may be quoted without absorbing another qualifying assertion's marker. Exclude routine table-time false positives; do not discard qualifying assertions because they repeat, are later corrected, or add no new elapsed time. Keep vague quantities vague. Label inferred values and YAML-derived date components; do not assign a date or clock to every passage.

Use `reading` for the interpretation, `basis` for why a structured value is supported, and optional `note` only for a material qualification not already expressed. Do not repeat the same explanation across these fields or put generic extraction instructions in the output. This prose discipline never authorizes deleting or merging assertions or their evidence.

Keep markers in transcript order for traceability, not as a claim of chronological order. Identify the session event or interval in `reading`; use supported relative anchors and relationships to distinguish earlier events, containment, overlap, and returns. A retrospective account of session downtime or travel remains session-time evidence. Past tense, recollection, or a flashback label does not make it irrelevant. Distinguish a scene enacted or resolved retrospectively from a character merely mentioning earlier history. If that distinction is genuinely unclear and affects chronology, retain the relevant candidate with a specific uncertainty. A played historical scene or vision may have its own temporal frame: keep its actual scene-time evidence separate and never count the gap to the party's present as elapsed adventure time.

Use optional context, actors, completion, and marker relationships for complications. Record flashback entry and return when identifiable; uncertainty about the return remains explicit. A retcon may insert an earlier scene without replacing an existing claim. Keep the original assertion and its correction as separate markers, even for an immediate self-correction within one turn; link the correction with `revises` when supersession is established. Mark containment, partial overlap, or the same interval only when evidenced; split actors alone do not prove concurrency. Retain retrospective confirmations as their own markers while making clear that they do not establish another rest or additional elapsed time. A study interval already containing a rest must not gain another hour. Preserve local-clock changes on teleport without inventing elapsed time or geographic offsets.

Use the user's one-hour short-rest/eight-hour long-rest conventions as labeled assumptions when a rest is established. A proposed or interrupted rest does not establish its full duration. Overnight is not exactly eight hours; rest benefits alone do not prove that narration reached morning. Preserve completed overnights and multi-day jumps without turning overlapping accounts into additive durations.

Compare YAML with the adventure interval covered by the session, including downtime, travel, and earlier events established through nonlinear narration. Use exactly `matched`, `compatible`, or `conflict`, as defined in the format reference. Do not restrict the span to events after the opening scene's date or derive it simply from the earliest/latest dates mentioned; establish which events the dates anchor and how they belong to the session's chronology. YAML start may anchor a relative span if disclosed; never infer missing days from YAML end and use them to validate that end. Check independently spoken dates too. Missing coverage is not a contradiction: use `compatible` when the full span is not established, and never turn an incomplete or ambiguous span into a supported total of zero days. Record ambiguous opening/closing interpretations as boundary uncertainties for later cross-session review, not automatic date corrections. Historical YAML used multiple boundary conventions; flag a conflict only when no reasonable supported boundary reading reconciles it. Do not normalize dates or require adjacent ranges to share a date.

Recheck relevance first: every marker must date, measure, or connect a session event or interval. Then recheck actual advances, both ends of jumps, actor scope, frame changes, bedtime/departure framing, and contradictory speech. Check that each qualifying assertion retains its own marker at its source position: no combined morning/clock, overnight/waking-date, duration/endpoint, or original/corrected assertions, and no dropped repetitions. Returning from an earlier scene or recounting an interval does not itself add elapsed time. Later evidence may support an inference at an earlier marker but must not silently move a later clock reading to that earlier moment. Verify source hashes, quotation membership, whole-turn UID boundaries, marker references, structured values, and the YAML assessment. Incomplete reading must be labeled and cannot produce `matched`; structural checks do not certify semantic completeness. Existing pilot builders neither extract arbitrary transcripts nor validate this revised format.

Deliver the time-marker JSON in the requested output directory or the bundle location above, with a concise handoff of the YAML result and material uncertainties. Present a simple review table directly reflecting every JSON marker, in source order: **time marker | brief explanation of the session event it anchors or measures | transcript UID range**. Briefly show uncertainty and nonlinear relationships in the explanation. The table is a display of the JSON, not a second timeline; no separate HTML or maintained table file is required. Preserve human corrections and stable marker IDs when revising; changed sources require reinspection rather than replacing hashes to pass checks. Record uncertainties without stopping over incidental wording. Do not edit YAML, transcripts, canonical dates, location artifacts, installed skills, or later timeline outputs unless separately requested.
