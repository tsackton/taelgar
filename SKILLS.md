# Taelgar Skills

Last reviewed: **2026-10-29**

This guide covers the 29 skills maintained in this repository under `.agents/skills/`. It does not list personal or installed plugin skills, which can vary between machines.

Describe the task in ordinary language, or name a skill explicitly with `$skill-name`. Include the relevant note, session, source, or folder and what you want done. The linked skill instructions define the actual workflow and approvals; this guide is a way to choose the right starting point.

## Session preparation

For a recorded session, the usual sequence is **transcribe-session → prepare-session-source → session-note-prep**. Start at the step that matches the material you already have. Each step has its own scope and approvals; requesting one does not automatically request the rest.

For transcript products, use **process-transcript** once a raw transcript or session bundle exists. It prepares/normalizes artifacts, makes reasonable contextual repairs to the cleaned UID text, and produces readable full-session Markdown with source mapping. Unrecoverable incidental speech can become `[garbled]` or `[cross-talk]` in the polished version only. Human review contains exact-span proposals and complete-exchange context for consequential choices. The review Markdown carries its own pending decisions and can be resumed alongside the retained references without the original processing state. Session-local state supports resuming work and processing different sessions concurrently. Finalization after review keeps the original, prepared UID, cleaned UID, polished Markdown, and session metadata, and removes temporary processing/review files. Spoken factual mistakes and contradictions are preserved. Transcription, fact-checking, recaps, and highlight extraction remain separate workflows.

### Individual session stages

For an optional smaller input to interaction extraction, use [prepare-interaction-transcript](.agents/skills/prepare-interaction-transcript/SKILL.md) after polished Markdown exists. It creates only `<stem>-interactions-draft.md`, preserves meaningful speech and lore, and replaces routine combat/mechanics/chatter with `((rolls))`-style labels retaining every original UID span. Ambiguous remarks remain even beside technical chatter. Metadata is limited to format version, session YAML path, and polished-source hash; snips need no separate records. Restoring an edited turn recovers its complete polished text. Example: “Make a conservative in-world draft from Dunmar 10’s polished transcript,” or “Replace dice arithmetic with `((rolls))` while keeping the surrounding lore.” Original transcripts and session YAML remain unchanged; this experimental derivative is not yet wired into the interaction extractor.

For provisional scene indexing and the key session-date validation, use [map-transcript-scenes](.agents/skills/map-transcript-scenes/SKILL.md) after polishing, optionally with an interactions draft as a reading view. It checks transcript chronology using YAML as the calendar anchor, leads review with conflicts or material timeline uncertainty, accounts for every UID as a scene or contextual passage, and renders a compact table on demand. An unstated spoken calendar date alone is not a review concern. It preserves source files and YAML, and does not generate entities, interactions, or recaps. Example: “Map this session's scenes and check its YAML dates against the transcript.” Its scene map feeds entity checking and v7 candidate projection; final interaction generation is still to integrate.

For entity identity and vault-link checking, use [check-session-entities](.agents/skills/check-session-entities/SKILL.md) after scene mapping. It inventories NPCs, divine beings, significant items, and organizations. Meaningful discussion of lore-rich items qualifies even when unused; routine combat gear use is excluded. It verifies candidate pages against original speech, and gives an entity-first review with confidence and meaningful exclusions. Divine/NPC inventories can be viewed separately. It reuses YAML and the scene map without generating interactions or changing source files. Example: “Check the entities in this session,” or “Show the evidence for entity e003.”

These new stages are experimental; assisted review and Obsidian integration are unfinished. The [pipeline and model contract](.agents/skills/check-session-entities/references/pipeline-model.md) documents current scene/entity maps and the concrete v7 `session.json` schema, including divine entities and contextual mentions. A read-only helper validates v7 or projects current maps into a candidate; it does not generate interactions, apply human decisions, or publish final outputs.

## Reviewing and completing vault notes

| --- | --- | --- |
| [review-stub-staging](.agents/skills/review-stub-staging/SKILL.md) | Works with you to complete a person or place page from evidence and your corrections, including identity information, pronunciation, and the generated header. After approval, it retires stub status and files Staging pages where appropriate. Full linting is a separate choice. | “Help me complete this person stub,” or “Review this place in Staging and prepare it for filing.” |
| [lint-taelgar-note](.agents/skills/lint-taelgar-note/SKILL.md) | Reviews eligible notes for metadata, structure, links, source support, and editorial completeness or currency. Records open findings or a completed clean review. Worldbuilding and dot/underscore directories are excluded. | “Lint these three reference notes,” or “Re-lint this previously reviewed page.” |
| [lint-cleanup](.agents/skills/lint-cleanup/SKILL.md) | Resolves existing findings that concern only names, language, or pronunciation in small reviewed batches. It does not perform a new full lint. | “Find a batch of name-only lint findings and work through them with me.” |
| [batch-lint-cleanup](.agents/skills/batch-lint-cleanup/SKILL.md) | Presents name-only lint proposals and full original reports in an open review document, with language guesses for adoption, batch approval, and selective row regeneration. It does not re-lint. | “Prepare ten name-only lint reviews; let me approve the batch or redo selected rows.” |
| [review-linked-notes](.agents/skills/review-linked-notes/SKILL.md) | Collects the context pages linked from selected notes or session recaps, presents the scope, and coordinates approved creation and linting. It reviews the surrounding pages rather than rewriting the source note. A named ready subset can proceed with approval while blocked pages remain pending. | “Review the people and places linked from this session.” |
| [vault-brainstorming-cleanup](.agents/skills/vault-brainstorming-cleanup/SKILL.md) | Finds substantial noncanonical idea passages and proposes their extraction, organization, consolidation, or retirement in reviewed batches. It preserves uncertainty and does not adopt ideas as canon. | “Find overlapping brainstorming about this region and propose a cleanup.” |
| [summarize-lore-changes](.agents/skills/summarize-lore-changes/SKILL.md) | Reviews vault history for meaningful lore and campaign-content changes, optionally by contributor. Quotes small changes, identifies substantial rewrites, and skips lint, metadata, and header maintenance unless it changes the lore. | “Summarize tsackton’s lore changes over the last four days.” |
