# Planar Dualism

Planar Dualism is a minority school of metaphysical cosmology that treats the [[Astral Plane]] and the [[Ethereal Plane]] as the only truly fundamental planes. In this model, what other scholars call the [[Plane of Souls]], the [[Land of the Dead]], and the [[Plane of Creation]] are regions or densities within the Astral Plane, while the [[Plane of Magic]] is a deep region of the Ethereal Plane rather than a distinct plane.

Planar dualists often describe the multiverse through images of flow rather than geometry. The Ethereal Plane is imagined as a vast sea of magical energy, with [[Ley Lines]] as rivers or cascades running through the planes toward the deepest wells of magic. The Astral Plane is the corresponding realm of [[Soulstuff]], with the Land of the Dead and Plane of Creation understood as places where soulstuff becomes especially dense.

This model is not dominant at the [[University of Chardon]], but some of its metaphors are common even among scholars who reject the doctrine itself. In particular, planar dualist language is often used to describe [[Extraplanar Weak Point|extraplanar weak points]] as currents or conduits of planar energy rather than simple places where two planes are geometrically close.
