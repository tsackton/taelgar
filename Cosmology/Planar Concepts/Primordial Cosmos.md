# Primordial Cosmos

The Primordial Cosmos names both an age and a state of existence; without settled time or fixed places, the distinction between when and where is unclear. Its nature is beyond reliable reconstruction. Cosmologists can describe the planes of the present [[Multiverse]], but disagree about their origins and even their fundamental divisions. Accounts of the [[Riving]] often speak of the planes being separated; they do not establish a map of what existed before that separation.

Some folklore traces [[Giants|giants]] and [[Dragons|dragons]] to powers of this lost existence. Giants retain traditions of an age of extraordinary craft and magic, while dragons remain creatures of myth and immense power. Tales of the [[Fey|fey]] and the shifting time and geography of the [[Feywild]] may suggest something of its strangeness, but do not establish the fey's origins or provide a reliable account of the Primordial Cosmos.

%%^Campaign:none%%

In the Primordial Cosmos, neither time nor causality followed the patterns familiar to mortal minds, and even the existence of one stable reality was uncertain. What came before the Riving cannot be dated, and although events could still happen, for many events the distinction between happening “during” the Riving and “before” the Riving is not clear.

The Primordial Cosmos was inhabited by powerful beings that could reshape reality with a thought or a gesture; these creatures were neither mortals nor gods. They were highly magical, with an undefined variety of forms, including at least some predecessor form of [[Dragons]] and [[Giants]], as well as representatives of primordial forces such as chaos, order, growth, and death, among others. The [[Material Plane]] was not part of the Primordial Cosmos. In a fundamental sense, there was no geography or fixed places.

Although dragons and giants are the only creatures with canonical predecessors before the Riving, it is likely that some other creatures, in particular [[Fey]] and [[Elementals]], have some relationship to the primordial beings. The tales of these and other stranger creatures may contain echoes of the Primordial Cosmos.

The origins of the Primordial Cosmos, the [[Divine Presence]], and the [[Void Mind]] remain undefined. The primordial cosmos was simpler than the present Multiverse, but its actual form is not established: it could have been one or more planes. The [[Energy Realms]] were created in some way by the Riving; this does not date the origins of every other plane.
