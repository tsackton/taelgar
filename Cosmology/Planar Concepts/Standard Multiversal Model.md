# Standard Multiversal Model

The Standard Multiversal Model is the dominant scholarly classification of the [[Multiverse]] taught by the [[Faculty of Metaphysics]] at the [[University of Chardon]]. It grew out of earlier Drankorian work, especially the synthesis associated with [[Gaius Devarro]], and remains the most widely used framework among Chardonian planar scholars.

In its common form, the model divides the planes into four broad classes:

- Fundamental Planes: the [[Plane of Creation]], [[Astral Plane]], [[Plane of Magic]], [[Ethereal Plane]], [[Material Plane]], and [[Plane of Souls]].
- [[Energy Realms]]: planes associated with the fundamental energies of the multiverse, especially the elemental planes.
- [[Spiritual Realms]]: the planes of gods, demons, and other spiritual beings, generally lying beyond the [[Land of the Dead]].
- [[Echo Realms]]: reflections, distortions, or reverberations shaped by the [[Riving]], including the [[Feywild]] and the [[Dreamworld]]. [[Demiplanes]] and the [[Shadowfolds]] are usually also included in the Echo Realms.

The model is a classification scheme, not a universally accepted statement of metaphysical truth. [[Yendalism]] rejects many of its distinctions in favor of the division between the [[Inner Realms]] and [[Outer Realms]], while [[Planar Dualism|planar dualists]] collapse much of the model into the paired fundamentals of the Astral and Ethereal realms.
